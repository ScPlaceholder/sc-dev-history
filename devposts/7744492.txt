As far as we know the issues with the latest NVidia drivers are problems in the driver itself, and impact a variety of games to differing degrees, but most games use DX12 rather than Vulkan and so use different driver code. We've verified there are no validation issues on our side, and replaying our frame through 3rd party applications or an other hardware/drivers works fine and has done for years.
I believe the latest driver has remedied some of the issues for some games, but some issues remain, and so we'll be contacting NVidia to see if any more fixes are in the works or whether they can assist in diagnosing what is going wrong with the new drivers on Star Citizen.
