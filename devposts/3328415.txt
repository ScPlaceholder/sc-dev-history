The HOTAS reset issue is basically just a use-case that the input system didn't really know about. If an action is turned off (e.g. IFCS actions are turned off when you go into your  MobiGlas) then the input system resets the values of those actions to 0 ... which caused the ship to stop because IFCS is still on when that happens. We just needed to teach the system to not do that for certain actions. It's just more complicated as it is a low level change in the input system that affects the whole game and is not something that's isolated to IFCS.
