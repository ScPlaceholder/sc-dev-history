For 3.23, we have introduced a full revamp to the character customizer system. This includes the introduction of DNA Sculpting, facial hair, hair dye, and more. This also includes the ability to save, import, and export your created characters. These will be saved in the user folder in a folder called CustomCharacters.

- What is your least favorite function in the Character Customizer?
- What is a feature that you would like to see next for the Character Customizer?
- Describe the flow of creating your character in your own words
- What information is missing on the UI?
Looking forward to hearing from you, and feel free to share your creations in this thread!
