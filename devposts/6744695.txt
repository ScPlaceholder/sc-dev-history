Greetings,
In 3.23 we have updated the way items in shops interact with players to give clear and detailed information about the physical item they are browsing. Looking at an object while close to it, a new AR informational window will appear next to the objects in view with descriptions of what the item does, it's stats, price, your current funds, and a new hold "F" to buy option.
Give this new shopping UI a try and let us know what you think below. We are specifically interesting in hearing about:
- What is your favourite part of the new Shopping Experience? Least favourite?
- What items have you used the new shopping interface for?
- Do you think that the information supplied from the card is sufficient enough for day-to-day use? If not, what information is missing?
- Is the feedback provided from the UI clear enough?
- What is your expected flow for the shopping experience, and where does it differ from what is in this patch?
- How would you compare the new shopping experience with the old shopping experience?
Looking forward to hearing from you!
