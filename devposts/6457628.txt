Design has put up a change to ship tractor beams to test how they function without volume limitations.  All ship tractor beams have had their volume limits raised so that only massive ships (Idris sized or larger) have volume based restrictions.  The restrictions are now solely on mass.  We are putting this out to determine if there are unforeseen consequences of this change or edge cases that may disrupt gameplay flow, especially for salvage.
Let us know if you run into any issues below or how this change is working out for you.
