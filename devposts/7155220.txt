this simply moves the problem... if the population is shown and its too low, you wont bother joining and matches never get a chance to get rolling.
From what i've observed in our analytics the average player is _extremely_ impatient. Most players enter, see too few players and leave within seconds. Meanwhile, atleast in majority of cases i've observed, if a solo player to small group sit and wait even a couple minutes then full lobbies get rolling relatively fast. Racing is a great example, i saw comments like "all this work on new racetracks i join and no one is there" meanwhile I can see in the analytics the playercount has skyrocketed; so why is this player saying this?
Further investigation found that the vast majority of instances had 2 players, despite the max being 8. And since you cant join an in-progress race and the average player wont wait the average race-time in a low-pop lobby for the other races to cycle into them. Most players waited for the minimum required (2) then hit the ready button; so I spent an afternoon testing the theory by entering and not hitting ready, allowed the ready timer to tick away; forcing the lobby to wait, and sure enough more and more players joined - resulting in an awesome heated 8-player races.
If you can recall back when we had higher required player counts this also reflected the same impatience from players. Even when we lowered the required to 4 the number of players who didnt want to wait was very high; but as soon as we lowered it to 2 and allowed matches to play out anyway we see player counts climb back up; as we found if you join an in-progress match, even in low population, you're far more likely to stick around than one waiting for players; which is (and my point being) all you will be doing on the Frontend if you could see player numbers.
