As usual, you can use this thread to submit and vote on questions related to the Hammerhead.
So tune in this Friday on twitch.tv/starcitizen and post your questions below.
POSTING GUIDELINES
- Only make replies to the OP.
- Only one question per post.
- KEEP IT SUCCINCT.  No links. No paragraphs. No introductions. No thank yous. Distill it  down to what you really want to say. This IS NOT the place for attempts  to direct the course of development (that's literally the rest of  Spectrum.) This IS your place to ask brief questions of our developers.  Your responsibility in this process is to break it down to one or two  sentences at most. Let's work together to save our developers some time.
- Consider the type of answer you're looking for. Is it  possible for your question to have a 1-2 minute answer? Are you asking  how an entire game system will function? Is it a topic better suited to  the sort of deep-dive ATV segments that have visuals? If so, you will  get votes, but you will not get the question used on the show. It just  won't fit here. There are other broadcasts for this kind of topic.
- Do not submit one question, gets votes with that question, and then reword the question or replace it with another one entirely.
- Do  not make use of special formatting in an attempt to draw more  attention, and therefore more votes, to your questions i.e. BOLD, H1,  etc.
- No "post-launch" questions. This is a program dedicated to  the continuing development of the game, a focused on the immediate and  near-term future of the project.
- No pictures. Make it easy for people to find the question they want to vote on without having to scroll through pictures.
- Post violating these rules and inline replies to other questions will be deleted.
- Have fun with it.
