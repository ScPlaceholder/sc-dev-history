1) Yes, you can use noise fields to disrupt an AG lock on yourself. Noise fields will disrupt sensors (if the target signature is low enough) and by extension any form of target locks and aim assists. That includes auto gimbals, fixed assist and gun convergence. It's simply because if a radar can't see a target, it won't be able to lock on to it ... by extension that means that the attacking ship cannot compute a valid fire solution.
2) We're quite happy with how the turrets turned out and do not plan to add auto gimbals to them at this time. We rather focus on QOL improvements for turrets.
