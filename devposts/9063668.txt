## Features & Gameplay
Recco Battaglia Story Missions
We have made the Battaglia story missions unlock sequentially one after the next without any rep gain required for easier testing.
Ship & Module Availability
Added/removed the following ships and modules to in-game shops:
- Removed Aurora Mk1's from Shops
- Added Aurora Mk2 as a replacement to Mk1's at locations where relevant
- Added Aurora Mk2 Cargo and Missile modules to various locations including cargo offices, pyro trader outposts, and Teach's item kiosk
- Added Kruger L-22 Alpha Wolf to Astro Armada
- Added MISC Hull B to New Deal and Teach's
- Added Drake Golem OX to Pyro Station Dealerships and New Deal
- Added Greycat UTV to Pyro Station Dealerships and Astro Armada
## Technical & Bugfixes
- Potential Fix: Contracts that grant item rewards can no longer be shared with other players.
- Potential Fix: A locker in a Battaglia location has been repositioned away from a doorway to prevent players clipping into it.
- Potential Fix: Mission progression in the Missing Persons mission could stall
- Potential Fix: Battaglia mining objectives now track progress against the mineable entity itself rather than the surrounding action area, improving reliability when mining objective rocks.
- Potential Fix: Battaglia scanning objectives could stop functioning entirely if an asteroid was spawned during the objective. The scanning module no longer shuts down in this situation.
- Potential Fix: Refueling missions could advance before refueling was complete.
- Potential Fix: Contested Zone NPCs were not spawning armed and now correctly receive a pistol.
- Potential Fix: The vendor at Cordrys in Levski was not standing at his counter correctly.
