Invictus Launch Week is among the clouds this year, and we want to see your best shots from the show floor!
To encourage visiting the show every day, we're kicking off a screenshot contest where we challenge you to take your best pictures from Invictus Launch Week's show floor. There's a new prize with every set of manufacturers, and you can enter to win up to five times!
We'll be looking for screenshots specifically taken from the Vision Center show floor on Orison during Invictus Launch Week, and featuring the manufacturer from that day. Bonus points for creativity, humor, and style!
Everyone is eligible to take part, even if you're just here for the Free Fly Event! Submit your screenshots either on the dedicated submission thread for each set of manufacturers or on Twitter using the hashtag #Invictus2952.
## Submission Threads
- Day 1-2: Anvil Aerospace
- Day 3-4: Aegis Dynamics
- Day 5-6: RSI, Origin, Consolidated Outland, Argo
- Day 7-8: Crusader, Tumbril, MISC
- Day 9-10: Drake
## Notes
- Be sure to read the Contest Rules.
- Screenshots must be taken by you, be in their original format, and cannot have any image processing or effects applied (such as ReShade). Only one submission per manufacturer set, make it count!
- Group submissions are not allowed. Multiple people can be in the screenshot, but only the person submitting is eligible for a prize.
- You can only win once during the contest
- Only post screenshots that include a shot from the ILW show floor. Any other subject will be disqualified.

- Invictus 2952 Day 3-4, ending May 23, 11:59pm Pacific - Aegis Redeemer Standalone ship with Lifetime Insurance
- Invictus 2952 Day 5-6, ending May 25, 11:59pm Pacific - RSI Scorpius Standalone ship with Lifetime Insurance
- Invictus 2952 Day 7-8, ending May 27, 11:59pm Pacific - MISC Hull A Standalone ship with Lifetime Insurance
- Invictus 2952 Day 9-10, ending May 29, 11:59pm Pacific - Drake Cutlass Black Standalone ship with Lifetime Insurance + Drake Mule Standalone vehicle with Lifetime Insurance
