Heya, if you correct the IPD setting down to 0.1 you're effectively telling the game that your eyes just a few millimeters apart which will cause the world to seem gigantic. The scaling is based on what IPD (distance between your eyes) the headset is reporting (the lowest value on the Reverb G2 is 60mm).
I would strongly advice against using the IPD scale to get a different feel for the markers ... the markers don't fully work yet (which is in the patch notes) and if you use the IPD scale with such extreme values to fix that you will mess up other parts of the VR experience.
