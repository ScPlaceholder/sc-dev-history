That's also on the list to fix (its for the same reason that VR offsets are currently not synced in mp). The issue is that any kind of VR rotational will also induce a small change in position because the unified actor rig will move the head joint with it (you can enable that behaviour with the custom CVARs .... it is fine with normal head tracking but in VR it will induce more motion sickness because it doesn't completely align with your actual head movement). Long term we have to try to do it in a different way (so that the VR offsets define where the rig is going instead of just being dragged along). But it was way too much for a t0 implementation.
