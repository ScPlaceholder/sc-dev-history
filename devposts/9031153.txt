Hello everyone!
Every two weeks, we accompany the Roadmap update with a brief explanatory note to give you insight into the decision-making that led to any changes. This is part of an effort to make our communications more transparent, more specific, and more insightful for all of you who help to make Star Citizen and Squadron 42 possible.
The 1.0 column showcases features and content planned for inclusion in or before Star Citizen 1.0. This means you may not have to wait for the 1.0 release to experience them. As features/content in the 1.0 column become ready, they will migrate left into a point patch column for release.
Be sure to check back every two weeks for the latest updates.

## Release View
## Alpha 4.9
The following cards have been set to Released.
- "Support the Miners" Mission Pack,
- Combat Clothing,
- New Hairstyles (4.9.0),
- Ordnance Cargo Holder,
- Behring "CQ7" Bullpup Rifle,
- Ship Combat UI Improvements,
- Juvenile and Adult Valakkar Behavior Updates,
- Ship Fly-By Audio,
- Grey's Market Basher: Implementing the Grey's Market Basher. Smash, strike, and strafe your way through the black in a light fighter built from your old favorite parts.
- Nine Tails Armor Update: Implementing updated armor sets for the Nine Tails criminal organization, refreshing the appearance of grunts, elites, and boss NPCs. Moved from 4.10 to 4.9
- Battaglia's Alliance Ships: Implementing three ships attainable through mission progression with Recco Battaglia in Levski, Nyx: the Drake Golem, MISC Prospector, and Argo MOLE, each featuring unique "Alliance" base liveries.
