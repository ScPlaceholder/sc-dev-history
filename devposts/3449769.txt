Programmers aren't interchangeable, and the programmers working on the various parts of icache and global persistence have a specific understanding of the game, engine, and backend systems that cannot be easily duplicated just by adding someone new to the team, even if they are a very good programmer. As an analogy, consider that it takes two to tango, and adding more dancers isn't exactly helping. As far as having them pulled, that is part of the normal day to day priority calls that have to get made, and yes on occasion some difficult decisions result in longer term efforts being briefly paused to get something with more immediate impact (like LTP) done for a release. Finally, we still have to make quarterly pushes, and so yes that means we still need people working on other parts of the game, not to mention S42.
