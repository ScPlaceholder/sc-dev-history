Star Citizen reads out all axes from head trackers (TrackIR and FOIP) but we only use the rotation axes. The reason why we don't have the 6DOF movement like in other sims is because our first person view is tied to an actually existing pilot / actor body. So when you move your head around with a headtracker, the actor itself moves the shoulders and by extension the head ... which is also networked. We could use the XYZ offsets to inform a pose offset that somewhat changes the head position of the actor but it's not a trivial problem to solve so that won't happen in the near future. But we will definitely try to not decouple the head from the body ... so that naturally resticts the movement already ... up / down e.g. is not easily possible in a sitting position.
That said, 3.12 will ship with a couple of head tracking updates. One thing we added is allowing head tracking in the external F4 camera ... this includes the XYZ axes.
