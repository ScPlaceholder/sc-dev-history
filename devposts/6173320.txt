The attitude limiter has been added for the same reason as the trichording limiter. The pure mathematical advantage from axis combination is too large for effective balancing and not all control devices (e.g. gamepads) can reach the corners. We did however increase the general rotation rate of the Gladius.
Edit: Also keep in mind that all ships will be retuned when MM is rolled out to the wider game. There is no need to think that certain ship classes will get weaker with this change.
