Get ready, DefenseCon returns bigger & better from May 14 through May 27.
When the UEE pulled Invictus back to Sol, DefenseCon didn’t wait for orders, Drake Interplanetary CEO Anden Arden has seized the moment to throw the galaxy's boldest arms expo in their place. Whether you're a seasoned pilot looking for your next weapon of choice or just someone who believes every citizen deserves the right ship, DefenseCon 2956 has one message for the galaxy: the Navy can keep its formalities, we'll keep the explosions.
Join the celebration at the Bevic Convention Center in Area18, where manufacturers will showcase their fleets and offer free test flights of some of the most iconic and formidable military ships in the ’verse: Throughout the event, you’ll have ongoing opportunities to pilot a diverse selection of cutting-edge spacecraft, all available in one place. Also, be sure to stop by the IO-North Tower as well, where you can take in the sight of the massive Anvil Odin battlecruiser, perfectly positioned for a breathtaking view over Area18.
But there’s more than just ships to fly, as DefenseCon comes with a full lineup of events and festivities. Everyone can take to the skies during the Free Fly event, an exclusive Referral Bonus rewards those who bring new recruits into the 'verse, and Twitch Drops opportunities will be enabled!
