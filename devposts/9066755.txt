## Features & Gameplay
Quantum Drive Balance
We have balanced the acceleration and top speeds of all quantum drives and added variation to cooldown times, spool up times, and interdiction effect times. This should reduce the average travel time by roughly ~22% with the intention for small Quantum drives to have high acceleration but slow top speed and larger quantum drives to have slower acceleration but higher top speeds.
