We're excited to bring you a new contest as part of the annual Coramor event! This year, we’re looking for your most creative comic strips that show us “How Not To Get A Date” in the 'verse.
Another year brings another chance to remember the ‘verse’s favorite star-crossed couple, Yuri and Corazon, separated by cruel circumstance over 500 years ago.
No fictional characters were maimed during the making of these comic strips. For educational purposes only. Emotional Damage not included.
## How to Participate
Whether it's awkward conversations with your fellow pilots or disastrous attempts at impressing a potential date, let your imagination run wild... but don't bring a Kopion to the table! We can’t wait to see how you depict the funniest (and possibly cringeworthy) dating blunders in Star Citizen.
So grab your pens and start sketching, or even take screenshots in-game, to craft your best story!
Please read the ⁠⁠⁠Rules Page carefully to avoid disqualification.
- :scroll: Prepare: Submit a single entry to the category throughout Coramor 2955.
- :art: Craft: You are free to create your comic strips as you see fit, whether made of screenshots or even your very own handcrafted art. Show us your creativity!
- :outbox_tray: Submit: Host your image(s) to the ⁠⁠Community Hub with the title "Coramor 2955" followed by your title, such as "Coramor 2955 - I took a love blow to the knee"
## Keep In Mind!
- Read the ⁠⁠⁠Rules Page.
- Multiple submissions are not allowed.
- Post-processing effects are allowed.
- Group submissions are not allowed.
- Post your image to the Community Hub. Replies to this thread will NOT count as a submission.
- Use 8 panels or less for your comic strips.
- As we only accept images up to 10Mb in the Community Hub, feel free to post your submission in multiple images: for example 4x4 panels on two images.
- Your assets must include the ‘⁠⁠⁠⁠Made By The Community’ logo.
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your submissions on the following:
- Quality
- Creativity
- Humor
- Rule Adherence
## Dates
The submission period ends on Monday, March 3, 2025, at 12 PM (Pacific) / Monday, March 3, 2025, at 8 PM (UTC)
