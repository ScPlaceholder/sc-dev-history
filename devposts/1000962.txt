All planets charted by the UEE have an official designation based on the name of the system/parent star and the planet's position around said star (i.e., Sol III). A lot of planets also have an official name (i.e., Earth). Not every planet has an official name. So, in the starmap, planets with a designation and a name are listed with the name first and the designation in parentheses. Planets that don't yet have a name are always referred to only by their designation.
For example:
- Earth (Sol III)
- Serling (Horus I)
- Horus II
...and so on.
R.ai'puāng is the Xi'an name for the planet. Tovaroh is an old name we used before the language was crafted, and will be retired. The UEE official designation for the planet is Kayfa II. As we update our documentation, it's likely that we'll change "Kayfa" in our established texts to "Kai'pua" in order to match the language standards of proper Xi'an. We've discussed establishing the word Kayfa as an "incorrect romanization" of the proper Xi'an word.
tl;dr: R.ai'puāng (Kai'pua II) is the answer you're looking for.
