The official Star Citizen keybinding draft for the Thrustmaster T.16000M FCM Space Sim Duo is now available. It takes just a few steps to import the profile to the game and hit the ground running with your Thrustmaster T.16000M dual sticks. You can download the XML profile file here https://media.robertsspaceindustries.com/cvk94jryjn4iy/source.xml .
We'd love you to try out the current draft and share your feedback. This will help us create the best possible final keybinding profile, which we plan to add to the list of default profiles in the Star Citizen game options.
