NPCs do not cheat, they will continue to fire at your last known location whilst you move into cover for a short duration after losing vision
If this piece of cover is penetrable (IE it's a thin surface or a flimsy surface material) and they have something like an LMG, that means you will take damage.
There are NPCs who will mag dump more in these situations, if you move into a piece of cover and stay still behind it, you will most likely take damage.
Positioning is an important element in combat and you can't play peekaboo with NPCs.
