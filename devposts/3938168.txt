Hello everyone!
Today, we are seeking feedback on a topic that’s a bit different than usual. Specifically, we want to hear your thoughts about the accessibility of the RSI web site.
As our community grows, we want the RSI site to grow as well, and we want the site to be accessible to its users. We would love to hear from you if you have any physical or neurological issues that affect the way that you use the RSI web site or the Internet in general. If you are blind, partially sighted, deaf, hard of hearing, colorblind, have pain or mobility issues, or use corrective lenses, we want your feedback.
Do you use text-to-speech applications? Or maybe you have colorblindness that limits the visual range of some areas of the site?  Even if you simply experience eyestrain from reading certain pages, this is welcomed information.
But you don’t have to fall into any category for your stories to be valuable. Even if you have family or friends that use accessibility services OR if you're simply an ally who wants to share, that would be amazing!
Respond below and let us know your thoughts.

Participation in this topic is of course voluntary. Applicable privacy laws require that we provide you with notice about the collection and use of your personal information. Accordingly, we note that the information which you may volunteer here is of a health care or medical nature and that we collect it here for the purpose of obtaining feedback that may potentially improve the accessibility measures of the RSI Services, and as otherwise indicated in our Privacy Policy.
