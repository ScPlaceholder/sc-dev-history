This is both a simple and also quite complex issue :slightly_smiling_face:
As mentioned above we do support separate wear and dirt controls per object, and these will change dynamically over time. Currently the degradation system handles wear, but it’s only hooked up to certain entities at the moment (due to art setup, see below). The dirt system has previously  been very basic but with Citizen Con we showed the very early stages of its successor, the “biome accumulation“ system which added frost to the players suit. This system will be extended in the new year to handle various types of dirt on different types of assets (vegetation, rocks, buildings, ships, items etc).
However, the more tricky issue is the art setup. Many assets were set up prior to the more recent wear and dirt systems being developed, and as such these assets will need some tweaks to unify the visuals. This will vary from minimal material changes on some assets to texture or mesh changes for others. This will be required to achieve the consistency we require across all assets.
On the canopies in particular, we have a quite complex glass shader, and this is often tricky to setup. However we’re looking to get agreement on an approved setup which we can then roll out across all ships.
With these systems there is always a degree of subjectivity, and art certainly love their scratches and aren’t too keen on the idea of pristine, but once we have consistency I think we’ll be OK as you’ll only see pristine ships for a short period of time. Obviously full persistence is going to factor into all this, because at the moment most ships are close to new, and no one wants to see a 100% pristine universe.
