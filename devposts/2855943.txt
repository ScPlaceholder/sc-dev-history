I'm not part of the support team, but I suspect that a support ticket is the right thing to do in this situation as they may be able to help out with a short term solution for the situation that you find yourself in.
However, since I am a DevOps engineer, I took a look into what happened with the account.
Bit of a preface: we know that your hangars are still on the account because they're listed in your orders on the website but the problem is that they're not appearing in game for you. The reason I wanted to mention that is because it appears during the course of the character reset flow, the hangars were not recreated in the game side database. Normally as part of the character reset process, the tool deletes things from the game side database ( leaving the website data untouched ) and then when the account logs back in, the items are recreated. Your account is in a bit of a weird state because it didn't recreate the hangar items on login even though the system knows you're supposed to have them.
I've put a bug in for this with all the backend service logs related to what happened so that this can be investigated as to what went wrong so we can look into what a long term fix for this might be like.
Now with regards to why the character had no clothes, that was a different bug. Turns out you managed to log in and join a game server before your character was fully created. Normally that's not possible, as there are safeguards in place to prevent this. However as the game develops and features are added ( such as a character reset tool ) it's possible for safeguards to miss some things. I've also put a bug in for the fact that this happened, but the good news is that your character finished being created shortly after you joined the game server so it has clothes again.
