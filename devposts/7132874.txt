As we continue to develop Star Citizen, there will be more security measures, such as a Crime status, penalties for people stealing cargo from a hauler loading their cargo, security response (whether it is system-driven or player-driven), and more defenses, but we don't want to overprotect the loading area, even in a green zone.
If you are coming to an outpost that is not a green zone, the EvR system will know it, and the rewards will be greater, for example, on Pyro. You will need to be on the lookout for incoming threats and understand where you are operating. As more players will be on the same servers, with Server Meshing, there will be more opportunities for lawful-protection and unlawful-piracy players. We're also working on features to better cooperate with players.
Thanks for sharing your feedback, we continuously work on improving the experience for everyone :+1:
