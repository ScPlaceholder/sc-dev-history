Rewind time as discussed in the recent Pillar Talk does not refer to rollback netcode.
What Chris and Sean were talking about is the replacement of a fixed delay in actor networking with a variable-length delay that automatically adjusts depending on the network latency and tick rate of the remote host. The end result is that the overall latency of remote players being rendered on your client can be much lower than before. This works best when the server runs at a high tick  rate and both you and the remote player have low pings. As a result, Star Marine is where you should see the biggest improvement in latency, due to its higher server tick rate. The PU servers currently aren't optimized enough for this technique to make much of a difference to latency there.
