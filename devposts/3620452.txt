The final four ships in our recent Ship Showdown event will receive the Best in Show liveries. They are the:
Anvil Carrack
Anvil Valkyrie
Aegis Eclipse
Drake Cutlass Black
If you already own one of these ships, you'll receive the IAE-exclusive paint (and relevant wearable) on the last day of the Expo at no cost. To be eligible to receive the paints, the ships need to be in your hangar - having them in your Buyback List won't be granting you the Best in Show Liveries.
Everyone pledging for a Best in Show 2950 ship during the IAE will receive the paint, too.
Thus, yes, you will receive the BIS paint for your Eclipse on the last day of the Expo.
