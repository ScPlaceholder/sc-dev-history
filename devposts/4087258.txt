June is Pride Month! :rainbow-flag:
We warmly invite each of you to celebrate Pride all month long by showing us your colors! There are literally no limits to your creativity for this contest. You can take a screenshot in-game, craft a card, bake a cake, make a video, the sky is the limit. Just make it as colorful as possible!
We'll take all submissions and organize them in our annual Pride gallery, hosted on our official website.
For this one, we won't have the usual 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place though! Instead, we're simply going to give away TEN Drake Cutlass Reds! We'll be on the lookout for submissions we think are especially awesome!
Multiple submissions are allowed, but no group submissions and you are only eligible to win one Cutlass. Your submission must be related to Star Citizen, but that is the only restriction. Any medium of creativity is allowed.
Post an image/video in the Spectrum submission thread or on Twitter/Instagram/Facebook (using hashtag #PrideMonth2951) by Friday, June 25th at 11:59pm Pacific for a chance to win one of ten:
- Drake Cutlass Red Standalone Ship with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance)
Be sure to check out the rules before submitting!
Good luck to all of you! We can't wait to see your submissions! Wishing everyone a happy #PrideMonth2951 from all of us here at Cloud Imperium Games! :rainbow-flag:
