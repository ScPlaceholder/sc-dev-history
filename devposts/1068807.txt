## FYI More Info as we research!
If you need to reclaim your ship with purchased weapons on it, try fully logging out from the game entirely. Reopen the game. Check if those items are back and then enter a claim. What happens if you try this?
Players reporting typically, the issue for lost UEC purchased weapons seems to be in PU (crashes, logouts, insurance claims, etc). Arena Commander seems to working as intended so far.
Account resets are not fixing this sorry! Reinstalling, clearing cache, clearing USER folder, etc won't solve this. It's on CIG/server/etc. side. We are working to find the issue in code/services to get this resolved for everyone. Providing as much info as possible is a massive help to determine what you are experiencing, what's affected, etc!
