To clarify the stats from each component work independently from each other, for example
2 shield gens one with 1000 HP and a delay for 10 seconds before it starts to charge 100HP per second and another with 100HP and 0 delay and recharge of 50 per second
You would have a total HP of 1100, after you take damage you would then instantly get 50 recharge then after a further 10 seconds of no damage the 100HP would kick in
From a meta standpoint will leave that up to you folks to discuss just wanted to clarify as there was a little confusing as to how it puts them together.
