Without getting into specifics about APIs, the answer to this is, unfortunately, no. While heaping dozens of discrete GPUs into a box can be great for supercomputing work or offline rendering, the bandwidth between them is pretty limited so it's hard to achieve anything that needs low latency. Copying even a few large images from one card to another every frame (and bearing in mind that has to compete for bandwidth with any resources being loaded) is heavy enough work that you can't rely on them arriving in time.
