## REVERSE THE VERSE: Ships of Alpha 3.2
Greetings Citizens. On this Friday's RTV (AT A SPECIAL TIME OF 10:30 PACIFIC) we're hosting John Crewe, the Vehicle Pipeline Director for Star Citizen and Squadron 42.  This will be your chance to ask questions about ships in the persistent universe.  Topics can range to whatever ships you're interested in, but we'll be giving preference to any question related to the Ships of Alpha 3.2.  These include:
- Aegis Avenger Rework (and variants)
- Vanduul Blade
- Anvil Hurricane
- Aegis Eclipse
- Origin 600i
Post your questions here, and we'll see you tomorrow at 10:30 Pacific on twitch.tv/starcitizen.
## POSTING GUIDELINES
- Only make replies to the OP.
- Only one question per post.
- KEEP IT SUCCINCT. No links. No paragraphs. No introductions. No thank yous. Distill it down to what you really want to say. This IS NOT the place for attempts to direct the course of development (that's literally the rest of Spectrum.) This IS your place to ask brief questions of our developers. Your responsibility in this process is to break it down to one or two sentences at most. Let's work together to save our developers some time.
- Consider the type of answer you're looking for. Is it possible for your question to have a 1-2 minute answer? Are you asking how an entire game system will function? Is it a topic better suited to the sort of deep-dive ATV segments that have visuals? If so, you will get votes, but you will not get the question asked. It just won't fit here. Recommend watching the pilot episode.
- Do not direct a question to a specific developer.
- Do not submit one question, gets votes with that question, and then replace the question with another.
- Do not make use of special formatting in an attempt to draw more attention, and therefore more votes, to your questions i.e. BOLD, H1, etc.
- No pictures. Make it easy for people to find the question they want to vote on without having to scroll through pictures.
- Post violating these rules and inline replies to other questions will be deleted.
- Have fun with it.
