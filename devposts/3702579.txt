Just to follow up on that and give a couple of more explanations:
We always wanted a single source of head movements. Having mouse look and head tracking controls active at the same time (especially since they added up) was just a bug really.
Manual controls for mining heads/lasers has nothing to do with it, its an entirely different topic. We had this in the past but the way how it was implemented was not future proof so we had to remove those input axes a year ago. Nowadays we can implement them in a somewhat nicer way and it is already tasked up to try it out. What it probably will end up with is something like an explicit manual gimbal submode. Atm we have "follow the ship rotation" (thats the VJoy in ships) and "follow the head direction". There is no reason why we couldn't have another submode mode for manual, but we'd want this to be explicitly set up by the ship avionics (aka simulated via a proper mode) than rather being just a consequence of certain configurations like it was in the past. Finding time for that is a different matter of course.
