Citizens,
Ruthless criminal factions are raiding low-orbit stations across the system, interrupting the supply chain and intercepting vast quantities of the critical resource Zeta-Prolanide!
It’s a grim scene, but that’s where you come in. We need you to step up to break the enemy blockade, take these stations back, and secure any stolen resources. Oh, and keep in mind that Zeta-Prolanide deteriorates at a rapid rate... Your skills are going to be put to the test, but we know you’re up for it. The system is counting on you.
From September 17 through September 30, players will get the chance to join the CDF in attempting to break outlaw blockades and liberate low-orbit stations across the system. Alternatively, if you're not too fond of the so-called authorities, you'll have a chance to pocket some credits through less-lawful means.
Blockade Runner is a server-wide event that combines space combat and cargo hauling. Enemy forces have attacked a station and set up a blockade, seizing crates of valuable Zeta-Prolanide. Players must team up to recover the stolen supplies and transport them past the blockade, all while battling enemy ships trying to stop them.
