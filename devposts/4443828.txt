Technically speaking you get one PIP for all gimbals and one PIP for all fixed guns. And yes, the PIP that is chosen for each of those categories is usually the one of the slowest weapon (though we can swap that over). In a mixed loadout (meaning weapons with low and high bullet velocities) the high velocity bullets will still fly towards the correct impact position as all weapons have a certain "convergence freedom" (you can see that in action if you target something, fly within lets 50 meters and then fire your guns next to it ... you will see the shots cross over each other).
However that only works up to a certain degree and was more emphasized with the clear projectile velocity separation in 3.14 (which was necessary to give different weapons a defined purpose).  In general you will get more consistent hits if you stick to a weapon loadout with less variance in the projectile velocity. You also have the option to power off weapons which you do not need at a given time. Their projected impact points will then be removed from the overall calculation of the PIP positions.
That said, we're not completely done with the gunnery system and will evaluate more options in the future.
