As the above advice says, try and darken your viewing conditions as much as possible, as that’ll help all of this. In a very dark room, ref-white of around 200 is easily enough. Brighter rooms can need a bit more.
If you enable HDR, then your peak white is reserved for the very brightest effects in the game (the sun, bright clouds, lights, etc)... so your paper-white will have to be darker, so you sacrifice your “average“ brightness.
The ideal HDR experience is achieved with 1000+ nit displays, so that you can have a bright ref-white but also plenty of headroom for highlights... but such displays are not cheap.
