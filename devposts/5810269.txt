It would be interesting to see what the AI would come up with for SC lore as well as the images it would conjure. On the Art side of things I feel like AI is a worse alternative to actual artists as the feedback you give them could provide wildly incorrect adjustments. I suppose you could have an AI generate a base image and then have an artist go over it, making adjustments but then what are you gaining that an actual artist isn't already providing? Think of it like a canvas. The artist can create artwork to specific parameters and make adjustments on the fly. AI is more like a paint can splashed onto a template. You get the rough idea but telling to make fine adjustments just doesn't work.
On the lore side of AI, it would interesting to see what it comes up with but ultimately it would have as strong an understanding as a Narrative writer.
Also I feel there's a moral side too. I imagine a lot of people feel more comfortable with a person being the brush/pen than an AI.
I believe we are working on in-game AI to be more adaptive and actually feel alive. To what extent and whether or not we use machine learning, I do not know. I would imagine a would have a similar system to our Planet/POI (Point of Interest) tech where it's procedurally assisted. It's given a base idea and then tweaked by a human to better fit the situation.
I think ultimately Art, Lore and in-game systems all provoke responses from us as humans. If something doesn't look or feel right, we notice it and that's why we have human input at every level, so we know it feels right.
Edit: I am no expert or authority on what, how and why we use stuff. This is all just my personal thoughts on the matter.
