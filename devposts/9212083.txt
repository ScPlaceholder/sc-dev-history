## Features & Updates
Gameplay Updates
- Journal entries now organized by player with priority mission events at the top
- mobiGlas Assets Manager and NikNax now sort alphabetically and only show counters above one item
- Multitool now ships with a tractor beam attachment by default
- High Risk bounty contracts now award 4,000 reputation, up from 2,000, placing them between Medium and Very High Risk
- Added new hair, eyebrow and facial hair options
Ship & Vehicle Updates
- Centaurus: Armor deflection dropped from 531 to 390 physical and 380 to 330 energy. Increased hull health from 25,740 to 30,740. Fuel tank capacity reduced from 46.8 to 32 SCU and thrusters given fuel burn rate value.
- Polaris: Armor deflection increased against both physical and energy damage
- S10 and S3 bomb racks added for the Starlifter
- Default power distribution adjusted globally so critical components are no longer switched off when powering
- Starlancer, Perseus, and Carrack radars upgraded to Size 3, with the Starlancer radar relocated to engineering and the Perseus relay moved to the console
Salvage Vehicle Buffer Increase
Salvage ship modifier balance pass. The lineup of salvage ships has had their internal buffer storages increased.
- Vulture: 13 SCU → 16 SCU | Largest Box: 1 SCU (+23.08%)
- Reclaimer: 240 SCU → 448 SCU | Largest Box: 16 SCU (+86.67%)
- Moth: 64 SCU → 240 SCU | Largest Box: 8 SCU (+275%)
- Fortune: 13 SCU → 14 SCU | Largest Box: 1 SCU (+7.69%)
- Salvation: 6 SCU → 8 SCU | Largest Box: 1 SCU (+33.33%)
