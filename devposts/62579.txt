I think the more common misconception is not that we can talk about things we don't know about, it's that we know all the answers to all the things all the time.
No other game has a thriving community at this point in game development.  They usually don't open the doors until much later when things have been figured out, but the reality of game development is that it's the process of figuring those things out, so many of the answers people are looking for or accustomed to getting simply don't exist yet.
