@Ethan_Page, I can only talk about the head tracking parts at this time ... I would love to support 6-DOF head tracking movement but it would require the actor IK to support that motion as well. Head tracking inputs do not act on the camera, they act on the actor's head / torso and thus are restricted to the same limits. Given that translational head movement is not necessary for gameplay purposes and because it is quite complex to add new poses to the actor system, we will likely not approach that any time soon.
Other sims support free head movement mainly because they do not really have the focus on the first person body simulation like we do. We simply do not want to detach the first person camera from the body. What I could do is experiment a bit with using really small translational offsets (so just a couple of cm) ... but I doubt that it will be really useful ... and it will likely cause some clipping into seat geometry or similar.
