Invictus Launch Week, also commonly known as Fleet Week, is the period between Emancipation Day and Armistice Day that marks the beginning of the new recruitment year for the United Empire of Earth Navy (UEEN).
This year, we gather on May 21st at 4pm UTC / 9am pacific to start the celebrations for Invictus Launch Week and honor those in the Navy and other armed forces for their tireless service.
Join us for Invictus Launch Week 2951 and get a first-hand glimpse of the Navy’s biggest and baddest destroyers, corvettes, and carriers as they make their way through the Stanton system in a fleet that's bigger and better than ever before. You can even tour an in-service Javelin destroyer; as soon as you step on board, you’ll feel the history of this iconic behemoth!
Alongside, we're kicking off a Free Fly event the very next day on May 22nd, which grants free access to download and play Star Citizen for everyone!
