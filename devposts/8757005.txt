4.7.1 introduces a new MISC Hull series ship to the game! The Hull B is the natural progression for haulers whose demand has outgrown their supply, or for larger operations that want to transport significant cargo from orbit to ground level. Built around the Hull series’ signature external cargo spindles, it forgoes close-quarter manual storage for open-access tractor-beam-assisted loading, making every job you take simpler and smarter.
