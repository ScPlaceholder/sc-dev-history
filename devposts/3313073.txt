To add what a fellow Houdini artist has posted above.
If you think about generating a sphere around Crusader, between the "surface" of Crusader and the cloud top, there would only be a few hundred/thousand voxels, but there is also going to be voxels in the centre of the sphere that is now in the centre of the planet.
To get the detail we would want for the surface of the planet, subdividing the voxels in houdini would start pushing file sizes up, that is if i can even generate some at those resolutions. Basically it's just inefficient vs to us introducing detail into the gas clouds in space.
