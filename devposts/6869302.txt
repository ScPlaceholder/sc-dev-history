Attention, Citizens!
The UEE Navy wants YOU! Invictus Launch Week has arrived in the Stanton system, and the Empire wants to find the very best of the best amongst the citizenry. Invictus Launch Week is an event held the last week of May that marks the beginning of the new recruiting year and the graduation of the latest class of officers and pilots of the United Empire of Earth Navy (UEEN). The vehicle manufacturers of the 'verse also use this as an opportunity to showcase their wares, with an annual ship showcase convention, this year on ArcCorp.
The UEE won't recruit just anyone off the street, so they're looking for proof that you've got what it takes to do your part to serve. We want you to create a 15-second video showing how you're doing your part to benefit the Empire. Whether you're a pilot, a solider, a member of an elite organization, a cargo hauler, or just generally handy, put together the most creative display of your talents for a chance to win:
:first_place_medal: 1st Place - Aegis Redeemer Standalone in-game vehicle with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance)
:second_place_medal: 2nd Place - Aegis Retaliator Bomber Standalone in-game vehicle with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance)
:third_place_medal: 3rd Place - Aegis Sabre Firebird Standalone in-game vehicles with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance)
You have until May 29, 2024, at 11:59 pm Pacific to make your submission.
## Notes:
- Please read the Contest Rules.
- Only posts submitted to the Community Hub will be considered.
- Posts must have "I'm Doing My Part" as its title to be eligible.
- Only one submission per participant. Group submissions are not allowed. A video may feature multiple players, but only the participant who submits the video is eligible to win a prize.
- The video must not exceed 15 seconds.
- Submissions must be at least 1920x1080 resolution and posted to the Community Hub.
- Multiple submissions are not permitted.
- Music/sound/effects should not infringe on any third-party copyright. This means you're able to use originally created/sourced music or effects, as well as official Star Citizen music/effects.
