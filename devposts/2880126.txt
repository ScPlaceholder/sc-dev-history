It's the Xi'an date system. Xi'an years and Earth years are different from one another (the Xi'an year is longer than the Earth year), and each civilization uses different timescales.
The Roman numerals in front of the dates are markers for the imperial age! I for the first imperial age, II for the second imperial age, and III for the third imperial age (the current one).
The 111 is a typo, ha.
Edit: the current year (2950) is III.1166, near the beginning of the year. When it becomes 2951, it'll still be III.1166, nearer to the end of the year but not all the way there yet.
