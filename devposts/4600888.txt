And when it comes to GPUs, it’s subjective what 100% utilisation even means, because there are many shader units running in parallel, some of which are nearly always idle, and many diffweren’t potential bottlenecks (memory read/write bandwidth, cache, ALU, rasterisation, render target blending, context state limitations etc). So a GPU  at “100%” can still potentially be pushed harder, especially with optimisations or changes to the underlying rendering algorithms.
