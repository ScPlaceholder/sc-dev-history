Hi everyone,
What do you do when you are really thankful? You share.
November is a time to be thankful, a time to remember and embrace those who enrich our lives, but also a time to look back at the year and the things we achieved. We can be thankful for a lot of things, and to celebrate Thanksgiving, we want to see your best Star Citizen in-game screenshots of the feature you are most thankful for that was released in 2018. If could be anything from a new ship, like the Hammerhead, to a new weapon, like the Gemini R97 Shotgun. You could be thankful for the debut of mining, but whatever it is you’re thankful for, make sure to read the rules before jumping in:
- Each screenshot is considered a single entry, but you are allowed multiple entries (You can include multiple images in a single tweet).
- Use the hashtags #Thanksgiving AND #StarCitizen
- Include a one-liner telling us which 2018 feature you are most thankful for.
- No post-processing effects allowed. This includes 3rd party add-ons like ReShade.
- Any other subject will be disqualified.
Read up on the full contest rules here HERE before capturing your screenshots for a chance to win one of the following rewards:
- 1st Place: Standalone Banu Defender - LTI
- 2nd Place: Standalone Khartu-Al - LTI
- 3rd Place: Standalone Nox – LTI
Contest ends: November 19th, 2018 11:59 PM Pacific, and we'll announce the winners on Thanksgiving day.
If you're digging this contest, I've got good news for you: we're also running a second Thanksgiving themed contest which you can learn more about HERE.
