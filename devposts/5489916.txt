With the recent release of the new Community Hub, we announced that we had big updates coming in the month ahead. To prepare, we've opened this new section of Spectrum which we'll utilize to update you each release with the latest new features, bug fixes, and known issues.
Today, we've released Community Hub 1.19.0, which introduces a few bug fixes and the Follower Modal feature. Read on for details, and keep an eye out for regular Community Hub releases in the weeks and months ahead.
## Patch Notes
New Features
- Follower Modals: You can now view and interact with your own and other users follower/following lists on the Community Hub profile. (See attached image)
Bugs Fixed
- First round of performance improves which should increase general performance related to load times
- Filter post count improvements: previously, when selecting multiple categories, the filters field in the query response displayed incorrectly. The post type and honors (MVP, etc.) counts are the sum of all posts containing at least one of the selected categories, instead of the posts containing all of them.
- Users can now uncheck filter boxes on the Discover page.
Known Issues
- We're currently working on organizing our backlog and plan to share a list of upcoming features and known issues in future publishes.
