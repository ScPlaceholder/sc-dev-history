The rewards you earned in the previous referral ladder are yours to keep, and we wanted to give an early heads-up to anyone who was pushing for their next tier.
Looking back at the old reward structure, many of the items now feel dated or less exciting. For instance, in the current ladder, tiers 1 and 3 offered vehicle weapons, and reaching 25 and 42 referrals unlocked vehicles primarily for Arena Commander. These were much more relevant when the Persistent Universe experience was more limited.
With the updated Referral Program, we’re introducing more meaningful rewards - several of which are ships or vehicles usable in the PU - alongside other significantly improved items.
We’re also increasing the frequency of rewards earlier in the ladder. Instead of only receiving something at tiers 1, 3, and 5, for example, we’re aiming to offer meaningful rewards at steps 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5 - so that each milestone feels more rewarding.
We're also looking into increasing the UEC bonus for new recruits. The current 5,000 UEC doesn’t stretch as far as it used to, and we believe a higher starting amount makes sense.
A lot of this initiative is being driven by our Community team who regularly receives feedback on the Referral Program, and wanted to advocate to spice things up with better rewards, more often. We super appreciate all the feedback - we’re monitoring the thread closely!
