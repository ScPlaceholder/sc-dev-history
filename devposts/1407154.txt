Lights, Camera, Action!
As shown countless times on the hub, the Star Citizen community has a wealth of talent and passion that can deliver amazing videos, highlighting the beauty of the ‘verse.
Today, we’re sending out a call to all producers, editors, and camera operators to create a commercial for the MISC Prospector.
For years, the Prospector has been the universe’s preferred mining vessel for solo operators. Featuring MISC’s sleek design sensibility and a bevy of upgraded high-tech mining tools, the 2947 Prospector perfectly balances form and functionality. We want you to capture this miner’s dream and give it the limelight it deserves.
You have until 11:59 PM PDT on September 9th, 2018 to submit your MISC Prospector commercial video for a chance to win one of the prizes listed below.
The winners will be chosen by our Community team and highlighted on our social channels.
Haven’t seen our take yet? Check out the official trailer, released at GamesCom 2947.
Ready to create? Read up on the rules, then start rolling and post your finished video in the Spectrum submission thread:
MISC Prospector Commercial Contest Submissions
The Community team has been planning a wide variety of activities for you all to participate in throughout the year and into next, all coupled with sweet prizes. So even if this contest isn't for you, we’ve got some exciting opportunities coming for you to leave your mark in the Star Citizen universe.
## Prizes
Three winners will be selected. If you work on the video with two friends (3 collaborators max), all will receive the reward.
Place: RSI Orion Game Package w/ LTI
Place: Anvil Hurricane Game Package w/ LTI
Place: Avenger Stalker Game Package w/ LTI
## Rules
Please read the full set of rules here: https://robertsspaceindustries.com/MISC-Prospector-Commercial-Contest
- Video must not exceed 2 minutes
- Submissions must be at least in 1920x1080 resolution and uploaded to YouTube
- No more than 3 collaborators on a single video
- Submissions should clearly state all individuals involved in creating their commercial video at the time of submission
- Multiple submissions are NOT permitted
- No unoriginal music (Star Citizen music is OK)
- All content must be original
- No license is granted for the use of any game assets, RSI Content, etc. other than the permission to create and submit entries for the Contest. No other purpose is permitted under these Rules.
- A video must not include or require reference to, or incorporate by reference, any other video, submission, Contest Entry, or other external content
Good luck everyone! We’re looking forward to seeing your MISC Prospector commercials!
