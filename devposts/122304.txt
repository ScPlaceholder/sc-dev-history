The control manager is how the control of things like weapons and ship components can be delegated to users, such as the various crew members of a multicrew ship, or the pilot of a manned turret. It also allows for AI modules to control the same behaviors by providing an abstraction layer that both the player and AI modules interact with. This feature is the silent hero for how item 2.0 multicrew ships will work. Also when it break we can assign our bugs to @mabent_cig
