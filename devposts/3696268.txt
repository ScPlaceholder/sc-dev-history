You should head to the controls tab and just invert the "strafe (or thrust) forward / backward" axis for that throttle (make sure you have the right selection in the lower right ... input0 is HOTAS 1, input1 is HOTAS 2, etc. That will save the inversion persistently. A lot of throttles report -1 when you push them forward. In the long term we can try to provide better defaults for specific devices.
