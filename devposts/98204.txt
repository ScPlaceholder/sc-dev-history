The new interaction system does not negate any of the shortcut keys available in the game - anything you can do with a key press now you will remain able to do. It's harder to say whether or not the additional keys will be assigned features made available by virtue of being able to arbitrarily add buttons as needed (and without further burden on an already saturated key map). I expect that any such configuration would require some player investment into the customization of their keybinds.
In general, the philosophy is that the interaction system is a unified and flexible interface for all actions, big and small; it services people who have hyper specific actions in mind, as well as people who haven't learned the shortcuts yet. I expect that players will mix their use of shortcuts and the interaction mode according to their particular needs at the time.
That's certainly been my experience with it. :blush:
