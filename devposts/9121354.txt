## Features & Updates
New Event: Orison Relief Support
In the wake of the Nine Tails attack on Orison, Crusader Industries is hosting a relief initiative to help rebuild the affected regions and upgrade their defenses. Players can take on a rotating slate of contracts through the mobiGlas and earn points toward a shared reward track that unlocks blueprints for new weapons and armor along the way.
Contract Types include:
- Construction Aid (Cornerstone Developments): gather and deliver construction resources to Orison or Seraphim Station
- Medical Aid Hauling (Covalex): collect medical supplies and iodine from New Babbage or Port Tressler and deliver them to Orison or Seraphim
- Medical Aid Delivery (Ling): collect medical supplies and iodine from New Babbage or its rooftops and deliver them to Orison or the Orison rooftops
- Crafted Aid (Cornerstone Developments): craft and deliver a batch of prototype thrusters
- Ship Ambush (Foxwell): intercept and destroy hostile ships threatening supply lines
- Ship Wave Attack (Foxwell): defend against waves of hostile ships in space combat
- Eliminate All (Foxwell): clear out remaining Nine Tails from targeted outposts in FPS combat
Completing contracts earns points toward a single shared reward track. Rewards are distributed as blueprints unlocked along the track rather than individual mission payouts, covering three new FPS weapons and three new armor pieces.
