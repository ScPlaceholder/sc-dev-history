So there are a couple of things to note with your image. The cauliflower appearance you mentioned only really tends to happen when there is atmosphere, so you would tend to see these on planets as clouds form and get thicker, clouds would erupt a bit and gain the cauliflower shape you're describing. Being in space, we don't get these, we get more whispy/flowey wibbly wobbly shredding and shearing kind of look. Atmospheric clouds is something we may look at in the future, i'm not sure as i don't handle that aspect as i imagine it would be similar to the cloud tech that Crusader will use.
But Gas clouds on the other hand, that's my gas (jam)(it's a pun, it was funny!). Resolution wise i would like to say so, everytime i look at what artists have come up with and the resolution and quality they're producing week after week is only getting better. So because you're reference is an image, it's like comparing apples to oranges. So think of the gas clouds like traditional geometry. The models in Star Citizen is pretty wild and we hope and plan for the gascloud quality to be on the same level. But getting that cauliflower appearance was something we managed to do when i started building the toolsets, it was funnily enough the first tool i made.
