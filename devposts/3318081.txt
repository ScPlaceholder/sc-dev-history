Other people have mentioned this, but to be clear I said 90 programmers, not 90 developers. Artists are developers, so are designers, the audio team, narrative, animation, etc.. etc.., essentially anyone who actually works directly on the game is a developer as opposed to something like Marketing or facilities that are essential but don't work directly on developing the game itself. We have many more than 90 developers and the company's developers are the majority of staff.
