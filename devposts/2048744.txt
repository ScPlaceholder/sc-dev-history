In the future our ideal scenario would be to have a 'soundsim' module as a ship item, or something similar. The idea is that these items would use various tech to reproduce the sound of other ships in space at high fidelity to aid in the pilot's perception of the objects around them during exploration and dogfighting. The idea is that this component can be powered down or damaged/worn-down/destroyed and start to malfunction or stop working entirely, which is when we'd transition to the more realistic sounds as you mentioned. I can't give any sort of timescale for this being implemented, but it is something we'd love to do eventually.
What you're hearing at the moment is roughly what we'd imagine the 'soundsim on' version to sound like :slightly_smiling_face:
I think it would also be extremely cool if 'soundsim' could be knocked offline only momentarily after taking a big hit, so you get a sudden dropout to realistic audio before the item sorts itself out and comes online again.
