There is no STRICT stereotype for it, but as a generalization, they are accustomed to Humans who speak their language to speak it with a heavy accent and get the propriety wrong all the time. I think it's safe to say that while impressed by completely fluent speakers who are non-Xi’an, they are also somewhat suspicious in that scenario. There are MANY Xi’an who have spent 100 years or more perfecting their UEE Standard and often without many chances to actually speak with the Human aliens, so they are often quite keen to have conversations with us in our mother tongue. They will really appreciate it if you know how to speak clearly and politely, though, and not be too direct.
