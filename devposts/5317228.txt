I can't really do much in that case, we don't have WarBRD bases here. What you can try is to make sure to remove all flight inputs (that means all flight strafing and flight rotation inputs) from the mouse, gamepad and the keyboard and make sure they are only set on the stick. Star Citizen is quite flakey when it comes to automatic switching between input devices as this is often treated per axis and even tiny inputs e.g. from a damaged gamepad stick can cause the swap which then looks like broken inputs.
