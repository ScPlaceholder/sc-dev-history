Greetings!
4.3.2 brings in the Frontier Fighters Finale, which is a limited time event where players complete missions for one of the two factions. The faction with the most contributed points acquired through completing missions will be declared the winner of this event. These missions will become available week by week, and will remain available to accept and complete after each new week has begun, with the following mission schedule:
Week 1: The Search Is On
Week 1 features two mission archetypes that can an be done by either faction.
- Eliminate Specific + Delivery
- Missing Person + Delivery
Week 2: Pressure
Week 2 features two mission archetypes that can be done by either faction.
- Ship Ambush
- Destroy Regen Locations
Week 3: Apprehend the Suspect
Week 3 features one mission. This mission can be done by either faction.
- Hunt the Polaris
Please visit the Frontier Fighters Finale spectrum post for further details. We look forward to hearing your thoughts, and thanks for taking the time!
