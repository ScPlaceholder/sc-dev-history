## Pyro is burning!
Amid the turmoil in Stanton, the Frontier Fighters have launched an independent campaign to retaliate against the citizens of Pyro. They believe that the only way to prevent future strife is through the complete destruction of Pyro's civilization.
Meanwhile, other factions, the Citizens for Prosperity and the Headhunters, are rising up against the Frontier Fighters and are determined to thwart their crusade. Will you fight for Pyro, or do you believe that Pyro is beyond saving?
The time has now come for you to decide; where does your allegiance lie?
Be warned: The choices you make matter, and the first faction-specific mission you complete will determine your path (and rewards) in the future.
Completing Fight for Pyro will reward you with an exclusive in-game Ripper SMG.
If you have previously completed Save Stanton (Chapter 1, all Phases) as well as Fight for Pyro (Chapter 2, Phases 1 & 2), you will also receive a set of faction-specific ship liveries for the:
- Anvil C8R Pisces
- Anvil Carrack
- Drake Cutter
- RSI Zeus Mk II
In order of appearance below: Citizens for Prosperity, Headhunters, Frontier Fighters.
To get started, accept a faction (Citizens for Prosperity/Headhunters/Frontier Fighters) Pyro priority contract in your mobiGlas when available. Choose wisely because completing the first mission of a faction locks you to it.
Missions will update as the story evolves, so stay alert and keep checking back.
This FAQ, the live game, and the main website will update as the story unfolds.
Once you've chosen your faction, there will be a faction-specific 'Journal Entry' (Reclaim Pyro/Protect Pyro/Purge Pyro) to track your overall progress throughout the event. There will also be narrative journal entries to touch on what's happening in each phase.

## FAQ
▲ What is this event all about?
► Fight for Pyro is the continuation of the Save Stanton mission series. Read the contracts and scavenge locations for hidden clues about why all of this is happening.
▲ Will I be able to join the mission series late and still participate?
► Yes! Similar to the Overdrive Initiative, earlier phases can still be played until the event concludes. Keep an eye on your Contracts Manager in the Priority tab to pick up each mission.
However, if you haven't already completed Save Stanton (Chapter 1) in Alpha 3.24.3, you won't be eligible to receive the bonus reward awarded for completing both chapters.
▲ I didn't manage to complete Chapter 1 before this. Can I still earn the bonus reward?
► No, as the bonus reward requires you to complete both chapters to be eligible. Chapter 1 is no longer available to play, but you can earn the Fight for Pyro (Chapter 2) reward.
▲ Will you run this event again in the future?
► No, this is a narrative experience and will only run once! However, the new mission archetypes created for this event can be used again in future releases.
▲ How long do I have to earn the rewards before the event ends?
► You have until the next patch to complete this chapter. We will be doing callouts to give everyone a heads-up as we approach the drop of the next patch.  UPDATE: We've made the decision to extend Fight for Pyro into Alpha 4.0.2, so you still have some time to earn the rewards!
▲ How will I know that my progress is complete before Phase One ends?
► You can track your progress in a Journal Entry that has faction-specific mission status (See image below for an example.)
If you have any further questions, let us know.
One way or another, this ends now. The fate of Pyro rests in your hands.
