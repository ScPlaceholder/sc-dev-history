As the Standard Earth Year draws to an end, it's almost time again to gather your friends and family and light the ceremonial lamps. Banu and Humans alike come together to celebrate Luminalia on December 22 but we are giving you the chance to spread some holiday cheer early with festive greeting cards, and win some shiny prizes while doing so!
## Welcome to the 2952 Luminalia Holiday Greeting Card Contest!
We'd like to see you capture the essence of Luminalia by designing a holiday greeting card that includes an in-game screenshot and a festive (and kind) greeting. Please avoid references to real-life religious holidays as we want to focus on Luminalia.
If you want to learn more about this beautiful holiday, you can visit the Luminalia Galactapedia entry for more information.
## Before You Begin
Before you start on your creations, please read the contest rules carefully and remember to only post your entries to the submission thread.
Any submission not posted to the 2952 Luminalia Holiday Greeting Card Contest Submission Thread will not be considered.
## Keep in Mind
- Read the contest rules!:book:
- Only post Luminalia-themed greeting cards that include an in-game screenshot and a festive (and kind) greeting. Any other subject will be disqualified.
- Post-processing effects ARE allowed, including third-party add-ons like ReShade.
- Please post your greeting card to the Spectrum submission thread only.
- Each image of a card is considered a single entry and you are allowed multiple entries. You can include multiple greeting cards in a single post.
The CIG jury will judge your entries based on:
- Quality
- Creativity
- Luminalia lore and rule adherence
## Dates
The submission period will end on January 3, 2023 (EOD 11:59 PM Pacific).
