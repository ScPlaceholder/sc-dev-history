You can get anything from your buy back queue at any time using cash. However, if you wish to use any amount of store credit, be that half, full, or even just 1 cent/penny, you will need to consume a Buy Back token.
A Buy Back token can be used with a combination of cash and store credit or store credit alone. An order using just cash will not consume a Buy Back token on the account.
Hope this helped! :slightly_smiling_face: Don't forget that you can also find the answers to some of your questions in our Pledge Buy Back Tool Knowledge Base article.
