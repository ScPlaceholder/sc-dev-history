In a mathematical sense A and B together are expressed as a vector of (0.9 / 0.9). If you want to calculate the length of a 2D vector you do not take the maximum value of its components. You instead add the squares of the components and then take the root of it. In this that means taking the root of 0.9 times 0.9 + 0.9 times 0.9. That will be 1.2727 and it would get clamped to 1 ... that means a and b will get clamped to 0.707 each.
