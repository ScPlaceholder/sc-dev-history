We did a huge balancing pass for the refueling items, that will hopefully make it into the next patch.
A short summary:
- Increased the max Flow speed for all Fuel Pod variances drastically --> no hour long refueling of bigger ships
- Increased the Safe Flow speed for all nozzles
- Reduced the damage the nozzle receives from high flow rates
- Increased the capacity for the "GSX-HP"
With these changes each combination of items should feel very different and have its own benefits.
