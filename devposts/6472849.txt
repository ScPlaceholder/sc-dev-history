Some changes that will come in the next build:
- we updated the UI to help understand the alignment field
- we updated the internal storage / buffer on the vulture to 13 SCU
- we updated the internal storage / buffer on the Reclaimer to 240 SCU
- we updated the values for Construction Materials you gather to fit the alignment field better
To give you a little insight in our decision for the internal storage sizes:
We need to account for the common big ships that you find and how big their biggest piece is. Currently there is no way to break them into smaller pieces.
That means that ships you will find more regularly (through missions) will allow you to pull in the biggest piece fully.
For the Vulture we are making it slightly bigger than the cargo again to allow for "mistakes" since the amount of gathered materials is hard to control.
Therefore we need to allow you to do some collecting of a little more than 1 SCU and not screwing up your cargo management.
And reminder:
The values are subject to change based on our observations.
