There is still a bug that freelook is technically possible when head tracking is active. That will get resolved next year. The basic setup for gimbal head control is this:
1) Go to manual gimbal mode (press G to cycle, onlypossible on ships with gimbals)
2) Now the gimbal crosshair might follow your rotation inputs from the vehicle. If that is the case press LALT-G to swap between "follow Vjoy/rotation" and "follow the look direction". There is currently no specific UI for it other than the crosshair behaviour.
