## Keep In Mind
- Read the contest rules! :book:
- Submit your most epic screenshot highlighting the featured IAE manufacturer for the day, either in the respective submission thread on Spectrum or as a reply to the challenge announcements on Twitter using the hashtag #IAEbyDrake.
- Each daily challenge will have specific requirements so please read carefully to ensure your submissions qualify. :exclamation:

- Make it count: only one (:one:) entry per challenge.
- No group submissions. You may include your friends in the screenshot, but only the one submitting is qualified to win.
- Players can only win once during the contest.
- Winners of the daily challenges will be announced on the day of the next challenge issued.
