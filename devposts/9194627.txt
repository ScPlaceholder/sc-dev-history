Earlier this year, during Ship Showdown 2956, we invited you to unleash your creativity and rally behind your favorite vehicles to help us crown the top four beloved champions in the 'verse.
From creative Lego builds and cardboard art, cinematic machinima, breathtaking fan art, and intricate real-life crafts (and so much more!), you amazed us with your talent and passion.
Thanks to your submissions and votes, four ships rose to glory, each earning exclusive rewards to celebrate their triumph:
- RSI Perseus
- Drake Ironclad Assault
- Origin M80
- Drake Corsair
Want to see what makes these fan favorites stand out? All four Best in Show ships will be free to fly from November 22 through 27!
If you have one of these fine ships in your hangar, you will receive their respective 2956 Best in Show paints at the end of Intergalactic Aerospace Expo (IAE) this year, for free.
Pledging for a Best in Show (BiS) ship during IAE also gets you their respective paint and items!
You will also receive a commemorative challenge coin for each of the Final Four ships you have in your hangar, along with the IAE 2956 Coin Display Case, designed to showcase your coins in style.
And that's not all! To celebrate the RSI Perseus claiming the ultimate victory, all players will receive an exclusive in-game RSI Perseus-themed bomber jacket, commemorating this year's champion!
All of these items will be distributed at the end of IAE, on Monday the 30th of November.
This process can take some time, so don't worry if you don't see your items in your hangar right away.
As a reminder:
- To be eligible to receive the paints, the ships need to be in your hangar (having them in your Buyback List won't grant you the Best in Show paints).
- Please note that you need to have the specific BIS ship in your hangar and NOT a variant to receive the Best in Show paint. In this case, to receive the Drake Ironclad Assault Best in Show paint, you must have the Ironclad Assault in your hangar. Having the Ironclad will not grant you the Best in Show paint.
- The Best in Show editions only contain the rewards within that ship's package and will not grant an additional copy of the rewards.
