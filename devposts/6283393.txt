It seems like there was a hiccup with the badges.
The "AC - Experimental Tester" badge was a WIP name for the "999th Test Squadron" badge!
To clarify, to unlock the 999th Test Squadron badge, you need to play each AC Experimental Modes released with Alpha LIVE 3.20.
Make sure to wait until the post-game screen, to have your match count towards the badge progression. You should receive a pop-up at the end of the game stating "GG, Thank you for playing this Experimental Mode! Play them all this patch to unlock a special badge!"
Note that you will be able to unlock the badge through 3.21 as it will feature the same Experimental Modes (with some twists!)
Tl;dr: The Experimental Modes you must play to completion for the 999th Test Squadron Badge:
Master Modes: Vanduul Swarm
Master Modes: Duel
Tank Royale
Team Tank Battle
Single Weapon Elimination
Mirror Match
Gun Rush
