Quick update!
We’ve heard your feedback and wanted to share an update on the upcoming changes to the Referral Ladder.
First off, we’re incredibly excited about what’s coming. The new Referral Ladder, launching the beginning of July, will feature better rewards, delivered more frequently - and we think you're really going to like them!
To address a key concern we've heard: progress on the old ladder. Good news! If you were active in the original Referral Ladder, you'll retain access to it and continue earning rewards - in addition to participating in the new ladder. Both ladders will run in parallel for the time being.
Eventually, we do plan to sunset the legacy ladder, but that won't happen this year. And when the time does come, your total all-time referral count will still be visible in the new leaderboard, when sorted with the All-Time filter.
We've updated the FAQ in the original post to reflect this update. Thanks again for all the feedback! o7
