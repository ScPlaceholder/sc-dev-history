Hi everyone,
Last year, we introduced the RSI Website bug reporting category in the Issue Council, starting with a soft launch for Evocati testers and then for Concierge members. Today, we’re excited to open it to all backers! You can now report website-related issues directly through the Issue Council.
It will use the same features that you are already familiar with if you've used the Issue Council before: Report issues, confirm problems reported by other users, and indicate if you are experiencing a similar issue.
When submitting a report, you’ll have the option to choose specific areas of the website, the browser and devices you're using, and the severity of the issue you encounter. Providing reproduction steps and supporting evidence, like screenshots or videos, will greatly assist our team in triaging and resolving problems efficiently.
Please use this category only for RSI Website-related issues.
If your issue isn’t website-related, please refer to the sections below:
- For general website feedback or suggestions, use the Spectrum Feedback section.
- For game or launcher bug reports, please select the corresponding bug-reporting categories in the Issue Council.
- For game-related feedback, use the dedicated Feedback section.
Your participation is invaluable, and we appreciate your help in making the RSI Website better for the entire community. Thank you for your support!
