We have introduced a new mission type: Salvage Contracts. Salvage Contracts aren't like typical missions, rather then completing a mission and getting a reward, players pay for information on a specific salvage scenario and the salvage obtained is the reward. The scenarios include:
- Basic contracts: Players are given exclusive rights to a single salvage ship, often in safer zones.
- Open contracts: A large cluster of ships, after a significant battle in deep space. These contracts stay open, so multiple players can take them and they are outside of comm array range.
- Illegal salvage: Single ship recently described, in comm array range, but hostile salvagers and security will show up after a time.
Give these a go and let us know what you think!
