Most turret velocities haven't been updated but this will happen in the future with the idea that smaller turrets carrying smaller loads are faster to move around. The S5 guns that the two main Redeemer turrets carry are very chunky (especially with regard to the ship size) ... the smaller remote turrets on the other hand are a lot faster. We will roll out similar changes to other ships as well (some of them already got pass, e.g. on the Javelin side turrets which are however not player facing). The Redeemer is just the first player-facing ship where we're trying it out.
Further in the future we also would like to try to have the turret velocities being influenced by the actual weight of the loaded weapons themselves but that is yet a while off. The reason why most other turret velocities currently sit at 95 degrees per second is because that is simply the pre-defined default value for a turret axis in our data.
Anyways, balance and combat development is an ongoing thing, so do not treat the values as final. I am expecting that we'll try out different turret velocities over some quarters and even more changes once we resolve the issues surrounding high speed combat.
