The way how keybinds and axis assignments work are a pretty big issue for the Vehicle teams. We already formulated a ton of improvements and suggestions what we would like to see as especially vehicles need very precise ways to customize axes, dead zones, inversions, shift keys, etc. The reason why this was not approached yet is because that bindings screen is still based on the old UI tech and adding new features with it takes a surprisingly large amount of work.
At some point the bindings screen will be converted to the new UI tech ... when this happens we'll take the opportunity to rework how bindings are customized and will also approach the topic of saving and loading profiles. Though this won't happen in the near future.
