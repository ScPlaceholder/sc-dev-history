A huge amount of work happens across 64 CPU cores (if you have that many), and many hundreds/thousands of man hours have gone into all this thus far. But the game is monstrously complex with a huge number of systems, so even a slight bit of work on the main thread from each system can end up making it the bottleneck. But this is always the top focus for any optimisation work, and has improved drastically over the past couple of years while we've also been piling in new features, and should continue to improve.
