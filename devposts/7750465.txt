- I'm not quite sure what answer you are looking for here... we pick which technical approaches to use based on the general direction of all hardware, but when specific hardware is better/worse at certain features players just have to adjust their graphics options to suit. So from our side all we need to do is to make sure we don't rely too heavily on any one feature that is not practically usable on our min-spec cards (which will change over time). So for now this means no RT as a base-line, in the future it might mean some limited RT as a base line, but it'll be quite a while before developers can rely on heavy RT loads on their game's min spec cards.
