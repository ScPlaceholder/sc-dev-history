Good question. It's an interesting idea, however from my experience ChatGPT has given very blanket advice on development issues. I've used it before on a couple of my personal project just to see what it would come up with and it's given me some pretty solid advice. However the software I was using is pretty well known and widely used which means ChatGPT can pull from existing threads and sources to compile a solid response of possible solutions. Since a lot of our Tech and Software here at CIG is in-house it means the AI can't pull from existing sources and issues as we don't publish them outside of the company. I can imagine it would give some good general advice on what could be the cause but considering (as mentioned before) our tech is in-house a lot of the issues could just be down to how we handle stuff internally. You can however feed ChatGPT information on the particular issue in order for it to give a stronger answer, however this would be a breach of our NDA as ChatGPT uses information it's received to better itself, thus allowing anyone to access that information by simply asking ChatGPT the 'right' questions. We also have a particular way of doing things that doesn't necessitate the use of an AI as it might cause more issues than it's worth. We also have a huge amount of talented Developers, artists and designers that all work closely and tirelessly together to fix issues as they come through.
I am by no means an expert or authority on this topic but these are just my personal thoughts.
