We don't have a standardized idea of the number of people we expect to have for the different scale of future fights currently
Basically, we're not saying that all apex creatures in the future will be 4 players,  the sand valakkar apex At the time of that recording was 8 players for example but those numbers always change with play testing
It's healthly content wise to have different player caps for different content as the more people you involve the more difficult it can be to organize that many people, so having a healthy mix is key
In general as we start smaller, figure those things out and build up; we get more robust and we get better at building these boss fight style content.
Then we can start including more elements of the PU such as ground vehicles, ships and more players because in general with Star Citizen, you can't really cheat things like ammo and that effects the boss fight, what ships / weapons you bring and other fun things like dropping an A2 or even bringing an Idris.
So there is a lot to consider!
Once you put all those elements into the mix it just becomes a lot of work at the base level so it's good for us to build up to that because having huge space battles is obviously a really fun fantasy for the PU.
We want to include more Star Citizen elements as well which make boss fights super fun and different in the PU with how physicalized everything is (Tractor beaming things in fights, Salvaging, loads of different ships).
There's not many games where you can have things like landing on a creature mid fight, fighting on top of that creature and taking off but SCs scale is just like no other.
If you think to how we handled creatures we started with the Kopion and Marok which are relatively simple design wise and it allows us to build a solid idea of the best way to handle all the annoying bits about a big networked game like SC (despawning, spawning, how we handle loot and such).
Key take away is we're not defining that all future creature content 'is for 4 players' and we want to keep in line with that amazing sense of scale SC is known for
