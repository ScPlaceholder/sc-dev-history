it's balanced relative to the other DMRs A03 / S71.
The closest comparison is the S71
S71 has a pretty big clip for it's STK and a very nice recoil pattern.
P8-AR is really flexible with it's low ADS time (Relative to damage profile), decent first shot spread and consistent but challenging recoil pattern as well as a fast reload.
It's got a really high damage profile compared to the S71 so with all the other advantages the sacrifice for that is a smaller clip
It also has damage fall off a lot earlier then the S71 but has a lot higher minimum damage at max distances
So overall the P8-AR is a lot more consistent and more reliable (as a behring weapon typically is) compared to the S71 which while a good recoil profile has more prevalent horizontal recoil which is the true killer at ranges vs the straight vertical profile of the P8-AR.
The biggest take away though is that the recoil basically freely resets with the S71 where it does not with the P8-AR meaning if you prefer more 'spam tap' DMRs then the S71 is for you but if you are able to control substantial recoil and track the P8-AR is the preferred choice
