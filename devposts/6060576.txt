Happy (early) Foundation Festival! We love all the community-made video guides you make, on subjects ranging from the most elite dogfighting to where to find a double dog.
In the spirit of welcoming new players to the 'verse throughout July, we invite you to help give them a head start. We'd like you to come up with your best tutorial, five minutes or less, on a particular Star Citizen feature or piece of content from the Stanton system. What you choose is up to you, but we'd like to reward the most helpful tutors with some exclusive goodies!
Create your best video tutorial for Foundation Festival by July 10, 2023 at 11:59 PM Pacific for a chance to take home one of these prestigious prizes:
:first_place_medal: Crusader Mercury Star Runner in-game vehicle with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance) and Foundation Festival Paint
:second_place_medal: Drake Cutlass Black in-game vehicle with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance) and Foundation Festival Paint
:third_place_medal: Aegis Gladius in-game vehicle with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance) and Foundation Festival Paint
## NOTES:
- Please follow the Contest Rules.
- The video must not exceed 5 minutes in length.
- Submissions must be in at least 1920x1080 resolution, with either a 16:9 or 21:9 aspect ratio, and uploaded to YouTube.
- Submissions must be posted to the Community Hub with Foundation Festival 2023 in the title alongside the name of the tutorial, as well as tagged as "Tutorial".
- Submissions must be a tutorial guiding a new player through Star Citizen features/content.
- Submissions will be judged based on their quality, educational, and production value.
- Group submissions are allowed.
- Submissions are limited to one per participant.
- Prizes will be awarded to up to three total participants (up to two additional participants to be named in the post description).
- No unoriginal music (Star Citizen, royalty-free, and original music is OK).
Happy Foundation Festival, everyone, and see you in the 'verse!
