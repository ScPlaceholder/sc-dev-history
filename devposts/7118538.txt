Being able to purchase equipment/vehicles for a full season (currently defined by major patches) is the direction we want to go down. At the moment we're limited by the current flash implementation and rental service. However, in the meantime, last patch we restored the "Extend Rental" button so you can extend the rental period of things bought with REC so you can avoid losing access to them.
As I've said in other threads: we're not happy with the current REC system and have made these and more changes to alleviate the pain while wait for the time to do a proper rework. The intent, for now, is for REC to be easily acquirable (see the increased payouts and game-mode multipliers) and equipment/vehicles to be relatively cheap, so that it is not something blocking players from playing the game without fully removing it. Amongst more technical reasons for keeping it, REC is important factor for the future of the game, as a great tool for progression and method of acquiring rewards; so hope you understand why we havent "simply" removed it :slightly_smiling_face:
