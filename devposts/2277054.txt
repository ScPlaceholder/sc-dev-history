They unfortunately can't be made invisible - if the surface were a little rougher, you'd see that highlight start to smudge, and on rougher and curved surfaces it's what gives an object a lot of its realism. So they're unpleasant to look at, but they're just the unpleasant end of a necessary equation.
For things like fire and explosions, where you need a light but really it should be emitting from a diffuse area, I did add a light type called "soft omni", which gives a fuzzy-edged specular highlight. It's not as notiecable as these, but the downside to it is that it's a lot less mathematically sound -  if a scene gets a lot of its light from them it starts to look like all the objects are rougher than they really are, so they can only be used sparingly. In a case like this, we generally just have to push it back to the art/lighting teams to see if they can avoid the problem setup.
