We have updated hover mode with some new features and are ready to open up for more feedback.  Changes have come in over several patches and include allowing the Prospector to tilt downward up to 15 degrees without moving, removing low altitude turbulence to stabilize, bringing strafe forward/backward and right/left to full strength, and significantly reducing the auto-level.
Use hover mode for along surface traversal, but be sure to test speed transitioning, altitude transitioning, and manual landing in various scenarios. Let us know below what we still need to work on and dial in along with any difficulties you have with the system.
