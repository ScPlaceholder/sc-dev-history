It's a nice idea, but unfortunately not really feasible. We use several shortcuts to make sphere lights viable, that assume things like that any part of the sphere can contribute equally to a specular highlight, so it doesn't matter which one you select. To account for some parts being covered up, you'd need to make the model a lot more complex.
There's a possibility that you could handle complex light sources by treating them like the image lights presented here - (https://eheitzresearch.wordpress.com/415-2/ and https://youtu.be/ZLRgEN7AQgM?t=155), but it would mean including a special codepath just for the planet lights, which is something we generally try to avoid.
