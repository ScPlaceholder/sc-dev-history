" Question 1: Would you say that this stage that most of the work (foundation) is complete from your end barring tweaking and future refinements / bug fixes and that a lot of whats left is down to designer tweaking individual flight characteristics. e.g. as described in your ship tuning section in your document.  "
I think that could be the case.  This is the third major iteration of IFCS, and each time, I've needed to modify its functionality to support the growing set of requirements.  As long as those requirements continue to grow, the system will need to continue to evolve.  But if we've reached a point where we have a good understanding of what's required of the system, then it's more a matter of using it to its full potential.  Where are we today?  I don't know.  But I expect IFCS will continue to evolve.  How much, I can't say.
" Over simplification but would you say basic mechanics are in place and you have provided the tools to the wider team to tune the various aspects of flight and aiming ? "
I would like to think that the basic mechanics of flight are set and won't need to change.  With the exception of things I've indicated, like adding lift to aerodynamic sim.
" Question 2: With all the amazing work you and your team have done here and described in your document, when are we likely to see a concerted effort from wider the CIG teamd to make flight fun again ? "
There is an effort to build an IFCS team and get that team working better together toward the overall vision of the system, which, btw, is CR's vision.  So how much of the current system survives into the future largely depends on how much CR feels that it is doing what it needs to do.
