Hello everyone!
As you may know, CitizenCon 2951 will be held digitally on October 9, 2021, but that doesn't mean you can't still be a part of this big show.
One aspect of CitizenCon that we love dearly is being able to highlight the community with Community Booths! Now, this is a bit harder when the show goes digital, but we think we have a fun solution. We're giving you the opportunity to spotlight your org, reveal an awesome project you've been working on, create an in-fiction advertisement, or anything else (in-fiction) creative that you can think of! Get ready to be apart of CitizenCon 2951!
Welcome to the CitizenCon 2951 Video Contest!
Before you start creating, please read the contest rules carefully and only submit your entries to videosubmission@cloudimperiumgames.com.
Any submissions not emailed to the 'videosubmission@cloudimperiumgames.com' will not be accepted.
So what is this all about, you might ask? Well...
We're asking you for well-produced and entertaining in-fiction videos to play between dev panels at CitizenCon 2951. So, send us your creations for the opportunity to reach and entertain the entire Star Citizen community in one fell swoop. Your in-fiction video may promote your player organization, an NPC group, an in-game product, your community-driven event, or whatever else you can think of that would be a fun in-fiction addition to the show. The commercials can be serious or funny. You can even send in comedy skits or short machinimas; whatever you like as long as you're bringing the Community Booth spirit to CitizenCon 2951. Just bear in mind there are some important criteria to consider, so read the rules carefully.
## Please keep in mind
- Read the contest rules
- Video must be submitted to videosubmission@cloudimperiumgames.com by sharing an accessible YouTube link (we recommend unlisted)
- Post-processing effects allowed. This includes third-party addons like ReShade.
- Video must not exceed 45 seconds
- Submissions must be in at least 1920x1080 resolution and uploaded to YouTube
- Group submissions allowed
- The number of submissions is limited to one per participant
- There is no limit to the number of people who may work on the video
- A maximum of three account handles may be listed with the submission (submitter +2) to be considered for a potential prize
- No unoriginal music (Star Citizen music is OK and recommended, as is any music that does not infringe copyright, such as originally composed)
## Preferred Tech Specs
VIDEO
Format: .MP4
Codec: H.264
Aspect Ratio: 16:9
Frame Rate: 30fps (minimum)
Pixel Dimensions:
- 3840 x 2160 (4k UHD)
- 1920 x 1080 (minimum)
AUDIO
Stereo, 48kHz, 24bit (16bit minimum)
## The CIG jury will judge your videos on
- Quality
- Lore adherence
- Entertainment level
## Dates
The submission period ends on September 19, 2021 (EOD 11:59 PM Pacific)
## Rewards
- We will not sort winning videos by the first winner, second winner, etc. but select the appropriate amount of videos for CitizenCon 2951
- Those who submit videos via email may credit their own handle and those of a maximum of two other people who worked on the video
- A maximum of three people will each receive a Tumbril Nova Tank with LTI if their video is selected for the CitizenCon 2951 stream
- Fame and glory will be yours!
Good luck everyone and we are all looking forward to your video!
