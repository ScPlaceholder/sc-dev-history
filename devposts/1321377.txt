The rules are in place to maintain a healthy and pleasant environment for everyone to use, and such an environment can easily be maintained with the application of common courtesy and common sense.
Contrary to popular belief, moderation are not out to censor or suppress negative opinion. We are however obliged to keep feedback in a format that is both useful and respectful not only to the community, but to CIG developers as well.
More often than not, this is not the format we encounter, so it has to be removed, with the poster advised to present it in the aforementioned and more acceptable format.
Incidentally, all moderator actions are reviewed and checked daily, and if anyone has concerns about moderation, or how a scenario was addressed, you are welcome to send in a support ticket where it will be reviewed. Debating and discussing it on the forums however, is not the right channel to use. ( See Rule 7 )
Moderation is here to curate and help the community as well as maintain a standard everyone can enjoy.
Thank you.
