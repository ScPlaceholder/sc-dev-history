Game dev is a bit funny like that because from our end we go
"These are the archetypes of all of these armour sets"
and we make these nice balanced rounded out choices, get everyone on board, everyone is happy.
We then have the combat helmets have their functionality turned on, fab!
But from your end you just see these helmets that look like combat helmets at go 'why aren't they combat helmets!'
They make sense in our planning and future context but without that full context it can seem weird
But we'd rather get these more limited archetypes out, get feedback and adjust as opposed to waiting before it's too late to tweak
It's really easy to define what is which archetype on the dataside so we see your feedback and go 'maybe we should make some more combat that for now since that makes sense in the present context'
It's not so much unpreparedness but being flexible and willing to adapt to feedback for the present context
But that's the nice thing about working on SC is we can get things to you early, adjust course depending on what people say
But it's a double edged sword since you need to have a lot of future planning and context that you need to playtest / prove out
