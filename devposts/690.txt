Hello everyone!

As you may have already noticed, we have reorganized the way we communicate on Spectrum. It was always our goal with Spectrum to make the forums better and easier for citizens to interact with each other. Much like Reddit, having a central location where players can post their questions, ideas, feedback, and general chatter ensures a close, and collaborative community. We understand some players are worried their post can potentially get buried or gone unread. This is where Tags comes into play.

Tags have been around forum and message board software for years. Tags allows you to classify your thread as a specific topic or subject so it can be easily filtered by other players looking for the same type of topic. We've essentially removed subject-specific categories from the forums and moved them into their own tags. 

When you create a new thread, you have the option to assign it one tag that is closely related to the subject you are posting about.

Not to fear! We will still keep the old forums active for some time as we transition into Spectrum. Eventually, we will archive the forums and you will only be able to read, but not post. We haven't determined when that would be, and we will keep you up to date with that.
This is a big change, and we are look for feedback on this new style. Do you like it? Do you dislike it? If you have suggestions for more tags, or a new section let us know by replying to this thread.
