Duo Showdown -> is this Duel starting 21 Jan?
Duo Showdown and Duel are two different modes. Duo Showdown is 2v2 dogfighting, whereas Duel is 1v1 dogfighting and that will be activated on January 21.
Kill Collector (FPS) - What is this?
Kill Collector (Vehicles) - What is this?
Kill Collector is a fast-paced 12-player elimination mode, where participants collect trophies from downed opponents to earn points and take home the win.
You can read more about these different Experimental Modes on the comm-link if you click through the Experimental Modes slideshow.
