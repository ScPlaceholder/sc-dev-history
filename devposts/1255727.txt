Please reply with non-bug related feedback on the following: Ship Power Tuning
- Power during light use (normal flying, firing only a few weapons, light shield usage)
- Power during heavy use (boost/AB flying, firing all weapons, heavy shield usage)
- Power triangle usage
Expectation: The intention of the system is to allow ships to operate correctly under normal circumstances with no effect on systems.  However, under heavy operation you may notice your systems starved for power and be forced to make some decisions to compensate.For example, if you have upgraded and filled every weapon slot with energy weapons, are firing all at once, maneuvering with boost and afterburner constantly on, and shields taking damage you will notice your weapons slowing down, maneuverability cut down, and shields depleting on their own.
The response would be to use your systems more strategically, such as firing weapons separately or using boost/AB more sparingly and/or using the power priority triangle to keep some systems operating normally in extreme situations, at the cost of others.
