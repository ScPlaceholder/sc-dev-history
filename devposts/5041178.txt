Almost all games run one (or sometimes more) updates to the world, followed by rendering the updates world, and repeat.
Some engines however have decoupled the updates from rendering, but to achieve this you need to either predict where objects will be in the future (near impossible) or introduce a significant latency and interpolate (blend) positions between the previous two updates. The latency involved to do this can make games feel sluggish, unless you can update at a high frame rate, but then there would be real gain in decoupling updates and rendering, hence this not really being a mainstream approach.
