Star Citizen has a propagation system in place ... that means each sound playing knows whether or not it is actually audible. As such a pilot inside a space ship does not really hear stuff that's happening outside (especially in a vacuum). Our approach here is that the ship computer / sensor system / helmet synthesizes these noises (via "SoundSim") to make them audible for you. And that also includes the weapon fire from your own ship ... which explains why you would hear it almost unfiltered.
That being said we are not entirely clear yet on how and if synthesized SoundSim audio should be treated. But maybe a sound designer can say more about that.
