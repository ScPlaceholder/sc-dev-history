Hey everyone,
We want to address some of the feedback we've received regarding the UI that we have recently presented during our episodes of Inside Star Citizen. While the holographic aesthetic is intentional, readability is paramount. Rest assured, we're hard at work refining the interfaces to ensure better readability as we approach the 3.23 release.
Additionally, we're actively working on improving the notification system. We recognize the importance of prioritizing essential information and ensuring that non-priority notifications don't disrupt your gameplay by appearing front and center on your screen.
Your feedback is invaluable to us, and we're committed to continually tweaking and enhancing your experience both before and after the upcoming Update 3.23. Thank you for your input, and please keep it coming!
