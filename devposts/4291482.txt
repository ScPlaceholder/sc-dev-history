In 3.14 the distortion weapons are not working correctly, especially when you employ them against larger ships. That is based on a bug in the application of explosive damage on the affected sub parts of a ship (distortion impacts are always "explosive" / splashy to figure out which sub parts are hit). During the iteration of hit sub parts the code had a tendency to prematurely early out so large amounts explosive damage were simply ignored. We fixed that a couple of days before 3.14 went live but opted to not include it yet due to the danger of the now heavier explosive damage offsetting the entire combat balance. So the fix for the inconsistent distortion damage application will be shipped with 3.15.
Other than that I can't really talk much about the direction of distortion guns in general. Vehicle weapon development and gameplay is not yet done and I am quite sure we'll change a few more things going forward.
