## Welcome to the '2953 MISC Hull C Advertisement Contest'!
If the containers being hauled by the MISC Hull C could display advertisements of products, what would they look like? In this contest, we're challenging you to create advertisements that you might see on the sides of these containers. They can be of any product that already exists in the 'verse except for vehicles not made by MISC or its sub-brand, Mirai. Don't be afreight (ha!) to take risks and think outside the box, but please read the instructions below carefully and use one of the templates provided! Submit your creations to the Community Hub, Twitter, or both for a chance to win a cool hauler.
Disclaimer: This contest is just for ships and giggles, and the creations generated from it will NOT be making their way into the game!
.
## Before You Begin:
Before you start on your submissions, please read the contest rules carefully and remember to post your entries to the Community Hub or as reply to the contest announcement post on Twitter and not as a reply to this thread.
:exclamation: Any submission not posted to the official Twitter post or Community Hub (must include '2953 MISC Hull C Advertisement Contest' in the post title) will NOT be considered. Submissions must also use one of the templates provided to be eligible. :exclamation:
## Keep in Mind:
- Read the contest rules! :book:
- Only an advertisement of either a MISC or Mirai vehicle (not vehicles from other ship manufacturers) or other in-game products (such as CRUZ Lux, Double Dog, Whammers, ship weapons or components, etc) that already exist in the game will be considered. Any other subject will be disqualified.
- The advertisement should be an original creation and should not be a reproduction of an existing Star Citizen ad.
- An advertisement should have a slogan or descriptive text together with the images of the product.
- Submissions must use one of the templates provided and only digital or computer-generated ads will be accepted.
- Please post submissions to the Community Hub or to the contest announcement post on Twitter (don't forget to include #StarCitizen in your post). Replies to this thread will NOT count as a submission.
- Multiple submissions per player are allowed. You can upload them in one post to the Community Hub.
- Use '2953 MISC Hull C Advertisement Contest' as the title for your submission on the Community Hub.
- Group submissions are not allowed.
- Post-processing and use of graphic-design software (such as Photoshop) is allowed.
- You are limited to winning one prize, so you cannot win on multiple platforms (Community Hub, Twitter).
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your entries based on:
- Quality
- Creativity
- Rule adherence
