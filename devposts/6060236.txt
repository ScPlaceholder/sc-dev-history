Is your organization making the ’verse a better place? Do you lift each other up in times of need, making the Star Citizen community one of the best? Then this is your opportunity to place your organization in the limelight during Foundation Festival 2953, starting in July!
In the ’verse, Foundation Festival is a month-long civic program that strengthens the foundation of the community by encouraging Citizens and civilians to volunteer with local and imperial organizations. Here on Earth, we’re celebrating our incredibly welcoming Star Citizen community all month long! You can look forward to special Guide System rewards, FREE event-exclusive ship paints, and more.
Starting today, you have until June 30 to send an email with your org info to: communityteam@cloudimperiumgames.com
We'll then pick several organizations to feature during the Foundation Festival celebrations in July.
