My latest information is:
- FSR3 doesn't support DX11, and so won't be enabled until Vulkan is the default renderer.
- XeSS doesn't support Vulkan, so until that changes it's not an option
- DLSS4 is a relatively easy update, so will happen (but see above for all the reasons why I can't say exactly when you'll get it)
- FrameGen is not something we're actively looking at yet because we are prioritising getting the base level of performance up first (general recommendation is to use such tech to go from 60 to higher FPS). Also see the general sentiment of players hating temporal systems which introduce smearing/halos/hallucination etc.
