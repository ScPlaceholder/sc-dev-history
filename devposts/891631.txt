Edit: Looks like the cause had to do with saving loadouts while the UI was still updating. Please make sure to wait a few seconds before saving any loadout changes to avoid this.
Investigating issues like this tends to involve a lot of teamwork between Player Relations, DevOps, and QA. Player Relations tends to be the first people to notice these kinds of things because they have the most direct relationship with our players so they'll let DevOps know something is going on so that we can check the database and investigate things.
In this case, we also asked for some help from the Evocati in finding reproduction steps for this issue because while we could see it was happening, we weren't seeing any indications as to what the steps were.
Currently, the Evocati have helped us to find some reproduction steps for the issue that were passed on to QA and our QA team was able to reproduce the issue. We also investigated a couple different scenarios and found that it's much more likely to occur on busy game servers which is why it wasn't caught earlier.
Now that we know what the steps to get the bug are, the next thing we need to do for this will be to have our game play programmers look at why things are going wrong so that we can find out how we want to approach fixing it.
