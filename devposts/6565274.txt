Hello Asumay :slightly_smiling_face:
Firstly, a big thanks for sharing your feedback and ideas regarding the current gameplay state of hoverbikes in the PU.
As you pointed out, finding solutions to some of the mentioned issues is no easy task, and we consistently track your feedback to better understand how we can provide a smoother gameplay experience.
Before delving into the details below, please keep in mind that issues may be tied to the tuning of the system, the system implementation itself, or both, and sometimes even related to other gameplay systems and reasons. Addressing these complexities can be challenging, especially when code-support is needed.
As a general rule, pursuing a "band-aid" solution is not advisable (I genuinely liked your definition for those kinds of fixes btw :smile: ), as it may not effectively solve the actual gameplay problem and could generate undesired dynamics elsewhere.
Therefore, increasing the health pool disproportionally to other vehicles is not recommended, particularly in correlation with the high physics impulses involved in the collisions you likely referred to.
Secondly, thanks once again for providing clear examples of gameplay dynamics you find important (Paint ftw :smile: ).
You'd be glad to know that we're in the process of cooking up improved tuning and small fixes for all bikes in-game.
These updates aim to address some of the described issues, if not completely, at least to minimize them as much as possible. So, anticipate some tuning updates for all the bikes in future patches :wink:
