Pretty much all issues that SC players are salty about are somewhere in our backlogs, we have a whole team dedicated to collecting player feedback and turning that eventually into work tickets. To "conveniently forget" anything is not part of that process. Resolving work tickets is really just a matter of time and depends heavily on tasking priorities (e.g. fix explosion code issues first before tuning explosion data, otherwise we waste work time). So we certainly won't bury the Ares and we certainly did not forget the Sabre either. Specifically on the latter there are just some other things to get out of the way first, namely the item signal tuning and resolving the high speed problem.
