In any given scene, you'll either be CPU or GPU limited (or, I guess, with VSync you could just be waiting on refreshes). So in the void of space, the CPU isn't doing a lot of work, and the GPU is pumping out frames as fast as it can. In the landing zones, we've got a lot of stuff eating up time on the rendering thread on the CPU, which means that the GPU ends up waiting for work to be submitted.
