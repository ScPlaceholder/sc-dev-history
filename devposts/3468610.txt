Both passive detection and active detection will require power. The power received by the radar has a direct impact on its capabilities. The act of using power results in a higher emissions and the act of ping results in a higher emissions. I'll leave it up to the designers to crunch the numbers :slightly_smiling_face:
The ping visuals are getting a major overhaul. Like a major overhaul.
The current limitation is going away to some degree. You will be able to passively find things out further then 15k but those objects will need to be streamed in on your machine ( and assuming the signatures are strong enough to be detected at those ranges by your radar ). Research is on going to determine efficient ways to provide further out passive contacts without the need of streaming in those entities, but may come in at a later point as the technology changes. Ping will allow you to find things further out as it will be able to detect things streamed out.
