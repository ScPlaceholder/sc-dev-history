sharing "to the best of our knowledge" as personal opinion is not necessarily how it works. if the post is yellow it's often treated as an official statement.
i can only speak for myself, but due to the above i try to stay away from speculative questions about potential things in the future - even if i know what the current plans are for a specific thing i can't know if a) it will never happen due to unforeseen circumstances or if b) it will change so much that an answer i give now will be wrong in 6 months time or that it isn't c) planned to be part of some big reveal that i'm ruining by sharing snippets of relevant information.
for that reason i do not reply to threads that are not related to already / soon to be released / already announced planets and their tech features.
open development does not necessarily mean that everyone can share whatever they feel like with whoever they want. :smile:
