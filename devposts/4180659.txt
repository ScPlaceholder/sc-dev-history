We would like to collect feedback on the core functionality of power management v2 and capacitor gameplay.  Here are some of the changes:
- Weapon fire rates and damage have increased dramatically
- Ballistic weapons have had their ammo pools reduced
- Energy weapons now have limited ammo which is automatically regenerated when the weapon is not being used
- How fast the weapon regenerates is dictated by the weapon capacitor system on each ship or manned turret
- How big the ammo pool is for energy weapons is dictated by the ship or turret
- The capacitor system is limited in terms of performance. Regenerating 4 weapons at the same time will take longer than just 2
- Manned turrets have their own isolated weapon capacitor system which will regenerate weapons faster and have a much larger capacitor ammo pool, effectively making them much more deadly
- Boost, formerly afterburner, now uses a thrust capacitor system which drains under use with a cooldown after use before it will begin to recharge
- Thrust capacitor has a lower threshold where afterburner can no longer be activated
- Shields have been retuned to operate similar to capacitors
- The power priority is now a capacitor priority, which can prioritize charging one system (shields, weapons, thrusters) over another (visible in power MFD).
- Systems with higher than default (33%) priority will have secondary beneficial effects.
- The assignment via keys (F5, F6, F7, F8-reset) or power triangle will set the regeneration speed for thruster capacitor, shield regeneration, and energy weapon ammo regeneration
- Overclock and power sliders have been removed for now
The goal of this update is to create a more dramatic effect in gunnery, emphasizing precise shooting and timing of shots rather than a constant stream of fire or trading fire. Your weapons should feel more powerful but more limiting, missing should have more impact. We also want to emphasize trade-offs in priority changes and usage of systems.
Note: This change comes with a full update of ship flight and durability tunings to match roles, but that portion will be assessed in a separate feedback thread at a later date.
