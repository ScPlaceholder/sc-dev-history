" You talk about Drag and the Cross-Sectional Areas, how there are three cross sections calculated - one per axis. If the ship is moving/strafing in two or more axis simultaneously instead of directly forward, Do you calculate the effective drag based off of a weighted ratio of each relevant CSA based on the angle of relative travel? "
Yes.  The goal is to capture the general behavior one would expect, but this simulation will not attempt to be a full featured aerodynamic simulation.  There are already many simplifying assumptions being made, so this blending of CSA fits within the expected level of accuracy of the system.
" Regards to the Gimbaled thruster issues and workarounds, have you tested the IFCS with the realignment delay coded in so that it takes x seconds for the thrusters to fully realign and achieve maximum efficiency in the required direction? If you have tested it, does the thruster lag make the flight experience particularly unpleasant and needs to be actively avoided as a gaming experience? "
Yes, I tried this with the Hornet, which was the first ship to use the system, and the result was an unstable flight experience.  The system could generally stabilize over time, but it wasn't up to handling any kind of dynamic conditions the way the current system does.  I would prefer to solve the problem, if we do, by introducing a set of fixed RCS thrusters, allowing the gimbaled thrusters to merely augment your control rather than provide for it entirely.
