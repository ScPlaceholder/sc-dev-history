@Cintara - There's going to be an option to toggle 'SoundSim', as we call it.  I always make the point that it's a lot more feasible to do this, even with current technology, than it is to generate artificial gravity.  Ironically people don't listen when I say that, though I'm sure every child knows that you don't have gravity in space either.  Hmm!
But in all seriousness, simulated sound will be something you can turn off, within the game, should that be what you want.  I know some people want that experience, some don't, but that's going to be your choice.  However we need a certain amount of other tech in place before we can commit to this - sound has to propagate better than it does right now, and with a game of this scale and with variable atmospheres, that's exponentially more difficult to achieve than one would like.
I know it sounds odd to say we need to work on how sound propagates before we can deaden it properly, but it's true.  Audio engines don't tend to do the job of emulating how sonic energy actually travels through structures etc., they take a simplified route and to ensure the non-atmos version is right takes a lot more than simply turning off the SFX.  (Nothing is stopping you grabbing the SFX fader in the front end menu and bringing it all the way down, it'll get you 80% of the way there!).
What I think you'll find though, once you turn off SoundSim, is that you'll initially get that 'wow, sounds like space' effect, but then you'll be quickly disadvantaged in open play because other players will be receiving coincident informational cues that you will not be receiving.  There will be some visual cues to make up for this but you'll always be contending with a lot more information along the one sensory pathway.  If that's your bag, no problem, but you'll be (silently) shooting yourself in the foot.
