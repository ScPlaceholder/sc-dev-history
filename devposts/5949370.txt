## Welcome to the '2953 Welcome to ArcCorp Contest'!
We'd like to see you showcase the unique charm of ArcCorp, home to Invictus Launch Week 2953, by taking screenshots of your favorite places on this planet and creating a postcard or a travel brochure. Include the sentence 'Welcome to ArcCorp' in your creation and submit it to the Community Hub and/or Twitter, and you just might snag a shiny ship!
## Before You Begin:
Before you start on your submissions, please read the contest rules carefully and remember to post your entries to the Community Hub and not as a reply to this thread.
:exclamation: Any submission not posted to the Community Hub or without '2953 Welcome to ArcCorp Contest' in the post title will not be considered. :exclamation:
## Keep in Mind:
- Read the contest rules! :book:
- Only a postcard or travel brochure, which includes in-game screenshots of ArcCorp and the sentence 'Welcome to ArcCorp', will be considered. Any other subject will be disqualified.
- A travel brochure should have some descriptive or informative text about ArcCorp together with the images for it to be considered a travel brochure.
- Please post submissions to the Community Hub or to the contest announcement post on Twitter (don't forget to include #StarCitizen in your post). Replies to this thread will NOT count as a submission.
- Multiple submissions per player are allowed. You can upload them as a gallery in one post to the Community Hub.
- Use '2953 Welcome to ArcCorp Contest' as the title for your submission on the Community Hub.
- Group submissions are not allowed. Multiple players in screenshots are allowed, but only the person submitting the entry is eligible to win.
- Post-processing is allowed, but remember that sometimes less is more.
- Consider turning off the HUD when taking screenshots, as this might make for better composition. Check out some excellent tutorials by DigThat32, Mr. Hasgaha, or NotGemini.
- You are limited to winning one prize, so you cannot win on multiple platforms (Community Hub, Twitter).
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your entries based on:
- Quality
- Creativity
- Rule adherence
