Ahoy, ye scurvy space dogs!
⁠Pirate Week be upon us, and it's time ye show us the spoils of yer plundering! Set sail among the stars, honor the pirate's code, and capture yer finest moments of swashbucklin' and treasure-haulin' in the vastness of space.
Let’s see the booty ye've claimed on yer daring adventures!
## How To Participate
During Pirate Week, we invite you to capture your most exciting adventures in the 'verse as you plunder your way to richness: We want to see bounty and cargo stealin'! Whether you're faring on your own or with a group of friends, we want to see the best of your pirate spirit. Furl your sleeves, take sight of belayed treasures of tungsten and wine to show us your most daring moments, and let the galaxy witness the pirate within you!
Please read the ⁠⁠contest rules carefully, to avoid disqualification.
- :beer: Prepare: Fill up your tankard with your favorite grog (or any in-game beverage) and make your way to the 'verse to capture your adventures.
- :camera_with_flash: Snap: Take a clear, high-quality photo of your character alone or with friends while you're in-action doing piratey things. Your loot/plunder must be in the picture!
- :outbox_tray: Submit: Send your original creations to the ⁠⁠Community Hub with the title "Pirate Week 2024" followed by your title, such as Pirate Week 2024 - Ahoy mateys!
## Keep In Mind!
- Read the ⁠⁠contest rules.
- Use "Pirate Week 2024" as the title for your submission on the Community Hub.
- Multiple submissions are allowed.
- Post-processing effects are not allowed.
- Group submissions are not allowed.
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your submissions on the following:
- Quality
- Creativity
- Pirate Vibes, we must see your loot/plunder
- Rule Adherence
## Dates
The submission period ends on Tuesday, October 1, 2024 at 11:59 PM (Pacific) / Wednesday, October 2, 2024 at 6:59 AM (UTC).
