"Banu, Xi'an, & Vanduul Sugar Cookies"
Recipe
Cookies:
1 cup butter (room temp)
2 2/3 cups Flour
1 cup light brown sugar
1 egg
1/2 tsp baking soda
1/2 tsp ground ginger
1 tsp vanilla extract
Pinch of nutmeg
Pinch of salt
Beat the butter, brown sugar, egg, and vanilla extract together in a mixing bowl, then sift in the remaining ingredients. Once that's all combined, wrap 5 evenly sized lumps of dough in some plastic wrap and stick it in the fridge to chill for about four-ish hours. Once dough's chilled, remove it from the fridge and begin rolling it out. Cut Banu, Xi'an, and Vanduul head-shaped pieces. If you mess up a bit it's okay,  you can always use icing to cover up your mistakes. Once you've got some shapes you're happy with, toss those suckers in a 350°F oven until they're light golden brown. These portions will give you enough cookies for a medium-sized Bar Citizen... just saying.
Icing:
3 1/2 cups powdered sugar
2 egg whites
"lil bit" of water
Combine the sugar & egg whites in a mixer. Add a tiny bit of water to the mixture to soften it. You can overdo this easily, so be careful here.  Separate into smaller portions, and add food coloring to liking. Get to work icing the cookies.
