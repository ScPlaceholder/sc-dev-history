We're currently dealing with compasses and tools on both planet and space navigation while we're redesigning the HUD. I agree that the pitch and yaw tapes do not make a lot of sense in their current state. The problem here is that you cannot really use them in space as they do not give you an indication on how to rotate your ship to point at a specific 3D heading. On planets it is fine because you have a proper "up" and "down" so the horizon gives you that reference. But in space we are still working out how to show that info.
And yes the heading in the compasses in the current live build are inverted. That was an oversight and is because the quaternions in our engine turn left when you increase the rotation around the up axis. This will be fixed in the new HUD.
