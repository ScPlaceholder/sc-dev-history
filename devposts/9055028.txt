## Features & Gameplay
Arlington Lever Action Rifle
Hey Space cowboys, we've got something special for those who love the wild side, a lever action rifle from Hedeby Gunworks! Hedeby Gunworks you may recall from their salvo frag pistol, for those unfamiliar it's a high Calibur pistol that can be charged to freeze and shatter it's ammo into fragments. Taking their shattering technology, this versatile rifle can shift between a DMR and a shotgun! Whenever you shift fire mode, you'll also see the weapon itself shift and slide, allowing the projectile to shatter. You should be able to nicely 1 tap medium NPCs to the head and where better to do that in the new siege of orison!  You can find the Arlington in Siege of Orison, along with it's bespoke optic around the verse.
- Weight: Average
- Firemodes: Single (DMR) / Single (Shotgun)
- Damage: 70 / 12.5 (8 Pellets)
- Projectile Velocity: 550 / 300
- Aim down Sight Time: 0.15
- RPM: 85
- Mag size:  20
