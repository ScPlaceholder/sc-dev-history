We do not plan on having a backer-facing roadmap for the Launcher.
We are mostly following priorities coming down from the Games and the RSIweb platform.
As we do not want to keep our agility and ability to support them we are not going to share a detailed roadmap.

I can give you our main focus for the next few months:
- Stabilize Launcher 2.0 by fixing performance and CPU issues, and video background issues.
- Reduce errors on download (Error 2000,  3000, 5000)
- Add new features: Uninstall, delete local settings, manage cache files, improve games location...
