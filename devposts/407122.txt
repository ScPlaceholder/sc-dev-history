Within the modern form of the UEE, the Imperator is an elected position and there are laws against nepotism. While there is an upper class and sadly, sometimes credits to translate into power, I would say there is no nobility.
Probably the closest thing to a nobility class would have been back in the Messer period, as members of the Messer house and those who were considered friends of the Messers were granted favorable government positions and contracts. If you were a Messer, you would expect to be treated like one. However, that all ended when the Messers were overthrown.
