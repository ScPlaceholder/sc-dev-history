The Intergalactic Aerospace Expo (IAE) allows all players to see some of the verse’s biggest, best, and most iconic ships up close. Different manufacturers take over the hall each day, so keep checking back to see everything the event has to offer.

Anything on display is available to test-fly for free. Each day, we'll feature a new manufacturer (see the IAE 2949 Schedule for details), and all of that manufacturer's ships will be free to fly for 24 hours.
## Limited Ships
Ships with limited stock will be made available in waves of 3, to give players around the globe a chance to pick up their favorite vessels. Here are the times for the different waves:
WAVE 1: 9:00 am EST / 14:00 UTC
WAVE 2: 3:00 pm EST / 20:00 UTC
WAVE 3: 9:00 pm EST / 02:00 UTC
For more details please visit the expo page at http://www.IAE2949.com.
