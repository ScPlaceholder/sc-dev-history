Atm Combines are quite hard to balance because we still have a bug in the explosion treatment where shields are affected more than they should. Once that is resolved we will take another look at them.
However in general we do not want the combine to be slotted into the performance envelope as other cannons. Due to the splash damage the plan so far was to push it more into a suppressive weapon against  ground vehicles and infantry. As such it is okay that the damage output does not compete with other cannons of the same size. While we certainly won't reach that goal in 3.14, we ultimately want to find a role not just for every ship but also for every weapon.
