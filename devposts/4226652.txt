Is 'prota' the name of a species of slime mould?
Prota is the general name for a type of slime mold, like 'cat' is the general name for felis catus and other catlike animals.
Re: Banu trade: at the time, Humans thought Davien was very close to Banu space, based on the presence of that fugitive. The Banu noticed Humans wanted to trade there, so the ones closest to the system at the time went there. As Humans got new information and Banu trade expanded, new locations became centers of trade. There are other explanations for why the Banu fugitive was in the Davien system but I can't say what they are, for secret reasons.
