"Foundation Festival is a month-long civic program that aims to strengthen community spirit by encouraging Citizens and civilians to volunteer with local and imperial organizations."
That's what the lore has to say about Foundation Festival. For us, however, it's the tales of camaraderie, support, and friendship that make this community stand out.
Welcome, everyone, to the Tales Of Camaraderie contest!
We’re asking you to share a true story of when you received help from other players or helped other players themselves. Of course, there are prizes for the best stories to help you enjoy more adventures in the 'verse with friends.
## Rules:
- Before you take pen to paper, please read the full rules carefully
- Please post your Tales Of Camaraderie to the submission thread only
- Maximum length of 500 words
- Submission language is English
- One submission per person
- Helpfulness of or towards fellow players must be a theme
- Pictures are not required but encouraged
- The submission period ends on July 21 (11:59 PM Pacific)
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your stories based on:
- Creativity
- Entertainment Value
- Style
