My father and I used to play Wing Commander before he passed away in early 90s. He was a fan of 80s pop, a lot of Classical Music and Vangelis, Jean-Michelle Jarre, Tangerine Dream.
We heard a ton of Jean-Michelle Jarre together.
I still have right next to me the actual CD we used to hear together Concerts in China by JMJ, with his handwritten notes.
I remember we told each other how this intro felt like the launch of a SpaceShip, specially due to that amazing glide with the announcer saying "four".
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e5z6TMcNsvs&t=100s
When I saw the amazing Space Stations I couldn't think of a better reason to pay a homage to those moments. I didn't want to use arps, as they are too overused in all SciFi, I wanted to show that beautiful analog synths can sound as lush, emotional and beautiful like a real orchestra.
So this was a homage mostly to JMJ, not Vangelis.
I also remember hearing this table tennis recording so many times and being amazed by the amazing stereo image!
