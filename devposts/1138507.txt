That model is an equally valid one, it's just a question of tradeoffs. Some people would like to not only stop seeing anything from the other person, but also disallow the other person from contacting them or seeing anything they write. This is a very explicit way of punishing someone for having bothered you. They will know they're blocked if they try to message you, or if someone they know mentions some content you've produced that they can't see.
There are also some people who would prefer taking the non-confrontational route and just hide the content that person produces. The upside of this idea is you won't necessarily tip off the offender to the fact that you blocked them, potentially preventing them from harassing you on other platforms in retaliation. You can also temporarily block people who you're just seeing too often, and unblock them later, without triggering drama.
It's hard to say what proportion of the player base prefers one or the other (even if we did a survey there will be sampling and self-selection bias). We went with the option that creates less friction, but I understand that it's not ideal for you and that doesn't mean you're wrong. At some point down the line, maybe we could distinguish between these two modes of operation (ignore vs. block?), but I unfortunately can't make any guarantees or estimates.
