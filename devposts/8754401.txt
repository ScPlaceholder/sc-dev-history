Alpha 4.8 is introducing Tactical Strike Groups, a new cooperative activity built around teamwork. To make sure nobody's stuck looking for a group when it drops, we're putting out the call to orgs who are actively recruiting.
We'll be publishing an Org Spotlight Comm-Link ahead of the patch to help players find a group that fits their playstyle. If your org is recruiting and you've got a good home for new folks, we want to hear from you.
## Why should your org take part?
All orgs are welcome regardless of size or focus. Rescue and aid, mentoring, security, hauling, multicrew combat, or just a good group of people who enjoy playing together. If you're recruiting and want to be featured, throw your hat in the ring.
## Make sure to provide the following when submitting your org for consideration:
- Email subject line: "Org Spotlight Submission – [Org Name]", including the name of your org
- Your org logo (1080 x 1080 minimum resolution)
- A quick description of your org and its interests, and why a new player should join (maximum 800 characters including spaces)
- A link to the org page
:mega: Starting today, you have until April 30th to send an email with your org info to the following email address: communityteam@cloudimperiumgames.com
From the submissions we receive, we'll choose a selection of organizations to feature in the Comm-Link.
We're looking forward to seeing your submissions. Thanks, and see you in the 'verse!
