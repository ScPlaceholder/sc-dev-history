I understand the argument, but specifically for flight a lot of these numbers might not help you make a good decision, the dynamics are often too complex. Easy public facing numbers on the FM right now are things like top speed or so but with NFM that will change. Because the FM will become dynamic as changes in mass will not just affect the accelerations but also top speeds. We couldn't show you a lot of data on a ship that is powered off, literally all of the values are dynamically available only when a ship is fully powered and the physics calculate how heavy that ship is (as our thrusters actually produce thrust which in turn defines the available accelerations / Gs defined by the mass). You throw in heavier shield generator into the loadout, it will change your acceleration and top speeds.
We actually had discussion several times what to show ... some of the flight data is really hard to show in an offline manner so to speak. Personally I also don't think that every number needs to be public as min maxing can easily hide things or choices. There is a good example from WWII on the performance of FW190 vs the BF109. In raw performance the FW190 was advantageous in almost all categories, climb rate, speed, etc. ... but pilots often preferred the BF109 and the reason wasn't easily put in objective numbers ... it was much more pleasant to fly ... so picking the better plane is actually a subjetive feeling of the pilot. Some of these principles can be applied to SC's flight model in certain areas ... so we still have some discussions to do in that regard.
