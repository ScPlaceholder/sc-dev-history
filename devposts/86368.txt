This is a question we get quite often, and has been answered to varying degrees on miscellaneous Happy Hour/RTV/Town Halls.
The short answer is that the model, in hangars, as it is now, has to be rebuilt from the ground up using our newest metrics/style guides/and Item 2.0 needs.  There are very little shortcuts from where it is now to where we'll need it to be to implement it in game.
Right now, we are currently rebuilding the Aurora, one of our earliest ships, and quite frankly a far simpler ship than the Redeemer, and this process is one that will take several months when all is said and done.
Star Citizen has a lot of ships to make, and a finite number of resources to devote to this task.  That means we can't work on all ships at all times, and those we do work on are prioritized by a number of factors, including but not limited to:
- Need for role in Squadron 42.
- Need for relevant game systems coming online.
- Availability of current systems to support intended gameplay.
- Availability of resources to make the ship.
We know it's been a long time coming, and there are many folks in the same situation, some with ships older than the Redeemer that still need to be completed.  Not everything we want to do can fit on the immediate or near-term schedule.  That's the case with the Redeemer.
We haven't forgotten about it.  It's not "at risk of being scrapped."  It's just that it's time hasn't come, just yet.
