I think we all want the pirate experience to be worthwhile and exciting but also risky and dangerous. We want to empower traders to be able to defend their goods, and we want pirates to have to think twice about attacking every ship.
We want pirates to feel like they have a target on their back from mercenaries, law enforcers and bounty hunters but we also want pirates to have ways of slipping away and smuggling their ill-gotten gains through inspection.
We want traders to have ways of getting their stuff back if possible but we also want pirates to be able to make big paydays if they get away with it.
The whole dynamic should be a tight balancing act that rewards a heist done well but doesn't make it the most profitable way to be in our game. If everyone was a pirate it wouldn't work and if no one was a pirate it wouldn't work.
