"Freelook" in the cockpit turns automatically on in driver seats when you remove the vehicle rotation bindings for your ship from the mouse (as the rotation inputs would otherwise "block" the look changes). It's effectively a legacy problem that became apparent as we removed some obsolete axes on vehicles in 3.9 (which in turn blocked the look axes as well). There are other related cases with the interaction mode which also interferes with certain look axes and head tracking in some places.
So the issue is known and we will resolve it ... but I cannot give you an ETA on that because the solution is not straight forward and needs a lot of testing.
