Lights, Camera, Action!
As shown countless times on the hub, the Star Citizen community has a wealth of talent and passion that can deliver amazing videos, highlighting the beauty of the ‘verse.
Today, we’re sending out a call to all producers, editors, and camera operators to create a commercial for the Origin Jumpworks 600i.
Newly introduced with the launch of Star Citizen Alpha 3.2, the Origin Jumpworks 600i combines elegant styling and the heart of a workhorse to create a truly stunning and unique ship. We want you to capture this symphony in motion and give it the limelight it deserves.  Surely there are more than #600reasons. :blush:
You have until 11:59 PM PDT on July 15th, 2018 to submit your 600i commercial video for a chance to win one of the prizes listed below.
The winners will be chosen by our Community team and highlighted on our social channels.
Haven’t seen our take yet? Check out the official 600i commercial, released at GamesCom 2947.
Ready to create? Read up on the rules, then start rolling and post your finished video in the Spectrum submission thread:
- Origin 600i Commercial Contest Submissions
The Community team has been planning a wide variety of activities for you all to participate in throughout the year and into next, all coupled with sweet prizes. So even if this contest isn't for you, we’ve got some exciting opportunities coming for you to leave your mark in the Star Citizen universe. (Seriously, we're doing more contests in 2018 than EVER before!)
## Prizes:
Three winners will be selected. If you work on the video with two friends (3 collaborators max), all will receive the reward.
- Place: Standalone Aegis Eclipse - LTI
- Place: Standalone Anvil Hurricane – LTI
- Place: Standalone Avenger Stalker - LTI
## Rules
Please read the full set of rules here: https://robertsspaceindustries.com/600i-commercial-contest-rules
· Video must not exceed 2 minutes
· Submissions must be in 1920x1080 resolution and uploaded to YouTube
· No more than 3 collaborators on a single video
· Submissions should clearly state all individuals involved in creating their commercial video at the time of submission
· Multiple submissions are permitted
· No unoriginal music (Star Citizen music is OK)
· All content must be original
· No license is granted for the use of any game assets, RSI Content, etc. other than the permission to create and submit entries for the Contest. No other purpose is permitted under these Rules.
· A video must not include or require reference to, or incorporate by reference, any other video, submission, Contest Entry, or other external content
Good luck everyone! We’re looking forward to seeing your 600i commercials!
