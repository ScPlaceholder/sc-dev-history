recoil is separated from the rest of the actual weapon stats minus some things like aim modifiers, fire rate, etc
You have aim recoil (which think is like your spine binding vertically, horizontally and roll)
You have your hand recoil (which is visual recoil)
Body recoil (which is mainly for making it look nice in third person)
Head recoil (which is camera shake)
That's all data I manually enter to create something that feels punchy
Weight is just how heavy it is which effects your characters MS and such
We don't have it effect it systemically but it does effect it intentionally on our data side
Weight dictates data we enter with things like Aim down sight speed, reload speed, etc
So to answer your question yes but actually no
