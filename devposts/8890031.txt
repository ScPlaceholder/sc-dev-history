It all began with an accidental encounter in the Davien system. A moment that would change the course of history forever, now celebrated across the UEE as First Contact Day. Since then, we've traded with the Banu, built a lasting peace with the Xi'an, assimilated the Tevarin, and clashed with the Vanduul. Each race has its own history, its own culture, its own way of seeing the 'verse. This Alien Week, we want to see how that inspires you.
## Welcome to the Alien Week 2956 - Xeno Craft Challenge!
Break out your tools, unleash your creativity, and bring the alien cultures to life with your own two hands. Food, art, props, models, costumes: whatever you can dream up and build, we want to see it!
Before you start on your creations, please read the contest rules carefully and remember to post your entries to the Alien Week 2956 event page on the Community Hub, not as a reply to this thread. Also, make sure to document your journey along the way, as we love seeing how your creations come together!
## Keep in Mind!
- Posts must be submitted to the Community Hub under Alien Week submissions. The title 'Alien Week 2956 - Xeno Craft Challenge' will be added. Any post with a different title will not be considered.
- Replies to this thread will NOT count as a submission.
- All submissions must be handmade using physical materials of the creator's choice.
- Creations must draw inspiration from the alien cultures, designs, or lore found in the Star Citizen universe. Entries with unrelated subjects will be disqualified.
- Submissions must include progress photos showing the creation process.
- AI-generated content is not permitted.
- Each post counts as one entry, and you may submit multiple entries. Multiple photos can be included within a single post.
- Group submissions are not allowed. Only the person posting the images can win a prize.
- Submissions that don't follow these instructions will be disqualified. Make sure to read the full contest rules for details.
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your submissions on:
- Rule adherence
- Quality
- Creativity
- Aesthetic composition
## Dates
The submission period will end on Monday, July 6, 2026, at 20:00 UTC.
