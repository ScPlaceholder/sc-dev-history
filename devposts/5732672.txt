Hello again!
It's finally done. Any fullscreen visor effect like the frost and raindrops now work on all aspect ratios up to 48:9 (e.g. 7680x1440) without any clipping.
They do stretch but this will be fixed in the future by VFX. It's not as immersion breaking as any clipping.
The changes in detail:
1. The visor 3d-mesh now dynamically changes based on aspect ratio. Currently supported is 4:3, 16:9, 21:9, 32:9 and 48:9. Anything in between takes the closest one. Big shoutout to @CianSmyth for creating these for me.
2. The camera now dynamically moves along it's view direction depending on the FOV so that the visor always covers the entire screen.
This also has the positive side-effect that the UI has a coherent size regardless of aspect ratio and screen size now.
Bonus:
If you prefer to play with higher field of views now you can!
Since field of view does work with the visor now the upper limit has been extended by quite a bit:
Previous range was 54-67 Vertical field of view. New range is 54-84
Be advised that the limits in the Graphics Settings are horizontal. They get calculated automatically based on your current aspect ratio. (See http://themetalmuncher.github.io/fov-calc/ if you want to calculate it yourself)
Example:
16:9 Aspect Ratio -- Previous Limit: 85 - 100 -> Now: 85 - 116
This change also fixes the black bands/blurry visor some people have seen in Area18 (https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/50259/thread/black-bands-on-helmet-visors-at-3440x1440p/5682862)
