Yes, the normal camera zoom feature in remote turret has been removed to avoid conflicts with the precision target cameras.
In the future precision targeting (or lets say rather the camera behaviours) will be made available to all operator modes. The overall goal is to have right mouse button creating some sort of in-lore zoom behaviour. When we'll get to that step, we'll also think about how to add custom zoom controls and optionally disable the automatic zooming that precision targeting does atm.
