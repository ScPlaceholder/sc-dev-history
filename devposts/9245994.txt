Greetings!
The most recent TTK update is in 5.0.0 PTU! Thanks to everyone who play tested. However, our journey to a better balancing does not end here. Please keep providing feedback. The core takeaways from the previous test:
- Heavy fighters feel slow to fly and their shields are too tanky
- General shield slapdown time is too long
- General ship turret HP is too low
- Further weapons outliers were identified
Hence, these are our changes in Version 6 (compared to version 5):
- Ship turret HP increased by 100%
- Size 2 shields nerfed by -20%
- Reduced slap down time by -25%
- Weapon buffs and nerfs:
- Supremacy-10T Laser Beam -30%
- Relentless-L21 Gatling +10%
- Axiom L-22 Repeater +10%
- Revenant Gatling -30%
- Flight Adjustments on Heavy Fighters
