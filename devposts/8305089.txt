The Intergalactic Aerospace Expo makes its debut in Orison, Crusader, from November 20 to December 3!
Every year, we strap in for two weeks of excitement as pilots from across the ‘verse travel to Stanton for the Intergalactic Aerospace Expo (IAE), the biggest annual ship show in Star Citizen. This year, the event lands in Orison, where you can explore the expo hall, check out the latest and most iconic vehicles from the top manufacturers, and take them out for a spin. New ships and surprises will be revealed throughout the show, with rental access unlocking on each manufacturer’s designated days.
