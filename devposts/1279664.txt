What you're describing is how it worked for quite awhile.  But there are two main reasons I changed this.  One, because I often got feedback about a ship becoming uncontrollable under certain actions, and players just wanted to have more assurance that the ship would do what they asked it to do.  And two, because that kind of system requires a full thruster analysis every frame, and that's just too much overhead.  If you can do a full thruster analysis just once whenever the state of the system changes, then use those limits in a way where you're guaranteed to get the indicated accelerations on every control axis, that's just a much more efficient system.  I'm not sure that we could have the luxury to use the system as it was originally designed, with a more emergent performance, as we scale up to larger player counts.
