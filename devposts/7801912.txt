From our latest PTU 4.1 Patch Notes: Ship Collision damage handling is now done using full collision data passed down by physics. This means mass and speed is now fully calculated in the collision and will make collision damage between larger and smaller vehicles behave more realistically.
No longer will your Arrow/Merlin/Fury/... be able to go full speed into a Polaris, and provide it with a rapid unscheduled disassembly.
