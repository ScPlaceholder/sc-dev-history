## Star Citizen Patch 3.7.2c
Alpha Patch 3.7.2c has been released to PTU, and is now available to test! Patch should now show: PTU-3626325.
It is strongly recommended that players delete their USER folder for the Public client after patching, particularly if you start encountering any odd character graphical issues or crash on loading. The USER folder can be found (in default installations) at C:\Program Files\Roberts Space Industries\StarCitizen\PTU.
Database Persistence Wipe: NO
Please monitor sc-testing-chat in spectrum for updates and testing instructions.
UPDATED USER.cfg settings:
r_displaySessionInfo = 1
Testing Focus
- General gameplay
Known Issues
- Players can keep rental ships past the 24 hour rental period.
- The user can exit on the wrong side of the bed after a respawn from far away, falling through the map.
- Purchased commodities occasionally do not visually appear in ship cargo holds.
- Trying on a hat while already wearing one causes hats to float in the air
## Bug Fixes
- Fixed various holes in the no fly zone around Area18 Spaceport.
- QT HUD should no longer disappear when exiting and reentering the pilot seat when Quantum Traveling.
