While the Centralist party does share some things with the Libertarian party (lower taxes, increased privacy laws, decreased military spending), they are not meant to be an exact 1:1 copy of the party. Centralists care more about maintaining the status quo. Moving the capital, to them, would be going against the status quo and would end up being a huge waste of government resources, so they are firmly against it. They are also against the rampant repealing of government regulations for the same reasons. Hope this helps!
