Hello everyone,
Star Citizen Alpha 3.20 introduced a major revamp to the Arena Commander game module, introducing new and explosive Experimental Modes that get you straight into the action. These new game modes range from simple variants of existing modes, to brand-new experiences that explore new mechanics, locations, and even in-development features before they are introduced to the Persistent Universe. As previously mentioned, our intention is to rotate this modes in and out of the game in limited-time cycles.
Find the schedule below to learn more about upcoming modes across September and October.
Start and End times for new modes are 16:00 UTC / 9 a.m. PDT.
