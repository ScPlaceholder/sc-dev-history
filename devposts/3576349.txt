If you get sudden jumps in the axes it could be a sign that the calibration hasn't bee done properly. I don't have an X-56 at my disposal atm but I know that it comes with a custom calibration tool (do not use windows calibration!) ... use that to recalibrate the axes. Check especially the values jumping on the extreme movements.
