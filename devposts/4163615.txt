Planetary systems like the Sol system are hurtling through space. By necessity, jump points HAVE to move with the star in order to remain connected with other systems. If they travel with the star, then it's likely that they are orbiting the star.
Given all this: don't take my words here as how things are going to work in the game. When jump points are in the game, design will make the call whether or not they orbit the stars, not me.
