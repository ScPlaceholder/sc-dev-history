LTI for 3.0 is our first implementation of insurance. Over the next few releases we'll be looking at how it is used and making improvements so that it works for everybody.
As we do more work on it, we will be introducing additional policies and balancing those too so that we have all the insurance schemes that were sold in the game.
If 'for now' you have LTI but your ship doesn't come with LTI, then you'll only get to benefit from LTI 'for now' as we switch over to an insurance-based ship recovery system.
