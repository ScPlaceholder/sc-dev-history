Good question - After checking with the vehicle team, this is indeed intentional. The release of the long awaited X1 offered a good opportunity to start rolling out some of the work being done to improve vehicle component gameplay.
As mentioned by others in the thread, this change is  the first of many that will ultimately provide an experience aligned with intended design. For bikes in particular, the team wanted to put emphasis on how vulnerable and exposed you are when you decide to go out on a bike. This is in contrast to the advantages of being a smaller target with much better maneuverability, with a smaller signature output. With that said, the X1 Force still features a shield as a unique feature that this variant was based around, to ensure it has a higher safety standard. Most other bikes will have shields removed.
