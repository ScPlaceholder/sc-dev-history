Greetings Avos,
We have released Loot Generation in its base tier, which will now populate certain areas in the persistent universe with loot crates. In those crates you will be able to find a variety of usable items, that you can either directly wear or store in your inventory provided you have the space. Loot crates are generally found in and around the same areas where you can do FPS missions, but we don't want to give away specifics as their ease of locating is part of what we want to review. We have some specific feedback aspects that we would like to have answered by you in addition to the general feedback.
Loot crates, content, and distribution:
- Were you able to spot the loot crates easily in the environment?
- Where you able to tell if a loot crate was already looted?
- Was the loot you found in the crates in average worth picking up?
- How would you judge the amount of loot crates you can find in general? (too many/too few/enough)
- Where the sizes of the items fitting the size of the loot crate?
- How would you judge the “regeneration rate” of the loot crates? (respawn/refill too fast/not fast enough/ perfect)
General Gameplay feedback:
- How would you describe the looting experience?
Thank you very much in advance, your feedback goes a long way!
