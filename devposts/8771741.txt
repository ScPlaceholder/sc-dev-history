Greetings!
The new Transport System has been made available for testing in the most recent Tech-Preview publish, which is a complete rewrite of the old Transit System. This system governs all communal transport across Star Citizen such as Trams, Trains, Monorails and Elevators, and has been built with current and future development in mind.  During this Tech Preview the following locations have been recreated with Transport.
Area18
- Full Location, excluding Event Halls.
GrimHEX
- Central Physical Elevators
- Hangar Elevators
Orison
- Hangar Elevators
New Babbage
- Hangar Elevators
A feedback survey is available at: Transport System Survey Form. This survey will be open until April 22.
We look forward to reading your feedback!
