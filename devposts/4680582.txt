Gravlev has been overhauled for 3.16! The update includes some significant changes to the physics as well as manual control of height adjustment (using strafe up/down keys). Height from the surface is relational to speed, so that the lower you are to the ground the faster you will travel. Dismounting behavior has also been improved. Please try the following:
- Moving over smooth terrain
- Moving over rough, rock strewn terrain
- Moving over hills and through valleys
- Using strafe up to go over obstacles and strafe down to go faster on smoother ground
- Dismounting
- Loading in and out of ships
- Cornering
Once you feel you have gotten a good feel, let us know what you think below!
