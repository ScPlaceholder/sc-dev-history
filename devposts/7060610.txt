Update on this:
- For 3.24, the grace period you have to move owned ships (with shields down) within an Armistice Zone is now 10 seconds (up from 4). I've also added a visible timer on the warning because I found the warning doesn't appear for 2 seconds (I've bugged this).
- Towing an owned ship within Armistice Zones used to immediately trigger global hostility, causing station turrets and law enforcement to fire on the towing ship, even though they had not yet broken a law (due to the grace period). For 3.24, we have changed this so that it only generates personal hostility between the towing player and the owner of the towed ship (and their party members). This allows players to "test" whether a ship is owned or not without disastrous repercussions while the scanning remains broken. By "test" I mean try towing, get the warning and disable the tow before the warning time runs out.
- Sadly, we also discovered a nasty bug that renders a lot of these improvements moot in the majority of cases - ship ownership is not relinquished until the ship is streamed out and in again. So if you claim an existing ship, the old one will technically remain yours in all but trespass rights. If anyone were to destroy it, for instance, you would get the right to press charges. This is until it restreams, when your ownership would be removed, but it is rare ships stream out in high-traffic areas. This has been bugged and I'll push on it all I can.
