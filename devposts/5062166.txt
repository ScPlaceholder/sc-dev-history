I think you might have misread what I said above. Turrets and ships are two different things and it should be very clear since our changes in 3.14 that we want multicrew to matter. Every fighter with a manned turret is a lot more effective in combat when completely crewed, that should be clear for everyone who met a fully crewed Hurricane in the PU. The interesting choice here is not just if you pick a fully crewed fighter over two solo'ed ones ... the choice you can do here matters and should be dependent of the available hardware (not all turrets are the same) and people you have with you. Turreting and piloting are two different things, require different skills and thus might appeal to different people. If you have the luxury, take two ships into combat and have them fully crewed as well.
