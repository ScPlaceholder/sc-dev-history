Roads are not part of the current batch of work for V5. We’ve yet to decide what comes next for planets, roads are a big contender, but possibly more likely would be more weather related features as these probably bring more gameplay. But  the visuals for dirt roads would already be possible in V5 (we can adjust soil and rocks very easily now), it’s the tools for easy and logical placement and potential optimisations that would be the main problems to solve. Man-made roads would however be more visual work (decals, signs, etc).
