The value of UEC changes drastically with every new feature that we add to the game.
Every time we add an overarching system like Mining, Prisons, etc. the sudden existence of that system causes a large impact on the economy that's difficult to predict and to track.
Tracking the UEC/hour of players is something that we're keenly interested in, but it's a remarkably difficult problem. For instance, it is difficult to aggregate how much money the average player makes while mining because they're likely not exclusively engaging in that particular activity. When did they start mining? When did they stop? If they get attacked, does that count as mining? Both the Quantum Team and the analytics team are actively looking into answering questions like this so we can make informed decisions.
While we continue to add features during Alpha, our focus is not going to be on a target UEC/Hour amount, but to provide a balanced experience to the player based on player feedback and the playstyle goals the economy team tries to set out.
