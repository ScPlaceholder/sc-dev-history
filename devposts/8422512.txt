Yeah, you've got it basically right. This was one of the reasons we wanted to clamp the availability of scu which you can buy commodoties at into these brackets. To make it a bit clearer to see where the breakpoints at.
For instance, Tritium near the top of the 8SCU bracket, which is on average around 13k/SCU, vs Combboard, the bottom of the 2 SCU bracket at 15K/SCU.  when you are looking at these prices and the Supply/Demand calculation is running, it's really hard to track that these are different brackets.   But Tritium is going to be highly competitive, because it only has one or two places that offer that great price, however, even as a chase good, it might carry 128SCU at a time, While even though there are 4-5 places selling Compboard, they might only have 50 SCU of it available at each location.
A good example in the real world would be that even if you go to the local farmers market, and find a really good price on crates of prized peaches. you probably aren't going to be able to swing up your 18 wheeler and say "dope, I'll buy 1 billion boxes"
Another example I commonly use is actually the same one that BigSilver mentions above:
An Armored car carrying small volumes of gold per time. but cargo ships aren't full of gold, they might have a Little bit of gold on them, sure, but mostly they will be full of stuff that makes sense to be moved around in high volume (or they hire out their space to other companies, but that's not really the vibe we want the game to go, not that orgs won't be free to do that if it sounds fun)
It's not that it's impossible to carry gold in an 18 wheeler, but you are basically making yourself the biggest score for criminals possible, and that's a choice you can totally make, if you are willing to work for it.
