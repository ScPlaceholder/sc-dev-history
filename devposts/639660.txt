Ownership semantics can already allow for non-players to legally own an item. Moving forward with orgs these concepts will get expanded to include shared ownership and access. The question will be less "do I own this" and more "in the place I'm at right now how does the governing body/inhabitants perceive that I'm in possession of X", which is to say it's all relative  and some won't care and others will care a lot, and others somewhere in between or only if it's profitable for them to care.
