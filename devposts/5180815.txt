Ships fly usually worse because when you shoot parts they often loose what we'd call critical components like thrusters. Also the majority of a ship's mass is usually more around in the center parts of the ship, the wings are often small and only carry a bit of the mass with them. On the Blade that is a bit different because the majority of the ship mass is on the wings but they don't carry critical components. So you can slice them off, make the ship a lot lighter and the thrusters need work a lot less giving the ship insane accelerations. We have with similar problems with a few other ships but the Blade was by far the worst offenders in that regard.
