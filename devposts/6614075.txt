In video games you typically want a really nice 'view window' which the 2nd image you posted is a fantastic example of a really bad video game iron sight because so much of what you are aiming at is obscured.
In general the new sights focus on having really clean view windows so you can focus on where your target is going and moving (which is doubly so needed with the new procedural recoil because of how aggressive the gun movements are now).
The klaus and werner sights were probably the worst offender for this.
You'll see a lot of slimmed down geo, raised rear sights & front posts in the next patch
So overall it's less about the position (we still have latter sight rings, wings, tinted tips though ) in video games are more about how much of target window is obscured
