"I know it is my isp that causes this and it is totally out of my control"
That depends on what's wrong. Technically, you're paying them for service so they should be willing to help. I've found that it depends on how it's phrased a lot of the time. "I"m a developer and this node is causing issues with my upstream" tends to be more receptive than people saying "I'm being disconnection from a game I'm playing". Unfortunately, many internet companies default to tossing video game connection issues into the "unsupported contact" bucket and their official policy can be to refer the person to the tech support team of the video game company which doesn't help.
"So my question is will there be a change to allow a recovery for this?"
Technically there is some recovery logic for this, but any recovery logic is going to have tolerance limits with regards to what it will allow as a re-connection vs disconnecting and requiring the account to log back in.
