Besides wanting to support hardware that doesn't have any RT support, we're also considering that some hardware that nominally supports RT still might not have enough throughput to handle global illumination with good performance. We're going to try to make it scale to as much hardware as possible and we're not yet at a stage where we can can do meaningful performance profiling, but that was another driver for us to want to make an alternative.
When it comes to a software alternative for ray-traced DI... you already have it in your hands! Direct illumination without ray-tracing is a pretty mature topic and the current approach with light lists, shadow maps and analytic functions to calculate brightness will give far better results than anything I can cook up with a few cubemaps. Ray-traced DI could definitely look better but it won't look fundamentally different in most cases.
