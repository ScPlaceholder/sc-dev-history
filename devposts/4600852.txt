I’d bet that at least one of your cores will be at 100%. The other confusing thing is that both CPUs and GPUs adjust their clock speed depending on load, so if you were CPU bound, then the GPU will be starved and so it may reduce its clock speed to save power, but that in turn will increase its utilisation making it appear more busy than it really is.
