The dynamic range of audio in this sense means the difference in level between the loudest and quietest sounds. So maximum has the largest difference - the quiet parts will be quiet, and the loud parts will be loud. Having less dynamic range has the effect of bringing the loud and quiet sounds closer together in terms of volume, at the expense of some 'punch'.
'Maximum' is how we mix the game, most of the time.
Speech Volume is for any dialogue from NPCs in the PU, including the ATC. VOIP volume is covered in the 'Comms Settings' tab :slightly_smiling_face:
