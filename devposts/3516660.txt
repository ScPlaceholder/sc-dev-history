"The Lost Generation" is a fictionalized account of the life of the real Tonya Oriel. The stories are kind of like if Indiana Jones were a real person who taught at a real university and took part in real digs and wore real sunscreen, and the Indiana Jones character had exciting daring adventures finding legendary fictional artifacts. Real Indiana Jones never found the Arc of the Covenant. Instead, he took part in an organized dig in Egypt and found a papyrus dump that consisted of crumpled up receipts (very exciting to real archaeologists). The real Tonya Oriel never found a fragment of the Artemis in the Stanton system.
