Greetings to our Australian community!
We'd like to give you all an early heads up about an incoming change to store pricing.
As many of you may have already heard, beginning September 1st, 2017 we are required to charge GST(Goods and Services Tax) on all transactions in Australia to comply with changes in the Australian tax code governing e-commerce.
This means that if you are in Australia, you will soon notice a price increase by 10%. So, if you’ve been eyeing a certain ship, now may be a good time to add it to your hangar before this change takes effect.
