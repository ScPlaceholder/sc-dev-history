## Time To Kill Balance Pass
Note: This feature is currently not intended for 5.0 release but is in the build for this weekend for feedback and testing.
This is a direct iteration on the previous build, and all figures below are relative to it. Based on your feedback, armor health has been raised across the board, size 4 and 5 ballistics have been brought down, and laser beam weapons and ship turrets have been scaled up to match the overall damage increases made across these builds. With this pass the overall picture for fighters should be settled, which lets us begin addressing individual outliers and turn attention toward large and multicrew ships.
Health and Survivability
- Armour health increased by 15%
- Ship turret health and the overkill threshold, the point at which a ship explodes outright from a single large hit, both increased by 145% proportional to the overall damage increase across these builds
Ballistics
- Size 4 ballistic alpha damage reduced by 20% and size 5 by 10%
- AD4B alpha damage reduced by a further 20% and AD5B by a further 10%
- All Mass Driver projectile speed increased by 10%
Energy
- All laser beam weapons buffed by 145%, proportional to the overall damage increase across these builds
- CF Repeater series alpha damage increased by 10%
- MaxOx Neutron Cannons and the Yeng’tu Repeater alpha damage increased by 20%
- WAR Cannon alpha damage increased by 50% and projectile speed by 40%
- WRATH Cannon alpha damage increased by 80%
