That is an excellent question, @RiotRain. Hopefully this info helps to understand how it works, why some issues in PTU still hang around, etc.  And @BloodDragon has it right on needing more pings, related issues, etc. Here's a bit more.
We find many issues through internal formal testing in controlled environments, determine reproduction steps, and implement fixes (that may reveal other issues, iterating dev/qa/regression/hardening/etc of code). Through Evocati/PTU, we dig deeper into the issues in a more "live" like environment with players in actual game play. During PTU cycles, we call for specific, focused testing on some issues to get extensive logs, additional feedback, and start working through it to uncover specific issues to fix. We push updates and releases with those fixes, which may later uncover additional issues, different reproduction steps, etc. with new issues and similar/variations/new symptoms.
So sometimes you'll see an issue called out that is remaining or seems to come back like a Scooby Doo villain, which in the underlying code can be vastly different systems causing it this time (than the first time it popped up, zoinks!). We'll keep iterating and resolving issues, and provide as many updates here as possible to keep you informed. Thanks for feedback and questions! o/
