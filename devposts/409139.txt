Hello everyone,
Below are the Frequently Asked Questions for our Origin 600i ship naming system. Be sure to check out the Origin 600i sale here: https://robertsspaceindustries.com/comm-link//16082-Origin-600i
## Can I reserve a ship name if I’m not ready to buy a 600i?
No, the acquisition of a Warbond Edition 600i is required to reserve a name at this time. While, eventually, everyone will be able to name their ship(s), early access to the name reservation system is limited to those making a Warbond Edition purchase.
## Can someone else name their 600i to the same name as mine?
No, your 600i's ship name is unique to you and cannot be used by another player for any other 600 series ship in the universe. Please know that this name does not get reserved for any other ship series that may allow unique names.
## How do I name my ship?
In the ‘My Hangar’ portion of the website, locate your 600i with the ability to be named and click on the arrow on the right side of the pledge listing. This will reveal the option to rename your ship. Follow the instructions and pay attention to the warnings and you will be able to name your ship.
## What happens if I reclaim my ship?
You will gain the standard store credit associated with the value of your ship, however, the name will be removed and can be taken by someone else.
## What happens if I trade my ship?
The name will stay on the ship.
## What happens if I upgrade my ship?
The name will be removed and can be taken by someone else.
## Can I rename my ship after I’ve already picked a name?
Yes, you can rename your ship anytime, if the new name is not already taken or inappropriate. Note, you will place the previous name back in the ‘pool’ so it is possible if you wish to change back, that it may not be available.
## How long can my ship name be?
32-ascii character limit with no consecutive spaces or dashes
## What limits do I have on my ship name?
Other than not being inappropriate, you are free to name your ship within the character limitations.
Things such as Profanity, Racial Slurs, and Sexually Explicit names will be filtered out.
## Can I have a famous ship name?
You are free to have whatever ship name you would like, so long as it does not exceed the character limit or contain anything deemed inappropriate.
## If my ship’s name is found to be inappropriate, what will happen?
CIG reserves the right to remove any ship name found to be inappropriate.
## How do I get a ship name that is already used?
You cannot.
## Does CIG Plan to recycle inactive names?
No, there are no current plans to recycle claimed names that go unused
## If I change my ship’s name, will the old name be available immediately?
The older ship name should be available immediately.
## Can I name my ship after my favorite CIG employee?
No.
