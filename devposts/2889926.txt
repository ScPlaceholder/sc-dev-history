Dia dhuit!
Raise a glass to another year of revelry, success, and prosperity! Gather your friends, make sure your bar is stocked, and recite a verse or two of your own lyrical creations.
While nowadays the prime culprit in hijacking attention is the mobiGlass, Citizens back in the day kept themselves busy with the oral tradition of storytelling. Sitting in the Atlantic Ocean, Ireland birthed countless great poets and writers, with the town of Croom, County Limerick inventing its own form. Yes, you guessed it, we're talking about the limerick itself. This usually humorous verse comes in the form of a five-liner and an ‘AABBA’ rhyme scheme.
To make this St. Patrick’s Day contest the most challenging yet, we’re asking you to bless us with a Limerick in which you describe your luckiest Star Citizen gaming moment.
To give you an idea of how this could look, this is what our intern came up with:
There once was a fella called Jack,
He needed a ship to attack,
Punching over its weight,
And store many a crate,
He discovered the Drake Cutlass Black!
Horrible, right? We're 100% certain you can do much better (and know your way around Hurston's flora)!
