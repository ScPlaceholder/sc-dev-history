It's annoying. Anywhere the SSR misses (edges of screen, near object silhouettes) you need to fall back to not-SSR, i.e. currently the cubemaps, in future the new GI.
You might think that it doesn't matter that much, the cubemaps were good enough so why would they not still be, but the moment you've got diffuse GI that isn't following the same cubemap, you get areas where the diffuse is dark and the spec is bright, or vice versa and it looks wild. Until recently we thought the skin shader was going to need special reworking and it turned out it was just trying to use cubemap specular with RT diffuse GI.
