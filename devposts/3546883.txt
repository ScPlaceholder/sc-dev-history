A lot of content creators make content for a living. We like to celebrate and show our appreciation for content creators who are out there making amazing things! One important aspect of supporting our creators is trying to help avoid them being "scooped" by leaks. It's often a huge bummer for creators when leaks happens, who then feel they are reporting on "old news". The last thing I want is a bunch of deflated creators, especially when Star Citizen is largely marketed by word of mouth.
You could argue that we should let all pilots fly their ship at the same time, but we're often not ready for that kind of stress testing when a ship first comes online, which is why it went to Evocati only, with some content creators being added to try and solve the issue mentioned above. We'll definitely evaluate how the roll out went, as we do every time, and continue to optimize. I've been reading everyone's comments and take it all to heart.
