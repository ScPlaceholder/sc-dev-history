All ships that function in atmosphere are going to need to have some kind of gravity-compensation thrusters at some point.  I don't think we'll ever expect ships to rely entirely on lift, even once that's part of the aerodynamic simulation.  So in the case of the Cutlass or Freelancer, if it doesn't have devoted VTOLs, its +z maneuvering thrusters are going to need to support a VTOL mode, which eventually would mean they can generate a lower level of thrust for an extended duration to counter gravity.
