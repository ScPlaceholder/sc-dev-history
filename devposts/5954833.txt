Hey there, to clarify:
- As this is a postcard design competition, we are allowing the use of photo/graphics editing software, such as Photoshop, to create your submissions and add certain design elements such as text, shapes, vignette/texture effects, etc.
- Post-processing of the original screenshots to enhance them, such as cropping, contrast/brightness/color tweaking, is allowed. However, the screenshots should only contain elements that were present during your gameplay capture. For example, please don't use Photoshop to insert a Whammers at a location where it doesn't exist in the game. :smile:
- "Less is more" is just a friendly reminder that over-processing or over-tweaking the colors in an image, for example, can sometimes detract from its original beauty, not unlike dousing a dish with soy sauce can alter its taste entirely (not always positive!)
- Lastly, we want to give you the creative freedom to showcase your favorite places in ArcCorp and even tell us why if you would like to, so you can either create a postcard or a travel brochure.
Knowing how insanely talented many of you are, we're looking forward to seeing all your cool submissions!
Hope this answers your questions!
