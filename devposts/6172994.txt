There's a few ways, as @MonsterAvy said there's a button to your left, personally i like the PIT menu: hold F & press LMB & RMB -> Weapon Systems -> Enter Remote Turret, once you do it once it'll be on your PIT for the full game for easy access :slightly_smiling_face: - Or if you'd prefer not to mess with any of that in the keybind options there's a button for Enter Remote Turret 1, assign this to anything and you'll get in with a single click! Having all the different Enter's on numpad is quite useful on big ships!
