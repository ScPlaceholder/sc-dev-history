The smaller the weapon size, the more shots a turret will have, that always the plan and is in line what you see with pilot controlled weapons. The change in 3.15 is that some ships were updated to have properly sized weapon capacitors for the turrets. A heavy fighter like Cutlass with 2x S3 turret should get about 30 seconds fire time IRC. A Hurricane (all heavier fighters with 1 turret have the same turret capacitor) will have half of that because it has 4 instead of 2 guns mounted.
