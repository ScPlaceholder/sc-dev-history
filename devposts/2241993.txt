Hi JollySheep! We actually currently do some approximation of lighting from the planet surface. There's a dynamic cubemap which updates as you fly around which should pick up an overall amount of glow if it's present. However it's fairly subtle and shows up best on very light or reflective ship materials. So a Prospector shows the effect much better than say a Retaliator. To ensure that this cubemap updates frequently though we have to limit what it captures which might sometimes exclude objects or glows, but we're making some changes to improve this.
That said, we're always looking into ways to further improve the appearance and readability of flying around at night!
