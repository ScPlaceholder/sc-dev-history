Hi @C-Park,
the "VJoy" is just an indication how far your ship rotation has been commanded in pitch and yaw. Atm it is only "visible" when you are in manual gimbals ... you will see that when you rotate in any direction, your aiming reticle goes. That is effectively the Vjoy and it will be a bit clearer in 4.0 (as it will be visible in other gimbal modes as well).
In 3.9 we changed how the aiming worked in order to streamline the controls and allow a wider range of motions. Your specific case does not work anymore as the aiming reticle is now either tied to your head or to your VJoy. From what I understood from your use case ... you effectively want to have the ability to "lock" where your mining head is currently aiming at while looking at other things and releasing the rotation controls of your ship.
Would it help if we added a function that would simply lock the mining head in place? That would effectively allow you to quickly glance to a new target, press the button and your gimbals would keep aiming there.
