The turrets compass rose is not supposed to reflect the world directions but the relative offset compared to the "forward" of the ship as this is an effective means for communication between gunners.  This also does not require the gunners to deal with the "roll" part of the ship attitude. Given that some our turret UI does not offer any information about that and some of them are even inverted on the up/down axis syncing up the gunner and ship compasses would actually become very confusing.
We could think about adding another set with world directions but it would really just be a secondary readout.
