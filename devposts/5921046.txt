The new player experience tutorial is now available for testing. You should see an option directly on the front end at your first launch of the build. Please select that option and go through the entire experience.  You will want to approach like a new player, with the key helpers and the hint system turned ON. Read each hint as they come up. Keep in mind, we consider this a first step using in-game systems in a minimalist way that doesn't require frequent udpates as the game is developed.
One key consideration we have, in addition to general feedback on the experience, is where you would like to see the new player experience expanded. What elements are critical that you think might have been missed or need to be expanded into? What is the natural next step?
