Happy Friday, everyone!
In just a few moments we'll be launching a new tool that will give you the ability to manually reset your character at your convenience.
This was a highly requested feature in the past. No longer will you need to submit a ticket and wait for us to perform the reset for you - it will allow you to take control and reset your own account when you see the need. Keep in mind, this is our first attempt at a tool like this, and it is possible v1 may not be able to fix everything a traditional reset would. This is just a short term step on to our long term goal of never having to server wipe again.
Additional details about how the Character Reset tool works can be found on the tool page itself here:
https://robertsspaceindustries.com/account/reset
Edit: As a heads up, the reset could take up to 30 minutes, and it may give you an 15006 error until the reset is complete.
