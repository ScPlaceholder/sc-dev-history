Greetings!
We have introduced a new kind of mission which will task you with infiltrating an underground facility (UGF) and killing/searching for data pads that contain codes.  These codes will unlock a dispenser located within the facility which will cause the items listed on the data pad to be distributed one at a time.  All non-mission critical items can be kept and sold at commodity kiosks, but to complete the mission you will need to make way for the mission critical items themselves.  While all this is happening, enemies will continue to pressure you so you may want to bring a friend along to watch your back, especially at the higher difficulty levels.
These missions happen exclusively in UGFs, can be shared, and require a drop-off to complete.  There will also be legal and illegal variants and various levels of difficulty.
Give these a try and let us know what you think!
