Greetings,
We have added a tier 1 version of reputation to some NPCs and organizations in the game, specifically mission givers and bounty/assassination organizations. These build much more slowly, overtime, than the prior prerequisite mission system and as such reputation has now been added to long term persistence. Income potential related to these missions will now grow more slowly and failures may have impact, kept relatively small during this phase of development. Please see the patch notes for a more detailed description.
Dive into bounties, assassinations, and mission givers. Note that it can be difficult to track your reputation, but improve that visibility will be added in the future. Get a good feel, then let us know what you think below.
