Invictus Launch Week, popularly referred to as Fleet Week, is the period that falls between Emancipation Day and Armistice Day, marking the beginning of the new enlistment year for the United Empire of Earth’s Navy (UEEN).
From May 17 until May 29, the UEE Naval Fleet returns to the Stanton System and Area18, ArcCorp.
During Invictus Launch Week, you can get a taste of what it’s like to join the Navy, as all manner of military-inspired vehicles can be tested for free. Experience new releases, rediscover old favorites, take in the spectacle of the UEE Fleet, including a Polaris fly-by, tour an in-service Idris, and more.
During the Invictus Launch Week 2954 Free Fly, everyone can download and play Star Citizen for free! From May 17 (16:00 UTC) until May 29 (23:00 UTC), participants will receive an Aegis Avenger Titan ship, perfect for exploring the system.
