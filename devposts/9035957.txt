Join fellow Citizens and members of our team for an evening of talking ship, giveaways, great company, and, of course, a Kölsch or three.
Gamescom Bar Citizen
Date: Friday, August 28
Time: 6:00 PM
Location: BON - Brauhaus ohne Namen, Mathildenstraße 42, 50679 Cologne

We’ll be looking for great photos and posts from the event, so remember to use the #BarCitizen hashtag when sharing your pictures. You could be featured on our social channels!
We hope to see you there!
