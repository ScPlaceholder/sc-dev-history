Don't just level the playing field, level the competition.
The Ballista’s powerful turret with dual size-2 ballistic Gatling guns should keep all enemy ships from getting up close and personal. Just in case someone feels adventurous enough to take a closer look at Anvil’s latest air defense vehicle, you want to be prepared.
Design a bumper sticker for the Anvil Ballista to show that citizen behind you what you think of them. For inspiration and ideas, you can draw from features of bumper stickers we have in our world today as we’re sure that there’s been some on the road that have grabbed your attention. Whether you “need more space”, your "other ride is an 890 Jump", or you just want to draw an S7 sized rocket with a Vanduul on top... All kinds of colors, themes, shapes, and sizes are accepted as long as they fit the bumpers of a Ballista and are appropriate for all audiences.
And the best thing? If your sticker design is selected as the 1st place winning entry, not only will you receive a brand-new Anvil Ballista, but you'll also have your sticker physically printed by us and distributed to all attendees of CitizenCon in November!
Before you get started, please read up on the complete contest rules here.
Don’t know what a Ballista is? Protect what's yours, and find out more about Anvil's self-propelled air defense system.
Entry Requirements
• Each bumper sticker is considered a single entry but you are allowed multiple entries (You can include multiple images in a single post/tweet).
• Posts on Twitter/Instagram must include the hashtags #gettingstickywithit and #StarCitizen.
• Sticker designs must be original work and include a Star Citizen reference.
• Entrants MUST either own or have the rights to all content used in their designs.
Deadline
The deadline for submissions is Thursday, August 1st, 2019.
Entries can be posted in the submission thread on Spectrum or Twitter/Instagram including the hashtags #gettingstickywithit and #StarCitizen.
