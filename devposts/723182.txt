This shouldn't be too much of a problem actually. The atmosphere is calculated as a light scattering effect, and drawn over the top in the same way as any other transparent thing, so there shouldn't be an issue with drawing it over things that are definitely outside the atmosphere... the only problem is that at the moment we're sorting them as though they're nearer, and as far as I remember, the atmosphere isn't sorted into the list somewhere, it's just hardcoded as a process that happens before the transparent list is applied. Basically this means that we either have to make the atmosphere rendering into a sortable object, or we have to create a new list to contain things that will be drawn before it.
