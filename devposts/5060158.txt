We do not look isolated at specific game modes for balancing. The Ares was rebalanced because it was an offender in both AC and PU and it was clearly visible in both game analytics and player feedback. Sometimes rebalancing will be done in small steps, sometimes in larger steps ... as much as we playtest internally, actual playing with the ships in the community often produces different balance results. That is why we go through ETF and PTU phases, however they don't always catch those issues.
NONE of the ships is in a final tuning state and certainly not when they are released to the public for the first time. Doing post-release changes has nothing to do with intentional nerfing ... those are just necessary adjustments after seeing the ship perform with real players. We're not secretive about that process and don't have hidden agendas ... Star Citizen is in Early Access, i.e. pretty much everything is still up for change.
