## :heart: :heart: :heart: :heart: :heart: :heart: :heart: :heart: :heart:
It’s the night of Coramor and you’re on your way to meet a loved one in Stanton. Is it better to give flowers or sweets, though? What about a heart of Hornets, 500 feet tall?
Welcome to our Valentine's Day screenshot contest, where we’re asking you to form a heart in Star Citizen wherever, and from whatever, you want. Then, share your image with the world on Twitter.
The three best entries will be rewarded with a prize to help you travel the ‘verse in style with your loved one. Before you get started, be sure to read up on the full contest rules.
## Notes
•	Each screenshot uploaded to Twitter is considered a single entry. However, you are allowed multiple entries and can include multiple images in a single Tweet.
•	Post-processing effects are not allowed. This includes third-party add-ons like Photoshop, Reshade, etc.
•	Screenshots must feature an image of in-game items arranged in the shape of a heart.
•	Only the person posting the screenshot can win a prize.
