Some cameras are better for facial tracking than others ... sometimes my webcam at work also just gives up and stops producing new frames for some seconds ... but that is usually when the resources are getting tight. To check what is causing the glitches, go to the headtracking options, click the FOIP calibrate button and leave the image open. If the tracking fails, that debug window will show you that. The algorithm tracks you if the dots are visible. Sometimes seeing that image is enough to understand how to help the camera to get a better recognition. Sometimes its big glasses, facial hair and quite often bad lighting. Make sure your face is well lit.
