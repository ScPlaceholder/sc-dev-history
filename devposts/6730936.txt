Overdrive Initiative - Phase 4
We've learned that XenoThreat has been hijacking comm arrays and implanting Vanguard callouts, which is how they've been getting their intel.
► Hack and shut down comm arrays.
► Stay alert for more information, as ending this infiltration is  likely to be more complicated than turning off a few comm arrays…
Story recap:
► In a series of missions, players are tasked with countering the activities of XenoThreat, a dangerous group, within the Stanton system. It all begins with a reconnaissance mission to anticipate an imminent attack, followed by acquiring encrypted intelligence requiring the elimination of priority targets to decode. Next, players must counter XenoThreat's attacks by intercepting several scattered incursions throughout the system. Finally, a hacking mission is launched to disrupt XenoThreat's surveillance activities by disabling the communication networks they use to gather intelligence. Each step of the mission contributes to weakening XenoThreat's operations and ensuring the safety of Stanton.
- We have also added an additional reward to be distributed to every player completing the Overdrive Initiative missions (once all available and completed): a set of recovered XenoThreat medium armor
- The F7A Rental for completing the missions has also been increased from 3 days to 7 days
