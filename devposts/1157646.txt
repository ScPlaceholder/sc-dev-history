Hello Citizens,
We're excited to introduce another opportunity for you to win some fun prizes and leave your mark on the Star Citizen website.
You're likely familiar with our tutorial videos on the  “How to Play” section of the Star Citizen website. These videos are intended to help new pilots jump into the ‘verse by explaining basic controls, flight mechanics, and more. We'd like to invite you to help us in making this section even more robust throughout 2018, starting with Star Marine and Arena Commander.
Starting today, you have until  Wednesday, May 30th to submit your video tutorials for Star Marine or (and) Arena Commander for a chance to win one of the prizes listed below.
Our Community team will choose the winners, and the triumphant videos will become a part of our website for everyone to see!
What are you waiting for?

## Rules
Please read the full set of rules here: https://robertsspaceindustries.com/Q2-2018-Tutorial-Contest
- Tutorial must not exceed 5 minutes
- Submissions must be in 1920x1080 resolution and uploaded to YouTube
- No more than 3 collaborators on a single video
- Submissions should clearly state all individuals involved in creating their tutorial at the time of submission
- No unoriginal music (Star Citizen music is OK)
- All content must be original
- Contestants are only eligible to win once
- Must adhere to required content
- Tutorial must be in English
- No license is granted for the use of any game assets, RSI Content, etc. other than the permission to create and submit entries for the Contest.  No other purpose is permitted under these Rules.
- A tutorial video must not include or require reference to, or incorporate by reference, any other video, submission, Contest Entry, or other external content

## FAQ
If I work on the video with two friends and win the contest, will we all receive the reward on our account or will only one of us win be rewarded?
All of you will receive the reward (no more than 3 collaborators per video).
If I win the SM tutorial video contest AND come in 3rd for the AC tutorial video contest will I receive two rewards?
No, you will only receive one reward (highest reward applicable).
