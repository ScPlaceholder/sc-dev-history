That does not look like a bug to me. When you open the MobiGlas we only have two choices with the input axes ... we can either "reset" them or we can "freeze" them as we do it currently in the game by popular feedback demand.
In coupled mode you do not directly accelerate or decelerate your ship ... you instead command a "goal speed" that IFCS tries to reach. So if you re-centered the controls and then open the MobiGlas, the last given input to IFCS was to reach a goal speed of 0 ... so it will try to stop even when the MobiGlas is up. The goes for the other way as well ... if you put your stick / throttle to 50% forwarded and during acceleration get your MobiGlas up, IFCS should still try to reach 50% of the currently allowed speed.
Would you instead expect that the ship just keeps the speed whatever it had when you bring up the MobiGlas?
