You submitted your creations, you voted for your favorites, but now the dust has settled. The winner of 2953's Ship Showdown is...
## The Drake Corsair!
Congratulations to all the Drake fans who rallied behind not just the Corsair, but a wide selection of Drake ships that made it onto the bracket this year.
The Corsair will be available for all backers to fly for free in-game, from today until September 30!
Additionally, your top four ships are taking home a prize as well! The Corsair, the Vulture, the 600i Explorer, and the Redeemer will all receive new 2953 Best In Show paints when the Intergalactic Aerospace Expo rolls around later this year, along with manufacturer-themed posters for use in your future habs! All backers who have these ships in their hangar at the start of IAE will receive the paints and posters then. You also will have an opportunity to pick up the ships with their Best in Show prizes during IAE with their respective ships.
Congrats again to the Corsair, and see you all at IAE 2953!
