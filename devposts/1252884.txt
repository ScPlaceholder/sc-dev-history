Few quick updates regarding the Mustang Beta's setup with the Mustang-series Update:
The physical size of the Mustang will already accomodate the needed height/interaction spacing for the Mustang Beta's camper room, so the ship wouldn't be growing/changing shape from what we've already shown for the update. There is also a door that divides the camper and cockpit sections, so the concerns people had regarding sudden decompression will be covered there.
Internal ammenity wise, we're retaining the bed, toilet/shower, and small side-bar area in the ship, but some of the smaller compartments have been paired down, along with removing the 2 lockers in the old Beta. Along with that, we've also removed the couch-seat since we've had to rearrange the interior layout a bit, repositioning the bathroom to the rear of the camper section.
There are a few other things being explored for the Beta to give it some extra character/flavor as well, but I will go into details on those aspects later, as some would sound great, but aren't for-sure things we can do just yet. Overall though, the goal is still to make sure the Mustang Beta serves as a nice, usable, portable camper ship you could take and park anywhere.
