Yeah, this is true, and we will be looking at this more going forward. As it happens, all of the heightmaps for stanton are now immutable as it would break all the locations we have. Going forward, we won't design individual heightmaps for each river (as they can be used in 100s of different locations on the planets), but the global base heightmap will be shaped around floodplanes and watersheds with intention for specific river placement. This will allow us to have longer rivers that lead to the ocean.  We can then distribute individual heightmaps in such a way that we don't need to move mountains where a river will go. This will go a long way to making our rivers more in-situ :slightly_smiling_face:
