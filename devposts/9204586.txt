The Intergalactic Aerospace Expo 2956 has landed in New Babbage! While you’re poking around the ships, we’ve been wondering what the rest of your days look like.
Some Citizens work hard for the money. Others are workin’ 9 to 5, while a few are workin’ for a livin’. There’s cargo to haul, patients to save, bounties to chase, and wrecks to salvage.
A funny thing happens when you’re busy takin’ care of business: adventure has a habit of finding you.
We want to see short videos that capture those moments, whether it’s an everyday routine or an extraordinary outcome. From heartfelt to hilarious, or harrowing to heroic, all tones welcome. Just keep it bug-free.
Been doing the same job since 3.0? Here’s your excuse to change careers for a day. Nearly everything on display in the show halls is available to test-fly for free, so now’s a good time to try something new. Your first shift as a pirate counts!
## What we’re looking for:
- Day-in-the-life vignettes
- Dramatic reenactments of everyday events
- Slice-of-life moments with an unexpected twist
- New job, who dis?: your first shift in a profession you’ve never tried, and everything that goes wrong (or right)
- Any profession or playstyle: miner, salvager, pirate, hauler, medic, racer, and anything else you love doing in the ’verse, whether on foot, in a ground vehicle, or in a ship
## How To Participate
You picked your profession for a reason (or 10!). Showcase what you do and why you do it in a short-form video.
Please read the contest rules carefully to avoid disqualification.
- :toolbox: Plan: Choose your adventure and decide what you want to show us.
- :movie_camera: Capture: Record an in-game video of 60 seconds or less.
- :outbox_tray: Submit: Create an IAE 2956 post on the Community Hub, submitted as a Video, with the Machinima category. Entering with a collaborator? Don’t forget to list both RSI handles!
## Keep In Mind!
- Read the contest rules!
- Posts must be submitted to the Community Hub, as a video hosted on YouTube.
- Videos must not include any third-party music, AI-edited content, effects, sounds, soundtrack, or other third-party content. Star Citizen music and sounds are accepted.
- Visual post-processing effects are allowed, as well as on-screen graphics from the editing software of your choice.
- Videos must not include any other video, submission, contest entry, or other external content.
- Videos must include the provided end slate, shown for at least 3 seconds: https://media.robertsspaceindustries.com/qxub1wbq2zm6r/source.zip
- Videos must not feature bugs, glitches, or exploits.
- Maximum video/machinima length: 60 seconds, including the required end slate.
- Minimum video resolution: 1920 x 1080, but 3840 x 2160 (4k UHD) is highly preferred.
- Videos may have a maximum of two contributors or collaborators. You may include more players in the video, but only up to two people per submission are eligible to win.
- Submissions should clearly state the RSI handles of all those being credited as contributors or collaborators at the time of submission.
- Multiple submissions by or involving a participant are NOT permitted.
- Replies to this thread will not count as submissions.
## Preferred Tech Specs:
VIDEO
- Format: .MP4
- Codec: H.264
- Aspect Ratio: 16:9
- Frame Rate: 30fps (minimum)
- Pixel Dimensions:
- 3840 x 2160 4k UHD (preferred) / 1920 x 1080 (minimum)
AUDIO
- Stereo, 48kHz, 24bit (16bit minimum)
