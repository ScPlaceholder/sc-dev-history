When we say you delivered, we mean YOU DELIVERED. Thanks to your creative nominations and votes, we were able to narrow the list down to the following four champions, all of which fly away with sweet exclusive goodies.
- Argo MPUV-1C - "Argo Cargo"
- Crusader Mercury Star Runner
- Crusader C2 Hercules Starlifter

If you have one of these fine vessels in your web-hangar, you'll receive their respective 2951 Best in Show paint at the Intergalactic Aerospace Expo this year, for free.
In addition to the Best in Show Paint, you'll be flying away in style in a matching in-game jacket, adorned with your champion.
Plus, all backers will receive an Argo Astronautics backpack to celebrate the ultimate champion of Ship Showdown, the Argo MPUV-1C (or belovedly referred to as the "Argo Cargo"), which was this year's ultimate champion (1st place). You'll receive the backpack even if you don't have an Argo MPUV.
