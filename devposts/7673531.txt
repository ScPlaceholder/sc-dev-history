## Welcome to the 'Squadron 42 Newsletter Contest'
To celebrate the debut of our revamped Squadron 42 Newsletter, we're challenging you to unleash your in-game creativity by submitting stunning screenshots of your Star Citizen character in action, dressed in their finest UEE attire and embodying the true spirit of a Citizen of the Empire - and the reward will be a true piece of Squadron 42 history: a signed music sheet used at our recent soundtrack recording sessions at Abbey Road.
## Before You Begin
Before you start submitting, please read the contest rules carefully.
:exclamation: Remember to sign up for the Squadron 42 Newsletter at squadron42.com within the contest duration and to upload your entries to the Community Hub, NOT as a reply to this thread. :exclamation:
Any submission not posted to the Community Hub or without 'Squadron 42 Newsletter Contest' in the post title will not be considered.
## Keep In Mind
- Only posts submitted to the Community Hub will be considered.
- To be counted as an entry, posts must be titled 'Squadron 42 Newsletter Contest', uploaded to the Community Hub, and have the 'Screenshot category' selected.
- Each screenshot uploaded to the Community Hub following the aforementioned two rules will count as a single entry. Multiple entries are not allowed.
- Post-processing effects are NOT allowed. This includes third-party add-ons like Photoshop, Reshade, etc.
- Screenshots must feature an image of a player's character dressed in their best UEE garb in action. Any other subject will be disqualified.
- Only the person posting the screenshot can win a prize.
- Participants must also be signed up to receive the Squadron 42 Newsletter at squadron42.com before the submission period ends to be eligible to win.
- Read the contest rules carefully.
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your submissions on:
- Quality
- Creativity
- Rule adherence
- Aesthetic composition
## Dates
The submission period will end on Tuesday, February 4, 2025, at 11:00 UTC (03:00 AM Pacific).
## Prize
:sports_medal: A page of sheet music from the Squadron 42 score, used in the official soundtrack recording sessions performed at Abbey Road Studios, London, and signed by Chris Roberts.
May the muses that inspired Geoff Zanelli also guide you in this contest! Good luck!
