Greetings!
Outside of Guild Halls we have added Local Contract boards that have contracts that occur various distances away from the hub that you picked them up from. These contract boards have a new visual design compared to the mobiglass screen that show more contracts in a panel style and exposes more information to players, with the ability to choose what faction you want to work.
In addition to the survey linked below, we would love to hear your thoughts on:
- What do you think of the missions overall?
- What did you think of the mission boards?
- How far (distance wise) did the missions take you from the mission board?
- What did you think of the combat/infiltration into the locations?
- What mission was your favourite?
- What location was your favourite?
A feedback survey is available at: Local Mission Boards Survey Form. This survey will be open until Nov 30.
Thank you for taking the time, and we look forward to reading your feedback!
