Yela currently has 0.01 atm of pressure, 100% of which is oxygen.
The player currently needs 0.2 atm of oxygen to breathe safely.
The personal life support system (Undersuit + Helmet + Gas Tank + Gas Purifier) tries to maintain a 0.25 atm of oxygen.
With each breath, you take a part of that oxygen and replace it with carbon dioxide, but by your next breath the purifier clears it up and the gas tank refills it.
When the oxygen in the atmosphere is low (like on Cellin) your stamina regeneration drops and you suffocate slowly depending on how tired you are.
However, when the pressure of the atmosphere is low, you start bleeding
0.15 atm : 1 damage per second
0.1 atm : 2 damage per second
0.05 atm : 3 damage per second
0 atm : 4 damage per second (in vacuum you last ~18 seconds)
So on Yela if you take your helmet off you will take 3 damage per second and die in ~24 seconds. But if you keep your helmet on, your gas tank can absorb the oxygen from the atmosphere and refill itself indefinitely.
^ All this information is subject to change.
