Item 2.0 ships will have separate healths for the vehicle and the various components. For instance the powerplants can be independently destroyed from the coolers. The plan is definitely to have various ways a ship can get destroyed or stop being operational, some of which result in dramatic explosions, but not all, and some of which end with the crew surviving, and others might kill some or all the crew. In general we want this to be more emergent from the underlying systems, and less fixed and scripted like it is now. This is where we want to go, of course it's going to take time to get there. Will be cool though!
