The Intergalactic Aerospace Expo 2955 has landed, and this year, we’re challenging you to capture your favorite ships and vehicles in action. It’s Showtime!
Show us how you’re mastering your ship, how you’re fighting enemies, loading cargo, salvaging, mining or any gameplay that you have in mind while enjoying the vehicles presented during the IAE 2955.
## How To Participate
We’re calling on the community to celebrate the IAE 2955 by showcasing their eye for detail and cinematic flair.
Please read the ⁠⁠contest rules carefully to avoid disqualification.
- :brain: Prepare: Choose your favorite ship or vehicle and plan an exciting hero shot, solo or with others.
- :camera_with_flash: Capture: Take the screenshot in-game. Feel free to take the vehicle for a spin, as you can rent them for free during IAE 2955!
- :outbox_tray: Submit: Send your entry to the ⁠⁠Community Hub by submitting your entry to the dedicated Showtime! category. You can submit multiple entries, so enjoy the many manufacturers present during the event from November 20 to December 3rd!
## Keep In Mind!
- Read the contest rules.
- Posts must be submitted to the Community Hub, entitled “Showtime! IAE 2955”, and select the Image category. You can do so by clicking on "Create an Event Post".
- Each submission must feature a “hero shot” of your ship or vehicle in action.
- Post-processing effects are not allowed.
- AI-generated or AI-edited content is not allowed.
- Replies to this thread will NOT count as a submission.
- Group submissions are not allowed, but feel free to feature multiple players in action in your image. Only the person posting the image can win a prize.
- Multiple  submissions are allowed, but please limit them to a maximum of five of your highest-quality shots (this is not a hard-and-fast rule).
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your submissions on the following:
- Quality
- Creativity and composition
- Hero shot, create an iconic or memorable moment
## Dates
The submission period ends on Wednesday, December 3, 2025, 20:00 UTC.
