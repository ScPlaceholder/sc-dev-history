They are not tied to a specific planet but enabled for certain cloud quality settings - starting at "High" and fully enabled with "Very High" and above.
Since its simulating the real phenomenon of clouds casting a shadow onto the particles in the atmosphere, they are most visible in specific lighting conditions. Strongest impact is with dense, individual clouds and  in the twilight region. Crusader's cloud structure lends itself less to that effect being very visible, but it does happen from time to time.
