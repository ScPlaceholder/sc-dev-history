As several people correctly picked up from the Microtech presentation, it’s only the local height variations that are limited to 2km, globally they can be any height we want.
The 2km limit isn’t ingrained in the tech and can be modified, but it comes from a desire for detailed first person environments. To get good local details the terrain textures need to cover around 4km, any less and we can’t do satisfactory mountains, any larger and everything feels super low res and boring from first person (or if you keep the 4km width and use 4km verticals then you’d need a LOT more terrain textures to achieve the required structure and variety). So while there certainly are some examples on earth of extreme slopes beyond 2km (eg in Himalayas), they’re relatively uncommon and not worth the sacrifice of first person details everywhere (we need all terrain on the planet to match size and resolution for performance reasons). For comparison, the European alps mostly have height deltas of around 1-2km within any 4km area.
