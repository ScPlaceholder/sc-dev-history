Ship sales have nothing to do with the ship balance itself, they just happen to happen at the same time. Whether we're re-tuning parts of the Corsair or not is highly dependent on what data and feedback pops up. The Corsair is out for the first time now but you should never expect that we hit a good balance on the first release, because a lot of things only pop up after the wider player base starts using it. If things feel not right or if the Corsair feels overpowered we'll nerf it, if it doesn't perform to our expectations we'll buff it, it's as simple as that. There is really no good way to have balancing adjustments not around the time when the ship released.
The state right now for further balance is that we're still collecting data and feedback.
