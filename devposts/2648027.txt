Yeah I agree, the problem with the new target tracking is that you cannot really tell it's tracking or not. The decision to loose the circle from target focus was done on purpose though, we really would like to avoid any non-diegetic UI feedback on the visor. We already had chats with the UI team on how to approach that and we will likely do something about it in Q1 2020.
Meanwhile you could use the auto function in the game settings ... it allows you to automatically track the target with your head if its visible ... that way you can be sure it's tracked.
Also the target tracking will automatically re-enable when you lost the target for just a small amount of time.
