Hi! The semi-major axis of Kellog I is 0.176 au. It's not tidally locked to the sun; it actually rotates even slower than the time it takes to orbit the sun. This keeps the whole surface molten (sorry about saying it was tidally locked before; I was looking at the wrong numbers).
We consulted with two astrophysicists in order to get the numbers to build our planetary systems with as much scientific accuracy as we can.
I hope this helps!
