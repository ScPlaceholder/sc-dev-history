The statistics I measure aren't user specific but rather, are representative of the overall player experience so I'm not referring specifically to disconnections that you encounter but rather am comparing the amount of timeout errors vs disconnections caused by server crashes for all players over a period of time. If you're having issues with the game frequently running into problems, I'd suggest getting in contact with either the players on our tech support forum, or our Player Relations team if that doesn't help. Without spending some time investigating it, there's no way to say for sure if what you're experiencing is a bug or a tech support problem so it's a good idea to approach the problem with the mindset that it could be either.
