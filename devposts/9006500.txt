Weapon & Item Updates
Added New FPS Weapon: Behring CQ7 Bullpup Rifle
Design Notes from Zac: After a year of very interesting sci-fi weapons  (and some very rusty ones), we heard a lot of feedback of wanting a nice standard lower RPM rifle to be added into the mix. So what better missing 'standard' than a bullpup? that and the amount of times I got sent the old concept art for the behring bullpup on spectrum over my time working here, I knew it had to be this.
To make this stand out this weapon has a nice sharp TTK for a rifle but having a consistent and followable Y recoil shaped pattern, to those who learn it will be able to consistent land shots on target, Behring weapons typically always have very consistent and readable recoil and this weapon is no different, having a bit of 'bite' to its power but still in the 'feels smooth to use' that you expect from behring. Overall this is a great, all rounder daily driver type of rifle that won't let you down.
The weapon can be found in Ship In Distress missions as well as Moraine Data Retrieval and Moraine Movements both take you to the base where you can find the QB7.
- Weight: Average
- Firemodes: Automatic / Burst
- Damage: 16.5
- Projectile Velocity: 550
- Aim down Sight Time: 0.25
- RPM: 650
- Mag size:  40
