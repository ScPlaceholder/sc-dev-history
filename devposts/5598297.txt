Hello all!
3.18 introduces physicalized cargo and updated kiosk functionality for buying/selling.  Physicalized cargo can be tractor beamed, dropped when a ship is destroyed, and offloaded from one ship to the next.  Grab your favorite trader and start making some runs. Let us know, via Issue Council, if you encounter a location that doesn't have the proper kiosk setup or any other issues you encounter. Also, be sure to stress test the new system. Want to know what happens when you blow up a C2 full of cargo over Lorville? Go find out!
Feedback we are ready for includes moving/manipulating cargo, cargo drops on ship death,  transferring cargo ship to ship, cargo markers on ship death, selling of stolen goods at no question asked terminals, kiosk UI and functionality, and general cargo manipulation and functionality.
Looking forward to hearing from you!
