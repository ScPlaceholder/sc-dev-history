Greetings!
Star Citizen Alpha 3.6 is live! This means we once again want to assess current content to get a more clear picture of how things are functioning, which bugs you feel should be highest priority for fixing, and determine which areas of the game you would like to see improved most. One of the most rewarding aspects of open development is the ability to collect, analyze, and act on community feedback in near real-time. Open development during content creation means things can definitely be a bit buggy, but it also means you have the chance to give feedback on the content as we develop it, allowing you to truly leave your mark on the Star Citizen universe.
Feedback and iteration is a core part of the Star Citizen experience and we have been taking the time to assess player feedback and general feeling towards the gameplay experience in quarterly intervals. We recognize that some of the issues raised may end up not being the focus of that particular quarter or may not of changed since the last time you gave us your input, but it doesn't mean the feedback wasn't heard. Due to resources and prioritization, it's very possible old issues may not of been addressed, and some feedback ends up carried over to a future quarter for focus. Please do not hesitate to bring up egregious issues that have carried over from prior patches.
These types of threads help us dial in on unintended effects or new issues that may of appeared with a new patch. They also help us understand which aspects of the game that you are really enjoying. Your comments and survey answers also help us update old issues as we began working toward solutions, providing new findings and up-to-date information as you all dive deeper into both the weakest and strongest parts of gameplay. You are an integral part of the game development process for Star Citizen.
The survey can be found below. You can edit your responses to fill in other areas of the game you want to assess at anytime you like, as well as edit prior selections. We look forward to hearing from you and appreciate you taking the time to help improve Star Citizen!
