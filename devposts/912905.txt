Hi @CreditRisk, @Morisson covered a lot of the bases. It was initially conceived to be like the FBI or Interpol, a imperial law enforcement bureau that handled multi-system crime. Their specific role has fluctuated a bit as we've continued development and they're getting involved in more local crimes, but the idea was that there would be local law enforcement and then Advocacy would handle crimes that move between worlds or occur in the space between the planets. The ranking structure was heavily modeled after the FBI. As for their jurisdiction in regards to the military, currently it's still a little fuzzy, so I'll have to get back to you once we firm up where the lines are.
