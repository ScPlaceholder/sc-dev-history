Hey y'all,
In the wake of vicious and unprovoked attacks by the Nine Tails gang on Orison’s platforms, and for the safety and security of the Stanton system as a whole, it has become vital that we begin efforts to rebuild immediately. In conjunction with several valiant support organizations, we are coordinating a widespread campaign to supply and secure relief efforts. With your contribution, we can restore Orison to its rightful glory.
We've put together answers to some of your common questions about Orison Relief Support below.
## ▲ What is the Orison Relief Support event?
Orison Relief Support is a new limited-time event arriving with Alpha 4.10.1: Orison Relief Support, where you'll lend a hand in rebuilding Orison through missions spanning resource gathering, crafting, hauling, and combat.
## ▲ Where does Orison Relief Support take place?
You can accept the Orison Relief Support missions from anywhere within the Stanton System.
## ▲ How can I accept the new missions?
Missions can be accepted under the priority tab in the Mission App, and all will start with "Orison Relief". Fourteen new contracts will be available to the citizens of Stanton to help repair the damage to Orison. These include:
- Resource Gathering
- Collecting and Delivering Medical Supplies
- Crafting and Delivering Replacement Platform Thrusters
- Taking the Fight Back to the Nine Tails
## ▲ Can I replay this content?
Absolutely, Orison Relief Support missions are replayable.
## ▲ When does Orison Relief Support run?
Orison Relief Support will run from now until October 7.
## ▲ What rewards can I earn?
Every Orison Relief mission you complete builds toward your points total. As that total climbs, you'll unlock set rewards along the way, so the more missions you run, the more you earn.
Rewards you can earn are:
- S-38 "SecondWind" Pistol
- FBL-8a "SecondWind" Light Armor
- P4-AR "SecondWind" Rifle
- ORC-mkX "SecondWind" Medium Armor
- Lumin V "SecondWind" SMG
- ADP-mk4 "SecondWind" Heavy Armor
## ▲ Where can I see my reward progress?
You can track your progress toward rewards in your Journal in your mobiGlas.
## ▲ Where can I see the rewards I earn?
Event rewards are attributed directly to your RSI Platform account and are viewable in the My Hangar section of the website. Every contribution to Orison's recovery, and every blow struck against the Nine Tails, will be remembered.
## ▲ Do I need a specific ship or equipment to participate in Orison Relief Support?
Requirements vary by mission. Some may need specific equipment or take place in hostile environments, while others, like Resource Gathering missions, call specifically for a Salvage ship. Be sure to heed the mission details so you know what to bring, or whether you'll need backup on standby.
