The answer for questions of this nature are usually going to be along the lines of "as many as we can possibly can while maintaining reasonable functionality of the game". One of the challenges for any company that is supporting a game that is hosted on servers that the company is paying for is that they need to be able to get their player population dense enough for the price of hosting the game to be a reasonable expense. As games evolve and new technologies are developed, that number may also change.
