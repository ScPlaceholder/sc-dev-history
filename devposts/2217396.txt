While I don't know much in particular about Orison, it's usually the case that different tasks will require work from specific teams, so one of those teams might get ahead on something further down the line. Other things that can happen:
- Someone has a higher priority task, but they're waiting on another team, so they're getting on with a lower-priority task until they're unblocked
- Production has spotted a long dependency chain between different teams, so they want to get the early dependencies done and prevent the case above from happening close to release
- The further-away task has some risks or uncertainties associated with it, so people are digging in early to make sure there aren't surprises in store
