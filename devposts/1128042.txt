I think there is a team that's managing those budgets.  I generally only hear about it if there's an issue.  I don't know, for example, how much processing load IFCS is allocated.  I do know that I'm supposed to aim for the greatest simulation fidelity possible, providing motion that's both smooth and stable under all reasonable conditions.  There are limitations on how much can be done there.  For example, the push for greater optimization is probably going to compromise stability, as 3.0 did for IFCS.  My current changes attempt to recover stability without compromising the gains we made on efficiency.  It's definitely a balance.  But I'm not aware of any rigid allocation of resources available to my systems.  Especially early on, it was all about "figure out a way to do this", and then later we'll figure out how to do it more efficiently.  "Later" hit my systems like a ton of bricks in 3.0, and I've been recovering since.
