Hi everyone,
The Server Meshing Tech Previews continue! Thanks to your help, we’ve made great progress towards our upcoming milestones.
We are currently running another Tech Preview playtest and would greatly appreciate your participation. This time, though, we’d love to see more screenshots of your adventures! Snap a shot, share it on the Community Hub, and we’ll pick our favorites to feature at CitizenCon 2954. We’re looking for stylistic screenshots with the themes of “Meshing in Action,” “Social & Community,” and “Cooperative Play.” Whether it’s a tram full of fun, a party at Wally’s, or an epic ship meet-up - let’s see what you’ve got!
## Before You Begin:
Head over to the Tech Preview post here for information on the playtest.
:grey_exclamation:NOTE: Screenshots should be submitted to the Community Hub and use “Server Meshing Tech Preview” as the post title.:grey_exclamation:
## Here’s how you participate:
- Playtest :desktop_computer:: Jump into the Tech Preview playtest while it is running.
- Snap :camera:: Take clear, high-quality screenshots with the following themes: “Meshing in Action,” “Social & Community,” or "Cooperative Play.”
- Submit :memo:: Upload your original creations on the Community Hub with the post title “Server Meshing Tech Preview.”
## Keep in Mind:
- Only screenshots featuring the Tech Preview Playtest will be considered.
- Screenshots should have the HUD turned off and depict one of the following themes: “Meshing in Action,” “Social & Community,” or “Cooperative Play.”
- Screenshots should have a minimum resolution of 3840 x 2160 (UHD) and ideally above 4096 x 2400 (4K).
- Screenshots should not be post-processed.
Thanks, everyone, and we’ll see you in-game (and hopefully in Manchester!)
