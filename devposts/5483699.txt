When a player purchases commodities those are owned by the player that purchases them. Anyone else trying to sell them will have to use a "No Questions Asked" terminal and they will not receive full price for anything that they are trying to sell. Even if I give a cargo container to a friend, they would have to sell it at a NQA terminal and they would not get full price for the commodity. GrimHex for example would be a location I could fence my stolen goods.
