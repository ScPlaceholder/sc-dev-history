## Feature Updates
Ship Availability & Pricing
- Basher is now available at Pyro Rundown Reststops
- Gatac Tyilui is now available from Astro Armada at Area18
- Both have also been added to the Arena Commander inventory
- aUEC increases for the Drake Ironclad, Drake Ironclad Assault, and Gatac Tyilui
Weapons
- Handling pass across the 4.10 weapon set to improve how they feel to shoot
Vanduul Incursion
- Introduced a new one-shot entity spawner for one-time contract rewards taken from spawned ships, now used by the Vanduul cryopod, which no longer behaves as though under gravity in space
- A blueprint for the LBCO Skarnov Rifle magazine has been created and added as a contract reward
- Vanduul now call out during combat
- AI at quantum stations engage without needing to be shot first
- Contract reputation now splits evenly across party members on shared contracts
- Board Ship contracts no longer place the mission where the player and their Caterpillar can be attacked on arrival
- Neutralize Target contracts no longer spawn an unmanned Polaris as the target
Insurance
- Ground vehicles and powersuits can now be recovered through insurance claims
