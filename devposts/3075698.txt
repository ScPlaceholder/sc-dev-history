I read Spectrum and Reddit multiple times a day. Sometimes even forwarding posts directly to my work email to chase up in the morning, be that with our embedded qa or to chat about with the others in my team or to raise with others I know to be working on said things.
I want to make another point. All devs have their own methods and channels we might feel more comfortable to interact through.
Personally, I very rarely respond directly to posts on either platform as it is very common that although I may very well have an opinion or some thoughts on the discussions or questions being asked, I am often not the authority on the given subjects.   As a dev I am well aware that my actions, writings and musings are going to carry much more weight, as every post I make has official connotations, therefore my comments need to be well considered and right if I'm going to voice them at all. As you can imagine it can be quite wrong and unhelpful for someone who doesn't know the inner workings or current thinking of a thing to hypothesise or speculate on it when their word could be taken as gospel. So, for me, I feel it's generally best all round to say nothing unless I know.
My point and the real take away here is there is no official mantra on where we should all interact with the community. Many of us just do a lot more watching, reading and listening and we don't all necessarily interact in all the same ways or via the same channels.
