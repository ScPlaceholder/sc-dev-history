Ship Escort Missions are a new mission archetype available in alpha patch 4.2.1, and we'd like to hear what you think! In these new missions, players will be tasked with fending off enemy ships whilst escorting friendly ships into a hangar around the asteroid mining bases, as well as escorting them out until they can QT away. This mission type comes in 6 difficulty levels under the Foxwell group in both Stanton and Pyro and awards Scrip upon completion.
We look forward to hearing your thoughts, and thanks for taking the time!
