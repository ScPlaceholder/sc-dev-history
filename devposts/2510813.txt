Hi there! Like the Xi'an thread before it, we welcome you to the Banu language group. We're finally here!
This area is intended to help anyone who wants to learn Banu, offer suggestions, help one another, or just hang out and chat about Banu things. The Banu language is a work in progress, and will develop alongside the Xi’an language as the 'verse expands. Please introduce yourself below! Let us all know what motivates you to learn the language, how you want to engage with others, or anything else on your mind. Are you more excited about learning to speak, or read and write, or both?! Learning a constructed language is not really any easier than learning a natural human language. It takes engagement, time, and lots of practice. We’re all in this together, so let’s keep it friendly and supportive.
