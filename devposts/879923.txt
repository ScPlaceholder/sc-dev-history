What I mean is, as an example, we'll pick a PC spec that we consider "medium", then we'll adjust a whole bunch of things so that a medium PC set to medium settings will see 60 FPS. The same process for high, low, ultra etc.
That said, the optimisation process will probably include a bit of levelling out - suppose one area were 50% slower than the rest of the game, for a 1% visual benefit, that's going to be a bad experience for a lot of people so the art team would probably try to bring it in line with everything else. Conversely, if there's an area that looks pretty good but has way better performance, they might see if they can pretty it up a bit further since the budget's there to spend.
