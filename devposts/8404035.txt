Yes. Given that screens generally wider than tall, and vary more in their horizontal dimensions than vertical, the solution is generally to have a fixed vertical FOV and then use the monitor(s) aspect ratio to show more or less on the horizontal axis, as required.
Obviously I can't claim to know how every other game did this. But it was common in many to simply  hard-code their aspect ratio (assuming 4:3, or 16:9). Then if your screen didn't match the aspect ratio, it was just stretched. The bigger the difference, the bigger the distortion.
