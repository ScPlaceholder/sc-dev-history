If your account is broke, my suggestion would be to contact the support team. Sometimes there are account specific issues that need to be handled on a case by case basis. In some situations they might know about workarounds that can help with the issue. In cases where that isn't possible, they can check to see if it's an issue we're already investigating or if it's a new problem. Having example accounts for new issues is super helpful so people contacting the support team when they can't log in is a big help.  We've worked on addressing a lot of the one's that we're aware of so far but I imagine there's still some that need our attention.
