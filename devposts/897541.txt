1. Just to clarify, a system does not need a Represented planet to be part of the UEE and only planets that are Represented (or eventually want to become Represented) need a Governors Council. If a planet does not want Representation they are free to govern themselves however they wish as long as they don't try to enact laws counter to the UEE's laws. So, to answer your question, Gurzil does not need a Governors Council.
2. Gurzil does not have a Senator in the system.  ---- However, it is possible that they could have a Senator without a planet later down the line. Even though it hasn't happened, a very large station or moon with a population that is contributing to the embetterment of the Empire could establish a Governors Council and eventually apply for Represented status.
3. The system is not represented by another external Senator. Rather it is simply expected to follow the full breadth of UEE Laws.
Hope that helps answer your questions and thanks for writing,
