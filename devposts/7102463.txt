Really I'm not very familiar with mutexes or the things that go with them. Most of the stuff at my end of the engine is, while multithreaded, quite a linear process. So we've got a lot of spawning jobs and waiting on fences for them to complete, then the results going into thread-safe containers to be processed serially elsewhere or fed to another set of jobs. We do have some read-lock/write-lock stuff but a lot of that is kept in very low level code to avoid safety issues.
