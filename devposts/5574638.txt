Right now there is not a good use case for manual aim axes as you can slave the gimbals to either your yaw / pitch inputs or your head look direction which covers the main manual gimbal use cases (you can use a keybind to swap the "source" over, it's either LALT or RALT + G ... one of them swaps the manual gimbal source, the other one freezes the manual gimbals in place, there is also an option about this in the game settings).
We will experiment with a couple of more manual gimbal sources (manual axes among them) in the future but right now there are more important things to do first.
