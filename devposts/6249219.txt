Male names generally consist of a single syllable, but two syllables are also not uncommon:
- Thl.oan
- R.ēth
- Kli’thla
- Ro'to
Female names are on the contrary in a two-syllable rising-pitch configuration, but sometimes also exist in a single syllable:
- N. il'ē (space added to break automated hyperlink)
- T. il'a (space added to break automated hyperlink)
- Āi
- Nē”
Note that names can vary a lot like you mentioned:
- Over fancy like "Emperor T.uēng Kr.ē, Beneficent and righteous caretaker of the Xi’an people and protector of all our worlds", Tao’yusao Kr.ē – Nya’t.ōng e p.uthle’a se Xi’an : Nya’sēng se Sao’teth e m.oa se nyo.āng”
- Or very simple such as "Master Professor Tai", Nya’to.y’anhyi T. ai (space added to break automated hyperlink)
