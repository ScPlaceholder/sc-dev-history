Google: Why are ichthyosaurs not dinosaurs?
The answer is simple: Taxonomy is based on ancestry rather than non-ancestral similarity. It's the difference between modern cladistics and outdated phenetics respectively. Thus, Ichthyosaurus is a reptile simply because it evolved from a reptile.
