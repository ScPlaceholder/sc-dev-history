It sounds like the issue is the facial tracking itself ... that part is not really under our control as the image recognition runs inside the Faceware libraries. 20 fps however are not really good for giving you a reliable experience. If you do not want to post a video you could reach out to Faceware and ask for pointers to improve the tracking quality. We recommend cameras with 60 or more fps at the used resolution. Turning off auto-exposure and other helpers that affect the framerate (in your webcam settings outside the game) might also help.
