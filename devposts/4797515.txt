Greetings Citizens!
This weekend on the PTU we are going to be running a full XenoThreat playtest! Since the last time you have played this we have made numerous bug and crash fixes that should greatly improve the experience! Let us know what you think!
Known Issues
- The Javelin sometimes cannot complete its QT transition from Jericho as it does not align with the wreck site (This may stop mission flow)
- Players will receive a friendly target warning after destroying Xenothreat hostile AI
- The Idris may spin uncontrollably
- AI vehicles will occasionally become unresponsive during poor server performance
- Idris will focus UEE ships despite the player being in close proximity
Instructions for the Test
The playtest will occur on the PTU servers (all regions) shortly after the new build has been released and run for the entire weekend through Monday. Due to the known issue above with the Javelin QT not always working, we may have to cut the playtest short if it becomes too much of an issue.
Thanks for playing and thank you for all the help!
