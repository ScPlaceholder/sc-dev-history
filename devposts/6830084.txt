We're happy to announce that Star Citizen Alpha 3.23: Adventure Beckons is now available to all backers! As one of our most expansive and game-changing releases to date, this patch adds life, excitement, and usability improvements throughout the vast Persistent Universe. From the introduction of the very first wildlife across Stanton’s planets to the Master Modes feature that streamlines vehicle control and ship combat, Alpha 3.23 elevates the Star Citizen universe to its most immersive and engaging height yet.
This release also marks a significant milestone toward the upcoming Pyro system, laying the groundwork and adding many of the front and back-end necessities that will empower players to leap to the new star system in the next patch release.
