We are making a lot of behind the scenes changes to Radar, Ping, & Scanning. So, I can help clarify the terminology for the immediate future.
- The Radar is responsible for passively finding and tracking contacts just by being turned on
- Ping actively finds additional contacts, or highlights volumes of space to investigate weak signatures
- Scanning is used to obtain information about a specific entity.
Scan requires a contact to be on the radar before it can unlock information about it. The scanner will have the ability to perform an active detection on the contact, so it will appear on your radar as you scan it.
- Passive detection is signature detection by just having the radar on.
- Active detection is signature detection requiring the player to perform an action like ping or scan. You can see contacts further out but that allows contacts to see you further out as well.
Capitol ships have stronger radars so their passive detection can see things much further out than a smaller ship - but it really depends how strong the ships radar/ping is.
To be stealthy, a player can use the following
- Be within the vicinity of a larger ship/entity or ship/entity emitting a stronger signal to mask your own
- Turn down your emissions enough to be hidden by ambient signatures ( including nearby emissions of stronger ships or asteroid fields or gas clouds )
You can still be found with a ping. The person doing the ping would light up like a light bulb, so you would know the hunt is on.
We are also working on a UI display to show your emissions versus the ambient emissions. This will help gauge how stealthy you are.
