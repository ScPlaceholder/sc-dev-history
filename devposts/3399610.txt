we're actually pretty well educated on modern age weapon systems due to both our own research and access to SMEs (... ahem ... and playing flight sims ofc :D).
However modern missile tech in all its depth is not something that translates easy into Star Citizen. While our game has very strong simulation aspects it does not want to be a study-level sim and thus we're taking our freedom when it comes to things like missile gameplay or specifics in how signatures work. We do necessarily not need to be realistic, but we're aiming for a high degree of believability and lore team will work out the science behind it.
Interestingly enough, the change that you cannot fire two separate missile types at the same time is actually grounded in the real world. Real missiles and other types of ordnance require vastly different methods from the pilots and gunners to employ them. E.g you'll not see an airframe launch both an AIM-9 and an AGM-65 at the same time as both missiles have entirely different purposes. Ultimately we also want that kind of difference in Star Citizen as well. A torpedo will not just be a slightly slower Ignite missile ... both will have their unique spaces in which they perform well and they will require their own methods to be successfully launched. Most of that is not in the scope of 3.11 of course. But forcing the pilot to focus on one missile type at a time is the first step in the process. In the future the missile operator mode will expand on that.
