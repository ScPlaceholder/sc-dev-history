Hi everyone,
Following the success of having Org Booths at CitizenCon 2948, we plan to replicate this opportunity for CitizenCon 2949, with some minor changes. The biggest difference is that this year we're rebranding to Community Booths - this is an effort to emphasize that you don't have to be an Organization to participate.
Community booths provide an opportunity for members of the Star Citizen community to represent in person at CitizenCon 2949. Whether you are representing your org, an event group, or a sub-community, we're expanding this year to allow all backers to make their presence felt in an official capacity at the event.
Details: https://robertsspaceindustries.com/citizencon/community-booths
We look forward to celebrating with you!
