@Barthez, different rotation speeds for turrets are already on our todo list. Our first pass in 3.10 only included turrets that the players can currently control. What rotation speed each turret will have in the end depends largely on what purpose we see for it. E.g. turrets with a focus on bringing down small fighters will naturally need to rotate faster than anti-capship turrets. So it's all in the works, just not done yet.
