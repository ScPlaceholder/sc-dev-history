As mentioned in https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/50259/thread/ships-manifest, the plan is to add more intuitive ways to track your cargo as well as being able to move 1SCU containers. Beyond that we'll be scaling cargo with the outposts and demands of the system, as new stations and locations are added expect new commodities and smarter trade routes to come up to meet it.
Internally, some of the biggest questions we'll be looking at answering is how we handle loading/unloading of cargo, particularly with cargo ships that store their cargo externally.
Tangentially, we'll be growing the cargo design to support other related systems, notably smuggling, piracy, salvage and mining. As they come online, expect cargo to grow to support them.
