I should point out that this isn't my photo. I had to throw away most of my game boxes a number of years ago because I ran out of space. The only ones I still have are for Elite, X-Wing, Wing Commander: Prophesy, and Flight Unlimited (and I've only now realised the pattern).
I guess Flight Unlimited is the odd-one-out, because it's not space-based. It was a great game though. It was a DOS game from 1995 that had an amazing flight model based on "real-time computational fluid dynamics." It felt so realistic to fly, including stresses on the airframe that could cause your plane to break up if manoeuvring too aggressively. It's a bit of a treasure, because apparently the flight model was incredibly hard to understand, so when the programmer responsible was fired after the first game, they couldn't understand it well enough to integrate into the sequels.
