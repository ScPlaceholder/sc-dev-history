## Edit: This AMA has ended, thanks so much for all your questions. We've got to get back to work, but we had a great time and hope you did too! Can't wait to share more.
Happy Wednesday, everyone!
Welcome to our AMA on the upcoming game mode, Theaters of War (working title).
In our monthly preview post, we let you know about our plan to open up testing to testers at home in at an upcoming weekend. In the past few weeks, ToW has run the gauntlet of internal testing with the CIG team and closed tests with the always helpful Evocati. We've gotten valuable feedback and loads of data from these tests, and we've been able to translate that data into actionable changes that will improve the overall game experience.
Since our last playtest two weeks ago, we implemented over 150 changes based on player feedback alone. The main priority for the team has been to improve the game performance, while further updates included adjustments to the Out of Bounds area size, ship match-ups, sight alignment, and fixes for VoIP issues, UI issues, and more.
We're looking forward to another round of Evocati testing and plan to implement more changes and then will look to additional playtesting with a larger audience in the near future, so stay tuned for updates.

## ____
Theaters of War is our new game mode that allows players to battle on foot, in vehicles, and in ships. Matches feature two teams, 20 UEE Marines and 20 Supreme Outlaws, that fight for victory in three fast-paced, action-filled phases:
Phase 1: Disable to the AA Turrets
Phase 2: Destroy the Radar Tower
Phase 3: Destroy the Mining Lazer
