The current implementation of trespass zones is temporary - we currently have to give a CrimeStat for security and defences there to turn hostile and put up a fight. We are working on a security system which, amongst many more things, will allow for players to trespass in a zone and only receive a CrimeStat if spotted/arrested/killed there, while being attacked by the defences.
In the future, having a certain level of reputation will allow players to enter some locations without repercussion, but not all so it's a risk you'll have to choose to take or not.
