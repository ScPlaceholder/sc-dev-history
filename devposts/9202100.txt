The orange/yellow notification usually means Spectrum has been updated, with bug fixes, new features, or other improvements. The red notification (error toast) can appear for several reasons. For example, a firewall may have blocked your action, or rate limiting may apply when too many requests are sent at once. It can also mean the server or the Spectrum client ran into an error, which may point to a bug on our side.
We monitor errors across all our web applications and work hard to fix known bugs. Some issues may remain until we rework the affected part of Spectrum, though.
That’s where your Issue Council reports really help: we review and prioritize them when planning quality-of-life and bug-fix releases.
