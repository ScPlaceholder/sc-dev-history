Hi everyone,
It's time to kick of the ultimate aerospace tournament with Ship Showdown 2951 - so get ready to champion your favorite Star Citizen vehicle!
Ship Showdown is all about making noise for your favorite flyable or driveable vehicle by submitting an original artistic creation to the Community Hub. The top 16 ships with the most compelling/numerous submissions will make it into the Ship Showdown finals, where they will go head-to-head to determine the community's ultimate Star Citizen vehicle.
Raise the profile of your favorite flyable ship or drivable vehicle by taking your creations to the Community Hub and Twitter. Be sure to use #SCShipShowdown and #StarCitizen to catch everyone's eye. Vote, like, and share your favorite creations on both platforms to secure your favorite vehicle among the top 16.
What kind of original creation can you submit? ANY kind! No, seriously. Write and sing an original song, make something out of Lego, take an epic in-game shot, take an epic out-of-game shot, make a trailer or a music video, heck – build the ship for real if you're up to it! Your submission can literally be anything, as long as it celebrates your favorite vehicle currently available to use in the 'verse.
In addition to helping secure your favorite vehicle a spot in the finals, we're handing out ten Origin 100is with LTI for submissions we feel are particularly outstanding!
Last but certainly not least, there will be extra surprises for the vehicles that make it into the final four.

See you in the 'verse!
