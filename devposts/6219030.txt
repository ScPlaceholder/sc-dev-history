Thanks for your question! As stated in the original contest announcement, we're challenging you to design advertisements of
any product that already exists in the 'verse except for vehicles not made by MISC or its sub-brand, Mirai.
This means that you can design an advertisement for a Whamburger, Carnita-ritas Burrito, or even a P6-LR Sniper Rifle, for example. It doesn't have to be of the Hull C. As long as the product you're advertising is already in the game and you use one of the templates provided, you're good to go. You can also use in-game screenshots of your chosen product to help design your ad.
Hope this helps! :slightly_smiling_face:
