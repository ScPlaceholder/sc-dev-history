Welcome, citizens! Welcome to the 2954 Hangar Games!
The newly introduced ⁠Personal Persistent Hangars allow for uninterrupted management of cargo, inventory, and even vehicles: A perfect way to start your very own player housing! These hangars can be found at a player's home location, and allow for customization and organization of the hangar interior.
## How To Participate
Alpha 3.24 sees the release of the Personal Persistent Hangars, an eagerly awaited feature in the persistent universe. It is a chance for players to celebrate the features, showcase their creativity, and earn rewards for the most amazing entries. Decorate your hangar with everything that you can find in the 'verse, and make it your very own! Send us your best screenshots for a chance to earn a beautiful ship!
Please read the ⁠⁠⁠contest rules carefully, to avoid disqualification.
- :hammer: Prepare: In your Home location, go to your Personal and Instanced Hangar.
- :art: Decorate: Trinkets, posters, vehicles, ships, and anything that you can drop in your hangar will stay there, so go wild!
- :camera_with_flash: Snap & Record: Take a clear, high-quality photo of your beloved new house
- :outbox_tray: Submit: Send your original creations to the ⁠Community Hub with the title "Hangar Games 2024" followed by your title, such as "Hangar Games 2024 - The odds and ends are in my favor".
## Keep In Mind!
Read the ⁠⁠⁠contest rules.
- Use "Hangar Games 2954 Contest" as part of the title for your submission on the ⁠Community Hub.
- Multiple submissions are not allowed.
- Post-processing effects are not allowed.
- Group submissions are not allowed.
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your submissions on the following:
- Quality
- Creativity
- Originality
- Rule Adherence
## Dates
The submission period ends on Wednesday, September 18, 2024 at 11:59 PM (Pacific) / Thursday, September 19, 2024 at 6:59 AM (UTC).
