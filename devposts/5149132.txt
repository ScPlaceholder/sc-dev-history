Hi there,
while not always perfect replicas of their real world counterpart, a lot of our vegetation is obviously inspired by them.
The puffy white flowers would be based on Eriophorum (Cotton Grass).
The leafy bushes are loosely based on Myrica (Bayberry).
The lower purple ones are Calluna (Common Heather) based.
The tall purple ones are based on Chamaenerion angustifolium (Fireweed).
For the ferns I dont think there's one specific type that was the main inspiration, so I'd suggest to pick whatever grows well where you want to plant them.
Keep us updated how it goes, would be nice to see the results :slightly_smiling_face:.
