## Can You Save Stanton?
With Stanton under assault by system-wide pirate attacks and the local authorities flummoxed at every turn, the people need heroes willing to put it all on the line to defend their home.
Stave off the pirate menace and get to the bottom of the mysterious wave of violence before the stakes get even higher. Do you have what it takes to save Stanton?
:trophy: Become a hero! Confront the ruthless pirates terrorizing the Stanton system, defend its people, and (if you complete the whole event before 4.0 goes live) exclusive permanent in-game ship weapons will be yours!
- x2 S2 Pyro Laser Repeaters
- x2 S3 Pyro Laser Repeaters
To get started, accept the ‘⁠⁠Save Stanton’ priority contract in your mobiGlas when available. Missions will update as the story evolves, so stay alert and keep checking back.
This FAQ, the live game, and the main website will update as the story unfolds.
There will also be a Journal Entry to track your progress throughout the event called 'Save Stanton Mission Status' along with narrative journal entries to touch on what's happening in each phase.

Started on Wednesday, November 27
The recent attacks in Stanton have ignited a movement to clean out the criminal element in Pyro. Citizens for Prosperity is hoping for a peaceful solution, while the Frontier Fighters militant group wants it done by force. Both groups need a hand before they jump:

## FAQ
▲ What is this event all about?
► Stanton is under threat, and that's all we're willing to say! Read the contracts and scavenge locations for hidden clues about why all of this is happening.
▲ Will I be able to join the mission series late and still participate?
► Yes! Similar to the Overdrive Initiative, earlier phases can still be played until the event concludes. However, we'll disable this mission as soon as 4.0 drops to Live. Keep an eye on your Contracts Manager in the Priority tab to pick up each event.
▲ Will you run this event again in the future?
► No, this is a narrative experience and will only run once! However, the new mission archetypes created for this event can be used again in future releases.
▲ Are there any other rewards for completing the event?
► Yes! We have many permanent rewards for this planned. However, to get every reward, you have to play all of it and finish it BEFORE 4.0 goes Live.
▲ How will I know that my progress is complete before Phase One ends?
► We track your progress in a Journal Entry titled 'Save Stanton Mission Status.' (See image below.)
If you have any further questions, let us know.
