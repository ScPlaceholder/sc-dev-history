That's a very high level version, there's a few more that goes into balancing a sniper rifle.
Scapel VS P6LR can be boiled down to one is for further distances meaning the Scapel handles quicker with it's aim time but has a worse damage falloff profile them the P6LR which is for longer ranges
The bread and butter for snipers is mainly
- ADS time
- Projectile speed
- Damage fall off
- Weapon specific balancing (Atzkav AOE, Arrowhead Charge)
- Does it 1HK just light / medium, since damage profiles massively change with even a basic injury
