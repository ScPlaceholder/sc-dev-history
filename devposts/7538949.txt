Greetings!
We have begun testing instanced hangar spawning, which allows players who have completed (or skipped) the NPE mission to get faster into space by spawning in their own property. This is not a temporary change to avoid transit. You will also not spawn in rental hangars, so if you park in a different LZ or station you will wake up in a Hab. This will impact every player, and we'd like to hear what you think of these changes along with any suggestions you may have to improve the experience.  Please let us know!
Thanks for taking the time, and we look forward to hearing your thoughts!
