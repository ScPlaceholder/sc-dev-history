Hello everyone,
Love is in the air as Coramor 2951 approaches in the 'verse!
To celebrate, we're giving you the opportunity to connect with those you care about and spread some Star Citizen themed love for prizes.
Before you ask, yes, Coramor isn't actually for a couple of weeks... but it was important to us that we kick off this contest early, so that when the magical day arrives, we'll all have plenty of choices for what to send our dear co-pilots.
Welcome to the Coramor 2951 Card Contest!
Before you start creating, please read the contest rules carefully and only post your entrees to the contest submission thread.
Any submissions not posted to the 'Coramor 2951 Contest Submission Thread' are invalid.
Let's get started!
Show us that your heart is on fire by creating a Star Citizen or Coramor themed Valentine's Day card.
Paper, glitter, glue, or Photoshop, the only limits are your creativity (and that a reasonable person would consider your creation a 'card').
## Rules
- Read the contest rules
- Post your card to the Spectrum submission thread only
- Create a Star Citizen themed Valentine's card or a Coramor-themed card
- Entrees can be digital or physical and created any way you want
- Each image of a card is considered a single entry, but you are allowed multiple entries and can include multiple greetings cards in a single post
The CIG jury will judge your cards on:
- Quality
- Style
- Rule adherence
- Creativity
## Dates
The submission period ends on February 11, 2021 (EOD 11:59 PM Pacific)
