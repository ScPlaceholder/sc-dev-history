We have added new attachments for the barrel and under-barrel of FPS weapons, available in shops that sell compatible goods. The attachments include flashlights, laser sights (both activated with the "U" key), suppressors, flash hiders, compensators, and energy stabilizers. Give these new attachments a try in both the PU for FPS missions as well as Star Marine (FPS missions have updated AI and Star Marine has a brand new elimination map).
As always, this thread is just for feedback on the look and function of these attachments. Any and all bugs go to the issue council. Thanks for your input!
