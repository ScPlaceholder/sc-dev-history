## Relay rework clarification
Relays are still the connectors of all ship components, but if you lose all the fuses inside items will not lose the connection anymore. The reason here is that some ships are just build in a way that the relay that connects all items or is critical are just too easy of a target. Hence we did decide to rework the system.
What happens now is that each relay has still fuses inside. Once all fuses are destroyed or depleted, the relay will go into a "red" state.
The "red" state of the relay means that it will block some power.
This means:
If you have lost a relay (by it not having any fuses anymore) the available power in the ship will drop.
Depending on the ship size and the amount of relays present that can have a significant impact on your ship. So they are still an important factor to be taken care of.
