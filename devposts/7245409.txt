At the moment with these tests we are validating the low level tech. Our objective is to go as high we can from the technology side with the current architecture to help us decide on a starting player cap.
The parameters we are looking at are latency, stalls, throughput/bandwidth and interaction delay events. Those all need to be within acceptable parameters.
With regards to 4.0 we will take an informed decision on player caps closer to release. For this decision we will reconcile the performance of the tech with the elements of the game that are affected from having larger shards. i.e. can we spawn this many players with them having wait? Are wait times and queues at gateways acceptable?
We will settle with a starting cap that maximizes having a good experience with the game and Pyro, we are not chasing a player cap.
