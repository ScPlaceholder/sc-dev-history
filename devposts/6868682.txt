Regarding tuning down the AI for 3.23.1, what we've actually done for .1 patch is to reduce a bit the accuracy for AI ships found in early level missions, so new players have a chance to learn the mechanics without being punished. However our long term plan for future patches is to work with the Mission teams to look at each combat encounter and scale them appropriately based on the expected difficulty of the mission.
