I use Joystick Gremlin and VJoy (along with HIDHide).  VJoy creates a virtual joystick (or several) and HIDHide hides your actual joysticks from other applications (like SC). Then Gremlin maps buttons from your physical devices onto the virtual joysticks.
It works well, and empowers my favourite SC game mode: spending hours hunting for the perfect controller setup! :heart_eyes:
Note: These are all third-party programs, for you to use (or not) at your own risk.
