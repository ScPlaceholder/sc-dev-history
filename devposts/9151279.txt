For this weekend's PTU build we are folding in the TTK balancing updates! This is just a test for the weekend and is not intended to arrive with 4.10.2 but this will be a great testbed for this!
For this fourth TTK balancing update our main objective is to deliver a fixed and refined version of the previous:
- Shields have been adjusted to intended values, this should bring the TTK down again
- Ballistic DMG has been decreased by 20% compared to TP3
- AD4B, AD5B, Deadbold IV and Deadbolds V Aplha DMG has been provisionally nerfed by an additional 20% as their they have been called out as OP and impacted feedback
- We looked closer into shield and health values of large and capital ships and adjusting their values generally they increased to match them closer to the 2/3 shield, 1/3 Armor and critical parts goal.
- Energy and Ballistic Deflection has been adjusted.
- AI Modifier has been decreased.
Further details can be found in the patch notes for tonights publish.
