Thanks for bringing up these issues. I can assure you that we take these reports seriously, and we're actively working to address as many as we can. We have dedicated internal discussions focused solely on glitches and exploits, and we truly appreciate when players reach out privately with detailed reports about important exploits—especially those that include reproduction steps or videos. Once we have enough information, these are escalated to the relevant teams to investigate and resolve.
Here’s a quick update on some of the points you mentioned:
- Box Glitching: We're aware of it. Some fixes have already been rolled out, but more work is ongoing.
- aUEC Exploits: Our team monitors for abnormal aUEC activity. If you spot anything that seems off, mission rewards, bounties, etc, please share it with us.
- 8 SCU Containers: A fix is on the way—possibly already live in the PTU. We're currently confirming the fix.
- Invulnerable/Invisible Players: This is tied to known issues with character states and is being tracked internally.
Since the game is still in Alpha, bugs and exploits are unfortunately part of the process. While we welcome reports, we discourage sharing exploits publicly, as this can lead to abuse. Testing an exploit to help report it is appreciated—but repeatedly using one for personal advantage isn't.
Thanks again for your support and vigilance! :slightly_smiling_face:
