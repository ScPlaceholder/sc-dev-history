The Red Festival is a celebration of renewal and remembrance with roots in old Earth customs that marked the end of a 'lunisolar year,' an ancient calendar system based on Luna's (Sol 3a) cycles. Celebrants honor the festival with a variety of traditions that differ greatly depending on the system and community participating. Common activities include wearing red (a color that symbolizes good fortune), honoring friends and relatives by sharing stories, eating foods long in length, such as calossk tentacles or dyed-red noodles, and exchanging gilded red envelopes representing good fortune to come.
To ring in a prosperous Year of the Snake here on Earth in 2025, and Year of the Pig in 2955, we’re inviting you to celebrate the Red Festival with us. As is tradition across the UEE, red envelopes have been hidden throughout Stanton. Alongside this, a red-hot referral bonus, a Free Fly event, an Arena Commander takeover, and a selection of auspicious ship paints are ready to bring good fortune in the year ahead.
