Today’s launch of Star Citizen Alpha 3.16 marks the exciting return of the PvP event Jumptown 2.0.
Inspired by the historic, player-driven events of Jumptown on the icy moon of Yela this dynamic event will see both lawful and criminal players battle over control of valuable drug crates at various outposts in the Stanton system. Expect large-scale space battles as players team up or fly solo to secure the drugs or deliver them to criminal hands. resulting in a large scale battlefield as players battle for control over these outposts.
The community will compete to create a video showing their best attempt at a Jumptown heist. The drug lab of Jumptown has seen many a battle, so many battles that the proprietors decided to fortify the building considerably. You can find out more about Jumptown 2.0 and a detailed schedule on this page.
You have until 11:59 PM Pacific on January 16th, 2022 (EOD 11:59 PM Pacific) to submit your video for a chance to win one of the prizes listed below.
Ready to create? Read up on the rules, then start rolling and post your finished video in the Spectrum submissions thread, or on Twitter using the hashtags: #StarCitizen and #JumptownReturns.
Prizes
Three winners will be selected. If you work on the video with 4 friends (5 collaborators max), all will receive the reward.
:first_place_medal:1st Place –Drake Cutlass Steel in-game vehicle with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance)
:second_place_medal:2nd Place –Tumbril Nova Tank in-game vehicle with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance)
:third_place_medal:3rd Place – Anvil Spartan in-game vehicle with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance)
Notes
- Video must not exceed 2 minutes
- Submissions must be at least in 1920x1080 resolution and uploaded to YouTube
- Multiple submissions are NOT permitted
- No more than 5 collaborators on a single video
- Submissions should clearly state all individuals involved in creating their commercial video at the time of submission
- No unoriginal music (Star Citizen music is OK)
Good luck everyone! We’re looking forward to seeing your heist videos!
