There are still countries on the individual planets although they are called States. Although the UEE doesn't require planets to adhere to this system, most of them adopt a political structure where each of these States is run by a Governor and they convene in the Governors Council to talk over planetary issues. Again, the idea was that the Governor system lends itself the best to Recognition from the Empire, but it's not required, so they could conceivably have any government that the people wanted as long as they adhered the Common Laws that every planet within the UEE needs to adhere to.
