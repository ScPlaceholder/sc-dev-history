Heya, in 3.9 you'll be able to gimbal your weapons with your head again if you set it up like that (as long as you are in manual gimbal mode). Essentially you have the ability to tell your ship if its weapons should follow the Vjoy position (which is effectively where the mouse cursor points during flying) or the head direction (does not matter if it is head tracking or free look). There is also an option and a keybind to swap the slaving if necessary. Do not expect that to be a game changer for combat though ... independent aiming with your head is very hard and there is no aim assisting during manual gimbal mode.
Atm we only support head tracking via FOIP and TrackIR. We had a couple of requests to support Tobii Eye Trackers but at the moment we do not have the resources to make a proper implementation.
