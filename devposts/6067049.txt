As mentioned before the Ares weapons will get a small overhaul once we move the new aiming / gunnery system over from S42. However do not expect a large amount of aim assist for it. Balance wise we see S7s to be employed against large or static targets, not against small and agile fighters ... thus the amount of aim assist will likely not change. On the plus side the new aiming system produces better PIP indications so it is actually easier to hit your targets.
