We have begun testing Item Recovery and Snapshotting! By default, when your ship is destroyed and you file a claim, you will receive the base loadout for that vehicle.
This loadout is defined by either: Your RSI web hangar configuration or the default in-game vehicle configuration. You can save the loadout current ship loadout using the camera icon in the ASOP. When going to claim back this vehicle, you will see an option to claim back the loadout you saved.
A feedback survey is available at: Item Recovery Survey Form. This survey will be open until May 7.
We look forward to reading your feedback!
