## Question Guidelines
- Please read your fellow citizens' questions first, as someone might've already asked your question.
- Post only ONE question at a time. Replies with more than one question will be removed.
- You may post as many questions as you'd like, as long as they are in individual replies to the original post.
- Only post replies to the original post. This is for easy voting and sorting.
- This thread is not for conversation, nested or otherwise. Replies to submitted questions will be removed.
