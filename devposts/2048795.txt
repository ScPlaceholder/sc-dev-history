Sorry to hear you feel this way but I just wanted to take a few moments to discuss why these things get said and what it means in the context of your posts and the subsequent replies.
Contrary to popular belief I'm not the sole arbiter of ship creation, design and balance in the game, nor do I have a vendetta or crusade against Drake. I may be a part of the process but there are plenty of people above me (not just CR) giving direction to how they envisage the game to play out and what their requirements for vehicles are and my job is to fulfill their requirements not push a personal agenda. In terms of being the face (and not other staff members) for a lot of these videos or Q&A's that's a combination of a few factors:
- For concept reveals we tend to only film the actual designer of the ship and the concept artist
- If the concept artist we use are external contractors then we'll traditionally use Paul Jones as he manages them
- If the designer does not want to appear on camera (we don't force people to do this and a lot of them dont want to do it) then I'll step in to cover them. For the Corsair the actual designer of the ship had left the company a month prior to the release so whilst he managed to record some footage for ATV, being on RTV and doing the Q&A was not possible.
- For a ship being released as flight ready, we'll get the actual production artists/designers on it which may not be Elwin or Coons, if they or their teams didn't work on it then there is little they can add. The Anniversary sale videos were just something different we tried and given their history on the ships is why they took part.
- I will happily take the flak on any touchy/risque subjects to protect the team from it, be it designers, artists or others. Putting a Junior Designer or Artist on camera to answer questions about mechanics they have little knowledge of is unfair and has caused lots of issues in the past.
Whether you (or I) describe them as paper thin, lightweight, underarmoured, industrial vs military it all is the same thing at the end of the day in terms of where those ships stand versus others. Perhaps its a British phrase that doesn't translate well as someone replied to in the comments, describing something as paper thin doesn't literally mean as thin as paper it just means relatively less compared to another. Drake ships in general  have less armour, more agility, more guns than comparable other ships. Every manufacturer has its outliers so you cannot compare the entire fleet against another manufacturer 1:1 as a dedicated Salvage ship will not fit that description nicely for example.
In terms of Lore, the lore is evolving just like the game design. Back in the original pitches the quantities of vehicles the game was originally planned around required them to have quite different roles but as it has expanded over time and as such adjustments need to be made, same for item/loadouts. Whilst we never want to do a complete 180 on the role of a ship (at least without good reason and forewarning) some of their attributes have to naturally change over time and until we near a more final state all stats are subject to change, hence why ships gain/lose items over various patches.
