All objects in the game are positioned in some cartesian space, with the axes aligned to the zone that they're in. The Z axis of the root zone of a solar system is aligned to the ecliptic plane's normal (or, I guess, the ecliptic plane is aligned to the root zone), all the other zones' orientations are defined relative to the zone that contains them. Unlike the equatorial system, planet zones rotate with the planet, so that static features on the surface of the planet will always have the same zone-space coordinates.
