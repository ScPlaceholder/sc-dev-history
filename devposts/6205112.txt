Ahoy, ye land lubbers!
You think you got what it takes to be a pirate during Pirate Week? We're challenging you to fill up your tankard with your favorite grog (or any in-game beverage) and make your way to the bane of ne'er-do-wells everywhere, a certain correctional facility on Aberdeen. Yer ol matey Jake here has provided a map to light your way.
Take a screenshot at either location marked on the map with drink in hand for a chance to win a fine vessel to sail the stars with. The most creative, original, and piratey screenshots will take home the booty!
## Before You Begin:
Before you start on your submissions, please read the contest rules carefully and remember to post your entries to the Community Hub or on Twitter and NOT as a reply to this thread. Also: Any submission posted to the Community Hub without '2023 Pirate Week Treasure Hunt' in the post title will not be considered.
## Keep in Mind:
- Read the contest rules!
- Only post screenshots at the locations marked on the map below, with an in-game beverage of your choice in hand. Any other subject will be disqualified.
- Please post submissions to the Community Hub or to the contest announcement post on Twitter (don't forget to include #StarCitizen in your post). Replies to this thread will NOT count as a submission.
- Each screenshot uploaded to the Community Hub is considered a single entry. However, you are allowed multiple entries and can upload the entries as a gallery on the Community Hub.
- Use '2023 Pirate Week Treasure Hunt' as the title for your submission on the Community Hub, and select the screenshot category for it to be eligible.
- Group submissions are NOT allowed. You may include your friends in the screenshot, but only the person submitting the entry is qualified to win.
- Images that include player handles will NOT qualify.
- Post-processing effects are NOT allowed. This includes the use of third-party programs/applications like Photoshop, Reshade, etc.
- Consider turning off the HUD when taking screenshots, as this might make for better composition.
- You are limited to winning one prize, so you cannot win on multiple platforms (Community Hub, Twitter).
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your entries based on:
- Originality
- Creativity
- Rule adherence
## Dates
The submission period will end on Wednesday, September 20, 11:59 PM Pacific.
