I'd forgotten there was that one PC TBR card, way back. Based on what I remember from doing mobile dev, TBR cards traditionally had drastically different things that would completely wreck their performance, basically they were great at single-pass forward rendering but anything that involved copying output back to input caused stalls, but more recent cards just announced that they'd thrown TBR in there without giving much guidance on what to avoid doing, so it's kind of a black box in that case.
In terms of difficulty I'd argue that while there are more things that we do, and need techniques for, it's balanced by a reduction in the amount of really annoying difficulty that there used to be (mostly before my time) surrounding patchy hardware support for basic features, hardware that misreported its own capabilities, etc, so I'll take this over that :slightly_smiling_face:
