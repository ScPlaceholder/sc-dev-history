I think that's what's going on, yes. Area lights in games are something that's improved a lot in recent years, but area shadows are still a very different proposition. There is an upcoming feature to do long-distance landscape shadows in their own special format, which means not having to solve the general case, I'm guessing they'll have some kind of penumbra calculation which might improve this, but I can't remember what was described. Even so, even a very good shadow system would be an approximation, since what you'd get is the illumination of an un-shadowed sun multiplied by the percentage that's visible, rather than the illumination that that percentage would give you - so in this case the light would be coming in from a lower angle than you'd expect, and the reflections on smooth surfaces would still be circular.
The other lighting in the scene during the sunrise is ambient light from the sky-glow. Up close it's captured in an environment map, further away it's approximated analytically. The flatness of that lighting might be improved by better large-scale ambient occlusion, though I don't believe such a thing is currently on our roadmap.
