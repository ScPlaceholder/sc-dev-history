As @Wayne-CIG says, I wouldn't rely on Reddit for getting bugs resolved. Even when we find issues on Reddit or Twitch or Discord channels etc, we still need an existing IC or Jira to resolve the issue against. What doesn't get seen behind the scenes is that usually when an issue like this is discovered on another platform (or even on Spectrum), the devs usually reach out to our Live QA guys to see if we have a ticket they can use. I know it can seem like IC can be futile at times but it's a massive part of our day-to-day internally. We have a large (and relatively new) team who are now fully committed to ingesting IC reports, reinforcing them and then getting them in front of the right people, as well as guys who are dedicated to improving the IC pipeline, so things will continue to improve in that regard. I'd certainly not worry about devs being proactive in tackling things as they see them in the wild though, it always comes from a place of wanting the game to be better :slightly_smiling_face:
