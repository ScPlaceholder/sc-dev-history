The 4.1.1 patch brings new Ship Battle missions which are focused around a set of missions featuring ship combat. This feedback thread is for the Patrol missions, which take the player through a series of patrol points. Patrol missions will be offered by Foxwell Enforcement (Legal) and the Head Hunters (Illegal). At each point a semi-random event occurs and escalating ship archetypes may appear depending on mission difficulty and previous events. Please let us know what you think about these new missions!
