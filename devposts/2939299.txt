Predicting what kind of CPU the server will run on in the future isn't going to be doable right now. As the game evolves, we look at the different performance metrics of the various components that make up the entirety of the backend and, when needed, adjust things based on their needs. In addition to that, there's always a chance that something new might come out that is better suited in comparison to what's currently available and in the event that this occurs, we'd need to evaluate if it is, in fact, a better match for our use case.
