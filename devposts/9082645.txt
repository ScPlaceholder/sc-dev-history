## Bug Fixes & Technical
- Fixed 1 Server Crash
- Universal corpse cleanup: Corpses in tonight's PTU build will cleanup on a set 10 minute timer instead of not cleaning up at all. This is currently an aggressive timer for testing and is global to all AI (includes bosses and ship pilot corpses). Let us know how much this short timer is doing as we will be looking for feedback!
- More instance performance improvements, entity cleanup, and fixes
- Particle effects across the game updated to stream textures correctly, reducing memory overhead from effects that were previously loaded as static non-streamable assets
- Further instance handling improvements
- Potential Fix: All active Siege of Orison instances simultaneously collapsing and becoming unphysicalized, causing all players inside to fall to their deaths
- Potential Fix: Players crossing from the dogleg transition instance into the main Siege of Orison instance not transitioning correctly on the server side, causing desync and damage when moving through the barrier
- Potential Fix: Siege of Orison Command Center entrance airlock not resetting after a player died and respawned inside it, permanently blocking other players from entering
- Potential Fix: Battaglia Story Mission 2 "Emergency: Blinding Hope in Trouble" not appearing in the mobiGlas after completing Story Mission 1
- Potential Fix: Collision missing at the Battaglia office exit in Levski which could cause players to fall through the floor when leaving
- Potential Fix: Siege of Orison dogleg staircase missing collision after transitioning to the main instance, causing players to fall through
- Potential Fix: Loot boxes not spawning at multiple locations
- Potential Fix: Freight elevator platform getting permanently stuck showing "Transferring..." after lowering mission cargo and then attempting to raise it again
- Potential Fix: Players able to clip through pinched collision proxies in various locations due to an aggressive tunnelling prevention algorithm; the tunnelling prevention logic has been disabled pending a rework
- Potential Fix: Refueling missions showing 0 SCU of hydrogen required in the contract description for some Rank 3 Urgent Convoy Refuel missions
- Potential Fix: Battaglia Story Mission 2 hook audio line playing when accepting the mission instead of the correct mission accept line
