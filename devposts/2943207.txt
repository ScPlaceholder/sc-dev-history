Greetings!
We have released the Prowler for all owners to fly and try out!
The Prowler is an unusual design and style of ship, so put it through the paces. Do missions, multicrew, fill the ship up, and see how it functions. The Prowler functions a bit differently than other dropships in that it can boost quite well, has a somewhat stealthy signature read, and keeps its engine trail visibility low for a low, quick in and out style profile. Although the guns are beefy, this ship is not meant for dogfighting and is more fragile than other drop ships, preferring to rely on quiet entry.
Currently the Grav-Lev features on the Prowler are not implemented in 3.9 and will be coming at a later date.
Let us know what you think below and use the IC tag "Esperia Prowler" for any bugs/issues.
