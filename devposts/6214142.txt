Unfortunately this is a common missconception... HDR is about increased range, not increased brightness.
When you run with HDR disabled, you are actually extending the narrow SDR content up to the full 550 nits of your monitor... so all white's are now super bright, whether that be paper, snow, or the sun. When you enable HDR you are seeing a greater range of intensities, but this by definition paper (ref) white must be lower than your max of 550 nits, otherwise it's not a larger dynamic range.
So if you just want a very bright image, use SDR (or use HDR with a higher ref-white, but once you hit 550 nits its going to be identical to SDR).
