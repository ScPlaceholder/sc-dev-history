We’ve answered your questions in the latest Kruger L-21 Wolf's Q&A.
Will the fin affect the ship’s aerodynamics? Can it be toggled using the VTOL keybind?
The fin in the initial release is tied to the landing gear state only, we are investigating player control options to allow it to be manually toggled in a future patch. It currently does not dynamically impact aerodynamic handling, though when the Control Surfaces feature hits the PU you can expect it to have an impact in atmospheric yaw ability.
