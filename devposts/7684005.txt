The annual Red Festival is here! Alongside the traditional festivities of wearing red and gold, feasting on dyed red noodles (or calossk tentacles!), and hunting for hidden red envelopes across the 'verse, this year, we're inviting you to embrace the spirit of Red Festival by enveloping the 'verse in kindness.
## Welcome to the '2955 Envelop the 'Verse in Kindness Screenshot Contest'!
Acts of kindness can take many forms, from helping a stranded pilot, delivering essential supplies, and sharing your resources with those in need to organizing community events that bring people together. Every act of kindness, no matter how small, leaves a lasting impact and contributes to a larger wave of goodwill that can transform the 'verse. This is your chance to help shape it into a more welcoming and compassionate place.
Perform acts of kindness in the 'verse, capture these moments in a stunning screenshot, and inspire others to pay the kindness forward. Who knows, maybe good fortune will find its way back to you! Don't forget to include this year's Red Festival envelope in the shot; 2955 is the Year of the Pig, and the pig is a symbol of wealth and good fortune, after all!
## Before You Begin
Before you start on your submissions, please read the contest rules carefully and remember to post your entries on the Community Hub and not as a reply to this thread.
:exclamation: Any submission not posted to the Community Hub or without 'Envelop the 'Verse in Kindness Screenshot Contest' in the post title will not be considered. :exclamation:
## Keep In Mind
- Read the contest rules! :book:
- Please post your screenshot to the Red Festival 2955 event page on the Community Hub. Replies to this thread will NOT count as a submission.
- Use 'Envelop the 'Verse in Kindness Screenshot Contest' as the title for your submission and select the 'screenshot' category.
- Each screenshot uploaded to the Community Hub is considered a single entry. However, you are allowed multiple entries and can include multiple images in a single post.
- Screenshots must feature an image of a player performing an act of kindness in the 'verse and include a 2955 Red Festival envelope somewhere in the frame.
- Post-processing effects are NOT allowed. This includes third-party add-ons like Photoshop, Reshade, etc.
- Group submissions are NOT allowed. Only the person posting the screenshot can win a prize.
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your submissions on:
- Quality
- Creativity
- Rule adherence
- Aesthetic composition
