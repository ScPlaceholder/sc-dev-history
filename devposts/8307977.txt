CitizenCon Direct 2955 is less than two weeks away, and the countdown is officially on!
## Watch Parties
We’ll be gathering together on Twitch and YouTube on Saturday, October 11 at 1900 UTC, and while we wish we could all share the same room this year, we’re thrilled to be connecting through nine official Watch Parties across the globe with countless more community-hosted events happening everywhere. Many of these are already at capacity, but it’s still worth checking to see if there’s one in your area!
At the events we’re attending, we're bringing along exclusive physical loot and Bar Citizen World Tour scratch-off cards, so come out, say hi, grab some goodies, and talk ship. You can see the list of events we’re attending here, and discover even more at barcitizen.sc.
## Digital Goodies Pack
Pick up this year’s new collection of thirteen in-game wearables, weapons, décor, and collectibles. Includes two new armor sets, an exclusive flight suit, a bundle of new weapons and more, all alongside the classics like our CitizenCon challenge coin and trophy to add to your collection.
The full list of digital goodies will be unveiled at CitizenCon Direct 2955, with all items being delivered in upcoming patches.
## Discounted CitizenCon Starter Package: Duelist ‘CitizenCon’ Edition
To celebrate CitizenCon, we’ve put together a special discounted starter package designed for new pilots ready to begin their journey. This limited-time bundle includes everything you need to get started — a versatile starter ship, a unique ship paint, an exclusive armor and weapon set, and more.
Whether you’re setting out for the first time or helping a friend join the adventure, we’ll see you at CitizenCon Direct!

Submissions are now closed and our team is reviewing entries you’ve sent in. We want to extend a heartfelt thank you to everyone who participated. The sheer number of submissions and the creativity and passion behind them blew us away.
