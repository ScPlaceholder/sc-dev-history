If the NPCs, etc. feel too small you likely have a wrong IPD value set. IPD describes the distance between your eyes. Since most headsets provide that value based on eye tracking or sliders, we just offer a scale value to modify it. Make sure that in the settings the IPD scale is set to 1.0 and then start adjusting things up and down from there until the sizes feel right.
If the IPD too large, then characters, etc. will look too small and vice versa.
