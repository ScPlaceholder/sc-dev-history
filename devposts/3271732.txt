@EnerGI, cheers for the feedback. We are aware that just as space combat the racing parts of the game have devolved over the last couple of years. This is of course not intended but a consequence of working on the complex beast that Star Citizen is. Since the beginning of this year we put a very strong focus on the core flight and combat experience of Star Citizen. In later quarters we will be specifically working on extensions for the FM and thruster models ... and those changes will greatly benefit the racing parts of the game. The exhaustion model of the g-forces will also change over time which will then allow you to on ride the edge of unconsciousness again. It's all already planned up but it will take a while until we get there.
I know it's not much of help right now ... but as I said we know about it and we ultimately want to make it more fun again.
