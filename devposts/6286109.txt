Greetings Citizens!
We are TWO DAYS out from our biggest CitizenCon event yet, and the atmosphere is electric here in Los Angeles! We've bumped into some familiar faces already, and we can't wait to hang out with the rest of you.
We want to make it easy for those of you attending in person or participating from home to share your images, videos, live streams, watch parties, etc., so we've launched a CitizenCon 2953 event page on the Community Hub that will be up until Monday, October 23, 2023.
We'd love to see CitizenCon through your eyes, whether it's your travel pictures, the panels you watch from the show floor, the grub you grab (Torpedo Burrito, anyone?), the friends you reconnect with, or the swag you snag, so don't forget to share your experiences! This means that the Day of the Vara event page will be on hold for the duration, but don't worry; you will have ample time to submit your spooktacular machinimas and creepy carved pumpkins when the event page goes back up on Monday.
Looking forward to making epic memories with you at CitizenCon!
