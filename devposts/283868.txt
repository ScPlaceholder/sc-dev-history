here are different manufacturers for the alien races! Each culture works a bit differently than how Human corporations work.
The Xi'an have large houses who vie with each other for government granted monopolistic rights to control various sectors of industry and manufacturer. For example, this means that currently only one group has the right to make small ships in the Xi'an Empire and that's Aopoa. You can read more about it here:
https://robertsspaceindustries.com/comm-link/spectrum-dispatch/15292-Portfolio-Aopoa
For the Banu, I will quote the recent brochure for the Defender ( https://robertsspaceindustries.com/media/q47zalzz81ml4r/source/Banu-Defender.pdf )
In stark contrast to Human industry, the Banu don't copyright ship designs. Any individual manufacturer can get copies of blue prints and are free to tweak ship models while selling under the same name. The shape, hull, and design remain constant across all Defender models, but no two manufacturers will sell completely identical ships.
As for the Vanduul ... well the specifics of Vanduul ship manufacturing I'm going to keep secret for now.
