What does "3 contributors" mean? Only three people to work on the video (edit, record, sound, ect) but we can still have the rest of the org in place as actors on screen? Or only 3 people doing all of it?
You can have as many people as you like working on the video or appearing as actors on screen, but only up to 3 publicly identified players (defined here as "contributors" or "collaborators") are eligible to win a prize per submission.
Also the rules suggest to state that only 1 of the contributors may upload the same submission ont the community hub, is this correct?
Correct! To facilitate the process for everyone participating, we will not ask video contributors to upload the same video as individual submission posts on the Community Hub.
One submission per group is all that is required, but be sure to include the RSI handles of all contributors in the submission post description.
If you've made a spelling mistake in a contributor's handle, you may edit this, provided it is done before the contest submission deadline.
Additionally, since the same Contributors may not be in multiple submissions, can multiple submissions still be created by the same Org, provided they use different people to create such videos? (Depending on the stance of Actors as in the first question)
Orgs may send multiple submissions, but please note that each individual may only be associated with ONE video submission, i.e., a player can only be publicly identified as a contributor and be eligible to win on one submission.
There is no limit on how many videos a player can appear as an onscreen actor as long as they only "submit" one video, either by creating the submission post on the Community Hub or being credited as a contributor/collaborator on a submission post created by another player.
