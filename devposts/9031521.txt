4.9 introduces updated cleanup code for abandoned ships around locations in the PU. This new code adds ship cleanup rules that will cleanup ships in an abandoned state after 5 hours to better reduce ship clutter around high impact locations. This code will affect all major landing zones and space stations in Stanton and Nyx, as well as Grim Hex, Levski, and other social stations across Nyx.
A feedback survey is available at: Abandoned Vehicle Cleanup Updates Survey Form. This survey will be open until Aug 31.
We look forward to reading your feedback!
