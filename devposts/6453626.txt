Greetings,
For 3.22, every weapon in the game has had some kind of adjustment made to damage and the majority have seen recoil reworks.  In addition, the Arclight pistol is now burst fire with alt fire single and LH86 is now fully automatic.  There have also been fire rate changes for the Demenco, Lumin, S71, and P4AR.  Other adjustments include healing amounts for med pens, durability increases for the player, more consistent recoil patterns, removal of ADS spread, tweaking of staggers, knockdown, and force reactions.
Give this a try in various FPS scenarios!  We are specifically interesting in learning about:
- Time to kill including one shots and knockdowns
- Weapon handling and fire rate changes
- Overall feel and combat flow
Looking forward to hearing from you!
