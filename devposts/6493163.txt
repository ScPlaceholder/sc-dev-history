As the Standard Earth Year comes to an end, we're excited to celebrate Luminalia with our Human and Banu friends. On December 22, the ceremonial lamps will be lit and we'll invite business associates, friends, family, and even strangers to join us in a festive get-together.
That's what the Galactapedia tells us about this peaceful holiday. However, we're curious if, during 2953's festive season, film and TV (or Spectrum vids) are still dominated by action-packed blockbusters. If the answer is yes, shouldn't we incorporate this enthusiasm for action into our Luminalia Greeting Card Contest?
Welcome to the 2953 Luminalia Greeting Card Contest :boom:
We're challenging you to design a holiday greeting card featuring a festive message and an action-packed, in-game screenshot in the style of a blockbuster vid from 2953.
Let's spread the joy of the season together with some action!
Please avoid references to real-life religious holidays, as we want to focus on Luminalia.
## Keep in Mind:
- Before you start on your creations, please read the contest rules carefully
- Only post Luminalia-themed greeting cards that include an action-packed in-game screenshot and a festive greeting. Any other subjects will be disqualified
- Post-processing effects ARE allowed, including third-party add-ons like ReShade
- Each image of a card is considered a single entry, and you are allowed multiple entries. You can include multiple greeting cards in a single post
- No group submissions are allowed
- You can either win on Twitter or the Community Hub
- The submission period will end on January 2, 2023 (11:59 PM Pacific)
- Specific to Community Hub: Please post your greeting card to the Community Hub's Luminalia Event category only and name it '2953 Luminalia Greeting Card'
- Specific to Twitter:  Challenge entries must be posted as a reply to the announcing Tweet. Submitters must also post the Hashtag #StarCitizen alongside their submitted greeting card
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your entries based on:
- Quality
- Creativity
- Action-level
- Luminalia lore
- Rule adherence
