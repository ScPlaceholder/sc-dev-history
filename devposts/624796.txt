How characters move cargo around is another one of those subjects where the Lore team has been a little quiet on as we let design focus on what would be most fun for gameplay.
On a high level though, Star Citizen doesn't have Humanoid robots or droids, but what we do have is drones capable of performing some basic functions.
In the Cargo Interaction design doc released a little while back, there was mentions of drones and lifters for moving cargo around. While the design has certainly evolved since then, the document does show some of the team's initial thoughts on how cargo interaction might work.
https://robertsspaceindustries.com/comm-link/engineering/14677-Design-Notes-Cargo-Interaction
We have also had drones appear a few other places. There is the repair one that @Oroshi mentioned as well as the repair drones on the Crucible. The Reclaimer has  Surveyor-class drones for seeking out valuable items in the distant depths.  And on the lore-side, we have mentioned before comm-drones that are used to shuttle information between jump points.
