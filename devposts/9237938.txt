## Feature Updates
Inventory Overhaul
Armor, clothing and magazines have had a complete pass on how much room they occupy in your inventory. These previously used measured physical volume, so a full armour set would not fit in a backpack and a handful of magazines filled your torso. Volume is now assigned by category and usefulness, with magazines tiered by weapon class from pistol at the smallest through to HMG at the largest.
- Full armor sets now fit in backpacks, with light, medium, heavy, environment and super heavy all retuned. Environment suits fit a Combat Medium backpack and the BUL-H4 super heavy suit fits a Combat Heavy
- Shirts, trousers, jackets and gloves now compress enough to fit in any armor, undersuit or flight suit pocket, with footwear fitting backpacks. Every clothing item now has a defined size, where shirts, gloves, shoes and hats previously had none
- Flight suits and racing suits now occupy the same room as undersuits
- Light combat and utility backpacks now fit rifles, shotguns, SMGs, the GP-33 and the Novian Crossbow
VR Theater Mode
Theater mode has been rebuilt as a virtual cinema rather than a flat panel floating in front of you. The screen is now curved and sits in a room that it lights, with a selection of environments to watch from, and it can present the game image in stereoscopic 3D using scene depth. Theater mode is now always active in the frontend.
- Screen size presets covering Monitor, Living Room, Cinema and Giant Screen, with adjustable curvature and height
- Screen Follows Me option keeps the screen in front of you as you turn
- Environments: Black, Stars, Aurora, Day and Night, Underwater, Orbit and Storm
- 3D image option shifts the picture per eye based on scene depth
- Screen glow lights the floor and surrounding room
- Your monitor can mirror the headset view of the theater
- Options menu rebuilt around the new settings, replacing the old scale and distance sliders
- Potential Fix: VR settings not saving between sessions due to a missing configuration file
- Potential Fix: The view pitching when a standing player turned their head on a planet with wind
- Potential Fix: A crash in the options menu when a slider’s step rounded to zero

Genesis and Nyx I
The main change this build is to how neighbouring terrain patches agree with one another. Patches previously placed and elected their ground cells from their own data, which produced a straight crack along the border with unrelated ground textures on either side. Patches now elect each ground cell at its world centre, so every patch holding that cell agrees on it.
- Potential Fix: Visible cracks and mismatched ground textures along terrain patch borders
- Potential Fix: Scatter placement differing between client and server, with temperature and rainfall now read identically on both
- Ground cover assets enlarged, with new reed vegetation added
- Imposter wind bending enabled on mature and juvenile tree imposters, matched to full detail flexibility
- Bedrock roughness adjusted
- GPU instance culling refactored, and terrain shader optimised to pass fewer attributes between stages
- Water simulation now releases its resources correctly after submission
- Potential Fix: Broken planetary colour grading, with a planet-sized area restoring the streaming bubble so colour grade entities load
