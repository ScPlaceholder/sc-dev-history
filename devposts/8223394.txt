As mentioned in a recent Star Citizen Live, the Audio team is currently working on raising the bar for the audio quality in the game and addressing issues where audio is noticeably absent. Ships, environments, NPCs, and more will see improvements over time. We're going to invest more time, effort and resources to also address previously released content and bring it up to standard. Onyx Facilities and the Kruger L21 Wolf hopefully indicate what the team is going for.
