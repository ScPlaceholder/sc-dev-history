Currently in the game you  can destroy parts of a ship without killing the crew. For instance you can destroy the thrusters on the caterpillar and the vehicle might still be intact with crew. That said, if you completely deplete a vehicle's health we currently kill the entire crew, and de-spawn the internal object containers for the ship (everything inside the ship disappears!). I do have a task to address this issue as part of the Damage 2.0 refactor, which will allow for instance the caterpillar to get completely destroyed and have the internal crew survive inside the ship with the debris floating off into space. I can't comment exactly when this will go in, very likely in a post-3.0 release.
