Strafe forward / back exist, it is called "Throttle forward / back", but granted the term throttle might be a bit misleading here. You could still try mapping "Throttle forward" to hat forward and "Throttle back" to hat back while retaining "Throttle forward / back" on your axis. However Star Citizen atm will always use the last received input signal which effectively disallows combining input commands together. That is a known issue and will get fixed at some point.
