the last time we publicly spoke about combat was just in mid-November (see https://youtu.be/5jyE46u8Qrw). We always tried to be very clear that combat development is an iterative process with incremental steps that will take a long time spanning several quarters.
Right now all VET members are exclusively on combat related topics. In the last few months we touched on flight balance for big ships, component distortion and emissions tuning, R&D work for the Ares Ion and Singe weapons, staggered firing, operator mode extensions, AI development, high speed work, weapon balancing and a couple of other things.
But while there is always a huge amount of progress every month it does not mean that this progress is in a shippable state every quarter.
The balance on weapons and components is of course not abandoned. E.g. a recent focus on component distortion HP pools uncovered a few rather unpleasant damage application bugs which needed to be resolved first and delayed other work. As for weapon rebalance ... we have been running more experiments with different archetype tunings and capacitor settings recently, but it is nothing we feel confident to show yet.
So it's basically all work in progress ... nothing is abandoned, just not ready yet. :slightly_smiling_face:
