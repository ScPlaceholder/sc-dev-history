In-game social features and Spectrum/Organizations are related, but they currently use separate technology stacks.
For reference, the recent “10 for the Community” video covers the current state of social tools. Development is slower because the priority is overall game stability and system fixes.
Game-related social topics discussed include backend upgrades for real-time communication, changes to the chat system with location-based channels, a Social App to replace the Comms Link, a redesign of the chat widget, and VoIP and FoIP synchronization.
On the web side, current Spectrum work focuses on improving the user experience for thread editing, the thread page, and messages.
