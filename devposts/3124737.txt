for anyone who has swapped IDs after a relaunch you can manually swap the IDs with a console command:
1) Open the console (key left of 1)
2) Type in "i_DumpDeviceInformation" and hit enter. It will write out the device order of your input devices into the console.
3) Type in "pp_ResortDevices x y" where x and y are the device IDs you want to swap.
