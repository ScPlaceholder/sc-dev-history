PU balancing comes first before AC which is why snipers are like they currently are
With PU balancing, you can imagine the difficulty that comes from having such a large variety of engagement ranges
A big thing I want to lean into is giving weapons an identifiable 'range' they win at, mainly focusing on using the S71 and Gallant for anything beyond 50m-ish
these weapon will have longer ADS times and larger hipfire times as not to  be strong in CQC
Looking across what some other games do
- Battlefield solves this by having fairly low recoil
- Higher TTK games like apex typically have heavy ADS movement speed penalties to make their targets easier to hit at distance (dependent on the weapon class), we generalize this across all weapons currently.
For SC;
- So a good route for SC is having more direct medium to long engagement range weapons that have much lower recoil
- Couple that with damage falloff of weapons being too harsh in the current patch which will see some adjustments
- The weapon reset bug that causes tap firing to be really inaccurate
- Item blocking
- You basically have this design issue where you want the higher TTK because of the cost associated with death in SC juxtaposed with the engagement range variety but with the above tweaks it should make those longer range engagements much better
So it's a complicated issue that will take some time to feel out, AC is a good test bed for the meat and potato's of combat but doesn't scale up to what the PU needs for combat
