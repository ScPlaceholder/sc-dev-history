## ▲ Take Down
The fight has reached its breaking point as Citizens for Prosperity and Headhunters move to take down Frontier Fighters once and for all. Amelia Boyd, leader of Frontier Fighters, has been located commanding a Polaris. CFP seeks to end her assault and turn her over to the proper authorities, while Headhunters have been greenlit to join the fight and finish the job by any means necessary.
Citizens for Prosperity
Stop Frontier Fighters’ Attack
Thanks to everyone’s efforts, we’ve identified the location of Frontier Fighters leader Amelia Boyd. She is commanding a Polaris and a squad of escorts on a rampage across the system. The CFP is calling on all available pilots to help hunt down the Polaris, stop these unprovoked attacks, and bring down Amelia Boyd so she can be turned over to the proper authorities.
