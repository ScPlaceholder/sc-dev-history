Terrence Akari - https://robertsspaceindustries.com/galactapedia/article/RL6ZQLwnko-terrence-akari
Akari and Kr.ē were both instrumental in taking the first official steps to end the Xi'an-Human Cold War. Their non-aggression pact between the Humans of Terra and the Xi'an Empire showed everyone that peace was possible outside the Imperator's terms. And Akari's escape from Linton's agents made it clear that the Imperator's power wasn't as absolute as it seemed. All around cool dude. Never ran for Imperator, but I'd've voted for him.
