AFAIK from our side, we're currently not planning to add a "point to fly" mode for normal flight gameplay. Theoretically it's possible to do that (after all that is what AI is doing), but we're actually trying to make the flying parts more interesting not less. Handling oversteering is part of your piloting skill. If you have trouble controlling the oversteer remember that you can use boost, it does have a strong effect on the rotational accelerations. Also 3.18 has a couple of changes on the jerk tuning for some ships.
For turrets however we might try this out however as we are not really satisfied with any of the current turret control schemes.
