Weapon balance / diversification work is actively in progress in S42 and rebalances all weapons in terms of DPS, size progression, auto aim capabilities, projectile velocities, life times, etc. Once the work is completed we will evaluate moving it over to the PU.
However we cannot move it over just like that as the weapons are balanced with the new gunnery system in mind which has quite a few changes in the way how the whole gimbal system works (with a lot of changes to ship loadouts and UI as well). There are quite a bit of downstream dependencies here which is why it is really hard for me to tell when the changes will hit the PU in a workable state. I'm quite confident it won't be in 3.19.
