Who is invited to join SGGT is based on what kind of test we want to run. The selection is actually random, but we will pre-group the players into pools or filter players for analytics if we feel it is necessary. If we want to validate the FM for space dads, it doesn't help us if there are PVP sweats in the mix. Similar if we want to find edge cases in the FM, we might pick from a different group with more combat or racing experience. If we want to check the first pilot experience, we don't need players with 20+ hours.
So what players we pick is really just based on what we want to find out. SGGT has nothing to do with acceptance of the wider customer base ... for that we have different variations of PTU or EA experimental modes. SGGT is really just a tool for us developers to get earlier feedback while features are in active development.
