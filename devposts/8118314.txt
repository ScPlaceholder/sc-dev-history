I can’t really advise on specific crashes on Vulkan in SC right now because they’re mostly reported as general “GPU/driver crashes” with no clear cause until further investigation. The code you are running is over a year old and we’ve changed so much in the main branch in that time, including many such GPU crash fixes, but also the addition of various GPU error reporting systems.
So I’d hope these issue will be resolved, and we’re not aware of any issues with specific hardware with our internal testing, but thats a tiny sample size vs the public. So if we do find such an issue there would likely be a point patch to resolve it, with DX11 still being available.
Regarding visual issues with DLSS4, there is very little control a developer has over this. We provide basic information about the scene (colour, depth, motion) but DLSS has no specific game-provided knowledge about fog or transparency, so there’s generally nothing we can do.
