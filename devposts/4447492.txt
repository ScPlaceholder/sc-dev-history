@JODEIMOSJO, I certainly did not promise that we would resolve all weapon issues in 3.15 ... I simply stated that things will be re-evaluated after 3.14 and that is exactly what is happening. Atm we're reclassifying all weapons and tuning them for specific purposes. However that is a process that will take time as we have more than a 100 weapons in the game and releasing them will happen in steps.
Specifically the Singes and Tachyon weapons will take a bit longer due to their nature of using high velocity projectiles. Weapons like that have a tendency to offset the combat balance easily and they can quickly turn into meta weapons which is what we need to avoid as much as possible. Thus we will need to spend more time with them to make sure that they fit properly into the SC combat environment and have meaningful pros and cons (which is something we want to achieve for all vehicle weapons going forward).
