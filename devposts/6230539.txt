All reported client crashes will be gathered in a global database. They accumulate based on where the crash was coming from (callstack, aka precisely which code caused the crash).
From there they get distributed to the respective teams, e.g. a crash in physics goes to the physics team, crash in graphics goes to graphics etc.
And from there us developers see them and and can decide who will pick it up and investigate.
Some crashes are very obvious and can be fixed very quickly. Others like extremely rare race conditions or even GPU crashes can take a tremendous amount of time to find and fix.
It's a tradeoff between bugfixing and making actual progress on our current tasks.
For example i don't bother investigating crashes which just happened once. The more they happen though the more important they become and will get raised by production to be fixed asap.
