"Limiting trichording" does not mean that we are removing any control axis. You can still trichord if you want, you will just not get an acceleration advantage from it. If you have a ship with a spheric acceleration setup with lets say 10G forward, 10G to the sides and 10G up, trichording with the limiter enabled gives you 10G, not 17G acceleration. Thats the principle with the limiters.
