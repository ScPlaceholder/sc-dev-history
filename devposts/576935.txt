This basically means "you treated me like s#!t and I'm really never going to forgive you for it." There is no way to do this in English using only pronouns and verbs, but it's super simple in Xi’an. chong (even though it refers to the speaker) is one of the most horrible words in the language. It only gets pulled out when something has gone terribly wrong; someone has been ridiculously rude to someone else or an intimate parter has hurt another's feelings beyond repair.
