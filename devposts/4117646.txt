Welcome to the Alien Themed Cook-Off 2021!
We challenge you to cook, bake, grill, or otherwise create the best Star Citizen alien-inspired dish between here and the restaurant at the end of the universe. You can be as creative as you want. The sky  is the limit!
Your submission must be in the form of a comment in the submission thread, follow the contest's rules, and include:
- A dish name
- A picture of your alien meal
- The recipe and instructions for making it

## Please keep in mind
- Each participant may only submit one contest entry
- Submission must include one or more photographs featuring Star Citizen alien-themed food
- No image post-processing allowed
- The food may be photographed with decoration arrangement or lighting
- Your submission must include a recipe and instructions to recreate the food
- Provide a Star Citizen alien-themed name for your recipe
- Any pictures and recipes not posted to the contest's submission thread will be invalid
The CIG jury will judge:
- Quality
- Creativity
- Rule adherence
- Originality
- Presumed tastiness
