While there are more bugs listed than in the previous burn down, you need to know that sometimes “blockers” prevent further testing, so when they finally get resolved, new bugs show up. Together with the bugs collected during Gamescom, the number of must-fixes went up this week. We do understand that you’re eagerly waiting for a 3.0 release and that some would even settle for a "2 min until crash" version, only to get their hands on the new content. But while everyone is working hard to get the number of must-fix bugs down to zero, we all agreed to not settle for less and won’t release something that doesn’t meet our quality standards as we want you to have the best 3.0 experience possible.
