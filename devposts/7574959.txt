4.0 Introduces Contested Zones, which are a location feature coming to Pyro's space stations, introducing FPS gameplay with various layouts tailored for this purpose. The zones will feature a progression system requiring tokens to unlock different zones, giving access to the loot inside. Collecting all of the tokens will allow a player to open the final door and claim the grand prize. These zones include AI and other players fighting their way to the grand prize. We'd love to hear what you think of these zones!
Thanks for taking the time, and we look forward to hearing your thoughts!
