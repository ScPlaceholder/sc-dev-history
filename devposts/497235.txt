The major reason for the current release timing is because we want "Burndown" to be as accurate and informative as possible. We typically shoot the 'topical' portions of AtV (such as hosting) on Tuesdays and Wednesdays so that it can be edited and prepared for a midday Thursday release. Because the 'Burndown' segment requires even more up-to-the-minute information on bug numbers and status, we shoot it on Thursday and immediately edit it, which means the episode ends up going out later in the evening.
Like all things though, we'll continue to work to streamline the process, but we do anticipate that episodes will have later releases while Burndown is in effect.
