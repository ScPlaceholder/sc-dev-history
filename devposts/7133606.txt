The Anvil F8C Lightning has stormed into the finals of Ship Showdown in its debut year! To celebrate this milestone, we're thrilled to bring back the F8C Gold Licenses across the 'verse!
From August 30 to September 30, 2024, you’ll have the chance to discover these elusive gold licenses hidden throughout the system. Once you find one, head to any rental kiosk in Stanton to unlock a 24-hour F8C rental. Plus, you'll instantly gain the ability to pledge for the F8C on its dedicated landing page.
We’ve also made an update to looting—gold licenses can now be found not just in crates, but also as loot on fallen enemies!
Remember, you have until September 30, 2024, to redeem your license and secure your F8C. Good luck, and happy hunting!
