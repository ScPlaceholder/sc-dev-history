For an explanation of the HDR settings see this post: https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/50259/thread/hdr-color-space/6209142
Short version is, darken your room as much as possible,  set HDR Brightness around 100-250 (darker gives more “range” but requires a darker viewing environment) then HDR Peak Brightness should be set to the maximum.
