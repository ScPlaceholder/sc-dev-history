You’ve misunderstood what I mean by ‘silly’. Obviously Everest or K2 don’t look silly, nor any other real/extreme mountain. It’s rather that heightmaps can only represent slopes of up to about 70 degrees before they start to look quite bad, and to avoid these issues it requires mountains to be a have a width comparable to their height. Trying to use these heightmals alone for more extreme mountains just wont look good (lack of detail on slopes). Steeper slopes need fully 3D meshes, which we do support, but we haven’t yet investigated meshes of the size required for the extreme features you mentioned (issues with poly counts and physics meshes, which are solved through bespoke and complex terrain code for the heightmap terrain). Of course this doesn’t mean we won’t look at more extreme features, but game dev & graphics is all about picking your battles and compromises, and prioiritising what matters most given limited dev time, and achieving earth like terrain with great first person details was our first priority.
