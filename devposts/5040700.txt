Greetings Citizens,
With Alpha 3.17 on the horizon, we'd like to announce a new minimum required DirectX feature set, specifically version 11.1.
While the impact of this change is rather small (impacts less than 0.5% of players who have logged into the PU in the last 6 months), we still wanted to take the opportunity to give a friendly heads up. If you're still running on Windows 7 (which is unsupported) or using a graphics processor that does not support DirectX 11.1, this will affect you.
For more information on this update, please visit the dedicated Knowledge Base article, as well as the minimum requirements page.
