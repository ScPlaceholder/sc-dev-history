Hi there,
We've released 15 hotfixes so far since the release of Alpha 3.19, with more on the way as remaining issues become resolved.
One thing we'd like to note is that there are different types of hotfixes, and the rollout time varies depending on the type of fix being implemented. For example, a hotfix on our services will roll out rather quickly. On the other hand, server-side hotfixes require extra steps to ensure the fix is distributed gracefully, and can take longer to completely roll out to all servers.
One hotfix in particular that we deployed yesterday aimed to tackle the infinite inventory loading issue, and initial reports indicate that this was successful. If you were experiencing this one, you should be good to jump back into the game to give it a try!
We'll keep you updated as we have more information. Thanks all!
