## Bug Fixes and Technical Updates
Alpha 4.10.3 closes 129 issues, including multiple exploits. Outside the new content the focus is on the things you touch every session: ASOP terminals and hangars, cargo and contracts, freight elevators, and the vehicle HUD.
36 of these came to us through the Issue Council, and each one carries a link to the original report. Where the cause is worth knowing, the fix carries a short note under it explaining what was going wrong and what changed. Reports filed on the Issue Council, along with the confirmations and repro steps added to existing ones, are what let us reproduce an issue reliably and rank it against everything else.
A large concentrated pass on the hangar and fleet management loop has also been done for 4.10.3 covering 14 fixes directly related to Hangar and ASOP flow.
