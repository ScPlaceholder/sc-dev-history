PQ defines the brightness/gamma encoding we use, but we still use the bt709/rec709 colour space for defining the primaries, therefore we do not support wide-gamut at this stage. Hardware support for wide gamut is currently limited, as is support in the art pipelines we use to produce content.
This page covers some of the technical details and issues surrounding HDR: https://www.lightillusion.com/what_is_hdr.html
