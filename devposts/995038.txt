## POSTING GUIDELINES
- Only make replies to the OP.
- Only one question per post.
- KEEP IT SUCCINCT. No links. No introductions. No thank yous. Distill it down to what you really want to say. This IS NOT the place for attempts to direct the course of development (that's literally the rest of Spectrum.) This IS your place to ask brief questions of our developers. Your responsibility in this process is to break it down as succinctly as possible. The better you do with this, the more questions we can fit into the show, and the better chance you have of not having the point of your question lost in the reading.
- Do not direct a question to a specific developer.
- Do not make use of special formatting in an attempt to draw more attention, and therefore more votes, to your questions i.e. BOLD, H1, etc.
- Post violating these rules and inline replies to other questions will be deleted.
