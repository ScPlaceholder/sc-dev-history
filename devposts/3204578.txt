Hi everyone,
We're rolling out an update to the Guide System today, and expect no downtime or interruption. Here are the new features!
Rewards
You will now be able to earn special badges, that'll display your level of experience as a Guide. The badges are specific for the 8 topics we have  in the Guide System (Vehicle Combat, Trading, etc.) and have 6 levels:
- Escort
- Instructor
- Counselor
- Mentor
- Guru
- Master
This is the first iteration of a reward system for being a guide and we're looking to expand this to flair items and more in the future.
Remove from the guide pool when already paired
Guides who are in a current session won't show up in the pool of available guides anymore.
Filter Improvements (Languages)
We are introducing a language filter option for the Guide System, so you won't see Guides/Recruits in languages you don't have selected anymore.
Language Change in Guide System
You can now change your preferred languages directly on the Guide System page.
Automatic Friends
Guide and recruit can now automatically become friends, thanks to the unified Friends List introduced with Alpha 3.9.
When a Guide accepts a Guide request, they will automatically get a friend request from the recruit that they are going to teach. This way the recruit doesn’t have to do anything or figure out how to send a request and the Guide just has to accept the friend request to make it easy for them to pair up in game.
Whether you’re a new starter or a veteran: check out the system, lend each other a hand and let us know your thoughts in this thread.
