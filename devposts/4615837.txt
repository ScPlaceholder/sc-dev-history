The final four days of 2951’s Intergalactic Aerospace Expo (IAE) begin today, Sunday, November 28th. This will be the last chance to take advantage of the Free Fly event and play Star Citizen for free.
These final four days also offer everyone the chance to try out every ship from every manufacturer in the 'verse! Just head to one of the ship rental terminals and select your ship of choice. If you missed piloting the Argo RAFT, the Anvil Spartan, or any of the other 100+ vehicles on show during the expo, these final four days of IAE 2951 are your chance to do so – for free!
For more details on this year's IAE please head to the official site. And don't forget, if you refer a friend to the 'verse before the end of IAE, you both get an Argo MPUV with its Best In Show paint and jacket in-game! Details can be found here.
