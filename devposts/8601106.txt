Hi everyone,
A fast-spreading contamination has pushed Levski into a full-scale emergency, with failing air systems and a growing number of civilians falling ill. What began as a localized issue has now become a system-wide crisis, drawing attention and resources from across Nyx and Stanton!
You are being called in to move critical supplies, investigate the source of the outbreak, protect vulnerable operations, and keep trade and travel flowing under this outbreak. From hauling and courier work to combat and escort missions, every mission you complete helps stabilize Levski and push the crisis toward resolution. The population of Levski counts you, citizens!
Alongside Clearing The Air, Alpha 4.6: Lifeline for Levski delivers a wide range of new and updated gameplay. Industrial Resource Gathering missions expand mining, salvaging, refining, and scavenging into faction-driven contracts that send you across Stanton, Pyro, and Nyx (Coming in a .x patch). Cockpits now support the new LAMP light amplification system, improving visibility in low-light conditions for a growing list of ships. This release also brings the RSI Aurora Mk I to gold standard, significant VR improvements, new inventory options for Pyro rest-stop black markets, new supplies shops at major landing zones in Stanton, ground vehicle missile balance passes, and ongoing ship and weapon updates, and more!
Star Citizen Alpha 4.6: Lifeline for Levski is now available. Ready your hazard suit, the Molina mold is highly toxic.
