Hello Star Citizen Testers!
As we undock and prepare for another exciting year for Star Citizen, we want to hear from all of you who help make this universe possible.
We've made a huge effort to keep all of you informed in Spectrum about upcoming playtests, changes, and testing-related announcements. We've placed a special emphasis on the sc-testing-chat while actively monitoring  and engaging with various other chats and threads. We're committed to continuing this, but also to iterate and improve upon existing processes to ensure you feel like an extension of our team.
For this Feedback thread, we'd like to focus on the communication side of patch releases such as:
- Patch Notes
- Playtest Posts
- Spectrum Chats/MOTD
- Feedback Threads
- Regression Threads/Surveys
It's no secret that the game changes often, and it's easy to lose track of things between the myriad of publishes. Let us know what elements you like, what you don't, and what you'd like to see us introduce to better support the community.
Your feedback is truly invaluable; we genuinely mean it when we say we couldn't do this without you. Thank you all so much for participating and testing alongside us!
