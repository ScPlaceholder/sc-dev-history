If you’re CPU bound then unfortunately none of the graphics options will really help (though Vulkan and lowering Objetc View Distance may help a bit).
CPU usage will vary hugely with location and other players and their ships. Perhaps try joining the same server in the same location to compare.
if their GPU/resolution/settings are  lower then they may be GPU bound with a lower frame rate, which will then free up their CPU and make it look less busy.
I’ve said it a few times on this forum, but overall usage % for both CPU and GPU are not great indicators and are very easy to misinterpret. Instead just determine if you are CPU or GPU bound (r.ProfileGraph 1 can help here), and only focus on what can be done to alleviate that. Everything else is largely irrelevant.
