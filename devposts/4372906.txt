I do agree but technically there is no good way around this at this time. When you enter a dedicated "operator mode" such as "mining" or "missiles" we load the so-called action maps (which are sets of functions that can be assigned to keys that are associated with that operator mode ... and every set of functions you see in the bindings menu is an action map). However switching INTO that operator mode is something that needs to exist outside the operator mode itself, otherwise you'd not be able to enter it with a key. It's not ideal but there is a lot of complexity in Star Citizen's control schemes and we have yet to work out how to make it more accessible to players and easier to understand.
