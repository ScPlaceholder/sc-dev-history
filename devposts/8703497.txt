Hey there! We have seen this set of symptoms a number of times recently, and it appears to be caused by the latest update to Overwolf, which is a software many people have installed at one point or another for other games.
Uninstalling Overwolf should do the trick, or alternatively some players mentioned that using Direct3D 11 instead of Vulkan also works, if you would like to keep it installed.
Some players encountering this issue also experience crashes instead of freezes when attempting to open or close menus, or when pressing F.
