Hey everyone,
September 30, 2026 marks the official retirement of the RSI Aurora Mk I, bringing its production run to a close as RSI makes way for the newer RSI Aurora Mk II.
TL;DR: The Aurora Mk I will leave the Pledge Store on September 30. If you already have one in your hangar, it stays yours and remains fully playable and supported in-game.
We first announced the retirement of the Aurora Mk I back in March, providing a six-month sunset window for anyone who wanted to add this piece of RSI history to their fleet before production officially ends. We also covered the retirement plan in our Q&A: RSI Aurora, including what it means for those who already have one.
## What is not changing:
- If you already have an Aurora Mk I in your hangar, nothing changes about your ability to fly and use it.
- The ship remains yours and will continue to be fully supported in-game, including repairs, refueling, maintenance, components, and general gameplay, much like the previously retired Anvil Hornet Mk I.
- Additionally, as Star Citizen's development continues, we may introduce new ways to earn the Aurora Mk I in-game.
## What is changing on September 30:
- The Aurora Mk I will be removed from the Pledge Store.
- Any unretrieved standalone ships, upgrades, or CCUs specifically involving the Aurora Mk I in your Buy Back queue will no longer be retrievable after the retirement date.
If you have any questions about the Aurora Mk I retirement or how it may affect you, please let us know in the comments below.
