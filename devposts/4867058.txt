Every department at CIG has some sort of specialization (e.g. audio code, ship design, etc.) as it takes a lot of time building up knowledge and proficiency in that specific area. While people internally do switch departments or even careers (e.g. switching from QA to Design), etc. they will usually not work in multiple departments at once. So in that specific case of your friend, I'd say they should either pick the thing they are interested in most (which is probably the best career choice long term anyways) or send in multiple applications.
