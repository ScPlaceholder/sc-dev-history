As the Standard Earth Year draws to an end once again, the glow of Luminalia begins to spread across the 'verse. On December 22, Banu and Humans alike come together to light the ceremonial lamps, exchange small gifts, and welcome friends, family, business associates, and even passing strangers into warm, cheerful festivities.
Luminalia isn’t just about tradition; it is also about sharing joy across the stars. And what better way to do that than by spreading some holiday cheer with your creative festive greeting cards and winning some shiny prizes while doing so!
## :sparkles: Welcome to the 2955 Luminalia Greeting Card Contest :sparkles:
This year, we’re inviting you to share the spirit of Luminalia in whichever creative style suits you best. Whether you prefer crafting by hand or designing with digital tools, let your imagination guide you as you capture the warmth and wonder of the season with your very own Luminalia-themed holiday greeting card.
Once your masterpiece is ready, head over to the Community Hub and share it with the rest of the 'verse. We can’t wait to see the festive creativity you’ll bring to life!
Please avoid references to real-life religious holidays, as we want to focus on Luminalia.
## Keep in Mind!
- Before you start on your creations, please read the contest rules carefully.
- Posts must be submitted to the Community Hub under Luminalia submissions. The title 'Luminalia 2955 Greeting Card Contest' will be added, and you can then upload your creation and select the Fan Art category.
- Replies to this thread will NOT count as a submission.
- Only submit Luminalia-themed greeting cards that feature Star Citizen–themed elements and a festive greeting. Entries with unrelated subjects will be disqualified.
- Greeting cards may be created digitally using graphic design software or crafted as physical art, and you may use any materials of your choice.
- AI-generated content is not permitted.
- Each image of a greeting card counts as one entry. You may submit multiple entries, and multiple cards can be included within a single post.
- Group submissions are not allowed. Only the person posting the images can win a prize.
- While you can participate in both Digital Card and Real-Life Card categories, you can win a prize in only one of the two categories.
- Submissions that don't follow these instructions will be disqualified.
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your submissions on:
- Rule adherence
- Quality
- Creativity
- Aesthetic composition
## Dates
The submission period will end on Monday, 12 January 2026, 20:00 UTC.
