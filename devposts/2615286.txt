About the question if we add sliders to certain sound elements: The answer is no. We decided a long time ago that we will not allow volume sliders for anything other than NPC voices and music. The reason is quite simple ... Star Citizen has PVP gameplay and thus we cannot allow anybody to gain advantage by manually tweaking certain audio settings. That way everybody plays by the same rules.
So just mentioning the annoying sound elements here is the best way to get them fixed. The particular thing you are describing is actually bug which is on Josh's todo list.
Hope that helps!
