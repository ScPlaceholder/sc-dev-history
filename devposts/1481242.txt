There are definitely a few problems that would come up from trying to utilize data from multiple companies/products. You do a good job of pointing out some of them, being that games can be so different in their design that trying to draw conclusions from data can be a silly thing to try. Another area that would cause problems is that different companies would likely have different ways with which they handle their data ( types, format, naming conventions, etc. ). Someone I do volunteer work with on the weekend has this issue at a transportation company he works for. People argue over who's data formatting is correct, who's methods are correct, who needs to conform to who, etc.
" How do you tell the difference, without long term analytics?  And how  do you tell them apart from those who just evolve into other styles over  time?"
I wouldn't be able to give an official answer with regards to player behavior and game design, as I'm not a designer. The area that I handle statistics for is with regards to how often disconnections and crashes affect our players. I have data for this going back to 1.2.0 for every major patch and most of the minor ones so that we can have historical data to compare against. Historical data is important to provide context to anything that's currently happening and also allows for us to see if changes we've made to the game affect things.
