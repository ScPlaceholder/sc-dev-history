I definitely believe I can work out the issues with heavy load.  At least now we have a baseline of expected behavior to compare to and start to see where load is a factor.
For IFCS, I would definitely like to do a similar post for an IFCS Q&A, especially after my recent changes.  But for now, I will assure you that my changes aren't meant to make the vehicles feel massless.  The tuning of the ships is based on the amount of time it takes to achieve a given delta V, which is the SCM velocity.  This didn't change.  What changed was that my fixes tightened up IFCS considerably so it's responding much more quickly to input, so that for the same tuning, it feels a lot more powerful in accelerations.  My hope was that now that ships can feel a lot more responsive, designers can then turn down the accelerations to get back to the level of control they were comfortable with.  But it's a process.
