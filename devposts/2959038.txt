We actually want the VJoy to be primary mouse controls. The problem with the relative mouse mode is that it will never get you the full responsiveness of lets say an FPS shooter due to the inherent inertia in the rotational movement of the ships ... it also makes it very straining on the wrist itself.
So while we won't take the relative mouse mode away, we are first trying to make the VJoy experience better. One part of the problem here is that the current implementation is not always visible and thus players that are not in manual gimbal mode simply cannot see it (which ofc creates a huge amount of confusion as they cannot judge their input).
4.0 will sort out a lot of issues in that regard and get us a step closer to the optimal experience. And then we'll see if it works ... if it doesn't, we'll iterate and make it better ... step by step.
