Greetings testers!
As part of the 3.20.0 release we have included a complete overhaul of all of the ships associated with various missions through Stanton. The result should be a complete rebalance of difficulty through the mission progression tree as well as a much more complete representation of available ships among those that are AI controlled. The goal is for this rework to feel more representative of the ships available in game as well as create an improved difficulty curve as you move up to harder missions.
We are aware that some of the higher tier missions might be tricky to get to during a PTU cycle, but go ahead and give your favorite mission tree a try and if you get reasonable far in, let us know what you think about:
- The difficulty curve of hostile ships moving from easier to more challenging missions
- The pool of ships used
Thanks for taking the time to test and let us know what you think!
