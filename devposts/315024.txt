As @GrayHeadedGamer pointed out (thanks!), the Tarsus portfolio post is definitely one of the better places to read about jump tech.
TLDR; A Jump Drive is actually the term for a Quantum Drive with an attached Jump Module. The module allows for the Quantum Drive to break through the barrier into interspace tunnels. Jump points were discovered originally by ships with malfunctioning Quantum Drives, but Explorer Nick Croshaw was the first Human to jump on purpose. He did so by modifying his Quantum Drive, but the Jump Module tech developed later allowed others to do it more consistently and safely.
