Love persists...
Coramor has returned, and with it comes a feeling of warmth and love throughout the 'verse. To whom does your heart belong?
Whether it's your favorite copilot or your one true love, you can show your affection by offering your heart for all to see.
We're asking you to form a heart in Star Citizen wherever, with whomever, and from whatever you wish! Then, share your image on the Community Hub so that everyone can see whose heart shines brightest.
The three best entries will be rewarded with a prize built for two so that you and your special someone never have to be apart. Before you get started, be sure to read up on the contest rules.

## Before You Begin:
Before you start on your submissions, please read the contest rules carefully and remember to post your entries to the Community Hub and not as a reply to this thread.
:exclamation: Any submission not posted to the Community Hub or without "2953 Coramor Screenshot Contest" in the post title will not be considered. :exclamation:
## Keep In Mind
- Read the contest rules! :book:
- Please post your screenshot to the Community Hub, specifically, to the Coramor Event. Replies to this thread will NOT count as a submission.
- Use "2953 Coramor Screenshot Contest" as the title for your submission and select the screenshot category on the Community Hub.
- Each screenshot uploaded to the Community Hub is considered a single entry. However, you are allowed multiple entries and can include multiple images in a single post.
- Screenshots must feature an image of in-game items arranged in the shape of a heart.
- Post-processing effects are NOT allowed. This includes third-party add-ons like Photoshop, Reshade, etc.
- Group submissions are NOT allowed. Only the person posting the screenshot can win a prize.
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your submissions on:
- Quality
- Creativity
- Rule adherence
- Aesthetic composition
## Dates
The submission period will end on February 15, 2023 (11:59 PM Pacific).

:third_place_medal: 3rd Place: Tumbril Cyclone-MT in-game ground vehicle with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance) and a Star Citizen Game Package + Lovestruck paint. This prize is giftable.
Remember, all submissions should be posted to the Coramor Event on the Community Hub using "2953 Coramor Screenshot Contest" as the title to be considered.
