We have made a number of balance updates to ship weapons and ship defenses.  Shields have had their HP increased and their regen reduced.  Cannons have had their damage reworked and all weapons have had their velocities reduced.  The goal is to create different experiences for different types of weapons with some functioning as short range with high damage (scatterguns), long range with modest damage (cannons), and more all around weapons (repeaters).  Let us know what you think below.
