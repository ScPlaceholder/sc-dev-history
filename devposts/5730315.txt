Hi everyone,
During Digital CitizenCon 2022, we released our first iteration of the all-new Community Hub. Since then, sharing your content has never been easier. Whether it's audio, images, or video, the Hub makes it simple to quickly share with the rest of the Community. And through robust filtering, and the ability to follow your favorite creators, we've seen an influx in community-created content on the website.
We always knew we were going to iterate and release new features over time, which brings us to today. We've got a strong list of major updates coming to the Community Hub throughout 2023 and beyond, starting with Update v1.27.0, which debuts the new Events section of the hub. Read on for a breakdown of the latest release.
Community Hub v1.27.0 Patch Notes
## Events section
- The new Events section of the Community Hub, we've introduced dedicated interactive pages that will house community content related to a specific event, holiday, or promotion.
- Ability to post and browse content (including filtering/sorting) related to a Live Event/Holiday/etc.
- Homepage revamp when an event is live (selected events will take over the homepage of the Community Hub thematically, allowing you to browse trending content for that event easier.)
- Browse upcoming events
- Browse previous events in dedicated archive pages (we’ll be adding all events as they come in 2023, acting as an archive for all events moving forward)
## Fixes
- Many Community Hub Performance improvements
- Fixed issues with the Followed dynamic content section (Home page)
- Fixed issues with Discover page filters (Some users were losing them when coming from Home page)
- Fixed occasional errors some users were having with the Upvote button
- Fixed sharing system (Some users had problems with Facebook sharing)
- Fixed error displaying Tutorials in the wrong order
- Fixed errors some users were having when editing their post
## Upcoming

- New post type; Text posts!

- Dedicated livestream page (also removing them from the discover section)

- Community Content Calendar
