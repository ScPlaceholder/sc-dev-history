This starts to lead outside my department / knowledge sphere, but here goes... Obviously there‘s a physics component to the question, but the networking and persistence is the real issue. How many trees on a planet? Billions? What if I destroy all of them? Every one of them would need 1 bit of information at a minimum to store yes/no for their state (FAR more if it was to not disappear after some time), and this quickly becomes impractical. So the first thing you need to figure out is what shortcut do we take? Maybe trees respawn after 10 minutes and grow super fast? Something like this is needed to not have this be a complete time sync for us devs.
But to be clear, I don’t know if this is something that is planned, but so long as someone can’t cause mass deforestation then it probably won’t effect the rendering either way :slightly_smiling_face:
