## Feature Updates
Vanduul Incursion
Mauler weaponry made considerably more dangerous. Space combat music from Welcome To Nyx now plays when fighting Vanduul, and the tension detection system is active again. Board Ship objectives revised and balanced.
LBCO Skarnov Rifle
Particle light added to the LBCO Skarnov Rifle secondary fire mode. Updated rifle to 30 capacity.
Shader Cache
Removed the 4GB size limit on the Vulkan pipeline cache. Full cache builds exceed this and were previously discarded on load, meaning players lost the benefit of a prebuilt cache entirely. Existing caches will rebuild once
## Bug Fixes & Technical
