This is another one of those things that I have a bug in for though in this case, there's two issues.
When this happens, it's because the game server has either lost its ability to communicate with the service responsible for managing shopping data, or it failed to connect to it when it started up. Typically you'll also notice that these servers don't have any inventory in their shops either.
The second bug ( and probably the worse of the two ) is that the game server should be attempting to recover its connection to the other service and isn't. As we've mentioned in some of our videos, we're building the game with services that are decided to doing specific jobs.  Inevitably, things will lose connection with each other due to the inherent nature of the internet so the ability to reconnect after either losing or failing to establish connectivity is really important to have a fault tolerant product.  What 'fault tolerance' means is that the services and our game server need to be built in such a way that we expect things to go wrong, and then handle them in a way the limits the affect on players. So in an ideal scenario, a game server might temporarily lose its ability to get updates on shopping data or it might fail to connect to it when it starts up, but it would then either recover its connection or attempt to connect again.
