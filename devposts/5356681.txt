Despite the shocking crime scenes it leaves behind, Nine Tails is a relatively unknown criminal organization.
Well, landlubbers, we think it's time to change that and show the 'verse what proper pirates look like!
Welcome, everyone, to the Nine Tails Screenshot Contest for this year's Pirate Week. https://robertsspaceindustries.com/comm-link/transmission/18849-Pirate-Week-2022
We’re asking you to share your most epic Nail Tails-themed screenshot. You can either post in the Spectrum contest submission thread [ https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/3/thread/nine-tails-screenshot-contest-submission-thread ] or on Twitter using the hashtags #StarCitizen and  #PirateWeek2952 (don't forget to share your handle).
Before you dress in your finest Nine Tails loot, please read the full rules carefully: https://robertsspaceindustries.com/Nine-Tails-Rules
## Rules
- Please post your screenshots to the Spectrum submission thread or on Twitter including your Handle using the hashtags #StarCitizen and #PirateWeek2952
- Only post screenshots that include Nine Tails-themed material. Any other subject will be disqualified
- Multiple submissions per player are allowed
- NO post-processing effects are allowed (this includes 3rd party addons, like ReShade)
- Group submissions are not allowed. Multiple players in the screenshots are allowed, but only the person submitting the screenshot is eligible to win
- The submission period ends on September 20 (11:59 PM Pacific)
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your stories based on:
- Quality
- Creativity
- Rule adherence
- Pirate vibes
