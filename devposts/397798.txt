To recap, as many of you know, we are currently planning to include the landing zone of Levski in 3.0, but for those who follow the lore, the independent community is technically supposed to be in the Nyx system, not Stanton. It will exist in Stanton until Nyx comes online, after which, the plan is to have it bounce over there, but there will be a need to replace it in Stanton with a 'similar' type station to help maintain whatever gameplay and design loops were constructed.
Anyway, we set out to figure out some of the flavor for this replacement station and get a sense of the character and try to answer the question of "what makes this place interesting/fun/unique in our universe?"
GENERAL DISCLAIMER: The information that we are creating here is basically for a pitch to put to the rest of the dev team. It's possible that the other disciplines might have needs that require us to change the direction.
Here is the initial pitch that I had written for the replacement which we used as the launching place:
## A.R. Regency
Located on the pathway through the belt separating the inner planets of Stanton from the outer ones, the A.R. Regency is a Housing Exchange and Transit Hub servicing all the nearby independent mining operations. The HEX offers administrative support and office space for the larger companies as well as Housing, Shopping and a full marketplace for all tenants.

There’s a very strong underground fighting circuit here. The authorities know about it, but haven’t stopped it. They understand that people need to blow off steam and (so far) no one’s been seriously hurt.
Reference: Californian and Alaskan boom towns, Deadwood
