CitizenCon 2951 has officially kicked off, and it's time to celebrate all things Star Citizen and the community that makes everything in this universe possible once again.
CitizenCon veterans amongst you will know that every year, attendees of our event receive a goodie bag at the door, filled with items to remember the day. As circumstances still prevent us from gathering in person, we're celebrating all-digital this year. However, we still wanted to present you with an opportunity to get your hands on some exclusive items commemorating this very special day.
If you choose to acquire a CitizenCon 2951 Limited Edition Box, the following memorabilia will be yours:
- A challenge coin individually etched with a unique serial number
- Three all-new enamel pins
- Seven stickers
