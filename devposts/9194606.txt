The Intergalactic Aerospace Expo will be held in New Babbage, microTech, from November 13 to November 27, and Star Citizen will be free to play throughout the event!
Every year, we strap in for two weeks of excitement as pilots from across the ‘verse travel to Stanton for the Intergalactic Aerospace Expo (IAE), the biggest annual ship show in Star Citizen. This year, the event lands in New Babbage, where you can explore the expo hall, check out the latest and most iconic vehicles from the top manufacturers, and take them out for a spin. New ships and surprises will be revealed throughout the show, with rental access unlocking on each manufacturer’s designated days.
