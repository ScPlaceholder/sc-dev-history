Downstream teams are reactive as a whole, they depend on what the upstream teams are working on to determine what they schedule out. For example, take any ship. The Vehicle Content team creates the ship itself (upstream), then Audio, VFX, etc. jump in to finish it up (downstream).
In this case, tech is informed by the content being made. This is already how much of the Core Tech Group operates. (Persistent Tech, Engine, Graphics, etc.)
This feature is being temporarily removed as this was due to be worked on later in 2022, but the Vehicle Tech Team's schedule now only is one quarter in the future (currently Q1).
