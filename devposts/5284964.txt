Greetings Citizens!
To celebrate the recent launch of our official TikTok channel (https://www.tiktok.com/@starcitizen), we thought Ship Showdown would make the perfect opportunity to host our very first TikTok contest!
Therefore, in addition to the ongoing Ship Showdown contest, we will be hosting an additional contest hosted exclusively on TikTok.
Ship Showdown is all about campaigning for your favorite ship to take the #1 spot, and that's exactly what we will be looking for on TikTok for this contest. The sky is not the limit in this contest, so we want to see your creativity as you champion your favorite ship to win this year's Ship Showdown on TikTok!
You have until August 29, 2022 at 11:59pm Pacific to make your submission.
How to Enter:
- Follow contest rules: https://robertsspaceindustries.com/ShipShowdownTikTok
- Publish a video featuring any flyable/driveable ship currently in-game on TikTok
- Include the following hashtags in the post caption #StarCitizen and #SCShipShowdown
- Only one submission per participant. Group submissions are not allowed
- Does not have to contain in-game footage. Creativity is encouraged!
Prizing:
:first_place_medal: 1st Place - Crusader Mercury Star Runner Standalone in-game ship with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance)
:second_place_medal: 2nd Place - Anvil Arrow Standalone in-game ship with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance)
:third_place_medal: 3rd Place - Anvil Arrow Standalone in-game ship with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance)
Winners will be announced before September 2nd 11:59 pm Pacific. We will notify winners in the form of a public comment on the winning TikTok submissions from the official Star Citizen TikTok channel. You will need to be following this account once you are chosen as a winner so that we can collect your player information in order to credit your prizing via DMs.
Good luck, everyone!
