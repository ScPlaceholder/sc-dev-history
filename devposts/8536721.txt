Concerning IPD:
The IPD describes the distance between your eyes. If you set it to e.g. 2, then you will tell the game that your eyes are twice as far apart as the headset reports. This will "not" technically make the world larger or smaller, but your brain will make it feel like the world is shrinking in that case.
That setting has nothing to do with any markers. I know that some players use to get rid of the close projection of markers (which are still buggy and on the list to fix), etc. but it's not the right thing to do as it will mess up experienced world scale. What you should use it for is fine tune your eye distance. E.g. I am using a reverb which has a minimum IPD of 60mm (closest lens setting on the headsets IPD slider). So I am using a small correction downwards as my actual IPD in RL is 58mm.
