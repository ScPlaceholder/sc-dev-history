Design has done some updates to they would like tested under special circumstances to get specific feedback. Changes include:
- Instability no longer affects laser power
- Instability will instead impact transfer rate
- Instability now scales with rock size
- Laser power has an impact on instability, with higher laser power causing higher instability.
- Rock type now correlates to the presence of specific element meaning there are two drivers to looking for specific resource: the location within the galaxy for base resources and the rock type (ex. C-type) for the available common elements
To better understand the effects of the stability changes, instability values have been increased by a factor of 100 to really test your ability to cope and counter the mechanic. For stability, we are specifically looking for feedback on:
- How punishing to do you find the updated mechanic relative to the prior one?
- How predictable do you find instability?
- Are you able to counter instability and if so, how?
- Is stability generally better or worse than before?
Give it a try and let us know how it goes. Be sure to comment on the instability topics above if you develop a good feel for the update. As always, thanks for your input!
