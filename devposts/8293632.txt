Long ago, the Vara vanished into the silent depths of the Hades system and never returned, leaving only whispers and dread in its wake. Each year on this day, we honor the departed with spirit cakes and iced wine, flickering candles, spectral costumes, and tales of what lurks beyond the jump point.
Day of the Vara is upon us! Decorate your pumpkin with a Star Citizen twist, face the deadly Kill Collector mode, and share your most chilling story… before the dead rise from the void and claim you for the ever after.
