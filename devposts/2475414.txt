Hello everyone,
When the first wave of tickets for CitizenCon 2949 in Manchester went on sale, the team announced the details surrounding the Community Booths concept for this year. Following the success of Org Booths at last year’s event, the plan was to not only replicate this opportunity in Manchester but by re-branding to “Community Booths”, making it even bigger and open to everyone, emphasizing that you don’t have to be an organization to participate, event groups, sub-communities, and even solo pilots were invited to submit their application.
After receiving and reviewing a long list of submissions, the team has locked in 14 booths, that will provide an opportunity for members of the Star Citizen community to represent in person at CitizenCon.
So, without further ado, here are your 2949 Community Booths:
- Altama Energy
- Atlas Defense Industries
- The Corporation
- Daymar Rally
- GameGlass
- JRD+F
- MyRadar
- Pulsar42
- Republic of Lorell
- Star Citizen Italia
- SEMCORP
- StarRacing
- Test Squadron
- Xi'an Workshop
All of these booths presented an amazing concept and we can’t wait to welcome them to Manchester on November 23rd.
