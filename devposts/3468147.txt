Radar and Ping are going to combine into a single item but the Scanner will remain a separate item.
The next batch of updates would more or less provide these type of scanner with a longer range of retrieving information. Scanners unlock information based on the signature resolution ( fall off, ambient, modifiers, etc ). So if you have a poor scanner then you may have to get really really close to unlock all or important enough information compared to a better scanner that allows you to unlock the same information further out. Further updates to the exploration scanners will come thereafter as we need to handle jump point discoveries and other points of interest.
