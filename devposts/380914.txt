You are correct that according to lore, Levski is located in the Nyx system and not in the Stanton system, where it will be temporarily located for Alpha 3.0.
For the story, Collision Course, we wanted to capture the spirit of the upcoming patch and so didn't focus too much on the distances between locations. But, if we wanted to justify how the real-time comms would work in the story, it would be that Miles did contact Clara from Stanton, however, since he had business on Levski, he decided to set the meeting there.
Plus, as he says in the story:
“You’d be surprised how many people I weed out just by making them fly to meet me."
Of course, for the actual 3.0 patch, we're going to have to handwave past all of the Levski location questions for the sake of Design being able to test stuff out. It's the spirit of Alpha!
