I wasn't sure whether to reply given the tone, but the issue with the radar shown here is genuine and worth explaining.
The radar intentionally skips the anti-aliasing pass, because all temporal anti-aliasing techniques have major ghosting issues with transparent UI. As such it's expected that the UI team use approaches that have built-in anti-aliasing, but that clearly isn't the case here. I'll check with the UI team how this is built and what our plans are for improving it.
However I would also highlight that your image appears to be 700p, i.e. lower than an XBox 360 / PS3, as such it'll be exacerbating any aliasing issues. Maybe check if the issues is as severe for you at 1440p or 4k with upscaling. EDIT: iNgeon pointed out that if you right click to open the image it gives you the correct resolution of 1440p, and the aliasing is still terrible so this point can be ignored!
The rest of the game does use AA, and our AA and upscaling solution (TSR) has been generally reported to match or beat that of other temporal non-machine-learning approaches (i.e. not as good as DLSS, which is generally always the best).
