In 3.5 we made some pretty significant changes to what items are located where, along with their pricing.  The goal is to create a spread of items, many of which can only be found in 2 or 3 locations within the current universe.  Items found in multiple locations can also have different pricing based on the location, as the price is based on the distance it traveled and where it entered the system from.
For example, items that are created outside of the Stanton system can enter at one of the three jump points and travel to the  shop, with the final pricing varying based on that distance.  All of this means that better prices can potentially be found at other shops and knowing the area can sometimes result in finding the best deals!
Now that you have had a few days to get in and shop some, we would like to know what you think and how this is working for you, good and bad.  Let us know below!
