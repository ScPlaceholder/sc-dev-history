I learned Na’vi several years ago by writing in it (and speaking it from time to time with other learners). I write everyday questions and answers in it about whatever my shared context is with other learners. Asking about their day, their family, their studies, their work, etc. That said, I'm very impressed with the TinyCards and how they work and I'm thrilled that everyone seems to be doing what works for them individually. Since releasing the language, my time has been filled with complex travels and other obligations, so I'm looking forward to some more "quiet time" to interact with you all here coming up in a week or so. My basic encouragement to everyone is to just USE it in whatever contexts you can find or create for yourselves independently or together. I also encourage you to take the sentences and phrases that I write here publicly and make a list of them and start picking them apart. Just be warned that I'm also learning the language and I am LIKELY to make mistakes like everyone else. That's OK. t.o .ā .u s.o’e nyahyan ueth ching y.a’u. (Everyone is creating this reality together.)
