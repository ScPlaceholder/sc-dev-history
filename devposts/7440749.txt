Greetings, Citizens!
The ⁠Intergalactic Aerospace Expo 2954 has started! To celebrate the biggest ship show in the 'verse, we’re inviting you to capture and share your most stunning screenshots of the event across nine unique categories.
Show us your creativity in the Intergalactic Photography Awards 2954!
## How To Participate
You’re here just in time for the biggest ship show in the 'verse! Strap in for a look at the newest and most iconic vehicles up close, and participate in our Screenshot Contest.
This year, we’re inviting you to capture the spirit of IAE 2954 with nine unique categories. Whether you’re exploring the IAE halls, suiting up with friends, or showing off your loyalty to your favorite manufacturer, there’s a spot for you in this contest.
Categories:
- IAE Super Fan → You're their biggest fan! Show us your favorite manufacturer, and how supportive your are.
- IAE Elite Crew → A 3+ players screenshot featuring your crew in action on the showfloor, with a ship or vehicle presented during IAE.
- IAE Haute-Couture → Let's see you at your most stylish in the IAE convention center.
- IAE Cabin Comfort → A focus on a ship's interior, something that you like inside a ship or vehicle. Feel free to decorate your space.
- IAE Close-Up → A close-up capture of a ship or vehicle. The image must be composed mostly of that ship or vehicle, something that you like on the exterior of a ship or vehicle.
- IAE Snap → Snap a selfie and capture the essence of what IAE means for you.
- IAE After Hours → It’s late at night, and the day at IAE has come to an end. Show us how you wind down or what you’re up to after the expo!
- IAE Personal Showfloor → Create your very own mini-IAE in your Personal Instanced Hangar.
- IAE Fleet Formation → A 3+ players screenshot featuring your crew in flight formation above the planet microTech.
Please read the ⁠contest rules carefully, to avoid disqualification.
- :scroll: Prepare: Read the Categories, and submit as many screenshots as you'd like to any category throughout IAE 2954
- :camera_with_flash: Snap: Capture clear, high-quality shots that perfectly showcase the categories you want to enter.
- :outbox_tray: Submit: Send your original creations to the ⁠Community Hub with the title "IAE 2954" followed by the category, and your title, such as IAE 2954 SuperFan - Aopoa represents!
▲ Note: If the category name appears in the title or description of the post, it will be counted as a submission. Regardless of whether the box is checked, we’ll ensure it’s correctly recognized as a valid submission.
## Keep In Mind!
- Read the ⁠contest rules.
- Use the name of the category at the beginning of your title for your submission on the ⁠Community Hub.
- Multiple submissions are allowed.
- Post-processing effects are not allowed.
- Group submissions are not allowed.
- You can only win in one Category
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your submissions on the following:
- Quality
- Creativity
- Category adherence
- Rule Adherence
## Dates
The submission period ends on Thursday, December 5, 2024 at 11:59 PM (Pacific) / Friday, December 6, 2024 at 6:59 AM (UTC)
