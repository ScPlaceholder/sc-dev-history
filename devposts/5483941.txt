After 3.18 we want to separate Commodity Trading from Cargo Hauling. Commodity Trading will be high risk / high reward. If you invest a large amount of your money you will also have to manage the cost of loading and unloading as well as protection. On the other hand Cargo Hauling is just moving things for NPC's from point A to B. You are not tied to it financially, and it will be moving things that pirates don't care about as there will be no profit in it for them.
The first step here is we will be adding cargo hauling missions that extend the current box delivery missions once you can manually load and unload your own cargo. They will start small and get larger, and the only thing the players are risking is reputation.
