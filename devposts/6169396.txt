Greetings!
As part of the Arena Commander revamp in 3.20, we have added some of the Persistent Universe time trial races as dedicated tracks available in Arena Commander. These tracks are identical to those in the PU, just using the AC 3 lap format instead. They do, however, have updated AC specific checkpoint VFX, where we are primarily interested in getting some feedback for the team. Some topics we are interested in:
- Checkpoint VFX
- Feeling of racing in a group and number of racers
In addition, we have completely overhauled the New Horizon raceway maps and added a brand new one: Halloran Circuit. Give these a try as well, paying special attention to:
- Geometry and pathing of the course
- Visual cues for direction
- Checkpoint VFX
- Asset placement
- Performance
Thanks for your input!
