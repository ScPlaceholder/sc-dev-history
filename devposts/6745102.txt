Greetings,
Custom Lobbies, previously referred to as “Private Matches”, makes its return to Arena Commander! These utilize the new Frontend & Lobby Systems alongside new features such as moderation controls.
Amongst those new features this release includes the first of what makes this “Custom” over simply “Private”. Experience the first of our Custom Settings including Map Selection, Score Limit, Time Limit, Match Cycle options and Team Switching. This will allow players to set up and play matches for any game modes at any time, with their own rules. We’re excited to expand upon Custom Settings in future patches. Custom Lobbies will follow the same game mode rotation as other network types.
Please note that this feature is still in development and maybe expand over the EPTU cycle. This includes features such as Spectating, Match Recovery and more Custom Settings.
We are open to and encourage any and all feedback but we also have a few callouts:
- Have you found any limitations with the current Custom Settings that limit things you'd like to do?
- What Custom Settings, that don't exist yet, are important to you?
Looking forward to hearing from you!
