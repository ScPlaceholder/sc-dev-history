Greetings!
4.7 introduces Operation Breaker Stations, which are a location based in Nyx that will utilize new QV Breaker Stations to task players with rebooting one of these derelict stations so they can mine a large mineral resource at the center of the asteroid. This will primarily be a PVE focused activity with players fighting NPCs and creatures that have infested the place during the course of restoring the station’s mining capabilities. For feedback focus, this PVE focused experience is designed with 4-6 players in mind.
A feedback survey is available at: Operation Breaker Stations Survey Form. This survey will be open until March 24.
We look forward to reading your feedback!
