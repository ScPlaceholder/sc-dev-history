## Arena Commander 3.23 Patch Notes
Vehicle Balance
- Fixed an issue that caused the A2's core health to be significantly lower than intended
Fuses
- Fixed issue where the wrong fuse type was in players inventory
Game Mode Adjustments
- Engineering: Duel & Squadron Battle
To better allow teams to prepare for the upcoming fight:
- Increased Team Balance Pre-Game Phase from 10s to 15s
- Increased Game Start Countdown from 10s to 30s
- Movement & Interactions have been enabled in the pre-game.
- Player will no longer auto-equip their primary weapon.
- Increase health for A2 to increase time until it is fully destroyed
- reduced health on items to allow them to be destroyed faster
- Adjusted weapon balancing for Gladius and A2
- Cannons have lower speed and higher range  (~900m/s; ~2.5km range)
- Gatlings have higher speed lower range (~1100 m/s; ~900m range)
- Adjusted the UI
- Added light to relay to indicate state of the relay (if red relay is off)
- Fixed bug that prevented repairing items when shields were online
Game Mode Bug Fixes
- Fixed an issue that caused Engineering: Duel to end prematurely.
- Fixed an issue that caused suicide to win the game.
- Fixed an issue that caused Match Cycling to fail.
- Fixed an issue that caused players to lose Flight UI in the PU after playing an AC Engineering Mode
The team are also working on enabling VOIP across Arena Commander, this may be enabled mid-patch.
