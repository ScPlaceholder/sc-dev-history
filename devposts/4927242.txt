Star Citizen doesn't do anything fancy with regards to HOTAS devices, it only reads from Windows what gamepad (XInput) and joystick (DirectInput) devices are available. If the axis is not registered check if you have another axis that is constantly sending inputs during the bindings process. However if it worked before it's very likely that this is more related to the joystick itself than Star Citizen. In the worst case delete the related XMLs in your user folder to reset to default bindings and see if that works.
