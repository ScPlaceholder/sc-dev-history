It all helps. Sometimes, though, there are just problems that don't get picked up by these systems. The challenge with automated systems is always making sure they can identify when something has gone wrong. That's easy in the case of a full crash. But if it's a logic or gameplay glitch that doesn't cause an easily detectable error, they can slip past the automation.
Automated tests are a great tool, but they aren't as capable at spotting complex issues as a real-life human being.
