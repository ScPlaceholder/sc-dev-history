Interesting question. Musing on it, I think that in 2792 there might have still been a lot of pride in Humanity for the UEE and what it had accomplished over the centuries. It wasn't the Empire that was bad per say, but rather the people who had been running it were corrupt. While I am sure there were some who pushed for the dissolution of both the role of Imperator and the UEE itself after the Messers fell, there were many more who just wanted an end to tyranny. Preserving the trappings of the Empire also probably helped make the revolutionary transition easier for the old guard to embrace. With Citizens and the Military embracing the change it most likely made it a smoother process.
Regarding governance in the UEE - There are universal laws that all systems must follow, and additional regulations for planets who wish to earn representation in the Senate. That said, planetary Governors Councils can also add additional laws for themeselves as long as they don't conflict with the UEE's laws.
