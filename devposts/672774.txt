Hi, some of the objects procedurally scattered on the Daymar planet surface - especially the large cliff rocks elements - had some LOD ratio settings in their objects presets causing them to be switching between LODs too frequently and increasing the "popping" effect. This was fixed and in the next PTU build drop, the LOD popping on the large cliff rocks on Daymar should be drastically reduced.
Regarding your question about morphing one LOD mesh into another without having to render two different meshes at once: it is possible by morphing over time the higher LOD vertices into the next lower LOD vertices and then switching to the lower LOD after all vertices have morphed. However applying this to general meshes complicates things from the data structure point of view and you would need to keep connectivity information between the different LOD meshes, which complicates the development pipeline a bit. Also it is not just the vertices - morphing just the vertices positions would be the easy part, but morphing between different materials, textures etc. could be very complex in a lot of cases. So generally speaking it is a lot more GPU and production friendly to render two different meshes at once.
