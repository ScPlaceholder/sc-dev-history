When you invert a HOTAS / stick axis make sure that you pick the right device before hand. In the controls tab click multiple times on the device selection in the lower right until the right device appears. It needs to match the description of the assigned device in the keybindings menu. So when your Warthog throttle shows up as "input 2" in the keybinding tab make you select Joystick / HOTAS 2 in the controls tab before you apply inversion settings.
