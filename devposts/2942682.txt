Sure! I just had to double check the setup to be sure but there's two issues here:
1) For asteroid discs, like around Yela, this is currently made using simple geometry with a transparent shader to give the appearance of the disc's density. This shader is currently unable to cast or receive shadows and such soft/subtle transparent shadows in general are very tricky using our existing standard shadow map techniques. Certainly possible to improve it with some tech support, but as we only have one or two examples of this in game at the moment, there are other much greater priorities they need to focus on :slightly_smiling_face:
2) For the asteroids themselves, this comes back to my comment above about shadow cascades and dynamic objects. The asteroids themselves are spawned dynamically and actually do cast shadows if you are close enough to them. The nearest asteroids from Yela's surface to the belt is approximately 500km which is much farther away than our largest shadow cascades reach. It's definitely not ideal and something we will likely improve in the future (casting shadows on ships while in space at appropriate distances). One thing to keep in mind, due to the size of Stanton vs objects like Yela's asteroids, the shadows cast from such a great distance would likely be so soft as to not actually be noticeable while on the surface. The much larger asteroids next to Delamar though should definitely cast better shadows!
