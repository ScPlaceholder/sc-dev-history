Hi. While I can't give you an ETA on when players will have better control over their ship interior light states, I can confirm for you that this "aura" you mention is actually a bug.
What's happening is there is a dynamic cubemap that follows the player/camera and updates periodically, capturing the surrounding brightness and reprojecting it as ambient light. The flickering is probably the cubemap re-capturing the correct/dark ambiance, and then occasionally picking up something it shouldn't. This issue is obviously more noticeable on dark planet surfaces where the cubemap is already so dark that even a minor unintended highlight could have a large effect on the surrounding ambient light.
This issue is something we're continuing to try and improve and generally involves tagging certain assets and lights to always be excluded from the dynamic cubemap.
The other issue of this orange blobby glare in the cockpit glass is a very unfortunately placed light entity which is hitting the glass at a horrible angle for the player's seated position. I currently don't have a lot of involvement in the ship pipeline (which is why I sometimes don't catch issues with ship lighting) but this is simple enough that I'll fix it now.
Hope this clarifies a few things for you!
