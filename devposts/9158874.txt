## Features & Updates
Refineries
All refinery capacities have been increased greatly along with the refinery capacity is now data-driven, allowing the economy team to adjust capacities directly without a patch.
In-System Event (Stanton)
Balance pass across contracts including mining, salvage, and logistics rewards. Rental contract updated and rental duration adjusted. Points balance updated in the journal. Event reward components added. Event props and location dressing updates.
Distribution Centers
The external sections of Distribution Centers now have harvestable slots, so lootable harvestables can spawn outside as well as inside
