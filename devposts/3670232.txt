Yes BUT you have to know how these things work together. We are not really done with the topic of mining and ground vehicles yet but here are a couple of pointers for the Grin Roc mining:
1) Make sure you set up your manual gimbal sub modes right. In 3.12 you have the ability to slave the gimbal to your rotation inputs OR to your head movements (LALT-G swaps that over).
2) Enable VJoy controls for vehicles ... that is new to Ground Vehicles in 3.12.It is off by default and you can enable them by assigning the mouse axes to Turn Left / Right and Pitch / Up down.
When you have done that and have your vehicle manual gimbal mode (sadly we do not have a HUD indication for that yet ... press G to swap) then you gimbals will either follow your mouse movements or your head movements.
The other function that comes into play in 3.12 is "freeze aim vector". When in manual gimbal mode you can simply press RALT-G and your gimbals will freeze in place. So theoretically you look at a rock via head tracking and press this combination once. That will lock the gimbals in place so you can look around somewhere else. We'll have a separate article about that as it is not straight forward.
