Greetings,
With the release of 3.24 we have introduced hangar decorations for players to purchase and place. These new decorations can be purchased at Kel-To and Dumpers Depot then retrieved from your hangar's freight elevator. These decorations can be placed anywhere a player wants in their Hangar and have full interactions like sitting on chairs and couches, however the ship platform will delete items stored on the ship elevator when storing ships. Additionally, these decorations will persist in personal hangars but will get deleted in staging hangars (Hangars that you don't own outside of your main landing zone).
We're looking to gather your thoughts and general feedback on these decorations below, so please let us know what you think!
As always, thanks for your input! We look forward to hearing your thoughts.
