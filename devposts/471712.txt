Concept Ship Q&As are for concept ships.  They're meant to provide as many answers to your questions (as exist) in support of a sale at it's most early and least developed time, the concept stage.
For ships that are already in the game, players are invited to rent them with REC and discover what they want to know about the ship from there.
For ships that pre-date the Q&A period but are still not implemented in game, like the aforementioned Idris, we'll consider doing a Q&A when we get closer to the actual in-game implementation of both the ship and the game systems necessary to operate them.  No sense doing it after this long until we have the best chance of answering as many of your questions in the most effective and informative ways possible.
