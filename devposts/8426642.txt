Greetings!
Alpha 4.4.0 has implemented interstellar hauling missions that start in one system and finish in another, offering longer-distance cargo hauling for you to take on. These missions are procedurally generated across the Stanton, Pyro, and Nyx systems that route goods from one system to another. They come in a variety of shapes and sizes, for players with various ships, law alignments, and session expectations.
We look forward to hearing your thoughts, and thanks for taking the time!
