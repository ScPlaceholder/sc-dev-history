Hey y'all,
It’s time again for the Intergalactic Aerospace Expo 2956 to bring together the best that Human and Alien ingenuity has to offer in the ultimate ship showcase! To make sure you don’t miss out, we’ve pulled together answers to the questions we hear most.
## What is the IAE?
The Intergalactic Aerospace Expo, or IAE, is the universe’s premier spaceship show and convention. It's where everyone can see some of Star Citizen's most legendary ships and ground vehicles up close as different manufacturers showcase their latest and greatest in the expo center halls. More than 200 ships and ground vehicles are yours to test-fly for free! Whether you're a seasoned pilot or just starting your journey, this is your chance to take the helm of the universe's most iconic rides.
## When is IAE taking place?
IAE runs from November 13 at 16:00 UTC through November 27.
## When does the Free Fly start?
The Free Fly will start on November 13, simultaneously with the launch of IAE.
## I don't have a Star Citizen game package or in-game spacecraft. How do I travel to New Babbage for the event?
For the duration of the IAE, every account has access to an RSI Aurora Mk II. After getting started, head to the nearest ASOP ship retrieval terminal to access it.
## Where does the IAE take place? Where do I have to go?
IAE is being held at the Tobin Expo Center in New Babbage, the largest city on microTech.
## How do I get to the Tobin Expo Center?
Start off by heading to microTech. After you create your character, you'll be asked where to start. Pick the Stanton system, then New Babbage as your primary residence, and your journey to the expo begins. Once there, follow the signs, hop on the Metroloop, and you’ll be in ship heaven in no time.
If you’re coming from off-planet, New Babbage’s spaceport is microTech’s main travel hub. Land there and follow the IAE signs for easy transport to the expo.
## What ships are available to fly for free?
Each day of the IAE, there will be different ships available for you to test-fly. Check out the full Manufacturer Schedule for details.
## How do I rent a ship?
In the expo center halls, you'll find ships on display from a variety of manufacturers throughout the event. If something catches your eye, head over to the Traveler Rentals terminals in the main IAE hall during that vehicle’s Manufacturer Day and follow the on-screen prompts to take it for a spin.
In addition to the ships on the show floor, each Manufacturer Day also unlocks a wider selection of flyable ships, some of which aren't on display but are still available to rent through the terminals. Manufacturers cycle almost every day, so be sure to check back often! You can visit the full schedule to plan your test flights.
You can then head to the spaceport or any other ASOP terminal throughout the 'verse.
## How long can ships be rented?
Once you have rented a ship or vehicle, it will be available for 48 hours.
## How many vehicles can be rented?
You can rent every flyable or drivable vehicle exhibited at the IAE, and some that aren’t exhibited, too. Each ship or vehicle can be rented once.
## Which ships get the Best in Show paints?
All four ships that made it into the semi-finals of the recent Ship Showdown event (RSI Perseus, Origin M80, Drake Corsair, Drake Ironclad Assault) will receive the limited-edition Best in Show livery as well as a manufacturer-themed coin and coin case. These are available to all players with these ships in their hangars. Additionally, the Perseus will receive a collectible manufacturer-themed bomber jacket to commemorate the glory of being this year's Ship Showdown champion.
If you already have one of these ships, you'll receive the IAE-exclusive paint (and extra items) following the last day of the event at no cost. To be eligible to receive the paints, the specific Best in Show ships, and not variants, need to be active in your web-hangar at the end of IAE (having them in your Buyback List won't grant you the Best in Show paints). If you melt any of the ships before receiving the associated paints, then you will no longer be eligible.
Everyone pledging for a Best in Show (BIS) ship during the IAE will also receive the paint and items. If you don't already have the ship, it will be offered as part of a bundle with the prizes on Best in Show day.
Please note that these Best in Show Edition bundles already include the BIS paints, coin, and coin case, and will not grant the standalone versions of those items.
The Best in Show ships will also be available to fly for free between November 22 and November 27.

## Is there anything else that's new this IAE?
The price of the MISC Prospector will increase by $20, from $155 to $175 on MISC Day of IAE.
Until then, the Prospector will remain at $155. If you’ve been considering adding one to your fleet, there’s still time to get it before the change takes effect.
## Do I need a copy of Star Citizen to enjoy the Intergalactic Aerospace Expo?
No. For the duration of the event, anyone can play Star Citizen for free. All you need to do is visit the Play Now page and follow the instructions.
## Can I invite a friend?
Of course! All they need to do is visit the Play Now page, create an account, and jump into the game. Don't forget, using your referral code during account creation grants them an additional 50,000 UEC. And if they decide to stay after the Free Fly, you'll earn a referral point.
