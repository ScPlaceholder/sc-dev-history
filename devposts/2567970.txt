If we were to modify the climate maps dynamically you would get fairly logical changes in the landscape and vegetation, but they wouldn‘t be gradual. A single pixel of global climate data covers up to 4km, so a change to one pixel might cause 4km of snow to suddenly appear. There might be a way to mitigate this issue with more than the 10 seconds of thought I’ve put into it, however what’s less easy to solve is the vegetation. Trees And grass would suddenly appear or disappear, so you’d then need a method of growing and killing off vegetation (a significant task!). But maybe we could leave the vegetation as per the original climate and just adjust the textures. But for now nothing like this is planned.
