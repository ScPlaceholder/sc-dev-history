Turret capacitors were always shared among all turrets however in 3.14 the regen and ammo load numbers are so big that a single turret capacitor would fulfil all weapon requirements. That was done to limit the amount of work we had to do for 3.14. In 3.15. we now started to slowly move towards a proper capacitor sizing system (e.g. the pure fire time of a Hurricane turret is now half of a Cutlass turret ... both share the same turret weapon capacitor but Hurricane has twice the guns).
We originally wanted to go for single weapon capacitors per turret but it would have multiplied the amount of markup for the larger ships as we would have needed to create different pipes per turret hardpoint to make sure that they are not shared between turrets. That was important back then as it considerably cut down the work that had to be done on the content side. Whether we keep that system or work towards isolated turret capacitors we do not know yet. If items are shared there is certainly a need to communicate that between players which can make a big difference in the multi-crew experience.
Either way, we certainly do not want "to take the fun away", we're just sorting out balance numbers and figure out how to go forward. Hope that all makes sense.
