As you make your way around the Orison Landing Zone, you'll find some friendly faces available for purchase. This screenshot contest is all about our new Stormwal pals in the 'verse, Finley and Francis!
These endearing space whales are new to the Stanton system and desperately need some tour guides. Take Finley or Francis (or both!) to your favorite spots around the system and take a screenshot. Our favorite submissions will get a physical Finley of their very own, plus an in-game tonk to boot!
Before you Begin
To find your very own Finley or Francis for your screenshot, head over to Orison. The plushies can be purchased at either Kel-To in the Cloudview Center shopping plaza or at the gift shop in the August Dunlow Spaceport.
Before you start creating, please read the contest rules carefully and only post your entrees to the contest submission thread.
Any submissions not posted to the Finding Finley Screenshot Contest Submission Thread are invalid.
Keep in Mind
- Read the contest rules
- If submitting via Spectrum, post your screenshot to the Spectrum submission thread only
- If submitting via social media, you must include both hashtags #StarCitizen and #FindingFinley in your post.
- No post-processing effects allowed. This includes 3rd party addons like ReShade.
- Only one entry per participant. Group submissions will not be considered.
The CIG jury will judge your cards on:
- Quality
- Rule adherence
- Creativity
- Perceived level of Stormwal excitement and fun
Dates
The submission period ends on August 12, 2021 (EOD 11:59 PM Pacific).
Rewards
Our top five favorite submissions will receive:
:trophy: One Finley Plushie Physical Merch Item and one Standalone Tumbril Nova in-game vehicle with LTI (Life Time Insurance)
Don't want to enter the contest, but just absolutely want a Finley anyway? Check out our latest merch offerings and grab your own!
Good luck and whale-come to Orison!
