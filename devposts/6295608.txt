Hey folks as you saw we talked about the Zeus and as you noticed some of the component information seems much larger than seen in the ship to date. Part of this has to do with how our ships will interact with the resource network gameplay as well as having a more detailed vision for ship balance moving forward, this led us to do an internal components pass to plan out how we intend to bring our older ships in line with our current component goals for the future. Many ships will have their loadouts altered as a result of this pass, and it will help ships that have fallen behind due to our plans evolving over the years an example of this is the shields on a Valkyrie not being enough, we had planned to give way more details about it prior to the Zeus announcement but unfortunately am afraid that didn't happen and am truly sorry for that. We still aren't quite ready to talk about it yet and give you more details but it's something we are really 
excited about and you can expect in the future.
In regard to the cargo comparison, you're correct at full cargo capacity the Zeus Mk II does indeed hold more but to allow this RSI has accepted that this will block access to your ship components meaning if something goes wrong you're going to have a hard time doing repairs. For some people, that risk is acceptable, but for others, it won't. Players have a choice, if they want to play it safe or push it for bigger profit.
