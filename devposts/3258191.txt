We'll likely won't support custom zooming via Head Tracking until we get a chance to rework the input system. Atm the head tracking inputs are bespoke solutions ...  so they act directly on the actor itself instead of going through the input system. The reason for that is that our input system cannot accept any other inputs than USB controllers at this point.
Once that is out of the way it would be quite easy to use the head tracking axis for any game input. But atm we do not have the resources to work on that. Long term we also rather want to reserve those axes for "leaning" around in the cockpit which is much more immersive than a simple zoom functionality.
In the meanwhile please check the dynamic zoom commands we added a while ago. We even have a toggle that quickly swaps between no zoom and full zoom.
