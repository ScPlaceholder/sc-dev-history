## UPDATE: The contest's submission period is now over. Thank you all for participating and sharing your great cards with us and gamers around the world. We'll announce the winners soon.
The new Orison Landing Zone draws and enchants people from all over the Stanton system. High above the clouds of the Crusader gas giant, one wanders among flowering trees or enjoys a drink by the crackling fireplace... and who wouldn't want company on these occasions?
Welcome to the 'Wish You Were Here' Greeting Card Contest!
Show us your favorite place in or around Orison by taking a screenshot and creating an Orison-themed greeting card.
Add the message "Wish you were here..." and your personal referral code to your card and invite your friends to the city above the clouds.
## Before you Begin
Before you start creating, please read the contest rules carefully and only post your entrees to the contest submission thread.
Any submissions not posted to the 'Wish You Were Here' Greeting Card Contest Submission Thread are invalid.
## Keep in Mind
- Read the contest rules
- Post your card to the Spectrum submission thread only
- Create a greeting card that includes an in-game screenshot of the Orison landing zone and the message 'Wish you were here...'
- Add your personal referral code to your card
- Each image of a card is considered a single entry, but you are allowed multiple entries and can include multiple greetings cards in a single post
The CIG jury will judge your cards on:
- Quality
- Rule adherence
- Creativity
## Dates
The submission period ends on August 18, 2021 (EOD 11:59 PM Pacific)
