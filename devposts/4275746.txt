Players that have contacted us for help with issues like this in the past would have had the password on their account temporarily changed ( with their permission ) by the support agent  to log into the account in order to help with troubleshooting. This is a good thing because we want to make sure that our players are aware that we would never ask for their password. We don't need it and it's a good idea to never tell it to anyone for account security purposes. We don't have a way to access an account without credentials and realistically, we won't need one since we can change an account password as needed.  There's also inherent flaws to having that kind of workflow. The down side of having a workflow that bypasses normal authentication methods to get into an account is that it has the potential to miss legitimate issues that require natural authentication workflow.
With regards to the rest of your question, I don't work for our support team so I don't know what their policies on providing that information would be.
