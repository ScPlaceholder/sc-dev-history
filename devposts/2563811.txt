My first impression after reviewing a few ships today is that the wear is a bit excessive on some and I’ll push for this to be addressed, but the worst offenders are simply not set up ‘correctly’ and need a setting changed to cull reflections from exterior lights. While the scratches may be over done on some ships, a major flaw in some ships is they’re not designed with reflections in mind. Cars and planes are designed with glass at angles that won’t reflect the sun into the drivers/pilots eyes, but that’s not true of some spaceship designs. So we either nerf all glass effects on these ships (unlikely to be favoured with CR) or just cut out the unwanted reflections (our chosen solution, but needs enabling on all the problematic canopies).
Ali Brown
