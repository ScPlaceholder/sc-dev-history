## Housekeeping - Archiving Reports
As part of our ongoing effort to turn player feedback into actionable information for development, it is crucial that the information about each bug reported on the Issue Council is kept up-to-date to account for the following:
- A new version being released in which the issue may or may not occur
- Changes to reproduction steps
- Changes to frequency of occurrence or number of players affected
- Changes in the possible impact of the issue
We recognize that, at the moment, you can only add your contribution to a report once, and cannot update the existing contribution. Therefore, from now on a confirmed report will be archived if it does not meet one of the following requirements:
- Reproduced by at least three players on the current or prior publicly available version
- Reproduced by at least three players within the last two weeks
- Reproduced internally on the latest or prior version
- Work on the issue is ongoing
If a report does get archived but the issue persists, we highly recommend creating a new report for the issue with the latest information. Thanks for your co-operation on this one.
