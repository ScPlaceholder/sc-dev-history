With regard to #3, that is essentially what happened with the hornet series and the F7A. Keep in mind that legacy ships must still be supported by new tech, so keeping them around represents an ever increasing maintenance burden - as well as potentially hamstringing optimization efforts that may grow increasingly incompatible with old implementations. It's a call that must be handled on a case by case basis, as there are quite a few context sensitive ramifications.
Essentially, we are keen on the idea and the sense of history it can bring to the game, but for many ships it wouldn't make sense from a production standpoint.
