## Update:
Thank you for the fantastic watch party photos, everyone! The submission period has ended; we are now starting the internal voting process and will announce the winners very soon.
--
CitizenCon is all digital this year, and we want to see how you're watching it!
Walk away from CitizenCon 2951 in style with an exclusive and custom XenoThreat themed Astro Gaming headset. Prepare early for this contest by organizing the most epic watch party you can! Themed food? Decorations? Themed dress code? All of the above will help you secure victory!
Whether it's a massive Discord call with your friends... or maybe a bevy of snacks and friends piled on the couch... or even if you've got an inflatable projector screen in the backyard for full immersion... show us how you plan to watch Digital CitizenCon on social media for a shot at taking home one of three custom Astro Gaming Headsets!
Post a photo of your "watch party" on Twitter or Instagram using the hashtag #SCWatchParty to enter, and be sure to read the contest rules before doing so. Winners will be judged based on the photo's adherence to the spirit of a "watch party" as well as quality.
Multiple entries are allowed, but only one prize per entrant. Group submissions are not allowed. All entries must be submitted before 11:59pm Pacific time on October 11th, 2021 to be eligible.
We're excited to see all your setups! And of course, don't forget to tune in on October 9th! For all of the details on Digital CitizenCon, check out our CitizenCon page.
