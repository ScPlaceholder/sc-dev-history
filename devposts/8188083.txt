Heads up, Citizens.
If you thought the regen crisis was over, think again. You wanted answers? They are buried deeper than ever. Gear up. It's time to dive back in.
Because at the heart of it all is one name: Associated Science & Development.
If that name doesn’t make your skin crawl, you haven’t been paying attention. Since the regen crisis hit, they’ve been neck-deep in scandal: alleged illegal experiments, grotesque mutations, irradiated valakkar running loose. The list reads like a horror novel. It’s time someone turned the page. The truth is out there, and someone needs to dig it up.
The problem? ASD knew how to bury their secrets. The intel we need is scattered across every corner of the Onyx facility, and they made damn sure no one could just walk in and take it. So you’ll have to dig. Deep. Face whatever’s still lurking in the dark. And if you can… get out alive.
