One of the reasons that we wanted to move to this new lensflare approach is that it's legitimately generated by bright elements on the screen. This means that rather than whacking a big lensflare sprite onto arbitrarily designated bright spots, any very slightly overbright elements will naturally produce a more subtle haze, very bright elements will flare more intensely. It, or the lights themselves, obviously need a little more tweaking, but avoiding that nasty old sledgehammer lensflare was one of our goals going into that feature.
