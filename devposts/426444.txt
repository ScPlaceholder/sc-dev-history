It is Khartu-al. We were originally doing more mixed capitalization for the Xi'an (for instance writing Xi'an as "Xi'An") but once our linguist, Britton Watkins, came on board and developed the language more, we have moved away from that. So now it is: Xi'an, Aopoa, and Khartu-al. We have been trying to be consistent in our use moving forward and update the old uses where we can, but it is definitely an ongoing process.
