in regards to your initial question - the pop-in you see when flying to the surface should be purely asset-based. most due to not-ideal value setups, some due to larger discrepancies between different LOD stages of assets. having artists spend more time fine-tuning the values for when transitions happen and matching the actual LOD levels will certainly help and be our first course of action. the use of the new hard surface shader for organic assets will also help with that.
having a soft fade would certainly allow us a bit more leeway in how perfect the LODs need to match each other, but it does come with more than twice the rendering cost and with the way procedural assets are batched... let's just say that we will do everything to improve our asset pipelines first before we consider the sacrifices that would need to be made to have a soft-fade between LODs for procedural assets.
the terrain works differently in that its a large grid that's being displaced by a texture. it does not really have LODs, but rather changes it's resolution dynamically. the changes in resolution (both mesh and texture) should be so gradual and minute that it would hopefully be very hard to actually see those.
with star citizen a lot of things need to be done differently and more precise due to the nature of the project. being able to see everything from so many different distances makes hiding "dirty" practices much harder.
