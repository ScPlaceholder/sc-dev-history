what we're doing atm is regular testing of the latest NFM builds with players, but these are in a closed environments. All players who participate are under NDA. We're doing that to get more focused playtest feedback. For every test we invite between 100 and 200 players to take part and after the test weekend access is revoked again. That's been going for some time now.
You cannot apply to take part in the testing, the selection is done based on whatever player profile we need for the test (e.g. truckers, racers, PVE players, etc.) and then players are randomly picked after a bit of filtering. We roll the dice every single time to avoid bias and when they sign the NDA, they get access to the next build.
And so far that has been working really well for us. We can easily playtest, try out variations, validate concepts and can put a lot of trust in the feedback. The iteration is much faster than it would be with public testing. We will keep doing it until we're ready to go full public. But we're not there yet.
Hope that makes sense,
