in general the graphics team have always wanted both protects to be kept in sync, largely because that’s the least painful for us! But this isn’t a one-man-show and there are larger priorities at play. The largest complication is code dependencies, it’s very hard to stop largely unrelated changes becoming dependant on one another, and this means you very soon end up in the position of either taking ALL new code (with its risks and distractions from the wider project goals, eg stability and playability) or you spend ages unpicking everybody’s code (nobody wants to do this, we all want to crack on with shiny new tech!).
So the graphics team didn’t make the call here, we were just very vocal and persistent :slightly_smiling_face:
