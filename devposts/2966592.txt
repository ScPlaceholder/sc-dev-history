@Shaozen,
I got a work around for you:
- Go to your HOTAS bindings under Flight - View
- Remove the bindings for "Look left / right" and "Look up / down"
- Assign your look left up right down keys separately
- While in game, press Z to switch "freelook" mode.
That should give you the same behaviour as before. Let me know if that works for you or if you run into any problems please.
