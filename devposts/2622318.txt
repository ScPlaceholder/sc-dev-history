Hi everyone, we've got a quick update from the team about some new functionality you'll see in Alpha 3.8 for the MISC Reliant Series.
The Reliant has been unable to fully function in its intended various forms because we haven't yet developed a state machine that will allow players to rotate the wing and stand up to walk around while in flight. We've heard a lot of feedback from all of you on this matter, and the team has an update that we think Reliant pilots will be excited about.
In Alpha 3.8, we've made some changes so that the Wing Rotation is now tied to the 'J' key, and the Thruster Rotation + Landing Gear are tied to the 'N' key.  This means you'll all be able to toggle the wings between horizontal and vertical and fly in whichever state you wish!
It's worth noting the Reliant series does have a limiter in that getting out of your seat while landing gear is raised would cause some issues for your character, therefore you'll find that once you're seated, you'll be unable to get back up until you're landing gear is down. We are aware that you all would love that functionality - we fully agree. Allowing you to get out and walk around the Reliant while your landing gear is raised will require further engineering work on our part to make possible, but we're dedicated to getting it done and you can expect to be kept in the loop with updates from the team.
