Sure, the intentions are allowing players to "dodge" slower projectile versus faster projectiles.
So it is a player choice to have a long range slow projectile weapon that can be dodged but you have the distance advantage, versus you have a high speed projectile that makes it hard to dodge but you have to get close.
The goal is to synergize this intention with the different ship sizes and ship archetypes.
Like:
Capital ships move slowly, therefore if my targets are mainly capital ship I can go slow long range weapons.
