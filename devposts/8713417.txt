Hi everyone,
The Keeger Belt has been quiet for three hundred years, but not forgotten. Abandoned QV Breaker Stations dot the asteroid fields of Nyx, their colossal rock-cracking machinery sitting dormant and untouched. Shubin Interstellar is offering exclusive or shared access rights to anyone willing to go in and wake them back up, and the rare and remarkable resources locked inside make it worth the effort.
Gather your most industrious team and make way to Nyx. A breadth of skills is essential to uncover, claim, and secure the haul: miners, haulers, fighters, and crafters all have a role to play.
Beyond Operation Breaker Stations, Alpha 4.7: Welcome to the Rock brings long-awaited Crafting to the 'verse, letting you fabricate FPS armor and weapons from blueprints and gathered materials. Inventory management also gets a significant overhaul to help you make the most of everything you haul back.
Alongside all of this, RSI reimagines its beloved starter ship with the introduction of the Aurora Mk II, as the Mk I's journey comes to a close and the torch is passed on to light the next chapter.
Star Citizen Alpha 4.7: Welcome to the Rock is now available.
