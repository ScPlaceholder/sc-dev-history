## Before You Begin
Before you start on your submission, please read the Contest Rules carefully and remember to post your entries to the Community Hub and not as a reply to this thread.
:exclamation: Any submission not posted to the Community Hub or without "2953 Stella Fortuna Screenshot Contest" in the post title will not be considered. :exclamation:
## Keep In Mind
- Read the Contest Rules! :book:
- Please post your screenshot to the Community Hub, specifically, to the Stella Fortuna Event. Replies to this thread will NOT count as a submission.
- Use "2953 Stella Fortuna Screenshot Contest" as the title for your submission and select the screenshot category on the Community Hub.
- Each screenshot uploaded to the Community Hub is considered a single entry. However, you are allowed multiple entries and can include multiple images in a single post.
- Screenshots must feature an image of in-game combat, racing, stunts, or other exciting gameplay, especially against overwhelming odds
- Post-processing effects are NOT allowed. This includes third-party add-ons like Photoshop, Reshade, etc.
- Group submissions are NOT allowed. Only the person posting the screenshot can win a prize.
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your submissions on:
- Excitement (especially against overwhelming odds!)
- Quality
- Rule adherence
