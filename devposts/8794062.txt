4.8 introduces Tactical Strike Groups, which is a new endgame mercenary contract designed for large, organized player squads. The mission tasks players with assaulting a fortified Nyx asteroid base held by hostile forces, and working through a multi-phase assault that rewards coordination across ship classes and combat roles.
A feedback survey is available at: Tactical Strike Groups Survey Form. This survey will be open until May 14.
We look forward to reading your feedback!
