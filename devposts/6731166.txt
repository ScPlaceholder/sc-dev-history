For the 3.23 release, we will have a preview of this system (Markers). It will allow players to place markers on the interior map but not on the Starmap. Placed pins will not persist between sessions, nor will they be shareable.
In the future however, you will be able to place pins both on the interior map as well as the Starmap, and you will be able to share these pins with other players.
