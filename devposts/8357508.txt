Heya,  the difference between "in-engine" and "in-game" is quite subtle - in StarEngine the only difference is between them from a graphics POV is "Is the camera attached to an actor's head or is it just flying around", to which the answer is that it's flying around the planet on splines that the brilliant cine guys have made. This works just as well when you attach that camera to a player's head. Similarly, "pre-rendered" isn't really a thing here, it's filmed in engine as if it were being played - we don't have a way to generate planets/assets etc outside of StarEngine. I suppose the only thing that "pre-renderered" can indicate here is that it wasn't a live demo played in-front of a crowd - it was indeed recorded in the weeks leading up to Citcon DIrect. That isn't to say we don't boost everything as much as possible for cinematics - the cine guys use what is effectively an "ultra-ultra" sys spec. This makes everything look as good as possible, by removing optimizations like LODs and boosting the size of shadow atlases and the virtual terrain textures.
