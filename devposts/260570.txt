The behavior of ships in atmosphere should be what you'd expect from a spaceship flying in atmosphere.  None of our ships are designed for atmospheric flight.  Some have an aerodynamic design philosophy, but they're still spaceships, not aircraft.  So, yes, atmosphere will introduce new external forces that the ship must contend with.  There might be similar conditions if flying through a dense gas cloud in space.  We're modeling the behavior of a ship as it moves through some medium, the velocity-dependent forces of drag and lift.  Drag is always going to be present, and it depends on the cross-sectional area of the ship as it moves through the medium, as well as a coefficient of drag for the particular ship, and the density of the medium.  Lift is never "accidentally" present.  It will only be present in specific cases where a craft was intentionally designed for lift.  But even then, we're not an aircraft flight simulation.  We're a space simulation that happens to allow ships to fly into atmosphere.  I've actually had to push to even support lift (and it's not even in the game yet).
