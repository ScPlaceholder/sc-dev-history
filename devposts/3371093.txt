Deducting points per second isnt a bad idea. I'll bring it up to design. Right now when you leave the area if you're not back in the allocated time you'll blow up; that death counts as a suicide. On death the remaining score of your ship is distributed between all of your attackers (within a reasonable time-frame) based on their damage contribution up to that point - ie I do 30% damage you do 10% i'll be awarded an additional 45% and you 15%. We've been talking about adding an "assist as Kill" score type where if youve contributed enough damage (say 80%+) and the target dies from other means you still get awarded the kill, hopefully can play around with that when awards are refactored.
Additionally since the death counts as a suicide the out of bounds player is deducted a flat score (same as you would lose from crashing into something and dying).
As for just not blowing them up, contrast to Theaters of War where we want to give freedom for weird and wonderful dogfights this is a focused arena game-mode, I dont think allowing gameplay outside the normal bounds is healthy for it. It's not my call to make but I can imagine a meta for people that dont care about points sitting out of bounds. I'm sure its a discussion QA & design will have at some point during playtesting.
