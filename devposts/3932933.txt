While we are in early access we recommend to not re-use your old keybindings from the previous version. Often doing that is no problem but if you actually run into problems you should delete your User folder, redo your bindings and see if that fixes the issue.
That should be especially true for 3.13 as the underlying folder structure changed. Also the actionmaps.xml (which captures all the differences between the "default" profile and your keybinds) is now human readable and not saved in binary XML anymore.
