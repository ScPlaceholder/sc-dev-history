Greetings all!
In an effort to assess the status of combat, we would like your feedback on the various ships you have taken into battle. Although there are other issues that impact combat and balance (weapons, missiles, IFCS, ect), which are also covered, this particular survey will mainly focus on the ships and their operation. Please only fill out the survey for the kinds of ships and scenarios you have experience with, but note you can take the survey multiple times to provide feedback on the various combat situations you may of experienced.  The scoring is deliberately set-up to avoid "everything is perfect" style answers, so think hard and choose wisely.
You can find the survey here: https://goo.gl/forms/vhYld3Fd9QghxJgq2.
As always, feedback is not required but is very much appreciated. Thanks so much for your participation!
