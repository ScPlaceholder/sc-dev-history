We didn't get to go fully into it during the RTV but the UEES Stanton was originally named the UEES Caliban when it was sealed 2872. When she got heavily damaged in 2904, she would become the first ship to be repaired at the new Naval shipyards above Stanton II (which would later be bought by Crusader Industries). They decided to use this opportunity to rename her since Caliban had been lost to the Vanduul in 2884. While at first the Caliban name had been seen as a bit of a rallying cry, as the years passed and there was no sign that the system was going to be retaken, the name became a bit of a sore spot for the Navy. When the ship was re-sealed, a ceremony was held and the Idris was re-named UEES Stanton in honor of the system's recent addition to the Empire.
