For anyone who may be browsing this thread for a solution to this error code, I will note that the error 3221225477 is the most common type of crash, but doesn't necessarily have the same underlying cause, especially if you are reading this message in a later patch.
That said, right now it is most often caused by issues with running the game in Vulkan. We have a workaround outlined on our Vulkan Troubleshooting page, but feel free to send in a ticket with your game.log and dxdiag, then we will be happy to take a closer look and provide more direct guidance.
