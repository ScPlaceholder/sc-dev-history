## Welcome to the Red Festival 2953 - Moons Of Fortune Contest.
We're rewarding the best screenshots featuring one of Stanton's many beautiful moons. The only condition is that it must feature one of this year's Red Festival envelopes.
:bookmark_tabs: Before considering which of the twelve moons is your subject, read the contest rules carefully and only submit your entries to the Community Hub.
:fire: Any submissions not posted to this location or not named "Moons Of Fortune" will not be accepted.
## Keep In Mind
- Read the contest rules.
- Screenshots must be published on the Community Hub.
- Post-processing is allowed but remember that sometimes less is more.
- Only post screenshots that include your favorite Star Citizen moon. Each image must include a Red Festival 2953 envelope. Any other subject will be disqualified.
- Group submissions are not allowed. Multiple players in screenshots are allowed, but only the person submitting the screenshot is eligible to win.
- Multiple submissions per player are allowed. You can upload them in one post to the Community Hub (The title must be Moons Of Fortune).
- You are limited to winning one prize, so you can not win on multiple platforms (Community Hub, Twitter).
- Consider turning off the HUD when taking screenshots, as this might make for better composition. Check out an excellent screenshot tutorial by DigThat32 or Mr. Hasgaha.
- Use Moons Of Fortune as Title for your submission on the Community Hub. :exclamation:
- If you do not choose the screenshot category, your submission will not count.
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your submissions on:
- Quality
- Creativity
- Rule Adherence
## Dates
The submission period to post your contest entries to the Community Hub ends on February 6, 2023 (EOD 11:59 pm Pacific).
