Of course we are not storing it the conventional way otherwise yes, as you mentioned each planet would easily take several GBs of data if we were to store it in advance the conventional way, however this is not the case as the planet surface is progressively procedurally generated as the player is approaching/visiting it. The huge terrain texturing is not stored in an "ultra-megamap", they are generated at runtime too, blending on the fly several different texture layers depending on the ecosystems and planet setup, plus noise combinations etc. Each ecosystem has its own (artist-driven) small geographical sample data, for instance I think on Yela we use only 5 ecosystems or so, combined with several different properties. There is a fixed geometry pool which is continuosly getting re-generated and re-used as the player moves around the planet generating the planet surface. The objects scattered on the ground and on the planet surface are also procedurally distributed based on different rulesets setup by environment artists.
So basically a small amount of source data is getting combined at runtime to generate the much larger planet and environment.
