Yes, we changed the design ... and that shows the same problem with blogs ... they are just momentary records of the dev state of that time. It doesn't mean things can't change. We change things around a lot, that is how developing a game works. Designing games is usually not a strict waterfall process. You design, you iterate, you test, you change, etc. This is not different to any other game studio ... the difference is just that you all have visibility to that. It actually worked like that since the first public MM tests we did ...
