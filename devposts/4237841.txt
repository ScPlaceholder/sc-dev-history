This Friday at 10am Pacific / 5pm UTC, we'll have Rob Reininger, Chad McKinney and Ben Dorsey from the US PU Features Team on to discuss various aspects of what their team has worked on or is currently working on.
SPECIAL NOTE: We're on the Road to Digital CitizenCon. Remember that there are HOURS of discussion happening there in a single day between the various panels and presentations. As with every year, certain topics are being held in reserve until then.
Topics for this week's show include:
- Ninetails Lockdown - The new dynamic event.
- Cargo Refactor - Physicalized on grids, tractor beams, loading/unloading and looking ahead to persistent hangars.
- Selling - Loot is coming, and you're gonna need a way to unload it.
- Persistent Hangars - Ship spawning in the same place you docked it, localized inventory, not player-owned hangars (different feature.)
- ASOP Terminals - As they relate to Persistent Hangars
- Shopping - As related to localized inventory and persistent hangars
- Procedural Character Generation - Controlling the quantity and type of NPCs within a given location's population.
- Reputation - Current implementation, UI and experience.
- Asset Manager - New way to manage various inventories.
Questions outside the listed topics will not be addressed.
