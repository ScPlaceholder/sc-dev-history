It's still a little loose as we haven't really drilled down into how they will specifically work, so there will potentially be some shifts as we start working on the specific design of those elements.
As for AI, you'll find a section in there covering it, but in a nutshell, Humanity has pretty much stopped trying to develop it. This was one of those choices that was made for stylistic/creative reasons. AI pilots would presumably be vastly better than a Human one, but the idea was to have the SC universe be a bit more tactile. Chris wanted pilots to fly their own ships, rather than have a computer do it, so we tried to stack the 'history' to justify why Humanity, as a species, has been reticent to pursue AI. We have been saying that every so often, a scientist will decide that they're the one to crack the mystery, but the experiments have continually turned out poorly.
