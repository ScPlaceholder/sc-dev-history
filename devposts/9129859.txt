## Features & Updates
Orison Relief Support
The event requirements for completion have been reduced by quite a lot in today's ptu. Blueprints have been removed from the Orison Relief Support reward track and are now direct rewards granted to your account. These should be attributed to your PTU account only when completing on PTU.
Freight Elevator
An item limit has been added to the freight elevator platform to prevent unstowing more individual items than the platform can handle
Hauling Contract Distribution
Cargo distribution across multiple pickup locations now accounts for SCU weight per location, producing more sensible splits and percentile-based cargo amounts corrected
Added error reporting for contracts where dropoff and pickup counts do not match to help track down any further issues with these.
## BUG FIXES & TECHNICAL
Shard Recovery
Players who become stuck unable to connect to a shard, either because that shard is isolated or because their spawn repeatedly fails, are now automatically diverted to a healthy shard rather than looping on the failing one. This one is a tough test on PTU due to its nature so please let us know if you run into any issues getting locked to a shard!
Performance
Improved audio thread handling to reduce frame rate drops caused by repeated thread initialization
Better memory management for zone partition object page lists, reducing frequent allocation cycles
