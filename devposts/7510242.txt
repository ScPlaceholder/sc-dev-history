We have enabled the advanced flight HUD on all ships in the most recent 4.0 publish. This new HUD can be enabled per ship on the ship configuration page or fully enabled through the in game options, and allows you to see much more dynamic information than the previous HUD. This includes new acceleration displays, orientation, VSI, radio altimeter(r-alt) which is the altitude above terrain, above sea level altimeter, atmospheric pressure, and more.  We'd love to hear what you think!
Thanks for taking the time, and we look forward to hearing your thoughts!
