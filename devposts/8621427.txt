SC Alpha 4.6.0 has added the ability for Vehicle Cockpits to support light amplification (similar to NVGs) in order to better facilitate gameplay at low/no light conditions, which can be enabled and disabled with Right Alt + L by default while in a pilot or co-pilot seat.
In addition to this feedback thread, we have also opened a feedback form for you to fill out! This includes some specific questions that we would greatly appreciate your thoughts on, which can be found at: LAMP: Light Amplification System Feedback Form. This survey will be open until Feb. 11, however this thread will remain open for you to discuss your thoughts.
We look forward to reading your feedback!
