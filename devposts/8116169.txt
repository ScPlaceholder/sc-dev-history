Following on from my previous message on this topic (helpfully pasted by @Dr-Phibes below), we’ve been integrating the latest renderer and other tech from S42 over to SC‘s main branch and about to begin testing and bug fixing. But don’t get too excited just yet, it can take quite some time to get to the patch stream, and then an actual public release (all depends on how successful the testing is). This will include major changes to Vulkan, some upscaling changes, and both CPU and GPU optimisations... though some larger optimisations will come slightly later. GI will unfortunately not be included as that needs more optimisation and is missing a few features for SC that S42 didn’t need (eg support for our tint system).
