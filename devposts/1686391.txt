There's several different bugs there on different teams each of which have their own backlogs with other issues of varying priorities. The answer for why something hasn't been fixed is always something along the lines of either it hasn't been prioritized enough to get a fix in for the most recent release, or it has been deemed to require some more significant rework and that work is slated for a later release. Other times we try to fix things only to find that they didn't quite stick.
In the case of the missing ship items, that was a weird and difficult one. QA very very rarely saw this. Unfortunately even though we have a large QA staff there's still many bugs that we can't reproduce internally (easily or at all) but are common enough to be very annoying for the backers. These are the worst because we have very little to go on to know why it is happening or if any particular change has fixed it or not. Yet to the backers it will feel like we aren't even trying. Anyways for the missing ship items we think it was tied to insurance claims where the newly entitled ship attachments weren't fully pushed to the database. A recent fix went into 3.3.6 that might fix it, so let's hope we put that one into the dirt for good.
