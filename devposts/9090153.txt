We're getting into the home stretch for Alpha 4.10, and we'd like to spend the rest of this week putting the PTU through its paces before we roll out to the Live servers.
We've got a number of fixes in, along with some additional backend logging to help us keep a better eye on things while you're playing. Now we want to put some real load on the areas we're most interested in seeing hold up.
In particular, we'd love to see you spend some time with:
- Siege of Orison
- Recco Battaglia
- Cargo Missions
As part of this final push, we're giving active PTU testers a chance to take the Kruger S-65 Stingray out for a spin. Those who receive access will be able to fly it for free through Sunday.
If you've already put substantial time into testing the PTU, you may find the Stingray available immediately with today's new build. If you're just getting started with the PTU today, spend some time playing the focused activities mentioned above (specific criteria will be outlined in the upcoming Patch Notes), and we'll be keeping an eye out for active players to grant access to the Stingray as well. Once it's available in your hangar, take it out for a spin and let us know what you think.
The main goal here is pretty straightforward: we want to find the things that are going to break when we put this in front of everyone on Live. If something feels off, breaks, gets weird, or just doesn't behave the way you think it should, let us know on Spectrum and the Issue Council.
Thanks for continuing to participate on the PTU, and we'll see you in-game!
