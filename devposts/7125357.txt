It's time for the final standoffs between your absolute favorite vessels!
After you determined the 16 most popular crafts by submitting countless amazing screenshots, videos, miniatures, 3D models, songs, and more to the Community Hub in Phase 1 of Ship Showdown, Phase 2 pitted the sixteen finalists against each other in ruthless daily head-to-head battles!

So, let's get to this year's Final Four!
- Crusader C1 Spirit - Crusader Industries brings its usual commitment to excellence directly to the civilian market with the C1 Spirit, a multi-purpose starship tuned for mid-range cargo delivery and courier work with enough versatility to tackle multiple roles and unpredictable jobs.
- Anvil Terrapin - Outfitted with long-range scanners and encased in heavy armor and oversized shields, the Terrapin faces hazardous circumstances with confidence, making it the ultimate combat scout and reconnaissance vehicle (at least according to Anvil).
- Aegis Reclaimer - The Aegis Reclaimer is equipped with a reinforced cargo bay and long-range jump drive that make it the ideal ship for taking advantage of deep-space wrecks. Tractor beams, floodlights, scanner options, and docking ports round out the tools on this capable utilitarian spacecraft.
- Anvil F8C Lightning -  Few vehicles inspire the same awe as the legendary F8 Lightning. A force to be reckoned with, it has protected Humanity countless times from countless threats at home and far from civilization.
