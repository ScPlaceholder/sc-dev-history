HI @Ellisor, to expand on @Bacchus' explanation, we had originally tied the naming into Recognition, so it was immediately apparent which ones were inhabited and which ones weren't, but in conversations with Chris, having planets exist solely as numbered names felt too cold to him. He wanted them to have a little flavor, even if they were desolate, so at the moment, we're still working our way through naming them (whether they're on the starmap or not, the idea was that the locals would at least have a name for them). Ultimately though, all of the planets have a Name/Number designation (we call it a planet code) that can exist as a way to make communication easier. Basically, it was an easy alternative to clearly indicate what planet you're talking about without requiring people to remember all the planet names, i.e. If I say that I'll meet you on Pike and you have no idea what I'm referring to, I can also say Terra-II and you can find your way there.
