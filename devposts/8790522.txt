Many of you have spotted it on the Public Roadmap or in recent PTU builds. The long-fabled Novian Crossbow from UltiFlex is making its Star Citizen debut in the upcoming Alpha 4.8.
Like most weapons in the persistent universe, it will come in multiple variants, found through loot, crafting, and more. But there's something extra for a specific group of backers.
If you supported both Star Citizen and Shroud of the Avatar (at the ‘Immortal Adventurer’ pledge level or above) between 2012 and 2014*, a special variant is waiting for you. Submit a Customer Service ticket here with a screenshot of your Shroud of the Avatar account page displaying your User ID and proof of purchase. We will verify each submission individually, and we appreciate your patience as we work through the process. Once verified, the variant will be added directly to your My Hangar section on the RSI website.
We cannot wait to see what you do with it.
---
*Or if you pledged at the 'Adventurer' level during the original Kickstarter promotion.
