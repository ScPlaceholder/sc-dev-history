Hello, everyone!
After recovering their strength and resupplying their forces in Pyro, the XenoThreat gang is back to terrorize Stanton.
Once again, the Civilian Defense Force (CDF) has put out a call for assistance and will honor their best defenders by granting a set of CDF branded armor.
As soon as the XenoThreat are thwarted, check the Delphi App within your mobiGlas to check your reputation level with the CDF. Throughout the event, as you progress through the reputation ranks, you'll unlock additional pieces of the bespoke armor set.
If this is the first time you're diving in to help the CDF, check out our XenoThreat Comm-link for new players.
Suppose you are missing pieces of the CDF armor from previous attacks by the xeno-phobic attackers? You now have the chance to complete your set during the current event.
