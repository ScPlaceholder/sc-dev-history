Greetings,
Reload Improvements have been integrated from Squadron 42 into Star Citizen for 3.23. This includes many quality of life additions such as Ammo repooling and backpack reloading! You can initiate the ammo repooling process by using the default hotkey "LALT + 1" with a used magazine.
Re-loading during tense situations has been overhauled to allow multiple reload types, allowing players to first reload from their equipment slotted magazines and then from their backpack/personal storage with a bit longer of a search time once those have been exhausted. This new functionality includes UI on the player HUD to let the player know where their next reload will come from and how many overall munitions they have left in all personal inventories for the currently held weapon. Empty mags will now result in them being tossed away instead of being re-attached to your suit.
Give these improvements a try and let us know what you think below! We are specifically interesting in hearing about:
- Does the duration of ammo repooling feel right? Does this mechanic feel slow and unusable mid-combat, or does it feel fast and overpowered?
- Are you able to easily tell what is happening while performing an ammo repool? Does there need to be more visual cues?
- Would you prefer to trigger an ammo repool from the inventory UI, Loot UI, or with a hotkey?
- Are you able to easily discern between doing a backpack reload or ammo repooling?
- Does the flow of performing an ammo repool or backpack reload make sense and feel intuitive?
Looking forward to hearing from you!
