Greetings, citizens!
MISC seamlessly integrates Xi’an technology with state-of-the-art Human engineering to redefine all aspects of contemporary space travel. From the versatility of the Freelancer to the fueling behemoth that is the Starfarer, MISC has the right ship for the right job.
Design a bumper sticker for any MISC ship to show that these ships are built for life. For inspiration and ideas, you can draw from features of bumper stickers we have in our world today as we’re sure that there’s been some on the road that have grabbed your attention. All kinds of colors, themes, shapes, and sizes are accepted as long as they fit the bumpers of a MISC ship and are appropriate for all audiences.
Before you get started, please read up on the complete contest rules.
Want to learn about MISC's latest and greatest? Check out the full details on the new MISC Hull A.
Entry Requirements
• Each bumper sticker is considered a single entry but you are allowed multiple entries (You can include multiple images in a single post/tweet).
• Posts on Twitter/Instagram must include the hashtags #gettingstickywithit and #StarCitizen.
• Sticker designs must be original work and include a Star Citizen reference.
• Entrants MUST either own or have the rights to all content used in their designs.
Deadline
The deadline for submissions is May 17, 2022, at 11:59pm Pacific.
Entries can be posted in the submission thread on Spectrum or Twitter/Instagram including the hashtags #MISCsticker and #StarCitizen. Entries posted below will not be considered.
Rewards
:first_place_medal: 1st Place: MISC Starfarer Gemini with LTI (in-game Lifetime Insurance)
:second_place_medal: 2nd Place: MISC Starfarer with LTI (in-game Lifetime Insurance)
:third_place_medal: 3rd Place: MISC Hull A with LTI (in-game Lifetime Insurance)
Good luck everyone! We can't wait to see what you come up with!
