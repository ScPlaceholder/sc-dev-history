Scope glint is on sniper scopes is positive for gameplay
It's designed to give some counter play to snipers as you'll see if you've played jump town, players can easily hide 200m and kill people, the cost of death is very high in Star Citizen so having something as powerful as a sniper rifle needs to have some drawbacks
It only appears when you are actively aiming down sight so you'll be able to choose when you want to reveal yourself
With wanting medical gameplay to be more present in the PU, injuries reduce max HP which allow for a lot of sniper to body shot 1HK if a person has been in a sustained fight, there overall needs to be effective counterplay towards snipers that aren't 'post shot' related as by then it is already too late
Now looking towards the future, things such as smoke grenades will allow players to counter act enemy snipers
