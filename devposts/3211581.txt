Hey @Meepowski, I can only give you a couple of pointers. Make sure that your camera runs with the highest possible FPS. If possible get a camera that supports 60 fps and make sure that the room is well lit. Also I'd recommend turning off any auto features (zoom, exposure, focus) as those usually drop the frame rate or cause loss of tracking. Sadly there is no unified way to turn those features off ... so that is a per camera driver setting.
More info is available here: https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/360033246154
