## Updating everyone with our edits to the FAQ, and clarifying the recent communications:
► Mission 5 prepares players for the final XenoThreat assault (Mission 6), which will begin soon after Star Citizen Alpha 3.23 goes live. Players must complete the first five missions before the final XenoThreat assault begins, as the previous missions will not be available during the XenoThreat event soon after
► Reward, F7A Mk II Hornet Upgrade: All players that complete missions 1 to 5 of Overdrive Initiative will receive a complimentary upgrade from the F7C Mk II Hornet to the F7A Mk II with the release of Alpha 3.23. When the new patch launches, just accept the mission titled ‘Overdrive Initiative: Reward’ and you’ll be granted your upgrade (and free 7 day rental).
► Reward, XenoThreat Armor: The XenoThreat medium armor will be granted to all players who complete each Overdrive Initiative mission, including the following XenoThreat Assault, Mission 6, in Alpha 3.23. Make sure that you complete the Overdrive Initiative missions 1 to 5 before the Xenothreat event starts after the release of Alpha 3.23.
