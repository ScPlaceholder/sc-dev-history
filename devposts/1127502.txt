At the point I was given the task of implementing the system, atmospheric flight was not supposed to be supported.    What we talked about then was that you could fly in space, but couldn't enter atmosphere.  The only way to cross the region between space and planet surface was supposed to be in the bay of a ship.  At some point, word came down that we wanted to support both atmospheric and over, and the plan was outlined to use strafe up to go from hover to flight, and strafe down to go from flight to hover.  I wasn't involved in any of those discussions.
But it was always expected that a Gravlev vehicle could fly in space and also hover, and that it could use thrusters in its hover mode.
