Greetings!
Nyx I has been populated with points of interest (POIs) using the Starchitect tool, which is a new technology that dynamically scatters content across a planetary scale to flesh out an entire world with places to explore. This pre-export tool is used by designers to generate the following hierarchy of items:
Nyx I (Planet) > Sectors (Like a country) > Clusters (Like a region) > Locations (A POI) > Tiles (Buildings, OC’s etc).
Each part of this hierarchy has been given their own properties, data and rules to define their identity, allowing us to make sure that every part of the planet feels unique. Every cluster in a sector has a singular Hub location which is visible on the Starmap, along with it’s amenities. The rest is found by exploration or by engaging in contracts.
A feedback survey is available at: Starchitect POIs Survey Form. This survey will be open until Nov 30.
Thank you for taking the time, and we look forward to reading your feedback!
