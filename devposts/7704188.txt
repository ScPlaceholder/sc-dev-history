The increments differ on optics for balance reasons, they are intentionally not a standard so different optics can behave in different way and have different use cases beyond aesthetics with certain manufactures in lore having more tech in their optics vs some being low tech but cheaper, etc
for example those with range finders have large increment increases for zeroing as to increase the skill expression for snipers
if you use something with a range finder, you're going to have to deal with larger increments on the scopes to compensate for that aid
Those without range finders have increments of 100 for example
So it's a trade off of feeling out a range vs being told and adjusting for it
Also gives us some nice room with things like crafting
As an example;
A higher tier scopes could have lower increments as well as having a range finder
Regarding the orange markers, due to planets having different gravity and snipers having different projectile velocity, there's not a great size fits all solution for these indicating range
