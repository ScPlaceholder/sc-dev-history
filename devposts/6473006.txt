BTK is a factor I've been looking at, the reason why a lot of the fire rates are higher is mainly because it looks really good with the new recoil system, more slower firing weapons tend to look less angry, what we've built is essentially an animation system so I'm also learning to pseudo animate at the same time as creating these recoils and found more success with higher fire rates, as time goes on and I build more of these, I imagine getting slower firing weapons in will look nicer, the P8AR you see in the hold line which fires slower is a good example of this, the P4 was the first weapon done with the new tool so that's why it's sped up.
Mag size is something I'm keeping an eye on, I want to avoid changing too much at once because then it's harder to track what is/isn't working.
Sniping, if it should 1 hit or not is always contentious, it'll come more into play when the armour work to make heavy more of a cost is factored in
But you should be able to 1 shot someone in the head if they are wearing medium with a high powered sniper.
Plus if someone is in a fire fight you can typically nab them before they heal, injuries included.
The P6 for example does 90 damage (before armour resis, limb multiplier) so you have a good chance to do it
With regards to how controllable they are, with longer TTK more controllable weapons are key because the skill is more in tracking the target then controlling the weapon.
I think we could increase the amount of recoil the weapons have a bit but with changing recoil and player health at the same time, going on the side of caution with this.
Overall a careful approach was taken with some of this work because I wanted to get it into players hands faster and then build up from there as opposed to lots of internal testing and then judging feedback before pushing it out
