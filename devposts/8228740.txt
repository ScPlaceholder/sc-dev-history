The wait is over: The Ship Showdown 2955 Final Four is here.
After weeks of incredible community creations in Phase 1, with countless fantastic screenshots, videos, even 3D models, songs, and more, we entered Phase 2 and its intense duels. Now, only four ships remain! They’ve fought through the brackets, earned your votes, and now stand just one step away from glory.
Each finalist secures a brand-new Best-in-Show paint, a manufacturer-themed poster, and a spotlight during Best-in-Show days at this year’s Intergalactic Aerospace Expo. But there can only be one champion: the true community favorite of 2955.
If you're unsure which ship deserves your vote: The Showdown Free Fly is on until September 2, giving you the chance to fly all of the Elite 8 ships for the remainder of the tournament.
And while you’re flying, check out our discounted Ship Showdown Starter Packs, only available during Ship Showdown.
But now, let’s get to this year’s Final Four!
- Anvil Asgard: A  heavily armored vehicle-dropship equipped for boundless resilience in the hottest combat zones. Power through relentless fire to deliver vital reinforcements, hold the front line, and keep the enemy retreating.
- Anvil Gladiator: The civilian model of the Gladiator appeals to those who want to explore the ‘Verse with a bit of added security. Supporting a maximum of two,  the Gladiator is perfectly equipped to explore and fight with or without a wingman. The Civilian model allows pilots to choose between an extra cargo hold or a bomb bay.
- RSI Zeus Mk II ES: Ready to ferry a whole new generation of travelers across the universe,  the Zeus Mk II pays homage to the classic ship that launched Humanity to the stars in 2140. With a focus on exploration, the Zeus Mk II ES  features a robust radar package for making new discoveries along with enhanced weaponry and shielding.
- RSI Polaris: Nimble corvette-class capital ship packing a powerful punch with a full armament of turrets and torpedoes, small craft hangar, and habitation for a crew of 12.
Be there or be square, visit the Ship Showdown page daily and vote for your favorite in the final rounds!
