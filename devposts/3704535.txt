that is an interesting edge case indeed. In general there are only two ways to move the aiming reticles ... either by head movement or by using the rotation inputs. We had independent gimbal movement axes in the past but they were not setup in a consistent way causing other conflicts. We already have it on the todo list to readd special gimbal axes which would enable you to move gimbals, ship and head independently ... though they would be tied it into another manual gimbal submode (i.e. LALT-G) so everyone is able to use them with any device.
As for the look input ... we have found a bug where the behaviour could slightly change if head tracking is active AND freelook active in the same moment. In that case we have a somewhat flipped condition on the manual aiming submode. We already have a fix for that (which is to not allow free look at all when head tracking is active) but it was too late 3.12. It will get submitted early next year.
