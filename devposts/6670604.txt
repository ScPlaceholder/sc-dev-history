## Welcome to the 'Stella Fortuna 2954 - Bannermonium Design Contest'!
Whether you prefer hearing "Zoom! Zoom!" or "Boom! Boom!", we're giving you a chance this Stella Fortuna to show your undying support for your favorite racing or combat teams in the 'verse by designing a fan banner! Don't have a favorite team? Don't sweat it; design a banner for a fictional team and share your team spirit anyway.
Push the limits of your creativity and tempt fate with your choice of materials because we're also bestowing the Most Ridonculous Material Award (and a prize ship!) to the design that awes us the most with the audacious use of non-traditional art supplies! Paint, yarn, macaroni, cheese.... if you can think of it, you can use it. Ready? Get set. Cheer(s)!
## Before You Begin
Before you start on your submissions, please read the contest rules carefully and remember to post your entries to the Stella Fortuna 2954 event page on the Community Hub and not as a reply to this thread.
:exclamation: Any submission not posted to the Community Hub and without either ‘Stella Fortuna 2954 Banner Design Contest – Racing’ or 'Stella Fortuna 2954 Banner Design Contest – Combat' in the post title will not be considered. :exclamation:
## Keep In Mind
- Read the contest rules! :book:
- Submissions must feature images of a design for a supporter banner or flag for your favorite Star Citizen racing or combat team, as could imaginably be seen being brought to racing or combat events in the 'verse.
- The racing or combat teams featured in the designs can either be real teams that exist in the 'verse or fictional teams if you have no preferred team you support.
- The banners or flags can be made either digitally with graphic design software or crafted as physical art and may incorporate the use of unconventional materials.
- Designs should inspire team spirit, and rallying slogans or catchphrases may be used.
- Please post your images to the Community Hub, specifically to the Stella Fortuna 2954 event page. Replies to this thread will NOT count as a submission.
- Use ‘Stella Fortuna 2954 Banner Design Contest – Racing’ to submit your racing-themed designs, or 'Stella Fortuna 2954 Banner Design Contest – Combat' to submit your combat-themed design, and select the Fan Art category on the Community Hub.
- Any submission not following the above instructions will not be considered.
- Group submissions are not allowed. Only the person posting the images can win a prize.
- You can only win 1 prize, either from the 'Racing' or ' Combat' category.
- The top 3 winners from either the 'Racing' or 'Combat' categories will NOT be eligible for the Most Ridonculous Material Award.
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your submissions on:
- Quality
- Creativity
- Execution
- How well your designs evoke team spirit
Bonus points will be awarded for the use of creative, non-traditional materials!
## Dates
The submission period will end on Monday, 25 March 2024, EOD 11:59 PM Pacific (Tuesday, 26 March 2024, 07:59 UTC).
