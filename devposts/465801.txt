While we are the most transparent development studio I have ever seen, there are still some things we need to keep hidden to protect the game and our players. Many critical and blocker issues can end up being things like frequent server and client crashes. If we were to strip out all of the sensitive information from those bugs, the list would basically look something like: client crash, server crash, server and client crash, etc.
I know I don't speak for everyone but at least to me, that's not interesting and is a lot of work.
The example uses crashes but the same can be said to many of our other bugs. Both here at CIG, and at other studios that I've worked at, there is often times a lot of internal information that goes into the bug writing process in order to better assist developers in getting the issue fixed quickly. In order to protect the integrity of the game, we'd need to strip all of that information out of the bugs which would be a huge amount of work without really adding much value to what burn down already covers.
TL;DR: A lot of the most critical and destructive bugs are boring to read about if they have all the internal information removed and it doesn't really add much value for the time it would take.
