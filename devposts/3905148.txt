After last week's 3.13 Patch Watch on the MedPen update and additional character customization options, we're following up with another feature announcement for the upcoming build.
To make the most of the all-new cave entrances (including sinkholes and vehicle-accessible openings), Alpha 3.13 introduces three new FPS mission types:
FPS Cave Assassination
Criminal players will be given targets in caves from mobiGlas-mission-giver Vaughn after completing one assassination for him. Players must navigate to a cave and find and kill a civilian target. (If the player is in a monitored zone, this will be classed as murder.) In support of this, the team fixed a bug that prevented players from receiving Vaughn's missions via mobiGlas if they had to reset their character.
FPS Cave Bounty
Lawful players will be asked by Crusader and Hurston security forces to hunt bounties in caves. These bounties will become available once the Crusader and Hurston Bounty introductory missions are completed. Players must navigate to a cave and find a criminal target protected by armed guards who will attempt to kill the player on sight.
FPS Cave Clear All
Players will be asked by Crusader and Hurston security forces to clear a cave of outlaws. Player’s must navigate to a cave and kill all the criminals they find inside.
