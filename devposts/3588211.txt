We can't really recommend any cameras for FOIP ... there are too many out there. What you should look out for is what camera "modes" they have. The recommended camera mode for FOIP in SC is 640 x 480 at 60+ fps. There are some cameras that allow 60 or even 120 fps but only in very low resolutions which will negatively impact the FOIP performance. If your camera has more fps at higher resolutions it will be less of a problem as we scale them down 640 x 480. But overall the fps is dominant factor on the FOIP performance. Whether or not the camera can hold up the fps in low light conditions is ofc another matter.
Happy hunting!
