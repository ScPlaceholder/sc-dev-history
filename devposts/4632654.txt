Hello everyone,
We've noticed reports that Alpha 3.15.1 is crashing for some players who have Overwolf installed on their machines.
This comes in the form of an exception access violation on game launch and relates specifically to Vulkan layers that Overwolf installs on the machine.
If you experience this crash, you should update your Overwolf installation to the latest version, as this has now been fixed. Uninstalling Overwolf will also remove the Vulkan layers from the system.
Keep in mind that while not always installed explicitly, Overwolf will sometimes be installed with other downloaded software, as certain programs use Overwolf's framework.
Thank you for your understanding, and apologies for any inconveniences this may have caused.
