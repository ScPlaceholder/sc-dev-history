@Blue_Ghost, when I talk about "available resources" I solely refer to the amount of available development time of our developers ... in addition to that it needs to be right developers for the right jobs. We all have different backgrounds and CVs, so not every developer is the best choice for every task (e.g. I for one have no experience in GFX coding, which is why although I am a coder I won't get assigned to GFX tasks).
The same principle applies to the input system. While we have a couple of people who have experience and knowledge of the input system, they are not available to work on it in the near future as they are bound by working on other parts of the game. CIG is always trying to employ more developers ... if you look up CIG's cooperate website, you will see that we have more than 80 open positions world wide at this time, a lot of them directly related to core gameplay development.
I know that this reply does not really help with the issue, but I hope it makes clear why there was no progress on this specific part of the game. It is not intentional, but we have to be very careful on how we balance our available resources vs the work we have to do.
