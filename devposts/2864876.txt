Hi! I mentioned this in my presentation when I introduced the Galactapedia, and in subsequent threads. We are publishing as many one to two paragraph entries as possible so we can get as many pieces of canon information out there as we can as fast as we can. As we build the Galactapedia, we will be expanding on existing entries to create full length ones, which necessarily take more time and care.
We will not be copy/pasting articles that we've written in the past because they do not fit the tone of the Galactapedia. Edit: Additionally, as we further solidify the universe, some information that has previously been published has changed. You can see this in the development of our star systems, adaptations to ships from their initial designs, the refinement of the Xi'an and Banu languages, etc.
