To clarify the topic, we have recently updated our Engineering Design Doc with what is currently in 4.5:
All components can currently be replaced or repaired (including wear & tear) at stations. While Size 1 and Size 2 components can be manually replaced while in flight, Size 3 and larger components need to be repaired or replaced while docked at a station.
Thanks for your feedback in this thread and others; we look forward to releasing 4.5 to all waves as soon as we can. In the meantime, enjoy a new Inside Star Citizen | Engineering Talkin' with Thorsten :fire:
