It's all being considered. While we do want allow a single small fighter to be technically able to grind through the shields of a much larger ship we want that the time to be a lot longer than it used to be. Capital sized ships (in terms of size that is a Connie or above) cannot maneuver easily so they are all supposed to be a lot tankier on the shields and hull. So the idea here being that you need to bring specialized equipment to bring them down fast. Heavier fighters are supposed to have an advantage in that area as they can dish out a lot more damage over time due to their higher weapon capacitor capabilities and bigger hardpoint sizes.
Concerning the shield display ... the supplied HP of the generator are split between all four faces on bigger ships. So even if we'd quadruple the shield HP they would still show up as 25 per face on shield display and that is intended. For bubble shields they show "100" but that is mainly because we were unable add a proper shield display for them ... so we decided to show 100 on every face for bubble shields ... because technically there is only one face if that makes sense. But UI wise we're currently restricted to always show quad faces.
