In 4.1 Ship Collision damage handling is now done using full collision data passed down by physics. This means mass and speed is now calculated in the collision and will make collision damage between all ships, big and  small, behave more realistically. This should be a major change to how collisions felt in the past, and we'd love to hear what you think!
We look forward to hearing your thoughts, and thanks for taking the time!
