Invictus Launch Week is upon us, and there's a ton of sights to see!
To encourage visiting every part of the event, we're kicking off a contest to see your best touristy photos of all the happenings during Invictus.
We'll be looking for posts that have the most variety in locations from the event, along with photos that fit the theme of being a tourist (if there were a Leaning Tower of Pisa, we would want you to try to keep it from falling in your screenshot).
Everyone is eligible to take part, even if you're just here for the Free Fly Event! Your submissions must include either shots from the show floor, shots from the Javelin Tour or the Navy fleet, shots including promotional clothing from the event, or a combination! Screenshots must be taken by you, be in their original format, and cannot have any image processing or effects applied (such as ReShade).
Post a gallery of up to ten screenshots in the comments below or on Twitter/Instagram (using hashtag #Invictus2951Tour) by Wednesday, June 2nd at 11:59pm Pacific for a chance to win one of these awesome packages:
