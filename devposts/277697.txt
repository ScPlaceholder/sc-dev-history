Hi, the planet surface in SC is generated based on a small amount of ecosystem layers disitrbuted on the planet, so it is not really meant to load and use a massive amount of Nasa raster data.
In case you are interested in a Mars looking planet I have shown on an old ATV an example of using some low res Mars data as base layer input for the planet tech, generating a Mars-looking planet.
Video here: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JqMsyLfItVw
Please note that if you watch a recent ATV video of Yela and/or Daymar the planet surface ground is much better looking now,  since we have made a lot of improvements since then...
