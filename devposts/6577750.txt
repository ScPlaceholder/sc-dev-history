This refers to the reload animation / action blocking other actions
Situation;
- you have 13/30 bullets in your P4
- you reload
- during this reload, you attempt to sprint, this action is blocked till the reload is over
- You finish reloading, 30/30
- you can sprint again
This change would be
- you have 13/30 bullets in your P4
- you reload
- during this reload, you attempt to sprint
- you enter sprint, cancelling the reload
