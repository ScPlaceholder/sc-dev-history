Alien Week 2956 begins on June 17, honoring First Contact Day and celebrating the rich cultures, history, and craftsmanship of humanity's closest galactic neighbors!
From iconic alien-inspired ships and community celebrations to creative contests and Arena Commander action, this year's festivities offer plenty of opportunities to discover what makes each species unique. Whether you're a longtime admirer of Banu trade traditions, Xi'an engineering, Tevarin resilience, or simply looking for your next adventure among the stars, Alien Week welcomes all citizens to join the celebration.
