Hey everyone!
Another year, another chance to connect with this incredible community. The Bar Citizen World Tour has evolved into something truly special. What began as a simple idea to reconnect after difficult times has grown into one of our favorite annual traditions.
Year after year, we've been blown away by the warmth and enthusiasm you bring to these gatherings. From bustling city centers to quieter corners of the globe, the spirit of Star Citizen shines through in every handshake, every conversation, and every shared story. These in-person moments remind us why we do what we do, and they've become as meaningful to us as we hope they are to you.
We're excited to confirm that the Bar Citizen World Tour will return in 2026! As always, we're counting on you to point us in the right direction. Tell us which cities and regions you’d love to see on the tour. Your suggestions help us choose where to go next and make sure every event - big or small - brings citizens together in the best possible way. And remember, you don't need to go big! Some of our favorite Bar Citizens kept it simple. A pub, some passionate pilots, and plenty of good conversation.
Not comfortable posting publicly? No problem. Feel free to reach out directly to me or any member of the Community Team (@Freyja-CIG, @Nicou-CIG, @Synthwave-CIG, or @Stamp-CIG) with your suggestions. We'll make sure every location gets proper consideration.
Here's to another year of adventures together. Drop your location ideas below, and we'll see you out there in 2026!
