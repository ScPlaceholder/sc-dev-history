Hello everyone,
Starting today, you will notice you now have the ability to reclaim items purchased from Voyager Direct with UEC. We made this functionality available in an effort to assist you with cleaning out some of your old items. When reclaiming an item, the item will be removed from your account and the value of the item will be added to your UEC ledger to use on other items in-game.
In addition to this functionality coming online, the Voyager Direct store has also been closed, and items previously available can now be found in-game.
For a step-by-step guide and additional details, check out our Knowledge Base article here: https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/360006492734
