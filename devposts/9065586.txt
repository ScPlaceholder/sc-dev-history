Hey y'all!
The notoriously vicious criminal gang known as Nine Tails has launched an unyielding barrage on Orison, slaughtering security and civilians without distinction. Its twisted agenda? To irreparably mar the image of the supposedly safe city and its governing body as a weak and failing experiment.
Crusader has sought our help and we're ready to dish out a little payback to these bloodthirsty extremists. Nine Tails has managed to block all air traffic to the area, so your assault team will have to deploy via secure shuttle to quash this insurrection before more lives are lost. This is not a contract for the weak, so squad up with the toughest you've got.
Alongside Siege of Orison, Alpha 4.10 rebalances fuel tank capacity and consumption across the ship roster, with refueling missions updated to match. It also introduces the Kruger S-65 Stingray.
