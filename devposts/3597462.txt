The final four days of 2950’s Intergalactic Aerospace Expo (IAE) begin this Sunday, November 29. This will be the last chance to take advantage of the Free Fly event and play Star Citizen for free. These final four days also offer everyone the chance to try out every ship from every manufacturer in the 'verse! Just head to one of the ship terminals and select your ship of choice. If you missed piloting the  Mercury Star Runner, the Consolidated Outlands Nomad, or any of the other 100+ ships on show during the expo, these final four days of IAE 2950 are your chance to do so – for free!
For more details on this year's IAE please head to the official site: https://robertsspaceindustries.com/iae2950
