As @CanguroGuro said, our default audio setting is the highest dynamic range, meant for quite high end playback systems where you can really crank it a bit :slightly_smiling_face:
If you want the level difference between the loudest and quietest sounds to be smaller then try the 'Medium' and 'Minimum' dynamic range settings in the audio menu. These weren't working for a couple of versions, I think, but they're definitely working now :slightly_smiling_face:
We're still testing these out as to whether three settings is enough. We might introduce a 'night mode' that reduces the dynamic range even further if we feel it's needed.
