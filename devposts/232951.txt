When I get stuck with a choice between two ships I ask myself, "What am I looking for?"
The Caterpillar is great for hauling lots of cargo, but the Aquila has versatility. I already have BMM, so I see the Caterpillar as less value to my fleet and I prefer versatility because I can't stick to doing one thing. Take a look at what you have now and see if either of those ships roles is missing from your fleet. That's how I try and pick between one or the other.
