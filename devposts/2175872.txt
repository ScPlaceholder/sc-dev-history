Greetings!
Ship customization is available on the website (PTU website for testers: https://ptu.cloudimperiumgames.com).  When pledging for a new ship with customization options available, there will be an additional button on the pledge store page to allow a player to apply their personal touches immediately after completing the pledge. If you already own a customize-able ship, there will be customize button on the pledge when viewed in the "My Hangar" interface. For this first release, you will be able to customize the 300 series hull color, cockpit finish, yoke, seat (model, material, and finish), bedding, decorations/flair, and select from various component packages (powerplant, cooler, weapons, ect). Prices are not final and are numbers that are being used during the testing phase, which is an area we welcome input on.
We are collecting feedback on this process for the following topics:
- Pricing
- In-game results
- Cosmetics
- Loadout kits
- Website flow
There's also a general survey that covers some of the ship cosmetics found here: https://forms.gle/vSdRPnBaohCkcPTd6
Issues and bugs should be reported to the customizer issue council.
Let us know what you think below and, as always, upvote shared sentiment.
