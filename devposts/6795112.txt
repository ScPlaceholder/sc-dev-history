We already have higher res versions of many textures (but certainly not all), but we don't want to make existing builds significantly larger, especially as textures already make up the largest part of the build. As such, my main interest for now is on any textures which appear lower res than others. The planet ground textures probably fall into this category, and so we'll be re-assessing these textures as part of our next iteration of the planet tech where we'll decide on whether we increase the resolution/texel-density/quantity of planet textures.
As for increasing the resolution of ALL textures, that would more than double the size of our builds, which then comes with a very substantial cost in distribution, especially when we frequently release patches. This also requires significant changes on the dev-ops and installer side to manage two build/package types. As such it probably feels unlikely before 1.0, but I haven't recently checked with the execs on that as they would be the ones to actually make the decision.
