that's a really interesting perspective, the rotational force on srv was added to make the game feel more interesting. From physics side it is a additional simulated rotational force based on deflection from towed ship and is not cause by the main towing force. The main force is always added through center of mass since that makes it much more stable. tbf when working on it I was thinking more in context of trucks/cars where you'd often have some rotational force on the towing vehicle.
