Balancing of capacitor sizes is still ongoing, specifically for the weapons we're currently in the process of trying out different things which will change how much ammo you'll have in the guns.
However keep in mind that weapon capacity is always shared across all guns, so if you monoboat with energy weapons you naturally will get less shots out of them. You can try that out by powering certain weapons off, you will see that the capacity will then be applied to other weapons. Also try to be selective which weaponry you mount. Energy weapons with low projectile velocities are in general "cheaper" for the weapon capacitor so they are able to dish out a longer continuous bullet stream.
