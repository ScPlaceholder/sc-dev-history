Out of interest, how much perception of the world around do people think you have when using a scope in real life?
There a no easy options for scopes, you need to sacrifice either accuracy/quality/performance etc... however one approach is to accept the quality loss on the main view (outside the scope) and not within the scope. This way what you see through the scope is highly detailed with the full rendering features, but what is outside the scope needs to sacrifice in some way. This would be my preference, but it has the fundamental flaw that we'd need to somehow transition the main camera from the full quality render to the reduced quality one, and this would cause a big ugly 'pop'. One potential workaround to both the pop and the general quality loss could be to blur the content outside the scope, such that we don't really notice the quality drop or the pop, but when I've floated this idea internally some people are insistent that the world outside the scope remains crystal clear.
I'm interested to see what folks think of the above suggestion. I should note that this wouldn't be trivial to implement, so it's not like we could just flip a switch and use this in the next release, but it'd be nice to know if there's a potential path forwards which has sensible performance.
