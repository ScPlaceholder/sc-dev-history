I also agree that the trigger for atmo entry effects are currently not ideal, however they are the compromise we have found that works best with the current system and scale of planets.
Before Leo (Senior VFX programmer) changed the values to what they are now we were using a more realistic approach using density and speed. The problem with that is due to the way the planets are set up currently and the speed limits on ships the effects themselves were barely triggering and were only doing so when almost at ground level. As a compromise that works for both the expectation of the player and the aesthetic of the atmo entry experience the values are "artistically" nudged.
When coming up with the look I also thought about gameplay implications of atmo entry, angle of attack, potential damage from bad entry, orbital mechanics using orbital QT as a slingshot to get you enough speed to trigger the effects using the more physical approach we wanted etc. This might come later with an expanded more granular orbital QT but no eta on that.
In any case the basis of the visual effects system is there and because it is very easy to assign atmo entry to any new ship, variant or rework it means we can focus on making other aspects of the ship experience look cool.
Hope that answers your question,
