This is the action matrix work
It handles the process of action blocking / queueing, You'll be able to queue up a charge action during the down time
It'll work like this;
- Hold LMB to charge up devastator
- Release LMB to fire
Live
During that release, if you tap to hold another charge you won't be able to since the charge is on 'cooldown' meaning you feel as if you got a lost input and nothing will happen
Action matrix
But with the action matrix work you'll essentially 'queue' a charge to start as soon as possible meaning you don't have to wait for the weapon reset, it works as you'd expect in other words
Stuff like this people  don't overall notice but leads to that 'janky' feeling people use
It'll also work like this with single fire weapons (IE you tap just the cooldown you'll 'queue' the action) similar to other games
