## :bulb: Keep In Mind
► Read the contest rules: ⁠Guide to Professions Contest 2023 carefully.
► Multiple entries are permitted, but only one prize per participant.
► You are allowed to submit only one video by category.
► Videos must only be in English.
► Videos must be made within patch 3.21.1 (or subsequent point patches if applicable within the contest period).
► Group submissions are not allowed. A video may feature multiple players, but only the participant who submits the video is eligible to win a prize.
► The video must not exceed 180 seconds.
► The video must focus on a single profession, and you must add the category's name in the title of the video: [Mining] Title
► Submissions must be at least 1920x1080 resolution and uploaded to the Community Hub.
► Music/sound/effects should not infringe on any third-party copyright. This means you're able to use originally created/sourced music/effects or official Star Citizen music/effects.
► All submissions must be submitted to the ⁠Community Hub to be valid entries.
► Video must include the ‘Made by the Community’ logo at the end for at least 3 seconds. You can find the logo in our ⁠Fan Kit.

► Rule Adherence
