The work on headtracking and freelook was simply maintenance work to reduce further coding costs in the future. Head tracking and freelook went through separate code paths in the past (with a huge rats tail on it) and now it's performing a lot better and bugs are a lot easier to fix and it's easier to extend going forward without caring whether a player uses freelook or a headtracking device. And that is saving development costs in the future.
There is absolutely nothing special about it, it happens every day in software development. It's a bit unfortunate that we haven't had time to update the data yet, but those are perks of early access and PTU builds. It's definitely not broken ... the angles are slightly narrower (which will be fixed in the future) and there is a bit of an issue with EVA (which will be fixed as well) ... but that's hardly game breaking.
