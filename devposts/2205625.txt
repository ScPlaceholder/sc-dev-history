Hi everyone,
We have seen the feedback on Spectrum in regards to our recent screenshot contest, and it has all been noted and will be discussed with the team.
In regards to going around the no-fly zone to capture a unique screenshot, we understand that some feel this provides an unfair advantage. While it was not against the rules, we still hear your feedback and will take that into consideration when planning our next contest.
I'd also like to share our thoughts on repeat winners and give some additional insight into how the top 3 are selected. Each contest, we spend some time sorting through submissions and narrow it down to a list of finalists. This list can vary between 10 and 30 participants, based on the number of total submissions. Once we've built a list of finalists, we create a document that we share out to many other departments/teams for voting. If someone wins more than one contest, it genuinely means that their work caught the eye of team-members, and they managed to acquire a high number of votes in this process. There have been nearly half a dozen participants that have made the podium more than once, and that's a testament to the time and effort they have put in to mastering their craft. Every contest, you all raise the bar and the submissions become harder to judge. We encourage this progression in the spirit of competition, but also fun.
With all of that said, we are still closely monitoring feedback and evaluating ways to both innovate and improve the various contests we organize throughout the year. Last year, we organized 20 contests across a variety of disciplines such as screenshot, video, short story, caption, tutorial creation, emote design, pumpkin carving, and more. We plan to keep this momentum and we're always on the lookout for what types of creative contests you would like to see next.
