I'm obviously not familiar with the details of their tech, but I'm pretty sure it's not even an issue for them as they just render the satellite image at a distance (just a 2D photo projected onto the surface generated from height data), and the clever bit for them is somehow identifying these 'large green areas' in a photo as forests of a particular type of tree, and then spawning individual trees when you're up close. So they have almost the opposite problem to us, they start with the forest and have to generate the trees. We don't have the luxury of unique satellite images of the entire planets, and so we need to generate this on the fly. This would make sense to do on the GPU, but would require the GPU to have full knowledge of the asset spawning logic which is currently only on the CPU. So it's relatively clear how this could be solved, and is just a question of prioritization vs everything else.
