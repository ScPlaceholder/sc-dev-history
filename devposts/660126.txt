There was a slight change recently: You can put a small 1 unit box loosely into your ship, but when you buy commodities they populate the cargo grid of your ship with 1 SCU boxes, even if partially full, which locks your physical space down so you can't get like a hundred different commodities into the grid in a bunch of small boxes. Buying into a certain commodity requires some physical investment so that will be part of your decision making when doing cargo runs.
