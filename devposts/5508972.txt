Ready to explore this IAE Drake-style, with action instead of queuing? Try out the ships for yourself and take on our daily screenshot challenges!
Welcome to the IAE 2952 Screenshot Contest by Drake Interplanetary!
Here’s how it works:
Starting on November 18 (8am Pacific / 4pm UTC), different ship manufacturers take over an expo hall every day. There’s something new to see every day, and nearly everything on display is available to test-fly for free!
We’re looking for the best screenshots that encapsulate each day of the IAE. However, each day features a specific Drake-style challenge for you to take on in your screenshots!
We have 10 days of challenges for you - that means ten amazing screenshots and ten times the chance to win a ship!
All you have to do is visit this thread daily and prove that you have what it takes to live life in Drake style!
## Keep In Mind
- Read the contest rules! :book:
- Submit your most epic screenshot highlighting the featured IAE manufacturer for the day, either in the respective submission thread on Spectrum or as a reply to the challenge announcements on Twitter using the hashtags #IAEbyDrake & #StarCitizen.
- Each daily challenge will have specific requirements so please read carefully to ensure your submissions qualify. :exclamation:
- Only post screenshots that include the requirements of the daily challenges. Entries focusing on other subjects will be disqualified.
- Unless specified in the requirements of the daily challenges, where the screenshots are taken is up to you. They don’t necessarily need to be taken at the IAE venue.
- You have 24 hours after each official challenge announcement to add your image to the corresponding Spectrum submission thread or as a reply to the challenge announcement post on Twitter using the hashtag #IAEbyDrake. Submissions posted after this cut-off will not qualify.
- Images that include player handles will not qualify.
- Consider turning off the HUD when taking screenshots as this might make for better composition. Check out an excellent screenshot tutorial by DigThat32 or Mr. Hasgaha.
- Post-processing is allowed but remember that sometimes less is more.
- Make it count: only one (:one:) entry per challenge.
- No group submissions. You may include your friends in the screenshot, but only the one submitting is qualified to win.
- Players can only win once during the contest.
- Winners of the daily challenges will be announced on the day of the next challenge issued.
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your screenshots on:
- Quality
- Creativity
- Rule Adherence
## Challenges:
Day 1 - Drake Interplanetary:
To the edge of the universe and beyond.
Instruction: Capture the feeling of “no limits, no fears” in the newly-flyable Drake Corsair.
Day 2 - Origin Jumpworks
Far-Flung Space safaris are no longer purely the domain of the privileged. No matter who you are, what you do, or where you come from, you too can see the sights of distant worlds.
Instruction: Take a selfie in a remote place with the Origin 600i in your favorite exploration gear.
Day 3 - Aegis Dynamics
“You should’ve seen the other ship.”
Instruction: Show us your best shot of a battle-worn Eclipse.
Day 4 - Aopoa, Banu, Esperia, Gatac
Defend what’s yours. Defend your freedom.
Instruction: Show us your mettle in combat with the Banu Defender.
Day 5 - Anvil Aerospace
What do you do when the chips are down? True heroes leave no one behind in times of crisis.
Instruction: Share your most dramatic moment with the new Anvil C8R Pisces.
Day 6 - Argo, CNOU, Greycat, Kruger
“Bring some attitude to the altitude, Steve!”
Instruction: Submit your sickest aerial shot of the Greycat STV. Extra points for creativity.
Day 7 - Crusader Industries, Tumbril Land Systems
Who doesn’t like a good ol’ honky-tonk? We wanna see you dance, pilgrim.
Instruction: Take a picture of you dancing on, in front of, or near the Tumbril Nova.
Day 8 - MISC
Dangerous jobs, deadly locales.
Instruction: Park the MISC Prospector at or near GRIMHEX and take an image of the ship with the station in it.
Day 9 - Roberts Space Industries
Goofing around the Galaxy has never been this easy.
Instruction: Go to the holoviewer and show us your best Drake behavior at the exhibit in front of the RSI Galaxy.
Day 10 - Best In Show
Which ship do YOU think can stand up and challenge Drake’s dominance? Stand by your choice. Literally.
:incoming_envelope: Instruction: Take a picture in front of the ship that you think should have won in Ship Showdown 2952.
