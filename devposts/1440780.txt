## Curious which emotes they'll be directing? Join us at CitizenCon to find out!
As shown countless times, the Star Citizen community has a wealth of talent and passion when it comes to content creation. If you're attending CitizenCon in person this year, you can win the opportunity to leave your mark on the ever-expanding Star Citizen universe like never before.
Ticketholders can win the opportunity to direct a Motion Capture actor (male/female), LIVE and in person at the event on October 10th in Austin, Texas.
So, grab a camera, read up on the rules, and record yourself acting out an emote that you think should be added to the Star Citizen universe!
We will review all submissions,  select up to 4 winners, who will then be given the opportunity to direct a professional Mo-cap actor (male/female) live in front of an audience at the event on October 10th in Austin, Texas.
Submissions will be accepted until 11:59 PM Pacific, on the 16th of September 2018.
Please review the Contest Rules here: https://robertsspaceindustries.com/CitizenCon-2948-Emote-Contest
How to submit an entry
- Applications must be sent via email to EmoteContest@Cloudimperiumgames.com
- Submission should include your RSI Account Credentials such as username and registered email
- Submission should be in video format and should include a brief introduction that explains your emote (less than 30 seconds)
- Submitted emote must be under 15 seconds
- Give us the /command that you think should be used for this emote
- Sitting/Standing is allowed (Cockpit/bench/bar for sitting)
- Nothing offensive/inappropriate/no nudity (please see our Terms of Service)
- Multiple entries are allowed
- No props
Judging Determination:
- Quality
- Originality
- Rule adherence
- Fits into the Star Citizen universe/lore
Good luck everyone, looking forward to seeing those emotes!
