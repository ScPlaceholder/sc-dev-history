## Alpha 4.11
DGS Planet Subdivision
Implementing a tech that allows us to subdivide planets and moons into smaller territories, so a single Dynamic Game Server (DGS) is never responsible for an entire body. Territory boundaries are generated from spatial clustering of seeded entities scattered by Genesis, and dynamically assigned to available DGSs, improving load distribution and paving the way for finer‑grained server meshing at planetary scale. Nyx 1 will be the first planet to use this tech.
:scroll: This card has been added and set to Tentative.
Nyx I
Nyx I is a coreless planet that orbits closest to the sun in the Nyx system. Once considered impossible to terraform, Nyx I has undergone a drastic transformation thanks to new experimental technology developed by Genesis Terraforming and now features a robust ecosphere capable of supporting a wide diversity of life.
:scroll: This card has been updated with a new image.
Nyx II
With high temperatures and a high-pressure atmosphere dense with thick clouds, Nyx II is an inhospitable planet where protective gear is recommended. While some resources dot the surface, the risk involved in harvesting them has left the planet almost void of commercial development.
:scroll: This card has been updated with a new image.
Nyx III
Nyx III is an ice giant that lacks a breathable atmosphere and is considered a difficult candidate for expansive commercial resource extraction.
:scroll: This card has been updated with a new image.
