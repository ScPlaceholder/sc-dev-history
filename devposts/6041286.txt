Greetings, Citizens! Are you feeling extraterrestrial yet?
We hope so, because Alien Week is here! From June 15 through June 26, we celebrate all things alien and First Contact Day, commemorating Humanity's initial interaction with the Banu in 2438.
Whether you serve up scintillating Xi'an-spiced snacks, throw a Tevarin-themed party, or create beautiful Banu baubles, don't forget to share your alien festivities with us on the Community Hub!
As part of our celebration this year, there are some exotic paints (both new and returning favorites), a "First Encounter" community contest, drinks with the devs on our International Bar Citizen Weekend, and time-limited offers that give you the opportunity to pick up advanced alien technology ships befitting your spacefaring adventures. Banu see, Banu do. Banu got ships for you!
