There's some randomness but it isn't 'random', it's very intentional
Break it down like this
Hand recoil (visual)
Aim recoil (literal)
Hand recoil
Hand is your your hand movement whilst a shot occurs, so procedural hand movement,
This doesn't effect the accuracy of a weapon expect for SMGs and Pistols (as of 3.23, even then it's reduced, 3.22 has it in some places it shouldn't).
This can effect accuracy by having 'barrel offset' meaning that your shots are not aligned with your sights (think a less extreme pre recoil change tarkov)
Overall this is what sells the weapon 'feel' with how much the weapon jumps around and is angry on screen basically, with the new system we have completely control of where and when with this.
This is random to an extent (that being going on between shots) in that there's a base value with noise on top of it but it's all very deliberate.
Look at something like the LH86 for lots of hand recoil
Aim recoil
Aim recoil is how much the weapon 'kicks up' horizontal and vertical, think what you'd call a 'recoil pattern' and the consistency of this is highly dependent on the weapon type and manufacturer.
IE Behring is standard military type so they're consistent with very little noise but kastak arms have more inconsistent patterns with far more noise and a lower base value .
The reason the term 'procedural' is used is because it's generated as data points as opposed to animated in things like Maya
