Get into the Halloween spirit with this year's Pumpkin Carving Contest!
Choose a pumpkin, pick up your multitool and start carving a Star Citizen themed jack-o-lantern. When you’re done, share pictures of your creation on Twitter for a chance to win one of the rewards listed below. All submissions must have #StarCitizen and #Halloween hashtags included to be valid entries into the contest.
Don’t forget to light the pumpkins with a candle or other light source, to nail those Halloween feels, and make sure you tell everyone what the pumpkin is supposed to be, just in case someone doesn’t know his Avenger Stalker from his Warlock.
There are no restrictions in pumpkin size either, AND if you can’t find a pumpkin in your area, a very large potato will do!
We’ll share the best creations on our social channels for all to see and enjoy!
Before you get started, make sure to read up on the rules here.

2nd Place: Standalone Constellation Andromeda - LTI
3rd Place: Standalone Tumbril Cyclone RC - LTI
Contest ends: 10/29/18 (11:59 PM Pacific)
