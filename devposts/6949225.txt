Happy Pride Month, everyone! The 'verse is a welcome place for ALL, and we want to celebrate each and every one of you!
We invite each of you to celebrate Pride all month long by showing us your colors! There are no limits to your creativity for this contest. You can take a screenshot in-game, craft a card, bake a cake, or make a video. The sky is the limit. Just make it as colorful as possible!
As is tradition, for this contest, we won't have the usual 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place! Instead, we're simply going to give away TEN Crusader C1 Spirits, complete with LTI and the Allegiant paint! We'll be on the lookout for submissions we think are incredibly awesome!
Multiple submissions are allowed, but no group submissions, and you are only eligible to win one C1 Spirit. Your submission must be related to Star Citizen, but that is the only restriction. Any medium of creativity is allowed.
Post an image on the Community Hub with the title "Pride Month 2954", or on Twitter (using hashtag #PrideMonth2954), by June 24th at 11:59 pm Pacific for a chance to win one of ten:
- Crusader C1 Spirit Standalone Ship with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance) and Allegiant Paint
Be sure to check out the Contest Rules before submitting!
We can't wait to see your submissions! From all of us at Cloud Imperium Games, Happy Pride Month! :rainbow-flag:
