There are quite a few considerations we need to account for to get it right, such as dev resources on the ship and lighting teams, performance impacts from multiple players casting massive shadow casting lights (the eternal struggle) in concentrated areas, balancing flight via instrumentation and/or visuals, whether certain ships or types of ships would have unique exterior light/headlight setups, and how to balance them, etc.
