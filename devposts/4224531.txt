If a missile locked on a lawful target hits a target that it was not locked on it will, for now, cause the player who launched the missile to get a crime. We have it down as a bug, but it could not be tackled for 3.14 due to it requiring a significant rewrite and being such an edge-case since once a targeted missile loses lock it travels forward in dumb fire so you would have to be quite unlucky. If you fire a torpedo dumb fire and it hits any allies, that's considered a crime fullstop as we have no way to determine your intention.
