The primary difference in this situation is that the Phoenix was sold individually with insurance.  The URSA was not.  The URSA will need insurance to function properly in our universe, so we are retroactively adding it.
When the Connie variants were introduced, we were in a period where LTI had "ended" and was no longer being offered, so it came with 6-month insurance.  It was only feedback from the community that brought the return of LTI to concept ships later.
In subsequent years, the Phoenix has been offered with varying insurances all the way up to 48-Month insurance.   As it has always had insurance, and there is a solution available to players already to get one with LTI should they choose to, we have no plans for retroactive action regarding the Phoenix or other Connie variants at this time.
With regards to the Greycat, we're still working with Design to determine the best course of action there.
