The tuning process is still happening as we speak. The HPs reported by that tool are not the actual HP the ship has. Taking a quick look at the numbers it seems that the tool is blatantly adding up the HP from all the single parts of that ship. That is by no means an indication for the actual durability of the ship as it includes parts which have little to no meaning for the overall ship's effectiveness. Some ships have more parts and are more detailed than others, but that does not mean that they are automatically stronger. The actual "effective" HP is far below any of numbers on that screenshot. You can expect similar combat performance from all Hornet variants when it comes to their durability in 3.14.
