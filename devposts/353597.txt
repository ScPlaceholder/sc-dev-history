We've heard this response many times over the years: a fear of losing one's ship that is negated by LTI.  We don't want that to be the case.
We anticipate having a system in place that will warn and remind you when you're insurance is close to or has already lapsed, same as in the real world.  We're not trying to take anyone's spaceship away from them.  If you lose your ship because you haven't renewed your premium, we expect that will be a deliberate decision on someone's part.
Insurance is a minor element intended to add more robust sense of authenticity to the universe, not a complex system designed to cause you to lose your belongings because you aren't paying attention.  I'd invite you to check out our recent Happy Hour to see more about how simple we intend the system to be.  LTI really is just the most minor of conveniences.
