The Vehicle Command Module has been added in 4.8, which is a new ship pairing mechanic where a smaller, fully pilotable command ship takes control of a larger host ship that cannot fly on its own. This applies to both the Drake Caterpillar and Drake Ironclad series of ships.
A feedback survey is available at: Vehicle Command Module Survey Form. This survey will be open until June 15.
We look forward to reading your feedback!
