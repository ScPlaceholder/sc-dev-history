The trim is just a helper for left handed sticks ... setting a trim just "remembers" the current throttle position and treat that as the new velocity goal for the devices center position. That is mostly useful for a left handed stick as they are spring loaded and always return to the center. So it allows you to keep going forward (or backward) without needing to push the stick forward all the time.
For a HOTAS you really don't need it as a HOTAS is not spring loaded.
