Your GPU likely has 8GB of VRAM, and if you pick higher resolutions graphics settings it can be quite easy to exceed that, so make sure you're under this. If you still have issues then try DX11 and report back here.
DX11 drivers do a lot more 'magic' behind the scenes to make games that are over-budget still function OK, but Vulkan does no such thing (and due to it's design it cannot).
