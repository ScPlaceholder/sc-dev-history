## Features & Gameplay
Gameplay
Missile Cargo Grid Visuals & Ship Servicing Improvements
Missiles/Torpedoes placed on a cargo grid will now display a bounding box frame around them, giving a clearer visual reference for the space each missile occupies. This frame is visible anywhere a cargo grid is present, including ship interiors and freight elevators. When snapping a missile into position, both the missile and its frame are now shown in the hologram preview, and players can choose the snapping direction during placement. Additionally, capital ships no longer need their pivot point fully inside a servicing zone to access hangar services, any portion of the ship overlapping the zone is now sufficient.
Vehicle Hit Marker Updates
Further hit marker polish and updates for hitting components, armor penetration, and shields.
