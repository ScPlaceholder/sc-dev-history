There won't be axis assignments for the Grin Roc mining arm in 3.12 but we're adding a couple of improvements to make the Grin Roc easier to use. We're adding VJoy controls to wheeled vehicles and just like in ships you can slave the mining arm to the VJoy or your look direction (via LALT-G) during manual aiming mode. We'll also a button to "freeze" the current aiming vector (it will RALT-G).
