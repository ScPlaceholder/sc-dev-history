Of course, we also like to thank everyone else who submitted an entry and hope that your Day of the Vara celebrations have been spooktacular!
It's time again to carve the most magnificent Star Citizen-inspired jack o'lantern! So, grab your Multi-Tool or whatever instrument you prefer and get to work on that pumpkin (or any fruit or vegetable that does the job)!
Welcome to the 2022 Pumpkin Carving Contest!
Get into the Halloween spirit by carving your very own Star Citizen-themed jack o'lantern. Share pictures of your magnificent creation on Spectrum, Instagram, or Twitter (the latter two must contain the hashtags #StarCitizen and #Halloween) for the chance to win one of our fabulous prizes. And don’t forget to light your jack o'lantern to make your design shine.
:warning: Before you start creating, please read the contest rules carefully [https://robertsspaceindustries.com/Halloween-Pumpkin-Carving-Contest2022] and only submit your entries to either:
- This thread as a reply
- Instagram or Twitter using the hashtags #StarCitizen and #DayOfTheVara2952
Any submissions not posted to one of these locations will not be accepted.
## Keep In Mind
- Read the contest rules [https://robertsspaceindustries.com/Halloween-Pumpkin-Carving-Contest2022]
- Multiple entries are permitted, but only one prize per participant
- Group submissions are not allowed
- Only post shots that include carved pumpkins (or pumpkin substitutes)
- All submissions must be submitted to the Spectrum thread, or posted on Twitter or Instagram including both #StarCitizen and #Halloween to be valid entries
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your submissions on:
- Style
- Creativity
- Rule Adherence
## Dates
The submission period ends on November 1, 2022 (EOD 11:59 PM Pacific)
