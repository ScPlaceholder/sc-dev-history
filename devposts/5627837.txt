Thank you very much for playing our Salvage release so intensively already and of course thank you for all the valuable feedback you have been providing so far.
Thanks to you all playing we were able to collect some hard data on the profits and material gathered with the Vulture and the Reclaimer.
With this data we noticed as you all already pointed out that the profitability on the Reclaimer can be buffed a little (especially having in mind that you have to pay min 3 players with your profits)
Let me give you a list of what we updated:
- We increased the thickness of the hull a little to increase your yield (an intact Titan gives you approx. 2SCU of RMC)
- We increased the efficiency for all scraper beams for the Reclaimer (all scraper variances will run more efficiently on the Reclaimer compared with the vulture)
- We increased gathering speeds for all scraper beams to maintain the timings with the updated thickness of the hull material
TLDR:
Vulture should be still as profitable as it was before (maybe a little more), but Reclaimer should be way more profitable.
Cheers and looking forward to more of your feedback.
