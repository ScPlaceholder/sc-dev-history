As we spread the holiday cheer this year, we thought it was an excellent opportunity to remember that not everyone around the 'verse celebrates "Christmas." We want to be mindful of other celebrations and therefore want to concentrate on the lore aspect, which means Luminalia instead of real-world holidays. Sure, add Star Citizen related things to your sweater design, but stay away from items associated with historical terrestrial festivities. We want to integrate the winning design into the game, so stick to the lore!
