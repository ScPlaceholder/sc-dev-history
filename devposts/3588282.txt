If you take a look at FOIP preview window you might be able to see what's going on (hit the big calibrate button). It should clearly indicate where FOIP fails to pick up your facial features. Sometimes it's a beard, sometimes it's lack of contrast on the lips or similar, sometimes it's hair or very strong glasses.
Since FOIP is based on image recognition it is possible that certain facial features just throw it off.
Also pressing the reset button sometimes helps readjusting the face.
