CitizenCon 2948 is about celebrating the current and future developments in Star Citizen. At the same time, CitizenCon is also about celebrating the incredible community that has continued to evolve around the BDSSE. With that in mind, we’re happy to announce that we’ll have limited space available at the Long Center in Austin, TX for Citizens to represent their Organisations, and maybe even recruit the next ace to join their fleet.
Each booth will be roughly 4-6m wide and 3m deep. There’s enough space for up to four Orgs to represent, so if we recieve more than 4 applications, we'll start a selection process to select candidates.
Applications will be accepted until 11:59 PM Pacific, on the 3rd of September, 2018.
## How to submit an entry
- Applications must be sent via email to orgbooths@cloudimperiumgames.com, using the .pdf format
- Applicants must be a member of a dedicated org with a manifesto and a clear role in the universe
- Applicants must show a logistical capability to follow-through on their application
- Applicants must assign a Point of Contact who is available via Skype for an interview if shortlisted
- Applicants must submit designs of printed material, gifts, souvenirs, booth design, etc. that are planned to be showcased during the event for clearance
## Application
- Please provide a link to your Org page on robertsspaceindustries.com
- Does your Org have an external website? If yes, please provide a link
- Why do you want your Organization to have a booth at CitizenCon?
- What would be the purpose of your booth?
- Have you done something similar before?
- Does your Org already have existing logos?
- Please provide your booth design, including any lighting, art, materials, images, and text
- Please provide a Point of Contact, include their name, handle, email, and Skype
- Incomplete applications cannot/will not be considered
- Content is subject to the requirements of our Terms of Service.
## FAQ
Do Org booths have to be staffed all day?
In short: yes.  Booths need to be staffed continuously from 9am on Wednesday, the 10th of October (access from 8am), through until 7pm, with the exception of the opening ceremony.  Venue security will be on-site before/after these times. After 10pm, booths will be expected to commence their strike, with final strike and loadout completed on Thursday October 11th between 8am and 2pm. Teams will be able to tag-out, provided at least one person covers the booth throughout the required times above.
Do Org booth staff get free access to CitizenCon?
All booth teams must have tickets/access to CitizenCon
Will any gear/facilities be provided?
All Org booths will have access to power/internet - some simple furniture may be available upon request
