Greetings, Citizens!
As we kick off this year's Intergalactic Aerospace Expo (IAE), the biggest ship show in the 'verse, we want YOU to be a part of the creative celebration in our very first 'Design a Convention Badge Contest!'
## Welcome to the 'IAE 2953 Design Convention Badge Contest'!
If you were attending IAE 2953 in real life and were given a convention badge as a memento, what would you want it to look like? This is your chance to flex your creative muscles, so feel free to use vibrant colors, patterns, or even in-game screenshots to make your designs shine; the sky's the limit! Don't forget to incorporate 'Intergalactic Aerospace Expo 2953' or 'IAE 2953' in the badge design.
Please read the instructions below carefully and use one of the templates provided! Submit your creations to the Community Hub for a chance to win a shiny, new ship fresh off the manufacturing line!
Disclaimer: This contest is just for ships and giggles, and the creations generated from it will NOT be making their way into the game!
## Before You Begin:
Before you start on your submissions, please read the contest rules carefully and remember to post your entries to the Community Hub and not as a reply to this thread.
:exclamation: Any submission not posted to the Community Hub or without 'IAE 2953 Design Convention Badge Contest' in the post title will not be considered. :exclamation:
## Keep in Mind:
• Read the contest rules :book:!
• Only submit entries that include a design of a convention badge that could imaginably be given to attendees of the Intergalactic Aerospace Expo 2953 (IAE 2953). Any other subject will be disqualified.
• Use 'IAE 2953 Design Convention Badge Contest' as the title for your submission on the Community Hub, and select the 'Image' and 'Fan Art' categories.
• Designs should include either 'IAE 2953' or 'Intergalactic Aerospace Expo 2953'.
• Submissions must use one of the templates provided and only digital or computer-generated designs will be accepted.
• Please post submissions to the Community Hub. Replies to this thread will NOT count as a submission.
• Multiple submissions per player are allowed. You can upload them in one post to the Community Hub.
• Group submissions are not allowed.
• Post-processing and use of graphic design software (such as Photoshop is allowed).
• You are limited to winning one prize.
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your entries based on:
• Quality
• Creativity
• Rule adherence
## Dates
The submission period will end on Thursday, November 30, 2023, 11:59 PM Pacific (Friday, December 1, 2023, 07:59 UTC).
