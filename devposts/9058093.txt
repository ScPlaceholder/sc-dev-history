## Features & Gameplay
Recco Battaglia returns
Recco Battaglia is back as one of the new in-person mission givers with three story missions that introduce players to Nyx, followed by a repeatable contracts. She is located in Levski inside of her workshop acting as a new instanced location. Recco's story missions take you through industrial mining, missing person boarding, FPS gameplay, and locating special people for her. Each one of the 3 story missions are sequentially unlocked once you build a reputation with Recco. Doing all the story missions unlocks special rewards and new repeatable contracts for Recco.
Note: There is a known issue preventing Mission 2 completion, and as such mission 2 has been disabled in tonight's publish. This will impact the narrative flow but will be re-enabled in a future publish. Additionally, there is a chance an instance might not load initially for Battaglia Mission 1 and require you to to select the Employment Center again for it to load.
Wikelo Updates
We have added 3 new Wikelo missions for Armour, Weapons and a new Ship. The Mission for the ship has changed, Wikelo will now give you a blueprint on accept and ask you to craft the item and return it to him so he can give you the ship
AI & Combat
AI combat balancing pass for Siege of Orison.
