Lights, Camera, Action!
As shown countless times on the Community Hub, the Star Citizen community has a wealth of talent and passion that can deliver amazing videos, highlighting the beauty of the ‘verse. 
Today, we’re sending out a call to all producers, editors, and camera operators to create a commercial for the Consolidated Outland Mustang Series.
Your commercial should focus on one version/variant from the Mustang lineup (other variants are permitted in your commercial, but the main focus should be a single version/variant from the Mustang lineup.)
So take your pick between the Alpha, Beta, Delta, Gamma, or Omega, and show us what you've got!
You have until 11:59 PM PDT on October 29th, 2018 to submit your Consolidated Outland Mustang commercial video for a chance to win one of the prizes listed below.
Haven’t seen our take yet? Check out the official trailer, released at CitizenCon 2948.
Ready to create? Read up on the rules, then start rolling and post your finished video in the Spectrum submission thread: https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/3/thread/mustang-commercial-contest-submissions
The Community team has been planning a wide variety of activities for you all to participate in throughout the year and into next, all coupled with sweet prizes. So even if this contest isn't for you, we’ve got some exciting opportunities coming for you to leave your mark in the Star Citizen universe.
## Prizes
Three winners will be selected. If you work on the video with two friends (3 collaborators max), all will receive the reward.
- 1st Place – Standalone Constellation Phoenix w/ LTI
- 2nd Place – Standalone Aegis Eclipse w/LTI
- 3rd Place – Standalone Avenger Warlock w/ LTI
## Rules
Please find the full set of rules here: https://robertsspaceindustries.com/Mustang-Commercial-Contest-Rules
- Video must not exceed 2 minutes
- Submissions must be at least in 1920x1080 resolution and uploaded to YouTube
- No more than 3 collaborators on a single video
- Submissions should clearly state all individuals involved in creating their commercial video at the time of submission
- Multiple submissions are NOT permitted
- No unoriginal music (Star Citizen music is OK)
- All content must be original
- No license is granted for the use of any game assets, RSI Content, etc. other than the permission to create and submit entries for the Contest. No other purpose is permitted under these Rules.
- A video must not include or require reference to, or incorporate by reference, any other video, submission, Contest Entry, or other external content
- Your video does NOT need to feature all Mustang variants to be able to participate in the contest
Good luck everyone! We’re looking forward to seeing your Consolidated Outland Mustang commercials!
