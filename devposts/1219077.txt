In terms of fixing up code, there'd be a few things we'd need to change. Quite a few things here and there have a line that says something like "Get the sun", while others say "Get nearest sun" the results would still not be great as you passed from one to the other.
What might be a bigger challenge is the performance impact - we spend a pretty big chunk of memory and CPU/GPU time on rendering sun shadows. Theoretically one could treat them as a single star in the distance, and carefully avoid putting anything expensive in the area where you'd need to treat them as two, but that becomes problematic when you give players the ability to pilot huge ships around. Another alternative would be a massive drop in shadow range/quality when you're having to draw both, but again that has downsides :slightly_smiling_face:.
