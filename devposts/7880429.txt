The 4.1.1 patch brings new Ship Battle missions which are focused around a set of missions featuring ship combat. This feedback thread is for the Ambush missions, which are offered by Foxwell Enforcement (Legal) and the Head Hunters (Criminal). These missions have players heading to a location where their signature will be masked, powering down their ship, and waiting for the hostiles to arrive. Once their targets arrive, the player must attack and destroy all of them. Please let us know what you think about these new missions!
We look forward to hearing your thoughts, and thanks for taking the time!
