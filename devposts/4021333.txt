Hey all,
Thanks for all the notifications, PM's, reddit posts and Twitter DM's to bring this thread to my attention, it was even circulated around internally amongst the team so I can definitely confirm we've had a look at this. First up I want to say its always nice to see how much effort and time people outside of the team put into these threads and we certainly never intentionally ignore them, even if you don't get a personal response they'll always have been circulated around internally either by the team themselves or the community team poking us on them and discussed.
Now onto the meat and potatoes of swapping the floors around, its an interesting one and you can see from the attached image of our early concept exploration, we even considered something similar ourselves. As you can see we had the beds upstairs and the components downstairs to give the proposed layout. Its worth considering here this was an early exploration (late 2019 from concept in advance of production) and as such lots of things changed since which include:
- The crew size at that point was 5 players, and has now been reduced to 4 (This means the pilot can have control of chin turret, or let another player control it from remote station) after feedback from yourselves to try and get it closer to the original requirements
- Adding some non-crew seats to allow additional players to "bunk" transport if needed and not be subject to actor status forces, or for the crew to sit in outside of stations
- Adjustments to the size and quantity of some components, generally going from Medium to Large to power the large and numerous weapons which could be specced as energy based rather than ballistic
- Adding 2SCU of cargo capacity to provide onboard cargo storage for missions or for storing spares/extras
- Making sure every crew member has space for weapons and personal storage, as seen on all the recently released vehicles
- Making all this fit inside the exterior, which resulted in widening and lengthening the ship
So lets explain why we went with the layout you saw in the halls, which I'll reiterate is not finished art, over the attached or the proposal hereand that comes down to a few decisions:
- As mentioned in various SCL/ISC, the Redeemer is no longer aimed as a drop ship, it is not designed to carry troops and drop them into combat (the Valkyrie and Vanguard Hoplite now fulfill these roles) therefore having easy entrance/exit from the non-crew seats is not a consideration, nor an intentional artifical downside to "nerf" the ship.
- We wanted easy and quick crew access to ship components and subcomponents for repair during combat
- We still want to have modular rooms in this ship and others, but this is not planned for release timeframe and the lower room will be the one that is modular which due to its layout and accessibility from the outside, provides us with more options to fundamentally change the role of the ship via this system and keeps the top deck intact and seperated.
"But John, a lot of what you say can be achieved with the proposed layout"  I hear you say...
Obviously its hard to say one way or the other as I dont have the files shown here to compare against the actual ingame assets, nor were two images shown of the before/after ship side by side with the same perspective/alignment or reference point shown, but from looking over them and quickly replicating in engine with the actual assets then making my best interpretation there are three significant issues here and all of these contributed to our internal reason to not go this way from the start:
- The size of the component bays have not been considered along with the damage work under the skin, bringing them forward in the fuselage would necessatate a much larger "bump" around the upper hull to account for them. You can see looking at the rear of the existing exterior a bump that extends from the intakes backwards and this is solely here to cover the components in the upper rear of the current design, bringing them forward would need moving that bump almost all the way to the circular shield elements at the front.
- The upper manned turret is in its current position partly to provide a balanced coverage and also visually to balance it (the Redeemer certainly has some interesting styles so it can look awkward from some angles) moving it throws both them off and also requires more exterior changes.
- From the images it looks like the upper body and lower body were both increased in length, you can see this particularly relative to the rear ramp and the the current layout already gets quite close to the external mesh and thats without the full damage work done being spliced inbetween, doing the suggested changes would likely make the ship longer and/or wider. In addition the landing gear impinge significantly into the lower deck volume which I'm not sure are considered here.
Lastly, the interior shown in the halls whilst clearly not finished, it is actually at the end of greybox so pretty much locked down and as of next week the team will haved moved onto finishing it out to the same standard as the exterior, so whilst we appreciate the feedback and debate, in this instance we are staying the course with the interior layout. When we come to looking at modules for this ship, something along the lines suggested here would be a strong consideration for the lower deck module as it takes away the crew facilities and replaces with with drop seats/armoury fundamentally altering the role of how the ship would be played, rather than being a minor cosmetic difference.
