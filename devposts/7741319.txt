When it comes to bipod there's typically a lot of clunk involved but bipods are not something that I'm aware of for the near future (plans can always change etc)
My personal take is I would always prefer bipods to be simulated in gameplay via crouching / prone (with much more drastic effects when prone fully simulating a very effective bipod) as this gives the trade off of mobility and isn't as clunky an anchoring down and needed to have anims where you pack things away, worry about states ETC.
LMGs will get a general update once somethings come online, the main things that will effect LMGs are;
- New Sprint is in (hello sprint to fire! we currently don't have a sprint to fire mechanic in)
- longer reload anims
- recoil modifiers based upon stance
Once some of that is in we can look at giving them terrible hipfire, probably keeping their ADS time where it is (possibly), increasing their recoil and increasing their damage
Since LMGs are mainly about being 'prepped' into position and suppressing areas and you should win fights if you're aiming and static
Overall you'll get a simulated bipod without some of the finnickiness that can come from deploying a bipod
