## The Intergalactic Aerospace Expo is upon us once again! To go alongside the Free Fly event for IAE, we're launching a screenshot contest for the entirety of the event. From November 19th through December 1st, you have a chance to win a ship or vehicle every day!
Here’s how it works:
Starting on November 19 (8am Pacific / 4pm UTC), different ship manufacturers take over an expo hall every day. There’s something new to see every day, and nearly everything on display is available to test-fly for free!
We’re looking for the best screenshots that encapsulate each day of the IAE. We'd like you to either highlight the manufacturers' expo hall presence, or the ships and clothing available on that event day. You can check out the schedule here to plan your flights.
Good luck, everyone!
## Rules
Before you get started, be sure to read up on the Contest Rules.
You have until 11:59pm Pacific Time on the last day each manufacturer's display is active to add your image to the corresponding Spectrum submission thread, or post it on Twitter or Instagram using the hashtag #IAE2951.
