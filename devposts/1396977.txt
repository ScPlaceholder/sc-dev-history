I don't do this for two reasons:
One, if you want to find out what questions are being asked, I'd prefer you watch the show. That's why we make the show. :+1:
Two, I don't actually want to discourage repeat questions.  Now that we have voting for these, if a repeat question gets voted up, that tells us a lot about the quality of the information out there, our reach, and provides us the opportunity to look for updated answers when applicable.
Basically, it's entirely within the community's capabilities to maintain a list of questions that have been asked and in what episode if that's something the community wants to work towards.  For me, I make the videos so you'll go to the videos for the information. I believe in the inherent value in what's referred to as "exformation," the stuff you don't get when you see someone else's interpretation of the show through recap.
We put this information in video format because we want you to see the developer's expressions, hear the intonation in their voice, and the other associated aspects to a real human presenting real info on their real work that get lost when you just go to a list of questions and answers, or a minimized recap, video or stream done by someone else through the prism of their own particular understanding of the subject matter.
In other words, the community is, as always, free to organize and disseminate our info as they wish, but for myself and my efforts, I want you going to the shows themselves to find out what's discussed and how it's addressed. :slightly_smiling_face:
