Grab your multi-tool and head on down to the pumpkin patch! It's time for our second annual Pumpkin Carving Contest! :jack_o_lantern:
Get into the Halloween spirit by carving your very own Star Citizen themed jack-o-lantern. When you’re done, share pictures of your glorious creation on Twitter for a chance to win one of our fabulous prizes listed below. All submissions must have the #StarCitizen and #Halloween hashtags included to be considered.
Don’t forget to light your jack-o-lanterns to make your design shine. Make sure to include a description in the tweet to let everyone know what the pumpkin is supposed to be, just in case someone doesn’t know their Avenger Stalker from their Warlock.
Also, don't worry about the size or pumpkin-ness of your pumpkin. If pumpkins are not available in your area, a beautifully carved potato will work just as well.
We’ll share the best creations on our social channels for all to see and enjoy!
Before you get started, make sure to read up on the rules here.
