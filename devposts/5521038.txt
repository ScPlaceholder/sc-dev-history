There will still be aim assists but we have more freedom to tune it per weapon. The general rule will be along the lines of the bigger the gun, the less "nudge freedom" it will have and this defines how strong aim assists are. We had aim assists waaaay before 3.10. Fixed assist was just starting nudging a bit earlier to make hitting with fixed weaponry easy. It's not different to convergence either ... in the end it is just a little nudge towards the optimal aim point, be that a PIP or the convergence distance aim point. In general we will try to keep assist values at a minimum though.
EDIT: And yes, some aim assists are always necessary ... our ships just fly and accelerate too fast to actually live completely without aim assists.
