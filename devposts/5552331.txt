The final three days of 2952’s Intergalactic Aerospace Expo (IAE) begin today, Monday, November 28th. This will be the last chance to take advantage of the Free Fly event and play Star Citizen for free.
These final three days also offer everyone the chance to try out every ship from every manufacturer in the 'verse! Just head to one of the ship rental terminals and select your ship of choice. If you missed piloting the Drake Corsair, the Anvil C8R Pisces, the Drake Cutter, or any of the other 100+ vehicles on show during the expo, these final four days of IAE 2952 are your chance to do so – for free!
For more details on this year's IAE please head to the official site, as well as our Traveler’s Guide and FAQ.
