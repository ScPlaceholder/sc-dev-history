This decision is all coming from the gameplay side.
As an engine programmer i play no part in it.
All i can say that a dashcam is technically feasible albeit very taxing as people already said.
First, Rendering a second view is very costly, both on the CPU & GPU.
Second, if you are far away from your ship all the assets surrounding your ship have to be streamed in to be able to render it.
So ship alarm definitely possible (gameplay decision), long-distance dashcam most likely not. Short-Distance dashcam is possible. This could also be very useful for landing on uneven terrain without third person (VR! :grin:)
