Hi all, these should have been separate statements and I will try to explain further.
It is no longer illegal to damage, destroy, tow, scrape, or structurally salvage an unowned vehicle.
- Unowned vehicles are ones which players have claimed a replacement for via the ASOP or AI ships which no longer have pilots
- It is no longer illegal to damage, destroy, tow, scrape, or structurally salvage them anywhere, including in Armistice Zones
- However, having checked the details given via scanning, I can no longer see a way to determine if a ship is owned or not. I have reported this bug as it did used to display the owner. Please let me know if I'm missing something. Thankfully, you do not instantly receive a Crime Stat for towing as you get a warning first which allows you to try without repercussions.
It is no longer illegal to tow ships outside of green zones.
- Green zones are Armistice Zones
- It is illegal to tow an owned ship in Armistice Zones, although I am hoping to increase the grace period you are given before a Crime Stat is awarded so that players can move ships a short distance to remove obstructions
- It is now legal to tow owned vehicles outside of an Armistice Zone, so remember to leave your shields up!
These were quick fixes to try and ease the issue of abandoned ships clogging up locations, but other methods are being discussed. One major part which is missing (other than the ability to tell an unowned ship from an owned ship) is that an unowned ship's shield will remain up if on. I would like to change it so that the moment as ship is unowned, they are powered down to further ease disposal.
