I'd encourage you to skim through the General section of Spectrum. We're certainly not interested in locking or junking concern threads. That said, this can happen when feedback stops being constructive or when discussions cross the line into inappropriate discourse.
As you've mentioned, there’s not going to be a single patch that flips everything overnight - but improving playability is our top priority this year. Some early feedback included hopes for a complete turnaround by January, but this is a process - and one we're fully committed to. Each patch is part of a broader effort, with every update/patch pushing things forward. Our teams actively absorbing the wealth of feedback from all of you and are eager to make each and every patch better than the last. We have seen a great deal of improvement already from 4.0.0 to now, but there's still a lot of work to do.
To acknowledge your note on timing as well, there's been quite a bit of internal discussion around patch release timing. We're actively experimenting with this - including the recent introduction of maintenance windows and other adjustments.
