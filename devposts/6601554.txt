## Welcome to the '2954 First Date in the 'Verse Screenshot Contest'!
That's right, love is in the air, and we want you to show us how you would treat a special someone to a first date in the 'verse! What would you wear, where would you go, and what would you do? Would you show them unbelievable sights and soar through an endless diamond sky on a magic Carrack ride this Coramor? Or would you take a cue from some of the more... uh... adventurous couples and go loot-hunting? We can't wait to see the romantic rendezvous you dream up, so share your screenshots, and a voguish vessel could be thine! :heart: These prizes are also giftable, so you can share the love (and the 'verse!) with someone special! :heart:
## Before You Begin:
Before you start on your submissions, please read the contest rules carefully and remember to post your entries on the Community Hub and not as a reply to this thread.
:exclamation: Any submission not posted to the Community Hub or without '2954 First Date in the 'Verse Screenshot Contest' in the post title will not be considered. :exclamation:
## Keep In Mind
- Read the contest rules! :book:
- Please post your screenshot to the Community Hub. Replies to this thread will NOT count as a submission.
- Use '2954 First Date in the 'Verse Screenshot Contest' as the title for your submission and select the 'screenshot' category on the Community Hub.
- Each screenshot uploaded to the Community Hub is considered a single entry. However, you are allowed multiple entries and can include multiple images in a single post.
- Screenshots must feature an image that embodies a 'first date experience' in the 'verse, including an in-game location and activity. Bonus points for images that encapsulate that special 'romance' feeling.
- Post-processing effects are NOT allowed. This includes third-party add-ons like Photoshop, Reshade, etc.
- Group submissions are NOT allowed. Only the person posting the screenshot can win a prize.
