As far as we can tell the Leaderboards are updating. The issue most players are experiencing is the default selection on the leaderboard page is "ANY MAP", this is essentially a randomly selected map at the start of the season. This can cause players to perceive the leaderboards as not updating when reality is the map they played is not the selected map - this issue has became much more obvious as we add more maps (where prior to 3.20 it was a 50/50)
We want to change "ANY MAP" to "ALL MAPS" but the current leaderboard setup does not lend itself well to some changes (this is why theres no Gun Rush, Team Elimination, Tank Royale etc. leaderboards and why we havent resolved issues with how "Rating" is calculated). Ultimately we need to refactor leaderboards but I am hopeful for getting this last change in in the meantime.
