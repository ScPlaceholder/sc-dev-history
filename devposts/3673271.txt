There are multiple ways:
1) Turn off look ahead in the options
2) Turn off look ahead via LALT-L
3) Tweak the look ahead settings to exclude tracking off targets.
However padlock and look ahead target tracking are two separate things. Look ahead target tracking (as part of look ahead) is quite weak so you can also just dial down the "pull". Padlock on the other hand is a strong target tracking and should not automatically enable unless you have the options set in the menu (it should be off by default) or pressed a button (unbound by default).
