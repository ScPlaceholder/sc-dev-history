Our friends at JRDF are true masters of craftsmanship and, to celebrate CitizenCon 2952, we're sharing their masterpieces with the most-accomplished tinkerers and artisans among you.
Welcome to the CitizenCon 2952 DIY Ship Model Contest, sponsored by JR Design & Fabrication (JRDF).
We want to see pictures of your homemade Star Citizen ship models, built from nothing but common household objects. And, we want you to submit them to the newly launched Community Hub!
:t-rex: Before you go hunting for ship-shaped objects in your home, please read the contest rules carefully and only submit your entries to the Community Hub.
Any submissions not posted to the Community Hub will not be accepted.
## Keep In Mind
- Read the contest rules
- Only upload images of a Star Citizen-themed ship or vehicle made out of common household items that are not typically associated with building
- All submissions must be submitted to the Community Hub and include DIY Ship Model in the title
- At least one image must show your RSI handle handwritten and physically present :writing_hand:
- Multiple submissions per player are allowed
- Group submissions are not allowed
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your submissions on:
- Creativity
- Style
- Rule adherence
- Humor
- General "Goodness"
## Dates
The submission period ends on October 23, 2022 (EOD 11:59 PM Pacific)
## Rewards
:first_place_medal: First Place: JRDF Carrack (Standard or Expedition) & 2 dioramas (1:500 scale)
:second_place_medal: Second Place: JRDF Freelancer Max & 1 diorama (1:200 scale)
:third_place_medal: Third Place: JRDF Cyclone TR (1:40 scale)
Good luck and may creativity be with you! :hammer:  :couch_and_lamp:  :gloves:
