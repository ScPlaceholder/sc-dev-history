Will the distribution of cargo and mass on a ship affect the ability of the IFCS to maintain requested maneuvers? (i.e. If I have all of my cargo loaded onto the port side of my ship, would it possibly list in atmosphere or possibly not be able to maintain elevation?)
Yes, the IFCS will attempt to maintain its goals, but should it be unable to there would be impairments to the ships ability to respond to inputs. In the example you mention, if you load everything on the port side and take off from the ground by strafing up, it should list to one side initially as the thrusters counter balance the offset.
