NPCs would be an interesting solution since the increase in computation for AI pathing of taking cargo back and forth, the number of entities on-screen, the speed with which they will need to be able to move the cargo, and the possibility of interfering with the process (standing in the way, moving the ship, closing the loading ramp) creates additional overheads that will need solving.
Some ships require lift access to reach, others need ramp access or door access while the bigger ships require physically EVA'ing the cargo up and into place, so there will be AI pathing problems to solve too. None are insurmountable, but it's more technical limitations in a potentially very busy landing zone so we can't just dream it and build it, but we will be exploring a bunch of solutions to whatever problems arise.
Whatever we do, we'll be pushing to keep immersion wherever possible but the  'dream' might take a while to reach however 'obvious' the idea might be.
