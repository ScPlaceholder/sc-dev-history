This is the sort of consideration that would potentially lead us to cherry pick which actions can be bound and which cannot. It's a little beyond the scope of the current implementation, and I don't have any involvement with things like Voice Attack and support for external peripherals. The focus of the interaction mode is to ensure that at a base level, everyone has the same access to everything, freeing us up to implement those features to the desired depth without being tied so stringently to key mapping across devices.
Any ability to alter the physical configuration of your cockpit is also beyond this implementation. There will surely be interaction ramifications of such a feature, but we'll need to deal with those problems as concrete examples arise.
As for telepathy, I don't feel it is my place to comment on the plans of the animation team before they've deemed their work fit to make public. Besides, wouldn't pushing a button with your mind be telekinesis?
