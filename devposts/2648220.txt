Hey everyone!
I'm super happy to see everyone is excited about the Caldera suit that's been discovered on the 3.8 PTU, although I'd like to give a brief update on its status. This suit (and the CitizenCon Digital Goodies Pack version) will actually be implemented in a 3.8.X patch in the early new year. It's intended to be a multi-part suit, similar to existing suits in the game (helmet, chest, arms, legs, etc.), and simply needs a bit more time.
So, while you see some of the progress on the PTU now, the suit will be removed from the build before 3.8 hits the live servers, and re-introduced in the very near future. To be even more specific, the remaining work is currently scheduled for the first Sprint in January.
More information on the other items implementation coming very soon!
