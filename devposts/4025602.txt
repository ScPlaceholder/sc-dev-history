We are looking to gate many things in the game, both items as well as ships, behind the reputation system.  We have to remember that without a leveling system, reputation is one of our more significant progression in our game, if not THE most, so now that it’s in, we will be looking to get this hooked into all forms of rewards mechanisms moving forward.  As for what happens with people that already have these ships, you will always have access to these ships through the ASOP.  However, what you will likely see as we move forward is that mission content (which will ultimately include large scale hauling missions), will likely be gated behind reputation and/or org membership.
