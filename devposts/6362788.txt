We are opening up a thread for official feedback on the ship towing mechanic! Note, this is the towing mechanic by itself which is exclusively available on the Argo SRV, though the ship itself will have a separate thread for setup and systems. Please also be sure your observations are from build 8849787 or later.  Some key questions from the design side on this:
- What differences were you able to notice between the towing tractor beam and normal ship mounted tractor beams?
- What did you tow so far in your resting?
- What did you find to be the most enjoyable aspect of ship towing?
- Did you have all of the information you needed on the UI?  If not, what was missing?
- What information on the UI did you find to be irrelevant?
- What functionality or gameplay did you find was missing from ship towing that you would like to see in the future?
- How challenging was it for you to keep your target attached during a tow?
Thanks for taking the time to leave some thoughts for us to review!
