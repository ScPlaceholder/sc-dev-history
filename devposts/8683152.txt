Stella Fortuna 2956 has arrived, and luck loves a little chaos :four_leaf_clover:
This year, we’re looking for 30-second video stunts that celebrate speed, risk, sharp turns, super-hero landings, or even glorious miscalculation. We want to see your wildest racing lines, your boldest jumps, your sketchiest recoveries, and your most spectacular crashes.
Whether you're sending a ground vehicle off a cliff and somehow landing it, surviving an impossible jump, threading an Argo CSV through a C1 Spirit on microTech, or drifting something that was never meant to drift: If it feels slightly irresponsible but absolutely legendary, you are doing it right.
Stella Fortuna is about trusting your instincts and committing to the play. Clean run or catastrophic failure, if it's bold and real, it belongs here.
Fortune favors the brave. Sometimes it favors the lucky. Let’s find out which one you are.
## How To Participate
We are calling on the community to celebrate Stella Fortuna by showcasing high-risk stunt gameplay in a short-form video.
Please read the contest rules carefully to avoid disqualification.
- :four_leaf_clover: Plan the Chaos: Choose your ship or ground vehicle and design a stunt or race line that pushes limits. Think speed, danger, verticality, tight terrain, environmental hazards, or ridiculous ideas that somehow might work.
- :movie_camera: Capture the Moment: Record a 30-second in-game video. Keep it tight and focused. The stunt should be the star.
- :outbox_tray: Submit: Create a Stella Fortuna 2956 post in the Community Hub, submitted as a Video.
## Keep In Mind!
- Read the contest rules!
- Posts must be submitted to the Community Hub, as a video hosted on YouTube
- Videos must not include any third-party music, AI-edited content, effects, sounds, soundtrack, or other third-party content. Star Citizen music and sounds are accepted.
- Visual post-processing effects are allowed, as well as on-screen graphics from the editing software of your choice.
- Videos must not include any other video, submission, contest entry, or other external content.
- Videos must include the provided end slate, shown for at least 3 seconds: https://media.robertsspaceindustries.com/qxub1wbq2zm6r/source.zip
- Maximum video length: 30 seconds, including the required end slate.
- Minimum video resolution: 1920 x 1080, but 3840 x 2160 (4k UHD) is highly preferred.
- Videos may have a maximum of three contributors or collaborators. You may include more players in the video, but only up to three people per submission are eligible to win.
- Submissions should clearly state the RSI handles of all those being credited as contributors or collaborators at the time of submission.
- Multiple submissions by or involving a participant are NOT permitted.
## Preferred Tech Specs:
VIDEO
- Format: .MP4
- Codec: H.264
- Aspect Ratio: 16:9
- Frame Rate: 30fps (minimum)
- Pixel Dimensions: 3840 x 2160 4k UHD (preferred) / 1920 x 1080 (minimum)
AUDIO
- Stereo, 48kHz, 24bit (16bit minimum)
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your submissions on the following:
- Visual quality
- Creativity and originality of the stunt
- Execution and difficulty
- Strong interpretation of the Stella Fortuna theme, including risk, luck, and bold commitment
## Dates
The submission period ends on Monday, March 30, 2026, 20:00 UTC.
