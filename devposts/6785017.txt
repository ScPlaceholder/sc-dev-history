maybe worth clarifying:
We are still aiming for "hard deaths" beeing the result of a critical failure.
Which can be either your Power Plant exploding or something that has a similar destructive force.
In general something that will destroy the structure of a ship in an extreme way.
So what you will encounter more often in a normal "ship fight" is your ship items failing by them getting destroyed (which you can already experience in the AC mode).
Your ship items failing will render you inoperatable, you therefore lost the fight and either wait for someone to repair you or you might get boarded.
But let me be clear, this is not something we can do in this patch, this is something for the future. It requires a lot of planning and thinking to do it properly.
Changing this has a lot of consequences for the rest of the game.
So we should not do it just to make it work for this one experimental mode (There are 3 I know)
Let me give you an example:
In arena commander we currently define a Win by you making the other ship explode and kill the other pilot in that explosion while you are still alive.
This is something that with our intention would not work anymore since you might be still alive on a ship that would be floating inoperatable.
So TLDR
We did not forget about our intentions, but not something for now.
