It depends how the facial tracking and head tracking feels for you at this setting. See it like a graphics setting, really. Higher fps mean higher cpu impact. If you reduce the fps and do not see a negative impact on the game performance you should be fine. On older CPUs with less threads / cores even 60 fps can be a problem.
So I don't think its overkill. In the worst case you have a higher buffer when the camera wants to scale down the fps for any reason.
