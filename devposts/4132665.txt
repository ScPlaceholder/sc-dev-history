We've seen a lot of great entries so far for this year's Intergalactic Cook-off!
First, however, we would like to point out that part of the contest is to prepare the food yourself and prove it by sending a picture of the meal you cooked/baked.
After all, actually making a crazy dish is the most fun part of this contest.
Entries that use pictures from various cooking sites, we must, unfortunately, declare invalid. But you can still update your post with your own picture until the end of the submission period.
