Weapon will only nudge as much as they are allowed to via game data. It doesn't matter if the aim point comes from the convergence or not, if the weapon cannot reach it then this is how it is. The theoretical worst case would be e.g. a Scorpius with fixed guns (because the weapon hardpoints are very far apart from each other) but given that we're looking into removing N-1 that will not be a problem as this would also remove pure fixed mounts for the majority of ships (some special ships like the Ares aside, but even here the new PIP calculation produces a lot more precise aim results). Gimbals in fixed mode will not arrest themselves forward (as they do now in live) but will aim along the aim line (i.e. the line between your crosshair and your eyes) towards the correct aiming distance. So your weapons will still be able to converge unless a target is so close that a gimbal cannot physically look at it (which is usually the case when gimbals are very close to hull). From that point onwards it's really just a property of your ship and your chosen weapon setup.
