Kri’thla and Kli’thla could be seen as pronunciation differences on the same name, like pronouncing Katherine as KATH-RIN vs. KA-THE-RIN. That said, there are ways to differentiate the two in the native script. They are two different letters KLA and KRA. Xi’an is somewhat "messy" about its L vs. R distinctions. In general, anyone clearly enunciating an L consistently is trying to sound a bit more formal and proper. Ls turn into Rs when things are more informal, but it doesn't really go the other way (except in the Service Dialect (SDX), where there is almost only an R sound and and leaving out an L would create a bothersome ambiguity).
