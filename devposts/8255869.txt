just a note on the volt LMG here,
This is going to be a proper 'hunk down and suppress an area' LMG.
So for those who want to go prone at distance and be able to hold an area down with devastating big projectiles this ones for you.
You'll really struggle in CQC with this LMGs aim time and in general some of the sway factors that simulate 'weight'
As well as being able to put that S3 optic on for getting some serious distance.
You're going to have a big mag here that can really heat up and hurt things (also i may of accidently put 110 projectile speed when i meant 1100!)
There's some fun bits in here that shows that LMGs are LMGs not just big rifles.
Bipods are often bought up a lot and that was something i wanted to simulate,  stance effects is a nice way simulating that without the more awkward deploying of the bipod you can get in a fair few games (we have some long anims going from prone to standing so I believe it's a decent trade off).
This thing is really fun and sounds amazing, It's the first LMG I've gotten to do and the team has done some amazing work, please let us know what you think!
