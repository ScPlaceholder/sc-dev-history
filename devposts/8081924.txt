New Citizens, welcome to the 'verse!

## New Pilot Checklist
Do you feel ready to tackle your new adventures in Star Citizen? There’s no better teacher than hands-on experience, but don’t jump in blind. Before you launch, double-check that you’ve got the essentials locked in.
Here’s your quick pre-flight checklist:
- Download and install Star Citizen.
- Skim through the New Player Guide.
- Play the Tutorial to get comfortable.
- Watch a beginner video.
- Join a Guide session if you need help.
- Say hello in #helpdesk-volunteers or Spectrum!
You're prepped. You're cleared. Now it’s time to carve your own path among the stars. Whether you're hauling cargo, hunting bounties, or just exploring the unknown: the ‘verse is yours to shape.
