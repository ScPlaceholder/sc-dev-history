I see how the patch notes can be misleading. Trichording as an action is still possible but you will not get the extreme benefits that PVP and racing relies on so heavily. So 10G forward, 10G right and 10G up combined under trichording will give you 10G, not 17G.
Keep in mind that this is a change for Master Modes flight model. It is not applied outside the MM experimental mode.
