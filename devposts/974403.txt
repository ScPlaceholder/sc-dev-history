Hi everyone,
If you watched AtV/RtV and still have questions about the Vulcan, now is your chance to ask!
You can post your questions within this thread, and don't forget to up-vote the ones you want answered most. We'll collect the questions, present them to the developers, and post the answers for you in a Q&A Comm-Link in the near future.
Question Guidelines:
- Post only ONE question at a time. Replies with more than one question skewer the voting and will be removed.
- You may post as many questions as you'd like, as long as they are in individual replies to the OP.
- Only post replies to the OP. This is for easy voting and sorting of your questions. THIS THREAD IS NOT FOR CONVERSATION, NESTED OR OTHERWISE. Replies to submitted questions will be removed and thus not considered.
- You may (and should) vote on as many questions you want to see answered as you can.
