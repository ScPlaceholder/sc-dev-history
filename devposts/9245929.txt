## Feature Updates
Starchitect Locations
Freight elevators have been restructured. The elevator has been moved out of the interior physics grid so its console has proper collision, and the side room has been separated into a standalone maintenance building, which also resolved players being pushed back when approaching the console. Every landing pad that previously carried the old combined elevator tile now uses the new elevator and maintenance building, with the surrounding areas resized to fit.
Loot distribution has had a tuning pass across the board. Rifle, LMG, and SMG ammunition is now prioritized in loot boxes, shoes and jackets have been removed from the small generic container, and loose commodities are temporarily disabled until their quality can be set properly. AI now carry spare magazines in their backpack as well as their core, where they have a backpack to carry them in.
Exterior lighting has had a polish and optimization pass across all tile types, and interior dressing continues through habitation and command buildings.
- Potential Fix: Logistics chairs rotated the wrong way
- Potential Fix: A ladder at an incorrect height in the logistics building, and exterior ladders on the command building
- Potential Fix: A broken foundation on a compound corner, along with clipping cover barriers and missing proxies on compound walls, railings and skirting
- Potential Fix: Missing ledges on the 100m and 140m landing pads and the 20m maintenance tile
- Potential Fix: Spawn closets sitting below the mesh, now resized and placed correctly
- Potential Fix: Turrets at certain compound locations firing when they should not
Genesis and Nyx I
The terrain borders have had further work, with a GPU-driven skirt now hiding the discontinuity where neighboring patches meet. Planet scatter collision has also been broadened: assets flagged to ignore wheeled vehicles now ignore all vehicles, including ships and gravlev, so you will no longer catch on small scatter in anything you are flying or driving.
- Cloud ambient occlusion tuning on Nyx I
- Terrain pixel shader optimization, and further water improvements
- Potential Fix: Vehicle MFDs referencing the wrong names for the Nyx gateway stations
