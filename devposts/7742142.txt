Hello everyone!
As we emphasized in the last ⁠Letter From The Chairman and throughout our messaging this year, our top priority for 2025 is improving playability. A key part of this effort is refining how we manage feature development—with features now being developed in a separate branch and only migrating over when they are ready.
In a recent episode of ⁠Inside Star Citizen, we shared our commitment to delivering more content patches, more frequently. Because these updates are highly narrative-driven, we’ve adjusted how they appear on the ⁠Release View to avoid spoilers. As a result, some newly added cards in the Release View are marked as Redacted and will only be fully revealed once the content is officially announced or discovered.
As a reminder, the 1.0 column represents features and content planned for Star Citizen 1.0. However, this doesn’t mean you’ll have to wait until full release to experience them—rather, it serves as a snapshot of the experience you can expect. As features and content from this column become ready, they will shift left into a point patch column, preparing for release.
There are still more additions and updates to come, including possible changes and additions to immediate columns, so be sure to check back every two weeks for the latest updates.
With that, let’s dive into this week’s Roadmap Roundup!
-CIG Community Team

Additions to the Star Citizen 1.0 column:
Control Surfaces
Improving atmospheric flight dynamics with functional control surfaces, allowing for more precise maneuvering and realistic aerodynamics.
Quantum Boost
Introducing Quantum Boost, a short-burst travel mode designed for quick repositioning and high-speed engagements between distant points.
Item Misfire & Wear
Adding depth to equipment functionality with item misfires and gradual wear, requiring regular maintenance to ensure peak performance.
Fire Hazard
Bringing fire hazards to ships and environments, along with Fire Extinguisher mechanics, requiring players to manage and extinguish blazes before they spread.
Charge & Drain
Expanding the utility of the multi-tool with the ability to drain and transfer power between objects, unlocking new puzzle-solving and support roles.
Life Support
Integrating life support systems into ships, making oxygen management and environmental controls critical for survival.
Engineering Gameplay
Introducing dedicated engineering roles aboard ships, allowing players to actively manage power distribution, repairs, and system efficiency in real time.
Solar Flare
Unleashing powerful solar bursts within the Pyro system, creating dynamic environmental hazards that players must navigate and prepare for.
Vehicle Radar & Scanning Rework
Reworking vehicle radar and scanning for all vehicles.
FPS Radar & Scanning
Implementing first-person radar and scanning gameplay, improving situational awareness and bringing new tactical gameplay.
Hangar Upgrades
Expanding personal hangar customization with new upgrades, layouts, and functional improvements.
Weapon Overheat
Introducing heat buildup and overheat mechanics for energy weapons, requiring careful trigger discipline and heat management during sustained firefights.
Radiation
Implementing dangerous radiation zones in specific locations, forcing players to adapt with specialized gear and strategic navigation.
New Missions - Location Repair
We've placed it in the 1.0 column for now, but like all features and content there, it will move to a point patch once it's ready - Implementing new missions that introduce Repair gameplay into the persistent universe, first appearing in Pyro's outposts.
