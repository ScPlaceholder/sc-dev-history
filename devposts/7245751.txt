Greetings!
With the release of 3.24.2 we are updating the power system on ships, which can be manually adjusted in the Power Management MFD. Note that this thread is meant for feedback on the system functionality and rather than the UI itself. We are hoping to hear your thoughts on these changes, and more specifically we are interested in:
- Did you find the default distribution of your ships power balanced?
- Did you encounter underpowered or overpowered situations where you have too little or too much power available?
- What interesting tradeoffs have you had to make to get systems running at desired settings?
- What systems do you think are the most relevant, and least relevant to power in your ship?
- What items did you have active all the time and which did you always switch off?
- What distribution "presets" (as in use cases) do you use in various scenarios, and why?
- Describe in your own words what you understand of the effects that power assignment has on each item type.
- How easy or difficult was it to familiarize yourself with the Power Management MFD controls? Are there suggestions you would like to propose to improve either?
- Do you regularly interact with the MFD for tweaking your ship or to react to situations, or do you set it once and never touch it again?
Let us know what you think below!
