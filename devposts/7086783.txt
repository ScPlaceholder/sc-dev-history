Hello everyone!
Me and the Econ team have been reading your feedback on the payment of these missions so I'm here to ask some questions and get some info that might help us all balance these a little more accurately.
The numbers I'm going to ask for are ones we tested internally and got a rough time for, we knew we was always going to be off by some order of magnitude and we want to rely more on the player data, so as I mentioned we have been collating your feedback and making some plans.
So I would like you to provide us the following information:
- Choose a singular Hauling mission and only have that one accepted
- Use r_displayinfo 2 to provide us the mission record name
- The ship you are doing this in
- The pickup and Dropoff Locations
- The total SCU per Location (A screenshot of the contract in the Mobi can also help this)
- Number of runs you have to do to complete the mission (in that ship)
- Number of friends you had helping you and their roles (if you were in a group)
- From accepting the contract please time the following steps you take and how long it takes
- Time from the moment you accept the mission to using the freight elevator in your hangar to call up the mission cargo
- Time to Call Cargo up the freight elevator
- Time to Load your ship with Cargo
- Time to request takeoff and leave the hanger (And time to leave Atmo if thats a part of your flow)
- Time it takes to get to the dropoff location
- Time it takes to Land into the hanger (And the time to enter atmo if thats a part of your flow)
- Time to Unload the Cargo and finish the mission
If you do encounter any bugs please just note them in a footnote and how much time it extended it by
Edit: One thing to note is all of the missions are due a rebalance, we currently have two worlds of mission rewards
- Missions that have been balanced more inline with the future of the game
- Missions that are still the old hyper inflated balance
So we are taking into account that currently comparatively these missions vs existing ones are very much difficult to compare, this is something the Econ team is working on and will balance gradually and take time between these to analyise the effects in the game before doing another pass.
