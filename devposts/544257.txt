We discussed this very topic when putting the matrix together.
Given the likely final length that the Merchantman will end up with, it may certainly qualify in the Capital class, should size alone be the only criteria.  However, Capital ships also represent a sizable investment in crew and resources to operate.  Our current thinking, and I stress this is our thinking at present, is that even should the Merchantman make the lower end of Capital sizing, for gameplay reasons we don't feel it should also have the requisite operational overhead.
So for now, in an effort to keep the Banu Merchantman more affordable to operate and maintain, it is classified as Large.
