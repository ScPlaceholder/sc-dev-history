The Distortion Pulse Generator is intended to have a safety setting that will cause it to charge a bit slower and have a lower cap for max charge, but in exchange will automatically turn off your ship systems moments before detonation to protect you from the blast, restoring power moments later. Disabling this safety setting will allow access to greater levels of performance but will leave you susceptible to your own distortion blast, requiring you to manually manage your power to mitigate these effects.
For 3.0, the safety setting is the only setting, and as a temporary measure the ship doesn't actually power cycle, but is instead just made immune to its own distortion pulse, as it was far simpler to implement it this way in the short term.
You'll ultimately also be able to flub a detonation by taking too long - the maintenance cost of the generator just keeps growing, so you'll need to detonate sometime before it fails to draw all the power it needs. This creates a kind of natural timer from your components and power settings and prevents players from charging up a distortion pulse and then keeping it forever.
This is also not expected to be ready for 3.0.
