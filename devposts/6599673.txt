With 3.22.1 we are introducing a new Arena Commander mode: Grav Race.  Grav Race is available in offline mode as lap time practice and online modes as a multiplayer race.  Grav Race makes use of the existing Snake Pit track in both forward and reverse as well as the new tracks Clio Island, Shifting Sands, and River's Edge.    In addition, we have made some updates to the Gravlev mechanic with some bug fixing to hopefully make it a more pleasant experience.
Give this a try and let us know what you think!
