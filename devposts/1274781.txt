As I described in the document, I do have a decent model for calculating the amount of heat generated due to drag/friction as our ships fly into atmosphere.  I have proposed that we could use some kind of justification like reaction with shields to explain why our burn-in effect looks and behaves like it does.  But at the moment, the effect is triggered by altitude rather than drag/friction or heat level, and it remains active until you reach a set lower altitude.  Because of this, there is no synchronization between the extreme slow-down of the ship due to drag, and when the burn-in begins to glow.  At a minimum, I'd like to have these things synced up, so the burn-in seems to have a purpose other than a cool visual effect.  All I can say is the support is there to do this.
