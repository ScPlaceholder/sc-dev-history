FPS mineable resources are found scattered on the surface of all relevant planetary bodies and can be found visually or with the Prospector and players should, if possible, try to discover them in both ways. Harvestables can also be found on the surface of some bodies, currently Hurston, Ariel, Aberdeen, and Daymar but are only able to be located visually. In addition to surface discovery, both harvestables and FPS mineables can be found within caves. FPS mineables can also be found attached to mineable asteroids.
FPS mining will require a Pyro RYT Multi-Tool plus the OreBit Mining Attachment, purchasable at Lorville and Area18. Both FPS mining and collecting harvestables requires compatible torso armor with storage capacity.
