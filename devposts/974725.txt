Once upon a time, there were a dozen different starter packs.  With feedback, we determined this was sometimes a bit overwhelming for people at first glance.  Some time later, we decided to streamline the flow to getting started with Star Citizen by reducing the "starter packages" and inviting folks to use the Cross-Chassis Upgrade to move to the ship you want.
As a benefit of this change, it allowed us to make the introduction to Star Citizen a little less overwhelming to folks, reinforce the notion that $45 is really all you need to get started in the 'Verse, and serve as a great introduction to the CCU system after that.
The change has worked very well for the last two-ish years it's been in place.  :+1:
