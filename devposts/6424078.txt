Hi @sladuog, I just found this question so I thought better to answer it late than never.
Your helmet light does indeed bounce onto other objects already, but this is handled as a very special case where one light is selected out of the scene to have bounce. The technique is based on Reflective Shadow Maps / Virtual Point Lights, and the basic idea is that the flashlight just spawns a bunch of hemisphere lights on the surface that it hits. Once the realtime GI is more mature, we'll have to evaluate whether it's responsive enough that we can retire the special handling for the flashlight, or whether we want to keep it around. As for SVOGI, we evaluated it when it came out, but chose not to adopt it.
