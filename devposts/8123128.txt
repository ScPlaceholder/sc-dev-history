Yes, the Meteor hits hard, but its defenses will offset that raw power. You’ll learn more about the RSI Meteor in an upcoming Q&A.
Its bespoke S5 cannons pack a serious punch, but they come at a cost: you're locked into ballistics, which means limited ammo, and slower fire rates that punish missed shots, especially against smaller, more nimble targets. It's not the most forgiving loadout, and it's not meant to be your "death-to-everything" ship.
Since the weapons are bespoke, you also lose the flexibility to swap in alternatives. It’s a specialized ship with big highs and clear limitations, built to reward precision and thoughtful target selection.
