## ▲ Siege
The two factions are escalating their efforts against Frontier Fighters. Operations now focus on disabling the enemy’s ability to regen by destroying MedGel recharge stations and intercepting targets along flight routes. Review contract details carefully to assess the level of risk and resistance before engaging.
Citizens for Prosperity
Disable MedGel Recharge Station
We discovered Frontier Fighters' MedGel recharge stations used to regenerate their forces. Travel to the reported outpost, locate and destroy all recharge stations inside. The recon team reports additional activity in the area, so be prepared for a fight.
Surprise Strike on Frontier Fighters
We’ve tracked a routine Frontier Fighters flight path that passes through an emission cloud ideal for an ambush. If you’re ready, gear up and move to the ambush point immediately.
Headhunters
Ruin MedGel Recharge Station
Council’s ordered us to target MedGel recharge stations to slow Frontier Fighters’ ability to redeploy their troops. Head to the location, destroy any recharge machines you find, and deal with any Frontier Fighters in the area.
Ambush Frontier Fighters
We’ve got a target running a predictable route that cuts through an emission cloud. Perfect for a trap. Move to the ambush point, hide your ship’s signature, and strike when the target passes.
