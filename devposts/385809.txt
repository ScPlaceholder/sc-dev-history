This is a fairly standard labeling scheme for bugs and has been similar to other games I've worked on. There are always bugs of the various classes around, constantly being added, fixed, reviewed, closed out, etc.. during the entirety of development. When we move towards a release we'll make a branch and identify the bugs that are relevant to that particular release and work towards eliminating them in that branch (with back merging to mainline), with preference towards the higher priority bugs. During development these bugs will shift in priority depending on their impact on gameflow and severity.
