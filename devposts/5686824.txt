Final note:
This year, players get to keep a commemorative red envelope as a memento of their prosperous Lunar New Year adventure, as well as wooden roosters that are sure to bring your ship luck when perched in its cockpit. What’s more, long-time players will also receive the Red Festival envelopes from the previous three celebrations as in-game collector’s items. It's important to us that you all retain these items, for the eventuality when you can decorate your habs, hangars, etc.
Previous-year envelopes will be distributed closer to the end of the festival, so don't fret if you're not seeing them right away. We will distribute the Ram, Monkey, and Horse envelopes based on whether or not you logged in during their respective year. For example, if you logged in during Lunar New Year 2020, you'll be eligible for the Year of the Horse envelope.
