Put simply, Instancing lets us create multiple versions of the same location, so you and your party in Siege of Orison can have your own private space to explore and complete missions without anyone else getting in the way. Right now, when you take on Siege of Orison, you and your party of up to four courageous citizens hop into an elevator, pick your instance, and head to your own private waiting area. Once you pass through the forcefield, your adventure begins in your own version of the mission.
We're planning to use instancing in locations where it makes sense, so you always get the full experience. You won't have to worry about showing up to a mission where the loot is gone or the action is already over. In the old Siege of Orison, it was easy to arrive late and miss out, but now, with instancing, you and your party can jump in and enjoy the whole mission from start to finish every time.
Now more specifically to touch on the points raised here, we don't always want players to seek out instances. There may be times in the future where you enter instances during the natural course of gameplay without even realizing it. The number of players in an instance can be anything we want them to be, and it can range anywhere from one player to hundreds, but the decision is based on what provides the best experience for the players at that specific location, for that specific content, be it PvE, PvP, or a mix of that; So we're not moving away from an "open living world", the tech will be used to support specific pieces of content that require it.
On the performance side, moving specific content into instances means we can put each one on its own Dedicated Game Server, which helps everything run smoother and takes some pressure off the main servers. We've also made plenty of improvements and optimizations during the Alpha 4.10 PTU cycle to make sure your experience is as great as we can. Thank you to those who participated in the stress tests :+1:
