The Red Festival is a celebration of renewal and remembrance rooted in old Earth traditions that marked the end of a lunisolar year. Celebrants honor the festival in different ways across the UEE, often by wearing red to symbolize good fortune, sharing stories with friends and relatives, enjoying long foods like calossk tentacles or red-dyed noodles, and exchanging gilded red envelopes representing prosperity in the year ahead.
To ring in a prosperous Year of the Rat in 2956, we’re inviting you to celebrate the Red Festival with us. By logging in during the event, you’ll receive a free Envelopes Stand and the Year of the Rat envelope delivered directly to your hangar. You can also expand your collection of challenge coins by claiming the Red Festival 2956 coin on the promo page. As is tradition across the UEE, red envelopes have been hidden throughout Stanton.
Deck out your fleet with a selection of Auspicious Red Rat ship paints, ready to bring good fortune in the year ahead. Alongside this, our referral bonus and a Free Fly event are ongoing until February 23!
