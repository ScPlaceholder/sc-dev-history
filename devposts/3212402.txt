## Notable Changes for July 10th, 2020
Player Trading
This card has been added to the Roadmap under the Alpha 3.10 column | The player trading app in its tier 0 form, (called "mo.Trader"), will allow the players to send each other currency for a minimal service fee. Players can easily select people in their current party or members of their friends list, or if they need to trade with someone outside of that, they can use a filtered search.
The following card has moved into polishing on the PU Roadmap:
- Player Trading
- NPC Improvements: Bartender
With these cards moving, everything in the Alpha 3.10 column is now in polishing.
