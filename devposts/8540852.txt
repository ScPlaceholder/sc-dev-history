Quite annoyingly, "nanite-style" continuous-level-of-detail stuff pulls in the opposite direction from what current-gen ray-tracing APIs want you to do. With continuous-LOD, sections of objects are constantly swapping in and out, while the ray-tracing API wants you to generate whole-object acceleration structures that you don't change too often, so the two are kind of enemies. This also gives us challenges with ray-tracing and dynamic destruction, for similar reasons.
