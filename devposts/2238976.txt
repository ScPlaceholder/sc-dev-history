Free look and head-tracking (while related) are two separate things. If you want the freelook toggle, please put this in the surveys. Several people have asked about this already. There are toggle and hold bindings for head-tracking already.
The head and aiming is not entirely separated. The head tracking offset is currently calculated as an aiming point offset. I tried to decouple aim and look (and had a couple of guys at work try it out) but it felt really jarring and introduced some weird view movements when the hips of the actor started moving so I postponed my research on that for now. To give you some background, there are essentially three directions on the actor ... the base rotation is where the hip is pointing at (just yaw) ... then there is aiming point (upper part of the body in pitch and yaw) and additionally the head look direction (pitch yaw roll). What you would need are separate controls for any of these directions. That's theoretically possible but these movements (for gameplay and animation reasons) are strongly tied to each other ... ultimately your aiming with a gun dictates where your hip is pointed at. Decoupling that would be quite an intense job, so I wouldn't expect this to change in the near future.
We do have some limitations in our bindings setup which is why we cannot expose every input axis to every output axis. Some new UI tech needs to be finished first before we can even think about reworking that.
