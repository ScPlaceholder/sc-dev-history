## Features & Gameplay
Gameplay
Vehicle Quantum Fuel Updates
Today’s build brings sweeping updates to all ships to greatly reduce the amount of quantum fuel stored. This update will make managing and refueling ships a much less taxing experience. Along with this comes greatly reducing the fuel consumption from quantum travel to match the smaller fuel tanks. Current balance adjustment goal is to allow smaller ships to at least travel once through Stanton with overall range scaling with type of ship and QT drive.
Additional Updates
- Behring rifle design and balance pass
- Adjusted the size of the Star Citizen button on the main menu
