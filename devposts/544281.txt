Operating effectively as a solo player in this instance means with the use of AI crew.  These crew would be necessary to operate the turrets and secondary stations.  Operating the turrets and secondary stations is necessary for the Connie to reach it's fullest potential.
For the purposes of the Ship Matrix, we speak in a very literal sense regarding crew i.e. the number of capable bodies suggested to operate.
Playing a multi-crew ship (which is designed for more than one person by it's nature) solo and doing so "effectively/efficiently" will be a function of the AI crew system.
So nothing has changed.  The mechanism for operating the Connie solo has always been the use of AI Crew.
