It's definitely my goal that all fuel use is tied to thruster activity.  If that bears out, then yes, taking advantage of downward thrust to keep a vehicle down in low gravity would come with a fuel cost.
As far as I know, Gravlev should not use fuel.  However, I would point out that as fuel usage is currently modeled (not part of my systems at this point), where motion is converted into fuel burn, Gravlev could incur a fuel cost.  But it's my understanding that at some point fuel cost will return to the simulation where it will be directly tied to actual thruster burn, which in general is not 1:1 with vehicle motion.
