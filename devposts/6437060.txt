Earlier this year, we challenged you to crown the top 4 ships in the 'verse in our annual ultimate aerospace smackdown.
This year's Ship Showdown saw tremendous participation with creative submissions ranging from Lego creations, original songs, 3D prints, machinima, stunning screenshots, and more. Once again, the community absolutely delivered incredible creations of all sorts.
Thanks to your creative nominations and votes, we were able to narrow the list down to the following four champions, all of which fly away with sweet exclusive goodies.
All four ships that made it into the semi-finals of the recent Ship Showdown 2953 event:
► Origin 600i Explorer (Including Touring & Executive Edition)
► Drake Corsair
► Aegis Redeemer
► Drake Vulture
If you have one of these fine ships in your hangar, you'll receive their respective 2953 Best in Show paint at the Intergalactic Aerospace Expo (IAE) this year, for free.
In addition to the limited-edition Best in Show liveries, you will also receive their in-game manufacturer posters for each of our top four ships that you have in your hangar.
Note that the Best in Show ships will also be available to fly for free between November 26 and November 30!

All of these items will be distributed in two waves, starting on November 27th and another at the end of IAE.
This process can take some time, so don't worry if you don't see these items in your hangar right away.
As a reminder:
- To be eligible to receive the paints, the ships need to be in your hangar (having them in your Buyback List won't grant you the Best in Show paints).
- The Best in Show editions would only contain the goodies within that ship's package and would not grant an additional copy of the rewards.
