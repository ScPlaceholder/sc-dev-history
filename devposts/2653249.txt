Atm the angle to start the tracking is 35 deg (default angle) (forward dir of you actor's head view to target) ... after tracking has started it progressively increases to 50 deg (extended angle) to give you an easier time reacquiring it when you loose it off screen (or due to actor limits) for some time after hard maneuvering etc.  .... If the targer stays lost the angle shrinks down from extended back to default.
We really want to avoid to start the tracking if the target is not visible as it would it be extremely powerful to find your target. Anyways that is the explanation why the angles as they are ...
... though it does mean that the values are right (tbh we just put them just roughly in that ball park for the first implementation).
