We're happy to announce that Star Citizen Alpha 3.20: Fully Loaded is now available to all backers! This patch marks the flyable releases of MISC's enterprise-level cargo hauler, the Hull C, and Mirai's agile racer, the Fury LX. You'll also be able to tour Port Olisar's replacement, Seraphim Station, take on new missions, and try out updates to physicalized cargo. And for those looking to get right into the action, make sure you check out the host of new updates and features to Arena Commander!
