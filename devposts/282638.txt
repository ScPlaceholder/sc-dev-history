Usually, for the Spectrum Issue Council, I look at each new issue everyday. It takes around 15-30 minutes/day.
First, I never try to reproduce issues that have bad reproduction steps, or one time only errors... How can I reproduce it if I don't understand the steps ?!
I then check if the issue is a duplicate of another one, then I try to reproduce it using the various devices I have (Windows 10 PC with Chrome, Firefox, IE 11 & Edge, Macbook Pro with Safari, and a ton of mobile devices, from Android 4 to 7, iOS 9 to 10), and double checking with Sentry (our bug tracking software) to see if there is a Javascript error. Sentry helps us a lkot when it comes to uncommon bug with hard reproduction steps.
Once reproduced, I enter it on Jira with proper reproduction steps, a video, screenshots, the link to the Sentry error, and I assign it to one of the devs working on Spectrum, with a priority according to the severity of the issue. If I can't reproduce it, I let it on the Issue Council for the community to reproduce it.
When the bug is on Jira, I mark the IC report as Acknowledged by CIG, regardless of the number of contributions.
Once we push a fix for that bug, I mark it as Fixed.
Basically, the Issue Council allows us to be able to track the bugs you encounter and to smash them ! The better the report will be the easier it will be for me and the other QA guys to reproduce it and fix it. I want you to enter a bug everytime you encounter it, it will make Spectrum experience better for everyone ! :grinning:
