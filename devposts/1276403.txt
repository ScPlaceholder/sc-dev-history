The current accelerations are realistic given the performance goals dictated by the designers.  In the Starfarer example, the accelerations are correct for that ship to go from 0 to SCM in 2 s.  The question of whether that feels right is a matter for design to decide (and I don't think it is 2 s, is it?).  If they want it to take 20-30 s, that's an easy change and a few seconds to retune the ship.  However, I can guarantee you that if they went to 20-30 s and put that into the game, there'd be as much negative feedback as there is about the 2 s time.  It's not as if the designers haven't tried these things.  They are using the performance behaviors that they feel are most fun, based on their own taste and also the feedback that they receive from a wide range of players.  So it's not an easy decision to make by any  means.  There is no magical perfect performance.
But from my perspective, are the accelerations too high?  I personally think they are, and at the moment I take the blame for that, since I changed the acceleration model IFCS uses to make it more maneuverable at the current stop/acceleration times.  I think they do need to be across-the-board dialed down in their acceleration, and I think they will be.  After any major refactor of the flight model, the design team just needs time to re-establish the performance levels that they expect from each ship.
