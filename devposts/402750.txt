The Writer's Guide is definitely still a good resource on this topic. We haven't touched upon the idea of  genetically modified "designer" Humans or mutated Humans too much yet in the Lore. Most gene work would be more towards eradicating genetic health problems rather than creating some sort of alternate Human race. And while there are cybernetic limbs in the game, those come with their own sets of problems and limitations, serving mostly to replace lost parts, rather than to enhance. We have been discussing a little bit how generations of Humans living on non-Earth worlds might be effected by their environment and physiologically cope with various conditions, and might have more to share about that down the road.
Regarding Dragons, etc. - rather than Humans genetically creating creatures, we have been concentrating our weird and new species efforts towards alien creatures that evolved on other worlds.
Hope that helped to answer some of your questions!
