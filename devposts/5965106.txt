## ILW2953 AEGIS Javelin Challenge
The folks at AEGIS really know how to build giant ships. During my first month aboard a Javelin-class destroyer, I got lost at least a 100 times!
Instruction: Prepare for your active naval service on a Javelin and join a tour on board the UEEN Warhammer, which is open for civilians during Invictus Launch Week. We challenge you to post your best screenshot from your tour on the Javelin to the Community Hub or on Twitter. Read the rules for further details!
## Keep in Mind:
- Read the contest rules! :book:
- Only an image, which includes an in-game screenshot as described by the challenge, will be considered. Any other subject will be disqualified.
- Post your contest entry to the Community Hub (in the Invictus Launch Week event section), or to the challenge announcement post on Twitter (don't forget to include #StarCitizen in your post).
- Use 'ILW2953 AEGIS Javelin Challenge' as the title for your submission on the Community Hub.
- Replies to this thread will NOT count as a submission.
- Make it count: only ONE entry per challenge.
- Group submissions are not allowed. Multiple players in screenshots are allowed, but only the person submitting the entry is eligible to win.
- Post-processing is allowed, but remember that sometimes less is more.
- Consider turning off the HUD when taking screenshots, as this might make for better composition. Check out some excellent tutorials by DigThat32, Mr. Hasgaha, or NotGemini.
- You are limited to winning one prize during the Invictus Launch Week 2953 Screenshot Challenge, so you cannot win on multiple platforms (Community Hub, Twitter) or more than one of the six challenges.
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your entries based on:
- Quality
- Creativity
- Rule adherence
## Dates
The submission period will end on Tuesday, May 23, 2023, 16:00 UTC.
## Prize
- AEGS Redeemer in-game Standalone ship with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance)
Don't forget that you can also submit your entries to Twitter (including the hashtag #StarCitizen) for an additional chance to win!
