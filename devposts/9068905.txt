## Features & Gameplay
Quantum Drive Spool Time Rebalance
Spool up times have been adjusted across all quantum drives, this includes most drives receiving a 2-3x longer spool up while a smaller amount have had their spool times reduced slightly.
Ballistic Gatling S3 Damage
Ballistic Gatling S3 damage increased to be effective against ships of the same size class as it was previously dealing insufficient damage to be viable in its intended role
- Projectile damage increased from 19 to 32
Recco Battaglia Story Mission Rewards
- Mining modules added to the component reward pool for Battaglia story mission ship rewards
- Ore pod rewards from Story Mission 3 now correctly appear in the crafting fabricator
Additional Updates
- ADS no longer triggers on the fire extinguisher when right-clicking while firing; right-click now has no function while using the extinguisher, matching the correct design intent
- Performance Update: Fire hazard VFX optimized to reduce CPU overhead
- Performance Update: Off-screen particle emitters that were spawned as on-screen emitters now correctly expire and are deleted, preventing accumulation of invisible particles that were causing performance degradation over time
## Bug Fixes & Technical
- Fixed 1 Client Crash
- Potential Fix: Players becoming stuck in a respawn queue when multiple players die simultaneously inside an instanced zone, requiring a game restart to resolve
- Potential Fix: Entities and mechanics inside instanced zones incorrectly emitting events into restricted areas of the parent zone, causing unintended triggers outside the instance boundary
- Potential Fix: Battaglia Story Mission 3 blueprint rewards displaying as placeholder text and not appearing in the crafting fabricator
- Potential Fix: Battaglia mission hard drives displaying as placeholder text instead of their correct item name
- Potential Fix: Instance service calls being routed through the wrong DGS when a DGS that did not originally ensure the instance tried to send heartbeats or other service calls, causing instance stability issues
- Potential Fix: Battaglia HotCold proximity tracker failing to locate the target after extended searching; the activation range now doubles after five minutes if the target has not been found
