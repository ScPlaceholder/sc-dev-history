Hey! Definitely not a bad suggestion, but one concern with doing something like this is dev cost, especially when we're already focusing energy on reducing the issues that require a reset at all. Even though it would technically save time spent preforming individual resets, making internal processes like this public could quickly lead to the functionality being abused and exploited in a number of ways. We know issues like these can be frustrating, and we're dedicated to getting rid of the problems that cause these scenarios altogether.
