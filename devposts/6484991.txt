The cargo containers have size and dimension restrictions so you will find the larger boxes are better for certain items. Additionally there’s some legacy filtering on inventory that we are undoing but that will continue through the subsequent updates as we expand how inventory is used, as well as furthering support for dropping larger items which was not part of the original design.
We are also exploring options for naming/tagging/tinting to help organize and maintain larger collections, including the buy/sell flows.
