That's the closest to what will happen in our first iteration of the Launcher. A notification appears when the launcher has picked up an update release version for a game (think Toaster notifications on Windows). If you are online during the publish, you will lose your connection once the servers are updated and have to reconnect using the last version released of the client. To alleviate problems there, we plan to have announced maintenance windows (and site notifications) so that you know it's risky to play during that timeframe since the patch likely to  go out.
Of course, as we iterate on the launcher past the initial release of the new patcher, we plan to use the real-time communications channels available to give you in-game notices about game updates before they affect your gameplay. The goal is to make this as non-disruptive as possible.
That's the gist!
