Lots of great discussion happening in here, and we're reading through all of it. We'll also be closely monitoring how this change impacts the gameplay experience in-game too.
I'd like to chime in with a few notes:
- Previously, T3 beds did not offer enough utility outside of replenishing food and drink and minor injuries.
- Engaging in hostile actions or ground assaults should necessitate strategic planning and logistics. Remember, when you die, you won't respawn with all of your gear. You'll have to either navigate back to your body to loot or come with your vehicles equipped with extra gear before re-engaging.
- In the long term, suicide-to-respawn will not be your best option. We look forward to sharing more about this in the future, but generally, death in any capacity is intended to matter.
- The respawn distances shared in the patch notes are the current values for 3.23.0, but you should anticipate that these will be adjusted as balance requires.
