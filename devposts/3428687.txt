Welcome to the Day of the Vara 2950 Story Contest!
Why are we announcing a story contest, you ask? Well, let's take a look at the lore behind Day of the Vara:
The holiday's origin began in 2557, when the crew of the exploration vessel 'Vara' disappeared in Hades. From then on, morbid tales of the crew returning from the dead to haunt those traveling in the system spread throughout the 'verse. Usually, the ghosts sabotaged the ship (draining vital systems or leading it far off course) or methodically haunted living crew members until someone went insane and vented the ship. Other stories focused on scavengers or scrappers entering derelict ships to find them filled with frozen bodies that came alive when least expected. Stories playing on the supernatural and fears of space travel are still a major part of the Day of the Vara.
So, to celebrate the Day of the Vara, we want you to write your very own short horror story and share it with all of us.
The authors of the three spookiest stories will be rewarded with sweet prizes!
## Rules:
:rotating_light: Before you take pen to paper, please read the rules carefully. You can find them here.
:books: Please post your spooky stories to the submission thread only.
- Maximum length of 3,600 keystrokes/characters, including spaces
- Submission language is English
- One submission per person
- Limit it to a spooky space-travel-related theme taking place in the Star Citizen universe
- The submission period ends on October 27 (11:59 PM Pacific)
The CIG jury will judge your stories based on:
- Tension
- Creativity
- Rule adherence
