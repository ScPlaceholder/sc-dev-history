I'm no lead by all means but talking to some and seeing how they work the most obvious thing which comes to my mind is that they are missing working in silence and experimenting/trying out different things and LESS meetings.
Once you reach a certain level like a lead position you have countless people under you which YOU are responsible for. This means you have to plan, organize and schedule them and you will always be the first contact if they have any questions.
Additionaly LOTS of meetings with different teams/your team are on the daily list. All of this combined takes so much time that there is barely any left to do real work they used to do and love.
