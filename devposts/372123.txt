Great question. The original idea was that animals from earth would be transported to the terraformed worlds. Since we're basically resetting the atmosphere, soil, etc. (and since terraforming has been described as wiping out indigenous populations), Humanity would need to bring their own animals from Earth or other naturally-occurring oxygen planets.
I agree that it would be awesome to be able to go to zoos and see a variety of alien wildlife. We had even called out one of the Xi'an planets to be a massive zoo. The catch though is that this will be heavily contingent on art/animation to help bolster this, so it's not entirely our call. If you've seen Josh Herman's Happy Hour segments, it feels like we could get some really awesome animals going, but I think it's a bit too early to tell with any certainty.
As for the catching animals gameplay, again, I'm not sure. That would have to be worked out with design as well as art/animation. So I'll have to keep you posted on that one.
