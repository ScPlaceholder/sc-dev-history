Hello everyone,
With only 4 days left until Digital CitizenCon 2951, we couldn't be more excited to finally share the work of our talented developers and celebrate all things Star Citizen with you!
CitizenCon 2951 starts on October 9, 2021, at 3pm UTC and will be broadcasted on the Star Citizen Twitch channel.
Some members of the community have been asking if they could co-stream the event, and we're happy to announce that you are free to do so, with some minor caveats - so please read on!
:point_right: While we've taken every possible step to ensure that all the music played throughout the show is owned by us, be advised that we're planning to play submissions from the "CitizenCon 2951 Video Contest" in between the panels. The creators of the videos have assured us that they own the music they used, but as they are community-created (third party), we can't 100% guarantee that.
We're also planning to play a ~2-minute sizzle video at the beginning of the "LIFE IN THE 'VERSE" panel, which contains copyrighted, licensed music. Therefore, we advise you not to stream the audio for that short portion of the show.  The rest of the LIFE IN THE ‘VERSE PANEL and all other panels throughout the day are fine to co-stream! :point_left:
Join us on Saturday, as we celebrate Star Citizen and its fantastic community. :rocket:
Want to stay in the loop with all things #CitizenCon? Sign up for hour-by-hour updates on October 9th!
