The idyllic, peaceful atmosphere of the floating city of Orison has been disrupted as the Nine Tails pirates have captured several platforms on the edge of the metropolis. The CDF has enlisted you to take them back, and we want you to document your crusade!
We want to see your very best action shots of you, your friends, and your org mates taking on the Siege of Orison. The more action-packed the screenshot, the better! Submit your screenshots either on the dedicated submission thread or on Twitter/Instagram with the hashtag #SiegeOfOrison for a chance to win one of these awesome prizes:
:first_place_medal: 1st Place - Crusader Hercules C2 Starlifter Standalone in-game vehicle with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance)
:second_place_medal: 2nd Place - Crusader Ares Inferno Star Fighter Standalone in-game vehicle with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance)
:third_place_medal: 3rd Place - Anvil Centurion Standalone in-game vehicle with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance)
You have until August 14, 2022, at 11:59 pm Pacific to make your submission.
## Notes:
- Please follow the Contest Rules.
- Screenshots must be submitted on the submission thread or on Twitter/Instagram with the hashtag #SiegeOfOrison. Submissions on this post will not be considered.
- Only one submission per participant. Group submissions are not allowed. Multiple people can be in the screenshot, but only the person submitting the screenshot is eligible for a prize.
- Screenshots must be unaltered and be in their original format - no post-processing allowed.
- The screenshot must be taken within the Siege of Orison event. Screenshots showcasing the most action, creativity, quality, and style for this contest will be considered the most.
Good luck, and show the Nine Tails who's boss!
