After reviewing your feedback from the 4.0.0 and 4.0.1 patch cycles, we’ve taken the opportunity to further refine the overall capabilities and performance of the Crusader Ares series.
With 4.0.2 PTU, a new tuning iteration has been implemented for both variants, and I’m sure many of you have already checked the detailed changes. Here’s a quick TL;DR of what’s been adjusted:
- Flight Performance: The series now features reduced linear acceleration and angular velocity, making handling more in line with its intended role.
- Offensive Power: Both S7 weapons have been buffed, with a particular focus on improving the SF7E Laser Cannon, which was previously underperforming in combat scenarios.
- Durability: Hull strength and overall critical HP for both variants have been increased to improve survivability.
We’re eager to hear your thoughts as you test these changes in 4.0.2 PTU!
As always, we’ll be keeping a close eye on feedback and will make further adjustments if needed.
