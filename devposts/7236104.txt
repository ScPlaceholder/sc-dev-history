From my POV I am very much against the idea of buffing projectile speeds just because the weapon is mounted on a turret ... it basically just doubles the tuning work and it is also hard to pick on which turrets those increases would happen. What we could do (and which would be less headache in terms of balance) is e.g. use the RN setup to make sure that certain weapons so power hungry that they are practically useless on fighters ... whereas they can be powered easily on larger ships with bigger power plants. And most of the times it is the larger ships that have turrets and bigger powerplants.
But the actual issue is that we do not have effective medium range weaponry. Guns are always going to be a short range weapons because you cannot change the trajectory of the bullet after it left the barrel. There are certain ways to mitigate that and those things are on our todo list but they won't be for 3.24.2.
