Thanks for the feedback; I really appreciate it. We try to keep the contests varied, so there is something for everyone. Our goal is to design more contests as in-game events. However, we try to request as little developer time as possible, but with more developer tools and game features going live, it gets easier for us to make these fun little contests more professional. Lap times that are visible to everyone or track markers that are easy to place for us, are just some features we are hoping to have at our hands at some point.
That being said, if you ever organized a competition or an in-game event, you know that there are already hundreds of ways to create unique contests, and the need to think creatively sometimes is also a ton of fun. Of course, currently, we have to be a bit more conservative with our official contests, as we can't just jump into the game with each one of you to show you what we mean in case of doubt, which is why the contests have to be very clear and easy to understand. However, as I said before, we stand ready to go wild when more and more tools are available to us. I'm always amazed when I see the fun ideas our community come up with, and everyone should feel free to send me a pm if they have a great idea they would like to share.
