## Ship Showdown 2956 Phase 1 Has Begun!
The battle for the 'verse is on, and your favorite ship needs your help. Every entry you submit helps push your pick further up the leaderboard toward Phase 2, so don't hold back, post your creation on the Community Hub or X (Twitter) with the hashtags #ShipShowdown and #StarCitizen.
Write and perform an original song, build something out of any material, capture breathtaking in-game machinima, create a trailer, sketch, paint, sculpt, or even craft the ship for real! There are no limits, just make sure it's your original work and showcases your favorite ship. And even if the vehicle featured in your submission doesn't make it into the Phase 2 bracket, an exceptional entry can still catch the judges' eye and win.
## Keep In Mind
A Contest Entry submission may be any type of creative work, including, but not limited to, art, music, writing, or any type of material so long as it:
- Celebrates or highlights a Star Citizen ship or vehicle eligible for Ship Showdown at the time of the contest
- Is original, created by the entrant, and not reposted or copied from someone else's work
- Does not include AI-generated content, which will not be considered
- Observes and respects the RSI Terms
- Is submitted to either submission venue
- Videos must include the 2956 RSI Contest Made by Community Logo at the end for at least three (3) seconds.
- If submitted on X (Twitter), the post must contain the hashtags #StarCitizen and #ShipShowdown, and all images must include the same 2956 RSI Contest Made by Community Logo embedded in the content at a size where the text is legible.
The required asset is available here: https://media.robertsspaceindustries.com/qxub1wbq2zm6r/source.zip.
For full contest rules and to see some of the amazing submissions so far, please head over to the official Ship Showdown page.
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your submissions on:
- Rule adherence
- Quality

- Aesthetic composition
