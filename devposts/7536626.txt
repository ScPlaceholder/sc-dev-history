Your submission is valid as long as it has a resolution of at least 1920x1080 pixels, which is Full HD. This refers to the pixel dimensions of the video and not the DPI setting :+1:
You’re welcome to submit at higher resolutions, such as 2K (2560x1440) or 4K (3840x2160), but please avoid downscaling to lower resolutions like 1280x720 (HD). The final submission should be at least 1920x1080 pixels to ensure it meets the contest's quality standards.
