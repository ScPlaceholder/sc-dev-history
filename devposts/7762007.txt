## Welcome to the 'Stella Fortuna 2955 Bar Crawl Screenshot Contest'!
They say Fortuna favors the bold, and what bolder way to celebrate this holiday than traversing the 'verse on a tavern tour with your buddies?
This year, we don't want to hear, "You had to be there!" - We want you to share five screenshots of your in-game bar-crawling adventures' best (and funniest) moments. Whether it's images of hilarious mishaps, perfectly imperfect-timed toasts, a group of friends engaged in a sip showdown, or just showing off your Stella Fortuna spirit(s), we want to see it all!
## Before You Begin
:exclamation: Before you start on your submissions, please read the contest rules carefully and remember to post your entries to the Stella Fortuna 2955 event page on the Community Hub and not as a reply to this thread.
NOTE: Submissions should include 'Stella Fortuna 2955 Bar Crawl Screenshot Contest' in the post title. :exclamation:
## Keep In Mind
- Read the contest rules! :book:
- Please post your submissions to the Stella Fortuna 2955 event page on the Community Hub. Replies to this thread will NOT count as a submission.
- Use 'Stella Fortuna 2955 Bar Crawl Screenshot Contest' as the title for your post, and select the screenshot category.
- Submissions may have a maximum of five contributors or collaborators. You may include more players in the image (the more, the merrier!), but only up to five submitting the images are eligible to win.
- Submissions should clearly state all individuals involved in creating the images at the time of submission, including their RSI Handle(s).
- Submit your posts carefully, as posts edited after the submission deadline will not be considered. If you have made an error in the post, you can edit it, provided this is done within the official duration of the contest.
- Each Community Hub post comprising five screenshots featuring 'scenes' and/or activities at unique in-game bar locations is considered a single entry. Any other subject will be disqualified.
- Multiple submissions by or involving a participant are NOT permitted.
- You are limited to winning one prize, so you cannot win on multiple platforms (Community Hub, Twitter).
- Post-processing effects are NOT allowed. This includes the use of third-party programs/applications like Photoshop, Reshade, etc.
- Any submission not following the above instructions will NOT be considered.
