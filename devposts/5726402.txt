This is mostly correct. We have an established internal process for when vehicles are added to in-game shops. Vehicles are added to in-game shops one major release after debut, which is generally a ~90 day window. Therefor, the Hull A, Scorpius, Mule, Centurion, and STV will all be available in-game with 3.18. The Cutter, Corsair, and C8R will be available in the following patch, which will release ahead of Invictus Launch Week.
