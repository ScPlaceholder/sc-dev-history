I think that's what it is doing.  The designers specify what minimum level of gravity they want the vehicle to support, and whatever difference there is between this gravity level and the gravity level of  the planet, IFCS generates downward thrust to compensate for the difference and force the vehicle down in a jump.  And yes, if you're on a planet with as much or greater gravity than the minimum set by designers, there won't be any downard thrust at all.  The only downward force will come from gravity.
