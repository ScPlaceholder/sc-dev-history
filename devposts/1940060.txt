The amount of time it takes to debug issues will vary depending on a few things. For example, our backend services team is really good about making service status pages that show the data that our different services are managing which can make it a lot easier to see what is happening under the hood. However, the symptoms that we experience in the game as players might not always give someone a clear starting point for what to look for. In situations like that, the amount of time that it takes to debug something can change a lot depending on where someone starts investigating and if the clues they find lead them towards a clear answer.
On the other hand, there are some aspects of the game that don't have tools like these service status pages and since I'm an Engineer, not a programmer, I don't typically get involved with debugging game code. I mostly stick to debugging tools we have like server logs, analytics, and our service status pages. In the event that I'm not able to find a clear reason for why something is going wrong, I can help out by pointing out things that might be related to the bug such as errors, but I'd have to leave debugging the issue to a programmer.
I wouldn't be able to answer for your specific example about the smg skin because I don't tend to work on debugging any art issues.
