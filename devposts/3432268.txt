We already increase the texture pool size based on your GPU up to a max of 3GB (with the rest of the game using several GB more, especially at higher display resolutions), so it's unlikely there would be any performance or visual benefit going any higher, but you'd be free to try!... I'd expect doing so to just end up pre-loading more textures that are further away from you. I hope to expose a lot more options relating to memory and performance once our new Gen12 renderer is complete.
