## Feature Updates
Contracts and Mission Boards
Contract scope now has a full visual language in the interface. Each contract carries an icon and color indicating whether it is local, planetary, system-wide or universal, so you can tell at a glance how far a job will take you before you accept it. Hauling objectives have also been improved, with markers now pointing at the nearest valid freight elevator kiosk once you arrive at a pickup or drop-off rather than leaving you to find it yourself. Resource gathering quantities on notice boards have been rebalanced alongside this.
- Potential Fix: Notice board contracts failing to generate
- Potential Fix: Objective display on multi-to-single courier contracts
- Potential Fix: Contract generation failing when searching for locations across multiple shards (STARC-224367)
- Potential Fix: Data retrieval, download and sabotage contracts using incorrect location tags
- Potential Fix: Refuelling contracts not completing after the location streamed back in
Starmap
The track button now works both ways. Pressing it on a contract that is already tracked will untrack it, rather than doing nothing.
Starchitect Locations
Traversal and navigation work continues, with ledge markup now extended across guild halls and every landing pad size, and unnecessary climbing points removed from cluttered desks and similar surfaces where they were never intended. Navigation passes have gone through the compound tiles, and the spawn closet doors in maintenance buildings no longer trap AI trying to get through them.
A new lighting flare library has been rolled out across exterior tiles this build, covering watchtowers, extraction sets, command buildings and logistics exteriors. Compound and habitation interiors have had further lighting and dressing passes.
- Potential Fix: Missing collision on several common pipe sets
- Potential Fix: Bars over roof glass that players could snag on
- Potential Fix: Salvage vents not presenting their salvageable material
- Potential Fix: Oversized landing particle effects beneath some ships
- Potential Fix: NPCs spawning beneath the terrain at Nyx Social Station
Genesis and Nyx I
Reeds have been added to Nyx I with updated distribution settings, and scattered rocks now use their proper biome accumulation group, so snow and surface material build up on them as they do on the terrain around them. Rain and lightning have both had further polish passes, and cloud coverage settings have been adjusted to address atmospheric lighting reading too dark under overcast conditions.
- Water areas are now mirrored correctly into surrogate physics grids and kept in sync as those areas change
- Potential Fix: Vertex shimmer and fizzling on scattered instances
- Potential Fix: Temporal anti-aliasing incorrectly handling fast-moving detail
Audio
Ships can now be heard taking off and landing from outside the vehicle, with the audio varying by hull size across small, medium, large and capital classes. This sits alongside the existing pass-by and movement audio, so a ship departing a pad near you should now read as an event rather than passing in silence. Audio propagation has also been reworked for ships heard from inside buildings, and faction announcements now play across compound tannoy systems.
