Hi guys, I'm also disappointed that we couldn't get the new flight model into 3.4. But I like to look on the brightside, and in this situation we get an extra three months to really make the new flight model excellent. We've been working a lot with the feedback from CitizenCon and it's getting to a state that's really really fun and I'm really proud of it.
EDIT: Some extra background on why it had to be delayed. The new flight model is quite extensive in the amount of code and data it changes. We're talking tens of thousands of lines of code and thousands of files, and cherrypicking them into 3.4 would have destabilized and potentially delayed the release. It's a much better bet to just wait for 3.5 and not have to do any cross stream cherry picking.
