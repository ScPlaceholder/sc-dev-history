Hey NotGemini, I can definitely confirm that the harvestables are still in game as of 3.8. That being said...
The main issue with harvestables that are not inside caves is that we are missing some essential systems on the FPS Radar side which makes it so very hard to find them as you have to use your eyes and they tend to blend in a bit too well in the environment. Once the FPS Radar kicks in you will be able to scan for biological signatures if you have the right equipment and find them way easier.
Regarding the planet V4 changes, yes these affected the location where you could find these harvestables but they are still there.
