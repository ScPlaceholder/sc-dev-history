Hello everyone,
Today, we are excited to share the RSI Launcher 2.0.3 with everyone.
RSI Launcher 2.0.3 can be found here:
https://install.robertsspaceindustries.com/rel/2/RSI%20Launcher-Setup-2.0.3.exe
The focus of that version is to add the Accessibility feature with a reduce motion option to stop the video background.
## :hammer_and_wrench: New feature:
- Accessibility settings to reduce motion
:wrench: Fixed Issues:
- Not meeting OS minimum requirement will no longer prevent download and launch of the game
- Scroll bar behavior is improved
- Generic Installer Error
## :bug: Known issues:
- For some users the Launcher signs out too often (this is the focus of Launcher 2.0.4)
- A 3001/3005 error may occur when downloading the Base P4K. Verify to fix.
- Launcher is not able to create the StarCitizen/Live folders for some users. Error 2000 or Error 5008 or Error 5010. Please try creating the folder manually and run the Launcher as administrator.
- CPU usage is hight when the background video is running.
- For some users the background video stops or flickers.
Please try out these features and let us know what you think! Your feedback is crucial in helping us make the Launcher the best it can be.
