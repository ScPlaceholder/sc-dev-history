Hello everyone,
Today, we celebrate the four finalists of the Ship Showdown tournament we held in September earlier this year. The final four ships were the Anvil Carrack, Anvil Valkyrie, Aegis Eclipse, and Drake Cutlass Black.
As announced back then, the owners of the four ships that made it to the semi-finals will get a free limited-edition ship livery and themed shirt for their character!
The Anvil Carrack, our ultimate winner of Ship Showdown, also influenced the design of a new in-game helmet available for free to all backers at the IAE 2950.
We are happy to present the final designs at this year's IAE and can't wait to hear what you think!
If you already own one of these ships, you'll receive the IAE-exclusive paint(& relevant wearable) at the end of the Expo. To be eligible to receive the paints/wearable, the ship(s) need to be in your web-hangar - having them in your Buyback List won't be granting you the Best in Show Liveries. Everyone pledging for a Best in Show 2950 ship during the IAE will receive the paint, too.
