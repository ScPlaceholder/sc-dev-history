Some love VoIP; some avoid it because few people try to exploit it to troll others.
Personally, I love it. It has created some of the most atmospheric events in the 'verse for me. Had fantastic RP scenes with it! Negotiating in shabby backrooms to argue about smuggled goods with fences. Or fun random encounters when I explored the first caves in the Live environment with Ulf, and we met other cavers who got lost too. Or just the friendly 'Good day, Sir' when standing in the elevator with someone.
I can only recommend trying VoIP every now and then, and I know the team will continue to work on bugs and QoL improvements.
