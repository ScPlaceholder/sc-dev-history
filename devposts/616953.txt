To give a small sneak peek:
Few component manufacturers have a more colorful backstory than Yorm. Named for the famed racing pilot Gotlieb Yorm, the company brought his revolutionary shield design to the masses in 2822 and successfully spun it into a manufacturing empire. Today, Yorm remains one of the best known manufacturers of competition-grade components in the UEE. Partly because it embodies the colorful, eccentric and outlandish personality that made the pilot famous.
As we have been working with design on the components and manufacturers, part of the effort has been positioning companies to make the differences between what they manufacture a little clearer.
Yorm has been shifted to focus on producing competition-grade parts for ships, and they are particularly well known for their shields.
For example, as it's listing on the ship matrix, the M50 now is equipped with Yorm Targa shields, where as the Tali now has Gorgon Defender FullStop shields.
