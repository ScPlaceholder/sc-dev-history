Arena Commander is now multicrew!  Squadron Battle, Team Tank Battle, Free Flight, and Swarm Modes have been expanded to include a large variety of our crewable ships.  Players have the ability to enable multicrew spawning on their selecting ships and other players in the match and on your team will have the ability select turret and crew positions.
Give this a go in your favorite gamemodes and let us know what you think!
