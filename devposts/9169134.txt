## Bug Fixes and Technical Updates
Alpha 4.10.2 closes 71 bugs and 5 stability issues since 4.10.1 went live, including multiple exploits. These focus on long standing ship and item problems, vehicles going unresponsive coming out of quantum, contracts reporting themselves unavailable the moment you accepted them, landing gear and decals rendering badly across several hulls, armor and helmet sets with wrong names, missing parts or no storage, and vehicles coming out of a hangar tilted or with their engines already running. 13 of those came to us directly through the Issue Council, and each one carries a link to the original report.
