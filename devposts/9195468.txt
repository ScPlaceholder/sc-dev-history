Hey y'all
Since the recent announcement about Updates to Pledge Gifting and Game Access, we’ve received a number of questions from the community. Much like in our previous Updates to Game Access - FAQ, we’ve gathered some of your most common questions below and clarified the details where needed.
## When does this take effect?
The updates to gifting will take effect on October 30.
## Can I gift anything?
Only items where the "Buying as a gift?" option appears at checkout.
## How will the updated gifting process work?
When an item is gifted, the recipient will receive an email with a redemption code. They can select the redemption link (the code is filled in) or go to the Redemption Page and enter the code to claim their gift.
## Does the recipient need an RSI account?
Yes. They’ll need an RSI account with a confirmed email address to claim the gift. An account can be created after receiving the gift email, before claiming the gift.
## Can I gift an item if I am not signed in?
No. You must be signed in to gift or claim an item.
## Can I pay for a gift with store credit?
No. Store credit can't be used on a gift order.
## Will the gift appear in my Hangar before sending the gift?
No. The gift is emailed to the recipient after the transaction is completed and is added to the recipient's hangar when they claim it.
## Do I get badges, perks, or referral credit for something I gifted?
The badges and perks go to the recipient who claims the gift. Referral credit for the purchase stays with the buyer.
## How does the 30-day refund period work for a pledge designated as a gift?
If the gift isn't claimed after 30 days, the buyer is refunded automatically and notified by email.
## What happens to existing hangar gifts?
Existing hangar gifts and gift codes are grandfathered in and will continue working just as they do today, with the same Redemption Page available to redeem them.
## Do I have to give the gift right away or can I wait to send it to the recipient?
Gifts are sent automatically once the transaction is complete and cannot be scheduled for a later date.
