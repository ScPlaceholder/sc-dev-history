Hi all, the recent Emission and Stealth changes covered Cross Sections and overall Stealth modifiers enabling us to move towards Stealth gameplay we want in the future.
Cross Sections were reduced across the board with one or two exceptions.
Stealth ships have been given a modifier to reduce their overall Emissions, we're monitoring how that goes.
Currently the Stealth modifier on relevant* ships is a linear percentage after all other Emission values are calculated. As we move forward this year we'll put more into the players' hands to manage their ships.
*One of the engineers at Musashi Industrial & Starflight Concern put the Stealth armour on the Razor instead of the Razor EX, this will be patched soon!
Hope this clears up some confusion =]
