Hi everyone,
Following the release of Pyro last year, new Jump Points have been found, leading to our third system: Nyx!
For some, this is a homecoming. For others, it is a new frontier built on stubborn pride and hard-won independence. Levski has always stood apart, and its people have every intention of keeping it that way.
Explore the Grand Barter and meet the locals. Visit the new hospital, either out of curiosity or by misfortune. Check out the Teach's Ship Shop and see what the old-timers have to offer. Miners and freight crews will find plenty of work in Nyx, as new exterior freight elevators are coming to the major stations in our systems and new interstellar hauling missions are pushing the limits of long-range supply routes.
But don't get too comfortable. Activity along the fringes of Nyx has stirred something in the dark. Early reports suggest Vanduul scouts are testing the edges of the system. Nothing has been confirmed yet, but everyone knows how these stories typically begin and end.
Star Citizen Alpha 4.4.0: Welcome to Nyx is now available. Time to touch down, settle in, and write the next chapter together!
