As shown countless times on the hub, the Star Citizen community has a wealth of talent and passion that can deliver some incredible videos, highlighting the beauty of the ‘verse.
Today, we’re sending out a call to all producers, editors, and camera operators to create a commercial for the Anvil Arrow.  In a universe of towering Goliaths, it’s only fair to give the little guys a fighting chance. Agile, aggressive, and armed to the teeth, Anvil’s latest giant-killer, is featuring an ultra-aerodynamic frame, slight profile, and the most advanced maneuvering thruster tech available.
You have until 11:59 PM Pacific on May 15th, 2019 to submit your Anvil Arrow commercial video for a chance to win one of the prizes listed below.
Haven’t seen our take yet? Check out the official trailer.
Ready to create? Read up on the rules, then start rolling and post your finished video in the Spectrum submissions thread.
## Prizes
Three winners will be selected. If you work on the video with two friends (3 collaborators max), all will receive the reward.
:first_place_medal:1st Place – Standalone Aegis Eclipse with LTI
:second_place_medal:2nd Place – Standalone Freelancer MAX with LTI
:third_place_medal:3rd Place – Standalone Anvil Hawk with LTI
Please find the full set of rules here.
## Notes
- Your Video must not exceed 2 minutes
- Submissions must be at least in 1920x1080 resolution and uploaded to YouTube
- No more than 3 collaborators on a single video
- Submissions should clearly state all individuals involved in creating their commercial video at the time of submission
- No unoriginal music (Star Citizen music is OK)
Good luck everyone! We’re looking forward to seeing your Anvil Arrow commercials!
