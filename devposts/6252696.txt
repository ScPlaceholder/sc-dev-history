Hi Chimmi, yes it is intentional, not necessarily because it is the experience that we wanted , but rather to simply address corner cases around players being in the ship while the cargo spawns in. Cargo transactions and spawning is asynchronous which made handling this elegantly not something we could do reasonably confidently in the time we had when we did the initial physicalized cargo refactor. Now that we are moving on to replacing instant cargo spawning it isn't something we are looking to address because as I mentioned before we are changing the cargo spawning implementation fundamentally.
I appreciate not everyone is aware of all these small details during development and I know at times this can be frustrating. As I said in another thread, sometimes we just need to pick our battles when we know we are building up to something bigger. Thank you for the feedback though!
