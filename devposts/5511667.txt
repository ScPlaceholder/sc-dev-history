In 3.18 Evocati, soft death is no longer percent based in the sense of '70% chance' of occurring. It is now directly tied to the health of a critical part reach 0 health. There is an additional health phase thereafter, which once hit, will advance the soft death to a regular death. The buffer tends to be higher in larger ships to favor them reaching soft death more frequently then smaller ships. So you'll want to be careful with your shots if you want to facilitate soft death over a regular death. The buffer size and mechanism will be tweaked based on feedback/analytics.
So yes, you can now 'finish off' a vehicle.
