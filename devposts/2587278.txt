I’ve not been involved in any discussions/tech for roads/rivers, I mainly got involved in planets to help make some improvements to the shading. But there are two full time planet/procedural engineers that deal with the rest of the planet tech.
However, I do know that rivers are ridiculously more complex than they seem. You can’t just whack a valley in a terrain heightmap as we re-use these all over the planet, you need one continuous path that is always descending, and such a path may not even exist on a particular planet. But there probably/hopefully are usable paths on most planets (though perhaps not Nile/Amazon scale), in which case it’s a matter of finding/plotting them, then the shader work, then all the supporting tech (gameplay, VFX, audio, object scattering in and near river etc). But given my department isn’t running this I can’t answer if/when.
