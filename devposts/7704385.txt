Optics have different stats and use cases; it's important to allow them to be different from each other and provide player agency when deciding what works best for their play style as well an ensuring those who have prepared and invested the time to find out what works best for them are in the best position as prep is important in SC
Crafting gives us the option to lean into the weaknesses of those optics and either go further in their use case, making them stronger
Or mitigate some of the weaknesses of that optic
Strong side grades in crafting won't produce a god scope that can do everything
Scopes having upsides and downsides is not a 'poor decision' it's giving you options to choose what works best with your playstyle as opposed to making the scopes all the same leading to everyone using the behring 16x before 3.22 when it had
- auto zeroing
- any increment you wanted
- range finder
Now you have an actual choice and a trade off with the optic you choose
