This Friday at 8:00am Pacific we'll have Vehicle Director John Crewe and Vehicle Art Director Ben Curtis on the show for a big Post-IAE Free Fly, All Ships Q&A.
Unlike the Vehicle Experience Q&A we did about operation of ships in general a couple weeks ago, this show is intended for questions about any specific flyable ship.
- Special Note 1: Questions about ships not currently flyable are acceptable, but those related to flyable vehicles will generally be prioritized.
- Special Note 2: Questions about the recently revealed Perseus will not be addressed here and should instead be submitted to that specific Q&A here: https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/3/thread/rsi-perseus-q-a
As usual, you can post your questions below and don't forget to vote up the ones you want to see addressed most, and we'll see you Friday morning at 8:00am Pacific on twitch.tv/starcitizen.
POSTING GUIDELINES
- Only make replies to the OP.
- Only one question per post.
- KEEP IT SUCCINCT. Boil it down to the thing you really want to know. It helps avoid confusion and begets better answers, usually.
- Do not direct a question to a specific developer.
- Do not submit one question, gets votes with that question, and then reword the question or replace it with another one entirely.
- Do not make use of special formatting in an attempt to draw more attention, and therefore more votes, to your questions i.e. BOLD, H1, etc.
- No "when" questions.
- No "post-launch" or "release" questions. This is a program dedicated to the continuing development of the game, and focused on the immediate and near-term future of the project. Think 3-6 months out.
- No pictures. Make it easy for people to find the question they want to vote on without having to scroll through pictures.
- Questions should be related to the development of our games and focused on topics for our developers to answer.
- Post violating these guidelines or the subjects of the broadcast may be deleted, as may in-line replies.
- Have fun with it.
