The biggest difference between the 3D textures for the planetary clouds and the VDBs for gas clouds is how the data is stored, hence the different file format, but the input can be the same really.
Planetary clouds and gas clouds are similar in that they are both rendering volumetric data but they are not the same tech. The planetary cloud system is closely tied to and relying on planets and their atmosphere.
I have not been involved with the gas clouds enough to confidently say if there can be overlap between the two systems with how they work and if the learnings/assets from one can be transferred to the other.
