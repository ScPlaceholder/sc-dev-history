This wasn’t so much a nerf but actually a fix for a long standing issue with remote turrets, When a single station controls multiple turrets all the turret will attempt to converge on the same point but there are actually very few angles where those turret actually converge successfully, what then happens is when you shoot the turret from your current viewing angle will fire on target but the other will also fire regardless of if they can actually get to the same angle, this of course is absolutely not what we want as it risk hitting the wrong target or wasting ammunition. With the recent fix it prevents the other turret from joining in which was always the intention. Currently the gameplay to switch between turret isn’t ideal however as you must go out of turret view to go back into one of the other turrets, we are aiming to give player a way to toggle between them more seamlessly in the future.
