Another update:
We decided to prevent backpacks from being packed into backpacks (for now), to prevent the infinite nested backpacks.
This change will be in one of the next 4.0 builds.
To clarify in general:
We want to improve the looting experience especially with the introduciton of the Contested Zones that offer a lot of interesting loot for players and allow players to bring this loot withthem without going through "annoyances".
We still want to keep the simulation character of our game and yes the plan is that mass (weight) will matter in the future.
