Vulkan itself won’t require/provide any more options, however there are certainly additions coming to the graphics menu.
We have added HDR in 3.20, but after that we’ll add streaming memory budgets for textures and meshes, and we’ll likely rework the mesh LOD options too. Also we‘ve already mentioned in the monthly updates that global illumination is in development which will clearly have some settings, though that’ll not arrive for quite a while.
We’ll hopefully announce even more graphics options in the very near future.
