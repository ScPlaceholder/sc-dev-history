Future iterations of refining will change it so that its at least 2 inputs of materials to produce a refined material. So using the Iron example it would be Iron Ore (primary) + Carbon (secondary) to produce Steel.
Quality of primary inputs determine the quality of the refined product, to use the Iron Ore example again, if it had a quality of 901 then you would get Steel with a quality of 901 via refining. The quality of the secondary input determines the amount you need, so low quality Carbon means you just need more of it.
I see its been mentioned in this thread already but this is also covered with some of the old concept UI visuals in the CitizenCon presentation: Crafting Your Home.
Some optional material inputs in the refining recipes may also influence things like the refining time or quality to a certain degree.
Once this change to refining occurs, crafting recipes will require Steel, instead of Iron as some do now.
There are also advanced / exotic refined materials, e.g. Durasteel which is used for higher tier crafted items. In this case the example would be TBD Raw Resource (primary) + Steel (secondary), resulting in a chain of the most common basic materials being used to make the most advanced.
