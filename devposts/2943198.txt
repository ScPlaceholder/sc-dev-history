Different temperature extremes now require specialized suits to withstand those conditions for an extended period of time. In addition, players will experience hunger and thirst. Hunger and thirst are designed to be term statuses that you may not notice unless your character has been alive and in game for a large amount of time. Hunger/thirst persist between sessions, but reset on death.  You can check the status of hunger, thirst, and all other UI displays by activating personal inner-thought (F+RMB).

Try to keep track of how long your character has been alive and in game and see if you can figure out the range! We would love feedback on these systems, specifically:
- Survival times in extreme temperatures
- Updated actor status UI
- Hunger/Thirst frequency
- Availability of food/drinks
As always, thanks for your input!
