During 3.0, because of network sync issues, fuel cost was removed from the simulation where it was driven by actual thruster activity and placed into the server where it can only work based on actual ship motion. Obviously ship motion is not a good indicator of actual thruster activity, and I believe this is what you're seeing. The same is true of heat and power usage as well.
