Hello everyone,
Today, we are excited to share the RSI Launcher 2.4.0.
RSI Launcher 2.4.0 can be found here:
https://install.robertsspaceindustries.com/rel/2/RSI%20Launcher-Setup-2.4.0.exe
We’re introducing an important update with this version, featuring improvements to our Datapatcher and VERIFY feature.
This update ensures that all files are correctly placed and checks the integrity of each one effectively. The process is efficient, taking approximately 5 to 10 minutes depending on your CPU performance.
We’re pleased to introduce two new options available when clicking on the channel gear:
- ‘Show in Explorer’: Easily access your game files’ location with a single click.
- ‘Check for Update’: Verify that your Launcher channel is up to date.
## :gear: New Features:
- New Verify process
- Show in Explorer option to access you game file
- Check for update to check you are on the last game version
## :bug:Known issues:
- Some users encounter a flickering Launcher background, which can be partially mitigated by turning off accessibility settings. Please look at this comment for a potential fix: https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/1/thread/rsi-launcher-2-0-6-release-notes/7279512
- The Launcher sometimes fails to create the StarCitizen/Live folders for some users, leading to Error 2000, Error 5010. Try creating the folder manually and running the Launcher as an administrator.
- LCH-1030: Launcher suppresses Screen Saver
Give these features a try and share your thoughts with us! Your feedback is essential for improving the Launcher.
