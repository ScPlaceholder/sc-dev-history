## Welcome to the Killer Klips from Outer Space TikTok Video Contest
Dare to blur the line between reality and nightmares as you allow your darkest imaginations to go wild in 60 seconds or less using in-game footage to conjure an original horror-themed machinima on TikTok. Can you craft a moment that chills us to the bone?
## How to Participate
Follow the official Star Citizen TikTok account (tiktok.com/@starcitizen) and publish a video using the following hashtags in the caption #StarCitizen and #DayoftheVara. The video should be a max of 60 seconds and using the typical vertical resolution (1080 x 1920). Group submissions are allowed with a max of three contributors, who must be credited with their in-game name or RSI handle in the caption. Post-processing effects are allowed. Get creative!
## Keep In Mind
- Follow contest rules: https://play.sc/KillerKlipsRules
- Include the following hashtags in the post caption #StarCitizen and #DayoftheVara
- Videos may have a maximum of three contributors or collaborators. You may include more players in the video, but only up to three people submitting the video/machinima are eligible to win. Video caption should clearly state all handles of those involved in creating the video at the time of submission
- Post Processing effects are allowed.
- Must be following the official Star Citizen TikTok account so that we can contact you to collect your info to credit your prizes
- Video must include the ‘Made by the Community’ logo at the end for at least 3 seconds. https://robertsspaceindustries.com/media/29lj4fy0o9ezvr/source/StarCitizen_Contest_EndSlate_1080x1920-1.jpg
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your submissions on:
- Quality
- Creativity
- Rule adherence
Winners will be notified as a comment on the winning post, as well as a DM to gather additional information to distribute prizing.
## Dates
You have until October 27th at 20:00 UTC / 1:00 pm Pacific to make your submission.

- 2nd Place - Anvil Terrapin Standalone in-game ship with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance) and Purple Haze Paint
- 3rd Place - Gatac Syulen Standalone in-game ship with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance) and Purple Haze Paint
