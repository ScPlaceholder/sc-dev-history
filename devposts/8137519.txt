Follow these steps to ensure a cleaner game experience and make the most of the latest updates:
- In-game, store all your ships in your Home Location
- Clean your cache using our (rather new) Local Settings management tool, which you can find in your launcher through: Settings > Games > Local Settings Management > Manage Local Settings. Feel free to untick the Screenshots, Keybindings, and Custom Characters if you want to.
- If you're having persistent crashes, loading issues, performance drops... You might have kept your files for a very long time across many updates: a full reinstall might be a good idea if you're experiencing issues.
Besides that, LTP should ensure that your belongings are kept and retrievable through your new Home Location after a major update. If you are missing ships, modules, or gear, it is very unfortunate, but our teams are working hard on the Long-Term Persistence to pin these issues down and improve future updates. The following items will always be lost with major updates: Medpens, refinery jobs, ammo, consumables, and some types of cargo.
