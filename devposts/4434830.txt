Hi @WheeledWriter , thanks for your question.  One of the features that we didn't show in CC2021 was our ability to import existing markup, taking the old triggers that were stored in other places and transplanting them into Claudius data.  While this doesn't work for every case it does cover the vast majority of our existing markup.  I'm not a big fan of people wasting their time performing repetitive tasks that are better addressed by software, so this was one of our primary concerns when coming up with the design.
