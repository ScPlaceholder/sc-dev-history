This Friday at 11am Pacific, 7pm UTC we'll have Vehicle Pipeline Director John Crewe and Gameplay Programmer David Colson on the show LIVE from our Los Angeles Studio to discuss their work and process bringing both the vehicles and vehicle experience of Star Citizen to life. They'll reflect on the lessons learned in 2019 as well as discuss some of what they hope to achieve in 2020. All questions related to the general operation of ships and ground vehicles are on the table for this week's show.
BIG NOTE: Topics related to specific "professions" like mining, salvaging, trading, etc. while often done with a vehicle are NOT a part of the Vehicle Experience work, and cannot be addressed by these developers.
