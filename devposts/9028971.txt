## Features & Updates
Vehicle Armor Hit Feedback
Audio for vehicle armor hit feedback is now fully implemented and playing correctly
ASD Facilities
Valakkars added to areas throughout the ASD facilities, providing an additional creature threat
Combine C-788 S4 Cannon Balance
- physical damage reduced from 1200 to 1090 per shot
- armor penetration distance reduced from 12.24 to 2.24
- fire rate delay increased from 40 to 50 RPM
VR Feature Test Updates
- Stereo cursor is now fully body-aligned and no longer shifts position when affected by external forces such as water, elevators, or trains
- Fixed stereo cursor being invisible when hovering over radar UI elements
- Fixed a performance issue caused by an optimization that was submitted in a previous PTU build
