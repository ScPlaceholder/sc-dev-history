Do you feel that it has been softer, and soft enough, in the past?
With the latest iteration of IFCS, I took the approach that the system would use whatever thrust is available to it to maintain that max speed.  But as always, I didn't account for just how much acceleration our ships can have, and I can see where that might be too abrupt a stop.  I will definitely look at softening it.  The danger is always that players will find a way to exploit that.  I've found bugs in the past where a less rigid speed limit allows the ship to continue to pick up speed over time until it's flying over 1000 m/s or more.
I worked with Calix awhile ago on the idea of having an asymptotic speed limit, which is similar to how terminal velocity works in atmosphere, so the closer you are to the speed limit, the more acceleration it takes to get you to that speed.  But that would be more of a physical justification for a speed limit, and would require some kind of tech limit, as opposed to what we have, which is simply an imposed speed limit based on a desire to guarantee a certain level of maneuverability relative to some frame of reference.
