We have tried something a little different with the Crusader Hercules Starlifters to see how it goes and if it is a positive change to fuel balance, we have made both the hydrogen and quantum fuel capacity far larger then we normally would while also making its fuel scoop less efficient, the idea behind this is to allow the player to fly for longer before the need to refuel in conjunction reducing the fuel scoop rate to make refuelling something the player needs to consider and be mindful off before setting out on each trip even if they dont need to refuel every time.
As ever stats are subject to change and it might not stay like this forever but we saw this as a good opportunity to try it.
