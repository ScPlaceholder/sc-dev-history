If you're attending CitizenCon 2953, you're already covered, as the Digital Goodies Pack is included at no extra cost. If you're unable to attend the show in-person, but still have an interest in obtaining the digital goodies, the option to pick up a pack is available online. This is consistent with our previous physical events and one of a few ways we fund these conventions. The only deviation was during the pandemic, when we offered the Digital Goodies Pack for free due to the events being digital (no ticket required).
