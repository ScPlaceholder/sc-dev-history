1) The spooled quantum drive is what allows NAV speeds, that's part of our trapping mechanics so that will not change for now.
2) The enforced speed limit with landing gear is just an IFCS safety feature we added during the Citizencon. There will be an option to disable it later.
3) By default switching to NAV puts you into the QT operator mode. By pressing MMB you can switch to the next available operator mode. There is one called flight (abbreviate FLT, check the top left of your HUD) which has everything spooled but doesn't give you QT control and markers. There is ongoing discussion if FLT or QT should be the starting operator mode for NAV. Personally I am indifferent to this but we'll wait for further feedback before we'll change it over. It is possible we'll customize it later but since all you need to do is to swap the operator mode via MMB (and operator mode swapping is immediate as opposed to swapping master mode) it should not be that much of an issue right now.
