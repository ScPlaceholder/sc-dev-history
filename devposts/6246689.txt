## Keep In Mind
- Read the Contest Rules: ⁠Halloween Pumpkin Carving Contest 2023 carefully
- Multiple entries are permitted, but only one prize per participant
- Group submissions are not allowed
- Only post pictures that include carved pumpkins (or pumpkin substitutes)
- All submissions must be submitted to the Community Hub or posted on Twitter including #StarCitizen to be valid
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your submissions on the following:
- Style
- Creativity
- Rule Adherence
