## Atmo Esports Event
Last, but certainly not least, get ready for ENTER ATMOSPHERE. This post-CitizenCon community-organized event will feature live ship racing, a panel with members of the CIG team, and the Fight or Flight World Championship. Mark your calendars for Sunday, October 20, from 3-11pm UTC, and buckle up for a next level experience! Tickets are limited so don't miss out!
And for those wondering, as mentioned in the CitizenCon FAQ, day 2 of CitizenCon will be shorter (and day 1 longer), so you won’t need to worry about missing any content during the main show by attending the Atmo event. Rest assured that the main show will have concluded (tentatively ~3pm) and many of us will be heading over to the Factory to enjoy some good ol' esports mayhem - we'd love to see you there!
