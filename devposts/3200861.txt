While I’m not the one writing the light rig I’ve assisted the owner a little so can shed some (suitably dark) light on this.
The rig does indeed examine the surrounding lighting, though itevaluates the lighting slightly differently to the renderers lighting shaders for both aesthetic reasons and simplicity. It builds a localised light rig that is proportional and in-keeping with the colour and brightness of the surrounding, with key, fill and rim lights.
It‘s supposed to be relatively subtle, just like in films where they often change the lighting per shot but it never feels like a different scene.
