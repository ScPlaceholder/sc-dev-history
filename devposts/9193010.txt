This is a question you have to answer for yourself, as it depends on a few factors:
- Are you just going to play Star Citizen or any other games?
- Do you prefer a wireless or wired Headset?
- Is OLED Important to you (true blacks)
- Comfort?
- What is your budget? What GPU do you have?
If you have a Quest 2 any modern headset right now will be a serious improvement, even if it just a Quest 3.
The perfect headset in my mind would still be a OLED Steam Frame with high resolutions. Still praying we get that one day. I know there are rumours, but I don't expect it any time soon.
Overall the Steam Frame runs and works well with Star Citizen, but visually it is not better as the Quest 3, maybe even slightly worse. It is much more comfortable than the Quest 3 and everything just works.
If you have a capable GPU and the budget Dream Air is the best you can get right now. Also very comfortable and visually seriously impressive.
