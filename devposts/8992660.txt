## Features & Gameplay
Gameplay
Battaglia (Recco Battaglia)
Added weapon racks and specific loot configurations to the QV Station interior. Added loot placements and weapon racks to the Battaglia outlaw asteroid base. Added a minimap to the Battaglia outlaw asteroid bases. Fixed missing ledge grab markup on assets in the Battaglia outlaw asteroid bases' Cargo Room and Central Hub. Fixed missing lever blocking part of Island 1’s tower.
Instanced Siege of Orison
Replaced generic loot presets with Siege of Orison-specific variants. Checkpoint respawn flow implemented for the mission. Implemented fusebox interactable for comms tower node. Loot on civilian AI corpses updated to be contextually appropriate (food and drink, rather than knives and ammo). Further areas on Island #1 should now be more available after a fix to the lever missing inside the comms tower node.
Vehicle Armor Hitmarker Feedback
Hit markers when firing on vehicles will now reflect the result of each shot based on armor and penetration calculations. Players will see distinct markers to indicate whether a shot hit shields, impacted the hull, penetrated armor, destroyed an internal component, or was deflected entirely.
- Shield hits
- Hull hits
- Armor penetration
- Internal component destruction
- Deflection (shot blocked/bounced)
Note: The hit marker state for component/ship destruction is not yet fully implemented and will be addressed in a follow-up patch
