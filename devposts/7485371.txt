Earlier this year, during Ship Showdown 2954, we called on you to unleash your creativity and rally your votes to help us crown the top four ships in the 'verse.
From Lego builds and original songs to 3D prints, machinima, stunning screenshots, and so much more, you amazed us with your talent and passion.
Thanks to your creative submissions and votes, these four champions rose to the top, each earning exclusive rewards to commemorate their victory:
- Anvil F8C Lightning
- Anvil Terrapin
- Crusader C1 Spirit
- Aegis Reclaimer
If you have one of these fine ships in your hangar, you'll receive their respective 2954 Best in Show paint at the Intergalactic Aerospace Expo (IAE) this year, for free.
In addition to the limited-edition Best in Show liveries, you'll also receive in-game manufacturer posters for each of the top four ships that you have in your hangar. To celebrate the Anvil F8C's overall victory, every backer will receive an exclusive F8C Best in Show pennant.
Plus, the four Best in Show ships will be available to fly for free between December 1 and December 5!

These items will be distributed in two waves - the first on December 2 and another at the end of IAE.
This process can take some time, so don't worry if you don't see your items in your hangar right away.
To be eligible to receive the paints, the ships need to be in your hangar (having them in your Buyback List won't grant you the Best in Show paints).
The Best in Show editions only contain the goodies within that ship's package and will not grant an additional copy of the rewards.
