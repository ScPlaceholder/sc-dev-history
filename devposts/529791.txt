Hello (xē’suelen), everyone! Welcome to the Xi'an Language (uo'aXy'an) group.  We’re really glad you’re here! This is the community area where we hope everyone who is learning Xi’an will congregate and collaborate on the language. Xi’an is a work in progress, and it will remain that way for years to come as the Xi’an Empire and language expand. Please introduce yourself below and let us all know what motivates you to learn the language and how you want to engage with others in the Xi’an Language Community (XLC) to help them learn too. Are you more excited about learning to speak, or read and write, or both?!

Learning a constructed language is not really any easier than learning a natural human language. It takes engagement, time, and lots of practice. We’re all in this together, so let’s keep it friendly and supportive.
