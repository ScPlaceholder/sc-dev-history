Today's Tech Preview takes the server meshing of Nyx I one step further. Nyx 1 uses the same Quasi-Dynamic Server Meshing (QDSM) algorithms introduced in 4.10 (to load-balance the many SoO instances onto different servers), to distribute Nyx I’s many many clusters onto different servers. This latest approach works with much smaller units and can therefore load balance more finely, putting more clusters on colder servers, and fewer clusters on hotter ones.
A feedback survey is available at: Intraplanetary Server Meshing Survey Form. This survey will be open until Nov 30.
Thank you for taking the time, and we look forward to reading your feedback!
