In preparation for Foundation Festival 2952 starting in July, we’re sending out a call to all organizations that make the ‘verse a better place by lifting each other up and helping each other in times of need, making the Star Citizen community one of the best communities there is.
In the ‘verse, the Foundation Festival is a month-long civic program that endeavors to strengthen the foundation of the community by encouraging Citizens and Civilians to volunteer with local and Imperial organizations. Here on Earth, we’re celebrating our incredible Star Citizen community all month long! You can look forward to special Guide System rewards, FREE event-exclusive ship paints, and more.
Starting today, you have until June 30th to send an email with your org info to the following email address: communityteam@cloudimperiumgames.com
Out of all the submissions we receive, we’ll pick six organizations we’ll feature during the Foundation Festival celebrations in July, providing some well-deserved time in the spotlight.
Stuck on how to best initially present your org and its activities?

Keep in mind that we all like to help each other out and ask that all orgs are respectful and treat each other as such!
Also, feel free to introduce your orgs regardless of your number of members, as we will feature various orgs during Foundation Festival in July.
