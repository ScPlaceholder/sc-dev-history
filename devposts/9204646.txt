Greetings!
Nyx 1 has been built using the new Genesis planetary technology and is ready for you to help test. Biomes now blend seamlessly across the surface rather than transitioning at hard boundaries, and can be discovered naturally by flying, driving, or walking across the planet. New GPU-based scattering system drives vegetation and geology placement across the surface. Nyx 1 will include many biome types including grassy plains, forests, swamps, wetlands, beaches, mountains and deserts.
Feedback Focus
Visuals and Biome Quality
• Do biome transitions feel natural as you move between them?
• Does vegetation and geology density look appropriate for each biome type?
• Any visible pop-in, LOD transitions, or terrain seams?
• Anything that looks out of place for its environment?
Traversal
• Does the terrain read well when flying at speed, and when driving or walking?
• Any collision issues, geometry you can fall through, or areas you can get stuck in?
• Do ground vehicles handle the new terrain surfaces as expected?
Performance data is a priority for this test. When reporting performance issues, please include:
• The biome you were in when the issue occurred
• Whether you were on foot or in a vehicle
• Your full system specs
• Your graphics settings and resolution
A feedback survey is available at: Planet Tech V5 Survey Form. This survey will be open until Nov 30.
