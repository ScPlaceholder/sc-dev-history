As with last year, BIS rewards will be distributed in two waves. The cut-off time for the first wave is December 1, 4pm UTC. The first distribution wave of BIS rewards will be sent starting December 2.
For the second wave, we will perform another check at the conclusion of IAE to see if an account has a BIS ship that wasn’t present during the first check on December 1. Based on this second check, the remaining BIS rewards will be sent out once IAE has concluded.
This process might take a little time. If you don’t see the items in your hangar right away, don’t worry. They are on their way.
