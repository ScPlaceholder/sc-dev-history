Generally, moving the camera would be quite bad for a zoom function. Suppose you wanted to zoom in, but there was an object 1km away and another 100km away, both in the middle of your screen - where would you place the camera?
To backtrack to the initial question, the current LOD system does take into account the field of view when working out how big an object onscreen appears, but faces a difficult problem: if a camera is zoomed to, let's say, a 1-degree FOV, how do we know where you're going to point it next frame, or the frame after? At the moment, a strong zoom will force everything towards higher detail, so extreme zooms would be extremely bad news. This could be made more efficient by a system that can provide guarantees about which objects aren't going to be able to get into frame in the next few seconds, but anything with direct player control would likely limit what guarantees can be made.
