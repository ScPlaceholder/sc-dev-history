Hydrogen fuel has been rebalanced to dramatically reduce the size of fuel tanks. The goals of this are to make the sizes more realistic to their ships, since SCU is a volume and the actual fuel should roughly match the size of the ship. In addition to this balancing, fuel consumption rates have also been greatly reduced.
A feedback survey is available at: Hydrogen Fuel Balance Survey Form. This survey will be open until Aug 31.
We look forward to reading your feedback!
