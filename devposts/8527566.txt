Forget any kind of setups that tell you to set sys.OpenXR = 42 or stuff like that. Remove those entries entirely from your user.cfg.
Plug in your link cable, confirm within your head to launch Meta link. Disable any kind of virtual desktop thing that you might have enabled within the Meta app.
When you have the giant virtual 2D screen within the main menu, that's correct (we're forcing that in the main menu). That is the called the "Theater mode" ... you'll get "proper VR" within the actual game.
Once in the game (i.e. walking around or in a ship) you can swap between Theater mode and full VR via LALT+Numpad 5.
