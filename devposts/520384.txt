Re: the gas cloud and voxel fog question, those two items in the report aren't related to each other. The intention is still that the gas could will feed into the nearby fog, but that item is about adding support for hierarchical data within the gas cloud, and adding the ability for volume data to be imported.
Exact features of the particles-to-fog stuff are still being pinned down, but in answer to (1), yes, overlapping two volumes will just add them together, so the only problem would be performance issues if they over-use them. For (2), this is definitely going to be an art-driven feature, so the VFX team directly decide which effects should support this, choose which parts of the effect fade in and out at different distances, and directly create fog particles that can be independent of the billboard particles in the same effect, so for example they could have a clump of small billboard particles that fades into a single voxel fog volume when you put your face in it.
