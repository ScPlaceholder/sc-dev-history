That torpedoes hit agile ships has nothing to do with the EM signatures, that's just down to the flight tuning of a missile (a torpedo is technically the same). It is actually not intended for torpedoes to hit fighter sized ships (they should be fast enough to evade) but we have issues tuning the missiles correctly. Guided ordnance is owned by the server which often suffers from varying frame rates which in turn makes it hard to tune the PID controllers correctly that control by how much missiles can correct their trajectory. Plus there is still the occasional zone bug that suddenly makes a missile veer off in a random direction before coming back ... it's on all on the todo list to get looked at, some of it this quarter.
