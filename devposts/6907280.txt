Hi everyone,
We hope you're all enjoying this year's Invictus Launch Week. As we debut both new and updated ships, we've been closely monitoring your feedback.
After careful consideration, we've made the following changes to the Firebird and Retaliator:
- The Firebird's missile count has doubled from 12 to 24
- The Firebird's signature reduction has been corrected (increased from 20% to 40%)
- All Retaliator turrets have been upgraded to S3 Laser Repeaters
- All Retaliator turrets have had dead zone issues fixed
These updates will be implemented in an upcoming Alpha 3.23.1a patch.
As always, thank you for taking the time to share your feedback, and we'll see you in-game!
