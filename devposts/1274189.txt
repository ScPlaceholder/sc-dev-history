First, I wonder if you mean "curtailing strafe" or specifically decoupled flight?
The way our system works, our ships have supermaneuverability, which means we have a combination of thrusters and aerodynamic forces.  In our case, any aerodynamic forces either work with or against thrusters.  But in the absence of aerodynamic forces (and remember lift isn't yet implemented anyway), thrusters are available to provide for flight.  So stalling wouldn't be a thing.  Dives contributing to velocity certainly can, since IFCS utilizes gravity as a source of acceleration, using it first and then in addition to thrust when changing velocity.
But I think overall the way our ships perform in atmosphere will always be different from what you would expect from a pure aerodynamic simulation.
