1. Many things from Thai. For example, the elementals (tai) combine very fluidly like may words in Thai (and Mandarin). Also, modifiers come AFTER the things modified. The language is very strict about this. Also, the main way to answer a question is by repeating the verb in the question.
2. YES! There are only 9 verbs, but there is an Imperial register that is used ONLY for the Emperor/Empress. For example, the very common word for "be pleasing" (te) has the Imperial form yethē which means "be pleasing to the Emperor." It is IMPOSSIBLE to talk properly about the Emperor without knowing all 9 of the Imperial forms. There are NO pronouns for the Emperor because they are inherent in the verbs. To say "The Emperor is smart" all you say is yesā tūn. It is BUILT IN to the verb that you are talking about the Emperor.
