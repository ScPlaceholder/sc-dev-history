Greetings!
The new Master Modes flight system is now testable in three preview Arena Commander game modes.
- Master Modes: Duel
- Master Modes: Free Flight
- Master Modes: Vanduul Swarm (new)
This feedback thread is dedicated to the mode itself. A lot has changed with these modes even outside of the core flight tuning. For a full list of the changes associated with Master Modes, please check the relevant section in the patch notes.
There are several categories of feedback we are looking for, including:
- UI changes: Targeting bracket, PIPs, targeting UI (excluding mode designation, which is placeholder)
- Weapon changes: Ammo pool, autogimbal balance
- Flight tuning: Gladius handling, base SCM speed, afterburner mechanic, NAV mode usage
As a note, master modes dedicated Arena Commander modes are an experimental mode meant to preview upcoming changes. We will likely not iterate on these modes during the PTU cycle much and instead will be collating and collecting feedback through PTU and into the live release. Also worth mentioning on the "Miner's Lament" map there is a race track available on Master Modes: Free Flight.
Thanks for taking them time to share your thoughts!
