We have this section of the forums so that players can reach out to us and ask questions because want to continue to make an effort to have the most transparent development process of any game. As you have noticed, we don't answer all of the threads that get posted here and there are a plethora of reasons as to why that happens. Some of us do come here to answer the questions that our skill sets allow us to, but game developers can be highly specialized so they wouldn't be able to answer something that is outside of the scope of the work they do.
