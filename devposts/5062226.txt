Pretty much that ... the combat performance in mp scenarios has a lot of variables ... saying 2 solo ships are better than 1 multi-crew ship is too simplistic. It depends on the ships, their components, gimbal and turret angles and the people you have with you. It also depends on how well trained a crew is on a ship ... amazing multi-crew pilots know during combat how to maneuver the ship to give their gunners the best possible shots and this may work in some ships better than in others. So just because blades are coming at some point does not mean switching from 1 multi-crew to 2 solo ships is the best choice for you.
