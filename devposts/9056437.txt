## Features & Gameplay
Orison Relief Support
Fresh off the recent NineTails raid on Orison, they are asking for support to rebuild and ensure all civilians effected are back on their feet. This set of missions will task the player with delivering materials, medical supplies, crafting helpful items, and more!
Note: There is a known issue preventing the completion of the Small, Medium, and Large fabrication missions in todays publish.
