There is a setting which partially controls this, but currently it only allows the hiding of placeholders in chat lobbies (the text specifies that it only works for chat lobby placeholders). The rationale behind this was that it allows you to completely hide the ones which are most spammy, since seeing chat placeholders from someone you blocked over and over can get old fast. In a thread, if we completely hide the placeholder for thread posts, it would mean either completely hiding all the posts replying to that one, or showing posts replying to a non-existent post.
I'm with you on wanting to avoid being constantly reminded that someone you blocked is posting in threads. We could add a setting that would keep the placeholder but hide who it's from, which would preserve the structure of the thread without forcing you to acknowledge someone you're trying to ignore.
