► 5.21.2026: CL 11875683 - Today's maintenance introduces a new hotfix along with a feature update for implementing individual claim modes for item recovery.
Individual Claim Mode Update
Today's maintenance build update includes a new feature to item recovery. Freight elevators now have a new "Claim Mode" button on the terminal that will only be accessible at your home location. This new Claim Mode button brings up a window with all platform (website) owned ship items (other than vehicles, consumables, and items in a vehicle's default loadout) and allows you to claim one ship item at a time back to your home location. Claiming an item will add it to the inventory accessed by the kiosk (may require refreshing the window) and brick the previously owned item. For filtering, an "Exclude Paints" checkbox at the top hides all paints from the list for cleaner access.

## Using the HOTFIX channel
The HOTFIX channel will become available for periods of time when the client-side of the game needs the hotfix. This channel will point to the LIVE environment and using this will retain and add progress to your LIVE account as if you were playing normally.
The best way to play on HOTFIX is to rename your LIVE folder to HOTFIX and verify using the HOTFIX channel on the launcher. Reverse this process to go back to LIVE once it is released or you would like to stop using this channel.
