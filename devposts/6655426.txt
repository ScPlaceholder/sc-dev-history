We're not retiring Progress Tracker! As I've mentioned in a few comments, due to the re-integration of our Squadron 42 feature teams, our team structure is changing significantly. You've already heard a few of these updates in our video content, and we want to make sure Progress Tracker is accurate to what's being worked on, and by whom. We could just extend the work from Q4 into 2024, but that wouldn't be representative of the work our developers are doing.
In terms of specific teams not being updated, this is part of this process as well. Once Squadron hit feature-complete status, we transitioned many of those teams last summer to strike teams, who are now focused on polishing the game. There are also quite a few of the teams on there that have been deprecated entirely, but we wanted to keep them on the roadmap for your reference.
On top of all of that, our long-term scheduling is undergoing significant changes (I even saw a few changes this morning!). So to preserve the roadmap's improving reliability, we're waiting until we get the green light from our production team that we can publish our plan to Progress Tracker. I know it's been a bit of a wait, but we're actively working on our Progress Tracker update, and we'll have it to you as soon as possible.
