Greetings!
With 3.24.2 we have updated all attributes for ship components (excluding weapons) and are looking to gather your thoughts on the current state of these items. More specifically, we are interested in:
- Do you understand the effects that component types, classes, manufacturers, and sizes have on their performance?
- How does the new balancing affect the way you shop for ship components, as opposed to the previous releases?
- Do you find the "theory crafting" of optimizing your ship loadout compelling under the new balancing? Does it feel meaningful to create custom loadouts instead of staying with the default ones?
- How hard or easy is it now to gather the necessary components for your desired loadouts?
- Do you find the available component variants provide balanced tradeoffs, or do you think some are clearly overpowered and others useless?
We look forward to hearing your thoughts!
