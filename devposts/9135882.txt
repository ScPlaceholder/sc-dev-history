We're pleased to share that new bug fixes and improvements — based on your feedback — are now live.
- Your sort preference is now remembered — pick how you want channels sorted, and it sticks between visits (when signed in).
- New reply counts at a glance — see how many new replies a thread has, shown right next to the last person who replied.
- Accurate “new reply” indicators — the new-reply dot now clears properly once you’ve read a thread and no longer counts bookmarked threads twice.
- Clickable tags — thread tags in the header are now proper links you can click (including open-in-new-tab with Ctrl/Cmd+click).
- Cleaner mobile layout — a more compact, refined thread list on smaller screens.
- Tidier spacing & alignment — improved header spacing and consistent alignment of stats labels and counts.
- Channel access stays up to date — the thread list now reacts instantly to your channel permissions, so you always see what you’re allowed to.
- Fixed menu clicks — context menus behave correctly and no longer register clicks outside the menu.
We've got more improvements coming!
