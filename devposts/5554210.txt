The Pisces C8R is not eligible to receive the Best in Show paint. Previous messaging about different versions of the Pisces earning the reward took place when the C8R had not yet been revealed or released, nor did the C8R exist to be eligible at the time of Ship Showdown, when these paints were earned.
As previously messaged, variants are traditionally not eligible for Ship Showdown prizes, but we made an exception to give the reward to both the C8 and the C8X as they were neck and neck in submissions/votes during Ship Showdown, and both are so similar in role. This exception does not extend to the C8R, a variant which was only announced last week.
