We didn't get around to do proper bullet spread values for all vehicle weapons yet. We added some spread to Behring Gatling guns (S4 to S7) as these weapons were updated for the Redeemer and Ares releases and to some special infantry mounted guns. Most other weapons simply have the spread default values set.
We do have a ticket to do a spread pass across all vehicle weapons, just didn't have time for it in 3.17. But you can expect that varying levels of spread will be added to all vehicle weapons based on the RPM, weapon, ammo types, heat state, etc.
