Since 3.12 using the raw data is always enforced with TrackIR. In practice it was only an "internal offset" on top of the data that the head tracker submitted. It made little sense to keep the option in 3.12 as we now use the raw input of all head tracking devices (so Tobii, TrackIR and FOIP just submit what they consider the right offsets and use that right away). If you had the option set to "yes" before then you should not see a difference.
There is a small exception to the rule and that is when you use the head tracking "hold" function (see option "Head Tracking - General - Hold - Use Device Offset"). This is to avoid any sudden jumps when you activate the head tracking hold function (unbound by default). In that case only the relative offset since you have started holding the key will be applied.
