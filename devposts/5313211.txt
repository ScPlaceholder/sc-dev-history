@Orbiting,
what is currently experienced as the light fighter meta is not intended. We stated quite often that we want to avoid any kind of meta and that the current state is not reflective at all of the goal state we are aiming for / working on. In the last year's ISC (https://youtu.be/5jyE46u8Qrw) we talked about our combat plans and we went to work on that stuff right after 3.14 was shipped.
I cannot go into details at this point but I can say that the bulk of our iterations on the combat system since that ISC hasn't seen the eye of the public yet. In our current internal play tests (which includes a large chunk of FM changes) we do not see a dominant meta gameplay for light fighters.  E.g. even in the intended dominant area of light fighters (which is 1 vs 1), medium fighters and solo'ed heavy fighters are still competitive if they're used right.
However, that work is still not in the shape we'd want it for a release, so it will yet take a while until you can see it. I know that's all a bit generic but that's as much as I can say without Jared locking me away and loosing the key.
