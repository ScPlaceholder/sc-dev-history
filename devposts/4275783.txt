There's a concept called 'fault tolerance' that is good for us to keep in mind when designing complex systems. The idea is to basically design something with the idea that it's going to break at some point so that when that does happen, it's able to handle that failure in a way that's a better result than it would have otherwise.  Some parts of the game have things like this in place, and some don't. Long term, we'd want the game to be as fault tolerant as possible, as there are some server crashes that could be outside of our control ( I think there's a story somewhere of a solar flare causing various tech problems ).
