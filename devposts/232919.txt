Hi everyone!
We’re very excited to announce the 0.3.5 update to Spectrum is coming up this Friday afternoon (11am PDT/2pm EDT/7pm GMT)!
After a short downtime (ETA: ~60 minutes), you will see a slew of new features which come straight from the feedback of you, the backers.
Highlights of the 0.3.5 update include:
- Tags within a Forum Category are now more prominent and can be visited independently. Categories can be configured in the settings to enforce a tag be set on all threads in the category (or not!)
- You can now bookmark tags directly to your sidebar and rename your bookmarks aliases by visiting the "Manage Bookmarks" view!
- The Manufacturer and Ship Forums are returning to the Shipyard!
- User-specific global preferred settings to apply Nested or Classic view mode for ALL posts, regardless of the original post’s type. Want old school forum views on all threads? No problem, set all posts as Classic. Want Reddit-style on all threads? We got you, too, just use Nested!
- Forum threads with highlighted replies will now be marked as such in the threads list. This is important for showing CIG staff posts or posts from the leadership of your Orgs.
- Spectrum now supports Unicode 9 / Emoji 4 !
Later this week, we'll post a quick video showcasing some of the updates to the Spectrum UI (hosted by Monsieur Bault of Turbulent himsellf). Once the build is deployed on Friday, we'll publish full patch notes as well!
We’ve been very hard at work making Spectrum a diverse and customizable communications platform worthy of both the Star Citizen project and its community. The Spectrum 0.3.5 is an update from your direct feedback and we plan to continue to iterate this way to keep improving the platform with you!
Thanks everyone and see you in the ‘Verse!
The RSI and Turbulent Teams
