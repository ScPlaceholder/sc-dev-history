Once you crash the crashhandler will take over and opens up a new window where you can put in anything.
The crashhandler already does provide us with a lot of helpful information like Callstack, GameLog, Hardware, Screenshot etc. but user repro steps can be extremely helpful too for certain crashes.
For GPU crashes usually it's most relevant which location you were in or what you did at the time of the crash.
So anything like "Went to Area18 and visited the Plaza, opened the inventory, dragged some items and then it crashed" is sufficient
