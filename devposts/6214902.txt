Having everything enabled dilutes the playerbase too much, this was made evident during our tests on PTU. Having 17 game modes active at once is simply too many.
Instead we have 9 core modes with atleast one experimental mode active at a time. It also gives us a chance to make tweaks and re-enable the mode at a later date with reignited interest rather than everyone getting bored of it in the first couple weeks and not revisiting.
