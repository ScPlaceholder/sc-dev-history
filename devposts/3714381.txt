## Starlifter Hype!
We would like to set expectations early on this one so there’s no surprises. As many of you will remember from previous messaging, just because a deliverable is in a release column does not mean it's guaranteed to release in the initial release patch. When you see a feature listed for 3.13 release, that means it could come in any release from 3.13.0 to a later 3.13.x patch up to the final release before 3.14. Look at that Release Card as if it said 3.13.x instead of 3.13. In the Starlifter’s case, we are currently targeting to release both the M2 and C2 in a 3.13.x patch that will come after the initial 3.13.0 patch.
## In the Progress Tracker, I see that X feature shows it will be complete in June. Does that mean it is finished then and will be released to us?
One thing we want to clarify is that this Progress Tracker is team-based, and what that means is that the deliverable you are looking at is only the portion that this team in particular is working on. And it will finish its piece at the estimated end of its task. Just because the Planet Content Team shows that it will finish Nyx in June does not mean that Nyx will then ship to live. In some cases, multiple teams will be working on a deliverable concurrently, each taking a different piece. We don’t yet have downstream teams in yet, but Audio, VFX, Core Tech (Nyx will need server meshing after all), and other teams will also have deliverables on their roadmap for Nyx, and only when we start to see all these interconnected deliverables coming together can we begin to estimate when you might see Nyx delivered to you in a live patch.
As another example, we have the MISC Hull B on the Vehicle Concept Team. In some cases, a larger piece of content, like a ship, has to be developed sequentially. The Vehicle Content Team can’t work on the Hull B until the Vehicle Concept Team is finished with its deliverable. In that case, the content is like a relay, and the work gets handed off to a downstream or different upstream team to work on the next step of getting that content to a state where it can ship to players.
We know many of you are excited to see things like Pyro, the Polaris, and more, but keep in mind that what you see on the Progress Tracker is only when that team will finish its piece of the puzzle. Look to the Release View when we roll it out with four quarters of cards for a sense of when we might expect to release the content, tech, or features you are looking for.
