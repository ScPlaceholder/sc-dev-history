Greetings Testers!
Ship "soft death" is active for 3.18.0. Some notes on this:
- Vehicles can now enter soft death state before a regular death state
- The soft death trigger favors precise damage over blasting major damage
- In a soft death, the vehicle is left intact and the crew/pilot are left alive
- Seats can be exited, but not reentered
- Cargo is left fully intact
- Additional damage to the vehicle will cause a critical death that will kill the crew and break apart
- Mission completions related to kills will trigger on soft death
- Doors unlock on soft death
- Additional damage after a soft death will trigger a critical death that will kill the crew and destroy a portion of the cargo
- For now, soft death will kill the crew of AI vehicle
We are looking for feedback and possible edge cases/issues you encounter with this system. Experiments are welcome and encouraged! Let us know what you run into below.
