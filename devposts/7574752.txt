The Pyro Star system has been implemented in 4.0, adding a second Star System into the Star Citizen Universe. Pyro adds many new locations to explore and discover, from planets and moons to space stations and asteroid bases. Additionally, outposts are scattered around the planets and moons of Pyro, providing the basic necessities to support a small group of people for an indefinite amount of time. There is much to explore and do in Pyro, and we'd love to hear what you think!
