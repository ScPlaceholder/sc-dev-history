The clouds in the build at the moment are, in fact, just pictures. But then again, for very distant stuff that's probably the right approach.
We're working on some stuff for closer volumetrics that you'll hopefully see some of in the near future - it's not quite as fine-grained as you can achieve with an offline renderer, since to achieve realtime performance you need to make hard decisions about which parts to precalculate, how much memory you can afford to store those precalculations, etc, but it's started to look pretty good.
