Invictus Launch Week, also commonly known as Fleet Week, is the period between Emancipation Day and Armistice Day that marks the beginning of the new recruitment year for the United Empire of Earth Navy (UEEN).
Join us from May 20 - 31, as we welcome the UEE Naval fleet back to the Stanton system at this year’s Invictus Launch Week celebration.
Throughout the event, you can test-fly military vehicles, experience new releases, rediscover old favorites, and tour an in-service Javelin destroyer at the new event location among the clouds of Crusader.
Starting on the same day, we're kicking off a Free Fly event, which grants everyone free access to download and play Star Citizen!
