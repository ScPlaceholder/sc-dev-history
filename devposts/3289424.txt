As for simultaneous controls of remote turrets and pilots ... we do not allow being a "full screen remote gunner" and a "pilot / driver" at the same time because those are two entirely different "roles" you can play in the game. Pilots and gunners should different capabilities, both with pros and cons. The pilot can obviously drive the vehicle (and still slew around the guns using VJoy or the look direction) whereas the gunner role benefits from very stable and precise aiming solution and assists. So that separation is on purpose, we want to you to make a conscious choice which role you want to play.

The guy in the backseat of the Ballista should absolutely be able to take ownership of the forward remote turret guns, not just the missile turret, especially during driving. We'll take a look at that but it won't change though for 3.10.
