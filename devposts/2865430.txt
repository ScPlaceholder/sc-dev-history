Galactapedia is a portmanteau of "Galaxy" and "Encyclopedia."
what ‘tone’ is the Galactipedia designed to establish?
The tone is cool, dry, informative, objective, and simple to read. The language is varied but avoids epithets and laudatory language. Excessive use of adverbs ('hauntingly scarred'), marketing-like language ('the Genesis Starliner is second to none in luxury'), alarmist phrasing ('the pirate returned to her old hunting grounds'), editorializing ('though the vid is popular, it has little cultural value'), instructional language ('note that,' 'remember that,' 'keep in mind that'), presumptuous language ('obviously,' 'clearly,'), and baseless references ('it is known that') are to be avoided.
what is a good design example for what CIG is striving for?
We are not striving to emulate a particular established property. Ideally, each entry will be between four and five paragraphs long, some being longer. With future cooperation from other branches of the company, each entry will have accompanying art. Once footnotes are functional, footnotes.
