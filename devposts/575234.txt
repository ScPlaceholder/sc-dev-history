For sure there is still a LOT to come  the Xi’an on the science and philosophy. There is already a lot of innate classification even in basic words. "Asteroid" is "space-rock" and "beverage" is "eat/drink-liquid". In Xi’an the modifiers come AFTER the things modified (as in Thai), but once your brain recognizes the pattern, it's fairly easy to pick the words apart semantically, by the individual meanings of the individual parts (or tai ("elementals") as we call them when talking about Xi’an grammar. That said, I have no idea yet how specialized their scientific language might be. :slightly_smiling_face:
