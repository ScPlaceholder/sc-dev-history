We're happy to announce that Star Citizen Alpha 3.24: Cargo Empires is now available to all backers! Alpha 3.24 introduces a wealth of updates that improve life in the ‘verse for those vital cargo haulers and citizens of all walks of life.
Carve out a cozy corner in the 'verse in your new Persistent Hangar, whether you want to put your latest haul on display or show off your extensive Pico collection. You'll also notice a new set of terminals in your hangar, one to summon your ship, and the other to gather your goods! From missiles to freight, everything you need can be moved from storage by hand (or tractor beam) and loaded onto your ship exactly the way you like using the all-new Freight Elevators.
