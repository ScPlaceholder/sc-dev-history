During Phase 1 of this year's Ship Showdown, we challenged you to show your support for your favorite Star Citizen flyable ship or drivable ground vehicle by submitting an original creation to the Community Hub or Twitter using the hashtags #StarCitizen and #SCShipShowdown.
As in previous years, you blew us away with your creativity and enthusiasm, and we received tons of fantastic submissions. It would have been easy to pick more than just ten winners. However, we felt that each of the entries below had particularly outstanding content that truly represents the community's passion for Star Citizen and its ships.
