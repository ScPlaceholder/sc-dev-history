The math behind the estimation is:
gasConsumptionRate = totalGasInhaled / timeSinceSpawn
timeRemaining = tankOxygen / gasConsumptionRate
So the estimation is based on your lifetime average breathing rate, not recent breathing rate.
That means the estimation will get more accurate and stable the more you stay alive.
So in your video, the average gasConsumptionRate is fairly stable, but still decreases faster than tankOxygen, making the estimation go up.
