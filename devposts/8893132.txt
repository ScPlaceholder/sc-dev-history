Greetings!
The Xenothreat outlaw group has emerged once again to threaten the Stanton system and its ongoing support of peaceful coexistence with non-Human races. Until July 15, there are five ways to combat the outlaw threat:
- Come to the defense of alien-made ships that are under attack by isolationist forces
- Engage with waves of anti-alien fighters to relieve them of their misguided lives
- Lay in wait and eliminate the exclusionary elitists the moment they arrive
- Draw out their xenophobic lieutenants to weaken the villains' command structure
- Remove their Idris frigate by either destroying the ship or killing the pilot on board
A feedback survey is available at: Return of Xenothreat Survey Form. This survey will be open until July 31.
