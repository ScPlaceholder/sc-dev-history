Since ISC's inception in 2020 2019 (:eyes:) we've historically taken a hiatus at the beginning of each quarter. Last year's enormous Alpha 3.23 allowed us to change that up a bit, but we still took irregular hiatus periods throughout the year, especially after big releases. That's where we're at right now, and the primary reason for no show this week.
Additionally, as referenced in the OP, this year's change to a faster paced (four patches in the last four months) less feature-laden and more content-driven release cadence has necessitated changes to the evergreen show cadence and structure that have manifested in a few different ways, some of the reasons for which were discussed in our first broadcast of this year:
- To account for fewer features (and thus a narrower selection of stories) being in active development.
- To allow developers to focus on the increased patch cadence, ongoing bug fixes, and the continuing push towards Squadron 42 release.
- To preserve select content for discovery via the in-game experience.
We've reduced the overall evergreen show output, and spread the offerings across different formats as needed, being ISC, SCL and the new BTS for vehicles.
Sometimes there will be weeks with two shows, like Feb 11th and 13th, and others no shows like Feb 20th immediately thereafter.
Ultimately, like anything else in Star Citizen, and especially in this year, we're going to experiment a bit and continue trying some new things. Not everyone will be available to sit down for a video, and not every week will have one to begin with, but as the guy that's been charged with leading the team creating these things for 10 years... tomorrow... (WTH?!) I'm okay with these changes because it means the devs are spending more time where I think they're needed most: creating and improving and supporting the games we all want them to make.
Thanks for watching, and we'll be back soon. :+1:
