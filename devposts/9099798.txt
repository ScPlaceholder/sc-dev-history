With Alpha 4.10, we are revisiting fuel tank capacities and consumption values across the ship roster.
The previous setup created several inconsistencies, with some ships carrying hydrogen tanks that were disproportionately large for their size while having comparatively small quantum fuel tanks. In some cases, the calculated tank capacities would have required tanks larger than  the ships themselves. This update aims to make fuel capacities more logical and consistent while better reflecting each ship’s size, role, manufacturer, and intended gameplay.
A feedback survey is available at: Hydrogen and Quantum Fuel Balance Survey Form. This survey will be open until Sep 30.
We look forward to reading your feedback!
