just to sort out actual hardware issues ... did you follow the guide from Tobii in terms of how to set it up? They did a great job explaining all the different options you have in the game:
https://help.tobii.com/hc/en-us/articles/360016498378-Star-Citizen
The IC is not very descriptive so my first guess so lets rule out the basics first. If you walk around and set up the head tracker for the first time it won't do anything as the options are set up so that head tracking is disabled in FPS. Sit down in your ship's pilot seat and do the following:
1) Go to the options (comms foip & head tracking)
2) Make sure the head tracking source is "Tobii"
3) Make sure none of the options are set to disable head tracking
4) Press numpad div to enable head tracking (it is the same as setting head tracking on/off in the options)
See if that works ... you should some initial movement. Then set up the options to your liking. Also check the keybindings (somewhere at the bottom) for additional head tracking related buttons.
