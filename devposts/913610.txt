Hello Citizens,
The new RSI website is now live, streamlined to provide guests and backers a smoother and more intuitive experience. Check it out here: https://robertsspaceindustries.com/
The site has been organized into three main sections:
## Star Citizen
This section includes information on the game and its lore, along with video tutorials to help incoming players master the basics, including account creation, simple movement, and dogfighting.
## Squadron 42
Introduces players to the cinematic single-player campaign and aims to provide periodic updates on its development.
## RSI
This is where players can interact with the community and stay up to date on the development of Star Citizen. It includes a new live Roadmap (previously the Production Schedule) that dynamically updates features, content and optimizations.
We would love to hear your feedback so we can continue to share and improve the experience.
