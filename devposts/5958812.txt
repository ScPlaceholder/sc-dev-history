## Before You Begin: :warning:
Before starting your submission, please read the contest rules carefully and remember to post your entries to the 'Invictus Launch Week 2953' event section of the Community Hub and not as a reply to this thread.
:exclamation:  Any submission not posted to the Community Hub or without 'ILW2953 Mirai Fury Challenge' in the post title will not be considered.  :exclamation:
## ILW2953 Mirai Fury Challenge
Captain, radar reports that a swarm is closing in quickly!
Instructions: There are frequent sightings of Mirai Fury and Mirai Fury MX in 'verse these days. We challenge you to post your best screenshot of as many of these agile short-range fighters as possible to the Community Hub or on Twitter. Read the rules for further details!
## Keep in Mind:
- Read the contest rules! :book:
- Only an image, which includes an in-game screenshot as described by the challenge, will be considered. Any other subject will be disqualified.
- Post your contest entry to the Community Hub (in the Invictus Launch Week event section), or to the challenge announcement post on Twitter (don't forget to include #StarCitizen in your post).
- Use 'ILW2953 Mirai Fury Challenge' as the title for your submission on the Community Hub.
- Replies to this thread will NOT count as a submission.
- Make it count: only ONE entry per challenge.
- Post-processing is allowed, but remember that sometimes less is more.
- Consider turning off the HUD when taking screenshots, as this might make for better composition. Check out some excellent tutorials by DigThat32, Mr. Hasgaha, or NotGemini.
- You are limited to winning one prize during the Invictus Launch Week 2953 Screenshot Challenge, so you cannot win on multiple platforms (Community Hub, Twitter) or more than one of the six challenges.
- Exception: Group submissions are allowed for this challenge. You can form a team consisting of a maximum of five people. Please add the handles of your team members when submitting your entry.
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your entries based on:
- Quality
- Creativity
- Rule adherence
## Dates
The submission period will end on Sunday, May 21, 2023, 16:00 UTC.
## Prizes
Exception for this challenge: Group submissions will be considered. One team of up to 5 people, with each member winning a:
- MISC Starfarer Gemini in-game Standalone ship with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance)
Don't forget that you can also submit your entries to Twitter (including the hashtag #StarCitizen) for an additional chance to win!
So, jump into your Furys (which you can borrow for free at ILW) and take on the challenge!
Good luck, pilots!
