We have also asked you to tell us about the Bar Citizen events happening, as we're planning to come to you throughout the rest of the year (https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/1/thread/bar-citizen-world-tour-we-re-coming-to-you).

Thus, we are looking for Bar Citizen events around the globe to attend – so please post your events on Spectrum, Social, and https://barcitizen.sc/ so everyone can see where events nearby will happen.
