To piggy back on Rich's comment, you can easily get your items back by navigating to your account on robertsspaceindustries.com and selecting the Character Reset option -> this will re-attribute your items in-game.
This is actually no different than how it's already worked on previous patches - it's just more a topic of discussion because the frequency of dropping your items increases with 3.15 (in previous patches, the items were only lost if they were in your hands).
