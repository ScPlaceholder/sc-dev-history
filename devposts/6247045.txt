The HUD is something we are working on, and I wouldn't phrase it better than what Chris Roberts recently shared with the community:
There is a lot of great tech and features that we are bringing into Star  Citizen from Squadron 42 that will dramatically change the feel and  polish of Star Citizen. For instance a lot of your suggestions in In  Game Comfort and Readability have already been addressed by the changes  we made for Squadron 42. The plan has always been to bring this work  into Star Citizen, Squadron 42 was just a good way for us to focus and  really polish the feel and usability without the pressure of maintaining  functionality as we iterated on it for the always live environment of  the PU. We've now hit an inflection point in Squadron 42's development  that allows us to start bringing this work into Star Citizen in the near  future. - ⁠Chris Roberts
