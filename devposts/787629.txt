Hello, Testers!
RSI launcher 1.0.0-alpha.28 is out on PTU. Your launchers should auto-update automatically.
## Notes:
## 1.0.0-alpha.28 - 2017-12-14
## Added
- A new installation phase was added to support downloading a base pack for each game. This will alleviate slowdown problems encountered when the initial download was near 100%.
- Added application analytics across all installation phases and launch states.
## Fixed
- The launcher will no longer keep swapping background images while a game session is in progress.
- The launcher audio tracks will now properly stop during a play session.
- When no news or patch notes are available, an empty view is displayed.
- Fixed a bug where logging out during a patch or play session would result in unpredictable behaviour.
