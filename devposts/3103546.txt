That is a separate issue that has nothing to do with look ahead. It happens because you unbound your flight axes from the mouse. Therefore the mouse input "detects" that it is available for head movement and reverts to that which is not ideal in cockpit situations. The issue popped up as we removed a couple of obsolete axes which blocked that behaviour from happening before 3.9. So the issue is known but the solution is not straight forward so I can't promise yet when it is going to be fixed. The easiest workaround would for now would just be to not remove the flight rotation bindings from the mouse.
