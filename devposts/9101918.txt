With Alpha 4.10, currently being deployed to the live servers, we are revisiting fuel tank capacities and consumption values across the ship roster.
The previous setup created several inconsistencies, with some ships carrying hydrogen tanks that were disproportionately large for their size while having comparatively small quantum fuel tanks. In some cases, the calculated tank capacities would have required tanks larger than the ships themselves. This update aims to make fuel capacities more logical and consistent while better reflecting each ship’s size, role, manufacturer, and intended gameplay.
## Quantum Fuel Tanks
Quantum fuel tank capacities have generally been increased to better match the size and intended purpose of each ship.
Alongside these capacity changes, we have also reviewed how far ships can travel using their available quantum fuel and how much fuel is consumed by quantum drives of different sizes.
This pass takes several factors into account, including:
- Ship size and archetype
- Intended role and specialization
- Manufacturer
- Quantum drive size
- Expected travel range
We have also incorporated feedback from the previous version of this balance that was tested during Alpha 4.9. Several issues reported during that testing period have been addressed as the updated balance returns in Alpha 4.10.
Overall, ships should now have quantum fuel capacities that feel more appropriate for their size and intended use. Let us know what you think in the dedicated Feedback thread!
## Hydrogen Fuel Tanks
Hydrogen fuel tank capacities have also been reviewed across the ship roster.
As we continue adding more opportunities for players to participate in refueling gameplay, it became important to revisit the previous approach to hydrogen fuel balance.
Previously, tank capacities could vary significantly between ships of a similar size or archetype. The new pass brings these values closer together while still accounting for the individual characteristics that make each ship unique.
Hydrogen fuel tank sizes have generally been reduced or normalized, but this does not mean ships will spend less time in normal flight. Fuel consumption has been adjusted alongside tank capacity so that the overall amount of time a ship can remain airborne should remain broadly similar. We're aiming to make the numbers make sense without changing how the ships feel to fly.
These adjustments should also reduce the large discrepancy between hydrogen and quantum fuel capacities, creating a more consistent foundation for refueling gameplay as we continue to work on future systems.
Note that we've also rebalanced refueling missions to account for the updated fuel consumption and tank capacity values coming with Alpha 4.10.
The mission pool has also received additional tuning to prevent inappropriate targets, such as the Idris, from appearing in refueling contracts.
