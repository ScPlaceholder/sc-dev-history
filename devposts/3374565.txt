Hi all,
As we head into the period every quarter where we closer and closer to the release of a new quarterly content patch, this Friday we'll have a Q&A program with team members dedicated to the Builds, Publishes and Platform work that happen outside the persistent universe to make these aspects of Star Citizen possible. Our guests will be:
- Miles Lee, Director of Build Operations
- Ahmed Shaker, Director of Publishing Technology
- Benoit Beausejour, Turbulent
This one will be a little different as the topics appropriate for our guest are things entirely outside the game universe, such as:
- Publishing Technology: The planning, designing, provisioning, configuring, securing, maintaining and monitoring of global cloud resources required to run Star Citizen Dedicated Game Instances and its Universe Back-end Services, or put more simply - Deploy, Operate and Monitor in common DevOps parlance, the various game environments we run in the cloud, from QA/Staging up to Evocati/PTU to Live/Publish and everything in between.
- Build Operations: The creation and support of tools for assisting developer workflows within our version control, maintaining the build system for the game along with microservices which help support a more robust user interface into everything mentioned above, overseeing branching and integration and migration of data between the various builds different teams work in for various purposes.
- Platform Services: Operation, deployment and monitoring of platform services, community tools and game release systems. Workflows and distribution of game builds through the launcher(s). Creation & support of tools needed to run the platform with the game as a service through development and deployment.

POSTING GUIDELINES
- Only make replies to the OP.
- Only one question per post.
- KEEP IT SUCCINCT. Boil it down to the thing you really want to know. It helps avoid confusion and begets better answers, usually.
- Do not direct a question to a specific developer.
- Do not submit one question, gets votes with that question, and then reword the question or replace it with another one entirely.
- Do not make use of special formatting in an attempt to draw more attention, and therefore more votes, to your questions i.e. BOLD, H1, etc.
- No "when" questions.
- No "post-launch" or "release" questions. This is a program dedicated to the continuing development of the game, and focused on the immediate and near-term future of the project. Think 3-6 months out.
- No pictures. Make it easy for people to find the question they want to vote on without having to scroll through pictures.
- Questions should be related to the development of our games and focused on topics for our developers to answer.
- Post violating these guidelines or the subjects of the broadcast may be deleted, as may in-line replies.
- Have fun with it.
