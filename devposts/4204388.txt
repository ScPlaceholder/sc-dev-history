@daedalus, AFAIK we have a theoretical limit of spawning 30 rounds per second per weapon (that's 1800 RPM). The reason for that lies in the custom network (and in it's internal ticking) for weapons which makes sure that server and client never get out sync when it comes to the amount of bullets spawned.
In terms of pure gameplay performance you will not see a big difference between firing 16 or 30 shots per second as the relative amount of missed vs hit projectiles will roughly stay the same (and that is also what most other sims do, simulating every bullet from high ROF weapons is just unnecessarily taxing on CPU and network).
So it is really a question of adapting visuals and audio to make it seem more brrrtier. We'll poke the respective teams about it.
