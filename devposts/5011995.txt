## Notable Changes for April 13, 2022
## Progress Tracker
With this publish, we're adding the Q2 2022 schedule for most of the downstream teams, with the remaining teams' schedules arriving in a subsequent publish.
Mag Stripping/Refill
Due to a shift in priority to the ammunition work being done in Q3 and Q4 2022, this deliverable is being removed from the Weapon Feature Team's schedule temporarily until it has been properly rescheduled.
The following deliverables have been added to the Progress Tracker:
Seated Item Handling
This feature will add the ability for players and NPCs to use items or smaller weapons while seated in a passenger seat of a vehicle or ship. This deliverable has been added to the Actor Feature Team's schedule.
Stanton Fashion Clothing
Creation of additional fashion outfits, both masculine and feminine, for the Stanton system. This has been added to the Star Citizen Character Art Team's schedule.
Mantle T2
Improving the existing mantling feature, including mantling up to a surface without a wall to push up against, jumping over a gap to reach a mantle, and overall improved detection radius. This deliverable has been added to the Actor Feature Team's schedule.
microTech Lagrange Points
Implementing additional reststop locations surrounding microTech, including both the stations themselves and the gas clouds surrounding them. This deliverable has been added to the EU Sandbox 1 and Lighting team's schedules.
Sliding
This feature will introduce a new traversal method for players, allowing them to traverse short distances by sliding along the floor. This will add a new element to combat situations. Previously removed, this deliverable has returned to the Actor Feature Team's schedule.
