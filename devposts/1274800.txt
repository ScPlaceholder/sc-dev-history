The system was designed to support that level of flexibility, but at the moment, it's mostly in the hands of designers to propose the input and control mappings that they believe will be most useful.  There isn't anything special about coupled or decoupled, for example.  They're just specific mappings of input to core components of the flight model.  There are so many different ways to control a ship, and as you say, what works for one type of input doesn't necessarily work well for another.
