Greetings all!
We have introduced AI interdiction with the release of 3.0.0ao.  During a quantum travel, there's a chance enemy pirates will knock you out and attack you.  Additionally, there are random environmental encounters scattered throughout the verse.  You have a chance to stumble across these during normal travel.  Now that traversing Stanton  is a little less safe, we would like to know what you think and what issues you are encountering with this new mechanic.  As you travel about Stanton doing various missions and exploration, please tally the number of quantum jumps, the number of times you are interdicted, and the number of random encounters you happen across.
Once you have made 10 quantum jumps, please fill out the following survey: https://goo.gl/forms/nByv6Q6TEqC0O2xs2.
As always, thanks for helping make Star Citizen better!
