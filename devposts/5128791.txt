June is Pride Month! :rainbow-flag:
We invite each of you to celebrate Pride all month long by showing us your colors! There are no limits to your creativity for this contest. You can take a screenshot in-game, craft a card, bake a cake, or make a video. The sky is the limit. Just make it as colorful as possible!
We'll take all submissions and organize them in our annual Pride gallery, hosted on our official website. Take a look at the wonderful submissions received for the 2021 edition of our "Show us your colors" celebration.
For this contest, we won't have the usual 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place! Instead, we're simply going to give away TEN Anvil Spartans! We'll be on the lookout for submissions we think are incredibly awesome!
Multiple submissions are allowed, but no group submissions, and you are only eligible to win one Spartan. Your submission must be related to Star Citizen, but that is the only restriction. Any medium of creativity is allowed.
Post an image/video in the Spectrum submission thread or on Twitter/Instagram (using hashtag #PrideMonth2952) by Friday, June 24th at 11:59 pm Pacific for a chance to win one of ten:
Anvil Spartan Standalone Vehicles with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance)
Be sure to check out the rules before submitting!
Good luck to all of you! We can't wait to see your submissions! Wishing everyone a happy #PrideMonth2952 from all of us here at Cloud Imperium Games! :rainbow-flag:
