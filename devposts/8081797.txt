As we gear up for Foundation Festival this July, we’re putting out the call to all organizations eager to recruit! Whether your org offers aid to those in need, mentors new Citizens, safeguards the ‘verse, or just thrives on comradery and fun — we want to spotlight you. If building community is part of your mission, this is your moment to shine.
Foundation Festival is a celebration of fellowship, dedicated to uplifting one another and giving back to the community that supports new and veteran players alike. This year’s event includes the introduction of our improved Referral Program, an opportunity for experienced players to provide helpful guidance through our Guide system, and a Kindness and Comradery community contest highlighting acts of kinship that build lasting bonds.
:mega: Starting today, you have until June 30th to send an email with your org info to the following email address: communityteam@cloudimperiumgames.com
From the submissions we receive, we’ll choose a selection of organizations to feature in our Org Spotlight Comm-Link to spread the word about your contributions and community.
## Make sure to provide the following when submitting your org for consideration:
- The name of your org
- Your org logo
- A quick description of your org and its interests
- A link to the org page
- Tell us why this is the perfect org for a new player to join
Feel free to introduce your org, regardless of size or focus, as we'll be highlighting a diverse range or orgs throughout the Foundation Festival this July.
