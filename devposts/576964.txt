There are MANY different “registers” in the language. The Service dialect sounds more earthy and gruff. The Proper language is more precise and polished.
The life span difference gives the Xi’an profound advantages over Humans in the aggregate. They are slower to mature than we are, but once they are "adult" at about age 50-70 or so (during and after their Service) they still have 200 years or more to live. If they are linguistically gifted, for example, they have time to learn dozens and dozens (even a hundred or more) of human languages. That's something that humans typically cannot do, no matter how gifted the are. They have time to read ALL of the human classics. The life span kind of changes everything.
