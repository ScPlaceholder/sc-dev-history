You cannot divorce a technical endeavor from the context it works within. I can think of many different ways to create some concept of a server mesh, but the only one that matters is the one that solves the problems of the game we have. We have to make many decisions along the way about questions like "how fundamentally do we need to change the game?" and variations of approaches with time trade offs. It can be interesting to consider what it would've been like if we wrote the engine from scratch with this in mind instead of modifying the engine we have, but in the end they don't amount to much more than interesting introspection because the truth is that we have the engine we have, and the timetable we are trying to hit.
As an aside, just briefly as a programmer, I see thinking like this a lot that try to look at things from an overly abstracted point of view, but I don't think this is the most productive approach. The best solution is the most direct solution to the problem you have. For any programmers out there that want to know more about what that means in practice I highly encourage you to get more familiar with Data Oriented Design:
https://youtu.be/rX0ItVEVjHc
This is as much about a way of thinking about problems as it is about specific code implementations.
