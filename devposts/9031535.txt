We have done a balance pass on various Ballistic Repeater weapons to greatly reduce their damage. The following has been updated:
- BEHR Ballistic Cannon S4: damage increased from 325 to 1090 physical per shot. armor penetration distance reduced, and fire rate delay increased.
- BEHR Ballistic Gatling S5: damage reduced from 126.5 to 102.5 physical per shot
- Esperia Deadbolt Cannon S4: damage reduced from 683.1 to 610 physical per shot
- Esperia Deadbolt Cannon S5: damage reduced from 1024.2 to 950 physical per shot
- Vanduul Plasma Cannon S5: damage reduced from 1000 to 970 energy per shot
A feedback survey is available at: Ship Weapons Balance Pass Survey Form. This survey will be open until Aug 31.
We look forward to reading your feedback!
