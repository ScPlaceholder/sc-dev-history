Greetings,
We have made numerous changes and quality of life improvements to VOIP, including:
- Ship Hailing - You can now start a video call with the pilot of a ship/vehicle that you're targeting while in your ship/vehicle with a hotkey, selecting the target in either the Comms/Targeting MFD, or in your MobiGlas Comms app
- Video Calls - Video Streaming rendering (video calls made from large distances away) have been made much nicer, both in image rendering and more natural lighting
- Ship Channels - When entering a ship, you now join its ship channel. When selecting the ship channel after you've joined it, you can VoIP and Chat with other members that are actively in that ship channel. You'll automatically leave the ship channel if you leave the ship for a period of time or move too far from the ship
- MobiGlas Channel Preferences - You now have the ability to change the color and the name of your channels, and whether each channel gets directed to visor chat, making managing them much easier
- Audio Widget - Your visor will now tell you which VoIP channel you'll broadcast to, who else is in that channel, and which other players are speaking to you
- Invite Channel to Party - You can now invite the members of an entire channel into your party
We are now stress testing the services, so outages may be possible, but we would love your feedback on these changes provided they are in working order.  As a note, currently VOIP is still disabled by default and has to be manually enabled.  That is not our intent and we will be working on some stability fixes with the goal of having players automatically join proximity chat upon entering the PU.
