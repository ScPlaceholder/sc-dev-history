I hate that my system seems to require some kind of assistance, and I'm always considering the reason it is necessary.  It has always been my goal to make ESP unnecessary.  And it very much is in my hands.  If my model didn't require it, it wouldn't be there.
From my study of the issue, it just seems that 1st order control (velocity control) in a system that includes 3rd order motion, is too difficult for the pilot to manage on his or her own.  If we want to have 3rd order motion, and considering the issues we've had with higher jerk ships being far too maneuverable, I think we do, and we want to support velocity control, I don't see a way around it.  And primarily what ESP does, when working properly, is put a limit on your velocity as you're closing on the target so you don't overshoot.  If it's not strong enough, you end up oscillating over the target.  But too strong and you're fighting to adjust the aim point at all.  So it's mostly about dynamic damping based on targeting.  I do think it would be an improvement if we had a way to switch damping on and off rather than having a dynamic, "intelligent" system that tries to decide when to use it and when to not use it.  But I don't know of a way to do that universally well for all players and input systems.
Anyway, we do have a way to turn ESP off.  And I'd like to have a way to at least scale ESP relative to a baseline tuning so players can turn it down if they want.  But we can't have it going the other way because if it's turned up too much, it becomes an aimbot (obviously) and that's not what we want.  But if players have an option to dogfight without ESP or with a scaled down ESP, if/when we ever get to a point where ESP isn't needed, we should have a lot of players making this choice.  I've only heard of a couple of people who can play this game without ESP at the moment.
