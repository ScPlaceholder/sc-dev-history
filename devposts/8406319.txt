A few years back nvidia introduced a feature called SMP (Simultaneous Multi-Projection). That was a hardware solution to allow you to render multiple views without having to repeat lots of calculations (culling geometry outside of the view frustum for example). It wasn't something easy to just turn on, you have to put effort into using it, and this being back in 2015 or so, few people used tripple screens, so it was rarely implemented. Last I heard it didn't get much use outside of VR.
I'm not sure if there are modern alternatives.
