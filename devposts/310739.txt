Engineers focus on the repair and maintenance of the ship they are on, while Mechanics focus on the repair and maintenance of vehicles within their ship's hangar.
So in the case of the characters we met on the tour, Chief Engineer Morrow looks after the Idris' engines and systems, while Chief Mechanic Webster looks after the small crafts carried aboard the Idris.
