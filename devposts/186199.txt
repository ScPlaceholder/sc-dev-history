Hi everyone! Looking ahead at the next few months, there are some events coming up that are large enough they should be on your radar.
The first is Crux Cup, a community organised and run in-game space race event with close to 160 backers competing within three tiers of competition.  This is the third year for Crux Cup and it’s only getting bigger and better! Find out how to follow the action, and more at CruxCup.com. Congratulations to the community members who are organizing these events.
August sees us in Cologne again for Gamescom, from Monday 21st through Saturday 26th. If you’re attending Koelnmesse then make sure to visit us at our booth in Hall 9. There will also be plenty of opportunities to meet with the team in the evenings so stay tuned for further announcements. See here.
Friday 25th August sees us return to Gloria Theater in the evening with Chris Roberts hosting a live presentation. If you didn’t manage to get a ticket (sold out) then make sure to tune in on Twitch as we livestream the show. Doors will open at 8pm and the show to start 9pm.
Then in October we’ll be hosting CitizenCon in Frankfurt at Capitol Theater on Friday 27th October. Tickets have already sold out, so if you don’t have one, look out for further announcements about where to catch the live broadcast. There will also be alternate community run viewing parties around the world including Austin and Paris.
