Not in the short term but I wouldn't mind adding another gimbal submode for that in the future (atm we have "slave to head" and "slave to rotation/Vjoy"). What you can do in the mean time:
Option A: Set up VJoy controls for the wheeled vehicle. While being in vehicles then press LALT-G to swap between following the VJoy or the Head with your gimbals.
Option B: Look at the mineable you want to mine and aim with your head. The press RALT-L to freeze the gimbal in place.
