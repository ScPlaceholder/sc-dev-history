Indeed, straight compression would just make the scene look very murky and washed out, though it's worth mentioning that we (like almost all games at this point) do run the linear brightness values through a film-like compression curve (AKA tonemapping) after the exposure step.
In theory you could have scenes where the value gap between the bright and dark areas was so large that you could globally map all bright pixels down to close that range, but very often you're going to have cases where the highlights in the dark are still brighter than the lowlights in the light, so pulling one of them down would interfere with the other.
