Which specific terrain in the 2016 demo are you referring to? Most of the general landscape could be made very easily in V4, though the tallest mountain would probably need a local heightmap dedicated to it due to its scale. The cliffs in the small valley are 3D hand placed assets and not part of the planet tech itself, and will LOD then fade out at distance as with any other mesh. It’s certainly a goal of mine and the art team to get such natural looking steep cliffs in, but we’re not quite there yet.
With regards to POM, the effect itself is very high quality, so I’d need a specific example to understand your concern. It’s more likely just different art assets or a difference in resolution. On that topic, the resolution of ground textures is the same as V3, but V2 was fundamentally different and hard to compare. It didn’t support the variety we have in V3/V4 or the hand crafted / art directed visuals, but it could excel in some other areas. But I‘m hoping we can release 2x resolution ground detail textures in the future for those with more available VRAM (though there are costs involved with shipping larger game data).
