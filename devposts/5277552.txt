Thank you very much for your feedback. We really appreciate it.
We collect feedback from all players from the official Feedback Spectrum thread for Siege of Orison (https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/4/thread/3-17-2-live-special-feedback-siege-of-orison) and many other platforms like Reddit or YouTube.
For example, elevator doors currently close slower to give more players time to enter an elevator as a group, even at a slow pace. In the Siege of Orison PTU playtests, we noticed that the doors closed too fast, and groups were often separated. And the devs are currently working on several fixes for some elevator issues.
I would like to invite you to add your feedback to the official thread. This will make it much easier for us to evaluate your ideas and suggestions.
