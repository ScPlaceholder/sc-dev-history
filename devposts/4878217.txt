This was common practice for all games on optical media. Many platforms had a disk seek time of something like 100ms I believe, so scanning back and forth for dozens of files quickly adds up to many seconds.
For some games I’ve worked on the past we recorded every file load event to get into the first level, and that’s the exact order you burn them to CD.
Also the different read/seek speeds at different points on the disk came into play and factored into where you placed the files.
