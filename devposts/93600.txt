Hey VolumeDigital!
We looked at Impulsonic a couple of years ago when it was still indeed Impulsonic, and at that time they didn't have full support for CryEngine (now Lumberyard for us of course) so that was one major reason why we didn't go for it back then.  They said it was coming but never seemed to develop into anything.  It also didn't have Wwise integration to the extent that it'd be usable for us.
The system-based needs we have for audio propagation also aren't catered for by a lot of these 3d/spatial audio tech firms that have now jumped in with the VR companies.  I wish they were as it would have bought us a lot of time to work on!  However there are inherent shifts between the macroscopic (i.e. audio propagation in space) and the microscopic (on foot in ships/on planets etc.) that we have to put a lot of time and thought into and none of these more off-the-shelf middleware solutions deal with that issue.  So in many ways our audio propagation is a bigger problem than localised spatialisation etc.
