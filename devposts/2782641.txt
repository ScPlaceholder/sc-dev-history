Hi everyone,
As we've mentioned before, iteration is a large part of the fluidity of game development.
Recently, we've taken a look at the Reliant series, which led to a few changes we'd like to bring to your attention.
- A second gimbal twin mount by default will be added to the Reliant Kore.
- Two S3 missile hardpoints will be added to all Reliants. (Default 2xS2 missiles per rack)
- A minor health buff will be applied in key areas to help strengthen some wing points we felt were too weak.
These changes will be deployed in Star Citizen Alpha 3.9.0.
