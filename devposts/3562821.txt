To celebrate this year’s premier aerospace event on microTech, we’re launching a screenshot contest alongside our Free Fly event. From November 20 through December 2, you have the chance to win one of the most sought-after ships in the ‘verse!
Here’s how it works:
Starting on November 20 (4 pm UTC), different ship manufacturers take over an expo hall every day. There’s something new to see every day, and nearly everything on display is available to test-fly for free!
So, we’re looking for the best screenshots that encapsulate each day of the IAE. We'd like you to either highlight the manufacturers' expo hall presence, or the ships and clothing available on that event day. You can check out the schedule here to plan your flights.
