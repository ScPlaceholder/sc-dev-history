## Ye Be Warned!
Posts must be submitted to the Community Hub under Pirate Week submissions. The title "Pirate Week 2026 - Most Wanted Contest" will be added. Any post with a different title will not be considered.
- Replies to this thread will NOT count as a submission.
- Submissions must be created using the official Most Wanted poster template, available above.
- Submissions must feature an in-game screenshot of the entrant's own character.
- Submissions must be original, created by the entrant, and not reposted, copied, or resubmitted from someone else's work.
- AI-generated content is not permitted.
- Submissions must observe and respect the RSI Terms of Service.
- Each post counts as one entry, and you may submit multiple entries.
- Group submissions are not allowed. Only the person posting can win a prize.
- You may optionally include an original pirate persona and backstory in your post, which will be considered as part of the judging criteria.
- Submissions that don't follow these instructions will be disqualified. Make sure to read the full contest rules for details.
