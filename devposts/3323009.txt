## Edit: This AMA has officially concluded! We've got to get back to work now, but we had a great time hanging out and answering your questions. Thanks so much to everyone who participated!
Hello there, everyone!
Today's AMA is going to be a little different from AMAs past. Instead of focusing on one specific feature, we're going to be chatting about Environment Art in general! Feel free to ask both general questions about the craft and the industry, or specifics relating to their work on Star Citizen bringing the 'verse to life.
Here at CIG, our environment art team focuses on creating natural locations in the 'verse. From the large (planets & moons) to the small (details like the fields of flowers on microTech), this team does it all.
I'm opening up this thread a little early to begin collecting questions, but the team will hop in at 8:00 AM PDT (3:00 PM UTC) and begin answering! They'll be sticking around for about an hour to chat before they'll need to leave us and get back to work.
Just to give you a general idea on where to point your questions, here are some topics the team is excited to talk about today:
- Planet creation
- Planet tech
- Harvestables
- Mineables
- Caves
- Crusader & Orison
