@rabidCRABS, from our side we won't release TDDs for weapon or balance questions at this stage. We can think about it once the combat environment is a bit more complete and stable but until then any kind of official documentation would just invite a lot of scrutiny for which we don't have the time to respond to. Plus keeping documents up to date is an entirely different matter. It is quite common that players refer to statements we did years ago without acknowledging that the game has moved forward and had to change certain requirements ... so I'd like to avoid adding to that pile.
The current motivation for the changes is to give the missiles a more specific purpose and character, so we're trying out dedicated warheads to prepare for further gameplay like escaping, piracy, armor, etc. Combat development is a highly iterative process, so in the upcoming releases we'll have quite a lot of changes of that sorts.
