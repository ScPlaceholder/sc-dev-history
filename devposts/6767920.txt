DX11 will be deprecated once Vulkan meets or exceeds stability and performance on the vast majority of hardware (hopefully in the next 1 or 2 major releases). This is because DX11 doesn't support multi-threading or the latest hardware features and so doesn't support much of the tech we have planned or in active development (ray tracing, virtual textures, bindless rendering, etc).
DX12 is not planned at present as there's currently no clear advantage between either DX12 & Vulkan, and Vulkan is being continually updated to include the latest technology (we're using version 1.3 plus some extra 'extensions'). If the situation changes in the future (e.g. new APIs or versions come out) then we can re-assess. But the majority of the work for Gen12 and Vulkan wasn't specific to Vulkan, but was about supporting API's that are a much thinner abstraction from the GPU, and thus if we did want to move to another API the amount of work required would be vastly lower.
