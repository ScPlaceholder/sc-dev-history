Greetings,
We have brought the two remaining Vanguard variants to flight ready! As mentioned on Star Citizen Live, these ships don't have their full intended functionality yet. The AR cockpit or the e-war related items (decoy missiles and e-war pod) will be missing on the Sentinel, but it has been given an EMP generator to fill the void. The Harbinger's turret won't have retractable or reloading rockets at this initial release, but does have generic rocket pods. The nose arrays on all of the Vanguards are also now different with distortion repeaters on the Sentinel, ballistic repeaters on the Hoplite, and ballistic cannons on the Harbinger. These weapons can also be found in weapon shops around the Stanton system.
With all that in mind, we would love your feedback on these additions and their functionality in game. Let us know below!
