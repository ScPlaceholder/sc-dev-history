Heya, the topic of co-pilot seats and what those seats can actually do comes up quite often.
Right now we're somewhat limited what we can do as we have a static setup of which seat gets control over what functionalities at what point and there is no such thing as shared ownership. E.g. at any point there is only one seat that gets control over the flight controller (which is the entity that grants access to the thrusters, flight system, etc.) and we do not have a system in place that can purposefully swap control or parts of it over.
In the F7CM e.g. you can fly the ship perfectly normally from the back seat until the front seat is taken at which the control is handed over to the front seat (as the pilot seat has a higher priority over flight items). So right now, the architecture in that regard is a bit too rigid for purposeful (i.e. player controlled) dynamic swapping. The same accounts for systems like counter measures. Atm there is only player having authority over it but CMs would actually be a good case to allow control about for several players at the same time.
I absolutely agree with the use cases you listed (I'm personally a big fan of instructor two seaters, it's e.g. my favourite way to introduce players to VTOL VR which handles that case in a really nice way) and going even beyond that, I can imagine cases where you purposefully lock out functionalities out of seats via hacking or because your ship got boarded or so (a TNG "battle bridge" comes to mind). There is a lot of potential gameplay to be uncovered there.
So obviously we want to improve things long term but in the current list of things to achieve it sit's a bit lower in the priority list as it's a chunky piece of work. Hope that makes sense.
