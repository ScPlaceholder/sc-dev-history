The relative throttle is a lot more flexible as it allows you more (well infinite) "sticky" settings than just full forward. We actually adding a couple of helper bindings specifically for HOSAS which are related to that (not sure if they are already in 3.20, a couple of things accidently bled over). With dual stick you can set the absolute throttle axis to a value, press a button and your ship will use that throttle value as the new center point for the throttle axis. So you can set it to 50% and let go of the stick, your ship will hold 50% ... and then you get do a long press (or whatever is set up) and it turns itself off. That is a lot more convenient and controllable than trying to manage it with "cruise" plus a speed limiter axis. In all honesty cruise plus speed limiter I find is a very clunky way of controlling your ship's speed.
In general we've seen in internal playtesting that players using master modes plus the new internal controls neither use cruise mode nor the speed limiter and I see that as a good thing. So easily an argument can be made to whether we should try to get rid of these older concepts. I am growing to become very critical of necessary combination of cruise mode plus speed limiter as they are relatively complex interaction methods that serve as band aids due to high speeds. I do think we can find better ways to control speeds and that's what we're currently investigating.
Please keep an open mind about it ... while SC has a history there is a lot of stuff specifically in the control input and the way we control ships that can be made more accessible and improved on without losing fidelity.
