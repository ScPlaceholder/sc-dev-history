On the first point, I agree, that is probably a bit too close. Most people pick some balance between where they sit a little too far back but tolerate the distortion effects, though if you were doing some kind of sim-pit setup you'd probably want to balance the size and distance exactly.
On the second point though, big disagree! This distortion makes things towards the edges look bigger, while sitting close to the screen compresses your view of the edges. These both happen for the exact same reason, so at the right distance they'll cancel out.
