Hi, to clarify, we (at CIG) don't have any tile servers or any URLs to email you; as we have shown on ATVs etc. the planet surface is generated dynamically on demand as the player is visiting it. The way it works with the MyRadar app, is that during our collaboration with them I have made a custom tool which would take Gigabytes of screenshots (albedo and normal) by positioning the camera along a planet path and forcing planet generation at those camera locations. All the resulting screenshot data was handed over to the MyRadar app guys, and they would convert it to a GIS/tile format suitable for their app retrieval and storage on their servers.
