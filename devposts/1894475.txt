## UPDATE: This contest has ended. We're currently in the process of selecting the winnners and will announce them soon!
Lights, Camera, Action!
As shown countless times on the hub,  the Star Citizen community has a wealth of talent and passion that can deliver some incredible videos, highlighting the beauty of the ‘verse.
Today,  we’re sending out a call to all producers, editors, and camera  operators to create a commercial for the Tumbril Cyclone. Your commercial should focus on one variant from the Cyclone lineup  (other variants are permitted in your commercial, but the main focus  should be a single variant.) Whether it's off-roading on the beaches of Hurston, or flying over the craters on Daymar, we want you to get creative and show us what you've got!
You have until 11:59 PM Pacific on February 8th, 2019 to submit your Tumbril Cyclone commercial video for a chance to win one of the prizes listed below.
Haven’t seen our take yet? Check out the official trailer, released with Alpha 3.1.
Ready to create? Read up on the rules, then start rolling and post your finished video in the Spectrum submissions thread.
## Prizes
Three winners will be selected. If you work on the video with two friends (3 collaborators max), all will receive the reward.
:first_place_medal:1st Place – Standalone Constellation Andromeda w/ LTI
:second_place_medal:2nd Place – Standalone Freelancer MAX w/LTI
:third_place_medal:3rd Place – Standalone Reliant Mako w/ LTI
Please find the full set of rules here.
## Notes
- Video must not exceed 2 minutes
- Submissions must be at least in 1920x1080 resolution and uploaded to YouTube
- There is no requirement for which Cyclone variant to be used. Any Cyclone variant is permitted Your video does NOT need to feature all Cyclone variants to be able to participate in the contest.
- No more than 3 collaborators on a single video
- Submissions should clearly state all individuals involved in creating their commercial video at the time of submission
- No unoriginal music (Star Citizen music is OK)
Good luck everyone! We’re looking forward to seeing your Tumbril Cyclone commercials!
