Server Meshing is very much a research and development project and as such it's not possible to accurately estimate how long it will take. Chris's Letter From The Chairman carefully qualified the Q4 2023 date for Static Server Meshing as something we are aiming for, but not guaranteed. I recommend viewing it in that light, as a target we are trying to hit, intended to focus R&D effort, rather than an actual estimate of how much work is involved. My personal opinion is that this target, while ambitious, is not unreasonable and should allow enough room for us to hit some moderate speed bumps along the way. But if we encounter too many unexpected problems or enough of those problems can't be resolved quickly then it will obviously impact the final delivery date.
