Do you mean with ships in general, restricting the time thrusters can generate lift?  I definitely have.  The goal for awhile has been to move to a system where there are two kinds of thrusters, one that can generate a relatively low but sustained thrust level, and another than can generate a much higher but short duration thrust burst.  The first type would be used for something like holding a ship aloft in atmosphere while the second would be used for high speed maneuvers.  This way we aren't held to the unrealistic expectation that whatever maneuvering acceleration a ship supports is also the amount of VTOL thrust it can support.  This is why our ships, which are tuned for high speed space combat maneuvering, can generally counter gravity with such ease.  If a ship can maneuver at 15 G, staying aloft in 1 G isn't a challenge.  It can do so and still have 14 G for maneuvers.  So that's definitely the direction we're going.  That isn't in my current IFCS refactor, however.  I'm setting it up, but we're not there yet.
