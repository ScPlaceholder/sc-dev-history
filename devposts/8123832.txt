Vulkan allows us to spread the work over many more CPU cores, greatly benefitting multi core system. But the total amount of processing is quite similar. Nothing moves from CPU to GPU or vice versa from Vulkan itself, though there are some graphics techniques that can be implemented to move work from CPU to GPU, and some of these are future options for us… but a large chunk of the CPU costs you see in Star Citizen aren’t related to rendering, so this the holy grail.
