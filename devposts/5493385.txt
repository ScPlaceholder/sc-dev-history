Some changes of the gunnery stuff go in that direction. In general spread and individual aim assist values per weapon will play a bigger role and define important weapon characteristics. The UI is also prepared to show what I'd call precision markers ... a small indication around the PIP denoting the expected spread and best aim assist angle for that weapon. It basically means if you that your aim crosshair within these markers you should expect at least a couple of hits. There is actually a lot more to it and we'll talk about it when we're ready.
