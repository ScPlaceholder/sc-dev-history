Sorry, I just realized that this is indeed a separate topic. We actually had to remove the aim-only mouse mode in favor of streamlining the controls. What in fact happened is that this function caused a lot of problems with several other input axis. So instead we changed the setup slightly, it works as followed:
- During manual gimbal mode the pilot can select if the weapons gimbal towards the "VJoy position" or the "look direction" (aka center of screen)
- There are new keybinds to swap this "slaving" behavior (check in the bindings menu under Spaceship Weapons)
- There is also an option that automatically swaps that behaviour when you enter free look (z) (see general game options)
The default setup is that when you are in manual gimbal mode the weapons will follow the Vjoy ... however if you go into free look, your weapons will actually follow your head instead.  You actually have a much bigger angular range for you gimbals that way. The old "aim-only mouse mode" was restricted to a specific area in front your HUD ... whereas now you can use the whole range of the head movement. So you can effectively aim to the side and fire.
Hope that explains that change.
