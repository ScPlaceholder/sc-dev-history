In terms of allowing TET5 head tracking when the mix slider is all the way to the left you'd need to discuss that with Tobii as this related to how the hardware device outputs the data.You can either contact them directly or reach out to them via this thread here: https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/50174/thread/tobii-eye-tracker-feedback-2
In terms of jiggling the only difference between ships are the extend of which positional offsets in seats are allowed. This is to avoid clipping through geometry, etc. However we do not really use that much, only on a couple of remote turret seats (like in the Valkyrie) or in beds.
