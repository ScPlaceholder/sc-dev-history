It doesn't matter whose name is on the ticket to enter the LACC. So, if your friend has bought you a ticket and their name is on it, don't worry, you can still use it. It's all about having a good time and the Star Citizen community, so we don't let ticket names get in the way of that. :smiling_face_with_3_hearts:
However, if you have a VIP ticket, the name on it must match your ID to gain access to the LA office. If the name doesn't match, don't hesitate to contact our game support agents for further assistance.
