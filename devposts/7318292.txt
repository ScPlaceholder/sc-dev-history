regarding 4, it won't be cheeseable
Maximum distance is not that large (relative to ship speeds) so you have to keep in close to keep the worms attention, if there are no viable targets it will go back into the surface and return to full HP.
With ships being so fast we don't want projectiles to 'bend' too much because if anyone cops on it's bending at all it doesn't feel real so we want to keep ships within distance and have them perform strafing runs on the valakkar as opposed to just slowly moving horizontally as it keeps the action up, feels fun and tests you as a pilot.
But when we play it we're always 'how can we cheese this' because we know exactly that's the first thing people will do so it's designed with that in mind to make the most optimal way of playing against it also the most fun way
