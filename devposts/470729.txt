Karma is a rating system for the quality of your posts and replies. If you think an answer was helpful, upvote it and the author's Karma will increase. If you think it was useless: don’t (and maybe leave a Mr. Hankey emoji).
Why Karma? There are a couple of options this can be used further down the line. Maybe this is how we’ll pick our future mods. Or  maybe everyone with a Karma rating below XYZ can be flagged as a troll or a spammer... Generally, you’ll see how someone is viewed by other members of the community.
