It is impressive. I would say, if CIG implement an AI chat system it would be more complex than simply generating responses based on player input and pre-determined parameters. For example in that Bannerlord video. The NPC states that he's made weapons for a variety of people in and around his village. That works fine for that game but in StarCitizen if an NPC claimed they made something or killed someone or caused a similar impactful event then we would what that event to actually exist. For example: If an NPC claims they modified a ship then there should be a ship out there that's been modified that fits the AIs description. in Star Citizen the AI generated responses should fit with what has actually happened in the Lore. Another example is if an NPC claims they were in the UEE Navy, there should be a record of that somewhere.
With Star Citizen it quickly becomes more complex as we're trying to build a real, living breathing universe.
