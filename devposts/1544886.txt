This was something we were talking about just the other day actually! Currently, there isn't much difference in sound between being at a complete standstill and travelling at maximum velocity, which I guess is realistic, however, from a gameplay perspective I think we could do with some indication of speed when at the higher speeds. We do need to balance this against the risk of the sound becoming annoying/fatiguing on longer sub-quantum journeys though.
We do have the capability to 'dampen' the sound of the ship over longer journeys, as if the ship's systems and structures were 'settling' into the speed, and this is something we're experimenting with doing in the new system - VERY subtly, however.
Keep your ears peeled for what we bring out as a first iteration when you all get your hands on the new system :slightly_smiling_face:
VFX and ourselves use the same parameters from the IFCS to drive our respective systems, so there isn't any disparity there.
