Hello everyone!
We’re looking to bring more variety to our video lineup, with shows focused on the topics you want to see most.
We’ll soon be sharing a survey to hear directly from you about the kinds of shows you’d be interested in, whether that means bringing back an old favorite or introducing something entirely new. Keep an eye out for that soon.
In the meantime, we’re excited to introduce 10 From the Community, a new show debuting on October 5. The format is simple: short, focused, and straight to the point. Each episode, we’ll gather your questions and bring in the right people to answer them.
Every episode will feature a few developers representing a particular area of development, including gameplay, art, UI, technology, ships, and more.
For our first episode, we’re starting broad with the teams responsible for Star Citizen’s gameplay systems and content. We’ll talk about how those systems work today, how they connect, and how they’ll continue to grow on the road to 1.0.
Submit your questions below, and remember to vote for the ones you most want to see answered. We’ll see you October 5!
Guidelines
- Post only one question per comment.
- Only reply to this message. Do not engage with the other players' questions.
- Questions should be relevant to the topic of this episode.
- Try to be succinct. Boil it down to the thing you really want to know. It helps avoid confusion and begets better answers, usually.
- No "when" questions.
- Comments violating these guidelines or the subjects of the show may be deleted.
- Have fun with it!
