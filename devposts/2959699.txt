RyiXy'an is technically more correct, but both accent placements are correct!
And no, it hasn't! It's still uninhabitable. The catastrophe at the end of the Great Divide didn't just poison the atmosphere, it turned RyiXy'an into a smog world. Smog worlds resist terraforming. That's why Venus in 2950 is the same as Venus in 2020, and why the homeworld of the Xi'an hasn't been "repaired," though the Xi'an would very much like it to be. The capital of the Xi'an Empire is on Ka'ua (and the seat of the Imperial House), the fourth planet from their sun.
