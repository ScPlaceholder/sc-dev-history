There are certainly features like this in the Service/Military dialect. For example, if you already know that the order is going to be to "fire" then all you have to hear is the command "myā!" to know to do it. Even in the regular Proper language they like these efficiencies. Most of the raw ("elemental") parts of the language are only one or two syllables, and the Service/Military dialect is almost entirely based on pronunciation differences, so we've likely got that covered in general. But, hang in there and stand by for when we need military slang! :slightly_smiling_face:
