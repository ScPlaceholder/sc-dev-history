Hello!
We have done a major overhaul on ship personal inventory storage to accommodate the recent addition of ship components to inventory. These changes should allow you to move ship components and weapons to different inventory spaces, including into ships and transport them to other stations/cities. As part of this update, all ship components have been given new SCU sizes relative to their game size.
To better allow players to transport these, ships have had their personal inventory sizes increased, but still you may need larger ships or the help of someone with a larger ship to transport larger components. As a note, this is a temporary measure while we await the cargo refactor. Please test this out and let us know what you think!
Known Issue: You currently can not buy from kiosk direct into ship inventory, but this is intended.
