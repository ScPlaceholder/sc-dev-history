There isn't one person that looks at everything but rather we and other companies tend to have people that specialize in certain areas of the game and because of that, they are most interested in the data that is pertaining to the part of the game that they work on.
With that in mind, in order for someone to be able to answer the question of what is done with regards to research related to 'player types', we'd need to define what we mean when we use that phrase. Just off the top of my head, I can think of a few ( though keep in mind that I'm just being theoretical here and not actually listing things we track).
Preferred ship which could be used to extrapolate the types of roles they prefer ( support, dps, etc )
Preferred FPS weapons
Preferred game modes
Play style ( how they fly, which ship systems they use, are they aggressive or defensive, etc )
How they handle their money
Communication style
Organization rank(s)
In past jobs, I've seen customer service departments identify players using their communication styles and use these types to identify how best to communicate with someone in an effort to ensure that the conversation between the player and the support representative goes as well as possible. That's an example of how a player type can be used to help impact player experience that tends to be common across many different companies.
