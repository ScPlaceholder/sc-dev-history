Greetings Testers!
Mercenary missions, including "kill all" and "kill specific target" type missions now point to updated derelicts.  This includes several wrecked Caterpillars which have been updated with this gameplay in mind.  However, the gem of derelicts for this patch is "Whistler's Crypt" on Daymar, which we won't spoil and we will let you explore for yourselves!
Most of these will require rank 2 in corresponding reputations associated with those zones (Crusader, Hurston, etc.).  Gather up some rep and then go check these out! Let us know what you think.
