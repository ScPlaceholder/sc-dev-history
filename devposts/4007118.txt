Hello Citizens!
We have implemented the first iteration of Ship to Station docking for the 890 Jump and Hammerhead ships! This comes with new docking arms and levels for all space stations. This uses the same interface as the ship to ship docking to align the docking collar of the ship with the docking arm of the station so that you can manually align and dock or use the automated system.
Please give the following a try and let us know what you think:
- ASOP spawning at Stations
- Spawn queue for docking
- Request docking
- Storing a docked ship
- Automatic and Manual docking
- New Docking levels and docking arms
Thanks for your input!
