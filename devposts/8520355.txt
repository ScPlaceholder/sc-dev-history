Greetings!
We're extremely excited to introduce an early experimental implementation of VR into 4.5.0. This includes the ability to use VR anywhere, anytime, from the main menu, to FPS gameplay, and of course flying your ship and everything in between!  This also includes VR Theater mode, which allows you to toggle between full VR and Theater Mode at any time, and VR Dynamic Switch which switches between VR and Desktop mode automatically when putting on and taking off your VR headset.
Alongside this feedback thread we have released another thread containing the key features that can be found with this inclusion. We heavily recommend reading through this before jumping into VR, which can be found at: Star Citizen VR Support - Experimental Release
We look forward to reading your feedback!
