We have released new mining gadgets, which modify a rock's properties to assist in mining a deposit. Mining gadgets are available in New Babbage, Lorville, and Port Olisar and can be equipped to a special utility slot on backpacks then manually placed onto a mineable rock. For specifics on operation, please consult the patch notes.
Give gadgets a try! Keep these specific questions in mind:
- Was the place/retrieve time worth the added benefits?
- Is this competitive or better than other mining enhancement items?
- Do you find that the most lucrative mineables need or benefit from using these gadgets?
