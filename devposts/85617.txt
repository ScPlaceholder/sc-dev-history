If there was a gun fight on your ship, will there be bullet holes or  burn marks on interiors? Will these persist and need to be repaired?
At the moment there won't be any real noticeable effect on the interior because like Ben mentioned we don't have damage map tech for the interior. We've started to use wear and dirt shader tech for certain things which is a possibility, but hasn't been implemented for ship interior. With physicalized component you'd see increased wear and dirt on things like the power plant, and the wear level would be persisted. but If you're asking if individual bullet holes will be persisted, for 3.0 the answer is likely not.
