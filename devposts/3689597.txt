The history books are filled with accounts from the Tevarin wars of the effective attack methods and unique formations employed by Tevarin pilots. The Talon was the linchpin of these surgically precise strikes, maximizing each attacker’s strength as part of a greater combined assault.
Now, with the Talon and the Shrike finally flight-ready in Alpha 3.12, we're asking you to provide us with appropriate pictures for the history books.
Welcome to the Talon Screenshot Contest!
We’re asking you to take an iconic in-game screenshot of the Esperia Talon or the Esperia Shrike.
The three participants who most impressively stage the talon will win a unique prize.

- You may submit multiple screenshots.
