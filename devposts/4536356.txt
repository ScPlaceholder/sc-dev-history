We have opened this thread as part of the post-quarterly release feedback assessment. This particular thread is devoted to Arena Commander and Broken Moon Revamp!
3.15 comes with some much needed changes to Arena Commander and Star Marine that should make it a more enjoyable experience! Scoreboards in Arena Commander and Star Marine have been converted to work with Building Blocks with a new interface that will give players real-time access to position, name, rank, score, objectives, kills, deaths, assists, and ping. We also updated Arena Commander's Broken Moon map has with a greatly increased overall size, new playable areas, more cover options, and new space assets.
Be sure to upvote shared thoughts. We absolutely appreciate you taking the time to give feedback and voice your concerns!
