Speaking in general terms (as opposed to specific ladder/camera animations and such for this project)
Animation transitions will keep getting polished. It's generally a blending between Animations and Programming. We always try to make sure the blend won't be too brutal, but sometimes that sort of polishing takes place in later stages. But if a player is looking off to the left as he approaches a ladder, we may be forced to swing his head around.
Just getting the character to recognize the ladder and use it somewhat properly is a good start, and then we'll handle polishing. There are multiple ways to handle these things; sometimes proximity triggers, which then take control away form the player; sometimes the player gets to keep some control while it's happening.
Camera placement can sometimes have a few issues that will get sorted out at a later time. Sometimes a change in helmet design could offset camera or HUD placement. But the important thing is to get the underlying system working, and then refine it through testing.
The short answer is we see this stuff too, and over time we try to make these transitions as smooth as we can. Every game has these camera blend issues.
