While the ideal way to do this for a full moon would be to place an appropriately sized spherical light over the moon, the problem becomes harder when you're trying to get light from a crescent moon - like most lighting engines, we're restricted to a fairly limited set of primitive shapes for light sources.
The most consistent result turns out to be the same thing we're using for all the other bounced light in the scene - the moon is visible in the environment probe image, so objects will receive some light from it (though you won't get the long glittering moon-over-the-ocean effect, which is a shame).
