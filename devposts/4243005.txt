## Update:
## The contest's submission period is now over. Thank you all for participating and sharing your works of art with us. We'll announce the winners soon.
We’re excited to welcome the long-awaited RSI Constellations Taurus to the ‘verse with our latest patch, Alpha 3.14. Completing the Constellation series, the Taurus features potent defensive weaponry, impressive storage capacity, and a concealed cargo hold for discreetly delivering “valuable goods.”
To celebrate the release of the latest Connie, we’re inviting you to join us for our Constellation Coloring Contest!
To participate, download and print the contest sheet, color it, take a picture and submit it via Spectrum or social media using the hashtags #StarCitizen and #MyConstellation. Don’t forget to put your Star Citizen handle on the image!
To color your Taurus, you can use any physical medium, including crayon, charcoal, paint, or watercolor. Don’t forget that, for this contest, we won’t accept any computer-generated artwork.
Please submit your entries by 11:59 PM Pacific, August 20, 2021, and show us the Constellation colors of your dreams!
Quick notes:
- Before submitting an entry, please read the full contest rules
- You must use the template provided
- Post your art in the submission thread or on Twitter/Instagram using the hashtags #StarCitizen #MyConstellation
- No computer-generated artwork will be accepted - entries must be colored using a physical artistic medium (crayons, paint, markers, etc.)
- Multiple entries per person are allowed
But now to the juicy details… here are the rewards that can be yours soon!
Rewards

- :second_place_medal:2nd Place: RSI Constellation Taurus & RSI Ursa Rover in-game vehicles with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance)
- :third_place_medal:3rd Place: RSI Constellation Taurus & Tumbril Cyclone in-game vehicles with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance)
