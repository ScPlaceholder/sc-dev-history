Just to clarify, Interdiction was not turned off when 3.2.0 went live. However, it would be understandable to believe that was the case given that we significantly reduced the frequency at which Interdictions can occur.
In 3.1.x, it was not uncommon to be interdicted 3 times in a row going to/from Delamar. In 3.2.0, it is now actually quite common to go to/from Delamar without being interdicted at all. If you are interdicted, you're likely to only be interdicted once during that trip. HOWEVER, that does NOT mean you will only get interdicted once.
We've also significantly reduced the time that it takes for an Interdiction/Conventional encounter to be cleaned up by the server since 3.1.x, though we still have plans to improve this further.
In regards to mass AI swarms being generated by Interdictions/Conventional encounters, this should no longer be the case, and if you have recent video of this occurring in 3.2.0, we'd be very interested. However, since 3.1.x, we've significantly reduced the number of AI that can be generated by a single encounter. We've also put a system in place that prevents multiple encounters from being generated within the same area (which was the cause of the mass AI were spawns near Port Olisar early in 3.1.x).
Is it possible there's still an issue? Of course. But at this point, we need to monitor the servers, and gather internal performance data to know for sure.
