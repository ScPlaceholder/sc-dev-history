There are options in the game to turn off the VJoy ... but the Vjoy itself is just the line that extends from the middle to the crosshair.
If it feels annoying during mining try to switch to the "relative mouse mode" in a turret seat, press Q for that. That way the the VJoy (which is really just a mouse based joystick simulation) will be removed and the turret should only move when you actually move the mouse.
If you get similar problems in pilot/driver seats you can press RALT-G which will lock your gimbals in place. That useful if you e.g. want to look around while keeping your gimbals focused on a specific spot.
