Ahoy, me hearties!
Avast ye! Gather 'round, ye scallywags and buccaneers, for I've got news as exciting as a chest full of booty. Aye, 'tis that time of year again - it be Pirate Week!
"Deck yer hulls with pirate colors bright,
Take on the Pirate Swarm and test your might!
Or defeat yer foes, and their ships tear asunder,
And in this contest, show us your plunder.
Assemble yer crew, don't forget some gunners,
Liberate supplies and triumph in Blockade Runner.
There be plenty to do and treasures to seek,
Grow your legend and wealth this Pirate Week!"
But that ain't all, me hearties! This year, we be addin' a grand treasure map hunt to the festivities! Ye heard it right! Bring out yer finest spyglasses and gather yer wits, for the clues be clever, and the challenges be fierce. X doesn't always mark the spot, and ye'll be scourin' every nook and cranny of the 'verse to solve the riddles. But for the swashbucklers who prevail, be rewarded with a glitterin' doubloon worthy of any collector's shelf.
So, me hearties, let the plunderin' and carousin' begin! The treasure be awaitin', and the adventures be endless. Set yer course for Pirate Week, and may the grog always be in yer tankard!
