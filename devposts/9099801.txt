Greetings!
Siege of Orison returns as our first Instancing on Demand mission. The CDF is recruiting players to break the Nine Tails' grip on the Orison Inspiration Park Cluster, and this time you will do it inside a closed instance locked to you and your party, up to four players. A one-time introduction mission points you to the instance elevators near the Crusader showroom in Orison, and from then on the main mission accepts itself as you arrive.
A feedback survey is available at: Siege of Orison Survey Form. This survey will be open until Sep 30.
We look forward to reading your feedback!
