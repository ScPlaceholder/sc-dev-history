In the context of the quote,  the word "state" refers to the networked and persisted properties of entities, e.g. position, health, ammo count, fuel remaining, etc. Only one client or server can have authority to change any particular piece of state at a time. Those changes are sent to the Replication Layer and then sent out to the other servers and clients that have the entity streamed in. I think the point CR was trying to make is that the RL isn't running at any particular frame/tick rate - it just sends out the changes as soon as it has received them.
Deciding when and where to create new entities is still under server control so won't be any more immersion breaking than it is now, e.g. ships spawning on landing pads, or loadout changes via the inventory appearing on players. Ultimately  decisions about what is acceptable rest with the designers and aren't limited by the tech.
