We challenged you to support your favorite contenders in Phase 1, and your response has blown us away! Through countless creative submissions, as well as your dedicated in-game flight and drive time, your choices have determined which ships will go head-to-head in the Live Showdown. Now that the dust has settled, your top 16 vehicles have emerged, with each battling to represent one of eight divisions. While the competition has already been fierce, things only become more heated as we surge into Phase 2 of Ship Showdown 2955.
Old rivalries will be rekindled. Daring upstarts will square off against battle-hardened legends. For each division, two ships enter, one ship leaves — and only you can decide the outcome. The following matchups will determine who moves on to the Elite Eight, with one ship or vehicle ultimately carving a path to be crowned this year's champion. Choose wisely, as in celebration of their victories, your Elite Eight will all be available to fly for FREE during the remainder of Ship Showdown!
