You can always slave you gimbals to your look direction. That does not work just on the tanks but on ships as well. The requirements for that is that your weapons are manual gimbal mode. If the guns are not tracking your head  you can press LALT-G to make the gimbals slaving swap between the rotation inputs/VJoy and the head look direction. The swap is automatically executed when you enter freelook but there are ways in the options to set it up the way you prefer.
Other than that the remote turret view was not really created to be used with head inputs. We could probably find ways to slave it to the head but the main advantages of the view (being a gyro stabilized, precise and manual turret control) would get lost by that.
