A quick clarification regarding the Anvil Liberator:
- Repair, Refuel, and Rearm (RRR): Currently in Alpha 4.10.3 the repair, refuel and rearm feature is limited to internal garages as a mitigation of current exploits, but given player feedback we are fast-tracking a potential solve in place for 5.0 that will enable RRR for all available landing pads and garages on the Liberator, as soon as possible.
- Pledge Store Classification: We've also identified an issue where the Liberator is incorrectly listed as "Dropship / Dropship" on the Pledge Store. The Liberator is classified as a Light Carrier, and we're working to correct this listing as soon as possible.
Thanks for your patience, we'll keep you posted as things progress :+1:
