When I talk about heavy fighters or medium fighters I do not necessarily mean the market information about that specific ship. Internally we classify all ships into tuning archetypes and the Cutlass (due to the manned turret) in terms of weapon balance, HP and capacitor sizing sits mostly in the heavy fighter category. However due to the lack of a second shield generator in reality it is in fact a bit closer to the medium fighter archetype ... so for in terms of balance it sits somewhere between. It might not be the best example, I just chose it to make the turret difference to the Hurricane clear (2x S3 vs 4x S3).
