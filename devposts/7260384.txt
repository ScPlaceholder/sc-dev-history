Jira is pretty standard across the industry these days, depending on the requirements of a specific team however we'll use a range of tools including Trello, Excel etc.
Personally I'm a big fan of a whiteboard and some pens :slightly_smiling_face:
More important than the tools however are the methodologies, most teams at CIG (and indeed every studio I can think of) practice either SCRUM or Kanban to some degree so it's worth looking into those if you're considering a career in the industry.
