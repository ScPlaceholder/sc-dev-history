Welcome to the Trick-or-Treat Video Contest!
We're asking you to create a spooky video/machinima showing a trick-or-treat encounter (and its aftermath) in the 'verse. Needless to say, we're celebrating the Day of the Vara and are in a horrifyingly spooky mood!
[https://robertsspaceindustries.com/galactapedia/article/RkGQqpJwGp-day-of-the-vara]
:warning: Before you start creating, please read the contest rules carefully [https://robertsspaceindustries.com/trick-or-treat-video-contest] and only submit your entries to either:
- The contest's submission thread [https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/3/thread/trick-or-treat-video-contest-submission-thread]
- Twitter, using the hashtags #StarCitizen and #DayOfTheVara2952
Any submissions not posted to one of these locations will not be accepted.
## Keep In Mind
- Read the contest rules [https://robertsspaceindustries.com/trick-or-treat-video-contest]
- Must not include any third-party music, effects, sounds, soundtrack, or other third-party content
- Visual post-processing effects ARE allowed. This includes third-party add-ons, like ReShade
- Video must have a total running time of no more than 180 seconds, including any preamble or postscript
- Must be in at least 1920×1080 resolution
- Must include the 'made-by-the-community' logo at the beginning or end for at least three seconds [https://robertsspaceindustries.com/fankit]
- Must be uploaded to YouTube and named: 'Star Citizen – Vara’s Trick Or Treat'
- The link to the YouTube video must either be posted to the contest's submission thread (above) or to Twitter, using the hashtags #StarCitizen and #DayOfTheVara2952
- Submissions should clearly state the handles of all those involved in creating the video at time of submission
- Videos must not include or require reference to, or incorporate by reference, any other video, submission, contest entry, or other external content
- Multiple submissions by or involving a participant are NOT permitted
- May have a maximum of five contributors or collaborators
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your videos on:
- Level of Excitement/Horror
- Quality
- Creativity
- Rule Adherence
## Dates
The submission period ends on October 27, 2022 (EOD 11:59 PM Pacific)
