The visible PIPs (one for the gimballed and one for fixed convergence group) are currently based on the fastest slowest (edit here, thanks, @Greedy_Raider) projectile velocity within each group. The slower faster guns will still try to hit their own internal PIP as best as they can if the barrel is within the nudging freedom. We are aware of the issues that this brings and I do not think we have reached an ideal solution with this yet ... but this is how it works at the moment.
Please keep in mind that projectile velocities are not final at all. You can expect a lot of changes in the future but we'll likely approach this only after certain flight tuning changes have happened as projectile velocities and ship tunings are in very strongly tied together.
EDIT: Slight change of my statement above after I was corrected by some members of our awesome community! :heart:
