we are not planning to add any calibration tools or hotkeys to recenter input devices. We rely on Windows for telling us what devices are there and what they put out in terms of data ... as this has been the standard for decades.
That said I do understand your problem, I also went through two sets of TFRP rudders within a couple of years. Generally higher priced ones are usually more rugged and that is really across all manufacturers. So you could maybe check if there are any tools like Joystick Gremlin or so that might offer some reset functionality. But in general, if the hardware is defect, then it's defect ... so you have to either get it repaired or replaced. Or repair it yourself if the warranty has run out. There is a lot of machinery around that really just needs a bit of a clean-up at times. And pedals will accumulate quite a bit of dust over time.
