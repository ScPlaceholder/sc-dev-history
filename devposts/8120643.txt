It's that time of year again! The ultimate clash of the freighters and fighters, the tiny and the giant, newcomers and classics, has begun. It's Ship Showdown! Which ships will fight their way to the finals and earn a spot in this year's Intergalactic Aerospace Expo's Best in Show? That's for YOU to decide.
And while you rally behind your favorite, you could become a champion yourself and win a brand-new RSI Meteor. Read on to find out how!
## Ship Showdown 2955 Phase 1 Has Begun!
Cheer on your favorite ships in the 'verse, because they'll need every bit of support to reach Phase 2 and claim a spot in the Top 16 tournament bracket. Create an original artistic creation and upload it to the Community Hub or X (Twitter) with the hashtags #ShipShowdown and #StarCitizen.
Write and perform an original song, build something out of any material, capture breathtaking in-game machinima, create a trailer, sketch, paint, sculpt, or even craft the ship for real! There are no limits, just make sure it's your original work and showcases your favorite ship.
## Keep In Mind
A Contest Entry submission may be any type of creative work, including, but not limited to, art, music, writing, or any type of material so long as it:
- Celebrates or highlights a Star Citizen ship or vehicle eligible for Ship Showdown at the time of the contest
- Is original and created by the entrant
- Does not include AI-generated content, which will not be considered
- Observes and respects the RSI Terms
- Is submitted to either submission venue
If submitted on X (Twitter), the post must contain the hashtags #StarCitizen and #ShipShowdown. All images must include the following image embedded in the content at a size where the text is legible: https://media.robertsspaceindustries.com/awe61poy5rx2m/source.png
For full contest rules and to see some of the amazing submissions so far, please head over to the official Ship Showdown page.
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your submissions on:
- Rule adherence
- Quality
- Creativity
- Aesthetic composition
## Dates
The submission period will end on Tuesday, 12 August 2025, 20:00 UTC.
## Prizes
Each of the following submissions that meet all entry requirements and are selected by the judges will receive an an in-game RSI Meteor with Lifetime Insurance:
- 10 submissions on the Community Hub
- 5 submissions on X (Twitter)
Let the battles commence!
