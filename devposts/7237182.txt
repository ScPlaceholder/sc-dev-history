That's your starting point then you move onto 'vibes based balance' as Josh sawyer calls it.
Your starting point is based in data then where you end up is shaped by internal playtests
Making everything 'balanced' just leads to things being the same and 'feeling' the same.
If things are broken you adjust down obviously but you want things to feel good
You need things to stick out and be best in class at something as well as having things behave substantially different, If you took the raw data then the CODA would 4 shot because that's 'balanced' but it didn't feel fun to use to we upped it to 3 shot and in theory it should break everything but it doesn't, behold! Vibes based.
The whole industry is built this way and it's a much healthier way to build the game
But of course all of this information is tracked in my monster spreadsheet but if you balance by Excel you'll have a very boring game
