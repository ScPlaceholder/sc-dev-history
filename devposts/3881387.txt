Took some time to look into this today and the logs look a bit weird. The account look like it's being disconnected due to duplicate logins rather frequently. There's a normal expected order of operations for those being something along the lines of "log in, get disconnected, log back in, game sees the character is still online and kicks it out". Kicking the online character is to ensure that the account is able to log back in should the character not be disconnected properly after something goes wrong and this is something that's pretty normal for an mmo to do. However, the logs that I'm seeing for this imply that the same computer that is already logged into the game is trying to reconnect to it without the game client having ever closed and then it gets kicked off due to it being online already.
I'm not totally sure if my interpretation of what's going on in the logs is correct, but I've made a bug report for this for it to get looked at. With that said, if I'm right about this being a bug, it's pretty rare, as the duplicate login kick error isn't something I see happen all that often.  There may be something that is causing connectivity problems with the game client which in turn, may have exposed a bug with the duplicate login process.
