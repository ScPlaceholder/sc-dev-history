All our recent updates is to fix all the missing data and restore what we already had. Fixes to the formulas which determines the "Rating" is not part of this and will not happen until we do a full refactor of our Leaderboard service sometime in the future. We're aware the rating calculation is both not initiative and actually just broken in a number of ways. I do not think we will ever reveal the calculations behind these but I can say its a great number of things go into it; including infractions (ie early leaver punishment, friendly fire punishment etc) which can certainly contribute to some of the weirdness you're seeing (where say someone with way more time/score-per-minute could appear below someone with less).
So... with that context, finally getting to your question :smile:, for the Org ratings, it does the same(ish) calculations but then throws in various Org statistics such as player count to determine where that Org places as a-whole rather than individual level. In terms of racing I highly suspect the same formula is being applied here, rather than using the Fastest Race like the "Players" tab does. In the near future we'll be expanding upon scoring in racing which will make this make more sense.
