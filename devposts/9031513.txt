Greetings!
Ships now provide visual and audio feedback on your reticle each time a shot connects, telling you exactly what your weapons are doing to a target in real time. Previously, ship combat provided no meaningful feedback on how shots were having an effect. Hit markers now change state based on the result of each shot, with a distinct audio cue for each outcome.
A feedback survey is available at: Vehicle Armor Hitmarkers Survey Form. This survey will be open until Aug 31.
We look forward to reading your feedback!
