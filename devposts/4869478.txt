I actually don't have an exact date. Balance itself is a bit weird because it is more "maintenance" than feature work for us as it is always influenced by the feedback and data of the current live release.
While we often put balance time into our work sprints, "balance" itself is not a single step that can be executed in a quarter. Just on the components e.g. stealth tuning is different from distortion tuning or general performance or health tuning. Rebalancing also happens when new subsystems come online ... e.g. we know already that we need to rebalance quite a few weapons once the physicalized armor system comes online. We also know that we need to adapt flight tunings when the high speed combat changes come. So there is not really a single "balance" pass, it is a multitude of balance passes for a multitude of systems, some of them isolated, some of them not.
So "some" balancing has recently happened and chances are good that you will see some changes in 3.17 but it certainly won't be the big balance Jesus patch that you might hope for. Especially on weapons we will stay a bit longer in the Archetype tuning state as we're not yet happy with the capacitor setup and the weapon scaling.
