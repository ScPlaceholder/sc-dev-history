Whether it’s the Banu, Tevarin, Xi´an, or Vanduul, before you power on your engines and start your adventure in the ‘verse, it’s a good idea to familiarize yourself with the various spacefaring alien races out there. It’s important to know whether they’re friendly, could impale you with a deadly lance, or if you should outrun them if things get heated!
In this contest for Alien Week, we want you to create alien ‘trading cards’ (you might also know them as Top Trump cards).
Fire up an image-editing program and use one of the templates provided, insert the name of your alien, an image, info text, and some base stats of your alien. Your trading cards aren't limited to Star Citizen alien races, so let your creative juices flow!
The Community Team will vote for the most imaginative, well-executed, and funny entries. After the event has ended, we'll publish the best community-created cards in a gallery.
Ready to get started? Find all the details here.
Quick Rules
- Use one of the templates provided
- Create an alien trading card, including name, short info text, image, and base stats (apart from Height and Weight please choose between 1 to 100)
- Post your entry to the Spectrum submission thread or on Twitter/Instagram using hashtag #StarCitizen and #AlienWeek
- Each card is considered a single entry, but you are allowed multiple entries (you can include multiple trading cards in a single post)
- Cards aren't limited to known Star Citizen alien races
- We don't want to limit your creativity, but please only post alien-related cards. Any other subjects will be disqualified
Prizes
- 1st Place: Standalone Banu Defender in-game ship with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance)
- 2nd Place: Standalone Aopoa Khartu-al in-game ship with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance)
- 3rd Place: Standalone Esperia Talon in-game ship with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance)
Contest ends: June 21, 2021 (11:59 PM Pacific)
We are looking forward to seeing your creations and wish you a wonderful Alien Week!
