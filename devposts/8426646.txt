A new Ballistic Missile Launcher has been added in 4.4.0, the "Boomtube". This launcher consists of three rockets tapped together in a tube releasing this single deadly payload. The Design intent with this weapon is a high power explosive with a singular magazine, finding the sweet spot between being close enough to deal with the weapons inaccuracies but not close enough to trigger a dud (arming distance).
We look forward to hearing your thoughts, and thanks for taking the time!
