In Arena Commander you can earn REC (Rental Equipment Credits) from Online Matches, currently many game modes have a REC Bonus active to allow players to easily achieve enough to rent any light fighter after a match or two.
REC is used via the Arena Commander Frontend, select any flight mode and then select "Vehicle Selection", from there scroll down to see the selection of vehicles to rent. Once you have picked your vehicle, go to the customization page where you can again rent any equipment available in the game. Rentals last for the entire patch.
If you've only got a Starter Ship I recommend using it in any Swarm Mode, these are a great way to get better and earn REC. Alternatively, FPS modes provide free equipment and personally I find easier to earn larger scores. Currently your Score is how much REC you will learn (+ the bonus multiplier if the mode has a bonus active)
