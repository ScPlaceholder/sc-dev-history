Hi everyone,
A People's Alliance pilot has gone missing during a recon run to a station that should have been empty. InterSec Defense Solutions is calling for a coordinated strike to bring the pilot home, and the clock is already running. The station is running Vanduul tech and one wrong move could draw unwanted attention and drag your team into something far larger than a rescue mission.
Every specialist you can bring improves the odds. Fighters to clear a path, bombers to silence defensive turrets, dropships to deliver troops, and a capital ship to keep everyone armed and fueled. Assemble your strike force, deploy to Nyx, punch through the superstructure, take out the reactor, and pull off the rescue.
Beyond Tactical Strike Groups, Alpha 4.8 brings more tools built for the realities of large-scale combat and long-haul spacefaring. Select ships can now refuel, repair, and rearm vehicles directly from the flight deck, drawing real resources from the cargo grid. Refueling missions are now live for pilots ready to answer the call, keeping others moving across the vast stretches of the 'verse.
Star Citizen Alpha 4.8: Tactical Strike is now available.
