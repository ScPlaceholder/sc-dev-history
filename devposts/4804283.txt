@Alvatore, as for 3.16 the Ion weapon will stay as it is. Not because we think it's the best we can do, but because we could not spend a lot of time with it and had to be sure that it doesn't break the intended game balance. The first release of the Ion was an offender in that regard ... it was extremely strong in the dogfighting regime and also was an often-used tool for grievers as well ... and that is certainly not something we aimed for the Ares to be. That's why we pulled back some changes after the initial release (no one shot kills but retain the same DPS) and reduced the aiming helpers (no bullet nudging).
But that is just a temporary measure and certainly does not mean it will stay this way. Before XMas we experimented on how to give the Ion it's "cannon feeling" back but without making it so effective in dogfighting against small ships (it is a platform against larger ships after all). Needless to say that this work is not done yet but we also cannot give you a date or version yet when this will be changed. But it won't be for the 3.16 cycle.
