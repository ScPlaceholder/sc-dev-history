Okay slight change of plans, the solution we've worked on yesterday night and today is this:
- We are adding a new binding called "Lock Rotation" to the flight controls and default bind it to rshift
- The binding will suppress any rotation inputs you are commanding to the ship
- The binding will reset all rotation input axes but keeps the VJoy / manual gimbal reticle intact
- The binding is controller agnostic (means it also applies and can be bound to sticks and gamepads)
- That also means the default binding for "relative mouse mode" is removed again (so you have to bind it yourself)
That should give you almost the same experience as before. Not sure when this change goes in, it is still in review and I do not know the cutoff time for the next build. I will post the CL once it is committed.
