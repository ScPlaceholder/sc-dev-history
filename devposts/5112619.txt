Hello everyone,
As you may have noticed, there have been discrepancies in the amount of aUEC in wallets of some players. A series of services fixes will be deployed starting at 1600 UTC on 2022-05-27 to address this issue. In-game transactions will time out or fail for ~60 minutes while this fix is rolled out. After completion, players will likely see their aUEC values deviate from the current displayed total. This deviation is expected due to the number of services issues present since 3.17.1 went live. However, values should be significantly more stable after the deployment of this hotfix.
Thank you for all of your contributions to the Issue Council (STARC-36516)! This helped us identify the issue more quickly, and in turn helped get these services back online with expected behavior.
Thank all of you for your patience, and see you in the 'verse!
