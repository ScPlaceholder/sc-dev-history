- During the IAE 2950 you can only get your hands on one (1) of each stock-limited ship.
- As there will be three waves, you'll have three opportunities to add a total maximum of one ship of each stock-limited ship promotion to your account.
- If you already have a specific stock-limited ship on your account before the IAE 2950 promotion, you are still able to receive another of this particular stock-limited ship during one of its three waves.
