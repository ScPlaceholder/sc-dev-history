Hello everyone!
Every two weeks, we accompany the Roadmap update with a brief explanatory note to give you insight into the decision-making that led to any changes. This is part of an effort to make our communications more transparent, more specific, and more insightful for all of you who help to make Star Citizen and Squadron 42 possible.
Just a reminder: The 1.0 column showcases features and content planned for inclusion in or before Star Citizen 1.0. This means you may not have to wait for the 1.0 release to experience them. As features/content in the 1.0 column become ready, they will migrate left into a point patch column for release.
Be sure to check back every two weeks for the latest updates.
-CIG Community Team
## Release View
## Alpha 4.10
RSI Galaxy
Implementing the RSI Galaxy, a jack of all trades redefining the depth of modularity as only RSI can. The next-generation chassis is built around a configurable midship space that transforms into a heavily defended long-haul freighter or mobile field hospital.
:scroll: This card has been set to Released.
Anvil Liberator
Implementing the Anvil Liberator, a transporter and light carrier allowing you to load the decks with fighters, miners, salvagers, or whatever’s needed for your daily operations. The Liberator handles long-range jumps, so your ships reserve their supplies and always arrive ready to get to work.
:scroll: This card has been set to Released.
Drake Marauder
Implementing the Drake Marauder, a medium fighter that packs a punch, putting all its seven size 3 weapons under one pilot’s thumb, ready to blast.
:scroll: This card has been set to Released.
Gatac Hyūn
Implementing the Gatac Hyūn, the alien manufacturer's first combined mining and salvage ship.
:scroll: This card has been set to Committed, and will be set to Released once available in the PU.
Lightning Bolt Company "Skarnov" DMR (4.10.3)
⁠Implementing the Lightning Bolt Company "Skarnov" DMR, a fully automatic designated marksman rifle whose specialized projectiles create electrical discharges between multiple targets, making it highly effective against groups of hostiles.
:scroll: This card has been set to Released.
InterSec Defense Solutions Mission Pack (4.10.3)
Implementing an expansion of InterSec Defense Solutions' contracts in Nyx, introducing new missions and gameplay opportunities centered on fighting the growing Vanduul threat, alongside new blueprints for players to earn.
:scroll: This card has been set to Released.
