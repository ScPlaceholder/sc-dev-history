in 3.23 we're introducing a different approach to FM tuning and this means that the FM of all spaceships has changed. Ships are now in a better relationship to each other and that includes racers. However none of the existing racing tracks have been built (or picked by the community) with those new FM properties in mind ... therefore there is a good chance that they will not work well right now.
The level design teams dealing with racing tracks are aware of that and they'll come up with tweaks over time. But it is still a bit early, we're still tuning and we'll likely take a good chunk of the Q2 time to iterate on details specifically for racing ships.
