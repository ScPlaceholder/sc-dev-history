Greetings!
A new armor class and the weapon it was built around have arrived in the PU along with Siege of Orison. The BUL-H4 set is a single one-piece super heavy armor along with a BUL-H4 Helmet, and the H4-PBF Ammo Carrier backpack. This new armor trades mobility for defense and the ability to carry the heaviest weapon a player can yield.
That new weapon is Apocalypse Arms' Vendetta heavy machine gun. The Vendetta works together with the BUL-H4 armor and backpack to carry large amounts of munitions. The backpack is only capable of storing the new ammo munitions for the weapon. These can be reloaded by another player in your party by tractor beaming or hand placing a new one into the backpack. Both the armor and the weapon can be obtained in game through Wikelo exchange rewards and through Siege of Orison.
A feedback survey is available at: BUL-H4 Super Heavy Armor & Vendetta HMG Survey Form. This survey will be open until Sep 30.
