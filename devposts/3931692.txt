We're always looking for new and effective ways to share information, especially since not every developer is comfortable or has interest in engaging, and that's okay. There's always creative ways to gather info and it comes in many forms. ISC, SCL, CAD, AMAs (that's a lot of acronyms), Patch Watch, Monthly Reports, and others are all examples of our team pushing for more info to share with the community. The AMAs, Patch Watch, and having more staff on hand in chat during events/releases are all more recent additions to try and include more text-based comms. More recently we've had our eye on refreshing the Ask A Dev section to breathe some life into it. We've attempted to structure process/scheduling around it a few times, but it ended up not being sustainable. We have a few new ideas we're exploring now though. We're constantly looking for ways to maximize informational output, while minimizing development disruption. As Calix alludes, it can definitely be a challenge, but one we enjoy working to solve.
