Just to be clear, we're rolling out new BASIC and ADVANCED control schemes for pretty much all input devices.
Basic will now be the default but Advanced (set it in Options) is pretty much what everyone had before with all the modifiers for the extra functions.
We had found a lot of players, especially newer ones were completely overwhelmed with the controls and often hit buttons without any realisation of what they had done. We'll be working more on this in the future to make the whole keybinding/controls setup clearer too.
