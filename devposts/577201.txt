Some of them spend 100 years perfecting their UEE Standard and speak it very well. Others struggle especially with some of our consonant clusters and sounds that come at the end of words. Between the Proper Language and the Service/Military Dialect they can say MOST sound that we have in our language, but they can't deal perfectly in all situations with all the combinations that we put together, so often there are little epenthetic vowels put in on some consonants pronounced as other. For example, it's VERY difficult for most them to voice TH. They cannot say "the" or "thee" properly in most cases. When they say it it sounds like "thief" with the F dropped off. Great question!
