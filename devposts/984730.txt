## The Issue
After an Insurance Claim, you continue to have duplicate ships appear in your ship list with the "Name of Ship - #". Current reports indicate the following:
- The original ship can be used/insurance claimed, duplicates never work
- The original ship cannot be used/insurance claimed, one or more duplicates work
- The original ship and dupes don't work
- Vehicles may spawn in a duplicated ship, but may not work
See some example images below, captured from the Issue Council.
