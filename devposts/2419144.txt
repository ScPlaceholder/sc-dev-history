Major landing areas will no longer grant landing privileges to those who managed to get on the wrong side of the law. Port Olisar, Area18, and Lorville will not grant landing to any criminal wanted level 2 or above. Rest stops and Levski are a bit more lenient, and will let you land up to wanted level 3, but not if you're piloting a stolen ship. GrimHex will grant landing to all.
