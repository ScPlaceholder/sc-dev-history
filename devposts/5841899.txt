As already mentioned by a few people here and from me in the past the switch will happen gradually.
Once Vulkan is ready you will have the choice to use it but we won't force you.
It is to be expected that it's less stable than DX11 in the beginning due to it's complexity in nature and because it's new.
Once we see in our internal metrics that it's as stable as DX11 we will deprecate DX11 and remove it completely in a future patch.
