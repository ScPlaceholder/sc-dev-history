Want to help us gather data while you test/play on PTU? If so, the game designers would like to hear from you! To help out, please run the following test sequence:
- Choose any mission, but avoid missions given by any of the mission givers (i.e. Eckhart), as these are still WIP.
- Note the time you started the mission.
- Attempt to complete the entire mission from start to finish.
- Note aUEC rewards during and at mission end.
- Note the time you finished the mission.
- Once completed, head to the nearest Cryastro and fully resupply your ship.
- Note aUEC expenditure.
- Fill out the following survey: https://goo.gl/forms/GW5ROJMsr6DRvqTt1
- If you were unable to complete the mission, post/up-vote why as a reply to this thread.
- Please also share your thoughts and experiences as a reply, up-vote shared experiences.
