The current IFCS version does not cut any thrust. We changed that a couple of patches ago. The current version now keeps the TVI stable and utilizes all available thrust at any given time. What it actually changes is the velocity direction itself which is now determined by your available thrust input in any direction. So going full forward and left on your inputs will not establish a velocity vector at 45 degrees left off your nose ... but instead one closer to your nose because your back thrusters are stronger.
