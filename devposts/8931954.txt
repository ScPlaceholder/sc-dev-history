It doesn't need any new stuff adding to a scene for it to work, and in fact it still leans on the baked/runtime probes for transparent surfaces and mid/low quality settings, so a lot of scenes "just work". On the other hand, it changes lighting behaviour enough that some scenes look quite different and need sanity checking by an artist. Besides those, yes, you have scenes where people have added fake lights that might be doing strange things, or very bright emissive surfaces where the exact brightness didn't matter so much until now.
