We're excited by the tremendous interest in the Xi'an language and thrilled with the variety of community-created and driven Discord servers that have sprung up since CitizenCon. These look to be terrific resources for people looking to practice their pronunciation and overall speech with other backers. While we are not prepared to offer any "official" support to Discord servers at this time, we hope to offer similar services once Spectrum has suitable VOIP capabilities. At present, there is no ETA for this. Once there is, you'll be the first to know.
In the meantime, if you want to chat live with others who are interested in learning the Xi'an Language, please visit our official spectrum channel here:
https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/lobby/316619
Be on the lookout for more language lesson videos from Britton if you need a firmer grasp on vocalizations. If you have any questions about pronunciation, I highly recommend Lesson Two: Pronunciation and writing:
