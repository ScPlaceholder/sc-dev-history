We have added a new type of mission: Data Heist, which can be found under the Personal - Mercenary section of the contract manager.  In this mission players will be tasked with infiltrating locations defended by hostile NPCS to hack terminals and recovery/steal data, maintaining access via hacking or by using technician ID chips.  Terminals can be destroyed, either directly or by overheating, and will require defending and upkeep.  If too many servers are destroyed or not enough data has been uploaded, the mission will fail.
Some of these missions can be challenging, so you may want to team up.  Let us know what you think!
