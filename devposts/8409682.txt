Earlier this year, during Ship Showdown 2955, we invited you to unleash your creativity and rally behind your favorite vehicles to help us crown the top four beloved champions in the 'verse.
From creative Lego builds and original music to 3D prints, cinematic machinima, breathtaking digital art, and intricate real-life crafts(and so much more!), you amazed us with your talent and passion.
Thanks to your incredible submissions and votes, four ships rose to glory, each earning exclusive rewards to celebrate their triumph:
- RSI Polaris
- Anvil Asgard
- RSI Zeus ES
- Anvil Gladiator
If you have one of these fine ships in your hangar, you'll receive their respective 2955 Best in Show paint at the Intergalactic Aerospace Expo (IAE) this year, for free.
In addition to the limited-edition Best in Show liveries, you'll also receive the in-game manufacturer posters for each of the top four ships that you have in your hangar.
To celebrate the RSI Polaris' ultimate victory, every backer will receive an exclusive Polaris championship pennant.
Plus, the four Best in Show ships will be available to fly for free between November 29 and December 3!
All of these items will be distributed at the end of IAE.
This process can take some time, so don't worry if you don't see your items in your hangar right away.
As a reminder:
- To be eligible to receive the paints, the ships need to be in your hangar (having them in your Buyback List won't grant you the Best in Show paints).
- Please note that you need to have the specific BIS ship in your hangar and NOT a variant to receive the Best in Show paint. In this case, to receive the RSI Zeus ES Best in Show paint, you must have the Zeus ES in your hangar. Having the Zeus CL will not grant you the Best in Show paint.
- The Best in Show editions only contain the goodies within that ship's package and will not grant an additional copy of the rewards.
