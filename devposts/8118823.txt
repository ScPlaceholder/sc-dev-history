Boulders will be both more dense and less dense. They’ll be positioned more realistically, following natural erosion patterns in the landscape, so there will be areas near the bottom of valleys that will be quite dense, but further down the valley it might be totally rock free. Hopefully this will make driving more enjoyable, and more about picking a good path around the landscape rather than an exercise in dodging randomly spawned rocks. We also are aiming for rocks and rocky areas to be visible further away, which should aid route planning.
