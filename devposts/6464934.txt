As far as we've seen there hasn't been any errors in the attributing of the new AC badges, only that of human error (such as leaving the match before the post-game scoreboard appears). I've looked into dozens of reports of the badges apparently not attributing and every instance came down to that or they were playing in Offline (where rewards are disabled), or they simply forgot one (Master Modes: Duel was the most commonly forgot). Once we get the in-game tracker this should be a lot easier to self-troubleshoot.
