Hi folks, if you find a bug like this, please be so kind as to create an Issue Council Report. It's also worth checking beforehand if the bug has already been reported and then confirming it. Some here can already reproduce the bug, which is excellent.
When writing an IC report, please be as specific as possible when describing how the bug occurred. Sometimes the most insignificant details can end up being the cause of a bug, and the more detailed you are in describing everything, the easier it will be for our QA to reproduce the bug, find the cause, and work on a fix. Thank you for your active help. :heart:
