Hello Testers!
We have begun testing Jumptown 2.0!  In the event, isolated drug labs will begin manufacturing valuable contraband.  Local security will pay you to destroy it and the criminal elements of Stanton will charge you a finders fee and give you the location to collect and sell the drugs illegally.  On either side of the law, you will want to secure the site and the longer you hold it, the more drugs you can extract.  How you hold it and the tactics you use are completely up to you!
Let us know what you think below!   What side of the law you did you play on and how did the experience unfold?  Looking forward to hearing from you on this first test run!
