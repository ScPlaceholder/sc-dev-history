## Notable Changes: November 4, 2026
With today's Roadmap Update, we're officially renaming Alpha 4.11 to Alpha 5.0, reflecting the significance of the new technologies and features coming together in this release.
At the heart of Alpha 5.0 are several foundational technologies from Genesis, our initiative to transform how we create, populate, and bring planetary environments to life.
- Starchitect introduces a procedural world-building system that uses Genesis data to distribute locations across planetary surfaces and assemble them from modular components, allowing our teams to expand the universe at scale.
- Planet Tech V5 overhauls how planets are generated and rendered, introducing more natural biomes, improved vegetation and rock distribution, enhanced terrain materials, and GPU-driven generation for richer environments and improved performance.
- Population Manager introduces new systems for balancing server populations and populating environments with inhabitants suited to their surroundings, laying the groundwork for more dynamic interactions.
- Contract Boards bring mission opportunities directly into the world. Guild Halls at regional hubs allow players to browse contracts, complete assignments, and build reputation with different guilds, while Contract Boards offer faction and guild missions at local, regional, global, and solar system levels. Featuring a brand-new interface with improved contract information and faction-based browsing, Contract Boards support our broader vision for Hub and Spoke gameplay.
- DGS Planet Subdivision advances server meshing by dividing planets and moons into smaller, dynamically assigned server territories, rather than relying on a single server for an entire planetary body.
- Nyx I will be the first planet to feature these technologies, with plans to expand them to Nyx II, Nyx III, and other existing locations and planets throughout the 'verse.
Together, these additions lay the foundation for more detailed environments, more dynamic planetary populations, and a universe that continues to grow in both scale and complexity.
We can't wait for you to explore Nyx I and experience what's coming in Alpha 5.0. With that, let's dive into this week's Roadmap Roundup!

## Alpha 4.10
Lightning Bolt Company "Skarnov" DMR (4.10.3)
⁠Implementing the Lightning Bolt Company "Skarnov" DMR, a fully automatic designated marksman rifle whose specialized projectiles create electrical discharges between multiple targets, making it highly effective against groups of hostiles.

## Alpha 5.0
Item Recovery: Ship Loadout Improvements
Implementing Item Recovery: Ship Loadout Improvements, updating ship and item recovery to better support custom loadouts through timed claims, optional expedite fees, freight elevator item claims, and debuting a free recovery option when losses are detected as outside the player’s control.
:scroll: This Tentative card requires additional work and has been removed from the Release View for now.
Contract Boards
Implementing Contract Boards as a new way to discover and accept faction and guild missions at local, regional, global, and solar system levels through Guild Halls and hubs across the 'verse, featuring a brand-new interface that makes browsing contracts and filtering by faction easier and more intuitive.
:scroll: This card has been added and set to Committed.
S3 & S10 Bomb Racks
Implementing Size 10 and Size 3 bomb racks at shops in the PU, expanding support for different bomb sizes and loadout configurations.
:scroll: This card has been added and set to Committed.
Loot Refresh
Rebalancing loot at various sandbox locations and introducing previously Wikelo-exclusive rewards to the loot pool.
:scroll: This card has been added and set to Tentative.
Vehicle Combat Balance Pass
Implementing a weapon balancing pass for ship combat, with a focus on fighter combat and adjustments to weapon performance, time to kill, and the balance between energy and ballistic weapons.
:scroll: This card has been added and set to Tentative.
Kastak Arms "Hellion" Pistol
Implementing the Hellion Plasma Pistol, a high-velocity plasma weapon that creates hazardous impact zones, allowing operators to rapidly deploy multiple areas of denial across the battlefield.
:scroll: This card has been set to Committed.
Kastak Arms "Verdict" LMG
Implementing the Kastak Arms "Verdict" LMG, a medium-range weapon that builds heat through sustained fire before unleashing devastating superheated plasma projectiles with its alternate fire mode.
:scroll: This card has been set to Committed.
