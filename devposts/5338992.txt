Yeah that is a good point and the problem is known to us ... to get a bit technical here, it's not straight forward to fix in the current aiming system because fixed assist and convergence is basically the same thing (both create an offset just in different places) and the weapon specific nudging limit acts on both algorithms (the weapon just knows the aim point, not the source or reason).
Adding to the problem here is that the gun barrel in both ships is considerably offset from the fixed forward crosshair vector (which is based on the seat position) so below a certain distance it does not reliably show where to shoot in order to hit as the fixed crosshair never moves left and right (just forth and back based on the given convergence distance).
That is actually among the reasons why we want to update the aiming & gunnery systems.
