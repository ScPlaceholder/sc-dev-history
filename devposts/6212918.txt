Yes, you'll have to enable HDR in Windows first. For Windows 10, you can follow these three steps:
1) Make sure your hardware supports HDR. You will need an HDR-capable monitor and a compatible graphics card.
2) Ensure your graphics card drivers are up to date. Visit the website of your graphics card manufacturer and download the latest drivers for your GPU.
3) Enable HDR on the Display:
- Go to "Settings" by pressing Win + I or by clicking the Start button and selecting the gear icon.
- Click on "System."
- In the left sidebar, select "Display."
- Below the "Windows HD Color" settings, set the "Use HDR" toggle to "On."
- If your desktop and app windows appear overly bright or dark after toggling HDR on, you can adjust the "HDR/SDR brightness balance" in the "Windows HD Color" settings.
