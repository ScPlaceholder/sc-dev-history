We certainly won't go back to pre-3.14 weapon stats. The weapon work we did in 3.14 was mostly about getting a rough sketch of weapon archetypes in place  ... but we're heavily expanding on those now and are defining weapon "purposes" (think anti-fighter, anti-infantry, etc.) and rough properties the weapons need to have to fulfill that purpose (e.g. "anti-fighter weapons need a rather high bullet velocity").
That means we will go through every weapon in the game and deciding what purpose it needs to fulfill and then adapt the values for it. This also includes differences between weapons in the same category, like a cheap and a premium brand of an anti-fighter weapon. We also won't keep the current difference of cannons vs. repeaters ... that was just a temporary measure to ease up the balancing process. So you can expect anti-figher cannons, anti-large-ship Gatlings, etc. ... but as I said, those will appear step by step over several future releases.
Hope that all makes sense.
