- Ships cost a fortune, 100-150$ for a basic workhorse-ship like the hornet. And somehow, thats ok...cause we can see the work that goes into making those. A planet is procedual generated, we even got to see the videos on how fast and easy CIG can create good looking planets...the size of a planet is a simple number..."make virtual ball size X". Taking 50$ for a virtual 4x4 claim on a digital planet inside a digital universe is going to far.
Yes, ships are a lot of work but so are the planets and moons. There is the procedural tech but a planet in the end also requires A LOT of working hours of our environment teams nonetheless. Think of the procedural tool just painting at a large scale and without highlights. Without that extra work, you’d have very simple environments only, worlds without life.
One goal with the whole claim mechanic was to add gameplay to our much-expanded play areas, emulating a kind of landrush. But as said numerous times already, there is plenty of land for everyone.  If you don’t want to buy a claim license now, you don’t have to, as you won’t have a disadvantage and be able to buy them in the game later on.
