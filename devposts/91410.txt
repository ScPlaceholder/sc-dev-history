Hi @Bishop_Heahmund, we haven't delved too specifically into religions, but yes, it still exists in our future. Among Humans, there are a myriad of belief systems still at play with some new ones (Church of the Journey, for example). The Banu have a handful of deities that they worship (Cassa, patron of luck and the Great Traveler, Taernin being a couple examples). The Xi'an don't tend to worship gods, they have more of a universal morality path). So, yes, there are still some religions out there.
