In my 10 years, I can't recall a time we launched a patch and simply logged off. The team is just as eager as the community to get things running smoothly. We have numerous teams diving into feedback threads, testing to reproduce reported issues, gathering data, and actively working on fixes, especially on a weekend following a major patch like this one. Our global nature also means we have near-round-the-clock coverage, with our EU teams passing the baton to the US teams as they wake up, which is quite nice!
