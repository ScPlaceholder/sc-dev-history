Is the fueling interface available to the co-pilot of a Starfarer or is it a separate terminal?
It will be a separate terminal that is in the backside of the Starfarer's cockpit. In addition, it does not have to be operated by the pilot. So, anyone who you want on your ship can take the role of the "refueller".
Obviously, you can transfer fuel from one tank to another for load balancing reasons. Will this have any effect on the flight dynamics? If not: what is the state of the flight model rework to enable this (e.g. depending on cargo refactor)?
Yes, it is planned to have an effect in the future but needs some additional work from other teams.
