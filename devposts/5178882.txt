Yes, the Blade got a couple of updates which are being tested in EVO:
- retuned Blade to be closer to the Gladius. The Blade is slightly ahead in terms of raw performance numbers (about 1G). The Blade is better in forward boost than the Gladius, but the Gladius is better in lateral boost.
- Plasma cannons adjust to have 1400 m/s, this is offset by higher capacitor costs. So basically it holds the same amount of damage in the energy magazine as the Gladius (this is technically a debuff but remember that the bullets are a lot faster now).
- Plasma cannons turned over to full energy damage (was split between energy and distortion before, but it effectively made the cannons weaker)
There were also a couple of smaller health adjustments and certain parts (e.g. the wings) cannot be shot off for the time being to avoid situations where players slice their wings off and get rewarded with 50+ G lateral boost, a tiny target profile and virtually no downsides ... once we learned how to penalize flight behaviours when non-critical parts were shot off we will re-evaluate that.
