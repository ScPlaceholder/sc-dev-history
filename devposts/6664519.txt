Hey, decent write up, here's some thoughts
agree on the better feedback for snipers (vfx, sfx), a lot of discussion on the last thread was based upon post fire indicators where the issue is with snipers, 'post fire' can already be too late
It makes more sense in the context of not having 1HK but a lot of feedback we received was wanting snipers to 1HK so there's big costs associated with  snipers being in this territory
Want to play / feel out snipers having a much more obvious tell whilst having much more deadly damage profiles, combined with adjusting zeroing so  snipers have to deal with bullet drop a lot more
This is why it wasn't discussed as much on ISC because we are feeling things out still, was intended as a 'look what we are doing'
## Glint section
I think one of the big advantages of star citizens size is the fact you can have that sniper fantasy, it was one of my favorite bits of planet side 2 and I wouldn't want to push snipers closer nor gimp their damage profiles to ruin this player fantasy
With regards to missing the glint, entirely possible and a snipers careful positioning is a big part of that, if they're at distance and land these shots because of their skillful positioning ability, that's completely fair.
regarding the 4x "all a sniper has to do is equip a 4x scope", i said this was TBD last time and would like to expand upon that
Capping the zeroing out a lot earlier on these scopes combined with heavier projectiles making it harder to hit should compensate for no glint on these sights as well as limit the range that a 4x can be used.
Fighting under 150m? 4x should be fine, combined with the sniper being a lot more visible because of the range (as well as forcing a sniper closer meaning rifles & DMRs are a lot more effective)
So if 4x keep their powerful zeroing, a glint
if not, no glint (and im leaning towards that for 4x)
One thing SC does is flinch based upon what is shooting you not based upon what gun you have, this is how a lot of games balance powerful snipers by making you flinch a lot more and harder because you have a sniper
## 'Attempted TTK increase' section
Hard hitting weapons being looting only is one of the gameplay loops that i know that some orgs enjoy, there is gun running orgs that collect these weapons and have their own 'marketplace' which is some awesome systemic gameplay
Having these weapons in these locations incentivizes people to do bunker missions to prep for a big org fight, makes looting them of other players they've killed more exciting
The player who prepped better by farming these powerful weapons and putting themselves in these situations because which make their loadout should win these engagements over the player whos ran to the shop and bought an SMG
## Recoil
the issue with high recoil (and why a lot of games have 'calmer' recoil) is that it makes fighting at medium distances really challenging, combo'd with higher TTK you generally need steadier weapons combined with harsh ADS movement penalties to have slower targets
Battlefield is a good reference for this, you can see how they typically have lower recoil on their weapons (with their DMRs being very friendly with recoil)
we have more recoil then this as we're more 'grounded / hardcore' feel.
the main issue with 'add challenge to this' and 'keep it deadly' for rifles is a majority of people won't be able to take longer range engagements and will instead default to using snipers
Instead having some rifles that are easier to use and can contest these engagements whilst having some other rifles that are harder to use but hit a lot harder is a healthier path for the game
with regards to injuries, they're fairly impactful at the minute but in my opinion too heavily tied to RNG elements as opposed to damage caps
