From September 17 to the 30'th a new global event, Blockade Runner, has been enabled where criminal factions raid various low-orbit stations across the system, interrupting the supply chain and intercepting vast quantities of the critical resource Zeta-Prolanide. You can accept the event through the Contracts Manager, which will then provide instruction on what needs to be done through the mission itself. We're looking to gather your thoughts and feedback on the event as a whole, so please let us know what you think!
