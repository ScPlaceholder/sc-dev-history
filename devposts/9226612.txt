Greetings!
Thanks to everyone who tested the previous balancing update #4!  Reception of #4 has overall been really good, with our core takeaways being:
- TTK for PVP feels good but in the survey it still appears as too quick
- Balance between Energy and Ballistic weapons has been achieved for Weapon Sizes 1-3
- Size 4 and Size 5 ballistics are too strong
- Armor HP feedbacked as too small overall
- AD4B and 5B still considered as too strong
As you can tell, takeaways are starting to get more detailed. Hence with this #5 we hopefully have the big picture nailed down for fighters and can start addressing outliers and shift focus to large and multicrew ships.
Update #5 is a direct iteration on #4. Hence all changes are compared to #4
- Armor health as overall been increased by +15%
- S4 Ballistics alpha DMG has been reduced by -20%
- S5 Ballistics alpha DMG has been reduced by -10%
- Ship Turrets HP has been majorly buffed by +145% proportional to the overall DMG increase in the tests
- All Laser Beam weapons have been majorly buffed by +145% proportional to overall DMG increase in the tests
- AD4B alpha DMG nerfed by additional -20%
- AD5B alpha DMG nerfed by additional -10%
- All Mass Drivers projectile speed buffed by +10%
- CF Repeater Series alpha DMG buffed by +10%
- All MaxOx Neutron Cannons alpha DMG buffed by +20%
- Yeng’tu Repeater alpha DMG buffed by +20%
- WAR Cannon alpha DMG buffed by +50% and projectile speed by +40%
- WRATH Cannon alpha DMG buffed by +80%
- Overkill threshold (the value at which ships explode immediately if they receive too much DMG) has been increased by +145% proportional to the overall DMG increase in the tests
- AI Modifier is not included in this PTU
Additionally, we managed to address the bug that makes ships skip their overdamage health pool.
