Sorry, this is currently not possible within the game at this time.
If you are not scared of XML editing you could also load a profile in a text editor and tweak it yourself outside the game. Your current bindings are saved in "StarCitizen\LIVE\USER\Client\0\Profiles\default\actionmaps.xml". If you load the Warthog and T16k profiles after another and re-export them with a name, you will find them in your Star Citizen folder (I think under "StarCitizen\LIVE\USER\Client\0\Controls\Mappings"). You could then use a normal text editor to tweak them together.
However it's likely faster if you just manually load the Warthog profile and then manually bind the left handed T16k inside the game.
