These are examples of names that are COMMON among the Xi’an who operate primarily in the Proper Language (PLX). The Xi’an overtly mark names with diacritic marks so that virtually ANYTHING could be a name. That said, they would struggle with very long names and would have a tendency to try to shorten things and simplify the pronunciation of longer names. They would much prefer to call "Alexander" Ā’lek and "Jennifer" just Xye’ni. There is a big section on names coming up in the Overview Document. Soon. :slightly_smiling_face:
