You're right in pointing out that almost all ships have very similar 'flyby'/'movement' sounds when in atmosphere, this is something we have time scheduled to sort out :slightly_smiling_face:
This is something that everyone is hearing because we've recently split out the sound of flybys/AI/NPC ship movement into 'realistic' and 'soundsim' categories so that we can lay the framework to support the ever-improving Audio Propagation Component, which will let us make our audio react comprehensively to all of the different atmospheres (space, thin planet atmosphere, thick planet atmosphere) and contexts (on thick atmosphere planet without helmet, in EVA, pressurised cockpit in space, hearing a ship doing a flyby while you're inside an outpost with your helmet on etc) that our game has. What you're hearing right now I guess is the combination of sounds for ' medium sized ship on planet surface with decent atmosphere, not in ship, player's helmet on', which unfortunately are all quite similar at the moment.
TL;DR - We know, we're working on it, and it will be better than it ever was soon :wink: :stuck_out_tongue:
