We are aware that a lot of data on HUD is not collimated ... however the elements that would need to be collimated and are be prone to parallax errors (e.g. artificial horizon, aiming crosshair, etc.) are actually not part of the HUD itself but are drawn in 2D on the so-called player lens and are drawn based on their screen position. Thus these elements appear in the right spot in the world.
You can actually see that in the game ... when you have the g-force induced head movement activated, simply looking forward and strafing strongly left and right will slightly offset the head. You will see that the aiming reticle will stay fixed to "infinity".
