The Red Festival 2956 returns to the ‘verse to celebrate Lunar New Year, and this year, it’s all about resourcefulness and ingenuity in play. Welcome to the Year of the Rat! :rat:
Rats are quick, clever, and adaptable, and that’s exactly what we want to see from you in this contest. With good fortune on your side this Red Festival, we’re challenging you to pull off your most clever maneuvers on foot or aboard your most agile ride for a chance to be rewarded in our community contest. Think tight spaces. Risky routes. Hard-to-reach locations all in the goal of prosperity. The kind of shots that feel earned, not staged, and make other citizens stop and ask: “How did you even get there? How did you do that?”
If luck favors the clever, now’s the time to prove it. If it took planning, precision, or a little audacity to pull off, you’re on the right track.
## How To Participate
We’re calling on the community to celebrate the Red Festival by showcasing creative gameplay moments inspired by the Year of the Rat.
Please read the contest rules carefully to avoid disqualification.
- :izakaya_lantern: Prepare: Choose either your ship or your character and plan a shot that emphasizes agility and action. Think verticality, tight terrain, movement, and risk. If it looks easy, push it further and think like a rat!
- :camera_with_flash: Capture: Take your screenshot in-game. Whether you’re flying low through hazardous environments, perched in an unexpected location, or caught mid-movement, make sure the image tells a story.
- :outbox_tray: Submit: Post your entry to the Community Hub, using the Red Festival announcement at the top of the Community Hub page. Multiple submissions are allowed throughout the event period, so feel free to experiment and try different ideas.
## Keep In Mind!
- Read the contest rules!
- Posts must be submitted to the Community Hub within the correct Red Festival category.
- Each submission must clearly reflect the Year of the Rat theme through agility, action, or clever positioning.
- Post-processing effects are not allowed.
- AI-generated or AI-edited content is not allowed
- Replies to this thread will not count as submissions.
- Multiple players may appear in your screenshot, but group collaborations are not allowed. Only the submitting player is eligible to win
- Multiple submissions are allowed.
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your submissions on the following:
- Visual quality
- Creativity and composition
- Strong interpretation of the Year of the Rat theme
- Clear sense of action, agility, and/or difficulty
## Dates
The submission period ends on Tuesday, March 3, 2026, 20:00 UTC.
