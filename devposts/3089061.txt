## Notable Changes for May 29th, 2020
Thruster Efficiency Curves and Aerodynamics
This card has been added to the PU Roadmap under the Alpha 3.10 column. | Thruster efficiency curves will drastically change the way thrusters work in atmospheres. The work will include a major update to aerodynamics for ships, resulting in far more dynamic and believable behavior for ships in atmospheres.
Ship HUD Rework
The description has been updated on this card to more accurately reflect the work being done. As mentioned in the new description, you can expect to see further HUD work done in upcoming patches. | Rebuilding our ship HUDs to use the new building blocks UI system ahead of further redesigning the look of the UI. We will also take the time to improve some of the functionality of ship HUD.
