Where is that info from? The Cutlass nose in my 3.17.0 data is far above 3000 and hasn't changed since 3.14.
Please do not fall into the trap of producing feedback based on skimming through the numbers in the game data. While is sometimes useful to find specific balance issues (e.g. a typo in the damage) it can't serve as a basis for a healthy game balance discussion. Especially not before the actual release was played.
Edit: Specifically HP discussions should not ever be based on the game data because different ships have different amounts of parts on them. Just because a part is called "nose" on a ship does mean that this is the only part adding to the vulnerability from front attacks. Some noses are tiny, some noses are large in relation to the ship. Some ships seem to have more health on them because they simply have more parts which accumulate in data crawlers ... but that does not mean that this ship is more durable than ships with less health.
