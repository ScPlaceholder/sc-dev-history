When the simulation works properly, scraping on the ground will be extremely rare.  My goal is for the Gravlev system to be all-or-nothing, so it's robust enough to continue to function at acceptable levels (meaning you're not scraping the ground or bumping into things) even when damaged, but at the point where it becomes too damaged, it would switch you back into normal flight mode.  Because there's no point in even using hover mode if it's just dragging the ground.
