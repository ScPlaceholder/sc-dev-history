Inputs in general have nothing to do with MM but rather the wider game. But we do not specifically try to balance the game around specific inputs ... we're just trying to make it fun for all of them, that also includes avoiding gameplay implementations that are only possible on certain devices. But some input devices are just better for certain things than others and you can't really change that ... but it doesn't matter either. In the end we want users to use what they want without the input choice being the important one.
Now with regard to your question, no, we do not balance specifically inputs. PVP doesn't matter here at all because SC is not just a PVP focused game. We do balance the game based on functionality across all game modes. E.g. if we see in the analytics that people getting overly more kills with manual gimbal mode than fixed gimbal mode, we'll likely nerf the manual gimbal mode modifiers a bit. But that is independent of the used devices.
