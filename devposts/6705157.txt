Shooting tokens are the norm, we have shooting tokens which are limited per target, so only a certain amount can shoot you at once (it means we can have larger encounters that can be completed by groups and aren't impossible if you are solo)
It also makes encounters more enjoyable and it's in pretty much every FPS game from Doom to Tarkov
It's good not to re-invent the wheel and focus more on getting that good base and making it unique to size and scope of SC (Giving AI anti-vehicle weapons to deal with ships, letting them get in ground vehicles on higher difficulty as future examples for how it's our own experience)
You have some other metrics like mercy timers (on a large cooldown and mostly for bad luck protection) and a certain amount of free time when peeking out of cover (as to not make them feel robotic, it's set up to not be cheesable and allow NPCs to hold sight lines with that more 'hardcore' tuning)
We do somethings that aren't common though for example letting them hear your footsteps in combat to know where you are and where you're going which is more hardcore  as it makes escaping / hiding more difficult or f an enemy is within CQC they ignore token restrictions and various other things.
From experience the way it's tuned always feels 'fair' on high frame rate servers meaning you get the challenging experience you'd expect whilst not feeling that the game bullshitted you
