Hey everyone! The intention with the bringing back the F8 on Anvil day was to fulfill the numerous requests that came through after the previous promotion ended. With that in mind, we brought it back exclusively for those who had acquired a gold license during the October event, but had not picked up a Lightning.
We fully intended for people to be able to melt their previously acquired F8s and repurchase the IAE version with increased insurance, as has been possible for many ships at previous events.
Unfortunately, because of the way the badging for this one works, this isn't possible. The team is aware and will address this for future promotions when they are back in the office. In the meantime, if you're in the boat looking to melt and upgrade, simply submit a ticket to our support team and they'll get you squared away! (https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us)
