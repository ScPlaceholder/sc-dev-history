This is due to the exposure system. In each scenario the content on screen differs hugely, and the exposure system attempts to pick a brightness that works for most of the content onscreen (just like a digital camera). This is particularly challenging in a space game with a realistic sun brightness and pitch black shadows (though ours stars and night sky are much brighter than they should be in reality). The main problems are that the cockpit is not lit sensibly or an accurately which then throws off the exposure. So to fix this we need MFDs/holos who’s intensity varies based on lighting conditions (just like your phone and car do), and then we also need global illumination so the exterior lighting leaks into the cockpit and evens the brightness between the two spaces. Both of these are in progress.
