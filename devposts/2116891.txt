You're on the right track there. This option affects the panning rule of the audio listener: assuming your head/ears are the vertex of an angle, usually the front speakers are 60° apart (that is actually the suggested setup), while headphone speakers are 180° apart. By adjusting this menu option according to your listening device, audio in the game is rendered with a more accurate positioning that takes into account the speakers' angles. The effect is subtle and should be hardly noticeable, but it does help to better spatialise audio sources. If you want to read something more about this, here's a link to the Wwise documentation: https://www.audiokinetic.com/en/library/2018.1.7_6880/?source=Help&id=speakers_vs_headphones_panning_rules The documentation also includes a couple of explanatory pictures.
