## Features & Gameplay
Gameplay
Instanced Siege of Orison
Siege of Orison returns as a fully reworked, instanced co-operative FPS mission. This update introduces Mission Instancing to Star Citizen for the first time, a system that creates a private, closed environment shared only by you and your party.
This new version of Siege of Orison removes ship combat entirely and redesigns the experience as a focused, three-island FPS mission set in the Inspiration Park cluster. Ninetails have locked down access to the islands, overriding local security systems and stationing their lieutenants and the commander throughout the platforms. Crusader Security has contracted InterSec to get control back. Your objective on each island is the same: clear through the Ninetails resistance, reset the Security Network Control Towers, defeat the island Lieutenant, and flip the IFFI to restore Crusader's airspace control then move to the next island.
Each island is progressively more difficult, with the final island culminating in a boss fight against Mendo Ren inside the Admin Center barge. Level design across all three islands has been updated for better sightlines, improved combat flow, and navigation that suits the instanced, no-ship format. Tonight's test will focus highly on the 1st island.
// Instancing & Siege of Orison
- Closed Instance - Max 4 Players
When you enter the instance elevator, it locks to you and your party. No other players can join without being invited. The maximum party size for this instance is four. If you try to enter as a group of six, the first four are admitted and the remaining two are notified they cannot enter.
- Entering & Mission Acceptance
An optional one-time Intro Contract is available anywhere in Stanton that directs first-time players to the instance entrance at the Crusader Showroom Rooftop. On any subsequent visit, you can enter directly. When you step into the instance, the Siege of Orison mission is automatically accepted for everyone in the party with no manual pickup required.
- Entering Solo
You can enter the instance alone and play solo, or share the auto-accepted mission with other players to allow them to join your instance after you are inside.
- Entrance Location
Take the elevator to the Crusader Showroom Rooftop in Orison. From there, board the shuttle to the Instance Transit Platform and enter the instance via the elevator on the platform. The transit platform serves as a staging area between islands throughout the mission.
New Mission Giver: Recco Battaglia
In tonight's build, we will have the new narrative story missions from Recco Battaglia available. You'll get to play all three chapters for which the first one is available from by default, though Chapters 2 and 3 are available once you've gained enough reputation by completing other repeatable contracts Battaglia is offering. To ensure you get to experience all of these missions in a reasonable timeframe, we've lowered the reputation requirements for the later missions. You will be able to see and accept these missions in the mobiGlas while in Nyx.
