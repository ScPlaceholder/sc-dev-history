The move of PvP missions to their own category was for clarity, New players can get confused as they all show in the bounty tab leading these new players to accept a mission to kill another player, without fully understanding what exactly they are signing up for in that mission. So this allows players to be fully informed of what they are getting into, it also means those who are looking directly for PvP focused missions outside of Arena Commander have more of a direct route to find this gameplay.
PvP as a separate tab is one of the easiest QoL updates we can do, we have plans to ensure that all missions have a 'requirements section' that high-level touches on what things you would encounter in a mission.
However its important to be aware the nature of our game and its sandbox. Which means that most missions have a chance to be PvP as all it requires is a player to turn up at a location you are at doing a mission and a fight starts, so the organic occurrence of PvP is there still, this is only for Missions where we would directly send you against another player.
