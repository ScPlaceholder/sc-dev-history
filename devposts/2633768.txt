Head tracking always takes precedence over look ahead ... atm we only want one source that dictates head movement, so it's one or the other. If you have LAM enabled and then turn head tracking on, LAM will still run in the background but the head tracking inputs are used. If you then turn off head tracking it should automatically revert to LAM. We are currently unable to add UI indications, that will hopefull happen in Q1 2020.
