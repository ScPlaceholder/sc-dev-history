That specifically is not something we will add. I know that the Warthog Throttle e.g. has 2-position switches that only sends a signal in one position. But that is not very common for gaming hardware and most stick configuration tools (VKB, Virpil, etc.) allow you to set switches like that up to internally send more than an on/off signal. So if it is a specific quirk of the hardware, the hardware tools should solve that.
What we will add are simple "Set NAV Master Mode" and "Set SCM Master Mode" bindings that will act on press.
