Greetings!
4.1 introduces the first implementation (T0) of Item Recovery - a long-awaited system that gives players more control over their gear after death, and we would like to hear what you think! This initial implementation of Item Recovery will have players respawn with the exact gear they had equipped at the time of death minus stored items in their backpack and pockets. The following is the current intention for T0:
- Upon death you respawn with your entire loadout (including mags/weapons/medpens) BUT not your inventory. So you will lose anything in your pockets/backpack. This includes spawning at a medbed on ship and back at landing zone.
- Your armor/weapon is then fused to your corpse - so it would have a blocked interaction preventing looting indicated with a red icon and tool tip. On death, held weapon is moved to available holster, to be fused with corpse.
- Ammo, medpens, grenades etc along with anything in your inventory/backpack would be lootable by you or other players.
We look forward to hearing your thoughts, and thanks for taking the time!
