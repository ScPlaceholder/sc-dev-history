Both function exist in SC. You can invert any flight axis using the controls tab (just remember your device and select the appropriate one in the lower right of the controls tab in the options).
There is also an invert function that swaps the bindings for "thrust forward" and "thrust backwards", check under Flight - Movement in the bindings menu ...also here make sure Joystick / HOTAS is selected in the lower right of the key bindings screen.
