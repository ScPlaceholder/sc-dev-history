## Features & Gameplay
Performance Update: VFX Texture Streaming
VFX texture streaming now works correctly, preventing 20ms frame stalls caused by particle textures loading synchronously rather than streaming.
Along with this, we've added new CVars for advanced users: "r_texturesStreamingVFXDesiredMips" (set in user.cfg, default 2) controls the minimum mip floor for VFX textures, and "e_ParticleTexturePreLoading" (default 0) enables a pre-load pass for VFX effects
