We're not at all satisfied with our bindings menu. We have started to lay down the foundations to improve it but it is really a long while off yet.
If you use TrackIR the only way to exaggerate the movements is too create stronger curves in the TrackIR tool. However is has limitations as you do not control a camera with head tracking ... you control where the head of your actor is (you can see it moving when looking down on you). So there are natural limits in how far you can move both by the seats and by the animation rig of the actor. Sometimes it is something like 35 cm, sometimes just 5 cm.
If you want a better view of the MFDs ... in the cockpit view bindings you will see a function called Dynamic Zoom Toggle ... if you assign that a simple press on the button will switch between max and no zoom. It also works as a hold.
