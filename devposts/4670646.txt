Season's greetings come from the heart... and also from MISC, Anvil, Drake, and the other great manufacturers found throughout the 'verse. The question is, what would a manufacturer-themed season's greeting look like?
Welcome to the Manufacturer Season's Greetings Contest!
We're challenging you to take a close look at the corporate identity of our ship and vehicle manufacturers and design a season's greeting card or message following their style.
Before sharing your entry in the contest's submission thread or on Twitter (along with your Handle and the hashtags #StarCitizen and #maxsleighload), be sure to read the contest rules carefully.
## Rewards
:first_place_medal: 1st Place: Anvil Valkyrie in-game vehicle with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance)
:second_place_medal: 2nd Place: Aegis Redeemer in-game vehicle with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance)
:third_place_medal: 3rd Place: Origin 400i in-game vehicle with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance)
## Please Keep in Mind
- Read the full contest rules
- Create a Star Citizen manufacturer-themed seasons' greeting
- Post in response to the Contest Submission Spectrum thread or on Twitter, including your Handle and the hashtags #StarCitizen and #maxsleighload
- No group submissions
- Multiple submissions allowed
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your submissions on:
- Quality
- Creativity
- Capturing a manufacturer's design language
- Rule adherence
## Dates
The submission period ends on Monday, January 3rd, 2022 (EOD 11:59 PM Pacific).
