As for Nyx's attitude, it is a tough thing to generalize since a lot of the people who leave the UEE to live there do so for their own personal reasons, but we can use one of the larger factions in the area, the People's Alliance, as a loose guide. Formed during the Messer years, the Alliance believes that the UEE is so large and corrupt, that even under it's current, more democratic leadership, the best hope for society is if it was dissolved and power was redistributed. They want Humanity to live in an egalitarian society and feel that the UEE and it's brand of citizenship make that impossible.  It would not be unusual for someone living in Nyx to have a general distrust and distaste for the UEE and it's interests but at the same time, will often view the individuals still living in the Empire as lost and misguided rather than purposefully malicious.
“We, the People’s Alliance of Levski, are dedicated to the creation and continual development of a truly egalitarian community, where all sentients may feel safe and free to express ideas while supporting each other towards the communal goal of enlightened self-sufficiency.”
- People’s Alliance, Declaration of Intent, 2655
