Hello, everyone!
Over the past few years, the Bar Citizen World Tour has given us the chance to meet many of you in person. These gatherings have been a great way to connect face-to-face with members of the Star Citizen community who we usually only get to interact with online.
What started as a short-term initiative has become an important tradition for us. Each event has shown us the energy, passion, and camaraderie that make this community so unique. It’s been amazing to see how Star Citizen brings people together, no matter where they’re from.
We’re happy to announce that the Bar Citizen World Tour will continue in 2025, and we’d love your input on where to go next. The more interest a location gets, the better the chances of adding it to the tour. Whether it’s a large gathering or a smaller meetup, we want to hear your suggestions.
If you’d rather share your ideas privately, you can send a message to me or any member of our Community Team (@Nicou-CIG, @Freyja-CIG, @Wayne-CIG, @Synthwave-CIG, or @Stamp-CIG), and we’ll make sure your location is considered.
Thanks for being part of this journey. We look forward to hearing your suggestions and meeting even more of you in 2025!
