I want to let you know about several changes that were made to the game at various times to prevent the scenario you state.
- Should a player attack another unprovoked, the attacked player will be able to lawfully defend themselves for a short time which is constantly extended should the pair remainin combat until either the hostility timer runs down or the attacker quantum travels.
- Added to this, any innocent in the vicinity (including other players, security and defenses) will be able to act in defence of the innocent.
- Charges are no longer pressed automatically on behalf of the player. They must choose to press charges using the notification in the, sadly, fleeting time given (we need to overhaul the notification system).
