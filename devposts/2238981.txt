The underlying calibration tech has not changed between 3.5.1 and 3.6. Try to help the calibration process by supplying enough light in your face, make sure your eyes, nose and lips are well visible and if possible avoid glasses. Big beards can be a problem too, especially if they cover the mouth. Facial tracking is effectively an image analysis ... so it tries its best based on what it can see. A simple change in the background light can already improve things. If it's really a problem, PM me a screenshot of your calibration window and I might be able to tell you why it fails.
