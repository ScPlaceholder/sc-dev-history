## Economy Balancing
As highlighted in Alpha 4.0 Patch Watch #1, Star Citizen Alpha 4.0 brings a comprehensive overhaul to the in-game economy. This update enables us to collect valuable gameplay and trading data directly from your Star Citizen play sessions. Combined with your provided feedback, this data will drive ongoing adjustments and fine-tuning to ensure a balanced and immersive economic experience in the 'verse. Our team will also be monitoring closely for any exploits/loopholes that may negatively impact the economy.
One recent loophole allowed players to spawn ships in their hangars, remove valuable components (such as weapons and mining heads) for resale, and then reclaim the fully equipped ship through insurance. This process generated large amounts of aUEC in a short time, disrupting the game's economy and creating imbalances, which will be crucial for us to avoid.
To mitigate this and similar exploits, we're implementing a temporary adjustment: the sale prices of ship weapons will be reduced to significantly less than their purchase costs. This measure serves as an interim solution while we continue to refine the long-term design of our insurance and warranty systems, which will more comprehensively address fraudulent vehicle part duplication.
We'll be closely monitoring your feedback on the upcoming economic rebalancing and look forward to long-term implementations that will offer more robust support.

## Unique Resource Distribution
The Pyro system's resource distribution is shaped by its distinct environmental factors:
- Temperature Rings: Pyro is divided into zones based on proximity to its star. Closer to the star, temperatures rise, while the outer regions grow colder. For example, Ice is more abundant in the cooler outskirts of the system.
- Density Variations: Dense base materials are typically found in warmer regions, whereas lighter, porous materials like Ice and Silicon are more common in the colder, outer zones.
Some details have been intentionally left for you to discover. So, prepare your mining ship, venture into Pyro, and uncover its wealth of resources firsthand. Happy prospecting!
