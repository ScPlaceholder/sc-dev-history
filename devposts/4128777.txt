This is a list of new words that will aid the community in the translation of the Gatac archives letter. All of the new words included in the letter have either been constructed from terms already in the Xi'an Dictionary or from the list of words below.
*  .ēa - elm. - timeworn; ancient; undying; eternal
*  .uyai - elm. - solemn, grave, somber, grim
*  a.s’ehuang’moa - elm.CMP - critical supplies; necessary provisions (without which plans or lives will be in jeopardy)
*  Amuipuāng - PN. - The Ark; compendium of all important knowledge
*  ch.ōl - elm. - fail; fall short (typically with the verb e, when o ch.ōl: botch; bungle; flub; make a mess of)
*  chinii - elm.CMP - recent; novice; inexperienced; raw (untested)
*  e chi uo'a Nuana - idm. - young adult (an adjectival sense describing a person who has just matured enough to be inducted into the Service and leave one's maternal home. Literally: "just (old enough to) say Nuana (the way adults refer to their mother upon entering and after Imperial Service)); “fresh from the nest.”
*  ē’la - elm. - (metaphorical) “shadow” or alternatively “glow” cast by a person of great honor and respect (literary, poetic; formal)
*  hy.ūng - elm. - horrible; awful; viscous; vile; cruel; terrible
*  k.ang - elm. - stand; be standing; be on one’s feet; upright
*  lo sye te’an - idm. - be (tragically) lost (used primarily when people or critical infrastructure; art; architecture etc. are destroyed in war (literary metaphor, poetic): literally meaning: “to become fragments, skeletons, remains (of something eaten or destroyed)."
*  lye.x’o’ma - elm.CMP - mission (in a military context); task (in a work or project-centric context)
*  ngaoyao - elm.CMP - training (organized learning and practice for the purpose of expanding skills); t.o ngaoyao (ueth) = train (to do s’thing))
*  Ny.ām - TITLE.hon.arch -  “the People” referring (spiritually) to all Xi’an collectively who have ever lived, even before the founding of the Empire (rare, literary, formal, poetic, archaic)
*  r.uangl’ē'leth - con.FOR - “Please pardon the lack of formalities and brevity.”
*  Sanrailen - PN. - a spacecraft manufactured by Gatac
*  syo’ang - elm. - safe; unharmed; protected; saved
*  tao’ue - elm.IMP - special Imperial form of tang’ue used only by the Imperial house. A request that cannot be refused (without severe consequences). See tang’ue.
*  tuē - elm. - periphery; peripheral; adjacent to
*  tuē’hi.n’e - elm.CMP - orbit (established above and around a heavenly body)
*  u’nya. - elm.CMP.prfx - a group of people (generic sense). See also kyo.
*  xy.uii - elm. - send; send off
*  xyea - elm. - answer; respond; response; reply; feedback
*  yai.yu” - pn.hon.IMP - ‘this’/‘it’; a solemn endeavor (of a previously explained Imperial mandate, which can ONLY be used in an Imperial context (unless for hyperbolic or rude humorous effect with the verbal particle ki’a)).
For further information, please see An Overview of the Xi’an Language for Diplomats: https://robertsspaceindustries.com/media/9f3kqrtzkslfmr/source/Xian-Language-Overview-V6.pdf
