The current Gen 12 plan doesn't have anything on it (edit: in externally visible terms) that the engine doesn't do already. The idea is that it provides efficiency improvements and the framework to cleanly switch over to Vulkan.
Looking at the description of HDRP though, these features aren't the kind of thing that we'd think of as being "part of" this renderer. A lot of the concepts (eg light units) that they've neatly wrapped up as features of a specific pipeline are, for us, more like conventions scattered around random parts of the engine.
