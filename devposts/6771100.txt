The braking with gears down is just an IFCS safety feature. It is an enforced speed limiter that makes sure that you don't go too fast when you come in for a landing or move inside a hangar using your normal thruster output.
You can turn it off via keybinds, search at the bottom of the flight movement keybindings, it's currently called Automatic Precision Mode or so (in later releases it will be part of the IFCS config screens).
The strong deceleration comes from swapping from NAV to SCM, it does not come from putting the landing gear out. This is on purpose, though we will need to work on the effects for that.
