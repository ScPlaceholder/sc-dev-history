If you follow the link above, you will be directed to the home to the Xi'an Dictionary.  The dictionary is ever-growing, as the Xi'an language develops. If you have a suggestion for a Xi'an word that isn't included here, please comment below. Here's how to comment:
1. Include your suggested word, if you have one in mind. This isn't required. Just for fun!
2. Include the translation of your suggested world.
3. Include an explanation as to why you think this world would be important to Xi'an culture.
Example comment:
p.u n'oa :  good luck!
I looked in the dictionary and I couldn't find a word or phrase for luck. Even if it isn't highly important in their culture, they probably have a way of wishing each other good fortune.
Remember, this is a forum for discussing ideas. Words suggested here may not make it into the dictionary, but some of them will!
Enjoy!
