Rivers are never a constant width all along their length, there are no meandering twists and turns or ox bow lakes. They look more like canals than rivers.
The ISC doesn't show it, but the rivers DO increase/decrease their width.
The most striking (read jarringly un-immersive) thing is deposition patterns, for example on a left hand bend the beach side should be on the left where water flow is slowest and the deep side, with the most wall side erosion, should be on the right where water flow is fastest.
We also already have wider banks where we turn large corners, and these corners DO have slightly different terrain decals to simulate different deposition patterns, though oxbow lakes I haven't gotten around to yet. I am going to look at making the bank erosion slightly more dramatic.
One of the reasons the colour of the terrain doesn't change as much around the river as I'd like is that it'll break the distance read - ie it'll appear to change colour as you get closer. A limitation of our planet tech currently is that the terrain modification can't change terrain texture splatting. For rivers, this is partially remedied by decals, but they have to be pretty close in terms of colour as we can't render them from space, so at some point they have to fade in. So unfortunately no actual sandy banks yet.
