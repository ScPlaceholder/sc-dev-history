Yes, as @VIGlL said, this is something that we're working on :slightly_smiling_face: The current QT sounds are a bit of a holdover from when the longest jump you could do in Star Citizen was around 2,000,000km. We'd like to dampen these sounds over the course of the journey to gradually become less present.
Dampening them based on which room of the ship you're in, as well as depending on the type/role and size of ship is something that is in line with our overall vision of the ship sound too moving forward. I can't give a date for this, really, as there are a lot of ships, but rest assured it is on our radar and we're working on it! Things like dampening based on which room you're in, or how close to the exterior hull you are, requires some manual markup on each individual ship, but it's something I'd love to get in there. It's getting on my nerves a bit too on that journey to Hurston from Crusader :slightly_smiling_face:
