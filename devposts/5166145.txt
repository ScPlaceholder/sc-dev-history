Imagine for a moment you're a Banu, Xi'an, Tevarin, or fearsome Vanduul and you want to advertise your ships to Humanity with a poster...
Hello everyone and welcome to the Alien Ship Poster Contest!
You might remember the Banu commercial that Jax McCleary presented to us during the Intergalactic Aerospace Expo 2949. Well, now it's your turn to create a ship poster of your favorite alien ship in the style of its corresponding Star Citizen alien race.
Snatch an image of your favorite alien ship or vehicle (screenshot or concept art), take an in-game screenshot of your favorite location in the 'verse, and compose a poster via third-party editing software to sell the alien dream to the pilots of the UEE.
However, it's not as simple as it seems - the real challenge is emulating the marketing style of the Banu, Xi'an, Tevarin, or Vanduul.
Before sharing your entry in the contest's submission thread or on Twitter (along with your Handle and the hashtag #AlienWeek2952), be sure to read the contest rules carefully.
## Rewards
:first_place_medal: 1st Place: Standalone Banu Defender with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance) including the harmony paint
:second_place_medal: 2nd Place: Standalone Esperia Talon with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance)  including the harmony paint
:third_place_medal: 3rd Place: Standalone Aopoa Nox with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance)  including the harmony paint
## Please Keep in Mind
- Read the full contest rules
- Must be a 2D image (no GIFs or videos) altered via a third-party program like Photoshop
- The poster must be based on an in-game screenshot and an image of a Star Citizen alien ship (screenshot or concept art)
- Submissions must be submitted to the dedicated contest submission thread on Spectrum or to Twitter alongside the player's handle and the hashtag #AlienWeek2952
- No group submissions
- Multiple submissions allowed
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your submissions on:
- Theme
- Creativity
- Quality
- Rule adherence
## Dates
The submission period ends on Monday, June 27, 2022 (EOD 11:59 PM Pacific).
Good luck and enjoy this year's alien week!
