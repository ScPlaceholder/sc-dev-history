Let's go through them one by one:
1.) FOV and FOV slider in the options menu doesn't update when changing screen resolutions and certain elements like the player HUD were cut off sometimes (https://issue-council.robertsspaceindustries.com/projects/STAR-CITIZEN/issues/STARC-48836)
> The initial FOV ranges were dependent on the current screen resolution and subsequent changes to the resolutions didn't update it all. This was causing some weird behavior of zooming in when changing screen resolutions and the FOV slider didn't update.
This has been fixed entirely. Whenever the screen resolution changes the Vertical Field of View stays the same, but the horizontal extends if changing to ultrawide. The slider now also correctly updates.
There was one more issue with 32:9 ratio screens which was calculating a too narrow FOV which caused the player HUD to be slightly out of screen. I've added a new entry in our global FOV settings which fixes this as well.
What decides the min/max values of the FOV slider?
This is dependant on the aspect ratio/screen resolution. The engine calculates it in a way to keep a vertical FOV of roughly 54-66 degrees:
Aspect Ratio:       Horizontal FOV:
4:3                        69-83
16:9                      85-100
16:10                    80-95
21:9                      100-115
32:9                      122-134 <----- This entry was added (was 117-128 before)
48:9                      140-148
If your resolution does not has any of these aspect ratios: the two closest ones are taken and the average will be used.
