I know we've had interns here at some point in the past, though I can't remember whether any of them were programmers.
In terms of hiring people with CryEngine experience: I'm not the person doing those interviews but I'd expect them not to care so much about specific engine experience unless it was a lot of it, e.g. shipping a whole game. When I was in your position I decided to learn Java for similar reasons, it just meant that burned a lot of time going from "doesn't know Java" to "doesn't know enough about Java". More likely they'd be looking for strong C++ and engine-independent game-dev concepts.
Also, not to burst your bubble, but you might find Star Citizen to be a terrible first-time job. It's late in a project, the documentation is patchy, no one would have a lot of time to explain things and many systems require you to write code that works with their incomplete feature set while being structured to be easily adapted to their eventual feature set. Then again, what do I know? Maybe that's your bag.
