I see where you're coming from ... let me clarify with an example: We do not want to artificially buff turret properties (like rotation velocity, precision, etc.) just because a human is controlling it (just the same we also don't give ships more accelerations because a human is sitting in it). It's that kind of thinking because it was suggested in the past to do stuff like that to account for lets say comparable kill ratios of pilots vs gunners in multicrew environments. All the performance that you get out of ships, turrets or other multicrew positions are going to be systemic.
Does it mean that every multi-crew player has the same effect on the combat environment in the same ship? Probably not in all situations, but that's not the point. The point is to work together to get the best outcome for a whole crew, not the best outcome for a single crew member.
