Navigate a 'verse where preparation pays off. With Alpha 3.9 comes v1 of the Player Status System, bringing things like hunger, thirst, and temperature sensitivities. Although Star Citizens not a survival game at base, we believe the environment you are heading into to should affect your decisions and directly influence how you will prepare for your trip.
Roadmap: Player Status System v1
The player status tracks the various well-being attributes of a character, including health, stamina, and oxygen levels. This system is expanded to include thirst, hunger and temperature. These values will have knock-on effects for player stamina and ability. AI behaviors will use this system to make intelligent decisions based on their own condition or the condition of others.
