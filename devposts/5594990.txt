The problem with racers is that their role demands very similar properties that dedicated fighters have. Think about a racing ship that is greatly suited for the snake pit. Obviously that is a ship that can offset it's velocity vector very fast. On paper that is exactly the same requirement that a good dogfighter has. But we definitely want dogfighters to exceed as dogfighters and racers to exceed as racers ... both are somewhat related but still different ship roles.
So in that regard we're thinking about how to make sure that dedicated racing ships are not just better dogfighters. Given that some racers they will be very agile we can look at different balance levers to achieve less combat effectiveness, e.g. with smaller weapon capacitors (less offensive capabilities), different thrust capacitor tunings or simply less durability (which makes probably most sense because you really don't want to drag a lot of hull plating with you when you want to focus on racing). How far we go with those differences remains to be seen, it is currently WIP. We certainly don't want to exclude racing ships from combat or so ... we just want to make sure that they are best suited for their intended purpose without invalidating other ships.
So the topic is not STRICLTY related to master modes but given that we need to retune all ships for the master mode tuning pass, we will change certain ship characteristics to make them feel more suited to their intended role / purpose. I hope that makes sense.
