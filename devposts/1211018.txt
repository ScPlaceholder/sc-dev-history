While there are issues with the currently accessible Mustang series ships, it would be a lot more work to try and do any sort of stop-gap maintainence on them compared to just continuing the work being done to complete the updated models.
It can definitely be frustrating, especially for anyone whose only ship is the Mustang Alpha, but overall, it's going to be better for the ship-line to continue our work on the update rather than spend those same resources fixing an older, and with 3.3, soon to be fully deprecated model.
