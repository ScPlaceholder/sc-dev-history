Shouldn't really reply to this kind of comment. But I'll give you some clarification, because I wasn't clear enough.
No. You aren't punished for having a bigger ship.  The goal of each bracket of commodity is this:
A ship that can carry 20 32SCU crates, will make like 20% more than:
A ship that can carry 20 16 SCU crates, Which makes 20% more than
a ship that carries 20 8 SCU crates, which makes 20% more than
a ship that carries 20 2 SCU crates. Which still makes good UEC, since, as you pointed out, they have invested value into a risk for their profits.
The goal here is that the Ship that carries 320 2scu crates, isn't making 16x what the smallest ship is making.
but they ARE making more, each time you buy a bigger ship and move to a new bracket, you should be able to pick if moving up and making a better TOTAL volume/uec haul is better for your ships size, or if you should continue to compete at the lower bracket to make the best money.
but the lower cargo grid ships, will NOT be able to access the same total volume per haul that makes the larger brackets worthwhile.  Even if there's enough supply and demand that moving 4000 SCU of Steel at a time is valuable, the requirements of a grid to really hit that price point is too high for ships of only 100SCU.
I didn't mean to imply that larger ships wouldn't be getting access to better trade routes and significantly better total profit lines. But those are going to be different resources than the guy flying from outpost to outpost moving their rarest goods.
