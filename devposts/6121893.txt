## Welcome to the '2953 Arlington Gang Screenshot Contest'!
Greetings, operatives!
Over the next few months, we’re spotlighting some of our favorite missions and giving everyone who attempts them the chance to win ships perfectly suited to the challenges of the 'verse. We're kicking things off by rewarding the most epic images of the final mission in the Arlington Gang series.
Your mission, should you choose to accept it, is to capture exciting screenshots featuring the Idris flown by the NPCs in the final battle of the Idris Stolen by Arlington Gang bounty mission. Submit these screenshots to the Community Hub or as a reply to the challenge announcement on Twitter using the hashtag #StarCitizen (or both!) for a chance to win a fearsome fighter ship!
## Before You Begin:
Before you start on your submissions, please read the contest rules carefully and remember to post your entries to the Community Hub or on Twitter and NOT as a reply to this thread. Also: Any submission posted to the Community Hub without '2953 Arlington Gang Screenshot Contest' in the post title will not be considered.
## Keep in Mind:
- Read the contest rules! :book:
- Only post screenshots of in-game interactions with the 'Idris Stolen by Arlington Gang'  bounty mission. Any other subject will be disqualified.
- Screenshots must also include the Idris flown by NPCs in the mission.
- Please post submissions to the Community Hub or to the contest announcement post on Twitter (don't forget to include #StarCitizen in your post). Replies to this thread will NOT count as a submission.
- Each screenshot uploaded to the Community Hub is considered a single entry. However, you are allowed multiple entries and can upload the entries as a gallery on the Community Hub.
- Use '2953 Arlington Gang Screenshot Contest' as the title for your submission on the Community Hub, and select the screenshot category for it to be eligible.
- Group submissions are NOT allowed. You may include your friends in the screenshot, but only the person submitting the entry is qualified to win.
- Images that include player handles will NOT qualify.
- Post-processing effects are NOT allowed. This includes the use of third-party programs/applications like Photoshop, Reshade, etc.
- Consider turning off the HUD when taking screenshots, as this might make for better composition. Check out some excellent tutorials by DigThat32, Mr. Hasgaha, or NotGemini.
- You are limited to winning one prize, so you cannot win on multiple platforms (Community Hub, Twitter).
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your entries based on:
- Quality
- Aesthetic composition
- Rule adherence
## Dates
The submission period will end on Thursday, 10 August, 11:59 PM Pacific (Friday, 11 August, 06:59 AM UTC).
