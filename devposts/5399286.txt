The Tobii Eye Tracker 5 lets you keep an eye on your target at all times, whether it's an enemy fighter or a video screen at a CitizenCon watch party.
Don't miss out on your chance to win one!
Welcome to the CitizenCon 2952 Watch Party Screenshot Contest sponsored by Tobii.
We're rewarding the best screenshot of an in-game CitizenCon watch party with a Tobii Eye Tracker 5. Check out our website to learn how you can adjust the Tobii Eye Tracker 5 from Star Citizen's game settings to take advantage of head and eye tracking technology.
Before you start organizing a meet-up in the 'verse for your party images, please read the contest rules carefully and only submit your entries to the contest's Submission Thread. Any submissions not posted to this location will not be accepted.
## Keep In Mind
Read the contest rules
No post-processing effects are allowed. This includes third-party add-ons, like ReShade
Only post screenshots that include an in-game watch party. Any other subject will be disqualified
Group submissions are not allowed. Multiple players in screenshots are allowed but only the person submitting the screenshot is eligible to win
Multiple submissions per player are allowed
You are limited to winning one prize, so you can not win on multiple platforms (Spectrum, Facebook, Twitter)
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your submissions on:
- Quality
- Creativity
- Rule Adherence
## Dates
The submission period ends on October 10, 2022 (EOD 11:59 pm Pacific)
## Reward
:sports_medal: Tobii Eye Tracker 5
