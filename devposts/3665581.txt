@NovaFang, I am not a medical professional so take what I say here with a pinch of salt:
You can setup the eye tracker so that only your head movements are used. There is a slider in the "VOIP, FOIP and Head Tracking" options called "Head Tracking - Tobii - Eye to Head Ratio":
- pull it completely to the left and only your eye movements will control where you actor's head is pointing
- pull it completely to the right and only your own head movements control where your actor's head is pointing.
So if you suffer from Nystagmus you probably want to pull that slider to the far right to exclude any kind of eye movement readouts. The tracking will still work very well without the eye data.
