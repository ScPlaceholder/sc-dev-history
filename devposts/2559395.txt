Planet V4 vastly improves the popping on terrain itself, and while there were improvements to the ‘jumping‘ of assets such as trees and rocks when you approach them, the main two issues highlighted here are the view distance (trees fading when far away) and the popping between levels of detail (LODs). The former is something we may require bespoke tech for merged LODs to improve upon as we’re at the limit of how many individual trees can be handled. The latter issues is mainly one for art, and was being iterated on right up until the Citizen Con demo but it‘s certainly something we’d like to improve upon (it relates to the first point because the LODs need to be very cheap because of the sheer number).
