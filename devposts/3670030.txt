We missed updating the description. :disappointed: so a small follow up:
- The VJoy controls are NOT enabled by default but very useful for mining or precise driving in PTU. To enable it, go to your keybindings und Vehicle - Driving and assign the "Turn Left / Right" and "Pitch up/down" functions to the X and Y axes of your mouse. Please note that Star Citizen will only accept one input device for each rotation so pressing A and D AND moving your mouse at the same time will conflict with each other.
We have seen reports that some of the VJoy indications are not visible making VJoys controls very hard. If that happens for you please post a screenshot.
