We do plan to have enemy combatants that react to environmental events ("Huh? ... What was that? Hey Joe, did you hear something?").
But I imagine it would be tricky to have an AI that spooks at shadows that only he sees, but that you did not (because you are seeing the scene from a different angle).
It might end up looking like a mistake. I can see a Player thinking "Why did that idiot just turn and run for no reason?", not knowing it was because the AI thought he heard or saw something somewhere else.
(this is different from a Player throwing a rock or a can or something that leads an AI to go investigate a sound, leaving the Player free to do something else, etc.)
