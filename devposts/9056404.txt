The missing piece, in my opinion, is information.
I'm pretty sure I've said this on record before.
I really want to give players more in game information, The lack of information for traders in the game right now is not enjoyable, it doesn't serve a purpose.
I don't necessarily want 100% real time prices at all locations shown to all players. I do understand why lots of people think they want this. But perfect information gameplay is quite rarely good for supporting a meta, and at some level there will always be a meta between pirates, traders, and other traders competing for the same good prices. Instead of removing that meta, we can just make it fun instead.
