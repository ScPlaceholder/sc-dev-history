While I'm not from the environment art team, so I can't say it's only culling logic, it's certainly sometimes a question of culling. This isn't always something as simple as number of polys shown, it can also be a question of how many separate objects need to be drawn (or unique materials on those objects), things like lights that can affect performance or even cause visual bugs if too many overlap the same screen region, or objects like render-to-texture which have a lot of extra cost while they're in view.
