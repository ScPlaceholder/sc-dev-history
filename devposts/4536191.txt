Do you have what it takes to share an epic or entertaining moment of team-based medical gameplay in comic form?
Welcome to the ultimate challenge of an artist's eye or writer's wit, the Alpha 3.15 - Med-Crew Comic Contest.
We challenge you to jump into Star Citizen Alpha 3.15: Deadly Consequences and create a digital comic with up to eight screenshots that focus on medical gameplay. Arrange the screenshots of your comic in the way that best supports your narrative, and add speech bubbles and text boxes as needed.
Before posting your digital comic to the contest's submission thread or to Twitter (along with the hashtag #SCmedic), be sure to read the contest's rules carefully.
If you need some help and guidance with medical gameplay, feel free to check out our deep-dive about Hospitals & Healing.
## Rewards
:first_place_medal: 1st Place: Origin 400i in-game vehicle with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance)
:second_place_medal: 2nd Place: Drake Cutlass Red in-game vehicle with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance)
:third_place_medal: 3rd Place: MISC Reliant Mako in-game vehicle with LTI (in-game lifetime insurance)
## Please keep in mind
- Read the full contest rules
- Your comic must display the 'Made by the Community' logo, which you can download here (black) or here (white)
- Post your digital comic to the contest's submission thread or to Twitter with the hashtag #SCMedic
- Your comic may consist of up to eight screenshot panels
- Your comic must highlight medical gameplay teamwork moments
- Up to three participants per team
- Only one submission per team
- Submissions must clearly name the team members
- Screenshots may be altered with third-party tools
- Post-processing effects are allowed. This includes third-party addons like ReShade
- You may add speech bubbles and/or text boxes
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your comic submissions on:
- Quality of screenshots
- Creativity of the storyline
- Rule adherence
## Dates
The submission period ends on Sunday, November 21  (EOD 11:59 PM Pacific).
Good luck!
