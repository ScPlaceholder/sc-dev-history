## Bug Fixes & Technical
- Fixed 1 Client Crash
- Potential Fix: Undocking from a ship mid-refuel causing external fuel tanks on the Starfarer to dump their entire fuel load rather than retaining it
- Potential Fix: Instance elevator on the Orison platform suddenly becoming uncallable for all party members, caused by authority assignment messages being dropped during entity streaming and hierarchy changes; multiple edge cases in the authority propagation system have been addressed
- Potential Fix: QV Breaker Stations in the Nyx Dungeon having no collision, causing players and items to fall through the floors and walls
- Potential Fix: MISC Starlite fuel nozzle failing to deploy, blocking it from docking with recipient ships for refueling contracts
- Potential Fix: Siege of Orison Command Center entrance airlock not resetting after a player died and respawned inside it, permanently sealing the entrance for all other players; additional fix covering the case where a player dies while the airlock is in the process of opening
- Potential Fix: Mouse clicks, weapon fire inputs, and UI button presses being lost or stuck, caused by a raw mouse input optimization incorrectly dropping legacy input events; the optimization has been reverted pending a rework. (This also restores significantly improved input reliability for players running under Wine/Proton)
- Potential Fix: ATC landing and takeoff hotkey not working, requiring players to use the comms menu instead
- Potential Fix: Buying any commodity at a kiosk locking the player out of purchasing additional commodities until the first purchase was removed via the freight elevator
- Potential Fix: Ships with landing gear connected beneath destroyed thrusters unable to be repaired at a repair terminal, as the transaction manager was incorrectly rejecting items that were parented to other items in the same repair request
- Potential Fix: Keycards carried by Nine Tails Elite NPCs spawning inside armor item slots rather than the backpack slot, making them invisible and impossible to loot without stripping the NPC's armor first
- Potential Fix: Fusebox lever animation inclusion file missing, causing the lever up and down animations to not play at runtime despite the animation data being present
- Potential Fix: Commodity kiosk showing the wrong delivery subtype in certain transactions, causing incorrect delivery destination selection
