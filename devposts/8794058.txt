The Vehicle Command Module has been added in tonight's 4.8 PTU publish, which is a new ship pairing mechanic where a smaller, fully pilotable command ship takes control of a larger host ship that cannot fly on its own. For this 4.8.0 PTU, this applies to the Drake Caterpillar. Without a Command Module attached, the host Caterpillar cannot be piloted.
A feedback survey is available at: Vehicle Command Module Survey Form. This survey will be open until May 14.
We look forward to reading your feedback!
