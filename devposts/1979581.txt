Howdy Peoples!
In the four-plus years I've been working here I've striven to shine a light on all the aspects of video game development no other studio would ever think to touch.
Whether it's producers creating production schedules live, the props team that make a world seem lived in, network programmers making world-wide access possible or even the community itself that shapes our development journey in unique ways, I've always liked to take an episode here and there to explore the parts usually unseen and this week's episode is one of those I've wanted to do for a long time now.
In this week's episode, we're sitting down with our IT superstars in three studios around the world to discuss their work keeping five studios around the world talking to each other and making the distributed worldwide development of Star Citizen and Squadron 42 possible.  From branching build systems to publishing technologies, from major on-site presentations like CitizenCon to the day-to-day workstations and every piece of software used to make the game, these are the people that make gamedev possible, and I'm excited to give them their chance to meet you LIVE this Friday.
As usual, this thread will be your chance to post questions and vote on which ones you want to see addressed most.
We'll see you on Friday at 10am Pacific.
