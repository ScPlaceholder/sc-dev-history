As for the delay ... it has to do with the weapon systems itself. Countermeasure launchers are technically weapons with special projectiles that cannot be handled by the projectile manager yet. Especially with the noise fields it is imperative that they spawn for all players at the same time at the same spot so they can be accurately "placed" in the world so that ships can hide in them. So that's why there is always a delay ... your fire signal needs to be sent up to the server, executed and then replicated so that the outcome is deterministic on all clients. Otherwise we'd need to spawn and then teleport them around which would frankly look laughable.
Since the decoys are actually only important for the server (as it owns the missile tracking) were thinking of directly spawning them on the client without delay so it feels a bit better. They would logically be in different positions on server and client but it wouldn't matter because their positions are only relevant on the server. We might do that though for 3.13. It's just one of these things that came up pretty late in the 3.12 process and we just do not want to rush it out without further testing as it could have sever performance penalties during local spawning.
Decoys and noise fields have constant signature values but those values are always dependent on the distance to the receiver. In 3.12 you won't really see the signature of decoys yet. We would have loved to include more UI tools but the UI team is very busy with the general HUD development so sadly there was no room for it. There will definitely be more UI in the future that will allow you to make better decisions on how many decoys to drop.
