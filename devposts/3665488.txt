Yes in Star Citizen almost any axis can be assigned to a button as well. In 3.12 the button for the space brake is still "X" and it acts as before ... but if you assign it to an axis you have the ability to scale the deceleration. So if you hold it at 50%, only 50% of the available thrust will be used to get you on a stop. It is quite nice for soft decoupled landings.
