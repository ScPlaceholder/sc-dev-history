There are some good replies above which I think pretty much answer your question (as much as is possible anyway). In general we’re increasingly seeing better scaling over many cores, but overall framerate is still usually limited by the remaining single/threaded work on our “main“ thread. We’re already at a point with Gen12 where the render thread is generally no longer a bottleneck, and while improvements there will ease the overall load on the system (which can help the main thread run faster) we’re still going to see the biggest gains from removing/parallelising that main thread work. This is an on going and complex task, but great strides were made in 3.17 and will continue to be improved upon throughout the year. The combined result of a slimmer main thread and heavily parallelised render submission and other systems (game logic, physics etc) should hopefully result in our multi core performance improving significantly. But predicting performance on even a fixed hardware target would be near impossible, let alone for PCs with all the multitude of variations.
