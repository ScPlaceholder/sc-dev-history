Okay ... so the space brake will still apply an acceleration which is opposite of your current velocity vector to bring you to a stop. It will still enable AB (at least in our dev builds atm) but you can now smoothly scale that opposite acceleration. That's pretty much it. It's quite nice e.g. during landings when you want to feather your approach a bit.
When I speak of an axis I mean an input axis on your controller. Suitable axes are pedals, the triggers on gamepads or things like the brake lever on russian-style HOTAS grips.
