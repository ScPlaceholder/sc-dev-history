For Alpha 3.14, we brought the Constellations in line with each other, unifying their shared parts. With this change, we can optimize aspects once, and then all versions benefit from the change; plus, we have also made sure that they are now all working with the new tint system.
We have also updated the Constellation Phoenix Emerald paint with this transition, as the former version was less "emerald" than the Stella Fortuna (St. Patrick's Day) editions of the Ursa Rover and the Archimedes, which are both predominantly emerald with small accents. The new version matches better to the other Stella Fortuna vehicles, with green being its primary color.
