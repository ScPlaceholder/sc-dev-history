I want to inform you about our recent changes that will come with the next 3.19.1 PTU patch.
We revisited the explosion parameters per resource and the global rules for those explosions on each rock type.
Additionally we buffed the health values for all mining items.
That means there are some bigger changes coming your way, with the biggest being:
The UI now correctly displays the blast radius of the minable you are facing and it updates based on the Danger Pool (the red zone)
This should make it more predictable for you and should help you react accordingly.
On the balancing side we adjusted the values, that certain resources are more prone to damage you or your ship and might even have a bigger blast radius.
This was our intention from the beginning but it sadly did not make it for the 3.19.0 release. The intention being giving the "Shatterdamage Modifier" a proper meaning in your Mining Head Loadout.
Cheers
PS: For those asking about the instability issues for the mining, we have a fix in place but want to test it further. Hopefully will make it in the next patch.
