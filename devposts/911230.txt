Just as a follow up, we know combat mechanics are not finished and that there are issues that make commenting on it difficult.  One of the purposes of this survey is to gauge confidence in the assessment and what issues/bugs are the most egregious.  If the confidence is low, we can look at why and in what situations.  If you can't kill your target because you are fighting your ship's ESP, select that as a major bug and put a low confidence level.  If you are unable to modify your guns, select REC/UEC weapons as a major bug and default load-outs as a major issue (note: this gets at the experience of those starting out).  All of that is valuable information.  Additionally, we can repeat this survey if needed allowing patch by patch comparisons.
In other words there is room in this survey to prioritize issues and assess confidence in answers in addition to looking at balance. That can be just as important and useful to us as the balance itself.
