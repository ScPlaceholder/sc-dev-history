For anyone that might not have heard from me before, part of what I do is keeping track of error rates that players are running into and making sure that the issues are being looked at. Errors like this show up on graphs that I look at on a daily ( sometimes hourly) basis and I call attention to anything that needs being looked at. Often times with errors, there are multiple issues that can cause the same error number to appear. Thus far, there have been multiple fixes for the issues causing 40014 errors. Some of them allowed some accounts to be able to play the game while others still can't.
It's frustrating to me to see players that aren't able to play the game. However, with development for new and complex features, things often get worse before they get better with the end result being a better overall experience once the problems have been ironed out. Issues for 3.18 are still being worked on including the 40014 errors and we're continuing to find improvements and fixes that we can make. Some of these aren't able to be hotfixed and need a proper full build to be released.
