it makes use of a low-res unique baked normal map to define the large shape normals. this tends to be a lot more consistent between LOD stages compared to just meshes with vertex normals and tiling textures. since every LOD requires a reduction in vertex # the normals across the whole mesh can change fairly drastically and even transferring normals from LOD0 to the lower ones only helps to some extent(somtimes it's also worse :D) which leads to very obvious shading changes.
