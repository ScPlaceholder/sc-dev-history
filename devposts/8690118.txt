Greetings!
Crafting Fabricators have been added into 4.7, which allows you to craft a select amount of armors, fps weapons, ship weapons, backpacks, and more. These are crafted with Blueprints by utilizing materials you add to the fabricator. This implementation includes the ability to craft and queue items, dismantle crafted items, and more!
A feedback survey is available at: Fabricator Crafting System Survey Form. This survey will be open until March 24.
We look forward to reading your feedback!
