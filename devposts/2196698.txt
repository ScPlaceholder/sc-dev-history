Hey all,
We addressed this the other day, so we have some changes that should hopefully make it into the 3.6.0 release :slightly_smiling_face:
We have turned down the volume of the combat music, as this was definitely too loud when compared to the rest of the music and the wider game mix. I'm also very excited about another option we've included for you all which is the option to 'Disable Combat Music', for those who find combat music in general a little jarring and would rather only the ambient music played. This works by capping the intensity of the music logic system, so combat music can't trigger, which is my personal preference :slightly_smiling_face:
I'm excited for you to all try out the game with these music tweaks!
