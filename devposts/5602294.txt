I can finally say with certainty that the first version of Gen12 will be in 3.18.
There were a few GPU crashes we were facing and most likely there are still a few left somewhere but it's stable enough to release for 3.18.
Friendly reminder to add meaningful repro steps in case you still get some crashes which help us devs enormously to understand where a crash might be coming from.
We still have the whole legacy pipeline included in 3.18 as a safety fallback, but with Gen12 now on for everyone it does mean we can finally nuke the 20 year old legacy CryEngine renderer.
Let me emphasize again that we didn't spent any time on optimizing it yet and there are still a few things left after 3.18 but it's looking good so far and it will be a tremendous relief for us not having to support two rendering paths anymore.
Please watch my update video a while back in Inside Star Citizen for more information on Gen12 performance: https://youtu.be/-0cIaf3mPLw?t=368
So next step will be to finish off Gen12, optimize it and  add Vulkan support for all of you guys and something else i'm personally looking forward to the most :wink:
2023 will be very exciting for the Engine/Graphics Team!
