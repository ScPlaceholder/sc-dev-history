## Feature Updates
Siege of Orison & Instancing Performance
Completed Siege of Orison instances are now marked for immediate cleanup once all players leave, rather than waiting for the full TTL to expire; this frees up server resources faster after mission completion
Further Geometry, material, and general performance polish
## Bug Fixes & Technical
- Fixed multiple Server Crashes
- Backend Update: Fixed an issue with entitlement auto-delivery failing silently when the server connection was never established; delivery now retries correctly and failed batches are reported as errors rather than successes
- Backend Update: Mission system now waits for the Remote World Entity System to be ready before syncing player state, preventing mission state mismatches on login
- Potential Fix: Mendo Ren becoming unkillable during the final phase of the Siege of Orison boss fight due to an AI mission flow issue blocking her death state from triggering
- Potential Fix: Siege of Orison mission not automatically accepting when players entered the instance, requiring manual mobiGlas acceptance to begin
- Potential Fix: Siege of Orison AI enemies missing and the location not resetting correctly when repeating the mission after a previous completion
- Potential Fix: ARGO ATLS IKTI getting stuck underground after falling from a great height
- Potential Fix: Context popup window in the inventory appearing offset on widescreen resolutions due to incorrect UI scaling
