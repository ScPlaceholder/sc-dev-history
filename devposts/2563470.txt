Mostly a CPU cost issue with the engine. We have a super efficient system for managing huge numbers of objects, but at some point just doing ANYTHING near a hundred thousand times becomes expensive. There are ways to solve this, but they require a bit of a rethink about how we spawn assets, and rather than spawning 1 million trees and figuring out how to LOD them we need to take the same approach as the V4 terrain and build the system in such a way that we know exactly what the forest looks like from space first, and the individual trees are later positioned to match that larger scale pattern. You could say there are similarities with MSFlight as I believe it starts from satellite data and tries to figure out where to spawn individual trees from that (though I clearly don’t know their inner workings and I’m presuming a lot).
