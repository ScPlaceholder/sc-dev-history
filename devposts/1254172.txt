The basis of this confusion comes from what Rob meant by "in-game" time.
Many people are thinking he was referring to "played" time.
He was not.
When Rob was speaking of "in-game" time, he was referring to the currently accelerated clock of the persistent universe, where a moon currently rotates about eight times in a real world day.
In this week's Calling All Devs, Rob said it was real time, clarified again it was real time (after mistakenly saying game and then correcting himself to real) before making his "vacation" comment.
The "vacation" comment that is confusing the issue was intended to illustrate that real time is better than the accelerated in-game time because it is currently drastically sped up, meaning that if we used the in-game clock a vacation would penalize you eight times faster in the countdown of your insurance.
Where we fell down this week was in not properly explaining that at no point were we discussing played time.  He was discussing the advantage (and simplicity in tracking) of using the real world clock as opposed to the accelerated in-universe (referred to unclearly as in-game) clock.
