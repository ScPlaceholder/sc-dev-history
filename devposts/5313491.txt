There are a few problems in the aiming system which are very visible with the Blade. The main issue is that the ship has a very eccentric hull shape which puts the center of mass almost outside of the ship body. That in turn however is used for the aim prediction. We have already a ticket to not use just the physical center of mass but optionally using an offset point where it feels necessary. For the time being you can use sub-targeting (press 8 and you might get more precise impacts fighting the Blade.
Concerning the PIPs ... we're currently updating the aiming systems and gunnery gameplay, part of that will also be investigating how to make the PIPs less prone to network jitter.
