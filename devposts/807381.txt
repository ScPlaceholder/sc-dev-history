I don't think it would be much work, but it's still probably some work. We do a fair amount of colour remapping and postprocessing to turn the raw linear brightness into something that looks good in sRGB, but HDR displays aren't so wide-ranged that it's as simple as turning all that off and expecting the results to still look good. So we'd need some tech time and art direction to pin down the look of the game on HDR. That said, one upside of our recent switch to the ACES tonemapper is that (as I understand it) there's a standard, or at least plenty of literature, for how that ought to interact with HDR displays.
