All bugs are getting prioritized by how much they affect the player experience.
We have several classifications: Blocker, Critical, High, Medium, Trivial.
Blocker and Critical bugs are getting worked on as soon as possible.
High bugs are also being worked on fairly quickly.
However Medium and Trivial bugs tend to stay in the backlog for a while since there is just so much other - more important - stuff to fix or to do.
Also sometimes bugs will go away automatically if a system gets rewritten in the future so it's simply not worth fixing right now.
And then there is obviously a different amount of workload teams have. If there are too many issues with one system there isn't simply time to get through all them.
Game development is always a fine line between fixing bugs and making progress.
We want the game to be as stable as possible but at the same time we need to make progress.
But progress means we have to change things and that breaks other stuff again. Something unavoidable.
This is the nature of a live environment where the fundamentals are still being worked on while you can already use it.
