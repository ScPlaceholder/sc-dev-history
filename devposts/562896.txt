It's really not that difficult to learn to produce the differences in pitch if you model your pronunciation on individual words that you learn from another (good) speaker. We have similar distinctions in English with the word "desert" vs. "dessert." The harder thing is to learn to HEAR the differences from other speakers. However, if you practice phrases like «kua e k,ua» ("five opinions") where the first kua [KWA (opinion)] is neutral and the second is at a low pitch, your brain is likely to do a better job of latching on to the fundamentals.
