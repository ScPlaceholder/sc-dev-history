## Welcome to the 'Foundation Festival 2025 - Kindness & Comradery Screenshot Contest'!
Capture meaningful moments of teamwork and support, whether you're guiding a new player, teaching a new skill, or reviving a teammate after a tough mission. Share your best screenshot that highlights the spirit of teamwork at the heart of the Foundation Festival.
## Before You Begin
Before you start on your submissions, please read the contest rules carefully and remember to post your entries to the Foundation Festival 2025 event page on the Community Hub and not as a reply to this thread.
:exclamation: NOTE: Submissions should include 'Foundation Festival 2025 - Screenshot Contest' in the post title. :exclamation:
## Keep In Mind
- Read the contest rules!
- Only posts submitted to the Community Hub will be considered.
- Screenshots must be uploaded to a single post on the Foundation Festival 2025 event page, have the title 'Foundation Festival 2025 - Screenshot Contest', and have the screenshot category selected to be counted as an entry.
- Each screenshot uploaded to the Community Hub following the aforementioned 2 rules will count as a single entry. Multiple entries are allowed, and multiple images can be included in a single post.
- Post-processing effects are NOT allowed. This includes third-party add-ons like Photoshop, Reshade, etc.
- Screenshots must feature an image that captures moments of teamwork and support. Any other subject will NOT be considered..
- Up to three collaborators or contributors of each entry will be eligible to win a prize, which should clearly state the handles of all those involved in the description section of the submission post.
- Players can only win 1 prize.
## Winning Conditions
The CIG jury will judge your submissions on:
- Quality
- Creativity
- Rule adherence
- Aesthetic composition
## Dates
The submission period will end on Monday, 14 July 2025, 20:00 UTC.
