## Breaking News: Frontier Fakers
The Stanton system has been left in political and emotional turmoil this week after a shocking revelation of the Slicers’ true identity. The terrorist gang, responsible for last year’s devastating pirate attacks and the deaths of thousands of citizens, have been newly identified as the Frontier Fighters, an extremist faction known for their militaristic values and anti-Pyro rhetoric.
Now, with growing resources at the Frontier Fighters' fingertips, many are fearful of more targeted attacks and more Stanton lives being lost in the crossfire.
## Supply or Die
Dive into the chaos of the Pyro system with Supply or Die, the next chapter in the battle for control after Save Stanton and Fight for Pyro.
The Frontier Fighters have dropped the act and shown their true Slicer colors. Now it’s on you to pick a side: stand with the relentless Head Hunters or back the resourceful Citizens for Prosperity.
No supplies, no victory – every smart pilot knows that. Your faction needs resources, and it’s up to you to deliver. The Slicers didn’t pick the name FRONTIER Fighters for nothing – in Pyro, might makes right. Your employers? They don’t care how you get the goods, just get them. Will you be the noble trader running convoys with trusted private military escorts (we see you, orgs that thrive on logistics and security!), or will you channel your inner pirate, ambushing haulers and taking what you want? Or maybe you’ll gamble it all, weaving through danger with nothing but luck and skill on your cargo runs? Or, if you prefer the steady thrum of a drill to the chaos of a skirmish, consider mining and salvaging.
It’s your call. Plunge into the action, give it everything you’ve got – solo or with your crew – and the three unique rewards will make it all worthwhile.
Citizens for Prosperity
Following the brutal attack on Pyro and our efforts to combat the vigilante group the Frontier Fighters, our strategic reserves across the system are at an all-time low. Citizens for Prosperity are eager to replenish our stocks before we reach a point of desperation.
Mission A - Strategic Detatrine Reserve
This rare chemical compound is crucial for making vital medical supplies. After using our stock to treat injuries from the Frontier Fighter assault, it's important to replenish it. Normally, we'd recommend reliable sources for buying detatrine, but it's currently being sold at a significantly reduced price at several depots, likely attracting unwanted attention. If you pursue this deal, please be cautious
Mission B - Strategic Resource Reserve
I leave it up to you to decide how best to procure and refine the resources, but the asteroid clusters near LaGrange points might be a good place to start mining.
Mission C - Strategic Salvage Reserve
I leave it up to you to decide how best to acquire what we need, but should be able to find some wrecks to scavenge orbiting around most planets.
Headhunters
Kicking the crap out of those Frontier Fighter bastards seriously depleted our supplies and now our stash is practically out of stock.
Mission A - Out of Detatrine
The chemical is essential for making our sludge, and we can't afford to stop sales now, given our urgent need for credits. Currently, several depots are offering detatrine at rock-bottom prices, though the reason is unclear. Expect fierce competition as everyone is trying to acquire it.
Mission B - Out of Resources
Up to you how you get the stuff. Mine it, steal it, buy it from a trader - I don’t care as long as I get what I need refined and delivered. Might be worth checking the asteroid clusters near LaGrange points to start.
Mission C - Out of Salvage
Scrap it yourself or take it off some unsuspecting scav. Should be able to find some wrecks orbiting around most planets. Your call. Either way, as soon as we get the supplies, we’ll put it to good use building back up everything we lost.
Keep an eye on your Contracts Manager in the Priority tab to pick up missions available across both the Stanton and Pyro systems. Whether you're flying solo or with a crew, you'll find missions with supplies required in three different sizes—small, medium, and large—ensuring you can choose the perfect mission to match your crew size and capabilities. Additionally, these missions can be shared.
You can track your progress in a Journal Entry.
