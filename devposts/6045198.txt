## [Update!] APPLICATIONS ARE NOW CLOSED
Due to the major increase in attendees for this year's event, we're now requiring those interested in participating in the Cosplay Contest to submit an application before October 1st to ensure quality and variety among accepted participants.
Application Information can be found here:  https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/1/thread/2953-citizencon-cosplay-contest-application-1
Crafty Citizens! It is time to dust off the ole' supply bin, warm up those glue guns, and thread those sewing machines, because the CitizenCon Cosplay Contest is coming back in person to this year's event on October 21st & 22nd, in Los Angeles, California!
It's been a little while since we hosted a cosplay contest IRL, and we're very much looking forward to being dazzled by your creativity as you compete with other citizens on stage to earn valuable prizes.
Since we know that building cosplays can take time, we wanted to give you as much advanced notice as possible to get the creative juices flowing. If you're planning to attend CitizenCon 2953 and are interested in participating in this contest, make sure to keep an eye on this post, as we'll be updating it with additional information as we get closer to the event.
Judging criteria will be based on
- Creativity
- Craftmanship
- Stage Presence
Prizing:
1st: Tobii Eye Tracker 5 + Carrack
2nd: Tobii Eye Tracker 5 + 600i explorer
3rd: Tobii Eye Tracker 5 + A1 Spirit
Here's what you need to know right now:
- To be considered for participation, your cosplay must be related to either Star Citizen or Squadron 42
- All participants will be required to be physically present at CitizenCon, as the contest will be held on stage during the event.
- Weapons and anything resembling a weapon are not allowed inside the venue. This includes but is not limited to guns, knives, and even medpens.
- You are allowed to cosplay as a group. However, please consider that any physical prizes may not be extended to accommodate each group member.
- Previous costumes entered in CitizenCon Cosplay Contests will not be eligible. You will need to make a new and unique cosplay to participate in this year's Cosplay Contest.
If you have any questions feel free to comment below. Otherwise, stay tuned for additional updates!
