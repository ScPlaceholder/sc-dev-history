Story time.
We skipped Foundation Festival in 2021, for a number of reasons. The related patch was taking longer than expected and then we had a number of critical bugs that prevented us from launching the event. We made the difficult decision to cancel the event in 2021 for these reasons. However, we had already had these paints (and the character wearables) made to offer as rewards, so we've had them in our back pocket ever since.
Fast forward to 2022, both refueling and medical gameplay have been introduced, which did not exist when we initially made these ship paints. Unfortunately, the team does not have the bandwidth in their schedule to make a refueling/medical paint, so we had to get creative. Luckily, the Cutlass paint actually works for all variants, so we're covered for medical since the paint can be applied to the Cutlass Red. Then there's refueling. In short, yes we're aware it's not the best match, but it's the best we could do with the current schedule. For you refuelers out there, fear not, I'm confident we'll get a Starfarer paint for next time.
