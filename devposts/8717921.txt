In general, you do not need a lot of testers to uncover problems. Trends (be positive or negative) can easily identified with often just a handful of players and we usually have over a hundred in every test. Also it is not true that anything is set in stone, we've been constantly adapting the FM, updated the tuning, etc. ... you might disagree with certain design or tuning choices but usually they are done because they overall are better for the game.
