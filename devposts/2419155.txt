We have added personal inventory at a capacity relative to the torso armor a player is wearing. Harvestables and FPS mineable crystals can be stored in inventory and sold directly at commodity kiosks. Additionally, players have the option of generating a carriable box that can then be stored within a ship's cargo grid and sold at commodity kiosks. Personal inventory can be activated by using interaction mode while on foot and selecting the inventory "dot" as well as via a hotkey "i".
Let us know how this system is working for you!
