It's been ages since I was on the QA team so I don't think I'd be able to accurately answer that for the current state of CIG but I can go over some of the stuff I do know.
I started working here back when the Austin QA team was 5 people in a tiny room ( if you can find some of the which glitch episodes from our old shows, I'm the one with the beard). Depending on how someone defines an MMO, this is my 3rd or 4th mmo that I've worked on. At one of those prior jobs, I was QA specialist.
I introduced how QA specialists work to QA management here. The goal of having a specialist QA system is to facilitate better communication to QA leadership.  This is a necessity because an MMO is too big for anyone to be an expert on the entire state of the game. So to  make communication about the game health easier, the QA team will have testers that are assigned to be experts in specific areas of the game and have a close relationship with the developers responsible for their designated area. This sets things up such that if a QA lead needs to know something about a specific area of the game, they can ask the QA specialist.
