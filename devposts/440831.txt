We're still collecting all the great photos from the week, and we'd love yours too if you have them!  Otherwise, here's a rundown of the week in Cologne.
Wednesday started the week with a bang, with over 90 of us at the friendly Brauhaus Ohne Namen. The atmosphere was that of friends meeting up from around the world after a year apart.  Lots of stories swapped, ideas shared and great interaction between those there, including some of the staff, the streamers and volunteers.
Thursday night had another great turnout at the Brauhaus, though less of the team there as setup at Gloria lasted late into the night.
Even as the first backers were queuing at Gloria on Friday, there were still meetups happening around town like this great meetup at the Hyatt.
We finished the week with our traditional meetup at the Hyatt Beer Garden.  Wonderfully relaxed and casual with plenty of time for the team to spend an afternoon swapping stories with the community (and late into the evening for some).
On a final note, our thanks to all the backers who wished us well all throughout the week, even including our flights home!
