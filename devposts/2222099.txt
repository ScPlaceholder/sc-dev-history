This is indicative of everything we show on ISC each week.
By the show's mandate, nothing we ever showcase is finished, complete or entirely working as intended. This is one of the reasons we not only flag all footage as "work-in-progress" but often times don't showcase something for weeks or months later. It's a delicate balance because we want to inform the community on what's going on, and of course we want to show you the thing we're talking about as well as we can, but often times (and this case was no exception) that means the footage you're seeing isn't entirely representative of what we hope you'll see in the live game environment. That's what work-in-progress means.
The only alternative to showing you work-in-progress that is often buggy or incomplete is to wait until everything is finished, completed and working entirely as intended, and THEN show you like most other studios. But that would mean no weekly (or even monthly) show, and nobody wants that. :stuck_out_tongue_winking_eye:
This is my possibly overly-verbose way of asking everyone reading this to remind everyone else that isn't that when you see something in ISC each week, the intention we're working to achieve is just as important as the literal representation we can present at any given time as a "work-in-progress."  Because WIP is just that, and the only alternative would be not showing anything at all.
