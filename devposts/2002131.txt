Greetings,
We have made some pretty extensive changes to Star Marine including reducing the sizes or significantly altering some of the maps, reducing player cap sizes (elimination), and completely overhauling the capture mechanics. The goal of the changes to elimination is to increase the likelihood of encountering another player and reduce long stretches of no action. For the capture changes, the goal is to make the entire mechanic more dynamic with longer and more team interactive fights around the control points. Details can be found in the Feature Updates - Star Marine section of the patch notes.
We would love to know what you think, what's working and what isn't. Let us know below and upvote shared sentiment!
