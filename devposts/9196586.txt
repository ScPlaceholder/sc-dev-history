Greetings!
The Skarnov rifle, like the other LBCO weapons leaves the target with an electrified pulse that denotates and chains to other nearby targets causing devastation for enemies in tight proximity. It's got a really consistent but high vertical recoil profile allowing skilled operators to shut down highly armoured enemies easily but as an energy weapon the danger of over heating is present. Additionally, the skarnov is effected by the atmospheric temperatures its used in, causing hot environments to cause overheat faster and colder environments to build up heat slower.
For PTU phases, the weapon and ammo can be found in the following locations:
- Area18 - Cubby Blast
- GrimHex - Skutters
- Levski - Conscientious Objects
- Checkmate - Weapon Shop
