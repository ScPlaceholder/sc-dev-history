As we advance towards a more persistent economy, necessary adjustments are being implemented to ensure a balanced and immersive experience for all players. Our goal is to create a stable and engaging economic environment that supports smoother and fairer progression.
We understand that many of you are accustomed to the Alpha phase economy, where periodic wipes reset everything, including aUEC and ships. However, with the shift towards persistence, player finances will evolve, reflecting a more enduring economic system.
These changes might require some tweaking, but they are crucial for the long-term health and stability of the game's economy. While specific profit targets will remain fluid as we continue to refine the system, our overarching aim is to create an enjoyable and balanced experience for everyone.
