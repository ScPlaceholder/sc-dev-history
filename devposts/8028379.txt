The bug for the specific situation that this thread was made for is one of the highest priorities we can give to an issue, but things having a high priority doesn't mean that we're going to be able to find a fix for it quickly. It may end up needing a lengthy investigation before a solid fix for it is found. Similarly, while the issues that you called out in your response are different from the one this thread was made for, they are still incredibly frustrating and important.  Many of them are separate issues that would all need their own investigations and in some cases, may be multiple issues that all have the same symptom. In situations where it may feel like the same things may be breaking with the game with each patch, think of it more like going to the doctor for a cough. The end result may look the same externally but a lot of the time from patch to patch, the actual underlying cause is different and needs its own fix.
