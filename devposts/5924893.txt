Invictus Launch Week, popularly referred to as Fleet Week, is the period that falls between Emancipation Day and Armistice Day, marking the beginning of the new enlistment year for the United Empire of Earth’s Navy (UEEN).
From May 19-30, the UEE Naval Fleet returns to the Stanton System and Area18 as ArcCorp hosts the largest exhibition of military vehicles in history. Head to the Bevic Convention Center when you arrive at the Riker Memorial Spaceport in Area 18. Welcome back, all you proud Navy recruits; we salute you!
During Invictus Launch Week, you can get a taste of what it’s like to join the Navy, as all manner of military-inspired vehicles can be tested for free. Experience new releases, rediscover old favorites, tour an in-service Javelin destroyer, and more.
In honor of Invictus Launch Week, we’re launching a Free Fly event where everyone can download and play Star Citizen for free!
