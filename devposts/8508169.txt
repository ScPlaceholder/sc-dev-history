We still have a software mode because full-RT GI eats a lot of performance even if a card nominally supports ray-tracing, and we want GI on even at min-spec so that the visuals are similar. But because it's a performance mode, it's a lot different from Lumen and focuses on performance over quality. We're also experimenting with some middle-range options, like doing the diffuse lighting with live GI but the specular reflections from cube-maps.
