Hi, regarding instancing and scattering on a planetary scale, the system covering the planetary surface outside landing zones is an extension of the same procedural ecosystem tech we use for all planets, with the addition of some extra features, a different setup combined with a good amount of artists time spent in tweaking the objects presets generation values and building nice modular assets. The instancing is helping a lot  here, but underneath is the same system used for scattering large amount of rocks etc. all over the moons (pushed to the extreme on this planet type...). We were not using dx12 or Vulkan for this. Thank you again for the positive feedback and thanks to everyone that was at CitizenCon yesterday :slightly_smiling_face:
