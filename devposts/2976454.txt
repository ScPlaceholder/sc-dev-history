Hello everyone,
We’re looking for great community-made videos to help new players discover the wonders of the ‘verse!
The Star Citizen universe is vast and gives everyone the opportunity to be whoever they want to be. While getting started can be a bit overwhelming, we’re always aiming to soften the learning curve and help new players prepare for their lives among the stars. Last year, we introduced the Guide System and the Welcome Hub, two initiatives to help new players take their first steps into the ‘verse.
The freshly released Star Citizen Alpha 3.9 brings a lot of new content, such as the Player Status system, reworked Friends List, and the Klescher automated prisons, and changes some of the existing mechanics of the game. Because of this, we’re currently looking for helpful, community-made tutorials to add to the Watch section of the Welcome Hub. In this section, new players can discover the beauty of the universe at their own pace, one video after another.
So, if you’ve found an up-to-date tutorial that you think deserves a spot, or if you’ve created a great tutorial yourself, let us know! We’re always on the lookout for excellent tutorials to help today’s rookies become your wingmen of tomorrow.
The topics for the tutorial section are not set in stone and will get updated on a regular basis to always highlight the most helpful and recent contributions of the community.
