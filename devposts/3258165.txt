@dengo, what you describe is in general correct, but we cannot do any more changes on that for 3.10.0 as it is way too late in the dev cycle for that. We have talked internally about having more flight modes in the past, but the general consensus is that we want to avoid adding more complexity if it can avoided. That does not mean though that we won't take the content of this thread into account.
As you might know from the vehicle related ISC and SCL videos we have a tight focus to improve the combat and flight experience. As such the FM is definitely NOT finalized yet, especially in space. While 3.10.0 is a solid step forward in terms of aerodynamics, targeting and gunnery we are definitely not where want to be with general FM (and combat) balance. These topics are the focus of the upcoming quarters. So whether or not (or how) we will allow that specific 3.9 behaviour again will depend on the outcome of that work.
