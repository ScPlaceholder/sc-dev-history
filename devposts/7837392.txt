With the recent advancements in our server technology, especially with the arrival of Server Meshing, we're planning to reassess our approach to the VOIP backend. The Audio team recognizes the need for a more structured and flexible communication system in-game.
Currently, VOIP supports around 50 players per server, a limit that has become increasingly restrictive as server capacity grows. Our future backend update should therefore improve audio stability and quality. We have no date to share with you yet, but I can confirm it's in the works and we'll keep you updated when we have news to share!
