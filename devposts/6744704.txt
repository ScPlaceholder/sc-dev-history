Greetings,
In 3.23 we have introduced a new screen for looting critical items quickly, allowing for more reliable inventory management in tense situations. With this update players can rapidly transfer items to their weapon, magazine, consumables, and throwables slots or transfer all into their backpack. Selecting the "loot" option on a crate will bring up the new FPS looting interface while selecting "open" will bring up the original inventory window. You can also open this window by holding down the default inventory keybind, "I".
Give this updated FPS Loot Screen a try and let us know what you think below. We are specifically interesting in hearing about:
- What is your favourite part of the new Looting UI? Least favourite?
- What information if any is missing on the UI?
- Can you describe the looting flow in your own words? Is this what you were expecting/wanting out of a looting system?
- How often have you encountered the new looting screen in normal gameplay?
Looking forward to hearing from you!
