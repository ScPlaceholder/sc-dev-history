Greetings,
3.23 brings the full Squadron 42 EVA implementation into Star citizen for the first time. This greatly improves the EVA system by adding much needed control, animations, game-play, and finesse for players traversing zero-g from outside their ships.
The first thing players will notice is that they will no longer be vertical while maneuvering and instead be in more of a superhero pose. This pose gives much more flexibility with player maneuverability, rotations, and viewing angles while also feeling much more natural and overall more aesthetically pleasing to view!  Players will also now have a limited EVA fuel in their suits which makes planning trips into zero-g much more necessary.
Give these changes a try and let us know what you think below! We are specifically interesting in hearing about:
- What is your favourite change introduced in EVA T2? Least favourite?
- Does changing from EVA to a ship feel smooth and intuitive? Ship to EVA?
- How does the maneuverability feel compared to the previous EVA system?
- General thoughts on the new EVA Fuel System & Consumption rates.
Looking forward to hearing from you!
