## Bug Fixes and Technical Updates
Alpha 4.10.1 closes 37 issues, with a focus on problems that could cost you an entire session. These include contract cargo spawning in the wrong location, vehicles that couldn’t be retrieved from ASOP terminals, freight elevators getting stuck mid-transfer, and multiple exploits.
16 of those 37 fixes came through the Issue Council, and each one includes a link to the original report. Your reports, confirmations, and reproduction steps help us reliably recreate issues and prioritize them alongside everything else.
Many of the fixes in this patch were pinned down thanks to players providing detailed information from different angles. Thank you to everyone who took the time to contribute!
