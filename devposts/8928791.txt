Hi folks,
Quick update on several issues we've been tracking. Many are in progress, but here are the freshest ones:
## :white_check_mark: Hotfixed to Live recently
- Some ships could not be claimed through "Claim Insured Loadout"
- The mobiGlas Starmap "Route" button had an incorrect interaction area. In cases where the button itself was not responsive, the clickable area was shifted slightly to the right. You could still reliably use [R] to set routes.
- Docking issues preventing both manual and automatic docking at certain stations have also been hotfixed to Live.
## :white_check_mark: Fixed
- (May 21, reminder) Purchased pledge items not appearing in-game should now be resolved with the new Individual Claim Mode: Freight elevators now include a new "Claim Mode" button at your home location. This allows you to reclaim platform-owned ship items such as paints and gear individually back to your home inventory. Previously owned instances of claimed items will be invalidated automatically. For easier navigation, there is also an "Exclude Paints" filter option available. If you are still missing pledged items after the update, please perform a Character Repair and let us know if the issue persists afterward. We continue to monitor this one.
## :hammer_and_wrench: Reproduced and In Progress
- Scanning and targeting currently don't function correctly when the Command Module is docked to a host vehicle. Scanning is failing to return signatures for vehicles and objects in this state. The team has successfully reproduced the issue and work is ongoing.
## :mag: Investigating
- We are actively investigating your reports regarding lost Personal Hangars. We know how disruptive this issue is and are currently investigating solutions, though it may not make the 4.8 release window. We'll keep you in the loop.
## :hourglass_flowing_sand: Fixed, Awaiting Implementation
- An issue causing the Syulen interaction buttons to become difficult or impossible to press has been fixed two hours ago and is currently awaiting implementation into an upcoming build.
- Regarding duplication exploits, this remains an active area of focus for the teams. Several fixes have already gone out, and we greatly appreciate everyone continuing to report these issues through the Issue Council or directly to us. We are actively tracking and working on additional exploits fixes as well.
We'll have more news soon as we continue to hammer the issues you have been reporting recently.
Cheers,
