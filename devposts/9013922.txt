## Star Citizen Alpha Patch 4.9
Alpha Patch 4.9 has been released and is now available to test on the PTU environment! Patch should now show: VERSION 4.9.0-PTU.12170061
- Audience: All Waves
- Server Info: PTU Channel - US Only
- Long Term Persistence: Enabled
Testing/Feedback Focus
- Stability, Bugfixes, LTP between PTU releases
- Recco Battaglia Repeatable Missions (reputation sped up for tonight)
- Vehicle Hit Marker Updates
- Behring CQ7 Bullpup Rifle
- Vehicle Cleanup Updates at New Babbage/Lorville and Stanton/Pyro Stations
Known Issues
- PU - Comms / Audio / VoIP - Proximity VOIP does not take over and party VOIP stops working at 60m meters and below
- Destroy Narcotics missions disabled temporarily due to a structural refactor
- PU - Ship Battles - Missions / Locations - Escort - Hangar doors are not opening for the ship that needs to be escorted
- PU - Ship Battles V2 - EscortShips - Mission Content / AI - Escorted ship does not Quantum Travel away or Go to the Hangar after neutralizing the AI ships in the mission location
- PU - Missions - Bounty Hunter / Mission Content - When killing the Neutralize target the mission does not progress
- PU - Commodity Kiosks - Multiple Locations - "Failed" error appears when buying RMC from Commodity Kiosks
- Multi-System - PU - Locations / Inventory - There's a chance that their primary residence does not contain their items' (Workaround: Recopy account if you need your default local items)
## Features & Gameplay
Gameplay
- Ship mining laser power has been increased across the board by about 20%
- Added Behring CQ7 Bullpup Rifle and ammo to shops across multiple locations
- Tractor beams can now be used inside armistice zones at Distribution Centers
- Ship Attenuation and Flyby Audio playback rules adjusted across all ships for better audio falloff at range along with flyby audio mix polish
- Added updated cleanup code for abandoned ships in Lorville landing zone
