## What's been going on?
Stanton plunged into chaos when a coordinated wave of pirate attacks struck the system. You stepped in during Save Stanton, choosing to assist either Citizens for Prosperity or Frontier Fighters. You fought off raids, recovered stolen cargo, and traced the violence back to a gang calling themselves the Slicers.
The war then shifted to Pyro. In Fight for Pyro, Frontier Fighters launched an independent campaign against Pyro’s citizens, convinced that the only way to prevent future strife is through the destruction of Pyro's civilization. You picked a side: Citizens for Prosperity, Headhunters, or Frontier Fighters. Missions tested your loyalty, the conflict escalated, and the shocking truth was revealed - the Slicers were actually Frontier Fighters in disguise, spreading chaos to justify their extremist agenda.
In Supply or Die, the war entered a new phase focused on control through logistics and resources. With Frontier Fighters’ deception exposed, every faction scrambled to rebuild its strength. You were tasked with mining, salvaging, trading, and raiding to secure vital supplies for your cause. The fight was no longer about domination through firepower but survival through strategy, as the fate of Pyro now depended on who could keep their war machine running longest.
## Frontier Fighters Finale
Amelia Boyd, the leader of Frontier Fighters, has been spotted in Stanton, her movements coinciding with a surge in Frontier Fighters activity across both Stanton and Pyro, leading to speculation that another attack may be imminent.
In response, Citizens for Prosperity and Headhunters have mobilized for the Frontier Fighters Finale, aiming to take down Boyd and her forces once and for all.

The two factions are escalating their efforts against Frontier Fighters. Operations now focus on disabling the enemy’s ability to regen by destroying MedGel recharge stations and intercepting targets along flight routes. Review contract details carefully to assess the level of risk and resistance before engaging.

Disable MedGel Recharge Station
We discovered Frontier Fighters' MedGel recharge stations used to regenerate their forces. Travel to the reported outpost, locate and destroy all recharge stations inside. The recon team reports additional activity in the area, so be prepared for a fight.

We’ve tracked a routine Frontier Fighters flight path that passes through an emission cloud ideal for an ambush. If you’re ready, gear up and move to the ambush point immediately.

Ambush Frontier Fighters
We’ve got a target running a predictable route that cuts through an emission cloud. Perfect for a trap. Move to the ambush point, hide your ship’s signature, and strike when the target passes.

## FAQ
▲ What is this event all about?
► The Frontier Fighters Finale follows Save Stanton, Fight for Pyro, and Supply or Die, marking the final chapter in the Pyro Must Burn storyline. This limited-time event challenges players to complete missions for one of two rival factions, Citizens for Prosperity or Headhunters. Every completed mission contributes points to that faction, and the side with the highest total will claim victory at the event’s conclusion.
▲ What is the schedule of this event?
- Recon starts on October 16 with the Alpha 4.3.2 Live patch release.
- Siege starts on October 28 at 16:00 UTC.
- Take Down starts on November 6 at 16:00 UTC.
* We will message out and update this FAQ if there are any changes to the schedule.
▲ How long does the event last?
► The event lasts until the next patch release, and the winning faction (based on global cross-server player contribution) will be announced.
▲ How do I get started?
► Open your mobiGlas and check the Contracts App under the “Priority - CFP” and “Priority - HH” tabs to pick up missions from each faction.
▲ If I join the event late, can I still participate?
► Yes! You can join at any time before the event ends. Earlier phases remain available until the event concludes.
▲ How will I know my progress?
► You can track your progress in a Journal Entry in your mobiGlas Journal App. See image* below for an example.
* The screenshot was taken during the PTU phase. The numbers showcased in the image may not match what's in Live.
▲ How will the rewards be distributed?
► Once you earn enough points, your rewards will be delivered to the inventory of your main landing zone as well as your account.
▲ Are rewards permanent?
► Yes. Once unlocked, all earned rewards are permanently attributed to your account and can be viewed in the My Hangar section of the website.
▲ Can I earn rewards for both factions?
► Yes! You can complete missions for both factions and receive rewards from each. You can also continue running missions for one faction to contribute additional points and help them secure victory in the event.
▲ Will my existing Citizens for Prosperity or Headhunters reputation affect which faction I can ally with, or will these event missions affect my reputation during or after the event?
► No, your reputation isn’t affected. You can accept missions from both sides regardless of your existing reputation, and the event missions won’t change it.
If you have any further questions, let us know.
