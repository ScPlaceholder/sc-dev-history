While being in PTU it is expected that a lot of stick profiles do not work properly as new function and binding names from the game code change. If an axis is inverted, then you can usually just go into the controls tab and select the appropriate HOTAS in the lower left based on the order of your devices (when in doubt open the console (key left of "1") and type in "i_DumpDeviceInformation", that will show you the order of devices).
In that screen can you invert every axis in the game. The process is not ideal but it hopefully gets you closer.
More info here: https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000183328-Create-export-and-import-custom-profiles
