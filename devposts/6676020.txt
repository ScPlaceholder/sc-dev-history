## Calling all pilots! Urgent assistance required!
XenoThreat is putting our system and its people in peril yet again, and we can’t fight them alone. Join the Civilian Defense Force for a multi-week series of missions to play your part in foiling the most diabolical threat to Stanton yet. Over the following weeks, volunteers will be tasked with capturing intel, targeting key insurgents, countering strike forces, and more, all in the pursuit of victory.
Due to the severity of the situation, Anvil Aerospace has released its next-generation space-superiority fighter onto the civilian market. Stronger, faster, and harder-hitting, the Mk II is a complete evolution of the storied Hornet medium fighter revered by the Empire.
:sparkles: Those who prove themselves during this initiative will be awarded complimentary upgrades from the F7C Mk II to the military-exclusive F7A Mk II for their service AND a set of recovered XenoThreat medium armor.
:tada: The CDF needs as many pilots as possible to defeat the increasingly deadly XenoThreat incursion. In response, all new recruits (and those who refer them) will receive a complementary Kruger P-52 Merlin to aid them in the battle against XenoThreat alongside other bonuses and perks. Learn more on the ⁠XenoThreat referral page.
To rally behind the cause, accept the ‘⁠⁠Overdrive Initiative’ contract in your mobiGlas when available. Missions will update as the threat evolves, so stay alert and keep checking back (both this FAQ and the main website will be updated as the story unfolds).
## Overdrive Initiative Missions
▲ [AVAILABLE] 1 - INTEL RAID (Data Heist) - Started on Friday, March 15th
We have received anonymous tips that there is an impending XenoThreat attack! You are sent on a scouting mission to figure out where and when it's going to happen.
At the same time, we have detected that a small XenoThreat team has been sent into Stanton to presumably scout out for this attack. We need you to neutralize these cells and obtain any intel you can at the scene.

Our analysts have looked over the intel seized from those XenoThreat cells but it appears that they're using a sophisticated encryption system to encode their messages.
If we want any chance to decipher this information, we'll need to take down members of the XenoThreat command that are likely to have keys to decrypt the messages.

XenoThreat's been lashing out at being targeted. We're not sure if this is their ultimate attack or something to keep us off balance, but we need them stopped.
We used the decryption keys to learn about a group of scattered attacks that XenoThreat is looking to launch. This way, we have the intel to get to an area before XenoThreat arrives.
► Defeat 5 incursions.
▲ [AVAILABLE] 4 - XENOTHREAT SURVEILLANCE + 'stay alert for more'  (Hacking, Ships/EVA/FPS Combat) - Started on Friday, April 5th
We've learned that XenoThreat has been hijacking comm arrays and implanting Vanguard callouts, which is how they've been getting their intel.
► Hack and shut down comm arrays.
▲ [ONGOING] 5 - SUPPLY REQUEST (Transport) - Starts on Friday, April 12th
XenoThreat's gone dark. They must be on to us, shutting down all operations and communique in Stanton.
The CDF wants you to help call their bluff. Resupply the jump point station and stay prepared for an imminent attack.
► Deliver supplies to the jump point station to prepare for an attack.
:warning: Mission 5 prepares players for the final XenoThreat assault (Mission 6), which will begin soon after Star Citizen Alpha 3.23 goes live. ## FAQ
▲ What is this event all about?
► This event encompasses a multi-week series of missions, ultimately culminating in the next big clash against XenoThreat. The CDF is once again calling upon civilians to take up arms and fight back against the XenoThreat, this time, on the CDF's terms. Follow along as the story unfolds both on this FAQ and the main website for the event.
▲ Will I be able to join the mission series late, and still participate?
► Yes! You have until the end of Mission 5 to join the Overdrive Initiative and be eligible for the free F7A Mk II Token Upgrade.
▲ What happens to my F7A Veteran's Day cosmetic upgrade kit?
► We are very happy to share that for those of you who previously acquired the F7A Veteran's Day Cosmetic Upgrade Kit, you will be given a full F7A Hornet Mk I for free. This will be delivered to you during this year's Invictus Launch Week! Enjoy! o7
▲ Will you run this event again in the future?
► XenoThreat has a nasty habit of returning to Stanton, so it's always possible the CDF will need your help again.
▲ Can I acquire multiple F7A Mk II?
► You can acquire one (1) Upgrade Token per account, which will remain on your account, allowing you to upgrade your F7C Mk II to an F7A Mk II whenever you choose for free.
▲ Can I melt my F7C Mk II and will I get my Upgrade Token back?
► If you have used your Upgrade Token from F7C Mk II to F7A Mk II, and then melted your F7A Mk II, your token will have been consumed in the process. Our Support team won't be able to provide the Upgrade Token back to your account.
▲ What do I have to do to get the F7A Token Upgrade?
► By completing all of the Overdrive Missions 1 to 5, you will receive a mission called "Rewards" that you have to accept. Then you will receive your Token Upgrade in your account, and we will send you an email with more information.
▲ Do I need a F7C Mk I or a F7C Mk II to use the F7A Mk II upgrade?
► Upon completion of the event, you will be rewarded with the free upgrade token whether you have the F7C MKII or not. However, to use the upgrade token, you will need to have a Hornet F7C MKII in your hangar.
▲ Is the F7A upgrade permanent?
► Yes, if you have completed the chain and upgraded to the F7A, it's yours to keep - unless you choose to melt it.
▲ Are there any other rewards for completing the event?
► On top of the free F7A Mk II Upgrade Token, you will also be granted an F7A Mk II rental for 7 days.
▲ Is it intended that players can place the F7C Mk I turret in lieu of the F7C Mk II cargo?
► The F7C Mk II's cargo can be swapped for the F7C Mk I's turret or even the future F7A Mk II's military turret
▲ What is going to happen to the F7C Mk I, now that an updated version Mk II is available?
► The F7C Mk I will be getting its own gold standard pass in the future.
▲ How do I know that my progress is complete with each Mission of the Overdrive Initiative?
► If you don't receive any new Missions, it means that you have completed all the current available missions.
► Please note: the support team won't be able to answer tickets asking for an update on player missions progress. For Alpha 3.23, we are adding a CVAR (instructions on how to run the CVAR will follow) that will allow you to verify your status on the Overdrive Initiative event tracking and us to debug further. Additionally, we have resolved the problem with the mission history and the counter not working as expected.
▲ What is this additional reward you are offering: A set of XenoThreat Medium Armor
► During your adventures, you may have noticed NPCs wearing this set of armor from the XenoThreat faction. Once you have completed all the Overdrive Initiative missions, that set of armor will be yours to keep!
If you have any further questions, let us know, and we will do our best to answer them.
