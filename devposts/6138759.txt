## Welcome to the 2023 Ship Showdown Contest!
In the first phase of the Ship Showdown, noise for your favorite flyable or drivable craft is all that matters. Create an original artistic creation and upload it to the Community Hub or Twitter with the hashtags #SCShipShowdown and #StarCitizen.
:keycap_ten: Ten exceptional and outstanding original creations will be awarded with a DRAKE Vulture, including LTI. Take advantage of the opportunity to win! :rocket:
"What kind of original creation," you might ask?
ANY!!!
Write an original song and perform it, craft something out of any material you want, make a breathtaking in-game machinima, create an epic out-of-game recording, shoot a trailer or music video, grab your pens and draw, or build the ship for real if you feel inclined to do so! Your submission can be anything as long as it celebrates your favorite vehicle that you can currently use in the 'verse.
:ringed_planet: For all the details on Ship Showdown, the full contest rules, and the submissions so far, head over to the Ship Showdown page to do your part in crowning this year's champion. But hurry, Phase 1 only runs until August 21, 2023, 8 pm UTC/1:00 pm Pacific Time!
