Hi everyone,
With the regen crisis spiraling out of control, Stanton's four major corporations—Hurston, Crusader, ArcCorp, and microTech—have stopped watching from the sidelines.
In response to Imperator Addison’s call for scientific support, they're launching a high-stakes initiative in update Alpha 4.2.1: Race for Stanton. This is more than a mission: it’s a battle for control, prestige, and a foothold in humanity’s future. Citizens are called to retrieve, mine, defend, and haul for any or these mega-corporations in the new global event: Resource Drive. Track each corp’s progress on your mobiGlas as they compete to claim naming rights to the Stanton/Pyro jump point. Each offers unique missions, lucrative rewards, and a chance to be part of something massive. The race is on... time to pick a side!
UPDATE: Resource Drive has been extended a few more weeks into 4.3.0! This gives you extra time to jump in and enjoy the event alongside the fixes we’ve rolled out in 4.2.1, with more improvements coming in 4.3.0. Thanks for all your feedback and for flagging issues on the Issue Council.
