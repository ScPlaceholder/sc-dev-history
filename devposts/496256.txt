It's also important to remember that most of what we learn about the lore in Star Citizen's universe is presented from in-fiction sources.  You are learning about the galaxy from the inhabitants of the galaxy, and like many information sources in our own world, that information can be skewered, misinterpreted, or even downright incorrect.  That's all part of the fun inherent in this galaxy the Lore Team is creating.  We're not gonna just give you the answers up front.  You'll have to step out into the void and discover what's true, what's fiction, and what lies somewhere in-between.
