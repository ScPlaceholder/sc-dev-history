Its not on us to fix this. I think the OpenXR Toolkit is just outdated and doesn't work anymore with newer Openxr versions. I could be wrong.
You can put sys.OpenXR.Layer.DisableOpenXRToolkit=0 in your user.cfg and play around and check if you can get it too work.
Many people crashed when they had it installed so i disabled it in code, but using that cvar reverts this behavior.
