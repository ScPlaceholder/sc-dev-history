I wanted to thank you all for all your data; it came in super useful. As previously mentioned we had estimated some times based on our testing and we knew they would be off by an order of magnitude. We have used all of your data together to change our estimates to be more inline with what you all experience.
In the next few days we will be committing an update to rebalance all cargo hauling missions. This will make them feel more worthwhile whilst still respecting EVR. On the subject of EVR, we have used all of your fanstastic feedback during the development of the hauling missions to iterate a rebalance of the EvR numbers, which will have a wider effect across all missions going forward.
To note another change along with this we noticed people mentioning Ships as a progression. While reputation and other rewards will be more of a source of progression, we can see how it would be difficult to engage with some of the bigger SCU missions when you only have a Nomad. With this Nick and I are reducing the amount it costs to rent some Hauling focussed ships (and also some multi purpose ships that can haul) which also means some ships that are not currently rentable will become rentable. I wont list all of them here because there's like 20+ but we are looking at making rentable the following ships.
