I think what we do now breaks realism in very significant ways.  Because one of the main limitations on a thruster is how long it can thrust, and how much delta v it can produce.  Our thrusters currently have infinite delta v, and that's a problem.  And impulse thruster will have a limited delta v, but a very high rate of acceleration.  Then to have the infinite thrust that we currently have, you're limited to lower accelerations.  So it seems like a nice trade-off.
