Hello Citizens!
This Easter, in the spirit of seeking those which are hidden, we invite you to take part in a good old fashioned Screenshot Contest BUT with a twist!
We all know that some locations in the 'verse are bit more hidden that others, and we want you to seek these places out!
Starting today, travel to one of the following locations, capture your best image, and reply to this thread with your screenshot for a chance to win a MISC Reliant variant!
## Locations
- Jump Town, Yela
- Javelin Wreck Site/Derelict, Daymar
- Benny Henge, near GrimHEX
We'll select the best entry per location and announce the winners in this thread on Monday, April 22nd. Depending on the location featured in the entry, participants of this contest have the chance to win a MISC Reliant Tana, Sen, or Mako! That means 3 chances to be a winner, hooray!
## Rules
Before you take part, please make sure to carefully review the complete contest rules here.
Notes:
- Each screenshot is considered a single entry but you are allowed multiple entries (you can include multiple images in a single post)
- You are only eligible to win a prize for ONE location
- No post-processing effects allowed. This includes 3rd party add-ons like ReShade
- Contest ends: April 21st, 2019 11:59pm PST
- Your character must be in the screenshot
A jury of Cloud Imperium employees will judge the entries by voting on:
- Quality
- Style
- Rule adherence
## Rewards
The winning contest entries will be rewarded with the following ships:
- MISC Reliant Tana Standalone with LTI - Jump Town screenshot
- MISC Reliant Mako Standalone with LTI - Javelin Wreck/Derelict screenshot
- MISC Reliant Sen Standalone with LTI - Benny Henge  screenshot
We're looking forward to your screenshots - Happy Easter to you and your crew!
