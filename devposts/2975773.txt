Yes, there is a buffer (digestion for food and absorption for drink) that delays the nutrition/hydration meters from lowering when consuming something - you also start off with this buffer when you respawn although it is not completely full. So if you eat something or drink something you get a delay before the meter starts to drop again. This gives you a chance to eat/drink something when you are low and to get back to an LZ or your ship.
If you maxxed the meters and the buffers it would take you 7 hours to die from thirst and 10 hours to die from hunger if you stood still. Doing activities makes them drain slightly quicker.
