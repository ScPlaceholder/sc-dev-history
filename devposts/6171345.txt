We mentioned on the SCL that we were hesitant to say 3.20 because it was still actively being worked on, which at the time was already quite late in the release cycle, so anything could throw it off course and miss the release. It is very far along now, I can say that internal tests have been successful, but there is a few kinks to iron out and UI to finalize. Ultimately we decided not to deliver Custom Lobbies with 3.20.0 due to a number of reasons, the two main being given how close we were to PTU we did not want to cause unexpected delays to the actual release getting a feature in this late on and that simply we do not want to half-arse it and rush it out. Waiting for next patch gives us time to smash more bugs not just for Custom Lobbies but shift our priority back to polishing 3.20 for a better release for the mountain of other updates we have in there.
