@ninjedy,  the current IFCS behaviour is intended. During coupled mode you set a goal velocity vector with your inputs (be it keyboard or HOTAS) and your ship will try to reach only that vector and avoids changing into any other uncommanded direction. The velocity vector "slipping" was actually never intended and was caused by a normalization error in the input handling. Fixing that also resolved a couple of other problems like accidently going above the allowed velocity limiter, etc. So we will not revert that specific behaviour as it is the one we actually want.
That said work on the FM and spaceflight handling in general is not done yet. There are other upcoming changes on the FM that will benefit the racing community. But it's a bit too early to talk about these things yet.
