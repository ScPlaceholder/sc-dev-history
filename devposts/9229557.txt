We're aware that some ships are falling off the Liberator's landing pads when they should remain attached, and we're looking into ways to address this more effectively going forward.
This issue is closely related to the size of the Liberator's local grid, which was kept as small as possible to mitigate potential exploits. Some ships have their pivot, root, or origin point outside the boundaries of the Liberator's local grid when landed, preventing them from being recognized as being within that grid. The intended dimensions for each landing pad's volume are approximately 24 × 32 × 16 meters, based on the original "XS" landing pad dimensions established during the concept phase and reflected in the station metrics. The current height restriction is not intended to be a permanent limitation.
We're investigating a more robust solution to this issue, which will also be needed for the Kraken.
