That likely won't happen. For something like that we'd need to include more data to HUD to always tell you currently set "strength" of the space brake which adds a lot of UI work and would not be used by more than a handful of users. I am also not convinced it is the "right" way to offer space brake controls. It would almost always be easier to just use the space brake in its full strength and / or use it in a combination with the acceleration controls. We only turned the space brake into an analogue axis to give players with toe brakes, car pedals or brake levers more fidelity to use it as it makes sense in those cases.
That said I am sure you can use the VKB tools to configure the Kosmosimas to do something like that.
