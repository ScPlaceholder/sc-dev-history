For accounts that are stuck being unable to log into the game due to this, one possible course of action is to contact our support team to have them try logging into the account. If we're not able to log into the account either, we can use it as an example of something that's in a bad state and investigate what might be wrong. As some others in this thread have mentioned, this can also be a tech support problem so if our support team is able to log into the account, it lets them know that they can help out by exploring possible solutions for what might be causing issues for people connecting to the game.
