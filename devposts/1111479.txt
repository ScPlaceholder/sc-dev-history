Short answer is yes!
We've been discussing how to transition the stories and news updates that we post to the website to have them appear in game as well. The specifics are still being worked out with design and UI, but the hope is that we will be able to present content to players diegetically through monitors, mobiGlas, datapads, and even books and magazines. (Though current news might wind up being a primarily digital affair, stories and articles that are less time sensitive could still be physically printed out, like the books and magazines that you can find sprinkled around places like Port Olisar and Levski now.) In the much longer term, this content could eventually be generated more dynamically, incorporating player actions and player driven content.
