We have implemented the first iteration of ship to ship docking for the parasite fighters attached to the Constellation series ships. This also does mean these ships can now be deployed! Note, there are still some known issues for doing this while the parent ship is moving (see patch notes). Please give the following a try:
- Undock a Merlin/Archimedes
- Request docking targeting your parent ship and pressing "N"
- Following the UI guide
- Optionally press and hold "N" again when close to activate an automated system
- Repeat the above on someone else's Constellation
Please leave feedback on the UI setup, manual vs automatic difficulty levels, and general gameplay flow.
Thanks for your input!
