Yes. Galactapedia is updated by me alone and written by me and Ben Lesnick (shoutout to Ben). We do our best to fill the gaps in the lore with new, original content or to document and clarify what has already been referenced in portfolio pieces, spectrum dispatches, and more. Each article is a piece of original writing in the voice of the Ark, created with love for the lore and for the fans. As Jale pointed out, it is not meant to supplant the valuable, detailed, and wonderful work done by fan wikis like starcitizen.tools, who document development history and other important out of lore information.
