No, all of the HUD behavior is upstream of ESP.  ESP receives targeting data which corresponds with the HUD display, it processes that data to calculate the ideal ship control to bring the pip over the target, merges that with your input, then passes the updated input to IFCS.  Where ESP fails, it's typically from bad targeting data, or IFCS not doing what it's expected to do.  We've had both issues for awhile now.  I can fix the issues with IFCS, but have no control over targeting.
