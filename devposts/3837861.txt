When you use TrackIR the internal "look vectors" based on mouse movement (and other inputs) are suppressed on foot but it does not affect the "base rotation" (aka the hips) and aiming vectors (aka "gun aim vector"). So by using a headtracker what you do is to set a new "look vector" but it always based on the "aim vector". So while being on foot your mouse movement will still affect camera because because the normal mouse movement moves the "aim vector". That's at least how it is supposed to work ... is that not working for you?
