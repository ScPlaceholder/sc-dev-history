Update 1:
Hello everyone,
The Tempt Fate Contest submission period is now officially over, and we'll now start the internal voting process to announce the winners soon.
We have already seen many amazing videos from this contest being shared among the dev team, and we want to thank everyone for participating.
Stella Fortuna is a holiday all about good fortune, boldness, tempting fate, and embarking on new ventures.
We all know that the 'verse is full of opportunities to become buy-a-planet-and-never-go-there rich, yet it's full of risks and dangers of the same magnitude. This is exactly what this video contest is all about.
Welcome to the Tempt Fate 2951 Video Contest!
:bulb: Before you start filming, please read the Contest Rules carefully and only post your entries to the submission thread. Any submissions not posted to the 'Tempt Fate 2951 Contest Submission Thread' are invalid.
Let's get started!
We're looking for the best David vs. Goliath-style action video. Show us how Fortuna smiles on you in a battle against a superior foe and how you emerged victorious. Think 'action movie' and create your ultimate epic showdown, no matter if your under-gunned hero is on foot, driving a vehicle, or piloting a spaceship - as long as you're punching above your weight class, we want to see it.
## Rules
- Read the Contest Rules
- Upload your video to YouTube and post the link only in the submission thread
- Video must not exceed 90 seconds
- Submissions must be at least 1920x1080 resolution and uploaded to YouTube
- No more than three collaborators on a single video
- Multiple submissions are not permitted
- Submissions should clearly state all individuals involved in creating the video at the time of submission
- The prize will be delivered to up to three "contributors" who are identified at time of submission
- Music/sound/effects should not infringe on any third party copyright. This means you're able to use originally created/sourced music/effects, or official Star Citizen music/effects.
The CIG jury will judge your videos on:
- Excitement (Die-Hardiness)
- Quality
- Rule adherence
## Dates
The contest kicks of this Friday, March 12, at 08:00 am (Pacific). All entries must be in by Sunday, April 4, 11:59 pm (Pacific).
## Rewards
- :first_place_medal: 1st Place: Origin 600i Explorer with LTI
- :second_place_medal: 2nd Place: Crusader Mercury Star Runner including the Stella Fortuna Paint with LTI
- :third_place_medal: 3rd Place: Tumbril Cyclone MT with LTI
There’s more to Stella Fortuna than our video contest, so check out the St Patrick's Day 2021 page to find out what’s going on and how you can get involved.
May luck be with the brave! :four_leaf_clover:
