It was an odd and unusual combination of events really. The change was just supposed to fix the inability of all pilots to utilize all the available thrust for the linear motion at all times which is especially important during combat. After the fix was submitted I even gave this a quick check with my own HOTAS setup ...  but being focused on the problem (missing accelerations) I only checked if the max accelerations were corrected and if the TVI was stable (which was the case).
The next proper chance to catch this would have been in our company wide internal play test. They happen at least once per week and allow us to play the game in a "normal" way like all players do. However due to a specific delay our internal 3.10 playtest build last week was actually the same as the previous PTU version. So we effectively played an older version of 3.10 which didn't have the new bug.
Finally QA didn't have much chance to find it either. At that stage (PTU) they are often tasked with finding and reproducing specific bugs that the PTU testers are reporting. While we do have a fairly big QA department, they are just not enough to allow testing every part of the game in every PTU patch. They always have to be smart about how to assign resources and flight systems were not the priority during those last couple of days.
Anyways, we would have ultimately found the bug before the live release as we do have a lot peeps internally with quite varied control device setups (HOMAS, HOSAS, HOTAS). PTU simply found the bug faster, which is a good thing. It highlights that the process of PTU works ... though we will surely take more care about that kind of change in the future.
