" If I was to have a ship of any variety covered by insurance, and a  partner in crime "steals it" from me, what is to stop me from making a  claim on it for a replacement, being given the ship back by the thief,  and reselling it for scrap as a profit? "
Nothing is stopping you. It just won't be profitable given the premium you have to pay, which will increase with subsequent claims, which will reduce with time. In fact you can walk up to the ship selector just pick any ship and make a claim for any reason you want. Now this relies on correct balancing of prices, times, etc.. which is where design comes in and I'm sure while we're in evocati and PTU those values will see tweaking.
