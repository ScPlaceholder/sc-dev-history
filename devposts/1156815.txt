The egg-shaped planet thing is as expected - most games, including ours, use a rectilinear camera projection since this keeps all straight lines straight. Projections that allow curved lines, eg fisheye lenses, cause problems for polygon-based games as the triangles aren't able to curve. Under such a projection, things towards the edges will stretch out, though theoretically if you set the game FoV to match the angle your monitor covers in the real world, the foreshortening of one will perfectly cancel out the elongation of the other.
