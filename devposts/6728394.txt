►What ticket options will be available and when do they go on sale?
Tickets will be available in two tiers, both in limited quantities. Here’s a breakdown of what to expect:
CitizenCon 2954 Access Ticket:
Your ticket to both days of CitizenCon also gets you exclusive swag, both physical and digital.
CitizenCon 2954 Premium Experience Ticket:
Elevate your experience with this extremely limited upgrade. Add VIP perks to your ticket including a dedicated private lounge, priority access lanes, extra physical and digital swag, discounted merchandise, and more.
Starting on April 11th, Early Access will open exclusively for Concierge and Subscribers. The following day, April 12th, general sales will be open to the public.

The CitizenCon Gala is a separate off-site event taking place the day before CitizenCon. Tickets to this event will be extremely limited, with a very small number being made available. More information and ticket sales for this particular event to come in the months ahead.
