We play DCS and are well educated HUD design from pretty much all ages.
We actually went from design iterations that had all HUD elements projected in a narrow angle forward to the mixed setup we have now of using infinity projected elements for aiming/targeting and holographic representations for the speed gauges, etc
While our systemic ship based recommendations were pretty much "all info forward" this certainly doesn't play well with aesthetics that we want to achieve. E.g. we want to avoid clutter and only show stuff that is relevant to the player. Additionally we have certain ship brands like Drake which will prefer more low-end representations of data.
It does not mean that we are completely set or that we won't allow customization for placing elements yourselves later. What you saw during CC is just the "now" state of the UI design, not more, not less. You can feedback on that and we have open ears on it
