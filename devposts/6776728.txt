We did some updates to the balancing and a little preface from my side:
Disclaimer: These changes only apply to the A2 and Gladius in the AC Enginering Experimental mode!
We would like to test here especially the combat behavior and tactics of small fighters attacking larger ships.
What we are aiming for is that the attacker (Gladius) has to overcome a certain distance to be able to damage the bigger ship.
The bigger ship should be able to defend themselves while the Gladius is approaching.
This means if you do fly straight at the A2 as the Gladius pilot you should not even make it.
Once you get in range of the A2 the Gladius should be able to do some significant damage and the A2 gunners should “switch” to their Anti fighter repeaters (which are not effective for the long range)
What we additionally to the above questions would like to know from you here is:
As A2 gunner can you hit a Gladius from afar
- That does not do any evasive maneuvers?
- That does some evasive maneuvers?
- That does extensive evasive maneuvers?
- That races/boosts towards the A2?
