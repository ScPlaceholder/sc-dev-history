We have the option to do full decoupled rotations in dev builds (and actually quantum boost rotational gameplay is fully decoupled), but we never activated it for the normal space-flight gameplay because it is simply not fun and very hard to use. Our angular accelerations during normal flight are very high to make the ships feel reactive, most small ships reach the full rotation velocity in a fraction of a second. Controlling that in a decoupled fashion only works by heavily flattening the acceleration inputs.
So it is possible to do that but at the moment we do not have yet a good gameplay reason for it. It doesn't mean it won't happen in the future though.
