Xi'an names have the same connotation as names in English do; they absolutely have meaning, but the meaning isn't going to be clear just by looking at the characters. For example, my name, Cherie, means "beloved" in French. But if you didn't know French, you wouldn't know that, because the word "Cherie" is something that you can say in English that reads to us as a name.
In short, they're not empty names. But they aren't literal names, either (like "Bird" or "Flower") (well, I mean, there aren't any rules against naming your kids this in Xi'an. It'd just be a little unusual).
