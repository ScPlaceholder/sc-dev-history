The Pyro system is being featured on the Preview Channel, as an exception, simply because it's an entirely new system (massive content drop). Usually, new locations, features, and content, will go straight to the PTU, skipping the Preview Channel.
In the future, you can expect the Preview Channel to be utilized for major underlying technologies, such as Server Meshing, the Replication Layer split, etc. - these behemoths are better served isolated on the Preview Channel until they are ready to be graduated to the PTU, and then eventually live.
