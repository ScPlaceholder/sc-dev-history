This is not possible with the current tech. Coupled and decoupled is a binary system that cannot be mixed. It would first require to separate out the thrust axes and treat them individually which is something we haven't done since 3.5 (to avoid issues with overspeeding). And while we do want do some testing in that regard to improve strafing responsiveness during combat I am not sure that this will yield usable results yet. It's also not high on the priority list.
But using a normal flight sim throttle in SC is not necessarily the optimal choice, at least not for the combat and racing parts. I actually did use a Warthog throttle in the past for SC but I ended up 3D-printing myself a center detent so I could feel where the center is and so I used the full throttle axis for that without needing to use the inversion button. Other throttles come with that kind of stuff pre-equipped already. The Virpil throttles come with custom detents that can be placed in the axis center and specifically the VKB Stecs has an excellent support for "space throttles" that lets you set multiple detents and define custom axis points.
