Server Meshing has been enabled with the release of 4.0, a major milestone needed to fully realize a living, breathing and persistent universe. This implementation of meshing allows multiple servers per shard to improve load distribution and facilitates navigation between two star systems. We are currently running with a "5:5:500" Configuration which means 5 servers in Stanton, 5 servers in Pyro, and 500 total players on the shard. This will greatly impact your gameplay experience, and we'd love to hear what you think of this current implementation!
