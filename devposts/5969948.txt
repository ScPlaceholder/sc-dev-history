At the core of Star Citizen's development is a commitment to creating an immersive and engaging experience. As we continue to refine the game, we often find ourselves tweaking various aspects to strike the delicate balance between realism, challenge, and enjoyment. The recent adjustments to respawn timers were simply a part of this ongoing iterative process, and are by no means final. Experimenting with different values allows us to gather valuable data and insights, enabling us to make informed decisions when balancing.
We are fully aware of the concerns raised regarding the length of the new respawn timers, especially when considering the possibility of losing your ship due to something outside of your control. There's a fair bit of focus on tightening things up in this area, and we expect to make another round or two of changes soon. As always, we'll take all factors into consideration, including the state of the game, the types of potential ship loss scenarios, and the overall gameplay experience.
