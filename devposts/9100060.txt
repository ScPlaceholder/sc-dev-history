Hi everyone,
The second phase of the new Spectrum redesign is now live!
## What's New in Phase 2
This update gives the Thread List page a new, more linear design, making it easier to scan, browse, and find the topics you care about.
## This is Just the Beginning
With Phase 1 and Phase 2 behind us, we're moving on to the rest of Spectrum. Up next is the mini profile, giving the player card a fresh look. Threads and messages follow after that.
For threads, we're rebuilding Markdown support from scratch, along with richer formatting options and more ways to add structure and personality to your posts. We want Spectrum to be a place you enjoy contributing to, not just check in on.
## We Want to Hear From You
Before we finalize the quality-of-life improvements coming to threads and messages, we want to hear what would make the biggest difference to your experience on Spectrum.
Share your ideas in the comments below. Whether it’s better formatting tools, improved Markdown support, new messaging features, or something else entirely, we’re reading.
While we can’t make any promises, your feedback will help shape what we prioritize next.
