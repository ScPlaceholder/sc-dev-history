Hi everyone,
RSI is celebrating the arrival of the next-generation Constellation, the new Mk V Centaurus, and all pilots across Stanton are invited to join in. Special RSI installations have been set up throughout the Stanton system, honoring the legendary history of the Constellation Mk IV and introducing the new Mk V and its companion, the Skylark.
However you would like to take part, there's a role for you. Defend fellow pilots from the criminals drawn to the valuable ships and cargo surrounding the event, keep vital supplies moving across Stanton, or collect industrial materials and lost cargo. Every mission moves you closer to RSI-themed rewards across four tracks in your mobiGlas. Ship paints, flight blades, components, armor, and weapons are all up for grabs.
Best of all, every pilot who completes the Constellation Rental Offer gets five days of special rental access to the Constellation Mk V Centaurus and the Skylark. Don't miss your chance to see why this new generation is destined to become as legendary as the rest.
Beyond RSI Discovery Month, Alpha 4.10.2 also brings more updates. All RSI Constellation Mk IV variants receive their Gold Standard update, and the physics networking overhaul improves how physics are handled across the 'verse. Alongside these updates, a range of bug fixes continue to improve the Live experience and keep your journey smooth from launch to landing.
