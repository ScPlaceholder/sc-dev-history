Hey y'all!
Life outside the UEE is hard-won. Now, an elusive group known as the Moraine is threatening the fragile stability the independent people of Nyx have fought to build.
The People’s Alliance requires your help. Scout safe locations for future operations, search for missing workers, board hijacked ships to rescue survivors, and recover valuable cargo from pirate raids. Where vessels can no longer be saved, salvage what's left behind and ensure every resource reaches its destination.
It’s dangerous work, but the people of Nyx remember those who stand beside them. Earn the trust of the People's Alliance and catch the attention of Recco Battaglia, a respected leader among the free people, who may have even greater opportunities for those willing to prove themselves…
That's just some of what's coming to the 'verse. Beyond Frontier Tension, Alpha 4.9 brings updates to the combat experience with revamped indicators designed to allow for better engagement and more informed combat when you need it most, as well as a new generation of fly-by audio, rebuilt from the ground up, delivering greater positional awareness and richer audio fidelity. Distribution Centers across the planets and moons of the ‘verse are being refreshed to resolve many long-standing mission bugs.
