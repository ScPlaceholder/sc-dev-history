I tend to use the phrase low-light-enhancement internally, because that is pretty trivial to implement, wheres infrared or similar technologies see the world very differently and need the artwork to somehow be marked up to show how surfaces react to other wavelengths of light beyond R/G/B.
Design have been really keen to get light enhancement in, and it's not technically difficult, but it's just fighting with quite a lot of other priorities and hasn't quite made it to the top of the list as yet. Also it's obviously not just a graphics feature, we need the game code, UI, audio and AI to all do their part too.
