Hi everyone,
We've been closely following the conversation surrounding 4.8's upcoming wipe and wanted to give you an updated heads-up regarding what to expect.
Alpha 4.8 will include a comprehensive wipe. This means earned aUEC, in-game purchased vehicles, items, resources, reputation progression, and Wikelo and Executive Hangar rewards will all be reset with the launch of Alpha 4.8.
Here’s what you will keep:
- Blueprints
- Any item or vehicle entitled to your account and visible in the "My Hangar" section of the RSI website
As previously mentioned, this reset is an important step toward stabilizing the economy as new systems continue coming online. Alongside the wipe, we’ve also implemented additional safeguards aimed at reducing exploits and duplication issues, with more improvements to come.
We appreciate all of the feedback you've sent our way. We’ll be closely monitoring the rollout and look forward to seeing you all in-game!
