## Feature Updates
Reputation
Guild reputation pages are now populated in the mobiGlas. The first six ranks for each guild have names and icons, where the pages were previously blank.
- Bounty names are now color highlighted throughout the full bounty description rather than only in part of it
- Mission reward strings added across the Nyx I contracts
Starchitect Locations
Lighting work continues across the guild halls, compounds, turret tiles and interiors, with light leaks sealed, spill lighting added to light boxes, and shadow casting distances tuned on safes, drop-off points and career boards for performance. Extraction units and building exteriors have been tinted, since white at every location was reading as repetitive.
Performance has also had attention. The imposter shader is around 25% faster through moving work earlier in the pipeline, and the planet texture bake has been substantially sped up.
- Building architecture now reads more clearly on the minimap across habitation, landing pads and depots
- Administrator dialogue now takes priority when speaking to the player, so NPCs no longer talk over them
- Potential Fix: AI reactions resetting after a nearby NPC was killed, leaving enemies unaware they were under attack
- Potential Fix: Door panels in the 80m guild hall reading as "Locked" while the door was actually open (STARC-224270)
- Potential Fix: Fractured mining nodes highlighting orange instead of purple, and disintegrating when fractured with a mining vehicle
- Potential Fix: A broken ledge in the 60m logistics building, with further ledge adjustments across the landing pads
- Potential Fix: Overly bright lighting on a prop, and a sorting issue on a compound interactable screen
Contracts
- Potential Fix: Contracts sending players to a Tactical Strike Group location where station defenses would attack them (STARC-206902)
- Potential Fix: Boss markers not reappearing if players died and returned to the location
- Potential Fix: Ling mining contracts requesting plasma fuel, which was causing generation failures
- Potential Fix: Three item delivery contracts failing to generate
Genesis and Nyx I
Atmospheric lighting has been extended to account for ground fog and rain volumes, so sky light is correctly dimmed beneath them and diffused sunlight is added back.
- Ground cover now uses soil depth alone, so it no longer floats near bedrock (STARC-221075)
- More stable surface wetting, and further water and VRAM optimization
- Volumetric cloud improvements, including optional atmospheric reddening so sunset-lit clouds no longer wash out
