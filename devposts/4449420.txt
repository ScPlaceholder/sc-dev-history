Please keep in that the current ammo limits are just a temporary measure as the vehicle armor system is not finished yet. The difference of ballistics vs energy weapons is not supposed to be based on DPS only, it is supposed to work on different damage types that can resisted by the right shield and armor properties. Once that it resolved you can expect ballistics ammo numbers to go up again.
I don't it is fruitful to compare Star Citizen stats to real life planes. The weapon combat dynamics (even in pure dogfights) are just too different due, e.g. there no shields and effective armor systems (armor weights a lot and affects the performance which is what fighter pilots absolutely hate). Direct hits from typical military rounds would often cause catastrophic damage on a plane. Internally carried ammo in modern fighter jets is actually a lot more limited than Star Citizen. The F-16 and F/A-18 get about 5 seconds out of the gun before they are empty (it has an RPM of 6000 and if they fire it in one go the gun can likely be thrown away due to excessive heat damage). Same for the Eurofighter Typhoon, the internal gun has 150 rounds and the RPM of the gun seems to be at least 1000 RPM which lasts for maybe 9 seconds. But then again, if any of those guns scores even just a few hits on an opposing fighter, the fight is usually over.
So while we are trying to make the game feel as real as possible we will not just copy real world data into the game. At the end of day believability and a balanced gaming experience are more important than just pure realism.
