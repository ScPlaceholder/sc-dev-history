## Notable Changes for March 30, 2022
## Progress Tracker
The following deliverable has been added to the Progress Tracker:
Checkpoint/Skip Point Tool
Creation of an editor tool, as part of the Subsumption Visualizer, which allows the creation of files defining setups for specific checkpoints, such as spawned entities, their position, and loadouts. This is being added to the Squadron 42 Feature Team's schedule.
## Release View
This week, we've added a minor addition to the Release View interface. To benefit players who may be unaware of our patch release cadence, we've added a column for each quarter's release for the remainder of the year. As previously communicated, we'll add feature cards to those columns as we get closer to their respective quarter.
The following deliverable has passed its final review. For this reason, we have updated its card to Committed and changed its color to blue.
- MISC Hull A
We also wanted to confirm that the RSI Scorpius is on track to release in a subsequent 3.17.X patch.
