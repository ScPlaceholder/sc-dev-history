Hi everyone,
We wanted to give you a quick update on our progress with the upcoming change to how you summon your vehicles in-game, as well as an update on our next patch: 3.18.1. As we revealed in our previous post, we will be implementing a new system that should significantly reduce strain on the backend. We plan to roll out this change onto the PTU in a 3.18.1 patch for testing over the weekend, which also includes a salvo of fixes to assist with performance, connectivity, and stability. We encourage everyone to test the new system and provide any feedback or bug reports you may have.
Additionally, during our testing, we have discovered similar issues related to Arena Commander. To address this, we will be temporarily disabling vehicle customization in Arena Commander on the PTU, and if we see this does indeed alleviate strain on the backend, we'll carry this change over to the live servers. Again though, this would only be temporary and only impact Arena Commander, while we work on a long term solution which we'd roll out in a follow-up patch soon. For those still eager to get into some action in AC, we'll also grant everyone a handful of curated vehicles in with upgraded shield generators and weaponry.
In short, we're doing a bit of experimenting to ensure we can get the service into a better place. As we've mentioned, things are already vastly improved, but work continues to get to the level of stability we're after.
