I can only talk from my experience at one particular corner of the UK studio, and I don't have any particularly horrific horror stories from previous jobs, but my experience of staff treatment here has been pretty good. While there are sometimes immovable deadlines and last-minute stress, it's the exception rather than the rule, they're quite careful to avoid burning us out, and it's never felt like I'm being squeezed just to get a few more hours out of me.
I wouldn't say, though, that this is necessarily because there's no publisher, studios can be well or badly run whether they're crowd-funded, paid by a publisher, paid by another studio, or fully independent. In the end it comes down to the people running the project.
