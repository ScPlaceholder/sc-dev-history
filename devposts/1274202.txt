First off, everything about the flight model is highly flexible.  At its core it's a 6DOF acceleration model.  Everything else is control system logic.  So setting the velocity when you switch from decoupled to coupled is a deliberate action, certainly not a necessary one.  It would be easy to add a setting to switch this off, and I'm in favor of having lots of options to expose the flexibility of this system.  But at the moment, this behavior is following the requested design.  You can give feedback to the issue council and argue for it to be changed, and I'll gladly do it if I get the green light.
