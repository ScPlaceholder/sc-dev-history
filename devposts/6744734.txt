Greetings,
In 3.23 we have overhauled FPS combat with major updates, from new scopes & sights, to weapon balance changes to make the experience more consistent. The overall intention for many of these updates is to slow down the player in committed firefights, while also allowing them to retreat mid-fight. Additionally, we have adjusted a few keybinds. Due to the amount of changes introduced in 3.23 we will not list them all on this thread, instead please go to the patch notes linked below and read the 'FPS Combat Overhaul' section for all of the changes:
https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/190048/thread/star-citizen-alpha-3-23-0-eptu-patch-notes
Give these new changes a try and let us know what you think below. Looking forward to hearing from you!
