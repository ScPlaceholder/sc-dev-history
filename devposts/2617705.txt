Greetings!
We have put in numerous updates to the first person camera effects and first person look ahead mode. The first person, pilot camera has had updated camera shake effects toned down some, and has had new g-force induced effects added to the mix. Look ahead now has an optional "experimental" mode, now enabled by default, that allows the player to control where the pilot looks and the different strengths of each direction including looking into the velocity vector, looking into turns, rolling into turns, and looking toward the horizon near the surface of planetary bodies.  Players can also optionally activate a "look at target" mode which moves the pilot view toward the current target within their visual limit.  The strength of this mode can be customized in the setting menu and set to a toggle or a hold activation.
Many of these new features include game settings to customize or disable the effects, so please explore settings and combinations and give the updates a thorough test before leaving feedback! Keep in mind, we aren't quite finished with the feature but want to put it out and get some thoughts. We also an optional survey to better help us figure out where to go from here.
