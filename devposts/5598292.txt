Greetings!
We have introduced new race tracks and time trial races scattered throughout Stanton. The race tracks are currently all unlocked, though later in the PTU cycle we will be setting them up to sequentially unlock with increasing race reputation. Grab your favorite racer or whatever ship you feel like setting a time in and give them a go! You can find each race in the racing section of the mission manager. The mission itself sets up a time trial with payouts based on platinum, gold, silver, or bronze level times. As a note, at the moment the times are not set to the intended high difficulty. So just do your best and see how fast you can go!
Let us know what you think, what issues you ran into, and which tracks are the easiest and hardest to complete. We would love to see your racing videos too!
