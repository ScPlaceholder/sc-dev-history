## Weekly Wisdom: Look Ahead, Pilot!
With the ‘look-ahead’ function, we were able to increase the immersive flight experience for all pilots further. Of course, this function can also be adjusted individually!
Open the game settings in the options menu. Towards the end of the page, you will find a variety of different options. It is worth playing around with them to support your unique flying style!
