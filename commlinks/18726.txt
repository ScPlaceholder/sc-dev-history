## Happy Monday, everyone!
And Happy Pride Month! In case you missed it, we're inviting you to celebrate Pride all monthly long by showing us your colors to win one of TEN Anvil Spartans! Be as creative and colorful as you can be, and submit your creation on Spectrum for a chance to win. Check out the contest overview for how to participate.
Last week we announced the kickoff of the Bar Citizen World Tour, which officially begins this coming weekend! We're hosting an official Bar Citizen near each of our five development studios, and we'd love to see you all there. Looking to hang out with fellow Citizens, chat Star Citizen, and meet a few of our developers? Check out the Bar Citizen World Tour Comm-Link for details.
Not near one of our studios? Fear not! It wouldn't be a world tour if it were just five cities, so we'll be taking the show on the road and join you for Bar Citizens worldwide. We're taking suggestions on where to go, so be sure to post yours in the dedicated Spectrum thread.
## Now, let’s see what’s going on this week:
This Tuesday, we'll publish the Subscriber Comm-Link for June. The Narrative team also has this week's Lore Post, which is a portfolio on Brentworth Care Center, telling the story of how Dr. Jaleel Brentworth created the most highly rated and luxurious health care facilities in the UEE.
Wednesday has the latest update for the Public Roadmap alongside a Roadmap Roundup. We'll also publish last week's June Squadron 42 Monthly Report as a comm-link.
Thursday, Inside Star Citizen returns with a primer on Stanton's favorite pirates, Nine Tails, to prepare you for 3.17.2, plus a look at changes coming to the commodity kiosk later this year.
Friday we'll have an episode of Star Citizen Live on our Twitch channel. This week we'll meet the members of the Community Team and get ready for Bar Citizen weekend, going live on Twitch at 9am Pacific / 4pm UTC. Lastly, we'll close out the week with our weekly newsletter and a Subscriber Vault Update.
Have a fantastic week, and see you at Bar Citizen!
