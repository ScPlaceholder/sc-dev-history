## Weekly Wisdom: Quantum Linking
If you want to travel large distances as a party, use Quantum Linking! This allows the leader of a party to initiate quantum travel and any nearby party members to jump alongside them.
As the leader: Simply follow the same procedure as for a solo jump. When lining up your ship you’ll see additional information. The names and circles indicate who is in the party and who is ready to jump. Once the circles next to each party member fill up, they’re ready to engage. When everyone is ready, jump and the party will follow.
As a party member: When your nearby party leader initiates a quantum jump, you will see the normal quantum jump UI appear. Align with the target destination and spool up your engines. When the party leader jumps, you’ll automatically join them.
