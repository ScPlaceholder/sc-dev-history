TERRAPIN MEDIC
ARMORED EMERGENCY RESPONSE VEHICLE
SUPPORT WHERE IT’S NEEDED MOST
Sporting the same robust frame and armor as its pathfinding predecessor, the Terrapin Medic is outfitted with a fully loaded military-style medical bay complete with Tier 2 MedBed. A true miniature frontline trauma center or robust search and rescue vessel, the Terrapin Medic and her crew save lives and keep operations afloat in the harshest environments and situations.
ON BOARD MEDICAL FACILITIES
The Terrapin’s cabin is replaced with a comprehensive medical bay, including a Tier 2 MedBed. Get your allies patched up and back in the fight.
DURABLE BY DESIGN
The Terrapin chassis is renowned for its unbreakable frame. And, thanks to heavyweight armor, the Medic thrives in combat situations and harsh locations.
