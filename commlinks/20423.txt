Behind the Propaganda
Citizens For Prosperity vs Headhunters
James Herriman : Pyro Correspondent
Choosing loyalties during times of conflict is not only fraught with bias and
panic but also with the fear that the wrong decision could cost us more than
we imagine. Our writers share their cases for the Citizens for Prosperity and
the Headhunters, the two opposing groups hoping to achieve the same goal -
to protect Pyro from destruction.
Peace in Pyro
Support the Citizens
During my time with Citizens for Prosperity,
I learned one key thing about them. They are who
they say they are. Far from being a dig at the
Slicers, this is a heartfelt endorsement of an
organization that puts lives on the line daily
to provide and protect. With their dedication to
mining, resource gathering, and distribution to the
people, the CFP is the only choice for the future
of Pyro.
Fire With Fire
Support Headhunters
Many onlookers, with views akin to the Frontier
Fighters, incite fear as a means to justify the
vilification of our neighbors. Now, the very violence
and willingness to take what they need, for which the
Headhunters have been chastised, is set to be the
system's salvation. We must face the fact that the
gang we love to hate might instead be the gang we
hate to need.
