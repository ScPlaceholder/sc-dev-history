## Greetings Citizens,
Are you looking for a last-minute gift? By popular demand, we’ve added digital gift cards to the Star Citizen pledge store! If you’d like to introduce a friend to Star Citizen but aren’t sure what ship they’d want to pledge for, you can now pick up an allotment of store credit. Just check out like any other package, and then select “gift” from your My Hangar area to send it to a friend!
