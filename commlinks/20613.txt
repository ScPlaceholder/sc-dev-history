## BEHIND THE BADGE
Although much is known of the accolades of our proud 42nd Squadron, inside intel of these history-making achievements has been for military eyes only - until now. The 42nd Squadron made their mark during the Battle for Centauri, a standoff which ultimately concluded the second Tevarin war, when, under the leadership of Captain Alexandra Dunlevy, they feigned retreat in order to form an ambush of the Tevarin fleet.
This tactical maneuver undoubtedly led to the UEEN’s victory and is still studied by new recruits and military personnel to this day. Join us, as Invictus 2955 comes to an end, in celebrating not only our serving fleet, but also commemorating the ships, crew and lives of some of the UEEN’s finest, the 42nd Squadron.
