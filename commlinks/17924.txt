## Weekly Wisdom: Don’t fly – Drive!
Why always move 25 tons when you want to enjoy the spectacular landscapes of microTech? Leave your spaceship in the hangar, head to the New Babbage spaceport garages, and spawn a ground vehicle. Follow the stairs leading up past the gift store and the ship spawn consoles, and you will find the vehicle consoles to your left. Once you're chasing between the snow-covered trees, you won't miss your ship.
