## TWITCH DROPS:
## RSI DISCOVERY MONTH
From October 14 to 27, celebrate RSI Discovery Month and the legacy of the Constellation by watching any Drops-enabled Star Citizen stream for a total of four hours to unlock the Phoenix Insignia Display for free. Inspired by the signature fiery insignia found aboard the classic Constellation Phoenix, this holographic display brings a piece of RSI history straight to your hangar.
What are Twitch Drops?
Twitch Drops are rewards that players can earn by watching participating Twitch streams. When you link your Star Citizen account to your Twitch account and watch any eligible Star Citizen stream for 4 hours, you’ll automatically unlock in-game items as rewards. Once you meet the watch-time requirement, your reward will appear in your Twitch inventory and can then be claimed to your Star Citizen account.
For this campaign, the featured reward is the Phoenix Insignia Display.
How long will the current campaign last?
This Drops campaign runs from October 14 through October 27. Make sure to tune in to eligible streams or subscribe to a participating creator before the campaign ends.
How do I participate?
To participate, you need to link your Twitch and RSI accounts (see above for instructions).
Then watch any Star Citizen live stream marked “Drops Enabled.”
Once you’ve watched for 4 hours, claim your Drop on Twitch. It will appear in your RSI Hangar shortly after.
Which live streams count toward earning Drops?
Any Star Citizen stream on Twitch marked "Drops Enabled" counts toward the reward. Starting October 14, with the launch of RSI Discovery Month, any community streamer who opts in to Twitch Drops will be eligible to host the campaign and have their viewers earn rewards.
How can I check my progress or claim a Drop?
You can track your progress on the Twitch Drops Inventory page while logged in to your Twitch account.
When your watch-time or subscription goal is complete, click “Claim” on Twitch to receive the Drop.
Do I need Amazon Luna (Prime Gaming) to be eligible?
No. You don't need Amazon Luna or Prime Gaming. A regular Twitch account linked to your RSI account is all you need to participate.
Can I unlink or relink my Twitch account?
Yes. You can unlink your Twitch account anytime from your RSI Account Settings under Linked Accounts. To relink, simply repeat the connection process shown above.
Can I link multiple Twitch accounts to one RSI account, or multiple RSI accounts to one Twitch account?
No. Each RSI account can be linked to only one Twitch account, and vice versa. If you want to connect to a different account, you’ll need to unlink the previous account first.
My Drop didn’t appear in my RSI account. What should I do?
First, make sure you:
- Linked the correct Twitch and RSI accounts.
- Watched streams or subscribed using the linked Twitch account.
- Claimed the Drop on Twitch.
- Are logging into the correct RSI account.
If the Drop still hasn’t appeared after a while but it is showing that it has unlocked on Twitch, please contact Player Support for assistance.
