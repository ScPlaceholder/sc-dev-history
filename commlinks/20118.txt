## Happy Monday, everyone!
Last week, our team had an amazing time connecting with hundreds of you in Cologne. Whether we were catching up with familiar faces or welcoming new pilots, Gamescom has become a highlight of our Bar Citizen tour, with many of you traveling from across Europe to join us. Be sure to check out the photo gallery below the content schedule for a glimpse of the event. Thank you to everyone who came out, and we can’t wait to see you all again soon!
Join us in Seattle next week on August 30th as our team heads to PAX West! We’d love to see you at the Bar Citizen, where the fun begins at 7 PM at Stoup Brewing. Don't miss out!
In case you missed it, we recently restocked a limited batch of CitizenCon 2954 General Admission tickets. If you kicked yourself for not picking one up earlier, now’s your chance! We won't be restocking again, so secure your spot and join us at this year’s big event!
Meanwhile, the excitement in the 'verse ramps up as we enter the final duel of the Top 8 in this year's Ship Showdown! Today, two newcomers face off: Anvil’s formidable F8C heavy fighter and Gatac’s newly Human-ized Syulen light freighter. Cast your votes now to decide who advances to the Final Four, which will battle it out tomorrow before taking the spotlight during the Best in Show days at the Intergalactic Aerospace Expo later this year!
And finally, a huge thank you to everyone testing Alpha 3.24 on the PTU! Your support is invaluable as we prepare to launch the patch, bringing exciting updates for cargo haulers and citizens from all corners of the 'verse.
