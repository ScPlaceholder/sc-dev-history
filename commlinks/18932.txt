Introducing the New Community Hub
Share and discover content like never before
Every day we're taken aback by the incredible content flowing out of our Community. From a tiny 3D printed Mercury Star Runner, to a life-size sandcastle replica of a 300i, our community never stops raising the bar.
Content this consistently amazing of course deserves a platform of equal ambition: cue the new Community Hub, a platform where you can browse and share content, follow your favorite creators, even build a fan following yourself. Read on.
We'd love to hear your feedback on the new Community Hub, so please let us know what you think on Spectrum.
What's Next?
This is truly just the beginning - we'll be expanding this platform in the months ahead, and we want your help in doing so. We've already got several major features in active development, which you can read about below, but we're also eager to hear your input and suggestions.
Check out some of the bigger milestones we've got planned, then head over to our dedicated Spectrum feedback thread where you can share your thoughts and ideas. As always, THANK YOU for making this the best damn community in all of gaming.
