Star Citizen’s Art Producer, Harry Jarvis, is no stranger to space sims or Chris Roberts games! With a list of credits stretching back to the original Wing Commanders, Harry has just the experience we need to bring together Star Citizen’s world. You can meet him on this week’s Wingman’s Hangar, Friday at 11 AM CST (-5 GMT.)
## How did you get started in the game industry?
I was hired by Chris Roberts to be his Assistant Director on the live action shoot for Wing Commander III
## What projects have you worked on?
Wing Commander III & IV, Freelancer, GoldenEye: Rogue Agent, 007: From Russia with Love, C&C 3 & 4, Red Alert 3, expansion packs and DLC for the RTS games, Sleeping Dogs
## What will you be doing for Cloud Imperium?
Art Producer & Outsource Manager
## What are you most excited to see in Star Citizen/Squadron 42?
I loved the exploration aspects of Freelancer, so seein that get expanded to as far as we can take it ten years later is really exciting. I’m also huge on story-telling and cinematics being done right in games.
## What are you playing right now?
Last of Us PS3, Ni No Kuni PS3, Limbo iOS (finally out!), Game of Thrones 2nd Edition Board Game
