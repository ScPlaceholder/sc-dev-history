## Greetings Citizens,
We’re pleased to announce that we have published an update to the Ship Specifications page. The ship specs area offers the nuts-and-bolts technical details for each ship available in Star Citizen, with more added as they are revealed! The new update includes the Hornet variants and a number of corrections and balancing updates.
Remember: the specifications listed are not finished! We are continuing to refine and balance Star Citizen. This will continue through the game’s release. The specifications page is an effort to show you how we are treating ship balancing at the moment; the data may well change as development continues.
