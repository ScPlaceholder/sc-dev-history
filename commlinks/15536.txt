FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
## Is the Avalon Hollywood wheelchair accessible?
Yes, please notify us in advance of your needs through the CS ticket system.
## Will there be food available at the venue?
Snacks and full meals will be available to purchase at the venue after the conclusion of the presentation. Meal examples include burgers, pizza, nachos and more.
## Can I bring my own food and drink?
No, this is not allowed for the venue.
## Will there be seating available?
Yes, this is a seated event.
## Is there an age restriction for the venue?
Yes, the event is 21+. Please bring ID to ensure admittance.
## Can people bring their own seating for the wait?
You may bring a small seat to use while outside the theatre, however use of them inside the premises is prohibited. Please only bring what you’ll need with you to the event, as well as your Star Citizen merchandise!
## Is there a Coat Check?
Yes, the charge is $3. Please keep in mind that bags and other items may need to be checked due to space restrictions and fire regulations.
## Is there parking available?
There is paid parking next door.
## What time does the party finish?
All must exit the Avalon by 10pm, however 33 Taps across the street is open until 2am.
