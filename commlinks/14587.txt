The March Subscriber Flair is the coveted Patron of the Arts Award plaque given by the very prestigious Imperial Arts Foundation! The award is usually reserved for citizens who contribute to the arts but the Foundation wanted to acknowledge Subscribers continued contributions.
“The Imperial Arts Foundation is pleased to present you with this plaque in recognition of your continued support in bringing artistic content to the populace at large”.
