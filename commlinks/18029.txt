ALL-NEW VEHICLES & PAINTS
Alpha 3.13: Underground Infamy
Alpha 3.13 adds two vehicles and a wealth of ship paints, giving every citizen the opportunity to tackle the 'verse in something new.
Available now, you can lift the appearance of your Nomad or Mercury and head into Alpha 3.13 with a fresh look. Both ships have three paints to choose from, each with unique details that help them blend into the black of space or stand out for miles. Pick your favorite or choose the Paint Pack to tailor your appearance to the task at hand.
Read on for more about the new vehicles rolling into Alpha 3.13…
Greycat ROC-DS
The DS is the two-seat variant of the ubiquitous Greycat ROC mining vehicle. You and a partner can work more effectively for longer thanks to a hugely flexible manual mining arm and more than double the ore storage of the original ROC.
Tumbril Cyclone MT
Tumbril’s Cyclone MT takes everything good about the base variant and adds a Size 2 missile launcher and ballistic cannon. Designed to protect grounded ships and bases from airborne attack, the MT adds an explosive edge to the tried-and-tested Cyclone formula.
