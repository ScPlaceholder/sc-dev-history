Time to get rid of that old dusty wallpaper, and freshen up your screens with some fresh, new, and vibrant Star Citizen images. That’s right, it’s time for another wallpapers fan spotlight! This time we’re not stopping at your computer monitor. Oh no, we’re going to mobile as well, so let’s get started!
AC_Dassault starts things off with his rendition of the Carrack. This piece stood out to us as it feels like a clever blend of Star Citizen and the popular Starry Night. If you enjoy this, AC_Dassault has many other wallpapers that look just as amazing.
Check out his post!
Maximus shows us what how beautiful and serene space can truly be. Watching a couple of Gladius’ and a Carrack cut through clouds brings a bit of tranquility to an otherwise dangerous unknown area. If you’re looking for a bit more peace in your backgrounds, this is the one for you.
Check out his post!
If you’re looking to show off your Star Citizen love on the go, then look no further. Nehkara15’s mobile wallpapers are the perfect accessory for the latest Apple or Android device. In fact, his ArcCorp entry is so amazing that I’m using it myself!
Check out his post!
HardyHero brings something for those that want to be a bit more devious in the ‘Verse. Fellow pirates can use this wallpaper to gladly show where they stand on UEE law and to let anybody know that all they want to do is… talk. If you’re a fan of this piece you should check out his other work as he has a lot of wallpapers to choose from.
Check out his post!
