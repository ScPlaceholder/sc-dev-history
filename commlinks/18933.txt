## Happy Monday, everyone!
Over the weekend, we broadcast live on Twitch for CitizenCon 2952, our annual event celebrating Star Citizen and all of YOU who makes our shared universe possible. Although we were not able to gather in person this year (we’ll make up for it next year!), that didn’t stop us from a fun-filled day with development deep dives, surprise reveals, giveaways, contests, and more – all to celebrate our 10-year anniversary.
If you missed any of the action, we've got you covered, as we've already uploaded the developer panels to YouTube in beautiful 4k quality. We'll also upload the full show to YouTube soon, including the interview with Richard Tyrer and Chris Roberts on how the development of Squadron 42 will benefit the PU.
We're also proud to announce the launch of the new and improved Community Hub that will not only tremendously enhance your experience but also spotlight your creativity in a seamless way. Be sure to check out all the features and help us continue our efforts by sending us your feedback. We've already got some major additions in the works - more news coming soon!
Lastly, we've seen a lot of excitement/debate around the new features presented at CitizenCon. We're reading everything and look forward to diving into more details soon, answering your questions, and hearing more of your feedback.
