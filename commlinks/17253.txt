## Weekly Wisdom: Turtles
Did you know you can save (steal) turtles from certain doom (prevent them from being used to feed the citizens of ArcCorp)? Explore the edges of Area 18 and you may just spot a turtle, lying in peril near a food vendor’s cart. You can rescue it by picking it up and transporting it to safety in your hab, or bring your new best friend adventuring alongside you by placing them in your cockpit.
