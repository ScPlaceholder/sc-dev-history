## Yuri’s Night around Star Citizen bars by SpaceGamer
Yuri’s Night parties and events are held around the world every April in commemoration of Yuri Gagarin becoming the first human to venture into space on April 12, 1961. SpaceGamer and his friends brought the festivities into the ‘verse and celebrated Space Travel in the Bars of Star Citizen.
