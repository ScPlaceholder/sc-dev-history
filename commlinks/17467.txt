## Weekly Wisdom: Expedited Fee
Those who have ‘lost’ their ship can, thanks to insurance cover, re-claim it at a ship retrieval console. Touch the screen anywhere to begin and scroll to the lost vehicle. Within the Actions-column, press Claim and File a Claim to make use of your insurance on the next screen.
As we all know, the mills of administration grind slowly. However, with some extra aUEC in your pocket, you can speed up the process by paying an Expedited Fee. That way, you’ll be back to the stars in no time!
