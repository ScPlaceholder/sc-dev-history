## IN-GAME REWARDS (FLAIR)
The Paladin came out of Virgil’s industrial division as a prototype helmet for salvage and repair operations, yet its striking design so impressed company executives that they integrated it into their personal armor line. While the helmet’s look stayed the same, Virgil reworked the design to use a combat rated, durable alloy for its distinct frame and reinforced the plating protecting the back of the head.
## Savior Collection “Singularity” Paladin Helmet – This black and silver variant of Virgil’s new Paladin helmet is a perfect fit for the matching set of ORC-mkX armors sold throughout Stanton.
Current Centurion-level Subscribers get Virgil’s Paladin Helmet Black/Silver seen here as part of their subscription.
## Savior Collection “Iceborn” Paladin Helmet – This white and black variant is fit for any knightly or heroic champion and fits with a number of armors from Stanton’s vendors, including the new “Iceborn” ORC-mkX.
Current Imperator-level Subscribers get both Virgil’s Paladin Helmet Black/Silver and Paladin Helmet White/Black as part of their subscription.
## Savior Collection “Nightfire” Paladin Helmet – Arguably the most stylish of the bunch, this black, gold, and red variant says you’re not afraid to stand out in a crowd. And when paired with the matching armor from CDS, you’ll look as good as you feel.
The Savior Collection “Nightfire” Paladin Helmet is available to all Subscribers for purchase in the Subscriber-Exclusive Store.
