## Welcome to Citizens of the Stars, our weekly program exclusively about you, the Star Citizen Community. Whether through bug reports on the issue council, feedback on the forums, or the creation of content inspired by the game, Star Citizen is only possible because of the community. Citizens of the Stars not only highlights these contributions, but also puts a dev in the hotseat for a round of Quantum Questions, which addresses some fun facts about the dev and game.
## Frustmaster discusses how he makes his cinematic videos, and then Cinematographer JJ La Britt provides details on making various Star Citizen shows during Quantum Questions.
