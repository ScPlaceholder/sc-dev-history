Happy Monday, everyone.
February’s Free Fly is in full swing, and we want to take a moment to say thank you for being the most welcoming community in gaming! We’ve seen that many of you have already joined the Guide System and made new citizens’ first steps into the ‘verse truly epic!
If you didn’t know, through February 25, Star Citizen is completely free to download and play, so invite a friend to join you in the ‘verse – but don’t forget about the referral program. Recruiting a friend will grant you both the Lodestone armor and sniper rifle set from Quirinus Tech and Gemini for free! Check out the Free Fly Referral Bonus comm-link for details.
And don’t forget to jump into our ongoing Dynamic Events running until March 6. Check out the full Alpha 3.16.1 Dynamic Events Schedule on Spectrum to experience the mayhem of Jumptown 2.0 and Nine Tails Lockdown.
## Now, let’s see what’s going on this week:
This Tuesday, the Narrative team is back, with more content being added to our ever-expanding Galactapedia.
Inside Star Citizen returns on Thursday with an Origin Story highlighting another CIG team member, followed by an all-vehicle Sprint Report.
On Friday, Star Citizen Live will be rounding out the week on our Star Citizen Twitch channel with members of the EU PU Gameplay Feature team. The broadcast will begin at 9am Pacific / 5pm UTC, so be sure to get your questions posted to the Spectrum thread.
UPDATE: Out of respect for current world events, we have canceled today’s Star Citizen Live. Our hearts go out to those affected.
You also get our weekly RSI Newsletter delivered to your inbox, and Subscribers can look forward to an update to the Subscriber Vault and the February issue of Jump Point magazine.
Have a stellar week both in and out of the ‘verse! (Thanks Sitr)
