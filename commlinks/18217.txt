THE ARK, TAYAC SYSTEM
Welcome to this month’s Galactapedia update roundup. This month, we looked at the history of Trise Cordial, shared information about the Nyx and Davien systems, explored the ins and outs of quantum drives, and described the popular show StarWatch, among other subjects. Join the Spectrum thread for any discussion or feedback.
## Full-Length Article:
- Trise cordial – A Human liqueur with Banu origins.
## Short Articles:
- Nyx system – The system’s star was named for the Greek goddess of the night.
- Nyx I – A former hot spot of mining activity.
- Nyx II – Would be a happening place if not for all the poison in the atmosphere.
- Nyx III – Beautiful and deadly.
- Delamar – A revolutionary asteroid.
- Glaciem ring (Nyx belt alpha) – Some asteroids might be bases for pirates.
- Railen – A direct result of the Human-Xi’an Trade Initiative.
- Quantum drive – Necessary to drive your ship between distant planets without dying of old age before you arrive.
- First Jump Day – One small jump for Nick, one giant leap for Humans.
- Davien system – Quite a few politically important events have happened in Davien.
- Davien I – If you sliced this planet in half, it would probably look like a ball of foam.
- Cestulus (Davien II) – Humans eager to meet Banu moved here after Human-Banu First Contact.
- Penselin (Davien 2a) – Be sure to pencil in a visit to this moon during your stay on Cestulus.
- Davien III – One of the hottest spots in Davien.
- Davien IV – Check out the Banu Friendship Museum for reasonably priced souvenirs.
- Chemline Solutions – A company that was founded and ultimately dismantled by twin siblings.
- Parker Terrell – Some say Terrell has a loose definition of the word “truth.”
- StarWatch – The most popular show in the known universe.
- B0otycall – You have to be in the right place at the right time to catch B0otycall.
- Starburst Collective – Music critics were ecstatic when this band came back from hiatus.
