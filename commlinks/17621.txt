Happy Monday everyone!
Invictus Launch Week is in full swing, and amidst all the festivities, we are aware that not everyone can enjoy the event as planned. While great progress has been made, our team continues to work hard on crushing and solving any remaining issues that prevent you from playing right now. As a reminder, we recently released the Status Update page to help keep you in the know with how things are going.
We know some of you have missed out on playing so far, so we’ve extended the Free Fly until June 2nd and we’ll continue to monitor and make adjustments as needed. We’re committed to keeping you all in the loop and will be in close communication. Keep an eye on Spectrum for further updates.
We’ve already seen an incredible amount of community-created content and we encourage you to dive in and check out the Community Hub! Personally speaking, there aren’t a lot of things I love more than spaceships, and watching the UEE Navy arriving seriously brings a smile to my face. Thanks to you, we already have a ton of great videos showing off the fleet, and we’re still hungry for more.
Has exploring those screenshots and clips woke your inner artist? We got you covered as we kicked off not one, but two community contests last week. Whether you want to create a recruitment poster that will delight and inspire future UEE soldiers, or pull off your most stunning flight maneuvers, there’s something for everyone.
Looking for more inspiration? Then follow our Invictus Launch Week Guide and marvel at the most iconic war machines at the Bevic Convention Center!

Enjoy Invictus Launch Week and we’ll see you in the Bevic Convention Center!

## Weekly Wisdom: Where is the Navy?
Despite its size, the UEE Navy fleet can be hard to find in the expanse of the ‘verse. It shuttles between the space stations: Port Tressler above microTech, Everus Harbor above Hurston, and Baijini Point above ArcCorp. Look out for fireworks, or check out your Star Map on your mobiGlass and follow the fleet’s Quantum Beacon.
