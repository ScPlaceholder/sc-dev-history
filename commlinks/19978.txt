## Happy Monday, everyone!
Invictus Launch Week is once again upon us! From now through May 29, marvel at the sheer power and precision of the United Empire of Earth Navy (UEEN) through ship tours, military displays, and breathtaking aerial maneuvers as the impressive fleet makes its way across the Stanton system. Join us as we honor the formidable strength and unwavering commitment of the UEEN in keeping the Empire safe! You, too, can show us that you're doing your part for the good of the Empire and be handsomely rewarded for your efforts! Whether you're an ace pilot or an industrious miner, submit a creative display of your talents to our currently ongoing community contest and stand a chance at winning some fantastic prizes.
Further, don't miss out on test-flying the best military-derived ships at the Bevic Convention Center, as we're hosting a Free Fly during the entire duration of the event! To help you see all the cool sights and take part in the fun festivities, we've compiled a comprehensive FAQ, which answers many commonly asked questions about the big event.
## Now, let's see what's going on this week:
Tuesday sees the publish of the Aegis Sabre Firebird Q&A comm-link, with answers straight from the designers of this hot new vehicle!
This Thursday, in the last Inside Star Citizen episode of the Alpha 3.23 season, get your first look at the new concept arriving at this year's Drake DefenseCon. Then, tour the current greybox interior of the RSI Polaris, currently flying its way around the Stanton system during this year's Invictus Launch Week.
On Friday's Star Citizen Live, we're gathering members from the Vehicle Gameplay Feature teams to discuss aspects related to vehicles including the upcoming Engineering systems, newly released Master Modes, ground-vehicle improvements, ongoing balance work, and more. Don't forget to submit your questions for consideration on the Spectrum thread. The show starts at 8 AM Pacific / 15:00 UTC on Twitch. You'll also find our weekly RSI Newsletter directly delivered to your inbox.
Finally, Saturday will see the finals of the Stanton Seven series; one final race before a champion is crowned! Catch the action on ATMO's Twitch channel from 19:00 UTC.
Fly low, and fly fast!
