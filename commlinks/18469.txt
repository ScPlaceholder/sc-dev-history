Happy Monday, everyone!
Last week, we said farewell to the Intergalactic Aerospace Expo, and with it, Jax and Jimmy’s latest adventures. Thanks to everyone (both veterans and new players alike!) who came to visit the expo this year! We’d like to give an extra special shoutout to those veteran pilots we saw welcoming new folks to the ‘verse, helping make their first steps go smoothly.
Fast forward to this week, and we redirect the spotlight to our upcoming Luminalia celebration, which go live this Thursday, December 9th. We’ve gone ahead and made discounted starter packages available for those looking to get a head start, but you’ll definitely want to check back later this week for the full event. This year promises to be our best Luminalia celebration yet, with more gifts coming your way than ever before – seriously – don’t miss out on this one!
## Now, let’s see what’s going on this week:
This Tuesday, the Narrative team will publish a new episode of Loremakers: Community Questions. We also have our Subscriber Comm-Link and newsletter detailing December’s Subscriber benefits!
Wednesday we’ll publish answers to your questions about the self-sufficient exploration vessel, the MISC Odyssey.
Thursday welcomes a new episode of Inside Star Citizen where we’ll introduce you to the latest grav-lev improvements, and take a look at Jumptown 2.0. We’ll also add a sleigh-load of fun by kicking off our Luminalia celebration.
On Friday, you’ll see an update to the Subscriber Vault and the RSI Newsletter will wait for you in your inbox. There won’t be an episode of Star Citizen Live this week, as our developers are heads down working on 3.16, and we want to let them focus on that.
Have a great week, everyone.
Christian ‘Wayne-CIG’ Schmitt
Community Manager
