## HUMANITY'S MOST VERSATILE WEAPONS PLATFORM
One of Humanity’s most powerful warbirds, the Retaliator is a fearsome weapons platform originally designed to strike and kill capital ships.
A key portion of the UEE Navy’s power projection, Retaliator squadrons have served with distinction against the Vanduul, outlaw gangs,
and countless other threats to the Empire.
The ship’s interior can be outfitted to suit all operational needs via two modules that add further cargo space or dedicated torpedo bays.
## MORE THAN JUST A GUNSHIP
The Retaliator is fully customizable via specially designed modules that instantly expand its functionality; fly as a heavily armed hauler, destructive torpedo platform, or versatile combination of both.
Disclaimer for concept modules:
For in-game functionality, a vehicle module must be equipped in-game into the appropriate vehicle type. The Retaliator Hab and Dropship modules are being offered as limited vehicle module concept pledges. This means that these modules are in development, but are not yet ready to play in Star Citizen. They will be available as playable content in a later patch.
## CARGO-ENABLED
When outfitted with full bow-to-stern cargo modules, the Retaliator becomes a well-protected freighter, adept at transporting cargo and small vehicles through dangerous territory with minimal risk.
## TORPEDO-ENABLED
When outfitted with full bow-to-stern torpedo modules, the Retaliator becomes a devastating bomber, armed with a formidable payload of Size 9 torpedoes.
