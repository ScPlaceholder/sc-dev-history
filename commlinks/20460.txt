THE IDRIS PEACEKEEPER
For the leaders, the virtuous and the truly courageous, the Idris Peacekeeper has arrived. A ship that secured our past and continues to define our future, this steadfast, tactical, civilian class incarnation of the UEEN darling is available to fly for the first time. Whilst maintaining the same potential for destruction and fighter capacity as its military sister ship, the Idris-M, the newly flyable Idris Peacekeeper combined with the K and T kits allows each crew to completely customize their desired artillery, an incredible accomplishment in tactical ship building.
A limited number of Idris frigates are available to Chairman’s Club members only.
