## Happy Monday, everyone!
The galaxy's premier annual event, the Intergalactic Aerospace Expo (IAE) is in full swing on microTech! Don't forget to visit the expo hall every day as a new manufacturer takes over to showcase their biggest and best vehicles. The festivities will continue for another nine days, so invite your friends to check it out with you and take full advantage of the Free Fly!
For those of you with a keen eye for photography and composition, there is also an IAE-themed Screenshot Contest going on where you can be one of the lucky winners of some shiny, new ships; if you can rise to the challenge.
As we approach the end of 2022, we'll be heading to the final stops of the Bar Citizen World Tour for this year. Will we have the pleasure of seeing you in Milan on November 26, Berlin on December 3 (soon-to-be-announced), or Denver on January 13? Head over to the Bar Citizen World Tour page for more information.
With all the exciting things going on, it might be a great idea to keep an eye on Spectrum for the latest announcements, the FAQ, and more as we move closer to Intergalactic Aerospace Expo's finale!
## Now, let's see what's going on this week:
This Tuesday brings about the next installment of Loremakers: Community Questions, where the Narrative team will answer ten questions from the community.
Wednesday will have the latest Roadmap Update, alongside a Roadmap Roundup with all the details.
Thursday's Inside Star Citizen will be IAE-themed once again, to complement the ongoing festivities. We've got ships GALORE!
This Friday, there will be no Star Citizen Live due to our offices being closed for the holiday. You’ll still receive our weekly Newsletter delivered to your inbox, and Subscribers can look forward to an update to the Subscriber Vault.
Lastly, a warm hello to everyone in the 'verse! As you've probably noticed, mine is a new name on these posts. You can call me Freyja and I'm honored to be the newest addition to the Community Team!
