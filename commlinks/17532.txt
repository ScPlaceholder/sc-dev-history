## Weekly Wisdom: PTU Waves
The Public Test Universe (PTU) is a replica environment of the servers used to operate the game and the platform. This environment exists to allow public-testing phases of unreleased patches of the game and platform, but under tight access control.
Once a build is stabilized and features are completed, we roll that build out to a wider audience. The current PTU release format is as follows:
• Evocati: Our smallest group of testers who help us get our releases ready for a proper public viewing more quickly! They’re picked from the Issue Council posters with multiple submissions or contributions and from our most active PTU testers.
• Wave 1: All RSI accounts with active subscriptions and all our Concierge members will be invited to join the PTU testing when we open this wave.
• Wave 2: All members of the previous waves, plus players that have been very active and involved in the Issue Council, and players with the highest amount of playtime across all game modes. This group is also referred to as Wave 2.
• Open PTU: The PTU servers and access will be opened to all backers with a game package.
It’s of course worth mentioning that while this is the most common practice, in some unique situations for complex testing, we may deviate from the above.

## Seven different angles by SpaceGamerUK
We've all taken our fair share of Carrack screenshots by now. Some are alright, some are pretty, and some are truly unique like this fantastic compilation. SpaceGamerUK created seven different angles of his personal view on the ship using the same approach as one would in normal photography. As a result, we come to witness the play of light and shadows, the purity of the shapes, and the wonderful story behind each shot.
