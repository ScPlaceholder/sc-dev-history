Happy Monday, everyone!
Last week, we unveiled the Esperia Talon, a single-seat light-fighter infamous for its devastating hit-and-run attacks during the Tevarin Wars. We’re currently testing the Alpha 3.10.1 patch on the Test Servers, which will fix some pesky bugs that we discovered with your help in the first days of the live release.
Following the recent release of Alpha 3.10, we extended the Foundation Festival and added even more prizes! But, as all good things must eventually come to an end, you only have two more days (until August 19) to enter for the chance to snag exclusive rewards.
The Where in the ‘Verse is Pico? Contest ended last Saturday and we’re currently sifting through the many phenomenal screenshots of your favorite furry co-pilot. We’ll announce the winners, who will take home a real-life Pico and in-game Greycat ROC, soon!

## Weekly Wisdom: VEHICLE MAINTENANCE SERVICES
Is your fuel tank empty, do you only have one wing left, and barely any ammunition on board? The Vehicle Maintenance Service has you covered.
Navigate to any landing zone, R&R Truckstop, or Port Olisar, and while still neatly tucked away in the pilot seat, open your mobiGlas and select the Vehicle Maintenance Services option.
Select the services that you require (repair/restock/refuel), hit Confirm in the bottom right, and after a short while, your ship wil be ready for the next adventure!
