Greetings Citizens,
As promised on Monday, we’re proud to be able to show you the very first rendered body that will be part of Star Citizen! You saw his face, now see the rest: Artist Mark Skelton crafted the body this week. It’s a good indication of how the character will look in CryEngine in the finished game! We will use this man as a base for other characters, adapting his clothing and physical features to fit others. You will learn more about this model and the process next week when we introduce you to Mark and talk to him on Wingman’s Hangar!
