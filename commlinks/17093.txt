Hello everyone,
Following the launch of the Ranger, we took your community-voted questions to our designers to give you more information on the latest road warrior from Tumbril Land Systems. We published the Q&A last week and you should head over there to find out about the Cargo Vest, why an earthbound bike may be better than a flying one, and if you will be able to do wheelies or not. Spoilers: The designers love wheelies as much as you do!
If you missed Star Citizen Live last Friday, we strongly recommend to catch up on the replay of Patrick Gladys’ and Florian Sollaneck’s presentation of the current development of cave systems. They’ve made amazing progress since they first introduced their work in ATV back in March and we can’t wait to grab our gear and go spelunking in the ‘verse!
## Now, let’s see what’s going on this week:
Tuesday, the Lore Team publishes an updated company portfolio piece on Origin Jumpworks.
Thursday welcomes a new episode of Inside Star Citizen, our weekly look behind the scenes of Star Citizen development.
Friday will see a Roadmap update and the RSI Newsletter delivered right to your inbox. We also welcome a new episode of Star Citizen Live, which broadcasts on our Star Citizen Channel. Keep an eye out on Spectrum for the weekly question gathering thread and to find out who our guests will be.
See you in the ‘verse!
