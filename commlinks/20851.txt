## Happy post-CitizenCon Direct, everyone!
This year’s big event may have been beamed digitally, but it didn’t stop the excitement from spreading far and wide. Across North America and Europe, members of our team joined nine simultaneous CitizenCon Direct 2955 Watch Parties, sharing reactions, cheering on the reveals, and celebrating the 'verse that many of us call a home away from home. The members of our team who were fortunate enough to attend in person and share the day with you were met with a vivid reminder of the energy, creativity, and dedication that make this community truly one of a kind. We'd also like to extend a MASSIVE thank you to all the Watch Party organizers for their incredible efforts in making these events possible!
For those of you who couldn’t make it to a Watch Party, missed the live broadcast, or just want to revisit your favorite bits of the show, the full CitizenCon Direct 2955 stream is available on YouTube.
CitizenCon Direct gave us a glimpse of what’s coming, sooner than you'd expect. Get ready to welcome Nyx, our third star system (and the second to be released in the same calendar year!), in an upcoming PTU patch on the horizon — stay tuned!
And for those eager to jump back into the action, Alpha 4.3.2 is scheduled to hit the live PU later this week, bringing the thrilling conclusion of the Pyro Must Burn storyline, fresh updates, and plenty of reasons to dive back in.
Meanwhile, there's still time to join in on The Day of the Vara festivities! Whether you’re painting pumpkins and gouging gourds, or weaving wicked words for your short story submission, there’s plenty of opportunity to show off your creativity and snag a thrilling prize. And, Arena Commander goes spooky! Conquer the FPS Kill Collector Mode (and your fears) and unlock classic Halloween masks.
Fly low and fly fast!
