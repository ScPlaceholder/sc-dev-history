Fly Like a Pirate
A pirate captain’s only as good as their trusty ship, and Drake Interplanetary remains a perennial favorite shipmaker among the skull and bones crowd, despite efforts to clean up its act, corporately speaking.
And you didn’t hear this from me, but a squawky space parrot told me that if you head on over to the Pledge Store, a bevy of Drake ships are being offered to celebrate this weekend's #TalkLikeAPirateDay for a limited time throughout the duration of this piratical promotion. That means everything from the brazen Buccaneer to the crafty Cutlass, and even the colossal Caterpillar, can be yours. But act now, as the changing of the tide comes fast.
## Disclaimer: The Drake Interplanetary Vulture and Drake Interplanetary Corsair are being offered here as a limited vehicle concept pledge. This means that the vehicle is in development but is not yet ready to display in your Hangar or fly in Star Citizen. It will be available as playable content in a later patch. In the future, the vehicle price may increase and Lifetime Insurance or any extras may not be available. If you pledge towards a Drake Interplanetary Vulture and Drake Interplanetary Corsair, you will receive a loaner vehicle for use in Star Citizen until such time as the Drake Interplanetary Vulture and Drake Interplanetary Corsair is included in-game. This loaner vehicle will be a currently playable vehicle of similar approximate size and/or function to the concept ship pledged. We offer pledge ships to help fund Star Citizen’s development. The funding received from vehicles such as this allows us to include deeper features in the Star Citizen world. These vehicles will be available for in-game credits and/or will be otherwise earnable through play in the final universe. They are not required to start or succeed at the game.
