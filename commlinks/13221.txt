We recently shared concept art of the initial ‘discount’ hangar that comes with Aurora and 300-series spacecraft. Today, artist Forrest Stephan has collected some of the custom artwork used to design the second level ‘business’ hangar. This is where you’ll find your Freelancer or Hornet class spacecraft.
You’ll see more of all three initial hangar classes this weekend during Chris Roberts’ Gamescom presentation. It will be livestreamed right here at RSI on Saturday, so be sure to tune in!
