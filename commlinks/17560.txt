## Weekly Wisdom: Keep the Peace & Collect a Paycheck
With 3.9 right on the horizon, we figured there’s no time like the present to remind you that you can get paid for hunting down the ‘verse’s most notorious criminals.
Navigate to the Contracts Manager in your mobiGlas, and check out the “Bounty Hunter” section of the General tab to see if there’s anyone in need of your particular set of skills. If it’s your first venture into the wonderful world of bounty hunting, look for missions offered by “Northrock Service Group” such as the “Pro Tem Bounty Contract” to unlock the ability to go after the most dangerous targets in the system, including other players!
With Alpha 3.9 comes the first version of Prisons, and any convict that manages to weasel their way past the guards and steal their freedom will have a mighty fine bounty on their head – perfect for those looking to make a hefty lawful paycheck.
