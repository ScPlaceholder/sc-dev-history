Happy Monday, everyone!
Gather your favorite snacks and beverages and find a comfy place to kick it, as we’re only five days away from CitizenCon! Not only will this set you up to enjoy the show, but it also might win you a custom XenoThreat headset by Astro Gaming!
Stay in the loop with all things CitizenCon on the official webpage, with additional details including panel times coming later this week. You can also subscribe for hour-to-hour updates to ensure you don’t miss a thing this Saturday!
If you’re looking something a bit more spooky, we invite you to join us for our month-long Spooktober celebration, as the horror from beyond the stars has made its way to the ‘verse. You can either carve your way to fabulous prizes or show off your raw speed and steely nerves in the first Cave Runner’s Contest. And, last but not least, Drake joined the Day of the Vara with exquisite ghoulish green paints for a variety of their ships.
## Now, let’s see what’s going on this week:
On Tuesday, the Narrative team is back, publishing the Jump Point lore post Sojourner Travelogue, in which a sojourner snags a ride on a Reclaimer only to find themselves forced to face a past they were trying to avoid. We also have our Subscriber Comm-Link and newsletter detailing October’s Subscriber benefits!
Wednesday will not only see the bi-weekly Roadmap Update and Roadmap Roundup but also September’s PU monthly report Comm-Link published and the Squadron 42 report send out via email. Thus we recommend subscribing to the Squadron 42 newsletter here.
Thursday won’t see an episode of Inside Star Citizen as we brought the quarterly season to a close last week, but fear not, the show will resume in just a few weeks! Also, shoutout to Jared for successfully rhyming every episode title this season.
On Friday, you’ll see an update to the Subscriber Vault and the RSI Newsletter will wait for you in your inbox. Due to Digital CitzenCon this week, we won’t air an episode of Star Citizen Live.
Saturday, join us on the Star Citizen Twitch channel at 3pm UTC for CITIZENCON 2951!
Have a fantastic week!
