Happy Monday everyone!
Last week saw the release of the latest Pillar Talk episode, with Eric Kieron Davis, John Crewe, and Erin Roberts discussing Staggered Development, a new approach that allows us to continue delivering quarterly patches.
We invite you to visit the Staggered Development FAQ that will provide answers to your most burning questions.
Still got questions? This Friday, Star Citizen Live will also be dedicated to Staggered Development and the recent Roadmap updates, so please head over to our question gathering thread, submit your questions and vote for the ones you wish to see addressed the most.
## Now, let’s see what’s going on this week:
On Tuesday, the Lore Team will take a closer look at the Pallas system, birthplace of the common history between Humanity and the Xi’an Empire.
Wednesday brings the answers to your top voted questions about the Aegis Nautilus in our latest Q&A.
Thursday welcomes a new episode of Inside Star Citizen, our weekly look behind the scenes of Star Citizen development.
Friday will bring us the PU & SQ42 Monthly Reports alongside Updates to the Roadmap and the Subscriber Vault. Of course, the RSI Newsletter will also be delivered – right to your inbox. Furthermore, we welcome a new episode of Star Citizen Live, which broadcasts on our Star Citizen Channel at 8.30am Pacific. This episode will be an All About Development edition, so make sure to submit your questions about Staggered Development and the recent changes to the Public Roadmap here.
Last but not least, ‘mellow greetings’ to all citizens in the ‘verse. To briefly introduce myself, I’m another Chris! Working in the Frankfurt office, I am a keen space pilot, and looking forward to support you and the Community Team in my new role as Associate Community Manager.
See you in the ‘verse!
Christian A.W. Schmitt
