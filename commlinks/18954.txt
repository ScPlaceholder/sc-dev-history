## Happy Monday, everyone!
In case you missed it, we're kicking off a week of Dynamic Events starting tomorrow! Gear up, gather your friends and orgmates, and get ready for mayhem as Jumptown reopens and the Nine Tails look to lockdown a supply chain or five. Head over to our dedicated Comm-Link for the schedule and more details.
Also, last week we announced a few additional Bar Citizens we'll be attending: New Jersey, Illinois, Italy, and Spain! Looking to get your hands on a Banu cube from the community team? Check out the Bar Citizen Comm-Link for details on an event near you.
Also don't forget, this is the last week to get your submissions in for our Halloween contests! Head over to Spectrum for the details on how you can submit your creations for a chance to win some awesome in-game prizes.
## Now, let's see what's going on this week:
Tuesday, the Narrative Team brings us their monthly Galactapedia Update. Tuesday also kicks off our Dynamic Event Week with both Jumptown and Nine Tails lockdown available to take on in the Stanton system! Keep an eye out for a Priority mission in your contracts manager!
Wednesday has the latest Roadmap Roundup alongside the Roadmap Update.
This Thursday, Inside Star Citizen returns this week with a look at the Underground Facilities first shown at CitizenCon this year as they enter production. Plus, we'll be talking with Silvan Hau about the latest updates on the Gen12 renderer.
And on Friday, we've got another edition of Star Citizen Live with another two-for-one episode. We're starting things off with the Mission Feature Team with a follow-up discussion to their Investigations presentation at CitizenCon, plus additional guests starting at 8am Pacific / 3pm UTC on twitch.tv/starcitizen.
You'll also see an update to the Subscriber Vault and the weekly RSI Newsletter delivered to your inbox. We'll also release the latest Jump Point issue to subscribers.
Oh, and don't forget: we'd love to feature your commercials on Star Citizen Live! Post a 30-second, in-lore commercial for your org, a ship, or even Smoltz to the Community Hub for a chance to be highlighted.
