## Happy Monday, everyone!
Happy Monday, everyone!
Star Citizen Alpha 3.17: Fueling Fortunes is live, including a myriad of new ways to adventure and succeed in the 'verse. There are more ways than ever to make those sweet credits, such as selling captured loot, ship-to-ship refueling, or even leveling up your mining skills with new mining gadgets. Or, if you're looking for something a bit more relaxing, simply enjoy an ice cold Smoltz by microTech's river.
Also from last week, we kicked off a MISC Celebration, including the Hull A and the MISC Expanse Q&A, and our favorite part of the fun, our MISC Bumper Sticker Contest. Enter now for a chance to fly away in a new ship for free!
Last but not least, for all the new players who have just joined us, we have created a handy guide to help you with your first steps. Once you get through that, we've also put together a walkthrough of Things to Do in 3.17, with a fun bucket list sheet and all! Of course, better than any guide is engaging with fellow community members, so don't hesitate to extend a hand for help! 8 out of 10 times, you'll likely find help! The other two... well... pirates. ¯\_(ツ)_/¯
## Now, let’s see what’s going on this week:
This Tuesday, the Narrative Team will continue their lore series with another Comm-Link, answering some of the community's burning questions from Spectrum's Ask A Dev section.
Also on Tuesday, members of our subscribers' program can also look forward to their monthly subscriber's Comm-Link, packed with important information about the month ahead.
Wednesday, our teams will share their development progress with you as we'll publish April's Monthly Report for the PU and send out Squadron 42's report via email.
On Thursday, Inside Star Citizen welcomes a special Tools Time episode focused entirely on the internal tools we create to help game devs dev better.
Wrapping up the week on Friday, you'll see an update to the Subscriber Vault and the weekly RSI Newsletter delivered to your inbox. Last but not least, we'll welcome a new episode of Star Citizen Live, which will be broadcast on our Star Citizen Twitch channel. This Friday at 10am Pacific / 5pm UTC we'll have Ali Brown, Silvan Hau and Jonas Schneider on the show to answer your questions about graphics engineering and the Gen12 renderer.
Have a stellar week both in and out of the ‘verse!
