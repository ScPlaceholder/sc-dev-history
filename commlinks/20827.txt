What does it mean when a product is “officially licensed”?
An officially licensed product is approved by us and developed in collaboration with our team. Each partner works under a license agreement that ensures accuracy, quality, and respect for the Star Citizen universe and its designs.
How can I tell if a product is genuine?
Official items will clearly identify the licensee and may include our logo or an ‘Officially Licensed Product’ mark. If in doubt, check this page - if a company isn’t listed here, their products may not be officially licensed.
Where can I buy licensed products?
Licensed products are sold directly through each partner’s own store or official retail channels. Links to partner websites are provided on this page.
Who handles customer service or shipping for these products?
Each partner manages their own sales, shipping, and support. Please contact the relevant licensee directly with any product-specific questions.
Are there regional or shipping restrictions?
Most licensees offer worldwide shipping, but availability and costs may vary by region. We recommend checking each partner’s store for details.
Will more partners be added in the future?
Yes! We’re actively expanding our licensing program and will announce new partners as they join. Keep an eye on this page for updates.
