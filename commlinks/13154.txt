Meet Star Citizen Artist Ted Beargeon, currently hard at work on building some of Star Citizen’s pledge ships! Ted will be in the hot seat on this week’s Wingman’s Hangar, so tune in on Friday for a complete interview. Ted may be a man of few words, but his work on ships like the Starfarer tanker speaks for itself!
## How did you get started in the game industry?
Found a job on the Concept Art.org forums.
## What projects have you worked on?
- Warhammer Online: Age of Reckoning
- Warhammer 40,000: Dark Millennium Online
- And a wee bit-O-DLC for DarkSiders 2
## What will you be doing for Cloud Imperium?
Concept Art.
## What are you most excited to see in Star Citizen/Squadron 42?
Dog fighting in space. I’m really curious about how it will feel.
## What are you playing right now?
I’m waiting for BF4.
