Hi everyone,
The countdown to CitizenCon 2949 has begun and the first wave of tickets is already sold out. More tickets will be made available at a later date, so keep an eye on our CitizenCon website for more info.
Following the success of Org Booths at CitizenCon in Austin last year, we plan to replicate this opportunity for members of the Star Citizen community but with some minor changes. Whether you’re representing your org, an event group, or a sub-community, we’re expanding this year to allow all backers to make their presence felt in an official capacity at the event. To find out more about the application process and the booths in general, visit our Community Booths page.
## Now, let’s see what’s going on this week:
Tuesday, the Lore Team will publish a Galactic Guide for the Gliese system. This Banu system features bustling trade lanes, a large flotilla, and a terraformed world the Banu avoid for unknown reasons.
Wednesday will bring us both the PU & SQ42 Monthly Reports!
Thursday won’t see an episode of Inside Star Citizen as the show takes a break for three weeks, as communicated in last week’s season finale.
Friday will see a Roadmap update and the RSI Newsletter delivered right to your inbox. We also welcome a new episode of Star Citizen Live, which broadcasts on our Star Citizen Channel at 8am Pacific. This time, Vehicle Pipeline Director John Crewe and Gameplay Programmer David Colson will be talking all things spaceship.
And last but not least: If you like racing, there are still a couple of slots left in the Star Racing July Cup, organized by Obinotus and the StarRacing Org. Choose between the high-speed and open class and register until July 5th through their website.
See you in the ‘verse!
Ulf Kuerschner
Senior Community Manager
