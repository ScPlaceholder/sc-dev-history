## Greetings Citizens,
Star Citizen will be sponsoring the Gaming Closing Party at SXSW this March in Austin. This event will be open to the public, but worry not! Star Citizen backers will get early entrance and a chance to speak directly with some CIG employees in attendance. We at CIG have a longstanding relationship with Austin and SXSW is a great way to strengthen that bond by sharing our great community with the world. There will be a general overview of Star Citizen as well as new information surrounding the social module. Here are the details for the event:
## Event Details
## SXSW Gaming Expo
When: Friday March 13th – Sunday March 15th
Where: Palmer Events Center, 900 Barton Springs Rd. Austin, TX 78704
Time: 12-8 pm
Admission: FREE and open to the public (you do not need a SXSW badge to attend)
## Geek Stage Presentation
When: Friday, March 13, 2015
Where: Palmer Events Center, 900 Barton Springs Rd. Austin, TX 78704
Time: 6:30 pm
Admission: FREE and open to the public (you do not need a SXSW badge to attend)
## Closing Party
When: Sunday, March 15, 2015
Where: Palmer Events Center West Canopy, 900 Barton Springs Rd. Austin, TX 78704
Time: 7-10 pm (Backers get early entry at 6:30 pm. You do not need a SXSW badge to attend.)
Admission: FREE and open to the public
Age Restriction: All Ages
Sale Ends: Wednesday, March 11, 2015
Print Tickets: Thursday, March 12, 2015
Please note that in order to be admitted early you must bring a printed ticket
If you are attending SXSW or are going to be in the Austin area please stop by and join the celebration! We’d love to see you there!
