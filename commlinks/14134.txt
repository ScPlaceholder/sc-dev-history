While we are thrilled how our ships look, our fans continue to keep us on our toes with their own inventions. From stealthy fighters to carrier ships, they let their imagination loose and continue to astonish us. Check out a few of our favorites below.
We’ll start with Woden’s short range fighter, the Gerovit which has been painted by Ephalanx. Though it is built for speed and maneuverability, this ship looks mean. Watching a few of these flying around would make me think again before attacking.

Next up is the AIB-35 “Gavel” Gunship by EPhalanx. One of the coolest parts of this ships is the amount of variants that have been created. From a police ship to a mercenary gunship, the Gavel is the multipurpose ship for you.

st_Paulus_81 has taken quite a different approach to what a fighter ship should look like. The Switchblade’s nightmarish design can easily strike fear into the heart of any opponent it comes up across.
