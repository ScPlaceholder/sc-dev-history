## IN-GAME REWARDS (FLAIR)
Lightning Bolt Co. used PMC contracts to supplement the construction of its first prototype – an exhaustive process that took over five years. Since then, it’s slowly expanded its product line. This month, Subscribers will receive exclusive Deadeye-editions of its latest offerings arriving in the upcoming Alpha 3.10.
## Yubarev “Deadeye” Pistol – Engineering excellence only scratches the surface of the distinct Yubarev pistol. Former merc turned weapon manufacturing mogul Irina Arkadova oversaw every aspect of the gun, imbuing it with power, practicality, and panache. Featuring a cutting-edge barrel design, the Yubarev fires a charged electron shot that impacts not only your target but additional conductive targets within range.
Current Centurion-level Subscribers get the Yubarev “Deadeye” pistol seen here as part of their subscription.
## Atzkav “Deadeye” Sniper Rifle – The Atzkav is a unique precision rifle from Lightning Bolt Co. Once the charging handle is engaged, it accurately fires a charged electron to deal significant energy damage that spreads to nearby conductive targets. This special pulse effect also leaves a residual charge in the strike area that increases damage delivered by subsequent electron shots. Alongside the rifle’s special firing capabilities, the Atzkav is best known for its distinct barrel that crackles with energy when powering its next shot.
Current Imperator-level Subscribers get both the Yubarev “Deadeye” pistol and Atzkav “Deadeye” sniper rifle as part of their subscription.
If you aren’t a Subscriber and would like to receive the Yubarev “Deadeye” pistol or Atzkav “Deadeye” sniper rifle as part of a subscription, you can SUBSCRIBE by July 13.
