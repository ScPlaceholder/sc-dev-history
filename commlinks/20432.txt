## CODEBREAKERS NEEDED
VANDUUL LANGUAGE
Humanity’s hostile relationship with the Vanduul began almost 300 years ago after the alien species unexpectedly raided a civilian outpost in the Orion system. This first contact cost countless Human lives, as has every encounter since.
The Vanduul home world is unknown, their command structure is obscure, and with so little intel available to anticipate their agenda, all diplomatic efforts to improve Human-Vanduul relations have failed. It seems that the only language they understand is violence, but as Humans, we must continue to rise above.
Enlist and help today’s Vanduul become tomorrow's vanquished. Our next step toward victory is to crack their communications.
TACTICAL TRANSLATIONS
As a UEE Naval recruit, you will be given access to several approved phrases to begin your linguistic efforts. While official attempts to fully translate the Vanduul dialect continue at the highest levels, they must remain classified to prevent panic amongst the civilian population.
