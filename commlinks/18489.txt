January 2022 Subscriber Promotions
Welcome to 2952! We closed out last year with the launch of Alpha 3.16: Return to Jumptown, and we hope you’re enjoying the titular Dynamic Event currently running across Stanton. As luck would have it, this month’s Vehicle of the Month, the Tumbril Nova, is perfect for tackling the drug labs scattered throughout the system.
If an honest day’s work is more your thing, January’s Subscriber Flair lets you deck out your favorite industrial vehicles with new paint packs that feature new tints for your mining vehicles. There are three paint packs, and each one contains a tint for the ROC series, MOLE, Reclaimer, and Prospector, so there’s bound to be something you’ll love.
And as usual, if you can’t turn your back on Argo’s iconic deep-space digger, the outgoing Ship of the Month, the MOLE, is available in the Pledge Store with enhanced insurance for Subscribers.
