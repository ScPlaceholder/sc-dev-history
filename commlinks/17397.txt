## Weekly Wisdom: 3.8 Spawn Point
Initial spawn selection is coming to Star Citizen in Alpha 3.8!
When a new account logs on for the first time or when a veteran player experiences an account reset, you will now be prompted to choose your initial spawn location between Port Olisar, Lorville, Grim Hex, Area 18, and Levski.
Keep in mind that this is not intended to be used as a teleportation device, and will not function as one. This is intended to be a one time selection, so choose wisely!
