Hi everyone,
Only 12 days until CitizenCon 2949 kicks off in Manchester! We recently updated the CitizenCon website with a number of new information pieces, including the schedule, a Guide to Manchester, and details on which Community Booths you can expect to find at the big event.
Alongside the usual myriad of publishes last week, we released the latest PU Monthly Report, chock-full of details about what our teams around the globe have been up to (minus CitizenCon surprises of course!).
## Now, let’s see what’s going on this week:
On Tuesday, the Lore Team will release the Galactic Guide for the Tevarin inhabited Branaugh system.
Wednesday will bring the SQ42 Monthly Report in Comm-Link style, and subscribers will receive their monthly newsletter.
Thursday brings a brand-new episode of our weekly series Inside Star Citizen – the show that provides a behind-the-scenes look at the development of our Persistent Universe.
Friday will bring us updates to the Roadmaps and the Subscriber Vault, and the weekly newsletter delivered straight to your inbox. Finally, we welcome a new episode of Star Citizen Live, which will be broadcasting on the Star Citizen Twitch channel at 4pm UTC. This time Lead Designer for the Mission Features Team Luke Pressley will answer your questions about mission content already in the game, missions like the 890 Jump Hijack Scenario coming in Alpha 3.8 and general mission design philosophy. Head over to Spectrum to submit your questions and vote on the ones you want answered most.
See you in the ‘verse!
