Happy Monday, everyone!
Before we jump into things, we wanted to give you a quick check-in on Alpha 3.10. As you know, we’re currently in the Evocati test phase. We’re happy with progress, and as usual are so thankful for the hard work of the Evocati. If all goes well in the next few days, we’re hoping to open up to Wave 1 later this week. Stay tuned and we’ll keep you updated.
## Now, let’s see what’s going on this week:
The Narrative Team has a treat for us in the form of a new lore post featuring an interview with Imperator candidate Titus Costigan this Tuesday.
On Wednesday, we’re kicking off the Foundation Festival and celebrating Humanity’s coming-together by honoring the wonderful Star Citizen community and all the incredible things we’ve built both in the ‘verse and here on Earth.
Thursday brings a new infographic breaking down popular choices for character customization in the ‘verse. Keep in mind that we’re still on our quarterly hiatus for Inside Star Citizen, so there will not be a new episode this week. The crew will be back on the 23rd with all-new episodes!
And finally, on Friday, you’ll see an update to the Subscriber Vault, the RSI Newsletter, and an update to the Roadmap and Roundup. Also, you can catch Star Citizen Live on Twitch at 10AM Pacific. This week we’ll be joined by prolific concept artist Gavin Rothery. We’ll be diving into his body of work, taking a chronological exploration of his concepts for Star Citizen, running through his designs, and chatting about the work done along the way.
Have a great week!
Molly Joseph

## Weekly Wisdom: Feedback
You've got thoughts and opinions on the latest additions to the ‘verse, and we want to hear 'em!
Every time we bring a new patch or new feature to Star Citizen, we open up threads in the feedback forum on Spectrum for each new change. While we’re always scouring all corners of the internet for your thoughts on the most recent pieces of content, leaving your feedback on this forum ensures it will be looked at – responses in these threads are constantly being read and sorted by our team.
Community MVP: July 6th, 2020
We're constantly amazed at the contributions made by the Star Citizen community. Whether it's fan art, a cinematic, YouTube guide, or even a 3D print of your favorite ship, we love it all! Every week, we select one piece of content submitted to the Community Hub and highlight it here. The highlighted content creator will be awarded an MVP badge on Spectrum and be immortalized in our MVP section of the Hub.
Don’t forget to submit your content to our Community Hub for the chance to see it here!
