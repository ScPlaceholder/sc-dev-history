## Happy Monday, everyone!
Invictus Launch Week 2952 is well underway! We've had an absolutely incredible turnout to this year's event, and we wanted to thank not only the new citizens jumping into the 'verse for the first time, but also the veterans who have helped these new folks get their space legs. I want to put emphasis on the latter here. Our existing community has sincerely blown us away over the weekend and has demonstrated what makes our community so damn special.
Due to the tremendous amount of you checking out Invictus (seriously, shattering every traffic record), we've seen some degradation of a few services over the weekend (global chat, friends lists, Arena Commander matchmaking, rental/reclaim services). Rest assured, we've got the right people working to get these restored, and some of them have come back online this morning. We greatly appreciate your patience as we work to resolve these issues. Keep an eye on our status page for updates and details.
Curious as to all the things you can do during the event, both in and out of the game? Head over to the Invictus Launch Week details page for info on the in-game events, contests, an FAQ, and more.
## Now, let’s see what’s going on this week:
This Tuesday, the Narrative team is back with a new edition of the Empire Report. The show talks to a biologist about a scandal involving an explorer who submitted fabricated data that initially set the scientific world abuzz.
On Wednesday, you'll see an update to the Public Roadmap alongside an accompanying Roadmap Roundup Comm-Link with all the details.
On Thursday, Inside Star Citizen takes a look at the latest vehicles making their flyable debut in the persistent universe at this year's Invictus Launch Week.
Closing out the week, you'll see an update to the Subscriber Vault and the weekly RSI Newsletter delivered to your inbox on Friday. We'll also air a new episode of Star Citizen Live on our Twitch channel. This week's episode is a vehicle-centric episode with members of the Vehicle Content and Feature teams answering your questions in a special Invictus Q&A. NOTE: Due to scheduling this week, this week's episode will be recorded on Thursday, but will air on Friday on Twitch as usual. Get your questions in early!
And of course, all week long the Invictus Launch Week festivities continue in-game!
Have a fantastic week, and see you on the show floor!
Jake Bradley
Community Manager
