## Weekly Wisdom: Issue Council
As we prep to open up Alpha 3.9’s PTU, it’s a great time to remember that you can help us polish the game and squash bugs by contributing to the Issue Council.
The Issue Council is a community-driven tool that allows you to assist us in polishing the PU by reporting on issues you encounter while playing! Create a report of the issue you’ve come across, or contribute to an existing report created by another player.
Extra details and reproduction steps are always vital when we’re looking at how we will need to go about prioritizing and ultimately fixing these issues. Any and all information you give us helps in our effort to make the PU experience as smooth as possible.
Learn more about the Issue Council and how you can help us squash existing bugs here!

## Mining Yield Calculator by TRFF
We've all asked ourselves "is this one going to be worth it?" after scanning a rock on our mining expedition. TRFF has created a tool to help you answer that question with their Mining Yield Calculator. Plug in the stats of the rock in question, and this handy sheet will spit out an estimate of the worth of the rock!
