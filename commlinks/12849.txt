## Chris Roberts in Austin
Chris Roberts is in Austin to meet with the CIG team currently setting up shop there. Chris is normally based at headquarters in Los Angeles, but he’ll be traveling back and forth to make sure everything is going correctly! He spent yesterday and today meeting with the programming and design teams to talk about the next steps for the game… and he’ll be back next week alongside more of the Los Angeles team to officially kick off the next phase of development!
