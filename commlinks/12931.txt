On this week’s Wingman’s Hangar, you’ll meet Technical Designer Dan Tracy… a man with an inside connection to the CryEngine that we’re using to build Star Citizen! Be sure to watch the stream tomorrow at 11 AM CST (-6 GMT) to hear more about Dan and what he’s working on!
## How did you get started in the game industry?
I got into the industry through the modding scene. My brother and I started up MechWarrior: Living Legends which was excellently received and allowed me to show off my self-taught skills which in turn landed me a job within the industry
## What projects have you worked on?
I’ve worked on Mech Warrior: Living Legends Total Conversion mod for Crysis, Mass Effect, Crysis 2 and 3, and Ryse.
## How did you become involved with Star Citizen? What will you be doing on the game?
After seeing the Squadron 42 promotional video at the Crytek company meeting, I made efforts to get in contact with Chris Roberts and get onboard
## What are you most excited about seeing in Star Citizen/Squadron 42?
I’m most excited about the massive galaxy map and the modular weapons that we’ll be able to swap onto the ships
## What are you playing right now?
Guild Wars 2, League of Legends, Fire Emblem: Radiant Dawn, Smash Brothers Brawl
