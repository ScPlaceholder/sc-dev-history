## Happy Monday, everyone!
We hope you're having a ghoulish Day of the Vara and a spooky Halloween today! If you're hunting for treats in the 'verse, we recommend the ongoing Dynamic Events, Jumptown 2.0 and Nine Tails Lockdown.
On the other hand, if you're still working on your party decorations, you can get inspiration from the winners of the CitizenCon 2952 DIY Ship Model Contest and this year's Pumpkin Carving Contest. Participation in the latter is still possible until tomorrow, so knives out!
If you still need to get in the spooky mood for tonight's party, take a look at the entries in the Trick-or-Treat Video Contest. We announced the winners today - our jury was partially screaming with fright!
Otherwise, it will soon be time to buckle up and prepare for takeoff, as the galaxy's premier vehicle showcase, the Intergalactic Aerospace Expo, is fast approaching. Don't miss it from Friday, November 18!
Last but certainly not least, we're coming to you! We're attending two Bar Citizen events this weekend in Chicago, Illinois, and Madrid, Spain! We can't wait to see all of you, and we'll have goodies in tow for those who attend. For more details on the event, head to our Bar Citizen Comm-Link.
## Now, let's see what's going on this week:
This Tuesday, the Narrative will release Crossroads - a new short story about a Tevarin pirate on the run from a bounty hunter and their deadly face-off in an old scrapyard. November's Subscriber offerings will also be published on Tuesday, both as a newsletter and as a comm-link.
Wednesday will see October’s PU monthly report comm-link published and the Squadron 42 report sent out via email. Get all the latest development info by subscribing to the Squadron 42 newsletter here.
On Thursday, we've another episode of the only weekly, continuous behind-the-scenes docuseries in videogames (citation pending) where we peek Inside Star Citizen. This time, we're looking at the current progress of the Cargo Refactor coming online in Alpha 3.18 and revisiting the RaStar tool, discussing how it's evolved since CitizenCon 2951, and seeing how it's being used to place new outposts throughout the Stanton and Pyro systems.
Wrapping up the week on Friday, you'll see an update to the Subscriber Vault and the weekly RSI Newsletter delivered to your inbox.
On Friday, the talk-show shenanigans continue with another episode of Star Citizen Live. The response to the new format has been heartwarming, and while we'll continue to fine-tune the formula week after week and inject a few surprises along the way, this week's show will be another chance to discuss two different aspects of Star Citizen's continuing development. We are going to talk all things Master Modes, and you can tune in at 9am Pacific, 4pm UTC on twitch.tv/starcitizen.
UPDATE: Unfortunately, the captain of The Only Constant, Jared Huckaby, had to call in sick today. Therefore, we have to cancel today's show, wish Jared a speedy recovery, and will reschedule this episode of Star Citizen Live. Thank you for your understanding.
