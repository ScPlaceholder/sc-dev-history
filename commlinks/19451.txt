STORM SERIES
AN UNSTOPPABLE FORCE OF NATURE
Strike hard. Strike fast. Leave nothing but destruction; the battlefield belongs to the Tumbril Storm.
The Second Tevarin War saw the original solo tank change planetary combat forever. Today, the Storm is reborn to revolutionize warfare again. Offering complementary solutions for both anti-material and anti-aircraft operations, the Tumbril Storm series is an unstoppable force of nature.
EXPLORE THE TUMBRIL STORM & TUMBRIL STORM AA
Tracked Drive System
The state-of-the-art tracked drive system uses technology and materials first utilized in the Nova tank for effortless handling over all types of terrain.
Shield & Armor
A dedicated shield generator and emitter, along with tactical-spec armor, keep operators safe while behind the line.
Operator’s Seat
The user-friendly interface and central driving seat make for precise positioning during combat and when traversing rough terrain.
Component Access
Component access provides peace of mind should a stop be required during deployment.
Heavy Weaponry
Whether the heavy laser cannon of the Storm or the anti-aircraft battery of the AA, the Storm series is outfitted for maximum destruction.
