## Happy Monday, everyone!
Last week saw the culmination of the biggest throwdown yet in the clash for aerospace supremacy. Ship Showdown 2956 has its Undisputed Champion of the 'Verse, and it's the RSI Perseus! The gunship ended one more battle in the final, taking down the Drake Ironclad Assault after a fight the crowds are still talking about.
