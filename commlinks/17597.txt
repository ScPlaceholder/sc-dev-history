Hello everyone!
This is it! Invictus Launch Week is here!
From May 22 to June 1, we’ll honor those in the Navy and other armed forces for their tireless service, with high-flying public demonstrations from the UEE Naval fleet, a special display from the 999th Squadron, epic firework shows, and more.
Last week, we shared the Invictus Launch Week Guide, that aimed to help you find your way to the Bevic Convention Center in Area18, ArcCorp, where the galaxy’s top vehicle and defense manufacturers will showcase their state-of-the-art technology.
Today, we’ve published a schedule that details the ships that will be available to everyone to fly for free each day. This will help you get the most from this awesome display of military might and combat engineering, so check it out, plan your visit, and take in the biggest and most dangerous military vehicles the ‘verse has to offer.
Watch the Invictus Launch Week 2950 Teaser here.
While the naval fleet makes their way through Stanton and the manufacturers make their vessels ready for boarding, we’ll also kick off two community contests. One will demand your most breathtaking flight performances, while the other challenges you to help the UEE find new recruits! So, no matter if you want to demonstrate your ace pilot abilities or get crafty to support the UEE Navy, everyone will have a chance to win some fantastic vessels.
The Invictus Launch Week offers a myriad of exciting events and experiences, so make sure to share them with your friends and families using the #Invictus2950 hashtag!
