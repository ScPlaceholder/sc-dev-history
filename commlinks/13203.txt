As we approach Gamescom, the Star Citizen team is feverishly working on the Hangar Module. Today we would like to share some of the concept art selected by Technical Artist Forrest Stephan. Forrest showed off some of the basic hangar today on Wingman’s Hangar and now you can study some of the work that went into it in more detail. Take note of how the artists create a variety of different fixtures—lights, pipes, fans and other details—which can then be displayed in the CryEngine in different ways. Stay tuned for more information on the hangar as we approach the day you’ll first visit your very own home in the Star Citizen universe!
