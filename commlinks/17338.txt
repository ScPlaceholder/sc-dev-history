## IN-GAME REWARDS (FLAIR)
The In-Game Rewards for November are two collections of 2949 stylish eyewear from Element Authority, a popular distributor of such wares in the persistent universe. NOTE: These eyewear are 100% cosmetic, and do not affect or alter the vision of any character wearing them.
## Urban Collection by Element Authority – Glasses with a bit of class. The Urban Collection of fashionable frames from Element Authority is ideal eyewear for everywhere from your ship to the trendiest city street. Styles of glasses included are the Sarrab, the Ectio and the AvantX.
Current Centurion Subscribers get the following three items in the Urban Collection above as part of their subscription.
The Sarrab are stylish half-rim glasses that are so light you’ll forget you’re wearing them. Ideal for everyday use, these glasses feature clear polycarbonate temple arms that complement the lens without demanding too much attention.
Fashion and function merge with the Ectio glasses from Fiore. Adjustable temple arms provide the perfect fit to sit snugly in the ear, while crystal-tempered lenses guarantee a clear view under almost any atmospheric condition.
A distinct one piece lens with a unique geometric design makes the AvantX glasses stand out from the crowd. SPV expertly blends cutting edge style and technology for a distinct look and feel that’s fashion forward.
## Adventurer Collection by Element Authority – Adventure in style with this collection of eyewear designed to survive harsh environments, including the Avalos Scout, Haruspec and Aponte Explorer style goggles.
Current Imperator Subscribers get all six items in both the Adventurer and Urban Collections above as part of their subscription.
Tech meets tough with the Avalos Scout goggles. Their unique and sturdy frame ensures that the lenses remain intact even after unexpected impacts or sudden atmospheric changes. Built for harsh environments but bold enough to be worn anywhere.
Supremely comfortable yet light as a feather, the Haruspec goggles also provide excellent protection against the elements thanks to their sturdy side shields. Stegman’s fitted the goggles with polycarbonate performance lenses and a special slip-resistant nose pad to ensure they stay in place.
Outfitted with Rambler’s scratch resistant lenses, the Aponte Explorer goggles provides a clear view of the path ahead. Their wraparound design keeps dirt and debris away while on windy plains, but still look stylish enough to wear when walking through bustling city streets.

## SHIP OF THE MONTH
Presenting the Anvil Aerospace U4A-3 Terrapin-class Scanning/Exploration Ship. The Terrapin was developed near the end of the 28th century to serve as the first ship in the Empire’s defensive restructuring of the Navy. The Terrapin’s watchword is protection, with extensive shield systems and armor layers designed to provide the maximum possible defense for pilot and crew. While it lacks the maneuverability of a dedicated fighter, it does maintain an advanced, hard-hitting array of weapons intended to keep the most fearsome Vanduul raider at bay.
