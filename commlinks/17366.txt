## Celebrate in Style
Earlier this year we kicked off the ultimate tournament of aerospace champions: the Ship Showdown. You voiced your support for your favorite flyable in an effort to secure it a top spot in the Best in Show lineup at the Intergalactic Aerospace Expo.
Celebrate and commemorate their place in aerospace history with special limited-edition liveries, only available during the IAE.
Take to the skies and test fly all four from December 2nd through the 5th. Each Best in Show finalist will be available, packaged with a limited hangar flair schematic as seen in the image above.
Already own one of the Best in Show ships? You’ll be able to swap to the IAE exclusive version, schematic included, at no extra cost.
## One for All!
We had a fantastic time celebrating with you, and as a token of our appreciation for your involvement in the first annual Ship Showdown, every citizen who backed before December 5th, 2019 will receive an exclusive Drake Caterpillar Statue to decorate their hangars (and eventually ships!).
