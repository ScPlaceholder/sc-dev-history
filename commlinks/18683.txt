Things to Do at Invictus Launch Week 2952
Invictus Launch Week is the period between Emancipation Day and Armistice Day that marks the beginning of the new recruitment year for the UEE Navy.
From May 20 until May 31, celebrate the military might of the UEE with a display of the UEE’s awe-inspiring naval fleet. The celebration allows all players to see some of the ‘verse’s biggest, best, and most iconic ships up close at the Vision Center at Orison on Crusader.
Plus, the UEE is showing off their latest and greatest as they patrol the Stanton system. While they dock at each major port, you can take a tour of one of the UEE's most battle-hardened Javelin Destroyers, the UEES War Hammer. Also, keep an eye out for the fleet if you want to catch a glimpse of the UEEN's premier carrier, the Bengal.
