## Hello everyone, xē'suelen nyahyan
Last week, we unveiled the all-new Star Citizen Referral Program which officially goes live this Wednesday, July 2! The new program will introduce a refreshed rewards ladder (they're sweet!), a revamped interactive interface, improved tracking tools, and a whole lot more.
Alpha 4.2.1 is now available to all Waves, and we want to thank everyone who's taken the time to share feedback so far. Your input has already helped shape this update in meaningful ways, with many improvements and fixes implemented directly thanks to your reports and suggestions. In the meantime, feel free to check out the Release View on the Public Roadmap for a glimpse of what's coming, dive into the latest Patch Notes for full details, or try it out on the PTU yourself!
Also, a heartfelt thank you to everyone who joined us at Bar Citizen Copenhagen! We had an incredible time connecting with the Nordic community, and it was truly a pleasure to meet so many of you in person. Your passion, energy, and stories made the event unforgettable, so mange tak for being part of it!
## Now, let's see what's going on this week:
On Tuesday, we’re kicking off the new month with the Subscriber Comm-Link and Newsletter, bringing you the latest perks and flair to get in the mood for what is to come in July.
Wednesday is shaping up to be an exciting day as we kick off the annual Foundation Festival, a celebration of just how welcoming our community truly is. Alongside the festival, we’re launching a Free Fly event, where everyone can jump in and explore the game for free with access to eight diverse ships and vehicles.
That same day also marks the launch of our all-new Referral Program, rebuilt from the ground up with fresh rewards and streamlined tracking.
We’re also releasing the June PU Monthly Report, offering a detailed look at what our teams have been working on behind the scenes. And don’t miss the latest Roadmap Update and Roundup, which break down the newest developments heading to versions 4.2.1 and 4.3.0.
This Thursday, join us at 3pm UTC on Twitch for a new Star Citizen Live episode titled "Earning In The 'Verse". We will explore all the ways players can acquire items in the verse, whether it's by purchasing with aUEC, looting corpses or specific locations, earning rewards from missions, or visiting our favorite Banu at Wikelo's Emporium. While this episode won’t be a traditional Q&A, there will be a brief segment dedicated to player questions from Spectrum.
On Friday, be sure to check your inbox for the RSI Weekly Newsletter. We will recap the week's highlights, showcase standout community content, and provide a sneak peek at what's coming next.
