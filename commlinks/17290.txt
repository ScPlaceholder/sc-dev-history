## Roadmap Roundup
Hi everyone,
Each and every week, we accompany the Roadmap update with a brief explanatory note to give you insight into the decision-making that led to any changes. This is part of an effort to make our communications more transparent, more specific, and more insightful for all of you who help to make Star Citizen and Squadron 42 possible.
With that, let’s dive into this week’s roundup!

## Notable Changes for October 4th, 2019
On the Squadron 42 Roadmap, Usables v2 and Human AI Combat v2 have been marked as completed.
On the Star Citizen Roadmap, the Alpha 3.7 column has been marked as released following this morning’s patch release to live.
## PU & SQ42 – Vehicle Hud v4
The team is currently working on a more holistic priority list, resulting in Vehicle Hud v4 to be temporarily removed from the Roadmap until a better timeline is agreed upon.
