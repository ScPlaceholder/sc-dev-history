From travel posters to in-fiction magazines, today’s fan spotlight highlights a few of our favorite in-fiction creations. That’s right, these fan creations are not just about Star Citizen, but take place IN Star Citizen. So without further ado, let’s get started!
First up is Rocket Docket who has created vintage travel posters for a few of the Star Citizen planets. Also, Rocket’s vintage style makes these unique and amazing. If you’ve ever wanted to visit Terra, Cassel, or the Ellis system, then these are definitely for you!
Check out his thread!
Next up is Prometheus who has taken it upon himself to recreate the UEE logos. By adding elements such as eagles, or changing the original shape, Prometheus has given these logos new life. I love these new additions and could easily see myself sporting this new advocacy badge.
Check out his thread!
In addition to being our largest organization, Xplor has created an on-going magazine for all of the explorers out there. A combined effort by many members including CosmicTrader and Klaatu Apkallu, the first issue includes many things such as an interview with the founder Tiiga, and excerpts from the Diary of Cpt. Lewis. Definitely check this one out if you’re looking to explore the ‘Verse.
Check out their thread!
While Red Delta may not be great at creating thread titles, he is certainly a fantastic animator and proves it with his cartoon. Featuring the Dread Pirate Roberts, Red Delta interviews the veteran pirate about his past battles and to inspire his class. Just under two minutes, this video gives you a small slice of what being in a UEE classroom might be like.
Check out his thread!
Thanks to everybody who we highlighted. If you know of anything that we missed, let us know below! We’ll see you next week!
