Hello everyone,
The weekend saw parades and gatherings around the ‘verse as citizens embraced Imperator’s Day, a celebration honoring the forming of a unified Earth government and Humanity coming together to forge a brighter future for everyone. While the Parade for Independence contest has come to an end, you can take advantage of the accompanying promotion and snag limited “Independence” and “Liberty” packs, until 11:59 pm PDT today. The team is currently looking through all the contest entries and will announce the lucky winners soon!
With CitizenCon 2949 approaching, we recently announced the time and date for Wave 2 ticket sales for what promises to be an epic event. We’ve also updated our CitizenCon FAQ with more information about Cosplay and the event in general.
## Now, let’s see what’s going on this week:
Tuesday, the Lore Team will publish a story where a deadly Synthworld accident spurs a debate about whether the project is worth funding or even feasible.
On Wednesday we’ll kick off wave 2 of ticket sales, which will be available for purchase.
Thursday won’t see an episode of Inside Star Citizen as we’re still on hiatus, but fear not, your weekly look into the world of Star Citizen development will be back next week.
Friday will see a Roadmap Update and the RSI Newsletter delivered right to your inbox. We also welcome a new episode of Star Citizen Live, which broadcasts on our Star Citizen Channel. This week, members of our UI Team in the UK will join us live at 8am PDT / 3pm UTC to answer your questions about all things UI development, including vehicle HUDs, kiosks, in-helmet displays, and more.
See you in the ‘verse!
Ulf Kuerschner
