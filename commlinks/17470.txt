## SUBSCRIBERS
While this month might be a little shorter than most, there’s no shortage of offerings from the Subscriber Program. We start with our new Cold Weather collection from Escar Limited. These jackets don’t just look stylish, they provide an element of protection from colder locations like microTech – just in time for the upcoming Player Status additions in Alpha 3.9!
In spaceship-related news, the Dragonfly Black completed its stint as Ship of the Month and is now available for the rest of February in the subscriber-exclusive pledge promotion. It’s sleek, it’s sexy, and it doesn’t have wheels to hit all those rocks scattered around planetary locations. You can get it with subscriber-special one-year insurance below.
Also this month, subs get to fly the Banu Defender until the end of February. Plus, when the fabled Anvil Carrack arrives later this month, Imperator-level subscribers can look forward to a week’s access through the Imperator Test Flight Program. That goes into effect for seven days following the ship’s debut in the Persistent Universe, so keep an eye out for that.

## SHIP OF THE MONTH
Meet the Banu Defender, a multi-crew fighter with patchwork design elements that highlight technology from a variety of species. Though cargo space is limited, the Defender features modest accommodations for its crew and provides easy access to components. The Defender gets its name from the role it serves: the first line of defense against enemy attack. That’s why the Defender makes the ideal companion to the Merchantman – every trader worth their salt knows an investment in defense is an investment in their livelihood.
The Banu Defender is already available to all subscribers and will continue through March 2nd, 2020
