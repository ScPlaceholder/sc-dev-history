## Greetings Citizens,
For the next week the Drake Interplanetary Cutlass will be open to everyone! The Cutlass Black is available to fly in Arena Commander and we are also putting the Cutlass Blue back on the market!
Drake Interplanetary Cutlass is a low-cost, easy-to-maintain solution for local in-system militia units. A favorite of both the law abiding citizens and outlaws. For personal defense, or more devious intentions, the Cutlass has you covered. The Cutlass by Drake Interplanetary.
## Cutlass Blue
Sleek, mean, and royal. The Cutlass Blue adds missiles, a more aggressive engine, and Durasteel holding cells in the cargo bay to the standard model. The Cutlass Blue is the outworld militia standard ship of choice for patrols.
We also have a new shipment of weapons in the Voyage Direct store. Our newest weapon the TARANTULA GT-870 MK3 would be perfect for the recently unlocked Cutlass.
Fed by Gallenson Tactical’s patented SpinGlide drum ammunition system, the Tarantula GT-870 Mk3 is the Size 3 version of the previously released Mk2. It can smoothly and accurately deliver a variety of medium caliber ballistic shells. Though it only has moderate rate of fire, its trustworthy durability makes it a dependable addition to any fight. (Please note: this is not a Gatling cannon.)
The TARANTULA GT-870 MK3 can be purchased in the Voyager Direct Store .
We hope you enjoy this opportunity to test drive the Drake Interplanetary Cutlass. Testing these ships will be extremely valuable for both balance and to stress test the servers. Thank you Citizens and happy hunting.
