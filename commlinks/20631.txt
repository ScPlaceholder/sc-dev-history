Foundation Festival 2025 Org Spotlight
During the lead-up to Foundation Festival 2025, we put out a call for organizations to show us what their community spirit was all about, and your response was incredible! We were blown away by the numerous outstanding submissions we received, and we appreciate everyone taking this opportunity to promote their organization.
We wanted to shine the spotlight on some of your submissions from among the many worthy contenders. We hope you'll enjoy discovering the orgs in the gallery below, and you just might discover a new group of friends!

Quick Info
Ley Line Exotics has been running dangerous trade missions in Star Citizen since its conception. They specialize in finding and trading the galaxy's most valuable exotic materials and items. They're treasure hunters and frontier traders who go where the money is good and the danger is real. If you want to make serious credits and have some excitement doing it, this is your crew.

Synchronizerz is a perfect fit for any player who leads with and lives by the values of teamwork, excellence, integrity, and respect. Every citizen, new and veteran, is valued. Whether your goal is to become an expert navigator, ace pilot, rugged explorer, feisty combat specialist, or industry magnate, you can meet other like-minded players who are willing to assist you. Never go into the cold darkness of the ‘verse alone and unprotected. This is your home base. Join them!

Galactic Police Department
Quick Info
The Galactic Police Department (GPD) is a service-oriented organization focused on law enforcement, public safety, and community support across the ‘Verse. Their interests include protecting citizens in need, maintaining order, mentoring new players, and engaging in immersive patrols and operations. From foot patrols in city streets to full-scale investigations in deep space, GPD is committed to creating a fun, structured, and impactful presence wherever they operate.
Why should a new player join this org?
GPD is an ideal organization for new players because they prioritize mentorship, guidance, and teamwork. Their officers regularly assist new citizens by offering hands-on support, training, and ride-alongs through key locations. They'll help you learn the ropes, navigate the game’s systems, and feel part of a close-knit, active community. Whether you’re looking to serve, learn, or just be part of something meaningful, the GPD will have your back from day one.

Event Concept Association
Quick Info
Event Concept Association is the galaxy's leading specialized event agency. They have already organized several gigantic spaceship exhibitions, air shows, and aerial acrobatics festivals, as well as open days for several corporations in unique and prestigious locations. Several dozen corporations have already been won over by their events, and they continue to attract an ever-growing audience!
Why should a new player join this org?
The Event Concept Association isn't a corporation or union; it's more of an association; a volunteer organization from across the galaxy dedicated to helping organize complex events for the general public. If you enjoy organizing popular events like air shows or large open houses on an outpost with a collection of starships to tour, this is the group for you! Their members come from dozens of different organizations to help out, and as such, often become ambassadors, inviting their friends to the events.

Oracle Research
Quick Info
Oracle Research is a sovereign science, exploration, and intelligence organization founded on the belief that the truth of the 'verse lies buried beneath its ruins, waiting to be decrypted. They operate at the nexus of historical recovery, experimental science, and strategic autonomy. Oracle Research is not a military faction, nor a corporate extension; it is an evolving archive. They are survivors turned explorers, tacticians, researchers, and mentors who now guide the next generation of citizens to continue the mission. Their activities range from artifact recovery and site investigation to tactical mentorship and reconnaissance logistics. They combine academic discipline with field-tested experience to guide their members and protect new citizens venturing into the unknown.
Why should a new player join this org?
Oracle Research exists to guide, not command. They understand the chaos and complexity new citizens face and respond not with demands, but with mentorship, clarity, and community. Players interested in science, exploration, history, and lore will find themselves right at home.

EXPANSE UNION
Quick Info
EXPANSE UNION is a disciplined and ideologically-driven Star Citizen organization. They focus on structured gameplay, tactical cooperation, and onboarding through their unique Sputnik web platform. Guided by the Codex, they offer recruits clear roles in the Civilian Corps, Army, or Academy — with contracts, mentorship, and purpose at every step.
Why should a new player join this org?
At EXPANSE UNION, they believe that meeting every new recruit is the beginning of a story worth being proud of. Even the very first interview is not a formality, but a true starting point on the path within a team that values respectful communication. This simple tradition helps set the right tone and build trust from the very first moments. In addition to advice and support, they offer a full-fledged web platform, the academy: Sputnik. Here, their specialists create guides, gameplay knowledge tests, themed quests, and dynamic activities. By completing them, a recruit gains mastery of key mechanics and becomes a confident player - ready for everything and most importantly - ready to help others.

4th Fleet Auxiliary
Quick Info
The 4th Fleet is a semi-roleplay, military simulation org comprised of a wide variety of individuals from around the world with a shared interest in Star Citizen, community and cooperation. They've based their lore heavily within the SC universe and try to operate within the bounds of the UEE Navy including lawful gameplay such as security and law enforcement, haulage and emergency service. They've already held a few very successful in-org events including operations Stone Bounty and Serpent Flame. If you've ever wanted to belong to a group where you're more than just a number on a page, 4th Fleet can be that experience. You'll be joining a small group of friends for whom a love for Star Citizen is the core of the experience. Goals and ambition are the benefit, not the rule, and this group is always eager to have you join as a new friend.
Why should a new player join this org?
The 4th Fleet is open to not only teaching new members how they do things, but also learning from their members new and old to better improve the quality of play for everyone. They have a comprehensive system for internal ranking and merits to be earned for people who show exceptionalism. With players from all across Star Citizen's various gameplay loops, they've got something for everyone, from simple medical gameplay all the way up to entire fleet coordination. They're also fairly relaxed as an org, and respect that their members' real lives come first, operating around such obligations instead of "cherry picking" members to suit a hardline policy set.

PentaCore Dynamics
Quick Info
PentaCore Dynamics is a German-speaking organization where every play style is welcome, whether mining or salvage, in Stanton or Pyro, with capital ship or small fighter, whether PVE or PVP. They do not dictate how anyone plays the game, up to the point where another player’s experience is negatively affected by unfair means. They plan to specialize in base building and crafting in the future, and are willing to assist other organizations within these areas. They are always open to forming new, meaningful alliances with other orgs, regardless of whether they are military or purely economic. They are also happy to offer military support for current and future sandbox activities in the 'verse.
Why should a new player join this org?
As a a very active org with regular group events, they love taking along new players and introducing them to Star Citizen's gameplay. They are a mixed group, with members ranging from their early 20s to their mid-50s, and all ages are welcome here. Home to players of all skill levels, they follow the motto that every citizen was once just starting their journey. Experienced players are always on hand with help and advice and are happy to answer any questions that arise. PentaCore Dynamics represents every style of play, so everyone will feel they belong. They're a colorful group that has fun with the vision of Star Citizen and would like to share this fun with you.

Quick Info
Krampus is an Italian industrial organization aiming to evolve into the Unite Resource Worker guild. Rather than a military group, they are ordinary workers, engineers, and miners who, aware of the dangers of space, are ready to defend themselves from any and all threats. Their motto is: wreckers by profession, miners by passion, and fighters by necessity. Krampus welcomes both new and veteran players without forcing them to join the org right away: players simply hop into Discord and play. If, after a few months, someone feels at home, they can discuss joining the org while sitting in the Reclaimer mess hall over a fresh Cruz.
Why should a new player join this org?
Following several of their members' experiences with previous orgs, they realized that the hierarchies and constraints of many organizations made it complicated for new players to join a new group and ask for help. Krampus has members who spend evenings instructing new players, and will frequently offer to accompany those who would like to test new ships. They'll also acquaint new members with every aspect of the game as much as possible, and direct them on which package to choose if they want to stay. They're willing to help those who may only have a starter ship to acquire new ships quickly, either by doing missions or in contests. They want those who play with them to feel like they are part of a family, and will help to make your time in the 'verse as relaxed as possible.
