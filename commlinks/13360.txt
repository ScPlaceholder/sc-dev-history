Greetings Citizens,
Yesterday’s reveal of the Idris hangar and powerplant went over so well that we thought we’d go ahead and showcase an entire gallery of the work being done on the corvette interiors. These screenshots are in-engine renders of the Idris’ main deck engine room and internal hangar. The Idris will be able to carry multiple crewmen with different roles and a small squadron of fighters that can be launched and recovered at any time. The team is currently working on bringing the Idris from concept art (by renowned artist Ryan Church) into the CryEngine… and this is the first result!
