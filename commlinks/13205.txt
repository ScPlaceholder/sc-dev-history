Greetings Citizens,
Start your week off with some concept art! This artwork of Stanton IV, aka “Crusader,” was created by the team at Behaviour in Canada. Crusader is a unique world featuring sky-based structures. The goal with this art is to show how similar architecture already created for other worlds (in this case, the other worlds in the Stanton system) can be reworked for entirely different environments. The results speak for themselves! We’re now building these modular components in the engine itself. In the near future, you’ll see actual ground levels starting to come together!
