## Happy Monday, everyone!
The Intergalactic Aerospace Expo 2954 kicked off last week in New Babbage, drawing spaceship enthusiasts from across the galaxy to the frosty planet of microTech. The expo halls offer a rotating lineup of ships and ground vehicles each day, with over 150 available to try out for free. Check the schedule to make sure you don’t miss anything. You can also use rented ships to tackle IAE Academy tasks and pick up some extra equipment along the way. For new pilots, it’s a great chance to start exploring the 'verse - the IAE Free Fly runs from November 22 to December 5.
If you're looking to dive deeper into the latest ships and hear about upcoming gameplay, check out the Crusader Intrepid Q&A for more information about our latest starter. Also, be sure to catch the new IAE Show for some good ol' fashioned fun - Episode 1 and Episode 2 are packed with info-heavy updates on Time-to-Kill/Time-to-Disable mechanics and capital-ship combat, making them a must-watch!
If you're heading to the expo this year, don't forget to bring your cameras to capture your IAE adventures for this year's screenshot contest. You've got NINE chances to win and fly home in a new ship, so get to sharing!
## Now, let's see what's going on this week:
This Monday, the IAE will truly embrace its alien side, as the manufacturers Gatac Manufacture, Aopoa, Banu, and Esperia take over the expo halls. In addition, we will post a second Alpha 4.0 Patch Watch to Spectrum.
This Tuesday, the IAE will feature Roberts Space Industries' remarkable ships, and we'll release our second capital ship, the RSI Polaris, alongside the RSI Polaris Q&A. In addition, the Narrative team will share the latest monthly Galactapedia update.
Wednesday delivers our bi-weekly Roadmap Update and the complementary Roadmap Roundup, while the IAE halls spotlight the ship manufacturers Argo Astronautics, Consolidated Outland, Greycat Industrial, and Kruger Intergalactic.
Thursday brings the rugged and practical ships of Drake Interplanetary to the forefront of the IAE, with the third episode of the IAE Show airing alongside.
Friday is all about elegance and luxury as Origin Jumpworks takes the stage at IAE to showcase its exquisite ships, and the weekly RSI Newsletter will arrive directly in your inbox.
Saturday highlights Anvil Aerospace, renowned for its military ships trusted by the UEE Navy. The day also features the Anvil Terrapin Medic Q&A, where you can dive into the details of this all-new medical ship.
Sunday marks the grand finale of the IAE with Best in Show. The spotlight will shine on the Best in Show ships and an impressive display of weapons and armor. As an extra highlight, every ship from the past nine days will be available to rent and test for free, giving you the perfect opportunity to explore them all and find your new favorite. In addition, Episode 4 of the IAE Show will air - don't miss this one!
Have a stellar week both in and out of the 'verse and enjoy the Intergalactic Aerospace Expo 2954!
