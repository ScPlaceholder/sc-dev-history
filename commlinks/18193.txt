Happy Monday, everyone!
Alien week is well underway, celebrating all our friends and foes in the ‘verse with videos, contests, and more! Catch up on the festivities and look forward to what’s coming the rest of this week with our Alien Week Overview.
In addition to our Trading Card and Cook-Off contests for Alien Week, our Show Your Colors Celebration for Pride Month is still underway! ## Now, let’s see what’s going on this week:
On Tuesday, the Narrative Team has a special Lore Post for Alien Week! House Gatac has opened its archives for a special look at a pivotal part in its history.
On Wednesday, we’ll be updating the Star Citizen Public Roadmap, so keep an eye out for the accompanying Roadmap Roundup for all the details.
Thursday we’ve got a brand new episode of Inside Star Citizen coming to our YouTube channel. This episode is a look forward into the future of Dynamic Events with Ninetails Lockdown and more, followed by a Sprint Report.
On Friday, you’ll see an update to the Subscriber Vault and the weekly RSI Newsletter delivered to your inbox. Finally, we have a new episode of Star Citizen Live, which will broadcast on our Star Citizen Twitch channel at 8am Pacific / 3pm UTC. This week we’ll be sitting down with the EU Persistent Universe Feature Team for a roundtable Q&A. Be sure to submit your questions to the submission thread on Spectrum!
Have a great week! Happy #PrideMonth2951!
