Ship Showdown Grand Finals
Phase 2: A Champion Will Rise
Every year, Ship Showdown challenges the Star Citizen community to rally for their favorite in-game vehicle. And with more than 130 now in the 'verse, there are plenty of contenders for the title!
After receiving hundreds of creative submissions (screenshots, videos, original songs, 3D models, and more) in phase 1, we're now entering the epic head-to-head clash between the FINAL SIXTEEN! The era of a new Ship Showdown champion is about to arrive, but who will sit on the throne?
Starting August 31, it's on you to vote daily for this year's champion on the Ship Showdown page.
## Challengers to the Throne
## YOUR TOP 16
In phase 2, the top 16 ships will go head-to-head daily to narrow down the list to the ultimate aerospace champion. To help structure the battles thematically this year, we have broken up the top 16 vehicles into four pools that best represent their style. We can't wait to see how you vote, and ultimately discover who will be crowned... SHIP SHOWDOWN CHAMPION 2952!
Remember, the top eight ships will be available in our upcoming Free Fly, and the final four will unlock some fun goodies coming later this year!
