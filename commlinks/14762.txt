## Top 5 Things from Reverse The Verse #50
## 1. Controller balance is still hotly debated…
In one of the more serious moments of the broadcast, fans were treated to a nice TOASTING. Steven eloquently explains his own frustrations with controller balance, but also brings to light the delicate nature of developing a massive game like this. It is important to recognize the imbalances at hand, but at the end of the day building out the game needs to take priority over an alpha balance of an incomplete set. He said it far better than we could ever abbreviate, so check it out at around the 44 minute mark.
