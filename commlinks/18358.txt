Happy Monday, everyone!
Thanks to everyone who tuned into CitizenCon 2951 this weekend! We left our Stanton home and dove into the Pyro system, explored the upcoming Outpost Homesteads (and how you’ll build your own in the future), got previews of the Origin 400i and the Anvil Liberator, took a deep dive into Server Meshing and Persistence in Star Citizen, and much, much more.
Missed part of the show? We have all the panels on YouTube now in stunning 4K quality. Check out the CitizenCon landing page for all of the links to the panels, as well as all of the amazing community videos that played throughout the show.
During the show on Saturday we also released Alpha 3.15 to Wave 1 on the PTU! Healing, hospitals, our revamped inventory system, the A2 Hercules and its bombs, the Origin 400i, and much more await in the persistent universe’s latest update. For more information on the PTU, how waves work, and how to access the PTU once invited, check out our FAQ here.
## Now, let’s see what’s going on this week:
On Tuesday the Narrative team has a new Advocacy Archive lore post: Terra Freedom Coalition. With the 2951 CitizenCon at risk, the Advocacy office on Terra searches for a way to infiltrate a group of potentially violent political activists.
Wednesday we’ll be posting the Squadron 42 Monthly Report for September that went out last week via email, this time as a Comm-link. Additionally, as we lead up to the release of Alpha 3.15, we’ll be posting a series of Patch Watch posts and guides that will dive deep into the update’s features. The first, coming on Wednesday, is diving into the nitty gritty of the new Personal Inventory system.
On Friday, you’ll see an update to the Subscriber Vault and the RSI Newsletter will wait for you in your inbox. Star Citizen Live will be taking another week off to give the fine folks on our video team a much needed break after CitizenCon.
Have a fantastic week!
Jake Bradley
