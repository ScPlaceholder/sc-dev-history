## Notable Changes: May 7, 2025
## Release View
## 4.1
Missions - Local Site Maintenance
Implementing new missions into the persistent universe, first appearing in Pyro's outposts. Players will be tasked with going to the location and fixing equipment with fuses and repairing pipes (RMC Repair) with the your multi-tool.
- The card has been set to Committed.
Missions - Animal Population Control
Enabling animal population control Missions.
- The card has been set to Committed.
Mission - Hunt The Polaris
Adding a new Fleet Week mission. A Polaris has been stolen by the Frontier Fighters and the CDF are tasked by the UEE Navy to hunt it through the Stanton system, isolate it, and kill it. The mission will be offered rarely, can be shared, and will only have one active at a time. Once completed, a temporary stand-in reward will be given.
- The card has been set to Committed.
New Hairstyles (4.1.1)
Adding four additional hairstyles with Update 4.1.1.
- The card has been set to Committed.
VOLT SMG "Quartz"
A compact, yet devastating firearm designed for those who need maximum firepower in a minimal frame.
- The card has been set to Committed.
Components Balance Pass
Balancing ships components, and shield generators, based on their class types.
- The card has been set to Committed.
Wikelo Special Ships (4.1.1)
Adding the following Special Ships to Wikelo's Emporium: MISC Starlancer MAX, Aegis Sabre Firebird, Aegis Sabre Peregrine, RSI Zeus ES, RSI Zeus CL, Crusader Intrepid, with top-grade components. These ships can be unlocked by bringing the NPC Wikelo a number of favors in exchange of rewards.
- The card has been set to Committed.
## Retroactively (4.1.0)
"Armor with Horn and String" Wikelo Special
Adding Wikelo's Special version of the Antium armor, the Ana Armor Endro.
- The card has been set to Released.
Item Recovery T0
Adding Item Recovery T0, allowing players to respawn with the gear they had equipped at the time of death.
- The card has been set to Released.
Collision Physics Update
Updating ship collision damage handling, with full collision data passed down by physics. This means that mass and speed is now calculated in the collision, to make the damage more realistic.
- The card has been set to Released.
Heavy Fighter Archetype Balance Pass
Balancing Heavy Fighters joint health, thruster power, and max speed.
- The card has been set to Released.
Grenade Launcher Balance Pass
Balancing the Grenade Launcher's damage output and handling to reward aiming rather than spamming.
- The card has been set to Released.
Ships and Vehicles Update
Updating the Caterpillar's side elevators to improve its cargo management. Removing the 300i cargo railing to allow for better cargo gameplay, and greatly increasing the SRV fuel storage. Further Hornet and Starlancer MAX flight mass changes.
- The card has been set to Released.
Ship Turret Updates
Updating the Reclaimer, Carrack, Valkyrie, Raft, and Starlifter to have several of their turrets linked to the main pilots' weapons.
- The card has been set to Released.
Wikelo Special Ships (4.1.0)
Adding the following Special Ships to Wikelo's Emporium: Anvil F8C Lightning (Sneak), Anvil F8C Lightning (War), RSI Polaris, RSI Scorpius, RSI Constellation Taurus, Mirai Guardian, Crusader C1 Spirit, with top-grade components. These ships can be unlocked by bringing the NPC Wikelo a number of favors in exchange of rewards.
- The card has been set to Released.
