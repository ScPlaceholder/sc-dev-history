## Subscribers
Centurions will receive the RSI Venture Rust Society Torso Armor. RSI’s Venture is a lightweight armor set built for the unknown. This EVA-rated protection system features an undersuit built from a durable polymer weave that’s designed to withstand extreme environmental conditions and features component armor pieces to protect against impacts and particulates. The Rust Society edition adds a red and tan color scheme so you look good while working hard.
## Imperator Subscribers
Imperator-level subscribers get the RSI MacFlex Rust Society Torso Armor. Whether you’re planetside or in vacuum, RSI’s MacFlex industrial armor set has your back. Reinforced plating keeps you safe from environmental hazards while the array of pouches keep your tools accessible. The Rust Society edition celebrates blue-collar workers with an exclusive red and tan coloration that hides dirt and wear and tear well.
## If you’re an active subscriber, these items will be added to your account on May 8th.
## If you aren’t a subscriber but want to sport these fancy breast plates, make sure you subscribe no later than May 12th.
