## Weekly Wisdom: Invisible VJoy
Attention pilots!
Do you want to customize the indicators of your fighter further? No problem!
You can now make the VJoy-Indicator visible or invisible in your HUD, or only show it if you're using a mouse instead.
Just open the Options Menu and head to Game Settings. Scroll down a bit until you reach Pilot / Driver - VJoy Visibility or Turrets - VJoy Visibility and make your adjustments. Good luck out there!
