## Greetings Citizens,
The San Diego Comic-Con is almost upon us and Cloud Imperium Games will be there! Lead Writer David Haddock and Jump Point Editor David Ladyman will be in the Portfolio Area Thursday, Friday, and Saturday from 2:30 PM-7:00 PM. They will be reviewing writing samples and short stories, giving feedback and looking for potential writers for future Spectrum Dispatch entries and Jump Point serials. If you are at SDCC and want to say hello, please stop by! On-site fiction submissions from Star Citizen backers are welcome.
We hope to see you at Comic-Con!!
