## Happy Monday, everyone!
Last week, Alpha 4.10.1 arrived on our Live servers, introducing Orison Relief Support alongside the new Aegis Sabre Raven EX. From now until October 7, you can help rebuild Orison from the ashes through various missions, from combat and salvage to crafting and hauling supplies where they're needed most, with a full set of "SecondWind" gear waiting for those who see it through. If you have questions about Orison Relief Support or the new vehicles, be sure to check out the Orison Relief Support FAQ and Vehicle Q&A Comm-Link.
Thank you to everyone who tested throughout the PTU phase and helped push Alpha 4.10.1 out the door. A big shoutout as well to everyone who jumped into the Time-to-Kill Balance Test Tech-Preview, your feedback continues to shape how combat feels across the 'verse. With that said, there's more testing ahead: Alpha 4.10.2 will begin Evocati PTU testing shortly, with wider waves to follow. As always, if you run into any bugs or have feedback, don't forget to report them through the Issue Council and the dedicated Spectrum feedback threads. We appreciate your support.
Speaking of keeping busy, Pirate Week 2026 is far from over. There's still time to make a name for yourself in the Most Wanted contest and let the 'verse know who to fear. And for those chasing bigger treasure, the hunt for the "doubloon" draws to a close, with the final clue hidden within this very page for only those sharp enough to spot it.
Following up on our earlier announcement about changes to pledge gifting and game access, we have published an FAQ explaining how the new Game Library system will work when it takes effect on October 1, 2026. Also, you'll be able to select your preferred pledge for the new system during a window running through November 1. Read the full details here.
Last but not least, huge thanks to everyone who joined us for Bar Citizen Milan! It was a fantastic night filled with laughs, quizzes, giveaways, and plenty of great conversations with passionate pilots, all brought together by our shared love for Star Citizen. A special shoutout to TileMiaRut for organizing such an amazing event. You made the whole team feel right at home!
## Now, let's see what's going on this week:
Today, we’re introducing 10 From the Community, a new show debuting on October 5. It’ll be short, focused, and built around your questions, with each episode covering a different area of Star Citizen’s development. Head over to today’s announcement to submit your questions for the first episode, and remember to vote for the ones you most want to see answered.
At the same time, we’re taking a wider look at our show lineup and the kinds of content we produce. We’ll be sharing a survey soon to hear what you’d like to see from us. Whether there’s an old favorite you’d love to see return or an entirely new idea you think we should explore, we want to hear it.
Wednesday would normally bring the latest Roadmap Update and Roadmap Roundup, but with Alpha 4.11 development on track and no notable changes to report this week, we've decided to hold off for now and revisit the Roadmap Update in two weeks.
Friday, the RSI Weekly Newsletter lands in your inbox as usual. If you're not subscribed yet, you can sign up anytime through your account settings.
On Saturday, September 26, we're heading to Porto, Portugal, for our next Bar Citizen World Tour stop! Join us at MXM ArtCenter from 2:00 PM to 7:00 PM UTC for an evening with fellow Citizens and the development team. We can't wait to see you there!
Also on Saturday, the Fight or Flight 2956 3v3 dogfighting tournament reaches its finals! Don't forget to catch the best squads in the 'verse going head-to-head as they battle it out for the championship.
