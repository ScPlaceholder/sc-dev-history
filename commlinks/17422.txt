## Roadmap Roundup
Happy 2020 everyone!
Every week we publish a Roadmap Roundup, a brief explanatory note to give you insight into the decision-making that led to any changes in the Public Roadmap. This is part of an effort to make our communications more transparent, specific, and insightful for all of you who help to make Star Citizen and Squadron 42 possible.
Right now, key members of our team are heads-down in planning week, which is a regular re-evaluation you may remember us calling out in the past. We are looking to the future and evaluating current tasks, priorities, progress, and timelines. As we assess all of this, we’ll make adjustments where needed and communicate those directly to you.
Due to planning week, this week’s update will be minimal. We’re currently targeting a regular Roadmap publish & Roundup for next Friday, January 17th.
-CIG Community Team
