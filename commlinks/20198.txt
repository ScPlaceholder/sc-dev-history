Earlier in the year, our Ship Showdown event set out to determine the community’s ultimate in-game vehicle of 2954. You all fought hard for your favorites, creating original images, songs, crafts, models, and more to help them through to the finals. The voting was close, but a triumphant top four emerged.
- Anvil F8C Lightning
- Crusader C1 Spirit
- Anvil Terrapin
- Aegis Reclaimer
