Happy Monday, everyone!
Break out the hot chocolate, warm sweaters, and wrapping paper – it’s time once again for Luminalia in Star Citizen! We’ve enjoyed watching many of you already delivering presents and scavenging for hidden gifts spread across the system. Alongside the return of these in-game activities, we’re also giving you a gift daily from now until Luminalia on December 22. Wondering how to get started? Check out the Celebrate Luminalia 2951 overview page for details!
On top of these festivities, we’re running two Luminalia themed contests over the holiday. Take your very best Luminalia-themed screenshot, or create a holiday greeting card from your favorite manufacturer in the ‘verse – the choice is yours. There’s a total of six vehicles to be won, so be sure to submit your entries!
## Now, let’s see what’s going on this week:
This Tuesday, scire_facias gets word about drug facilities becoming automated in Stanton and angles to take advantage of the situation in this week’s Lore Post, titled DataCache: WiDoWmaker.
Wednesday brings our next update to the Roadmap, alongside a Roadmap Roundup.
Thursday will have the season finale of Inside Star Citizen as we take a look at the past year in the ‘verse, as well as what’s coming over the holiday.
On Friday, Jeremiah Lee returns to create a new mascot figurehead for this year’s Luminalia festivities in this episode of Star Citizen Live Gamedev, which will go live at 10am Pacific / 6pm UTC. We’ll round out the week with our Weekly Newsletter and a Subscriber Vault Update.
Happy Luminalia, everyone!
Jake Bradley
