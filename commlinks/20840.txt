DEFENSE DRIVEN
Clear the skies and set your sights with Greycat Industrial’s newest addition to its growing ground vehicle family, the threat-thwarting MDC. This sturdy rover keeps its wheels on the ground and its aim in the air as its automated PDT, the first to grace any ground vehicle, takes on incoming ordnance that descends to ruin your day. Another functional triumph for Greycat, and the ultimate ground support for your squad.
EYES ON THE ROAD
Automated Air Defense
Keep pushing to the front lines and far reaches while the rear-mounted PDT auto-engages approaching airborne threats. Stick with ballistics for enhanced armor and shield penetration, or switch to missile and laser options to suit your dynamic combat needs.
AT THE PUSH OF A BUTTON
Easy Defense
Activate a quick and effortless slew of strikes from the driver’s seat with dash-mounted PDT control, letting you keep cover as you power through combat zones.
BACK-UP BACKUP
Covering Defense
Set up base and let the bullets fly. The MDC’s automated turret activates even when you step outside, meaning you can take care of ground attacks while the PDT covers the sky.
