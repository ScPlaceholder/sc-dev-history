## Happy Monday, everyone!
Our annual Coramor celebration is still in full swing! Whether you’re enjoying the day with a special someone or reveling in solo festivities, you can spread warm, fuzzy vibes across the universe. And if you consider yourself a true romance connoisseur, share your most memorable dating mishaps in our contest for a chance to swipe a prize while helping others steal hearts.
For some, love is a battlefield, and what better way than to jump into the fray than the Fight for Pyro? We've made the decision to extend Fight for Pyro, giving you extra time to secure your rewards and wrap up your engagements in Alpha 4.0.2 (now in the final stages of PTU testing) before we bid farewell.
If your heart races at the thought of traversing through tricky tracks at full tilt, signups for the Crux Cup 2955 and System7 events are open. Whether you're a seasoned pilot or a fresh-faced neophyte looking to make a name for yourself, these events are a fantastic opportunity to showcase your prowess and connect with fellow racers.
Lastly, over the past week, we've performed several maintenance sessions on the PU, focusing on infrastructure tuning, adjustments to game shards, global database tuning and maintenance, and scalability improvements in preparation for upcoming updates. These efforts play a big role in our push for playability, and we greatly appreciate your understanding.
While Alpha 4.0.2 will introduce a host of fixes and improvements, we know it will take some time to reach the level of playability we're aiming for. Rest assured that our teams are hellbent on ensuring each and every release pushes us forward. Thank you to everyone who has taken the time to share your feedback and reports - we couldn't do it without you!
