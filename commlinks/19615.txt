## Happy Monday, everyone!
The Intergalactic Aerospace Expo is drawing to a close this week, and its been amazing seeing all of you check out the show floor, flying new ships, driving new tanks, and entering IAE's selection of contests. We've broken a few attendance records this year, and we can't thank you enough! We're entering the finale days this week, which means you're able to rent any and all of Star Citizen's lineup of vehicles for free! Be sure to get some flight time in before the convention doors close for the year on Thursday.
The submissions for the Convention Badge Design and Profession Guide contests are nothing short of spectacular; we've come to expect the community's creativity making our jobs judging a winner more and more difficult with each contest we put on! You still have time to get a submission in, so get creative and get your projects in before the contests both close on Thursday, November 30.
## Now, let's see what's going on this week:
Tuesday has the latest Galactapedia Update from the Narrative Team. Also be sure to have your gear locked and loaded, as Jumptown reopens once again! Keep an eye out for the schedule post going live soon.
On Wednesday, we'll publish the latest Roadmap Update alongside a Roadmap Roundup.
Thursday's Inside Star Citizen is a look at the Pyro Playground: the testing environment for Star Citizen's next solar system first seen at CitizenCon 2953. Also on Thursday, the Nine Tails pirates have taken advantage of the focus being on microTech as they've once again laid siege to Orison! Grab the priority mission to take on the Siege of Orison global event through the weekend.
This Friday, there won't be an episode of Star Citizen Live as the team heads to Dublin for the next stop on our Bar Citizen World Tour. We'll be back with a new broadcast next week! You'll also receive our weekly newsletter in your inboxes.
Saturday we've got a pair of Bar Citizens we'll be attending! The first is in Dublin, Ireland at Token Dublin, starting at 7 PM local time. There's also a Virtual Bar Citizen put on by the fine folks at Sol Citizens beginning at 12 PM Eastern US Time, head over to their social post to register if you plan to attend!
May your heart be your guiding key,
