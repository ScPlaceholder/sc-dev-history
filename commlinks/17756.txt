Happy Monday, everyone!
Ship Showdown is underway with Phase 1! This preliminary phase calls on you to show your support for your favorite flyable or drivable by submitting an original creation to the Community Hub or on Twitter using #SCShipShowdown. We’ve already seen hundreds of incredible submissions, and can’t wait to check out everything else you have in store! This phase will run until September 7th (that’s next Monday), so don’t wait to get creative and show your love for your favorite flyable or drivable! Remember that you can also participate by upvoting creations showing off your favorite ships – after all, the winning ships may unlock some cool prizes later this year! From there, we’ll slide into head-to-head match-ups between the top 16 ships from Phase 1! Check out the complete details on the main Ship Showdown page here!
In case you missed it, last week Lead Gameplay Engineer Chad McKinney and Lead Network Programmer Clive Johnson joined us to chat all things core tech in the latest episode of Calling All Devs. On Inside Star Citizen, we took a look at how spacescaping is making long hauls through the void more visually stimulating, and went on a hard-hitting tour of the devs’ force reaction amusement park.
