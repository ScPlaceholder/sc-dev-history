Things to Do in Star Citizen
Alpha 3.17
Ever wanted to live in a universe that grants the freedom of exploration, the thrill of combat, and the unique challenge of building a life in space? Star Citizen puts ultimate control directly in your hands, whether you're making your way as a cargo hauler, exploring the vastness of space, or scraping out a living outside the law, it's up to you to define your story.
You've got your starter ship, you've chosen a primary residence, and you're ready to start your adventures in the 'verse, but where should you start? There's a lot to see and do in the Stanton system. Read on for a quick guide for a few of the options available to you take to the skies for the first time.
Take a Tour
Looking for a place to start? We've put together a checklist that will take you all over the Stanton system, from the biggest sights to the coziest spots to hang out with your friends.
Visit a Hospital
Before you venture too far out into the 'verse, be sure to visit a hospital or clinic. If you meet an untimely demise while out adventuring, you'll respawn at your designated imprint location, which is your primary residence city by default. You can set this to any hospital or clinic by visiting them and selecting "Transfer Imprint" at a Regeneration terminal.
Hospitals and clinics are also a great place to stock up on medical supplies, medical multi-tool attachments, or even dedicated healing tools. Check out our Hospitals and Healing Guide for more information on how these tools work, as well as additional guides through the entire medical gameplay system.
