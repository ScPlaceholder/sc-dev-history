## Now let's see what's going on this week:
Later this week, we're targeting the release of Alpha 4.10.1, bringing another round of technical and bug fixes, including fixes to ASOP terminals and ship statuses, a fix for Mendo Ren's HMG spawn issues, and more. You can follow the current PTU updates on Spectrum.
The update will also introduce the Orison Relief Support event, where you'll lend a hand in rebuilding Orison through missions spanning resource gathering, crafting, hauling, and combat. Following your feedback from the PTU, we've significantly reduced the requirements needed to complete the event, while its blueprints have been removed from the reward track and will instead be granted directly to your account. We'll keep hammering on Alpha 4.10.1 in the PTU ahead of its Live release, while work continues on Alpha 4.11.
On Friday, the latest RSI Weekly Newsletter also lands in your inbox. If you’re not subscribed yet, you can sign up at any time through your account settings.
