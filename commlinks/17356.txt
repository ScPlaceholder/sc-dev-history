## Weekly Wisdom: The Atmosphere & You
Between the recent Free-Fly and this weekend’s CitizenCon, we’ve got a lot of new players in the ‘verse! So now’s the perfect time to remind everyone that you can not begin quantum traveling while you’re still in a planet’s atmosphere.
For example, when leaving Area18 on ArcCorp, ensure you reach an altitude of 80,000 before attempting to quantum travel!
