May the 4th be with you, everyone!
Alpha 3.9 Locked up & Loaded is LIVE! From the all-new Klescher Rehabilitation Facility on Aberdeen to the vastly improved Friends List that makes teaming up a breeze, there are countless game-changing features to discover. To celebrate the launch of the latest patch, we’re giving every new player brought in by referral (and the one who referred them) a Greycat buggy. It’s perfect for exploring microTech’s dangerous-yet-beautiful moons, Clio, Caliope, and Euterpe.
Last Friday, we announced that due to the impact of COVID-19, we have decided to postpone CitizenCon until 2021. Nevertheless, we’re still planning to celebrate Star Citizen’s birthday on October 10th. Read all about it on Spectrum.
Also last week, we published a look ahead to what’s heading to Star Citizen in the near future. From Fleet Week to Theaters of War, we’re keeping the momentum going! Find out what’s coming up in the next few weeks.

## Weekly Wisdom: It’s not you it’s me
Star Citizen won’t launch and you aren’t sure if it’s your PC’s fault or if the servers are down for maintenance?
Head over to our brand new overview page that shows the status of the Platform, Persistent Universe, and Electronic Access.
It also tells you about recent incidents, if they have been solved or are still ongoing, and all possible workarounds.
