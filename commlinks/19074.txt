January 2023 Subscriber Promotions
Welcome to 2953! This year will be Star Citizen’s biggest to date, and Subscribers can kick it off in speed and style.
Greycat’s latest little racer, the STV, is available to drive throughout January, while fans of the ‘verse’s other high-octane ground vehicles can enjoy them with brand-new paints.
We’re also tweaking how the Subscriber Exclusive Promotion runs. Now, the Vehicle of the Month and Subscriber-Exclusive Pledge Promotion will run together, meaning you don’t have to wait until the end of the month to add it to your fleet. So, while we catch up a month, Subscribers can pick up both the Anvil Centurion and Greycat STV with enhanced insurance until the end of January.
