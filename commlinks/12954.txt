We’ve had a number of requests to see Chris Roberts’ SXSW presentation, “Breathing New Life into PC Games & Space Sims.” Unfortunately, our cameras were not allowed in to film the presentation, so we haven’t been able to provide a copy… until now! Using the audio track and the slideshow created for the presentation, we’ve put together a podcast that will let you see what you missed! It clocks in at about 45 minutes, so get ready to hear some serious details about what went into the decision to create Star Citizen.
The original presentation included a slide showing off the Aurora… but you’ll have to wait just a little longer to see those images here! You can find video of Chris’ other SXSW panel on the Oculus Rift here.
