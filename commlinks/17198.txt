## Now, let’s see what’s going on this week:
On Tuesday, the Lore Team will publish the tale of how a hacked terminal in Nexus spelled trouble for all the UEE’s bounty hunters.
Find out what the CitizenCon 2949 Digital Goodies Pack will include this Wednesday alongside the pack’s launch. Also Wednesday, for those of you in Cologne for Gamescom, we’re kicking off our first night of Bar Citizen revelry. Find all the details of three nights of merriment here. We’ll also be roaming the show floor throughout the week and handing out exclusive swag. Stay tuned to our social media channels to find out where we’ll be at the event. Can you find us?
Thursday welcomes a new episode of Inside Star Citizen, our weekly look behind the scenes of Star Citizen development.
Friday will see a Roadmap Update and the RSI Newsletter delivered right to your inbox. We also welcome a new episode of Star Citizen Live, which airs on our Star Citizen Channel. Check back here soon for an update on who this week’s guest will be.
