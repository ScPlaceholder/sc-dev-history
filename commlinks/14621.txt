## Greetings Citizens,
As you may or may not be aware, we have a new monthly webseries from Cloud Imperium Games called THE WONDERFUL WORLD OF STAR CITIZEN. Hosted by Jared “Disco Lando” Huckaby, it’s a brief look at some of the best and most inventive fan-made creations from Star Citizens around the world.
If you haven’t seen the first episode, you can find that here.
And if you’re interested in contributing content to future episodes, take a look at the submission guidelines.
We think you’ll enjoy this monthly visit through the Wonderful World of Star Citizen!
