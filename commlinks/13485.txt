## Greetings Citizens,
We’re getting ready to launch The Next Great Starship, a competition which will put talented designers and artists against each other to design a ship for Star Citizen… and we’re asking YOU to choose the ship!
Please vote in the poll below to select a ship type for the contest. Do you want to see a fearsome gunship or stealthy recon craft?
