Hello everyone!
Every two weeks, we accompany the Roadmap update with a brief explanatory note to give you insight into the decision-making that led to any changes. This is part of an effort to make our communications more transparent, more specific, and more insightful for all of you who help to make Star Citizen and Squadron 42 possible.
Just a reminder: The 1.0 column showcases features and content planned for inclusion in or before Star Citizen 1.0. This means you may not have to wait for the 1.0 release to experience them. As features/content in the 1.0 column become ready, they will migrate left into a point patch column for release.
Be sure to check back every two weeks for the latest updates.
Note: REDACTED cards are purposefully hiding details, to avoid spoiling content for players. As we have now started our test cycle with the Evocati, you will learn more as we progress through the development of 4.2.0 and beyond.
With that, let’s dive into this week’s Roadmap Roundup!
-CIG Community Team
## Notable Changes: May 21, 2025
## Release View
## 4.1
Missions - Local Site Maintenance
Implementing new missions into the persistent universe, first appearing in Pyro's outposts. Players will be tasked with going to the location and fixing equipment with fuses and repairing pipes (RMC Repair) with your multi-tool.
- The card has been set to Released.
Missions - Animal Population Control
Enabling animal population control Missions.
- The card has been set to Released.
Mission - Hunt The Polaris
Adding a new Fleet Week mission. A Polaris has been stolen by the Frontier Fighters, and the CDF is tasked by the UEE Navy to hunt it through the Stanton system, isolate it, and kill it. The mission will be offered rarely, can be shared, and will only have one active at a time. Once completed, a reward will be given.
- The card has been set to Released.
New Hairstyles (4.1.1)
Adding four additional hairstyles with Update 4.1.1.
- The card has been set to Released.
VOLT SMG "Quartz"
Adding a compact, yet devastating firearm designed for those who need maximum firepower in a minimal frame.
- The card has been set to Released.
Components Balance Pass
Balancing ship components and shield generators based on their class types.
- The card has been set to Released.
Wikelo Special Ships (4.1.1)
Adding the following Special Ships to Wikelo's Emporium: MISC Starlancer MAX, Aegis Sabre Firebird, Aegis Sabre Peregrine, RSI Zeus ES, RSI Zeus CL, Crusader Intrepid, with top-grade components (Currently Stock modules. It will be fixed in 4.2.0). These ships can be unlocked by bringing the NPC Wikelo a number of favors in exchange of rewards.
- The card has been set to Released.
Space Combat Missions: UEE Training, Patrol, Ambush
Adding new missions to the 'verse.
- These cards have been set to Released.
