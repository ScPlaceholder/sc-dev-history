Ship Showdown 2956
The Elite Eight
Every year, Ship Showdown challenges the community to champion their favorite vehicle. With a lineup spanning iconic fighters, industrial workhorses, heavy hitters and plenty more, 2956 is shaping up to be one for the record books! From longtime favorites to newer contenders, you made your voices heard and decided which ships deserved a shot at the crown. We received countless incredible submissions, including screenshots, videos, Lego® creations, grain arts, 3D-printed ships and even cardboard models. Thousands of entries were tallied to bring us the Top 16, which battled it out in the first round of head-to-head bouts. Now, as we enter the ELITE EIGHT, all of our remaining contenders are available to fly for free to all backers! The final battle starts here, where your votes will ultimately crown this year's Ship Showdown champion.
