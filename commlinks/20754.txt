## Happy Monday, everyone!
Strap in pilots, because Ship Showdown 2955 has reached its final face-off! Legendary machines have gone head-to-head, and now it's up to you to decide this year's champion. Today's finale battle is one where steel and strategy collide: The RSI Polaris, the capital commander, takes on the Anvil Asgard, the armored advocate of the front line. Besides glory, a special champion's prize awaits the victor, along with exclusive manufacturer-themed rewards for the Final Four, to be delivered at this year's Intergalactic Aerospace Expo (IAE). Plus, there’s still time to take the Elite Eight out for a spin in the ongoing Free Fly event.
Speaking of events, CitizenCon Direct 2955 is fast approaching! This year's event is a special digital showcase, and we're sending out key CIG team members to select Watch Parties - we can’t wait to connect with you in person! And, don't miss your chance to be part of this year's show; submit your video by September 30 to our currently ongoing contest for a shot to be featured on the livestream and win a hot new ship!
Lastly, a huge thank you to everyone who came out to the PAX West Bar Citizen in Seattle last Friday. It was fantastic catching up with familiar faces and meeting new ones! The Bar Citizen World Tour rolls on, with our next stop in Lisbon, Portugal this Saturday. We hope to see you there!
## Now, let's see what's going on this week
Fury, fire, and fortitude - the Polaris and the Asgard square off in the final fight this Monday. Only one can be crowned the Ship Showdown champion of 2955, so don't forget to cast your vote TODAY and ensure your victor claims their place in the hall of legends!
On Tuesday, Subscribers can catch the latest update, including the Subscriber Comm-Link, Newsletter, and the August 2025 issue of Jump Point. Also on Tuesday, we'll see the ultimate champion of Ship Showdown 2955 announced!
On Wednesday, our servers and platform services will be offline for maintenance for approximately 4 hours starting from 11:00 UTC. During this maintenance window, all gameplay systems will be unavailable; you will not be able to access the game until maintenance is complete. We will provide updates on the RSI Status Page as the maintenance progresses. Thanks for your patience as we carry out this work.
Also on Wednesday, we'll be publishing the August 2025 PU Monthly Report for a behind-the-scenes look at what the dev teams worked on for Star Citizen last month.
Thursday will see the publish of the latest Squadron 42 Newsletter.
On Friday, the weekly RSI Newsletter will zip straight into your inbox for all the news and highlights you won't want to miss.
Fly low, and fly fast!
