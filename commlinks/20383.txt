ANVIL SUPER HORNET MK II
A NEW LEGENDARY CHAPTER
Introducing the magnum opus of the Anvil Hornet legacy. Delivering everything you have come to expect from the Empire's
most revered military shipbuilder, the Super Hornet enhances the Mk II chassis with stronger shields, upgraded
weaponry, and a dedicated gunner's seat, enabling you to defend your interests more furiously than ever. As effectively
as its forebears dealt with old threats, the Mk II eliminates new ones.

ANVIL SUPER HORNET MK II
A DEFENSIVE POWERHOUSE
Superior Weaponry
Boasting even more firepower than its predecessor,
the Super Hornet Mk II is closer to military spec than anything
