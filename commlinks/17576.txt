## Weekly Wisdom: Spectrum knows your Friends
With the upcoming release of Alpha 3.9 to the live servers, three separate friends lists (Spectrum, in-game friends, platform/website) will be transferred into one unified list. This will make it way easier to keep in touch with your friends when moving from one place to another. So, now is the perfect time to update your friends list and add all your friends or favorite org comrades.
If you’re already in Spectrum, click on the friends icon in the upper-right corner, then on the cogwheel. From there, select Manage Friends. Or simply use the direct link”. Click the +Add Friend button and enter your friend’s name. It doesn’t matter if you use their community monicker or handle. As soon as your friend pops up, send a friend request by clicking the button on the right.
As soon as your friend accepts your request, you’ll not only be friends on Spectrum, but will be connected in Star Citizen on the live servers.
