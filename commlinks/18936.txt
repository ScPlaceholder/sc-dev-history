November 2022 Subscriber Promotions
Welcome to November, the month of the Intergalactic Aerospace Expo! Between Day of the Vara and Luminalia lies the biggest ship show in the ‘verse, and we can’t wait to welcome you all to the event halls of New Babbage, microTech.
And you’ll be turning up in style thanks to this month’s Flair: Xi’an flight suits adapted to accommodate the Human physique. These items will be available to current Subscribers (and those who Subscribe between now and Nov 8th) with the launch of IAE this month.

As usual, last month’s Vehicle of the Month, the MISC Hull A, is available to all Subscribers with enhanced insurance.
