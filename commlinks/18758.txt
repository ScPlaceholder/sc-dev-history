July 2022 Subscriber Promotions
As we enter the real-world summer months, the ‘verse is heating up too thanks to Crusader and its mighty Ares Star Fighter series. Available to fly throughout July, both the laser-equipped Ion and Gatling-outfitted Inferno are ready and willing to tackle the biggest foes across Stanton.
Despite the Ares’ single-seat layout, you’ll never be alone on long missions thanks to July’s Flair – an adorable series of Sakura Fun bobbleheads that’ll brighten up the cockpit of any ship that has an appropriate hardpoint on it.
If living, breathing crewmates are more your bag, the outgoing Vehicle of the Month is available with enhanced insurance until August. Don’t miss carting your whole crew into battle with the Anvil Spartan troop transporter.
