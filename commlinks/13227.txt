We’ve shared concept art from the Discount and Business Hangars… now check out all the work that went into the massive Deluxe Hangar! The Deluxe Hangar is currently available only on accounts that include a Constellation spacecraft. With elevators that let you get a great birds-eye view of your fleet and plenty of room to expand, the Deluxe Hangar is a great place to start your Star Citizen experience! This artwork, complete with our art team’s sketches and notes, show how the Deluxe Hangar’s bulkheads and other modular systems were put together.
