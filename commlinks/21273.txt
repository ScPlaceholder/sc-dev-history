## Happy Monday, everyone!
Ladies and gentlemen, pilots and ground-pounders, it's time once again for the biggest aerospace smackdown in the 'verse: the arena opens for Ship Showdown 2956 this week! This year's contenders, flyable and drivable, are stepping into the ring for one shot at the title. YOU decide: who reigns supreme?
Phase one is all about the crowd, so get loud, rally behind your champion, and shove your pick into the Top 16 by any creative means necessary: snazzy screenshots, swingin' songs, masterful machinimas, whatever gets your favorite noticed; throw it in the ring. Stay tuned for more details as the showdown begins on July 28.
Manufacturer rivalries aside, this week also brings the launch of Foundation Festival, a celebration of just how welcoming and inclusive this community truly is. Pilots who've seen it all are always ready to share what they know with the ones just getting started. Battle-scarred veteran or fresh-faced rookie, there's something in it for you, including festival-themed rewards, Twitch Drops, a Free Fly, and a refresh to both the Welcome Hub and the Guide System. Full rundown coming your way soon.
Behind the scenes, we've also got some additional fixes rolling out this week, some as early as later today if all goes well, including a few addressing connectivity issues some of you ran into last week. While we continue improving Alpha 4.9, progress on Alpha 4.10 is well underway, currently in Wave 1 on the PTU. The team is working toward opening up to more waves soon, and your feedback helps us tighten every bolt along the way, so keep an eye out for your chance to jump in.
## Now, let's see what's going on this week:
Tuesday kicks off the return of Ship Showdown, where your favorite flyable/drivable vehicles go head-to-head for ultimate glory. Phase 1 is all about nominations, so rally behind your top pick and make some noise to get your faves in the Top 16 (and potentially take home a ship for doing so)! We'll share more details tomorrow, so get ready for Shiiip Showdooown!
On Wednesday, Foundation Festival fires up, bringing festivities to celebrate the folks who make the 'verse feel like home: the community.
Wednesday also sees the latest Roadmap Update alongside its complementary Roadmap Roundup, so be sure to check both out for a glimpse of what's coming down the road.
Come Friday, the RSI Newsletter swings by your inbox with the week's highlights from across the 'verse.
Fly low, and fly fast!
