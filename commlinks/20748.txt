For those who dive into danger without hesitation. For those who dedicate their lives to others. For those who champion goodness, wherever they go. RSI applauds your mission and is proud to present the Apollo Triage and Medivac, two life-saving ships with peerless mobile medical technology. INTO & OUT OF DANGER
Triage & Medivac
Unpredictable battlefields call for different tactics for first responders. While both variants provide effective aid and evacuation, the Medivac’s enhanced armor and missile capacity empower you to go deeper into hazardous conflict for frontline rescue.
A FIRST IN FIRST RESPONSE
Tactical Treatment
Imagine the ability to treat even the most severe wounds, no matter where you are. The Apollo series ships feature two fully modular medical bays, configurable in any combination, with the capability to house up to two Tier 1 MedBeds for an unprecedented advantage in the field.
