## Greetings Citizens,
It has been a while since the last RSI Museum, so we are kicking the series off anew! This episode, we talk to Richard Garriott, Dallas Snell and Paul Neurath (via tele-presence robot!) about their history at Origin Systems working on the Ultima Underworld series. It’s an hour-long look behind the curtains at some of the best games ever made. Whether you’re an Origin Systems enthusiast or a fan of game development, it’s an interesting interview!
Paul Neurath is currently head of OtherSide Entertainment, developing the next game in the Underworld series: Underworld Ascendant. If you’re interested in supporting that project, you can do so here on Kickstarter.
