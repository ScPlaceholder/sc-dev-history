## ALPHA 4.10.1
For the next three weeks until the release of Alpha 4.10.2, 14 new contracts (with themed armor and weapon rewards) will be available to citizens who help repair the damage to Orison. These include:
- Resource Gathering
- Collecting and Delivering Medical Supplies
- Crafting and Delivering Replacement Platform Thrusters
- Taking the Fight Back to the Nine Tails
Should you choose to help Orison rebuild and avenge the fallen against Nine Tails, event rewards will be attributed directly to your RSI Platform account. These will be viewable in the My Hangar section of the website.
