## Greetings Citizens,
The Mustang from Consolidated Outland might just be the new standard in starter ships. The Roberts Space Industries Aurora has become almost a right of passage for traveling in the ‘Verse but a new challenger approaches. At the same price point as the Aurora it is hard not think of the Mustang as the new king. Now you can see how this marvel of engineering is being made.
