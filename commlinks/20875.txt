## Notable Changes: November 5, 2025
## Release View
## 4.4.0
New Wikelo Ships Offerings (4.4.0)
Adding multiple new vehicles and equipment to the ever-expanding Wikelo's Emporium: RSI Meteor and the Guardian MX, with upgraded components and a unique base livery. These rewards can be unlocked by completing Wikelo's contracts.
- This card has received a new visual.
MaxOx Neutron Cannon Art Refactors
Updating several MaxOx Neutron Cannons' art style and geometry: Size 1, 2, and 3.
- This card has been set to Tentative.
Exterior Freight Elevators
Adding new ways of loading and unloading cargo in the currently available systems: Nyx, Stanton, and Pyro.
- This card has been updated to also include Stanton.
