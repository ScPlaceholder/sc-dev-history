## Weekly Wisdom: Civilian Defense Force
You may have already heard about the Civilian Defense Force via OP.NET, the spectrum show for all you indie mercs out there. Nevertheless, what might look like just a piece of Star Citizen lore actually made it into the game and helps you to make money while fighting outlaws. Let me tell you how:
Whenever you load into the PU, first open your mobiGlass and select the CONTRACTS MANAGER. Within the Mercenary section, you’ll find A Call to Arms.
By accepting this mission, Crusader Security will pay you a nice sum for every pirate you turn to dust. Nice!
