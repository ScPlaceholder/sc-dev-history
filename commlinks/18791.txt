## Happy Monday, everyone!
Alpha 3.17.2 is now available on the PTU for all backers! Thanks to all of you who have jumped in, checked out all the new features, tested our events, and reported bugs on the issue council! Curious as to how the PTU process works? Head over to our dedicated PTU landing page for details.
This week we're excited to finally kick off the competitive construction clash for the kids, the Battle of the Bricks. The Star Citizen and Eve Online teams will be broadcasting from Manchester, UK as they see who can build a ship out of bricks the fastest. The catch? Each team has to build the opposite game's ship! All the while, we'll be raising money for Extra Life and undergoing excruciating challenges along the way, so join us for a good time for a great cause.
## Now, let's see what's going on this week:
On Tuesday, the Narrative Team will publish their weekly Lore Post. On this episode of Untold Tales, a long lost ship discovered in the outer reaches of Sol baffles experts hoping to unlock its mysteries.
Wednesday has the latest Roadmap Update, paired with all of the details in a Roadmap Roundup post.
This Friday, the Battle of the Bricks livestream in benefit of Extra Life kicks off at 10am Pacific / 5pm UTC on our Twitch channel. Rather than an episode of Star Citizen Live, the team will be taking on Eve Online in a competition you won't want to miss.
You'll also catch our weekly RSI Newsletter delivered to your inbox, and Subscribers can look forward to an update to the Subscriber Vault.
Have a fantastic week, and see you at the Battle of the Bricks!

Community Manager
