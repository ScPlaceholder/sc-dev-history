Avast there! It be that time of the year again, when the timbers be shivered and the swashes be buckled, and all manner of briny bilge rats come out to engage in merry marauding across the ‘verse. That’s right, ya swabs, Talk Like a Pirate Day is once again upon us, and it be all hands on deck for a bounty of fun and plunder. The crew has been hard at work, and heartily motivated by threats of floggings and watered-down rum rations, getting all the cannons firing properly for the Pirate Swarm game mode in Arena Commander, and thus granting you a shot at the infamous pirate Gladius.
From now until September 20, we’ve also got a screenshot contest for all you crow’s nest photogs out there, a classic piratical paint scheme now in game, and a brand-new set of chrome liveries shinier than the shiniest treasure. Oh, and we heard a rumor that the infamous Nine Tails, emboldened by this freebooter’s free-for-all, have returned to Crusader, intent on taking Orison once and for all.
