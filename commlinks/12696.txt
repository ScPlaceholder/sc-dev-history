Wing Commander fans aren’t the only ones crafting real-world counterparts of their favorite starfighters. Marc “Edge” R.unde is a Freelancer player who has been steadily building up an Armada of Freelancer ships… out of LEGO bricks! From the speedy Dagger to the lumbering Dromedary, he’s already recreated more than a dozen of the game’s iconic craft in excrutiating detail. Many even include detailed interriors unseen in the game itself!
You can view all of Edge’s creations here, with plenty of detailed shots for each ship. Now dig out your old LEGOs and see what you can do!
