Hello everyone,
Last week, we celebrated All Hallows’ Eve across our studios and crowned the winners of two Halloween-themed community contests. Check out the winners of the Pumpkin Carving and Caves of Horror contests, the latter highlighting the community’s most terrifying screenshots inside the caves of Alpha 3.7. We appreciate the creativity you put into both your pumpkin creations and spine-tingling screenshots.
As part of our commitment to open and transparent development, we’ve started releasing monthly infographics, highlighting interesting and insightful statistics that illustrate how your play habits shape and balance the Persistent Universe. Visit Playable Now – The Stats to see the first graphic, showcasing everything from the most popular ships to the countries with the most unique total players.
## Now, let’s see what’s going on this week:
On Tuesday, the Lore Team will release a Portfolio piece about Yorm, a power plant and shield manufacturer.
Wednesday will bring us both the PU & SQ42 Monthly Reports. As always, get ready for an in-depth update on our development progress!
Thursday brings a brand-new episode of our weekly series Inside Star Citizen, the show that provides a behind-the-scenes look at the development of our Persistent Universe.
Friday will bring us updates to the Roadmaps and the Subscriber Vault, with the weekly newsletter delivered straight to your inbox. And finally, we welcome a new episode of Star Citizen Live, which will be broadcasting on the Star Citizen Twitch channel at 9am PDT/4pm UTC. Members of the audio design and programming teams will be answering your questions live on stream.
See you in the ‘verse!
