## Notable Changes for December 21, 2022
As mentioned earlier this week in This Week In Star Citizen, Alpha 3.18 is now targeting early 2023 for release to the live servers. This is now reflected on 3.18's Release View column.
No other significant changes to report this week! Our first Roadmap Update of the year will be on February 8. This update will include the extension of both Upstream and Downstream teams' schedules further into 2023, as well as the addition of features to the 3.19 column.
Happy Luminalia, and we'll see you in the new year!
