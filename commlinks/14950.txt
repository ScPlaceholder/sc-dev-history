The Puglisi collection is proud to announce the 3rd in their line of artifacts- the Vasli Fragment Stone! The Quasi ruins on Terra have been shrouded in mystery for centuries… that is, until 2832, when an expedition made a bold discovery: a fragment of stone found buried deep within Zaffre Bay.
Named after the discoverer, the stone was inscribed with what many believe is a remnant of the ancient civilization’s language, holding its secrets. The Puglisi Collection created a perfect replica of this tremendously important artifact for Citizens to enjoy!
