## Happy Monday, everyone!
First of all, we'd like to remind you that even though Alien Week is ending today, both the Intergalactic Mix-Off Contest and the Alien Ship Poster Contest are still accepting submissions for a few more hours. So jet to Spectrum after reading this and take the chance to win an awesome alien ship. The final hours are also ticking for the Star Citizen vs. EVE Online: Battle of the Bricks submission deadline so send us your Lego Star Citizen spaceship today before 11:59 PM Pacific.
You also still have the chance to have your organization highlighted by us during Foundation Festival in July. In the 'verse, Foundation Festival is a month-long civic program that aims to strengthen the foundation of the community by encouraging citizens and civilians to volunteer in local and imperial organizations. So, we want to give a place in the limelight to the many wonderful organizations that make the Star Citizen community one of the best out there.
And speaking of organizations, it's a great time to be part of one, as Star Citizen Alpha 3.17.2 is already with our Evocati on the PTU servers. If our PTU analytics have taught us anything, it's that you want someone to back you up in the new and explosive Dynamic Event, Siege of Orison.
How about some extra training before the new patch launches? At the end of this week, we're running not one but two Dynamic Events that let you face off against both NPCs and other players. Look out for Nine Tails Lockdown and Jump Town 2.0 to kick off June 30 through July 5! Detailed schedule coming soon.
