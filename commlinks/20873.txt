The Frontier Fighters Finale event continues as Amelia Boyd, the leader of the dangerous Frontier Fighters, has been spotted in Stanton. In response, Citizens for Prosperity and Headhunters have mobilized, aiming to take down Boyd and her forces once and for all. Operations now focus on disabling the enemy’s ability to regen by destroying MedGel recharge stations and intercepting key targets along flight routes. Enlist now and read our FAQ for the latest mission updates to help slow the Frontier Fighters' redeployment.
