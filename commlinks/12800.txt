With all the rush in hitting the October 10th reveal at Game Developers Online in Austin, not everything made it into the live demo.
In the spirit of sharing cool behind the scenes material and illustrating the kind of content you’ll find in “The Vault” for subscribers, here’s a title track for the Squadron 42 prototype level that was composed by George Oldziey, who composed the great scores for Wing Commander 3, Wing Commander 4 and Prophecy as well as other Origin games like Crusader: No Remorse and Ultima IX.
George composed music for the menu screen, inside the carrier, take off and combat. We had some of this working inside the level, but as the dynamic switching wasn’t functioning properly we we pulled it at the last minute.
So without further ado here’s George’s title track
