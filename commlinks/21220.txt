## THERE'S TENSION ON THE FRONTIER
Life outside the UEE is hard-won. Now, an elusive group known as the Moraine is threatening the fragile stability the independent people of Nyx have fought to build.
The People’s Alliance requires your help. Scout safe locations for future operations, search for missing workers, board hijacked ships to rescue survivors, and recover valuable cargo from pirate raids. It’s dangerous work, but the people of Nyx remember those who stand beside them. Earn the trust of the People's Alliance and catch the attention of Recco Battaglia, a respected leader among the free people, who may have even greater opportunities for those willing to answer the call…
