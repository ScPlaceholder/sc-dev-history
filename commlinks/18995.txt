## Happy Monday, everyone!
Kick the tires, light the fires, and make your way over to microTech. This year's Intergalactic Aerospace Expo is only 4 days away! We've got plenty of content in the lead-up to IAE, starting with an old favorite: a Traveler's Guide to this year's expo to help you prepare for your visit to New Babbage early. With a lore post and an IAE-themed set of shows on YouTube and Twitch coming this week to top it off, you'll be all set to hit the show floor.
Keep an eye on Spectrum for more announcements, an FAQ, contests, and more as we get closer to the event's launch on Friday, November 18. And don't forget to invite your friends! IAE kicks off a Free Fly that runs through the length of the expo, so now is a perfect time to jump in.
## Now, let's see what's going on this week:
This Tuesday, the Narrative team keeps the IAE spirit going with a new Lore Post, titled Clean Shot: IAE Predictions. Craig and Skinny get ready for the IAE by making a few predictions about what they expect from the expo. Plus, if this is your first IAE, our community team will be hosting an AMA on Twitter starting at 11:30am Central Time to answer all your questions about this year's event - I'll be there too!
On Wednesday, we'll be giving the full Free Fly schedule with details on every day's list of ships that can be rented on the IAE show floor, completely free.
Thursday's Inside Star Citizen is fully IAE-themed with an in-depth look at this year's sponsor, Drake Interplanetary. Plus, we'll be giving you an update on the long-awaited rework of Origin's exploration and touring craft, the 600i. We'll also have our annual IAE FAQ ready to go; keep an eye out for it on Spectrum.
Friday's Star Citizen Live kicks off Day 1 of IAE with our annual all-ship special with the vehicle team. You can catch the show LIVE on Twitch at 8am Pacific, 4pm UTC. You’ll also receive our weekly Newsletter delivered to your inbox, and Subscribers can look forward to an update to the Subscriber Vault.
And of course, IAE starts this Friday! We can't wait to see you on the show floor!
Have a great week, and see you in New Babbage!
