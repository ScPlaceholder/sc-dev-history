Hi everyone!
Jax McCleary is back and with him the 2949 Intergalactic Aerospace Expo this year on ArcCorp. Each and every day you can expect new exhibitions, inspiring video content from Jax McCleary, and exciting offers from all of our manufacturers.
So, jump into your fastest ship and race to Area 18 on ArcCorp, which is now decorated in celebratory style for the big event. While you’re at it, bring a friend – Star Citizen is now accessible to everyone with our latest Free Fly until December 5th. Each exhibitor’s ships can be flown for 24 hours, every day – that’s over 100 ships in total!
As not all of us are born pilots, we invite you to take a look at the brand new Welcome HUB and Guide System. Here you can find everything you need to become an ace pilot, miner, trader, and more!
If you want to help new players in taking their first steps in the ‘verse, read here on how to apply as a guide. It should also be noted that we will award the top Guide in each category between now and January 1st, 2020 with a special prize to be announced soon. ;)
Last but not least, with much of the team traveling home, and taking a short and well deserved break, we won’t have an episode of Inside Star Citizen or Star Citizen Live during IAE celebrations. We’ll be back to our regular schedule next week! Also, keep a close eye on our YouTube channel where we’re aiming to upload CitizenCon panels as quickly as possible. In addition to ISC and SCL, we also will not have our usual Roadmap publish this week. Instead, we’ll be simply be adding the 3.8 ships announced at CitizenCon today.
With that, I’m off to Area 18 to check out the RSI exhibition. Hope to see you there!
Christian Schmitt
Associate Community Manager
