## IN-GAME REWARDS (FLAIR)
The Pyro from Greycat Industrial is a pistol-sized power tool for the modern space engineer that’s capable of charging, cutting, and patching thanks to its wide assortment of attachments. It’s perfect for making a tough job a little bit easier.
## Pyro RYT “Ghost” Multi-Tool – The tried-and-true Multi-Tool with styling to match the UltiFlex knife, the “Ghost” edition features a grey frame with light blue accents meant to evoke the environments of microTech.
Current Centurion-level Subscribers get the Pyro RYT “Ghost” Multi-Tool seen here as part of their subscription.
## Pyro RYT “Mirage” Multi-Tool – The “Mirage” edition adds eye-catching gold highlights to brown accents, putting it right at home on the plains of Hurston.
Current Imperator-level Subscribers get both the “Ghost” and “Mirage” Multi-Tools as part of their subscription.
## Pyro RYT “Bloodline” Multi-Tool – The limited-edition “Bloodline” blends decorative red highlights throughout the tool, similar to those found across ArcCorp’s industry-covered surface.
The “Bloodline” multi-tool is available to all Subscribers for purchase from the Subscriber-exclusive store.
If you aren’t a Subscriber and would like to receive the “Ghost” or “Mirage” multi-tool as part of a subscription, you can SUBSCRIBE by June 15th.
Additionally, if you subscribe after June 15th, you can pick up these and previous month’s flair (going back to 2014) from the Subscriber-Exclusive store. You can fill in any gaps as well as pick up extras to gift to non-Subscribers.
## SHIP OF THE MONTH
The Anvil Carrack features reinforced fuel tanks for long-duration flight, an advanced jump drive, and a dedicated computer core for jump charting operations. Originally a military exclusive, the Carrack is now available for civilian use. On-board accommodations include crew, medical, and repair facilities alongside a mapping-oriented sensor suite.
The Anvil Carrack is currently available to all Subscribers until July 1st, 2020.
## SUBSCRIBER-EXCLUSIVE PLEDGE PROMOTION
