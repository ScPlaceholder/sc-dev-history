## Greetings Citizens,
The Aegis Dynamics Gladius has been the work horse of the UEE for a long time. Now the Gladius is entering the next stage of it’s life cycle. For the first time a civilian model is available. The Gladius has a simple design, is cheap and easy to produce, and because of this every citizen owes it a tremendous debt. Now you can see how the Gladius was made!
