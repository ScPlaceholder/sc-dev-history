## Greetings Citizens,
Your CitizenCon 2015 event ticket is now printable in your My Account > RSVP section.
Please bring your printed CitizenCon ticket and Photo ID for entry. If you are unable to print your ticket, you’ll need to have a copy available on your phone.
## When & Where
CitizenCon 2015 will be held at the Runway Visitor Park at the Manchester Airport (Sunbank Lane, Altrincham, WA15 8XQ) featuring a Concorde jet as a backdrop! Doors for the event open to all attendees at 6 PM local time on Saturday, October 10th, 2015. (Concierge & Subscribers: Please note that we do not have a VIP early entrance time for this event.)
## Who’s Coming?
CitizenCon will feature the entire Foundry 42 staff, over one hundred developers currently hard at work on multicrew and Squadron 42. Chris Roberts, Sandi Gardiner, Erin Roberts, Ortwin Freyermuth, Thomas Hennessy and other familiar faces from other studios will also be on hand.
## What to Bring
The event includes a buffet-style meal and an assortment of digital and physical Star Citizen swag! If you have a Citizen Card or any Star Citizen clothing, bring it! You’ll have plenty of chances to pose with Chris Roberts and the team in your Star Citizen attire. And don’t forget your ticket!
## Digital Merchandise
In addition to physical items at the event, all CitizenCon attendees will receive an exclusive digital ‘Manchester 2945’ poster and a CitizenCon 2945 trophy for their hangars. Additionally, all subscribers will receive a variant CitizenCon trophy as part of our hangar flair program. For those unable to attend the event, the trophy and digital poster will be available for purchase for a limited time later this week in the pledge store!
## Livestream
We’ll be livestreaming the entire event starting at 12 PM PDT (7 PM UTC/8 PM BST) on Saturday October 10th on our Twitch Channel.
We look forward to seeing you there!
