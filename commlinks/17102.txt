Hello everyone,
Star Citizen Alpha 3.5.1’s PU release is just around the corner! As always, thank you to everyone who has been helping test the patch on the PTU. We know many of you have lots of questions about the upcoming ship customization feature, so last week, we shared the answers to some of them in a special Q&A As this is our first foray into manufacturer-level ship personalization, we can’t wait to hear your feedback and are looking forward to the Origin Celebration event, and getting a look at your individual design choices.
John, Paul, Ben, and Jared – no this is not the reunion of some 1960s rock band, but the roster of the Star Citizen Live panel that went on air on our Twitch channel last Friday. Our very own fab four took us through the process of bringing the ships and vehicles of Star Citizen to life and answered questions sent in by the community. Re-watch the episode here and find out about the different phases of the ship pipeline from early design to flight prep, how gameplay features affect ship design, and improvements coming to the Freelancer – soon.
