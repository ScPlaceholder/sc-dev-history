## Weekly Wisdom: Heavy is just right
You don't always need a Pembroke environment suite to withstand the extreme heat of inhospitable high-temperature planets.
However, take a closer look at the stats of different undersuits and armor, as some provide sweet bonuses.
The ADP Core heavy armor, for example, protects you between -75 °C and +105 °C, so you won't break a sweat during a leisurely trip to Daymar.
