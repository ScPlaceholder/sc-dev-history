To get you ready for the Finale here is a quick recap of the competition so far. The Next Great Starship, the modding competition that gives fans a chance to create a space ship to be used in Chris Roberts’ upcoming epic space sim game, Star Citizen™.
But wait, there’s more! We’ve updated every Star Citizen Hangar this week to display the two TNGS finalists. Take a look around and pick the one you like best; the winner will be determined via live vote at this site tomorrow when the finale streams live! (3 PM PST.)
Don’t be alarmed if your Hangar seems a little different: because they will only fit in the larger Deluxe Hangar, we’ve temporarily upgraded everyone so that the gunship prototypes can be examined correctly. Your hangars will revert to their standard settings next week.
Sponsors for the competition include Alienware, AMD, Autodesk, Crytek, Cologne Game Lab, Maingear, Wacom and YouTube. The Next Great Starship airs Fridays at 6 pm Eastern/3 pm Pacific.
