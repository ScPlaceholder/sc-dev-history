## Weekly Wisdom: Buying Ships with aUEC
Currently, there are three major locations to purchase ships with your hard-earned aUEC – Teach’s Ship Shop at Levski, the New Deal Ship Shop in Lorville, and Astro Armada on ArcCorp.
To buy a ship, walk up to it, Hold F, and select the ship you want. You will be presented with a description, your balance, the price of the ship, and your remaining balance after the purchase. Click Confirm Purchase to add the ship to your fleet! As an alternative, you can view all purchasable ships from the Astro Armada terminals at each ship shop.
Once purchased, the ship will be available to retrieve from any ASOP terminal at your nearest spaceport.
If you want to test out a ship before spending a big chunk of your aUEC on something you might not enjoy flying, you can always rent it first.
