HULL A
## CARGO HAULER
BEGIN YOUR CARGO HAULING CAREER
Ask any seasoned commercial hauler, they’ll tell you plain and simple that anyone serious about interstellar freight need look no further than MISC’s ubiquitous Hull series. Simply put, it’s how cargo gets around this universe of ours.
The Hull A sports the slightest frame of the series, serving as an ultra-accessible point of entry into the Hull series and the world of big-time hauling. Sporting the same telescoping spindle system as its larger siblings and capable of pushing a significant 64 SCU when running at capacity, the Hull A is perfect for intrepid solo couriers looking to strike out into the galaxy and turbocharge their careers.
EXPLORE THE HULL A
The famous extra-wide cockpit allows for an unfettered view as you sow commerce across the ‘verse.
DRIVE UNIT
Three main thrusters anchor the Hull A’s compact-yet-powerful drive unit.
A masthead-mounted pilot-controlled weapon deters pirates interested in your payload
OPERATOR ACCESS
Easy access to the main cab.
DYNAMIC SPINDLE SYSTEM
The Hull series’ telescopic spindle loader sets each ship apart from the competition, allowing for payload flexibility through its modular accommodations.
CARGO BLADES
The Hull A comes factory-equipped with four Stor-All multipurpose cargo blades, capable of carrying up to 16 SCU in increments of 1, 2, 4, 8, or 16.
Images depict the MISC Hull-A with Empyrean, Horizon, or Dusk paints applied. Each such paint is available as a separate add-on.
