## xē'suelen nyahyan, hello everyone,
Last week, we kicked off RSI Discovery Month, celebrating the legendary Constellation and welcoming the next generation of this iconic ship series. Throughout Stanton, you can visit special RSI installations showcasing the history of the Mk IV and its successor, alongside the new Skylark. Take on a variety of activities, earn exclusive RSI-themed rewards and even complete the special "Constellation Rental Offer" to receive five days of access to put the new Constellation Mk V Centaurus through its paces! Don't forget to share your feedback on Spectrum with our devs.
Spooky season also got a head start with October's Subscriber Promotions: new skeletal-themed flair, including a rifle, suit, and helmet, while the Vehicles of the Month feature Drake's tiny but fierce Pitbull and the formidable Ironclad Assault.
We published the latest PU Monthly Report, offering a behind-the-scenes look at what our teams have been working on throughout September. From Core Gameplay and ship development to technology, art, and more.
The Roadmap Update brought new images for Nyx I-III and introduced a new card for Alpha 4.11: DGS Planet Subdivision, which you can learn more about on the Release View!
This past weekend marked Star Citizen's birthday on October 10, but it was also a very special milestone for Squadron 42 as we welcomed around 140 community members to Manchester for the first hands-on experience.
As we get closer to Squadron 42's launch, our community is going to grow. A lot. There will be countless new faces discovering this universe for the very first time, and we couldn't be more excited to welcome them. But there's something incredibly important to us that we'll never lose sight of: the community that helped get us here.
The Bar Citizen organizers who bring people together all over the world. The artists, crafters, and LEGO shipbuilders who bring our universe into their own. The loremasters who know more about the 'verse than some of us do! The content creators, the volunteers, the longtime supporters, and the countless Citizens who have spent years welcoming newcomers, guiding rookie pilots, and building something special together.
These are the people who have helped make this project so much more than a game.
We wish we could have invited every single one of you. There are so many incredible people in this community, and 140 guests doesn't even begin to scratch the surface. We hope this weekend was just the beginning of many moments we'll get to celebrate together as we get closer to launch.
Attendees spent five hours playing Squadron 42, followed by an additional hour experiencing a unique chapter of the story aboard an adrift Xi'an ship. If you had a keen eye during CitizenCon Manchester 2954, you might have already caught a glimpse of it! That moment gave me chills, especially as a Xi'an nya'to.y'an (teacher). As our guests make their way home, we're sure you'll hear plenty more about their experiences directly from them.
We're getting closer to the day when Squadron 42 will be in the hands of pilots everywhere. Thank you for being part of this journey. We can't wait for what's to come.
For now, enjoy the Squadron 42 Trailer, and Wishlist on Steam! We'll be sharing photos from the weekend Squadron 42 event soon, so keep an eye on our channels!
