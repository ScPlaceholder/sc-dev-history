## Happy Monday, everyone!
Over the weekend, the filthiest race in the 'verse lived up to its name as the annual, community-organized Daymar Rally took over Crusader's second moon. Our team had a blast tuning in to watch the largest gathering of players on a single shard in the Rally's history. Racers pushed the limits across a grueling 510 km course, battling it out in three divisions: Buggy, Truck, and Hover Bike.
With teams from 37 nations around the globe, this year's event was one NOT to miss - but just in case you did, check out the replay on the ATMO Esports Twitch channel. Congratulations to all the racers and participants, and a special shoutout to the countless individuals who helped organize and bring this year's event to life!
