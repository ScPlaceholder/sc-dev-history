## Weekly Wisdom: Mining Safety Lesson
You’re in a cave, you’ve found a nice rock, going to mine yourself some shiny crystal shards to trade in for some money, life’s good. You crank up that multi-tool and go to town. But something seems up, the rock starts to glow. You keep on going anyway, you’re sure all that beeping and red flashing is totally normal. Then, out of nowhere, the rock explodes, and you die. Your tool is lost in a cave on a faraway planet, and you wake up loot-less in Port Olisar.
You, like many others, made the mistake of not keeping an eye on your rock’s energy level (located on the left side of the multi-tool’s UI and the bottom left side of the Prospector’s UI). You’ll want to work your way up to the green zone, and stay there until the rock fractures and you can collect your loot! When you enter the red zone, you’re in danger of your rock exploding and taking you with it.
Some of you may say “Hey Molly, this isn’t wisdom, it’s common knowledge!”, but a bit of quick research shows that some of you may need a refresher.
