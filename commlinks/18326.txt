Ship Showdown Top Four & Free Fly
Every year, Ship Showdown challenges the community to champion their favorite vehicle. With more than 100 flyable ships in the 'verse, there's no shortage of passionate opinions! After thousands of creative submissions (screenshots, videos, Lego™ miniatures, original songs, 3D models, and more), and 2 rounds of epic head-to-head battles, we've finally entered the FINAL FOUR! However, now it’s time to crown the ultimate Ship Showdown champion and determine which ship is the true community favorite in 2021.
Without further ado...
## Ship Showdown Prizes
The Final Four of this year's Ship Showdown will be highlighted at this year's Intergalactic Aerospace Expo with not only their brand new Best In Show paints, but also exclusive manufacturer-themed in-game leather jackets, which all who have one in their hangar will receive on their accounts.
The competition isn't over, though, as the FIRST place winner will also get a special themed in-game backpack! All prizes will be released at the Intergalactic Aerospace Expo (IAE) later this year.
## Take the top four ships for a spin with the Live Showdown Free Fly!
To allow everyone to make an informed choice, we invite you to take the four best ships for a spin. So if you can’t decide between the upcoming finalists, join the Live Showdown Free Fly to put them through their paces. The Free Fly will run until September 27, 8pm UTC.
Last but not least, we have some discounted Starter Packages back on the pledge store from today through September 29.
