Today’s fan spotlight goes to the Cassandra’s Tears audiobook serials put together by the team at Tabula Stellarum. Tabula Stellarum is a guild dedicated to exploring space in the Star Citizen universe and a great example of the kind of unique organizations fans are already putting together! While they wait for their ships they’ve started creating excellent audiobook versions of Dave Haddock’s fiction, starting with Cassandra’s tears! If you’ve ever wanted to experience Dave’s Star Citizen prequel stories on the go, this is the way to do it! You can stream the first episode—or download it for listening on the go—here.
