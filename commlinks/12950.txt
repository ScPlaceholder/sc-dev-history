Shroud of the Avatar has made the initial goal, but there are still alost a million dollars worth of stretch goals to catch… including Oculus Rift support at $2.5 million dollars! Show your support for old school RPGs and a return to the kind of game design that went into the Ultima series by pledging now.
One special addition for Star Citizens: Richard has just announced that Shroud of the Avatar will feature a crashed Vanduul fighter in the game world as well as an alien artifact from the Star Citizen world that you can use in the game!
