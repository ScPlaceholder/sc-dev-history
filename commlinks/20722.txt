Get Ready for CitizenCon Direct 2955!
Watch Parties
With the announcement of CitizenCon Direct, we’re thrilled to share that while this year’s event is going fully digital, it doesn’t mean we won’t be celebrating together. In fact, we’re taking this opportunity to bring the show to you, literally!
We’re sending key members of the CIG team out into the world to join Bar Citizen Watch Parties across the NA and EU on Saturday, October 11, 2025, and we can't wait to meet you all in the following locations! CitizenCon Direct is shaping up to be a ~2-hour digital showcase, going live at 20:00 UK time (BST) / 12:00 PT / 15:00 ET. It’ll be streamed free on Twitch and YouTube, packed with updates, reveals, and surprises!
*All times listed below are local time
