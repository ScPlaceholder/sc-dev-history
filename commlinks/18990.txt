Traveler's Guide to the Galaxy: IAE 2952
Welcome to another edition of Traveler’s Guide to the Galaxy, your expert companion to exploring the most important and breathtaking destinations in the ‘verse.
This time, we’re wrapping up warm and heading to microTech’s frigid-but-friendly capital city, New Babbage, for the Intergalactic Aerospace Expo. With IAE 2952 starting on November 18, prepare for your visit to microTech early.
## Welcome to New Babbage
New Babbage, the city of tomorrow that has influenced some of the Empire’s most gifted creative minds.
For a taste of inspiration yourself, take a moment to carefully land on the rooftop of the Aspire Grand skyscraper for unmatched views of the city and surrounding tundral sprawl. This vantage point also offers the first glimpse of the Tobin Convention Center, the venue of this year’s IAE, and your next destination, New Babbage Interstellar Spaceport.
To get there, fly low over the gleaming frozen lake towards the city with the Metroloop tracks as your guide.
## New Babbage Interstellar Spaceport
Upon arrival at the main hall of the New Babbage Interstellar Spaceport, enjoy a second view of the city and the Tobin Convention Center, decked out for the festivities, through the sprawling panoramic windows.
They say that laughter is an instant vacation, so start yours with a joke told by the holographic greeter by the stairs to the surface entrance. The greeter is at your service all hours of the day should you wish to learn more about the station, city, or planet.
Further down the hall, Regal Luxury Rentals caters to all your vehicular needs. Experience the royal treatment and don’t miss out on the expanded fleet of rental vehicles during the last days of IAE.
Don’t forget to pick up a travel companion from the souvenir store, where adorable Pico (the microTech mascot) plushies are available in a variety of poses. And if you need a pick-me-up for the action ahead, the store also serves a selection of protein bars, soda, and Vestal Water.
## The Metroloop
This beautiful city was founded on high technology, and that's reflected in Stanton's most comfortable, fastest, and safest intra-city transportation system. The Metroloop will take you to any prominent place within seconds.
## Aspire Grand
It's time to explore your home away from home. Take the Metroloop and visit the Aspire Grand, where you'll find The Nest Apartments.
The Nest Apartments offer immaculate accommodations in the heart of the city and your place to stay during your visit. You can also feel secure knowing that a world-class healthcare facility is on the premises.

## Tobin Convention Center
It's finally time to head to the Tobin Convention Center!
Browse through the merchandise from your favorite vehicle manufacturers and snag mementos to commemorate your IAE 2952 experience at the Merchandise Store. This is also the perfect place to meet up with friends before hitting the exhibits!
## Expo Halls
The IAE is the undeniable event of the season for vehicle enthusiasts and this year's promises to be the best yet. With the biggest manufacturers coming together to showcase everything they have to offer, it's vehicles galore! Not only can you admire the vehicles on display, but you can also test-fly the almost 120 ships and vehicles in the expo halls.
You can also glance into the future at the holoviewers for a preview of tomorrow’s vehicles today.
And, did we mention that there will be surprises throughout the event?
Stay hydrated at the various refreshment stands in the hall, which also offer Torpedo Burritos for speedy refueling so you can get right back to enjoying the event!
Got tumbled around by the Tumbril tanks and you can't find the MISC mining ships? Consult the convenient maps that show the layout of all the manufacturers' exhibits in the expo hall so you don't miss anything.
Need a breather from the masses or some privacy to discuss the latest manufacturer reveals with your friends? Find sanctuary at one of the chillout areas around the expo hall.
## Take A Ride On The Ice
Ready to venture outside? Don’t miss out on the perfect opportunity to put IAE 2952’s vehicles through their paces. For those feeling more adventurous, test the limits of your ground vehicles (and nerves) on microTech’s numerous frozen lakes. Thanks to permafrost, you don't have to worry about falling through the ice, no matter your vehicle's weight class.
## The Commons
Take the city's pulse up close and personal by hopping on the Metroloop to the Commons, a place built especially for microTech’s highly skilled inhabitants to relax, network, and charge their creative batteries. The Commons also boasts unique shops to properly kit you out for sub-zero exploration and keep you on the bleeding edge of technology.
From the latest looks by Aparelli to heavy-duty environment suits from Shubin Interstellar, New Babbage’s extensive clothing options ensure you’re well-dressed for wherever the adventure takes you.
## A Pleasure For The Palate
Tantalize your tastebuds; whether you're craving classic comfort food from Whammers or want to sample something new at the food court, there's something for everyone.
If you've indulged in one too many burgers, take advantage of the indoor rock-climbing wall or the fitness center to burn those extra calories.
## Wally's Bar
Exhausted after a long day of exploring the expo hall? Be dazzled by colorful cocktails expertly prepared by the bartenders at Wally's, THE place on microTech for late-night mixers and social meetups!
## The Beauty Of microTech
microTech's unique climate ensures a myriad of outdoor cold-weather activities are available all year round. A short flight takes you into the breathtaking, rugged wilderness; a haven for the inhabitants of microTech when they want to escape the confines of the city.
Go for a hike in the evergreen forests, marvel at the hardy wild vegetation on the snow-capped mountains, or have a moment of quiet reflection by the river's clear waters and just enjoy being in nature. Trust us, it's worth it.
