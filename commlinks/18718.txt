## Happy Monday, everyone!
Only two days left of Invictus Launch Week 2952, and to celebrate another epic year, we've brought back all the ships that were previously available during the event to test-fly for free. So head to one of the ship rental terminals at the spaceport and select your military-inspired vessel of choice and enjoy the Free Fly with your friends until May 31.
We're humbled and stunned by the sheer number of awesome submissions we received for the Invictus Launch Week 2952 Screenshot Contest, and how you welcomed the tens of thousands of newcomers to our community. By helping them during their first steps in the 'verse, you filled the event's slogan 'United in Purpose' with heartwarming meaning. For that reason, and many more, we'd like to thank everyone in the community for making this Invictus Launch Week GREAT!
As a reminder, you still have until Tuesday, May 31, 2022 (EOD 11:59 PM Pacific) to enter the 2952 Dunlow Derby. Watching you fight for every second while speeding between the struts of the Providence platform makes our hearts beat faster, and the occasional explosions heard at the spaceport are a constant reminder of our truly ambitious racing community. Keep it up!
## Now, let’s see what’s going on this week:
This Tuesday, as mentioned above, will be the last day of Invictus Launch Week 2952 and the accompanying Free Fly. This is also the last opportunity to take advantage of our exclusive event promotions and to test-fly over 60 ships for free.
Also on Tuesday, the Narrative team is back, adding more content to our ever-expanding Galactapedia.
Wednesday will not only see the Anvil Legionnaire Q&A but also May’s PU monthly report Comm-Link published and the Squadron 42 report send out via email. Get all the latest development info by subscribing to the Squadron 42 newsletter here.
Thursday, we’re back with another episode of Inside Star Citizen, where we'll be taking a close look at the upcoming mission system improvements scheduled for Alpha 3.17.2.
Friday won't see an episode of Star Citizen Live due to the many holidays occurring this week, but you'll catch an update to the Subscriber Vault and the weekly RSI Newsletter delivered to your inbox.
Have a stellar week both in and out of the ‘verse!
