## Weekly Wisdom: A Beacon Of Light
Have you ever visited a beautiful place in the ‘verse, enjoyed the scenery, but somehow forgot to pack enough fuel to make it back home? Before you reach for the self destruct button, why don’t you let someone else pick you up?
You can do this by creating a Service Beacon! Just open up your mobiGlas, go to the Contract Manager, and select “Beacons”. You can now create a Beacon for your very own contract that will be broadcasted to nearby pilots. If someone deems the reward you’re ready to splash out juicy enough, they’ll pick you up and safely drop you off at the location you selected when creating your Personal Transport Beacon.
