## Hello everyone, xē'suelen nyahyan
Last week packed a punch with an all new patch, a fresh Comms-Dive, and events both in-game and out!
Monday kicked off with the conclusion of the Race for Stanton event, with Hurston Dynamics claiming victory in the Regen Crisis! Citizens rallied to haul, mine, defend, and recover cargo to support the cause. By September 16, Hurston Dynamics had amassed a staggering 337,131,104 SCU, followed by microTech, Crusader, and then ArcCorp. Your efforts made this one for the UEE history books, and rewards have already been distributed, with more coming!
Comms-Dive #3 dropped Tuesday, answering a variety of your recent questions, covering everything from ship lights to incoming UI improvements. If you haven't yet, dive in on Spectrum!
A new episode of Behind the Ships introduced the RSI Apollo, the perfect remedy for injuries and adventuring mishaps. You can also explore the official RSI Apollo page and its dedicated Q&A to learn more about RSI's latest.
The week continued with a new patch release, Alpha 4.3.1: Virtue's Descent. We've been excited to see you all exploring the dangerous depths of the Onyx facilities. If you haven't accepted Arken Mallor's new mission yet, do so now and discover Project Hyperion and its new challenges, improved puzzles, balancing, and of course, loot!
Finally, over the weekend, the Bar Citizen World Tour landed in Rome, Italy. The team had an incredible time connecting in-person with the Italian community. Truly a special event! Big shoutout to all the organizers for putting together such a fantastic Bar Citizen!
