## Happy Monday, everyone!
Last week, the doors of the Tobin Expo Center closed on the 2954 Intergalactic Aerospace Expo (IAE). We want to extend a huge thank you to everyone who traveled from all corners of the 'verse to be a part of it. Safe travels to your next destination, and we look forward to seeing you again at IAE next year.
The year may be wrapping up, but we're not done yet! Luminalia is just around the corner and it's time for sharing joy, spreading cheer, and of course, giving gifts! Join us on Tuesday for some festive activities and exclusive rewards, and yes, the Luminalia Calender will be back, so don't forget to claim your daily gifts.
Now, if you're like me and you have a need, a need for speed, you're in luck! This Saturday, from 18:00 UTC, our friends at Xtreme G Racing will be hosting the third annual Aurora All-Stars Grand Prix, where up to 20 Aurora pilots will go head-to-head to see who will reign supreme in high-octane races on Hurston. Catch the broadcast live on Twitch.
Speaking of races, this is the final call for anyone interested in testing their mettle (and metal!) in the filthiest race in the 'verse: the 2955 Daymar Rally. You have until December 15, 2024, to sign up, so don't miss your chance!
## Now, let's see what's going on this week
Tuesday marks the start of this year's Luminalia festivities! Complementing the spirit of the season, Tuesday's lore offering is a piece entitled 'Portfolio: 34,000 Beats'.
Wednesday will have the latest Roadmap Update alongside an explanatory Roadmap Roundup.
This Thursday, we have the last Inside Star Citizen of the season, with a Patch Report episode that will revisit some of the new features and updates coming online in Alpha 4.0.
Friday, you'll receive the weekly RSI Newsletter directly delivered to your inbox. There's no Star Citizen Live planned for this week; we'll return with new episodes in the new year!
Fly low, and fly fast!
