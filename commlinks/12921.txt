This week we’re proud to introduce you to Mark Skelton, Lead Artist on Star Citizen! You saw Mark’s work last week, with the very first 3D character model created for the game… and now you can learn more about him. Mark will appear on Wingman’s Hangar on Friday, so tune in to see him in action!
## How did you get started in the game industry?
I got a job right out of Art School with a company called KoP Media. The Art Director / founder there was Jerry OFlaherty who went on to become the Art Director of Gears of War at Epic. KoP Media was eventually sold to 7th Level. After working at 7th level for a few years, I bounced and landed a job at a small company named Blizzard Entertainment. I worked there for about 6 years and decided to get into movies. I worked in movies for a few years… BLAH BLAH BLAH. Ended up BACK in games. BLAH BLAH BLAH…. Yeah.

## What are you doing for Cloud Imperium?
Currently here at Cloud I’m working on the base Character mesh for our character customization, and working out the general 3d art pipeline. I also manage various aspects of the 3d art department as well. Making Chris Smith get me coffee and breakfast tacos is a top priority, as well as drinking fan sent booze.
