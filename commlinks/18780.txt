## What is Extra Life?
Extra Life is a fundraising program of Children’s Miracle Network Hospitals. The Extra Life community fundraises year-round to Change Kids’ Health to Change the Future. Donations go to children’s hospitals to fund critical life-saving treatments and healthcare services, along with innovative research, vital pediatric medical equipment, and child life services.
Since its inception in 2008, Extra Life has raised over $100 million to Change Kids' Health to Change the Future.

## How long will the campaign be open for?
Rather than make this a limited windowed event, we want to show that the generosity of Citizens is long-lasting and meaningful. We will be leaving the campaign open for the rest of 2022, closing it when we return back to the office in early 2023.
As such, while we dread welcome the donations meant to cause chaos on stream, if you're not able to donate at that time you'll have plenty of opportunities to do so later.
