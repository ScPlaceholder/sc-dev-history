## Happy Monday, everyone!
Last week, we launched Alpha 4.2.1: Race for Stanton, introducing a variety of new content and fixes. First off, we want to give a big thanks to everyone who took the time to report issues during launch and in the following days. Throughout the weekend, the team deployed a number of updates addressing issues related to the Resource Drive event. We’ve made solid progress improving the availability of the Intro mission, and we want to reassure you that we're actively working to resolve the remaining issues.
Additional fix candidates are currently undergoing validation, including updates aimed at various Freight Elevator issues, such as cases where entities interfere with their functionality. We're aiming to roll these out soon, along with further improvements.
To support these efforts, we’ve scheduled a Maintenance Window for Tuesday, July 22, beginning at 13:00 UTC. Our servers will be offline for approximately 4 hours. During this time, all gameplay systems will be unavailable and you won’t be able to access the game until maintenance is complete. We’ll provide updates on the RSI Status Page as the maintenance progresses. As always, thank you for your patience and support as we work to improve your experience.
Last weekend, Tampa hosted the Bar Citizen World Tour, bringing together both new and familiar faces in a day full of camaraderie and enthusiasm. We are grateful to all who joined us and helped make the Gulf Coast stop so special. Our journey continues next at Gamescom in Cologne, Germany — we hope to see you there.
