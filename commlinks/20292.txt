THE PALADIN IS NOW FLYABLE
The Anvil Paladin is now flyable as of Alpha 4.3.2. Please visit this page for details on the ship and how to pledge.
HEAVY BARRAGE GUNSHIP
Anvil Aerospace is on the frontline of Humanity’s defense, forging the weapons that keep Earth systems safe from the dangers outside and within. Continuing tradition, the Paladin spares no one, cutting down threats in a relentless hail of laser fire.
HEAVYWEIGHT TURRET
The formidable main turret, factory equipped with Size 5 Behring laser weapons, tears through shields in seconds, leaving even the most fearsome ships incapacitated. Track mounted for unmatched reach and visibility, the main gun is an offensive and defense powerhouse, whatever the target.
RELENTLESS SUPPORT FIRE
Two dual remote turrets safeguard the Paladin’s flanks, preventing dead spots and ensuring an all-round firing arc. The offensive loadout is completed by six missile racks, providing long-range engagement to end battles early or draw foes in toward the main turret.
UNITED ARMS
Each turret hardpoint can be manned by a dedicated crew member, allowing the pilot to focus solely on flying while gunners maintain pinpoint accuracy. When operating with a smaller crew, the pilot can take direct control of the wing turrets, ensuring no defensive position is left unmanned. During quieter moments, crew members can relax in their own private bunks, with onboard living amenities providing comfort and convenience no matter where the Paladin is deployed.
