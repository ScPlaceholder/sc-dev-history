Last week, we set the stage for some exciting developments as we head toward the end of 2955, and the momentum is not slowing down.

We have seen vast numbers of players pouring into Nyx through the new jump points, and your feedback has been seriously invaluable. Our teams are deep diving into your Issue Council reports, Feedback threads, and everything you’re sending our way as we polish the experience and prepare for the imminent release of Alpha 4.4.0: Welcome to Nyx.
We also launched Tech Preview Wave 1: Engineering, and the response has been incredible so far! Thank you to everyone who took the time to jump in, fight fires (no pun intended), break things, and help push this central new gameplay system forward. The Tech Preview will remain live for as long as necessary to make this focused testing effort worthwhile.
Thank you all for your feedback, bug reports, and the energy you bring to the community. We’re working hard to make the end of year truly fantastic, and we’re doing it with you.
