Richard “Lord British” Garriott is coming back to his fantasy RPG roots with Shroud of the Avatar, a crowdfunded project that aims to recreate the magic of his classic Ultima series with some impressive improvements. Richard originally worked with Chris Roberts at Origin Systems in the n the late 80s and 90s… and now they’re both developing games that should please their dedicated fans! We’re incredibly excited to see the spirit of Ultima make a comeback in a new Lord British RPG. To celebrate the endeavor Chris sat down with his old friend for a Game Changers Q&A segment to talk about their days at Origin and their new crowdfunded efforts!
