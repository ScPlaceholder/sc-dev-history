## Weekly Wisdom: Hidden Wonders of the ‘Verse
You’ve been to the tech-filled domes of microTech, walked the lawless halls of GrimHEX, and seen what Levski has to offer at the Grand Barter, but how many of the hidden wonders of the ‘verse have you visited?
Unique locations exist around the Stanton system, unmarked and uncharted on things so traditional as maps. Finding them will allow you to marvel at the mysterious power of Big Benny, explore the wonder of a partially buried giant, or even just make some cold hard cash with some less-than-legal deals at an off-the-beaten-path drug lab.
Rumor has it that the skills needed to find Benny Henge might be needed soon…
