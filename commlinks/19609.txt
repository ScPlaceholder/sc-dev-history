## Happy Monday, everyone!
The galaxy's premier annual aerospace event, the Intergalactic Aerospace Expo (IAE), is in full swing on microTech! Head out to the Tobin Expo Center in New Babbage every day to take full opportunity of what's on display, as new manufacturers take over every 24 hours to showcase their biggest and best vehicles. See something you like on the show floor? Take it out for a spin and put it through its paces, and yes, you can bring your friends! The ongoing Free Fly event will continue for another ten days, so grab your gang and delve into the festivities!
We're also inviting all mentors in the 'verse to share their wisdom with newer pilots in our Guide to Professions Contest; submit a video tutorial with advice or tips and tricks about your favorite professions and you might just snag a ship! Or, flex your creative muscles in our Design Convention Badge Contest for more chances to win a shiny ship straight off the manufacturing line.
With all the cool stuff happening right now, don't forget that you can look to Spectrum for the latest announcements, the IAE 2953 FAQ, and exciting updates as we approach the Intergalactic Aerospace Expo 2953 finale!
## Now, let's see what's going on this week:
On Wednesday, we'll publish the answers to your burning questions on the Argo's new nifty tow-ship in the Argo SRV Q&A comm-link.
Thursday's Inside Star Citizen is 'IAE, Part 2' with another look at new vehicles arriving during this year's event.
This Friday, members of the community team are off to spend Thanksgiving with the Bar Citizen Milano group, but there'll be a special Star Citizen Not-So-Live episode for you with the rescheduled Thorsten and Guillermo from the Resource Management presentation at CitizenCon. Catch it on our YouTube channel. You’ll also receive our weekly Newsletter delivered to your inbox.
As we prepare to wrap up 2023, we're looking forward to meeting you at the final stops of this year's Bar Citizen World Tour. Next is Milan, Italy, on November 25, and Dublin, Ireland, on December 2. If you're in the area, come by and enjoy a pint (and talk some ship) with us!
Have a great week, and catch you on microTech!
