Show Us Your Colors Celebration 2022
June Was Pride Month, and we invited each of you to celebrate with us by showing us your colors! There were no limits to creativity for this and we challenged you to take a screenshot in-game, craft a card, bake a cake, make a video, as long it was as colorful as possible!
Enjoy the incredible submissions we received for the Show Us Your Colors celebration, which helped make Pride Month colorful in the ‘verse!
Thank you, everyone, for the gorgeous submissions we received for the "Show Us Your Colors" celebration and for helping to make this Pride Month colorful! And remember, no one needs to wait until June to celebrate - we encourage everyone to celebrate your true colors 24/7, 365. You can click on each image to expand!
