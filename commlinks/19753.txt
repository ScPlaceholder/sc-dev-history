Overdrive Initiative
Overdrive Initiative is a series of connected missions leading up to the explosive XenoThreat Global Event.
Over the next few weeks, regular new content will task brave pilots with supporting the Civilian Defense Force
as it battles a growing terrorist threat to the system. All current missions are available in the Contract Manager
of your mobiGlas. Mission 5 is the last mission before the final XenoThreat assault, which is scheduled to begin
soon after Alpha 3.23 goes live.
Rewards
F7A Mk II Hornet Upgrade: All players that complete missions 1 to 5 of Overdrive Initiative will receive a complimentary
upgrade from the F7C Mk II Hornet to the F7A Mk II with the release of Alpha 3.23. When the new patch launches,
just accept the mission titled ‘Overdrive Initiative: Reward’ and you’ll be granted your upgrade (and free 7 day rental).
XenoThreat Armor: The XenoThreat medium armor will be granted to all players who complete each Overdrive Initiative
mission, including the following XenoThreat Assault, Mission 6, in Alpha 3.23. Make sure that you complete the Overdrive
Initiative missions 1 to 5 before the Xenothreat event starts after the release of Alpha 3.23.
Important note:
Players must complete all five Overdrive Initiative missions before the XenoThreat assault begins as the missions will not
be available during the event.
For more information, visit the Overdrive Initiative FAQ on Spectrum.
This Promotion Has Now Ended
MISSION ONE : Intel Raid
Naval intelligence has located numerous XenoThreat cells already operating in Stanton, preparing and scouting for the impending attack.
The CDF needs you to locate and neutralize these groups and recover all remaining intel.
MISSION TWO: Priority Targets
Well done, recruits. Significant data was seized during the Intel Raids. However, XenoThreat are using a sophisticated encryption system that we can’t decipher.
The CDF is now tasked with hunting down high-ranking XenoThreat members and recovering their digital keys so we can uncover more about their plans.
MISSION THREE: XenoThreat Incursion
The good news is the recovered decryption keys worked and we have eyes on XenoThreat’s immediate plans. The bad news is they involve localized attacks around the system.
Get the drop on the attackers and take them out before they can do any real harm.
MISSION FOUR: XenoThreat Surveillance
Evidently, XenoThreat has been getting its intel by hijacking comm arrays around Stanton. Get out there, find the compromised stations, and shut them down.
Stay alert for more information, as ending this infiltration is likely to be more complicated than turning off a few comm arrays…
MISSION FIVE: Supply Request
XenoThreat must be on to us, shutting down all operations and communique in Stanton. CDF wants you to help call their bluff. Resupply the jump point station and stay prepared for an imminent attack.
