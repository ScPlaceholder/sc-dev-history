## Now, let’s see what’s going on this week:
On Tuesday, the Lore Team will publish the latest Galactic Guide, this time highlighting the unique Min system.
On Thursday, we’ll welcome a new episode of Inside Star Citizen, providing behind the scenes looks at the development of our Persistent Universe. This week, we’ll take a closer look at the weather effects coming in Alpha 3.8.
Friday will bring us updates to the Roadmaps and the Subscriber Vault, with the weekly newsletter delivered straight to your inbox. And finally, we’re looking forward to a new episode of Star Citizen Live, which will broadcast on our Star Citizen Twitch channel at 8am Pacific / 4pm UTC. This time, we’ll be having Lead Environment Artist Michel Kooper and Senior Environment Artist Sebastian Schroeder on the show to answer your questions about the creation of Planet Tech v4, the conversion of all existing planets and moons to this tech and the creation of Microtech. As usual, you can submit your questions and vote on the ones you want to see answered most on Spectrum!
