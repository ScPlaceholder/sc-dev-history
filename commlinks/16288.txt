## It’s been a pretty eventful week around here, so let’s jump straight in to what’s going on this week:
Viewing the schedule below, you may find yourself wondering “Where are all the usual shows this week?” As many of you already know, we are smack dab in the middle of this year’s Anniversary Special and our regularly scheduled programs will resume next week. For now, make sure to stay in the loop with our daily AtV episodes here, and track which manufacturer is being highlighted each day here. We’ve had a few surprises along the way, and we aren’t finished yet!
And if the daily AtV’s were not enough, you can find the Q&A threads for the Anvil Hawk and Aegis Hammerhead here and here. Jump in and cast your vote for your favorite questions which we’ll be sending off to the design team for answers. Finalized Q&A’s will be released next week!
Lastly, to cap off the week on Friday, we’ll be live for a special edition of Happy Hour with Community Content Manager Jared Huckaby. Join the team where we’ll be discussing three options for the next Drake ship and YOU get to vote which one will actually enter the ship pipeline. This is your chance to add your voice to the Drake lineup of ships. Show starts at Noon PST on twitch.tv/StarCitizen – we’ll see you there!
See you in the ‘Verse!
