Tips & Tricks
The ships you’re tasked with salvaging aren’t always empty shells. Don’t forget to check the interiors and cargo holds for valuables left behind; you might stumble upon precious cargo, weapons, or items.
Sometimes, you’ll pull up on a slightly more intact ship. Providing you have the space, you can use your MultiTool’s tractor beam to remove functional components and sell them for bonus credits or use them yourself. Prioritize checking for undamaged weapons, thrusters, powerplants, coolers, shield generators, and quantum drives.
As you progress, the ships you’ll be stripping get bigger. Before trying to break down a goliath like a Hammerhead, it’s worth recruiting a support ship with a healthy-sized cargo bay
