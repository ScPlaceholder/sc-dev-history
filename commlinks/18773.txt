## Happy Monday, everyone!
Alpha 3.17.2 is now open to Wave 1 testers! Subscribers, Concierge, and the Evocati Testing group are now hammering away at the latest builds, and we plan on opening up to more testers soon. For more information on the current PTU and how it works, check out our 3.17.2 PTU Comm-Link. Also, in case you missed it, we announced a long-term persistence wipe for items and wallet coming in 3.17.2 last week. Head over to the announcement for the details.
Thanks to everyone participating in our Dynamic Event weekend! We hope you're enjoying the mayhem, whether you battled it out at Jumptown, or are fending off another Nine Tails Lockdown. There's still one more day of dynamic events to go, so check out the schedule and join in the fray.
## Now, let's see what's going on this week:
This Tuesday, the Narrative team has a brand new lore post titled Mining Rocks. This newsletter from Shubin Interstellar for July 2952 reveals a revolutionary new employee benefit. June's Subscriber offerings will also be published on Tuesday both as a newsletter and as a Comm-Link.
Then on Wednesday, we'll release the second 3.17.2 Patch Watch post on Spectrum, talking about the many improvements and fixes coming in our upcoming patch. Wednesday also has a Roadmap Roundup to coincide with the Public Roadmap's biweekly update. June's Monthly Reports will also be released on Wednesday, with Squadron 42's report coming via newsletter.
On Thursday, Foundation Festival begins! Complete with a Free Fly, Referral Bonus, and, most exciting of all, awesome rewards for helping out newer Citizens using the Guides System. Keep an eye out for Comm-Links on Thursday with all the details.
On Friday, you'll receive our weekly RSI Newsletter delivered to your inbox, and Subscribers can look forward to an update to the Subscriber Vault. We'll also air a new episode of Star Citizen Live on our Twitch channel. This week we've got a Gamedev episode with the Interactables Team starting at 8am Pacific / 3pm UTC.
Have an awesome week!
