Greetings Citizens,
The Star Citizen team is currently hard at work building the upcoming hangar module, which will allow players to walk around their ships! Over time, the hangar module will expand to add more functionality (like ship upgrades.) Today we thought it would be fun to show off some of the artwork that has gone into building the module. It may not be as sexy as spaceships battling or marine body armor… but we think it’s very interesting!
You’ve seen the hangar itself in recent videos (pictured here with an Aurora.) Now check out some of the detail work that went into creating the environment: modeling individual fans, designing ladders and concepting out computer interfaces, catwalks and bulkhead walls!
