Hello everybody and welcome to this week’s Fan Spotlight. Today we are going to highlight a few of our favorite YouTube content creators. In addition to the veterans that you all know and love, we tossed in a newcomer as well. Enjoy!
When discussing Star Citizen videos there is no doubt that the name Disco Lando comes to mind. Consistently creating hilarious and informative content, Disco deserves to stand at the top of this list. We at Star Citizen enjoy his videos so much that he even makes a small cameo in our hangar lockers!
Check out his YouTube page here!
While they have only been making videos for 2 months, Corporation Incorporated has exploded onto the scene with fantastic content. From serious videos like Vanduul Scythe takes flight, or silly ones like Boarding Actions Revisited, Corp Inc offers a little bit of everything.
Check out their YouTube page here!
Next up is the ever informative Byronyk. A few months ago we gave him an MVP for his Star Guide video about trading ships and he has since continued his Star Guide series. I highly suggest checking them out especially if you are a newcomer.
Check out his YouTube page here!
Lastly we offer a farewell to the recent MVP winner AnythingFPS. AnythingFPS managed to always find the right visual and musical cues to make his videos beautiful. Through his videos he challenged us to be even better and we thank him for that. Recently he announced that he is retiring from making videos and from all of us at Star Citizen we thank him for his hard work and wish him the best in the future.
Check out his YouTube page here!
Thanks to everybody out there that makes Star Citizen videos. If you know a creator that we missed post their page below and we’ll check them out. Thanks and we’ll see you all next time!
