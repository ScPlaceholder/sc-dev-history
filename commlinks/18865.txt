## Happy Monday, everyone!
Ship Showdown is in full swing and we're in the midst of whittling down your top 16! The 16 most upvoted and cheered vehicles of Phase 1 now compete in daily head-to-head battles in an attempt to be crowned Ship Showdown Champion 2952. Remember, not only will the top eight be available during the upcoming Free Fly, but the final four will also receive special prizes later this year.
Also, if you missed it, check out the latest episode of Inside Star Citizen where we provide an update on the latest with Persistent Entity Streaming development. We've seen a lot of questions pop up about what the experience will be like on launch, and are currently gathering up those questions and chasing down answers with the developers directly.
Lastly, for those looking to dive into charity fundraising this year, consider yourself invited to join our Battle of the Bricks team to help raise money for Extra Life to support children in need.
## Now, let's see what's going on this week:
Ship Showdown Phase 2 continues on Monday as the sturdy Freelancer Max faces the utilitarian Argo RAFT in a face-to-face battle.

Wrapping up the week on Friday, you'll see an update to the Subscriber Vault and the weekly RSI Newsletter delivered to your inbox. Last but not least, we'll be catching up with Star Citizen Live on our Twitch channel now that Jared's feeling better. We'll air at 11am Pacific / 6pm UTC with the Narrative Team.
