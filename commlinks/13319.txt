Greetings Citizens,
As Chris Roberts mentioned during Thursday’s livestream, Star Citizen polo shirts are now available! The polo shirts are 100% high-grade cotton and feature the Star Citizen logo on the breast and an Anvil Hornet silhouette on the back of the collar. They’re just like the team shirts worn by Cloud Imperium developers! (Plus, they’ll be great for the next photo contest!)
Unlike other merchandise we have put together, the polo shirts are not a long-term preorder; we will take orders until Monday, October 21 and then begin manufacturing and shipping them as soon as possible! We know that winter is coming and that you would like to be sporting a stylish Star Citizen polo soon.
