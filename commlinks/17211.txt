## Now, let’s see what’s going on this week:
On Tuesday, the Lore Team will publish Serialized Fiction: One Last Job, Part IV. This series, originally debuted with Part I as a subscriber exclusive in Jump Point 3.9 but you can start catching up on the action right now.
Thursday welcomes a new episode of Inside Star Citizen, our weekly look behind the scenes of Star Citizen development.
Friday will see a new episode of Pillar Talk, our quarterly series providing in-depth discussions on the progress of our game. Did you miss the first episode featuring Chris Roberts Tony Zurovec, John Crewe, and Eric Kieron Davis? Catch it here. The Roadmap Update and the RSI Newsletter will also be delivered right to your inbox.
Our subscribers can look forward to a vault update and the August issue of our Jump Point magazine. Lastly, we also welcome a new episode of Star Citizen Live, which airs on our Star Citizen Channel. At 8am Pacific, 3pm UTC we’ll welcome another Gamedev edition where we sit with Senior Environment Artist Joel Azzopardi from the Modular Content Team and explore the process involved in creating new procedural space station interiors.

Community MVP: AUGUST 26TH, 2019
We are constantly amazed by the contributions made by the Star Citizen community. Whether it's fan art, a cinematic, a YouTube guide, or even a 3D print of your favorite ship, we love it all!
