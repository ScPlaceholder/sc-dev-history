## Greetings Citizens,
We are proud to announce that we will be having another event during PAX East in Boston. In 2014 we showed the best step forward in Star Citizen at the time, Arena Commander. The world was able to see how space combat in the ‘Verse works, and needless to say a few iconic catchphrases were discovered along the way. This year we will be focused on presenting the FPS module being developed by Illfonic. We look forward to sharing the newest information surrounding the next step in building Star Citizen. Here’s all the info you’ll need to attend our event during PAX East.
## Details
Date: Saturday March 7th, 2015
Location: Royale Boston, 279 Tremont St. Boston, MA 02116
Time:
5:00 PM Subscriber and VIP Entry
5:30 pm Backer Entry
6:00 pm Presentation
Admission: $15
Age Restriction: 21+
Tickets will be on sale until Wednesday March 4th, 2015, or until sold out.

Please note that you do NOT need tickets to PAX East to attend the event. Royale Boston is an off-site venue and the event is organized for Star Citizen backers by Cloud Imperium Games. The event’s age restriction has been set by the venue and IDs will be checked upon entry.
If you are attending PAX East the FPS module be shown on the floor at Logitech’s booth:
When: Friday March 6th – Sunday March 8th

We are honored and excited to share the next stage of development of Star Citizen and look forward to interacting with those in attendance.
