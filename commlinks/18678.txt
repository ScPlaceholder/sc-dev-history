## Happy Monday, everyone!
Invictus Launch Week is upon us! Join us as we welcome the UEE Naval fleet back to the Stanton system at this year's Invictus Launch Week celebration. The annual event invites players to test-fly military vehicles for free, experience new vehicles, rediscover old favorites, tour an in-service Javelin destroyer, and much more. Check out the full schedule on the Invictus Launch Week hub.
Last week, we released April's Monthly Report, which is full of info on what some of our teams around the globe have been up to. Be sure to check out the Comm-Link if you haven't.
## Now, let’s see what’s going on this week:
This Tuesday, our latest Lore Post from the Narrative team is a short story titled Homesick, where a young woman displaced by the First Tevarin War attends Invictus and contemplates enlisting.
Wednesday brings the latest Roadmap Update alongside its companion Roundup with all the details. We'll also publish last week's Squadron 42 Monthly Report as a Comm-Link.
Inside Star Citizen returns on Thursday with an all-Sprint Report episode.
Wrapping up the week on Friday, you'll see an update to the Subscriber Vault and the weekly RSI Newsletter delivered to your inbox. Finally, we have a new episode of Star Citizen Live, which will be broadcast on our Star Citizen Twitch channel. On this week's Gamedev episode starting at 8am Pacific / 3pm UTC, we've got folks from the Modular Content Team showcasing the process of building out one of the many colonialism outposts they're creating for the Pyro System.
Have a fantastic week, everyone!
