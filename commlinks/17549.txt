## Weekly Wisdom: Overclock Your Ship Weapons
In combat, it’s the little things that often determine victory or defeat. For example, a higher rate of fire or weapon malfunction can quickly seal your fate. But don’t panic, there are always options to give you the edge!
Jump into the cockpit, launch your ship, and interact with an MFD. Select the menu followed by the POWER category. Then, navigate to the ITEMS submenu. Finally, scroll to your weapon and press OVERCLOCK.
But be careful, overclocking weapons not only increases damage or rate of fire; it also increases heat, energy consumption, and wear and tear.
