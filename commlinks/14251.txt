## The Aegis Redeemer is now ready for your Hangar!
The Aegis Aerodynamics Redeemer
You voted and we listened. The winner of The Next great Starship the Aegis Dynamics Redeemer is now Hangar ready! The dream of Four Horsemen can now be yours! THe team has been working hard getting The Redeemer hangar ready, it will be different then the first time you saw it, during the TNGS finale.
The Redeemer gunship by Aegis Dynamics features a unique set of reverse poly-vector tech engines. The unconventional design may appear unwieldy, but has been proven time and again to deliver exceptional performance. Coming standard with a multi-turret weapon system in addition to twin missile bays, the Redeemer can stand its ground against other fighters while offering high speeds and maneuverability.
Four Horsemen designed an amazing ship, and the Star Citizen team has been been working hard to make them proud. The Aegis Dynamic Redeemer embodies the most important aspect of Star Citizen. Our backers and our shared vision.
