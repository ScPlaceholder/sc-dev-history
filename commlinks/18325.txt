Happy Monday, everyone!
Every year, Ship Showdown challenges the community to champion their favorite vehicle. With more than 100 flyable ships in the ‘verse, there’s no shortage of passionate opinions! After thousands of creative submissions (screenshots, videos, Lego™ miniatures, original songs, 3D models, and more), and 2 rounds of epic head-to-head battles, we’ve finally entered the FINAL FOUR!
If you’re still undecided about who to support, you should activate your Free Fly Code now and put the four contenders for the championship title through their paces. But hurry up, ‘cause the duel between C2 Hercules and MPUV Cargo has already begun!
## Now, let’s see what’s going on this week:
On Tuesday, the Narrative team will release a special Lore Makers article explaining the concept and lore of regeneration in the ‘verse.
Wednesday will bring you the bi-weekly Roadmap Update and Roadmap Roundup. We’ll also dive into some fun data on the XenoThreat event in our latest infographic.
Thursday sees the return of our weekly episodic behind-the-scenes series, Inside Star Citizen. This week, it’s one episode-long Sprint Report. Yep.
On Friday, you’ll see updates to the Subscriber Vault and the weekly RSI Newsletter will be delivered to your inbox. Subscribers will find the latest issue of Jump Point in their account section. We’ll also welcome a new Star Citizen Live episode, which will broadcast on our Star Citizen Twitch channel. This week we have the Narrative Team answering questions about recent topics they’ve been working on including the new Loremaker’s Guide to Regeneration, the continuing work on the Gangs of Pyro and more. The broadcast will begin at 11am Pacific / 6pm UTC, so be sure to get your questions posted to the Spectrum thread.
Last but certainly not least, Digital CitizenCon approaches! This week we’ll be revealing all the details, including panels, upcoming contests and giveaways, and more… Keep an eye out on Friday for all the info.
Have a great week!
Christian ‘Wayne-CIG’ Schmitt
